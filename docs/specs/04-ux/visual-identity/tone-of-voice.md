# 🗣️ Tone of voice

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Voice

Modern and bold ([D40](../../06-decisions/ux/d40-modern-and-bold.md)): short, direct, active sentences that say what
happened and what comes next. In French, "vous" everywhere ([D51](../../06-decisions/ux/d51-vous-in-french.md)).

## Words to use

- The user's own business words: quote, invoice, delivery, time off.
- Verbs on buttons: "Send quote", "Approve".

## Words to avoid

- ERP and database jargon: "record", "entity", "transaction".
- Blame: "You entered an invalid date" becomes "The date must be after the order date".

## Examples

- "Invoice out. Acme pays by 12 Dec." (shown with the option chosen in D40)
- "3 approvals wait for you."
- "No network: your time is saved and will sync."
