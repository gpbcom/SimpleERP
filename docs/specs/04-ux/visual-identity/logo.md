# 🔰 Logo

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Brief

A wordmark "simple-ERP" in Plus Jakarta Sans 800, with "simple" in the text colour and "ERP" in the primary
indigo, and a square symbol (a bold lower-case "s" in a rounded indigo square) for the favicon, the phone app icon and
the sidebar.

## Variants

- Full wordmark, light and dark.
- Symbol only, at 16, 32, 192 and 512 px.
- Monochrome, for documents printed in black.

## Usage

- The wordmark heads printed documents (quotes, invoices) only when the company has no logo of its own; the
  company's logo comes first.
- Clear space around the logo of at least the height of the "s".
