# 🔰 Logo

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Brief

A wordmark "SimpleERP" in Plus Jakarta Sans 800, with "Simple" in the text colour and "ERP" in the primary
indigo, and a square symbol (a bold capital "S" in a rounded indigo square) for the favicon, the phone app icon and
the sidebar.

## Variants

- Full wordmark, light and dark.
- Symbol only, at 16, 32, 192 and 512 px.
- Monochrome, for documents printed in black.

The files are [logo-light.svg](../../../assets/logo-light.svg), [logo-dark.svg](../../../assets/logo-dark.svg) and
[symbol.svg](../../../assets/symbol.svg), the text drawn as outlines so that they render without the font.

## Usage

- The wordmark heads printed documents (quotes, invoices) only when the company has no logo of its own; the
  company's logo comes first.
- Clear space around the logo of at least the height of the "S".
