# 📱 Responsive

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Desktop

Left sidebar and top bar ([D47](../../06-decisions/ux/d47-app-shell.md)); the AI assistant opens as a right panel
([D49](../../06-decisions/ux/d49-assistant-side-panel.md)).

## Tablet

Drawn at 820 x 1180 px, portrait ([D456](../../06-decisions/ux/d456-audit-images-and-criteria.md)). The sidebar
collapses to icons; the AI assistant panel covers the right half.

## Mobile

Bottom tab bar ([D47](../../06-decisions/ux/d47-app-shell.md)); the AI assistant opens full screen
([D49](../../06-decisions/ux/d49-assistant-side-panel.md)); always comfortable density
([D42](../../06-decisions/ux/d42-density-per-role.md)). Every screen used by employees' self-service, managers and the
owner, sales, customers and suppliers works fully on a phone ([D34](../../06-decisions/ux/d34-phone-experience.md)).
