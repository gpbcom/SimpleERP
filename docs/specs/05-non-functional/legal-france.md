# 🇫🇷 Legal rules in France

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Requirement

- **E-invoicing:** every company must be able to receive electronic invoices since 1 September 2026; large and mid-size
  companies issue them since then, small and micro companies from 1 September 2027; e-reporting follows the same
  timetable. SimpleERP must therefore receive supplier invoices through a registered platform from the start (F7), and
  issue them (F6). See [E-invoicing platforms](e-invoicing-platforms.md).

## Where the rules live

Every French rule SimpleERP enforces is a page of the [🇫🇷 France country pack](../10-country-packs/france/README.md)
(D292, D293).
