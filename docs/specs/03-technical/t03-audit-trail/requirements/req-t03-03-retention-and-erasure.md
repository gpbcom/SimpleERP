# 📌 REQ-T03-03 Kept as the law says, erasable where it must

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Entries are kept as the country pack says; personal data in them is separable so that erasure leaves the trail whole.

## Why

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12; D293.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** an erasure, personal data is removed and the chain stays valid.
2. **Given** retention reached, entries summarised.

## Measure

Erasures per month.

## Priority

## Capabilities served

All: F1 to F25 and the sectors SC01 to SC16.

## Tests

(D426).
