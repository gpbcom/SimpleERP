# 📏 BR-F20-09 The customer knows it is an AI

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The AI assistant says it is an AI and offers a human at any time.

## Why

See [D393](../../../06-decisions/f20-hosted-support-assistant/d393-hosted-support-assistant-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 'Are you a person?' | 'I am an AI; I can bring in an engineer.' |

## Applies to

Every F20 use case concerned.

- [UC-F20-27 Get help on a task from the phone](../use-cases/07-employees-help/uc-f20-27-get-help-on-a-task-from-the-phone.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
