# 📏 BR-F20-11 Response times per support plan

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Support is part of the single hosted offer (D374): the AI assistant answers at once; a human answers within the offer's
published times, 1 hour for an urgent request and 1 working day for the others. Self-hosting companies and IT
providers buy a support subscription with the same times (business model).

## Why

See [D393](../../../06-decisions/f20-hosted-support-assistant/d393-hosted-support-assistant-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Urgent request from a hosted company | A human within 1 hour |

## Applies to

Every F20 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No: one hosted offer.

## Open questions

None.
