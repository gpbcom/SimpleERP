# 📏 BR-F20-12 Conversations kept and exportable

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A support conversation, its actions and screenshots are kept for the set period and can be exported by the customer.

## Why

See [D393](../../../06-decisions/f20-hosted-support-assistant/d393-hosted-support-assistant-coverage.md).
