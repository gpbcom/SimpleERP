# 📏 BR-F20-05 Fixes are verified and reversible

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

After a fix the AI assistant checks that the problem is gone; a fix with no rollback needs a backup first.

## Why

See [D393](../../../06-decisions/f20-hosted-support-assistant/d393-hosted-support-assistant-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Rollback to version 3.1 | Health checked, customer told |

## Applies to

Every F20 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
