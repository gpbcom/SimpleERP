# 📏 BR-F20-08 Escalate when unsure

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

When the cause is unknown, the fix is outside the allowed kinds, or the customer asks, the request goes to a human
engineer with the full context.

## Why

See [D393](../../../06-decisions/f20-hosted-support-assistant/d393-hosted-support-assistant-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Data looks corrupted | Engineer paged |

## Applies to

Every F20 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
