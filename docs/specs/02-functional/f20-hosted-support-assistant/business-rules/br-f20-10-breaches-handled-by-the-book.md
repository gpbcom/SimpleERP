# 📏 BR-F20-10 Breaches handled by the book

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A suspected personal data breach is escalated at once, documented, and notified as the country pack says, by the
publisher as processor to the customer, and by the customer as controller where required.

## Why

See [D393](../../../06-decisions/f20-hosted-support-assistant/d393-hosted-support-assistant-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Backup bucket public | Incident opened, customer told the same day |

## Applies to

Every F20 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
