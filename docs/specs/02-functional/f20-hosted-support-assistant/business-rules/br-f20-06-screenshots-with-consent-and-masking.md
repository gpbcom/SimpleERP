# 📏 BR-F20-06 Screenshots with consent, masked

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The AI assistant takes screenshots of the customer's SimpleERP pages only when needed, says so first, masks personal
data and amounts not needed, and keeps them with the request only.

## Why

See [D393](../../../06-decisions/f20-hosted-support-assistant/d393-hosted-support-assistant-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Screenshot of a payslip | Names and amounts masked |

## Applies to

Every F20 use case concerned.

- [UC-F20-28 Report something broken from the screen](../use-cases/07-employees-help/uc-f20-28-report-something-broken-from-the-screen.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Retention.

## Open questions

None.
