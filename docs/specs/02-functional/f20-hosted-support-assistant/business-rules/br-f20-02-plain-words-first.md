# 📏 BR-F20-02 Plain words first

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The AI assistant speaks to a non-technical person: no jargon unless asked, one question at a time, a simple status
(working on it, waiting for you, fixed) and a technical detail panel only on request.

## Why

See [D393](../../../06-decisions/f20-hosted-support-assistant/d393-hosted-support-assistant-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 'Pod CrashLoopBackOff' | 'Your invoices service keeps restarting; we are on it.' |

## Applies to

Every F20 use case concerned.

- [UC-F20-27 Get help on a task from the phone](../use-cases/07-employees-help/uc-f20-27-get-help-on-a-task-from-the-phone.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Detail level.

## Open questions

None.
