# 🔔 Notifications

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Notifications

| Event | To | When |
|---|---|---|
| Fix to approve | maintainer | at once, paged out of hours |
| Status changed | requester | at once |
| Incident | customers concerned | at once, then updates |
| Proactive alert | administrator | at once |
| Breach | owner (controller) | at once (BR-F20-10) |

## Channels

In-app chat, email, the status page; the maintainer's console and phone.

None.
