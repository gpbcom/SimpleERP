# 📈 Reports

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Dashboards

Replenishment report, forecasted stock, valuation, stock analytics (aging, dead stock, turnover, ABC).

## KPIs

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Stock value | By warehouse and category |
| Turnover | Cost of goods sold over average stock |
| Days of cover | Stock over forecast demand |
| Stock-outs | Count and lost sales estimate |
| Dead stock | Value with no move for 180 days |
| Supplier on-time rate | Receipts on or before the confirmed date |
| Count accuracy | Items counted without difference |

## Exports

Spreadsheets; the yearly inventory as a document.
