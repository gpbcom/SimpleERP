# 📏 BR-F7-15 Eco-contributions are shown apart and declared

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An item placed on the market carries its eco-contribution from its EPR scale; invoices show it apart where the law
requires, and the period's quantities are declared.

## Why

Extended producer responsibility (CR-FR-073).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Item without a scale | Warning and listed |
| Already paid upstream | Not declared again |

## Applies to

- [UC-F7-22 Charge and declare packaging eco-contributions](../use-cases/09-compliance-and-carbon/uc-f07-22-charge-and-declare-packaging-eco-contributions.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.
