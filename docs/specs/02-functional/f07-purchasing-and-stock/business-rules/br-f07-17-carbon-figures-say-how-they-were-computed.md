# 📏 BR-F7-17 Carbon figures say how they were computed

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each purchase emission figure shows its method: the supplier's own data, or a spend-based factor with its source;
uncategorised spend is not guessed.

## Why

Estimates must not pass for measurements.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Supplier data 0.8 kg per unit | Method: supplier |
| Spend 10,000 EUR food | Method: factor |
