# 📏 BR-F7-16 No current safety data sheet, no receipt

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A hazardous item is received only with a current safety data sheet, and put away only in a location whose storage class
is compatible and under its quantity limit.

## Why

Incompatible chemicals stored together cause fires and poisonings.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Sheet v3, supplier at v4 | Receipt blocked |
| Class 3 next to class 5.1 | Refused |

## Applies to

- [UC-F7-23 Store dangerous goods with their safety data sheets](../use-cases/02-receive-and-store/uc-f07-23-store-dangerous-goods-with-their-safety-data-sheets.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.
