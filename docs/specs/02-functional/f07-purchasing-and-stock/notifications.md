# 🔔 Notifications

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Notifications

| Event | To | When |
|---|---|---|
| A request to approve | manager, owner | at once |
| A supplier late to confirm or deliver | buyer | at once |
| Suggested orders ready | buyer | daily |
| A stock-out forecast | buyer | at once |
| An expiry alert | warehouse, buyer | at the alert date |
| A held supplier invoice | accounting, buyer | at once |
| A count difference to approve | manager | at once |
| A failed quality check | buyer | at once |

## Channels

In-app, email, phone push; suppliers by email with a portal link.

None.
