# 📏 BR-F3-42 Unanswered topics hold no names

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Unanswered questions are counted by topic without who asked; sample questions are shown with personal data masked.

## Why

Usage reports never show content tied to a person (BR-F3-33).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 14 questions on parental leave | Topic, count, masked samples |

## Applies to

- [UC-F3-19 Turn unanswered topics into knowledge proposals](../use-cases/12-personal-cost-aware-and-learning/uc-f03-19-turn-unanswered-topics-into-knowledge-proposals.md)

None.

## Configurable

No.
