# 🔔 Notifications

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Notifications

| Event | To | When |
|---|---|---|
| A conversation shared with you | the colleagues | at once |
| A conversation deleted in a week | its owner | 7 days before |
| A configuration request | configurators | at once |
| A request answered or its status changed | the requester and voters | at once |
| A knowledge proposal | the folder's editors | at once |
| A proposal decided | the proposer | at once |
| A knowledge item to review | its owner | on its review date, then monthly |
| A thumbs down on a knowledge-based answer | the item's owner | daily digest |
| A sync failed | the folder's editors | at once |
| An outside call cap at 80 % and 100 % | administrators | at once |
| A personal sign-in expired | the user | at once |
| A queued question answered | the user | when answered |

## Channels

In-app always; email and phone push per each user's notification settings (T10).
