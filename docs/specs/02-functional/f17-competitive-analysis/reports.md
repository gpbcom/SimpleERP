# 📈 Reports

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Dashboards

Competitors' prices over time, hiring, tenders, reviews, wins and losses.

## KPIs

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Win rate against | Deals won over deals met per competitor |
| Price gap | Our price over theirs, like for like |
| Signals per week | New facts per competitor |
| Share of voice | The company's mentions over all mentions of the competitors followed (UC-F17-25) |
| Price-index trend | Like-for-like price index per product family over 24 months (UC-F17-25) |
| Data cost against budget | Paid providers' spend over their F22 budget (UC-F17-22) |

## Exports

Comparisons, SWOT, battle cards, as PDF and CSV, within licences.
