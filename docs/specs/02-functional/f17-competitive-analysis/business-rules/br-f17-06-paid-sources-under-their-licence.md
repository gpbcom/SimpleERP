# 📏 BR-F17-06 Paid sources used under their licence

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Data from a paid provider is used within its licence (users, export, retention) and labelled with the provider.

## Why

See [D383](../../../06-decisions/f17-competitive-analysis/d383-competitive-sources-lawful.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Licence forbids export | Export button hidden for that data |

## Applies to

Every F17 use case concerned.

- [UC-F17-22 Follow data-provider costs against the budget](../use-cases/01-set-up-and-profiles/uc-f17-22-follow-data-provider-costs-against-the-budget.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Per provider.

## Open questions

None.
