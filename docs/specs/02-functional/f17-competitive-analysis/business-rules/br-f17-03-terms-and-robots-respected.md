# 📏 BR-F17-03 Sites' terms and robots rules respected

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A source is read only as its terms and robots rules allow, at a polite rate, without logging in to an account created
for it, and never behind a paywall the company does not pay for.

## Why

See [D383](../../../06-decisions/f17-competitive-analysis/d383-competitive-sources-lawful.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Robots rule disallows /prices | That page skipped, reason shown |

## Applies to

Every F17 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Rate per source.

## Open questions

None.
