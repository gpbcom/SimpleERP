# 📏 BR-F17-02 Every fact keeps its source and date

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each fact about a competitor keeps its source (link or provider), the date it was read and how it was obtained; a fact
without a source is not shown.

## Why

See [D383](../../../06-decisions/f17-competitive-analysis/d383-competitive-sources-lawful.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Price read on 3 Nov | Shown with its link and date |

## Applies to

Every F17 use case concerned.

- [UC-F17-21 Watch tenders to answer](../use-cases/05-tender-watch/uc-f17-21-watch-tenders-to-answer.md)
- [UC-F17-25 Report share of voice and the price-index trend](../use-cases/06-price-intelligence/uc-f17-25-report-share-of-voice-and-the-price-index-trend.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

None.
