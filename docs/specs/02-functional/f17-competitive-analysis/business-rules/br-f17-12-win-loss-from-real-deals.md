# 📏 BR-F17-12 Win and loss from real deals

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Win and loss analysis uses F6 opportunities whose lost reason names a competitor; no deal is counted twice.

## Why

See [D382](../../../06-decisions/f17-competitive-analysis/d382-competitive-analysis-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Lost to Rival SA, price | Counted for Rival SA, reason price |

## Applies to

Every F17 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

Reasons list.
