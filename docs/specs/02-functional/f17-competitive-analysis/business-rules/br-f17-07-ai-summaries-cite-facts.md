# 📏 BR-F17-07 AI summaries cite their facts

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A SWOT, positioning or battle card written by the AI cites the facts it rests on; a claim with no fact is marked as an
opinion.

## Why

See [D382](../../../06-decisions/f17-competitive-analysis/d382-competitive-analysis-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Weakness: slow delivery | Linked to 14 reviews |

None.
