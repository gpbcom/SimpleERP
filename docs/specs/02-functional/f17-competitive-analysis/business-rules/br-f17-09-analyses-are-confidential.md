# 📏 BR-F17-09 Analyses are confidential by default

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Competitor files are visible only to the roles the owner grants; battle cards may be shared with sales read-only.

## Why

See [D382](../../../06-decisions/f17-competitive-analysis/d382-competitive-analysis-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Sales opens a SWOT | Refused unless granted |

## Applies to

Every F17 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Roles.

None.
