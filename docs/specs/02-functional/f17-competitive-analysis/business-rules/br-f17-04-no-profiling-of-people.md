# 📏 BR-F17-04 No profiling of individual people

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The module follows companies, not people: a person's name appears only as a company officer or author of a public
statement, is never scored, and staff of a competitor are counted, not listed.

## Why

See [D383](../../../06-decisions/f17-competitive-analysis/d383-competitive-sources-lawful.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Competitor hires 12 developers | Counted, names not kept |

## Applies to

Every F17 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
