# 📏 BR-F17-08 Comparisons are like for like

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A side-by-side comparison compares offers that meet the same need, on verifiable features, with the date of each value;
a comparison reused in advertising is checked against the comparative advertising rule first.

## Why

See [D382](../../../06-decisions/f17-competitive-analysis/d382-competitive-analysis-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Price per user against flat price | Normalised, method shown |
