# 📏 BR-F17-05 No trade secrets, no deception

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Nothing is obtained by deception (fake accounts, pretend customers or candidates) or from leaked confidential documents;
such a document uploaded by a user is refused.

## Why

See [D383](../../../06-decisions/f17-competitive-analysis/d383-competitive-sources-lawful.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| A leaked price list uploaded | Refused, the user told why |

## Applies to

Every F17 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
