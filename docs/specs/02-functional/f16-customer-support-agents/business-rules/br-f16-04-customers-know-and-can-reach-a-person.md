# 📏 BR-F16-04 Customers know, and can reach a person

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Answers written by an agent say so; the customer can ask for a person at any time, and the request then goes to a human
with the whole history.

## Why

See [D237](../../../06-decisions/f16-customer-support-agents/d237-customer-support-agents.md).

Legal values from the France country pack:
[CR-FR-056 People know they deal with an AI](../../../10-country-packs/france/ai-and-recording/cr-fr-056-people-know-they-deal-with-an-ai.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| An automatic answer | Signed "Acme Bâtiment assistant" with a mention that it is an AI |
| "I want to talk to someone" | Routed to a person at once |

## Applies to

Every F16 use case concerned.

- [UC-F16-12 Follow a request's status](../use-cases/05-customer-side/uc-f16-12-follow-a-requests-status.md)
- [UC-F16-13 Ask for a person](../use-cases/05-customer-side/uc-f16-13-ask-for-a-person.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
