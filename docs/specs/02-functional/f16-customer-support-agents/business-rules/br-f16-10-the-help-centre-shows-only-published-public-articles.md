# 📏 BR-F16-10 The help centre shows only published public articles

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The public help centre shows only articles marked public and published by the support lead; it never holds a customer's
data, and each article shows its date.

## Why

The F3 knowledge base is internal (D473); public content must be chosen.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Procedure "late-delivery refund" summarised in a public article | published |
| Internal procedure with refund limits | never public |
| Article mentioning a customer's order | refused at publishing |

## Applies to

- [UC-F16-10 Publish the help centre](../use-cases/05-customer-side/uc-f16-10-publish-the-help-centre.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The support lead chooses the articles and the languages.

## Open questions

None.
