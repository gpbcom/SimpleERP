# 📏 BR-F16-11 Answers in the customer's language

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A request is answered in the language the customer wrote in when the company supports it; procedures stay in the
company's language; a translated answer is marked as translated and people see the original next to it.

## Why

Customers write in their language (D473); the AI assistant translates (F3).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Request in Spanish, Spanish supported | answer in Spanish, marked translated |
| Request in Finnish, not supported | answer in English with a line saying so |

## Applies to

- [UC-F16-10 Publish the help centre](../use-cases/05-customer-side/uc-f16-10-publish-the-help-centre.md)
- [UC-F16-11 Handle a request in the customer's language](../use-cases/04-human-agent-workspace/uc-f16-11-handle-a-request-in-the-customers-language.md)
- [UC-F16-01 Resolve a customer request automatically](../use-cases/01-resolve-a-request/uc-f16-01-resolve-a-customer-request-automatically.md)

## Exceptions

Legal texts quoted keep their original language.

## Configurable

The supported languages.

None.
