# ⚖️ Regulatory

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Rules that apply

- **EU AI Act, article 50:** customers are told they deal with an AI (BR-F16-04).
- **GDPR, article 22:** a person on request for decisions with significant effects.
- **Consumer law:** refunds, withdrawals and guarantees follow the Code de la consommation.
