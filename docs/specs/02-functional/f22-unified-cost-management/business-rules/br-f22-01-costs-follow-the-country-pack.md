# 📏 BR-F22-01 Bills and allocations follow the country pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every legal value of the costs' invoices, credit and allocation (invoice mentions, e-invoicing, VAT on services,
retention) comes from the country pack; in France:
[CR-FR-015 Mandatory invoice mentions](../../../10-country-packs/france/invoicing/cr-fr-015-invoice-mandatory-mentions.md),
[CR-FR-016 Electronic invoicing](../../../10-country-packs/france/invoicing/cr-fr-016-electronic-invoicing.md),
[CR-FR-100 VAT on services to clients abroad](../../../10-country-packs/france/tax-and-vat/cr-fr-100-vat-on-services-abroad.md),
[CR-FR-002 Accounting records kept 10 years](../../../10-country-packs/france/accounting/cr-fr-002-accounting-records-kept-ten-years.md).

## Why

See [D397](../../../06-decisions/f22-unified-cost-management/d397-cost-management-coverage.md).
