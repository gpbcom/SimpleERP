# 📏 BR-F22-11 Suppliers' invoices matched

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Providers' invoices are matched with the metered costs in F8; a gap above the set tolerance is flagged.

## Why

See [D397](../../../06-decisions/f22-unified-cost-management/d397-cost-management-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Invoice 212, metered 205 | Gap 3.4 %, flagged |

## Applies to

Every F22 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Tolerance.

## Open questions

None.
