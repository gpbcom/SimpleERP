# 📏 BR-F22-10 Own keys: billed by the provider

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

With the company's own provider key, the provider bills the company directly; F22 still meters and budgets the usage,
and the hosted quota is not used.

## Why

See [D399](../../../06-decisions/f22-unified-cost-management/d399-hosted-usage-billing.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Own AI key | Cost shown, not on the hosted invoice |

## Applies to

Every F22 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
