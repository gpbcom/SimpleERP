# 📏 BR-F22-12 Costs allocated to accounts

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Costs are allocated to accounts, departments, projects or clients by rules, so that they reach the books and the
projects' margins.

## Why

See [D397](../../../06-decisions/f22-unified-cost-management/d397-cost-management-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Scraping for client Durand | Charged to project Durand |

## Applies to

Every F22 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Allocation rules.

## Open questions

None.
