# 📏 BR-F22-06 Stop at the budget, legal duties kept

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

At 100 % new paid calls stop until the owner raises the budget, except calls a legal duty needs (sending e-invoices,
payroll declarations), which go on and are reported.

## Why

See [D398](../../../06-decisions/f22-unified-cost-management/d398-budgets-warn-then-stop.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Budget reached during e-invoicing | Invoices still sent, overrun shown |

## Applies to

Every F22 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The list of legal duties, from the country pack.

## Open questions

None.
