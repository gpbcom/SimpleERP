# 📏 BR-F22-09 Hosted: quota, then units

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

In the hosted edition each plan includes a monthly quota; usage beyond it is billed per unit at the published price list
excluding VAT.

## Why

See [D399](../../../06-decisions/f22-unified-cost-management/d399-hosted-usage-billing.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 120 % of the AI quota | 20 % billed per unit |

## Applies to

Every F22 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Plans and price list, by the publisher.

## Open questions

None.
