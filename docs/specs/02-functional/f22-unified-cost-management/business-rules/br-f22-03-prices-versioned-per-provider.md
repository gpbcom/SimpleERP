# 📏 BR-F22-03 Prices versioned per provider

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each provider's prices are kept with their validity dates, so that a past cost never changes when a price does.

## Why

See [D397](../../../06-decisions/f22-unified-cost-management/d397-cost-management-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Price rise on 1 Dec | November costs unchanged |

## Applies to

Every F22 use case concerned.

- [UC-F22-21 Record a provider's prices in a foreign currency](../use-cases/01-see-and-set-up/uc-f22-21-record-a-providers-prices-in-a-foreign-currency.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Price lists.

## Open questions

None.
