# 📏 BR-F22-05 Warnings at thresholds

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Warnings are sent at the set thresholds (default 80 %), once per threshold and period.

## Why

See [D398](../../../06-decisions/f22-unified-cost-management/d398-budgets-warn-then-stop.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 80 % of the AI budget | Owner and administrator warned |

## Applies to

Every F22 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Thresholds.

## Open questions

None.
