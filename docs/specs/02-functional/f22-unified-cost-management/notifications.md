# 🔔 Notifications

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Notifications

| Event | To | When |
|---|---|---|
| Threshold reached | owner, administrator, budget holder | once per threshold (BR-F22-05) |
| Budget reached, paid calls stopped | owner, administrator | at once |
| A legal duty overran a budget | owner | at once |
| Anomaly | administrator | at once (BR-F22-13) |
| Invoice gap | accounting | on matching |
| Hosted quota at 80 % | owner | once per month |

## Channels

In-app, email, the AI assistant.
