# ⚖️ Regulatory

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Rules that apply

- **Books:** Code de commerce L123-12 to L123-28; the PCG (règlement ANC 2014-03); archiving 10 years.
- **FEC:** Livre des procédures fiscales L47 A; format BOI-CF-IOR-60-40.
- **Inalterability:** accounting software must keep entries unalterable (CGI and BOI on accounting software).
- **VAT returns:** CA3 (monthly or quarterly), CA12 (simplified regime); DAS2 for fees paid; OSS for EU distance sales;
  Intrastat (DEB) for intra-EU goods.
- **E-invoicing and e-reporting:** from 1 September 2026 and 2027 (Q13).
- **SEPA:** credit transfer and direct debit rulebooks.
