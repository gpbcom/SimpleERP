# 📈 Reports

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Dashboards

Executive summary (KPIs, cash), bank reconciliation status, receivables and payables, VAT calendar, budgets.

## KPIs

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Cash | Bank balances and forecast |
| Revenue, gross margin, net result | Per period, excl. VAT |
| Days sales and days payables outstanding | Receivables and payables over sales and purchases |
| Reconciliation backlog | Bank lines not reconciled |
| Budget use | Actual and committed over budget |

## Exports

PDF, spreadsheet, FEC, the liasse fiscale, the accountant's software formats.
