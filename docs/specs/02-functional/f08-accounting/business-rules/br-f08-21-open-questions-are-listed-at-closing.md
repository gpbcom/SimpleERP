# 📏 BR-F8-21 Open questions are listed at closing

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every question or receipt request of the accountant stays open on its entry until answered, and open ones are listed in
the closing checklist.

## Why

A closing with unanswered questions hides errors.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 2 requests open | Listed at closing |

## Applies to

- [UC-F8-29 Answer the accountant's questions and requests](../use-cases/10-accountant-collaboration/uc-f08-29-answer-the-accountants-questions-and-requests.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.
