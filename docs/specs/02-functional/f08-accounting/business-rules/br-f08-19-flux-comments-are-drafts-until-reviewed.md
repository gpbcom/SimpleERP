# 📏 BR-F8-19 Flux comments are drafts until reviewed

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A variance comment written by the AI is a draft until an accountant confirms or edits it; only reviewed comments reach
the owner's report.

## Why

The AI proposes, people decide (BR-F8-09).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Draft | Not in the report |
| Confirmed | In the report |
