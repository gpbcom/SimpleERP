# 📏 BR-F8-10 Several books share one source

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every book (French GAAP, IFRS, management) is derived from the same entries, with adjustments kept per book; the
framework of each book follows the country pack.

## Why

See [D306](../../../06-decisions/f08-accounting/d306-f8-benchmark-gaps.md).

Legal values from the France country pack:
[CR-FR-064 Accounting frameworks of the books](../../../10-country-packs/france/accounting/cr-fr-064-accounting-frameworks.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| IFRS 16 lease | Adjustment in the IFRS book only |

## Applies to

UC-F8-15

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Books per company.

## Open questions

None.
