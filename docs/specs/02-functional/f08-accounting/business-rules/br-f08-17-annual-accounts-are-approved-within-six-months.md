# 📏 BR-F8-17 Annual accounts are approved within six months

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The annual accounts are approved within six months of the year's end with the earnings allocation, then filed; the owner
is reminded before the deadline (default: 15 days).

## Why

Company law deadlines.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Year end 2026-12-31 | Approve by 2027-06-30 |

## Applies to

- [UC-F8-25 Approve and file the annual accounts](../use-cases/08-year-end-duties/uc-f08-25-approve-and-file-the-annual-accounts.md)

## Exceptions

None.

No.
