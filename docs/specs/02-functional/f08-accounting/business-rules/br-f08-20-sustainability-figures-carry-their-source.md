# 📏 BR-F8-20 Sustainability figures carry their source

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each figure of the sustainability report shows its source (bills, purchases, HR records, entered by hand) and its
method.

## Why

Estimates must not pass for measurements (BR-F7-17).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Energy from bills | Source: 14 bills |
| Water entered | Entered by hand |

## Applies to

- [UC-F8-28 Prepare the sustainability report](../use-cases/09-pay-analyse-and-report/uc-f08-28-prepare-the-sustainability-report.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
