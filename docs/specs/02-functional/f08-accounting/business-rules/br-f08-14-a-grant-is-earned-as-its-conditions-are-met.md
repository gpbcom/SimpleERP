# 📏 BR-F8-14 A grant is earned as its conditions are met

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A grant is recognised in proportion to the expenses it financed or, for an asset, over the asset's depreciation; the
rest is deferred, and an unmet condition shows the repayable part.

## Why

The matching principle for subsidies.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 30,000 EUR for a 5-year van | 6,000 EUR a year |
| 8,000 of 12,000 spent | 8,000 EUR |
