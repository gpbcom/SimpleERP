# 📏 BR-F8-15 Dedicated funds keep their purpose

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Resources given for a purpose and unused at the year's end go to a dedicated fund, used later only for that purpose;
volunteer time is valued in the notes, not in the result.

## Why

Non-profit accounting rules (CR-FR-125).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 3,000 EUR unused for the shelter | Shelter fund |
| Fund used for rent | Refused |

## Applies to

- [UC-F8-23 Keep non-profit accounts](../use-cases/07-client-money-grants-and-non-profits/uc-f08-23-keep-non-profit-accounts.md)

## Exceptions

None.
