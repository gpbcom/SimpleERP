# 📏 BR-F8-11 Revenue follows performance obligations

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Revenue is recognised as each performance obligation is satisfied, with the price allocated on stand-alone selling
prices and contract changes handled.

## Why

See [D306](../../../06-decisions/f08-accounting/d306-f8-benchmark-gaps.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 12-month support sold with a licence | Licence at delivery, support monthly |

## Applies to

UC-F8-16

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Policies per product.

## Open questions

None.
