# 🔔 Notifications

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Notifications

| Event | To | When |
|---|---|---|
| Bills to post or approve | accounting, requester | at once |
| A payment batch to approve | owner | at once |
| An IBAN change to approve | second approver | at once |
| Bank consent to renew | accounting | 7 days before |
| A tax return to validate | accountant | when prepared |
| A return rejected by the EDI partner | accounting, accountant | at once |
| A question from the accountant | accounting | at once |
| An expense to approve | manager, accounting | at once |
| A budget overspent | budget owner | at once |

## Channels

In-app, email, phone push.
