# 📏 BR-F14-08 Vulnerabilities handled by publishers

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A publisher reports and fixes vulnerabilities in its plugins; in France:
[CR-FR-168 Cyber Resilience Act reporting](../../../10-country-packs/france/digital/cr-fr-168-cyber-resilience-reporting.md).

## Why

See [D368](../../../06-decisions/f14-plugin-store/d368-plugin-store-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Exploited flaw | Publisher and store notified, clock started |

## Applies to

Every F14 use case concerned.

- [UC-F14-21 Check a plugin's bill of materials and licences](../use-cases/03-publish/uc-f14-21-check-a-plugins-bill-of-materials-and-licences.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
