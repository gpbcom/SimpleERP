# 📏 BR-F14-13 Payouts reconciled

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Publisher payouts equal sales minus refunds, commission and taxes, with a statement per period.

## Why

See [D368](../../../06-decisions/f14-plugin-store/d368-plugin-store-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 000 sales, 20 % | 800 paid out |

## Applies to

Every F14 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Commission, schedule.

## Open questions

None.
