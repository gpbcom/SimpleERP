# 📏 BR-F14-15 VAT on store sales

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Store sales bear VAT by the buyer's country rules; in France:
[CR-FR-100 VAT on services to clients abroad](../../../10-country-packs/france/tax-and-vat/cr-fr-100-vat-on-services-abroad.md),
[CR-FR-071 VAT on distance sales in the EU](../../../10-country-packs/france/tax-and-vat/cr-fr-071-distance-sales-vat-oss.md).

## Why

The consumer threshold is still to read.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Buyer in Germany, business | Reverse charge |

## Applies to

Every F14 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
