# 📏 BR-F14-09 Traders verified

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Publishers selling plugins are verified and their contact details shown; in France:
[CR-FR-190 Traceability of traders on a marketplace](../../../10-country-packs/france/digital/cr-fr-190-marketplace-trader-traceability.md).

## Why

See [D368](../../../06-decisions/f14-plugin-store/d368-plugin-store-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Company number missing | Paid listing refused |

Every F14 use case concerned.

- [UC-F14-23 Take over an abandoned plugin](../use-cases/07-plugin-end-of-life/uc-f14-23-take-over-an-abandoned-plugin.md)

## Exceptions

None.

No.
