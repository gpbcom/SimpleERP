# 📏 BR-F14-12 Emergency withdrawal

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A plugin with an exploited vulnerability or harmful behaviour is withdrawn at once and installed copies are disabled or
patched.

## Why

See [D368](../../../06-decisions/f14-plugin-store/d368-plugin-store-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Data exfiltration found | Disabled everywhere, admins told |

## Applies to

Every F14 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
