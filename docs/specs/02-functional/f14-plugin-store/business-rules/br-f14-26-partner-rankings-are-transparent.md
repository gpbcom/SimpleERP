# 📏 BR-F14-26 Partner rankings are transparent

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A partner's rank is computed only from the published factors and their published weights, shown on the listing; a paid
placement is labelled as paid and never changes the rank, and the formula's changes are dated.

## Why

Partners compete for enquiries and companies choose on the rank; a hidden formula would favour whoever pays or whoever
the store prefers ([CR-FR-189](../../../10-country-packs/france/digital/cr-fr-189-p2b-platform-terms.md)). The owner
asked for a partner directory ([D533](../../../06-decisions/f14-plugin-store/d533-benchmark-gaps-f14-plugin-store.md));
the same transparency applies to plugin ranking ([BR-F14-10 Ranking explained](br-f14-10-ranking-explained.md)).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| A partner with 31 projects and a 4.7 rating | scores from those numbers and their weights |
| A certification without evidence | not counted |
| A paid highlight | labelled as paid, the rank unchanged |
| A change of the weights | dated, shown in the formula's history |
| Two partners with the same score | ordered by the most recent project |

None.

## Configurable

No: the weights are set by the maintainer team and published.
