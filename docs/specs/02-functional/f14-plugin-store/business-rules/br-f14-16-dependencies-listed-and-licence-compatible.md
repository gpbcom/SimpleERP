# 📏 BR-F14-16 Dependencies listed and licence-compatible

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

Every submitted version carries a software bill of materials listing each dependency with its version and licence; a
dependency whose licence is not compatible with the AGPL-3.0 of every plugin (D377) rejects the version.

## Why

Plugins and sector packs are AGPL-3.0 (D377); the bill of materials also serves vulnerability handling under the Cyber
Resilience Act (CR-FR-168).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Dependency under MIT, Apache-2.0 or LGPL-3.0 | passes |
| Dependency under a licence with no redistribution right | fails: version rejected with the dependency named |
| Dependency with no licence declared | fails: rejected until declared |
| Version with no bill of materials | fails: generated by the checks or rejected (Proposed) generated by the checks |

## Applies to

- [UC-F14-21 Check a plugin's bill of materials and licences](../use-cases/03-publish/uc-f14-21-check-a-plugins-bill-of-materials-and-licences.md)

## Exceptions

None: the store operator cannot accept an incompatible licence.

## Configurable

No.

None.
