# 📏 BR-F14-11 Notice before restriction

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A publisher gets reasons and notice before its plugin is restricted or delisted, except for security or legal
emergencies, and can complain; in France:
[CR-FR-189 Platform-to-business terms](../../../10-country-packs/france/digital/cr-fr-189-p2b-platform-terms.md).

## Why

The notice period is still to read.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Low quality | Notice with reasons, complaint possible |

## Applies to

Every F14 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
