# 📝 UC-F19-14 Publish it in the hosted store

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Summary

The hosting engineer publishes the promoted feature in the hosted store and monitors it.

## Personas

- [U48 Hosting engineer](../../../../01-users/personas/u48-hosting-engineer.md)

## Goal

Available to all.

## Trigger

Approved.

## Frequency

Rare.

## Preconditions

- Approved.

## Postconditions

- Success: listed.
- Failure: none.

## Main flow

1. The engineer opens Store listing (S-F19-14).
2. They write the listing.
3. They publish.
4. They monitor installs.

## Alternative flows

- 2a. Translations.

## Error flows

- 3e. Check fails.
- Any step: a service does not answer; the call is retried first (BR-F3-32), then the message follows the error pattern
  (D122) and what was typed stays on the screen.

- [BR-F19-05 Checks, review and signing before install](../../business-rules/br-f19-05-checks-review-and-signing.md)

## Permissions

Hosting engineer.

## Notifications

- Promoter.

## Audit events

Listings.

## AI involvement

Draft.

## API, MCP and A2A exposure

Through T5 and T6 with the caller's rights (BR-F3-34).

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** published **when** listed **then** hosted customers see it.
2. **Given** installs **when** counted **then** credits follow.
3. **Given** failing check **when** publishing **then** refused.

## Non-functional notes

D408's targets ([Performance](../../../../05-non-functional/performance.md)): 95 % of screens within 1 second; lists
paged on the server, the first page within 1 second; reports within 5 seconds or in the background.
