# 📏 BR-F19-12 The credit is a credit note

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each period's credit is a numbered credit note referring to the invoice it reduces, with VAT reduced accordingly;
the VAT treatment is confirmed by a tax adviser before launch.

## Why

See [D390](../../../06-decisions/f19-feature-factory/d390-feature-price-and-promotion-credit.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Credit of 600 | Credit note AV-2026-0042, VAT 120 |

## Applies to

Every F19 use case concerned.

- [UC-F19-22 Refund when acceptance fails](../use-cases/06-delivery-and-refunds/uc-f19-22-refund-when-acceptance-fails.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
