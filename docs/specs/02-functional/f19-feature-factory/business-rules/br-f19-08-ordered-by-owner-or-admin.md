# 📏 BR-F19-08 Ordered by the owner or an administrator

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Only the owner or an administrator accepts a quote, within the company's spending limit.

## Why

See [D390](../../../06-decisions/f19-feature-factory/d390-feature-price-and-promotion-credit.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Sales asks for a feature | Request sent to the owner |

## Applies to

Every F19 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Spending limit.

## Open questions

None.
