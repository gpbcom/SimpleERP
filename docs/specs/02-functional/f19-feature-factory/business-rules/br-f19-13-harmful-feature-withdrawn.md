# 📏 BR-F19-13 A harmful feature is withdrawn

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A generated or promoted feature found harmful is disabled for every company using it, with notice and an export of its
data.

## Why

See [D389](../../../06-decisions/f19-feature-factory/d389-generated-features-are-plugins.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Data leak found | Disabled in all companies within the hour |

## Applies to

Every F19 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
