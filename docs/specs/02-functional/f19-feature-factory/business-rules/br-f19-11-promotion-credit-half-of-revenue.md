# 📏 BR-F19-11 Half of the revenue credited

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The promoting customer earns 50 % of the amounts excluding VAT that other hosted customers pay for the feature each
month, as a credit note on its own hosted invoices, up to their amount, the rest carried forward.

## Why

See [D390](../../../06-decisions/f19-feature-factory/d390-feature-price-and-promotion-credit.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 4 subscribers (1 200) | Credit 600 on next invoice |

## Applies to

Every F19 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
