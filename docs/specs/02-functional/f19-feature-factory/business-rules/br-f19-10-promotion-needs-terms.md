# 📏 BR-F19-10 Promotion needs the customer's terms

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A customer promotes a feature by accepting the promotion terms (a licence to the publisher to offer it to every hosted
customer, no customer data in it); the feature is reviewed for genericity and privacy first.

## Why

See [D390](../../../06-decisions/f19-feature-factory/d390-feature-price-and-promotion-credit.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Feature contains a client's name | Removed before promotion |

## Applies to

Every F19 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
