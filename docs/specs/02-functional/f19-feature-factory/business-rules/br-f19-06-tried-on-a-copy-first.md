# 📏 BR-F19-06 Tried on a copy first

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The customer tries the feature on a copy of its data and accepts it before it is installed on its live company.

## Why

See [D389](../../../06-decisions/f19-feature-factory/d389-generated-features-are-plugins.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Customer refuses | Changes asked, not billed as accepted |

## Applies to

Every F19 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
