# 📏 BR-F19-14 The AI is disclosed and overseen

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The customer knows the feature is written by an AI; a maintainer reviews it before release.

## Why

See [D389](../../../06-decisions/f19-feature-factory/d389-generated-features-are-plugins.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Release without review | Refused |

## Applies to

Every F19 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
