# 📏 BR-F19-09 300 EUR a month excluding VAT

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A feature costs 300 EUR a month excluding VAT from its acceptance; VAT is added on the invoice; it is cancellable at the
end of any month.

## Why

See [D390](../../../06-decisions/f19-feature-factory/d390-feature-price-and-promotion-credit.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| French company | 300 + 60 VAT = 360 EUR |

## Applies to

Every F19 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The price, by the publisher.

## Open questions

None.
