# 📈 Reports

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Dashboards

For managers and the owner: meetings recorded per team, share checked within 3 days, actions created and
done.

## KPIs

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Meetings recorded | Per team, source and month |
| Review rate | Share checked within 3 working days |
| Action follow-through | Actions done by their due date |

## Exports

Summaries as documents; counts as a spreadsheet; never transcripts in reports.
