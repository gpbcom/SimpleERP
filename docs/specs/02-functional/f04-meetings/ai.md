# 🤖 AI

**Status:** ✅ specified

## What the AI assistant can do

Every action of this capability can be triggered from the AI assistant, within the user's roles; changes are confirmed,
irreversible acts prepared for the user
([BR-F3-34](../f03-assistant/business-rules/br-f03-34-every-capability-is-reachable-from-the-assistant.md)).

Transcribe, summarise with the kind's template, extract decisions and actions, suggest links, draft the follow-up and
the next agenda; during a meeting, answer when asked, at 70 % confidence or more (BR-F4-09); after it, answer from
transcripts the user may see.

## Permissions

Transcripts and summaries as BR-F4-04 and BR-F4-18.

## Models

"Meetings: speech-to-text" and "Meetings: summaries" AI feature settings (F1, D82), local by default; no voice prints
(BR-F4-02).

## Open questions

None.
