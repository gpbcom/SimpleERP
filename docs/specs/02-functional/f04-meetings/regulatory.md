# ⚖️ Regulatory

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Rules that apply

- **Recording private words:** recording someone's private words without their consent is an offence in France (Code
  pénal, article 226-1); every recording is announced and anyone can stop it (BR-F4-01).
- **GDPR:** participants informed (articles 13, 14); minimisation, audio deleted once checked (BR-F4-03); no biometric
  voice prints (article 9, BR-F4-02); access limited (BR-F4-04, BR-F4-18).
- **EU AI Act:** participants know an AI transcribes and summarises (the bot's name and notice).
