# 📏 BR-F4-04 Transcripts stay with participants

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Participants see the transcript and summary; those who may see the linked record see the summary only; HR meetings
(interviews, reviews) are visible to HR and the person concerned only, whatever they are linked to.

## Why

What is said in a meeting stays among its participants. See
[D211](../../../06-decisions/f04-meetings/d211-meeting-access.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| A sales meeting linked to Durand SA | Participants: transcript; Durand's account team: summary |
| An annual review | HR and the employee only |

## Applies to

Every F4 use case.

- [UC-F4-20 Analyse topics and trends across calls](../use-cases/11-insights-and-accessibility/uc-f04-20-analyse-topics-and-trends-across-calls.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
