# 📏 BR-F4-08 Any internal participant can start

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Any internal participant may start a recording of a meeting they attend; anyone, internal or not, can stop it
(BR-F4-01); the start is logged with who started it.

## Why

Whoever needs the record can start it. See [D218](../../../06-decisions/f04-meetings/d218-any-participant-starts.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| A sales person in a customer call | Starts it |
| The customer says "stop" | Stopped |

## Applies to

- [UC-F4-01](../use-cases/01-record-an-online-meeting/uc-f04-01-record-an-online-meeting-with-the-bot.md)
- [UC-F4-02](../use-cases/01-record-an-online-meeting/uc-f04-02-import-a-platform-s-own-transcript.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.
