# 📏 BR-F4-20 Scores come with their evidence

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every score (a call criterion, a candidate criterion, a match score) shows the quoted passages and times it rests on,
the criterion's definition, and who validated it; the user can change it, and the change is kept beside the original.

## Why

A score without evidence cannot be checked or contested. See
[D234](../../../06-decisions/f04-meetings/d234-scoring-calls-and-candidates.md).

Legal values from the France country pack:
[CR-FR-057 Human oversight of high-risk AI](../../../10-country-packs/france/ai-and-recording/cr-fr-057-human-oversight.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| "Next steps agreed: 4/5" | Quote at 38:12, "let's meet on the 20th" |
| A manager lowers it to 3 | 3, with "changed by Marc Durand, was 4" |
