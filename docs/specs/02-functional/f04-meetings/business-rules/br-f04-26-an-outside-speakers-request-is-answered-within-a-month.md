# 📏 BR-F4-26 An outside speaker's request is answered within a month

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An outside speaker's objection or erasure request is answered within one month: their passages and audio are removed or
anonymised, unless a law requires keeping them, which the answer says.

## Why

GDPR rights apply to everyone recorded (CR-FR-051).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Erasure asked, no legal hold | Passages removed, audio deleted |
| Erasure asked, dispute under way | Restricted, reason given |

## Applies to

- [UC-F4-23 Handle an outside speaker's objection or erasure](../use-cases/13-outside-people-and-follow-up/uc-f04-23-handle-an-outside-speakers-objection-or-erasure.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.
