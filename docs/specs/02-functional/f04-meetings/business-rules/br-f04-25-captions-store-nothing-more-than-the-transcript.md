# 📏 BR-F4-25 Captions store nothing more than the transcript

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Captions and their translations are shown live and not stored beyond the meeting's transcript and its retention
([BR-F4-03](br-f04-03-recordings-are-deleted-once-checked.md)).

## Why

Translation must not create a second copy of what was said.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| English captions of a French meeting | Only the French transcript is kept |

## Applies to

- [UC-F4-22 Turn on live captions and translation](../use-cases/11-insights-and-accessibility/uc-f04-22-turn-on-live-captions-and-translation.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.
