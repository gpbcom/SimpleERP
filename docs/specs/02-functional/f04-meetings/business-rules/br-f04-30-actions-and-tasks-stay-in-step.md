# 📏 BR-F4-30 Actions and tasks stay in step

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An action turned into a task shares its status, owner and due date with the task in both directions; on a simultaneous
change, the task's wins.

## Why

One truth for what was agreed (D461).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Task done | Action done |
| Action date changed | Task date changed |

## Applies to

- [UC-F4-27 Keep meeting actions in step with tasks](../use-cases/13-outside-people-and-follow-up/uc-f04-27-keep-meeting-actions-in-step-with-tasks.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.
