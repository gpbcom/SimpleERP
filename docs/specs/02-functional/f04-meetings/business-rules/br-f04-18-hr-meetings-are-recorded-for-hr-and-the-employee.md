# 📏 BR-F4-18 HR meetings stay with HR and the employee

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An HR meeting (interview, annual review, disciplinary meeting) is recorded like any meeting, announced (BR-F4-01); its
transcript and summary are visible only to HR and the employee concerned, whatever it is linked to; the employee can ask
HR to correct the summary.

## Why

The owner chose to record them like other meetings. See
[D231](../../../06-decisions/f04-meetings/d231-hr-meetings-recorded.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| An annual review | Recorded; HR and the employee see it; the manager does not unless HR shares the summary |
| The employee disputes a sentence | A correction request to HR |

## Applies to

- [UC-F4-07](../use-cases/05-hr-meeting/uc-f04-07-hr-meeting.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The meeting kinds counted as HR, by HR.

## Open questions

None.
