# 📏 BR-F4-29 Customers see only the actions sent to them

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The follow-up shows the customer only the actions the organiser checked and chose to send, never the transcript or
internal actions.

## Why

Internal notes stay internal (BR-F4-14).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Internal action "check their credit" | Not sent |
| Customer's action | Sent |

## Applies to

- [UC-F4-26 Receive and confirm the agreed actions](../use-cases/13-outside-people-and-follow-up/uc-f04-26-receive-and-confirm-the-agreed-actions.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.
