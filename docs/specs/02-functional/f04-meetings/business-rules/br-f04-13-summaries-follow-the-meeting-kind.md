# 📏 BR-F4-13 Summaries follow the meeting kind

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A summary follows its meeting kind's template, or the default shape (summary, decisions, actions, open points);
configurators create and adapt templates per kind (F2); the organiser can change the kind at review.

## Why

Site minutes and sales calls need different sections. See
[D224](../../../06-decisions/f04-meetings/d224-summary-templates.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| A site meeting | Minutes: attendees, progress per trade, safety points, decisions, actions |
| A sales call | Needs, objections, next steps, price discussed |

## Applies to

- [UC-F4-05](../use-cases/03-review-a-meeting/uc-f04-05-review-a-meeting.md)

## Exceptions

HR meetings follow UC-F4-07.

## Configurable

Yes: templates per kind, by configurators (F2).

## Open questions

None.
