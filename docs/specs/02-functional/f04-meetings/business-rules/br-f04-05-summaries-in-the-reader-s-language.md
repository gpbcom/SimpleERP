# 📏 BR-F4-05 Summaries in the reader's language

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The meeting's languages are detected, mixed meetings included; the transcript keeps what was said; the summary is in the
organiser's language, and any reader can translate it into theirs, marked as a translation.

## Why

Teams and customers speak several languages. See [D215](../../../06-decisions/f04-meetings/d215-meeting-languages.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| A French and German meeting | Transcript in both; summary in French for a French organiser |
| A German reader | "Translate": the summary in German, marked |

## Applies to

Every F4 use case.

- [UC-F4-22 Turn on live captions and translation](../use-cases/11-insights-and-accessibility/uc-f04-22-turn-on-live-captions-and-translation.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
