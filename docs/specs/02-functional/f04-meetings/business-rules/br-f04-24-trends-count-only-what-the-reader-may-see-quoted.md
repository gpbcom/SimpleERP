# 📏 BR-F4-24 Trends count only what the reader may see quoted

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Topics count every checked meeting of the reader's scope; a passage is quoted only from meetings the reader may see
([BR-F4-04](br-f04-04-transcripts-stay-with-participants.md)).

## Why

Trends must not leak passages of meetings one could not open.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Manager reads topics | Counts and quotes from his team's calls |
| Passage of an HR meeting | Never counted in sales topics |

## Applies to

- [UC-F4-20 Analyse topics and trends across calls](../use-cases/11-insights-and-accessibility/uc-f04-20-analyse-topics-and-trends-across-calls.md)
