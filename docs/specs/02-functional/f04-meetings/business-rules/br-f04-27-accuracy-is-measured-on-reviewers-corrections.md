# 📏 BR-F4-27 Accuracy is measured on reviewers' corrections

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Transcription accuracy is the share of words not corrected at review, per language and model, shown only with enough
reviewed meetings.

## Why

A measure from real use beats a vendor's figure.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 4 % of words corrected | 96 % |
| 8 reviewed meetings | Not enough data |

## Applies to

- [UC-F4-24 Track transcription accuracy per language and model](../use-cases/11-insights-and-accessibility/uc-f04-24-track-transcription-accuracy-per-language-and-model.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The minimum of meetings and the bar (Proposed: 20 meetings, 90 %).

None.
