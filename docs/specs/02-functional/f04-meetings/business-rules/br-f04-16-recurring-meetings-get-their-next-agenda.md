# 📏 BR-F4-16 Recurring meetings get their next agenda

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

For a recurring meeting, the next agenda is drafted from the previous occurrence's open actions and open points, and
shared with the participants before it; the organiser edits it.

## Why

Recurring meetings start from where the last one ended. See
[D227](../../../06-decisions/f04-meetings/d227-meeting-reuse.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Weekly site meeting | Agenda: 3 open actions, 1 open point, sent the day before |

## Applies to

- [UC-F4-06](../use-cases/04-find-and-reuse-meetings/uc-f04-06-find-and-reuse-past-meetings.md)

## Exceptions

HR meetings follow UC-F4-07.

## Configurable

When it is sent.

## Open questions

None.
