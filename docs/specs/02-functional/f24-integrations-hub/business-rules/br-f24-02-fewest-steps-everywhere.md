# 📏 BR-F24-02 Every connector in the fewest steps

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every outside connector of SimpleERP, in every capability, is enabled with as few steps and actions as possible: OAuth
sign-in where offered, settings discovered and pre-filled, the AI assistant doing the rest; a key, when needed, is asked
in one field with a link to where to find it.

## Why

See [D429](../../../06-decisions/f24-integrations-hub/d429-fewest-steps-for-every-connector.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Connect HubSpot | Choose, sign in, confirm the proposed sync |

## Applies to

Every F24 use case concerned.

- [UC-F24-21 Connect a bank, payment, shop, accounting or carrier tool](../use-cases/06-business-tools/uc-f24-21-connect-a-bank-payment-shop-accounting-or-carrier-tool.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
