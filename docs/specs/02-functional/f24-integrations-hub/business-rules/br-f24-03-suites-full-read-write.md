# 📏 BR-F24-03 Office suites: full read and write

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The openDesk and La Suite numérique connectors give read and write access to every component API of the suite, within
the user's rights in the suite and in simple-ERP.

## Why

See [D428](../../../06-decisions/f24-integrations-hub/d428-office-suites-full-api.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Save an invoice PDF to Nextcloud | Written in the company folder |

## Applies to

Every F24 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Components enabled.

## Open questions

None.
