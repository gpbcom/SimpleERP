# 📏 BR-F24-06 The list reviewed every year

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The default list is reviewed every year against market presence in Europe and France; a tool leaving the list keeps
working for its users until its end of support is announced.

## Why

See [D430](../../../06-decisions/f24-integrations-hub/d430-fifty-ats-and-crm.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Tool loses its market | Replaced next year, users told |

## Applies to

Every F24 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Review date.

## Open questions

None.
