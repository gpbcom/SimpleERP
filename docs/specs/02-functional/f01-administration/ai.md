# 🤖 AI

**Status:** ✅ specified

## What the AI assistant can do

Every action of this capability can be triggered from the AI assistant, within the user's roles; changes are confirmed,
irreversible acts prepared for the user
([BR-F3-34](../f03-assistant/business-rules/br-f03-34-every-capability-is-reachable-from-the-assistant.md)).

- Explain fields and roles; suggest the sector pack; prefill setup steps for approval (D62).
- Prepare invitations from a pasted list, for approval (UC-F1-03).
- Suggest who takes over a leaver's items (UC-F1-05).
- Answer who may do what (UC-F1-06).
- Suggest GDPR purposes and legal bases, for approval (UC-F1-12).

## Permissions

Nothing the AI assistant prepares is saved without the administrator's approval; it sees only what the administrator may
see (BR-F1-02).

## Models

The AI assistant's provider setting ([D82](../../06-decisions/f01-administration/d82-ai-provider-per-feature.md)).
