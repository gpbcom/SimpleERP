# 📏 BR-F1-30 A closed company keeps only what the law requires

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

After a company is closed, data a law requires is kept for its period (accounting, payroll, tills) in a sealed archive;
everything else is deleted.

## Why

Closing must not break legal retention, nor keep personal data longer than needed.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Accounting entries | Kept 10 years (CR-FR-002) |
| Assistant conversations | Deleted at closing |

## Applies to

- [UC-F1-23 Close a company and export all its data](../use-cases/14-close-a-company/uc-f01-23-close-a-company-and-export-all-its-data.md)

## Exceptions

None.
