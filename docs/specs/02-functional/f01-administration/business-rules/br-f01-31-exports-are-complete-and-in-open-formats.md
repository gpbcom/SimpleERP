# 📏 BR-F1-31 Exports are complete and in open formats

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A full export holds every record, document and log of the company in open formats (CSV, JSON, the files themselves, the
FEC), with a summary of counts to check it.

## Why

The company must be able to leave without being held by its data (CR-FR-170).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 12,400 invoices in simple-ERP | 12,400 in the export summary |
| A scanned bill | The original file, with its record |

## Applies to

- [UC-F1-23 Close a company and export all its data](../use-cases/14-close-a-company/uc-f01-23-close-a-company-and-export-all-its-data.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
