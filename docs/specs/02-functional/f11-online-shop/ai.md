# 🤖 AI

**Status:** ✅ specified

## What the AI assistant can do

Every action of this capability can be triggered from the AI assistant, within the user's roles; changes are confirmed,
irreversible acts prepared for the user
([BR-F3-34](../f03-assistant/business-rules/br-f03-34-every-capability-is-reachable-from-the-assistant.md)).

Build a first website from a description, write product texts, titles and SEO descriptions, translate pages, suggest
cross-selling and promotions within the reference-price rule, summarise reviews, explain a drop in conversion, draft
legal pages from the country pack's models for the legal assistant to check (F15).

## Permissions

The user's roles; publishing a legal page stays with the administrator (BR-F11-15).

## Models

"Website builder", "Product texts" and "Page translation" AI feature settings in F1 (D82), local by default.

## Open questions

None.
