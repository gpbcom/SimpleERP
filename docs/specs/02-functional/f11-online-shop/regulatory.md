# ⚖️ Regulatory

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Rules that apply

Every legal value comes from the company's country pack (D293). In France: pre-contract information and the order
button, the withdrawal function and refunds, delivery within 30 days, the legal guarantee, reviews, legal notices,
trackers and consent, prices and reductions, marketing consent, VAT on distance sales, accessibility, producer
identifiers; see the list below.
