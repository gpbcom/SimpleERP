# 📈 Reports

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Dashboards

Visits, sources, conversion, carts and their recovery, top products and categories, revenue per website, channel and
country, promotions used, returns and withdrawals, reviews, delivery times; visitor tracking only after consent
(BR-F11-10).

## KPIs

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Conversion rate | Orders over visits |
| Average order value | Revenue over orders |
| Cart abandonment | Abandoned carts over carts |
| Recovered carts | Carts ordered after a reminder |
| Withdrawal rate | Withdrawals over orders |
| On-time delivery | Orders delivered by the promised date |
| Channel share | Revenue per website and marketplace |

## Exports

Orders, customers and products as CSV; analytics per period; invoices and entries go to F6 and F8.

None.
