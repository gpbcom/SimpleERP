# 📏 BR-F11-17 Chargebacks are answered before their deadline

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

A chargeback gathers its evidence at once and is answered before the acquirer's deadline; accounting is alerted 2 days
before (Proposed).

## Why

A missed deadline is a lost chargeback.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Due 11-26 | Alert 11-24 |
| Lost | Loss and fee posted |

## Applies to

- [UC-F11-22 Handle a chargeback](../use-cases/07-payment-risk/uc-f11-22-handle-a-chargeback.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

None.
