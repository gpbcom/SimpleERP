# 📏 BR-F11-18 A channel shows only complete products

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A product is published on a channel only when the channel's required attributes, media and texts are complete in its
language.

## Why

Incomplete listings are refused or mislead.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Care code missing | Not on the marketplace |

## Applies to

- [UC-F11-23 Enrich product content per channel](../use-cases/08-product-content-and-listings/uc-f11-23-enrich-product-content-per-channel.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.
