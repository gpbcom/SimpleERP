# 📏 BR-F11-09 Reviews follow the review rules

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Published reviews follow the country pack's review rules: the checking process stated, the date shown, the reason for a
rejection given, a reporting function; in France:
[CR-FR-068 Consumer reviews published online](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-068-online-reviews.md).

## Why

See [D308](../../../06-decisions/f11-online-shop/d308-online-shop-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Review rejected | The author is told why |

## Applies to

Every F11 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Moderation before or after publication.

## Open questions

None.
