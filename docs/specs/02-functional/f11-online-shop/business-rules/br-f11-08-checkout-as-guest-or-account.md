# 📏 BR-F11-08 Checkout as a guest or with an account

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each website chooses whether checkout needs an account, allows guests, or both.

## Why

See [D308](../../../06-decisions/f11-online-shop/d308-online-shop-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Guest allowed | Order with an email only |

## Applies to

Every F11 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Per website.

## Open questions

None.
