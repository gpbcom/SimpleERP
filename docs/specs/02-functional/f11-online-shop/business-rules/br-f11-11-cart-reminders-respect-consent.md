# 📏 BR-F11-11 Cart reminders respect consent

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An abandoned-cart reminder goes only to a contact who gave an email and did not object; marketing content in
it follows the marketing consent rule; in France:
[CR-FR-063 Consent for email and SMS marketing](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-063-electronic-marketing-consent.md).

## Why

The CNIL position on cart reminders is not read yet.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Guest objected | No reminder |

## Applies to

Every F11 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Delay and template.

## Open questions

None.
