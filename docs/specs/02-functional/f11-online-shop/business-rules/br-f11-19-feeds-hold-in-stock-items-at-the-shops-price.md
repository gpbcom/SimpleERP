# 📏 BR-F11-19 Feeds hold in-stock items at the shop's price

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A feed holds only in-stock, published items at the price shown on the shop, including VAT and delivery, refreshed when
stock or price changes.

## Why

Comparators penalise and shoppers distrust wrong prices.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Sold out | Removed |
| Price changed | Feed refreshed |

## Applies to

- [UC-F11-24 Publish product feeds to shopping comparators](../use-cases/08-product-content-and-listings/uc-f11-24-publish-product-feeds-to-shopping-comparators.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.
