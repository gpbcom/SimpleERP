# 📏 BR-F11-01 The shop follows the country pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every legal value of the shop (what is shown before an order, the withdrawal function and refunds, delivery dates,
guarantees, reviews, legal notices, trackers, prices and reductions, VAT) comes from the company's country pack; in
France: [CR-FR-036 Consumer withdrawal right](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-036-withdrawal-right.md),
[CR-FR-065 Information before an online order](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-065-online-pre-contract-information.md),
[CR-FR-066 Delivery within 30 days](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-066-delivery-within-30-days.md),
[CR-FR-067 Legal guarantee of conformity](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-067-legal-guarantee-of-conformity.md),
[CR-FR-068 Consumer reviews published online](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-068-online-reviews.md),
[CR-FR-069 Website legal notices](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-069-website-legal-notices.md),
[CR-FR-070 Consent before cookies and trackers](../../../10-country-packs/france/data-protection/cr-fr-070-cookie-consent.md).

## Why

See [D308](../../../06-decisions/f11-online-shop/d308-online-shop-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| A French consumer orders | The pre-contract information and the withdrawal function are shown |

## Applies to

Every F11 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
