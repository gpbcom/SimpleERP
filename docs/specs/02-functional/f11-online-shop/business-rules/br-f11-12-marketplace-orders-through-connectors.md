# 📏 BR-F11-12 Marketplace orders through connectors

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Marketplace orders arrive through T12 connectors; stock and prices are sent back; fees and disputes stay in the
marketplace.

## Why

See [D309](../../../06-decisions/f11-online-shop/d309-shop-channels-through-connectors.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Amazon order | Imported, stock reserved, shipment confirmed |

## Applies to

Every F11 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Channels enabled.

## Open questions

None.
