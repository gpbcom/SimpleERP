# 📏 BR-F11-05 Reductions show the reference price

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A reduced price shows the reference price of the country pack; in France:
[CR-FR-035 Reference price of a price reduction](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-035-reference-price-for-reductions.md).

## Why

See [D308](../../../06-decisions/f11-online-shop/d308-online-shop-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| -20 % | Lowest price of the last 30 days shown struck through |

## Applies to

Every F11 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
