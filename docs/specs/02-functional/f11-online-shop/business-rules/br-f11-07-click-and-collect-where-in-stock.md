# 📏 BR-F11-07 Click & collect only where in stock

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A pickup point is offered only where the goods are in stock, and never for services.

## Why

See [D308](../../../06-decisions/f11-online-shop/d308-online-shop-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Lyon shop has 0 | Lyon not offered |

## Applies to

Every F11 use case concerned.

- [UC-F11-28 Fulfil online orders from a store](../use-cases/10-fulfil-from-stores-and-report/uc-f11-28-fulfil-online-orders-from-a-store.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Pickup points.

## Open questions

None.
