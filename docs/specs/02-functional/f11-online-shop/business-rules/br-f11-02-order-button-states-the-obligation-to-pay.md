# 📏 BR-F11-02 The order button states the obligation to pay

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The last checkout button says that the order carries an obligation to pay, in the country pack's wording; the
order is confirmed on a durable medium.

## Why

CConso L221-13 and L221-14, not read yet.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| France | "Commande avec obligation de paiement" |

## Applies to

Every F11 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The wording per country pack.

## Open questions

None.
