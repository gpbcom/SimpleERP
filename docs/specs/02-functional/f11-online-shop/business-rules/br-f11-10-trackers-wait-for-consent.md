# 📏 BR-F11-10 Trackers wait for consent

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Non-essential cookies and trackers are blocked until the visitor consents; in France:
[CR-FR-070 Consent before cookies and trackers](../../../10-country-packs/france/data-protection/cr-fr-070-cookie-consent.md).

## Why

See [D308](../../../06-decisions/f11-online-shop/d308-online-shop-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| No choice yet | Analytics and social embeds blocked |

## Applies to

Every F11 use case concerned.

- [UC-F11-29 Read the funnel, customer value and return reasons](../use-cases/10-fulfil-from-stores-and-report/uc-f11-29-read-the-funnel-customer-value-and-return-reasons.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Categories and blocked domains.

## Open questions

None.
