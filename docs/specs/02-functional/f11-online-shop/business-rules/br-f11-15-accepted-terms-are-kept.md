# 📏 BR-F11-15 Accepted terms are kept

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The version of the terms, notices and policies a customer accepted is kept with the order; a change publishes a new
version.

## Why

See [D308](../../../06-decisions/f11-online-shop/d308-online-shop-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Terms changed | Old orders keep the version accepted |

## Applies to

Every F11 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
