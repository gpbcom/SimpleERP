# 📏 BR-F11-22 B2B requests become F6 quotes; punch-out carts go back

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A quote request from the cart becomes an F6 quote with the cart's lines; a punch-out cart is returned to the buyer's
system and its order comes back as a purchase order.

## Why

Business buyers follow their own purchasing processes.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Ask a quote | F6 quote request |
| Punch-out | Cart returned |

## Applies to

- [UC-F11-27 Request a quote or punch out from a buyer's system](../use-cases/09-pre-orders-and-b2b-buying/uc-f11-27-request-a-quote-or-punch-out-from-a-buyers-system.md)

None.
