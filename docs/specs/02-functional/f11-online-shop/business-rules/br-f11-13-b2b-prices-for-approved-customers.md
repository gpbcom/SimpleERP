# 📏 BR-F11-13 B2B prices for approved customers

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A B2B website may hide prices until a customer is approved; an access request creates a contact to approve, with its
pricelist.

## Why

See [D308](../../../06-decisions/f11-online-shop/d308-online-shop-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Visitor not approved | "Contact us" instead of the price |

## Applies to

Every F11 use case concerned.

- [UC-F11-27 Request a quote or punch out from a buyer's system](../use-cases/09-pre-orders-and-b2b-buying/uc-f11-27-request-a-quote-or-punch-out-from-a-buyers-system.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Per website.

## Open questions

None.
