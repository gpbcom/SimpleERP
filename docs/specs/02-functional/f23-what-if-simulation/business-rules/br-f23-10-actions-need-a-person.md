# 📏 BR-F23-10 Actions need a person

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Turning a scenario into actions (prices, orders, hiring, budgets) creates proposals in the capabilities concerned; each
is applied only by a person with the right.

## Why

See [D403](../../../06-decisions/f23-what-if-simulation/d403-forecast-ranges-and-assistant.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Apply price rise | Draft price list for approval |

## Applies to

Every F23 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
