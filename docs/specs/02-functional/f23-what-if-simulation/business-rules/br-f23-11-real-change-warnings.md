# 📏 BR-F23-11 Warned when a real change hits

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A real change (a supplier price, a lost customer, a new law value) that moves the baseline beyond the set tolerance is
reported with its impact.

## Why

See [D403](../../../06-decisions/f23-what-if-simulation/d403-forecast-ranges-and-assistant.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| VAT rate change announced | Owner warned with the cash impact |

## Applies to

Every F23 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Tolerance.

## Open questions

None.
