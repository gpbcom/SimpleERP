# 📏 BR-F23-21 Valuations show their method and assumptions

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

Every valuation shows, for each value, its method and every assumption behind it with the source of each, and gives a
range rather than one figure; a valuation without a method or with an unexplained assumption cannot be issued.

## Why

A single unexplained figure invites false precision and cannot be defended to a bank, a partner or an heir. The owner
asked for valuation scenarios
([D542](../../../06-decisions/f23-what-if-simulation/d542-benchmark-gaps-f23-what-if-simulation.md)).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Multiple of revenue 1.2 to 1.8, source stated | range shown with the source |
| Discounted cash flows without a discount rate | not computed, the rate is named |
| Net assets of a non-profit | shown with the book value and its adjustments |
| A valuation issued without a purpose | refused |
| Two valuations of the same purpose | the newer one issued, the older one Superseded |
