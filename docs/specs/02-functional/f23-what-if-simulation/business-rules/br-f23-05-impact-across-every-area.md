# 📏 BR-F23-05 The impact across every area

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A scenario's result shows each area it touches against the baseline, with the chain of causes.

## Why

See [D402](../../../06-decisions/f23-what-if-simulation/d402-impact-through-the-real-rules.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Supplier +8 % | Purchases, margin, cash, prices to review |

## Applies to

Every F23 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Areas shown.

## Open questions

None.
