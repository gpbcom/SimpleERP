# 📏 BR-F23-06 Rights apply to scenarios

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A user sees in a scenario only the figures their role may see; a scenario shared with others is filtered for each
reader.

## Why

See [D402](../../../06-decisions/f23-what-if-simulation/d402-impact-through-the-real-rules.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Manager runs a payroll scenario | Totals only, no one's pay |

## Applies to

Every F23 use case concerned.

- [UC-F23-26 Simulate a price change on the pipeline](../use-cases/03-see-the-impact/uc-f23-26-simulate-a-price-change-on-the-pipeline.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
