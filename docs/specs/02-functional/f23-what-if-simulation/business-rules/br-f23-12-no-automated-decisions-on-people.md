# 📏 BR-F23-12 No automated decisions about people

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A scenario about staff shows totals and options; it never decides on a named person, and any HR action stays a person's
act.

## Why

See [D403](../../../06-decisions/f23-what-if-simulation/d403-forecast-ranges-and-assistant.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Cost cut scenario | Options, no names chosen |

Every F23 use case concerned.

None.

No.
