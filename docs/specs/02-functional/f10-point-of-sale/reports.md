# 📈 Reports

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Dashboards

Sales by hour, product, category, staff, point of sale and channel; payments by method; cash differences; refunds and
discounts; tables and covers; preparation times; bookings; loyalty and gift card liabilities; delivery-platform sales.

## KPIs

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Sales | Total including VAT per period |
| Average basket | Sales over orders |
| Covers and average per cover | Guests served, sales per guest |
| Cash difference | Counted minus expected per session |
| Refund rate | Refunds over sales |
| Preparation time | Order to ready, average and late share |
| Table turnover | Seatings per table per service |

## Exports

Closures and the legal archive in an open format with its French notice (BR-F10-01); sales and payments as CSV; entries
go to accounting (F8).

None.
