# ⚖️ Regulatory

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Rules that apply

Every legal value comes from the company's country pack (D293). In France: the till rules (certificate before selling,
D296), cash limit, receipts, restaurant bills, scales, prices and reductions, vouchers, e-reporting of consumer sales;
see the list below.
