# 📏 BR-F10-20 Recognised customers agreed, transferred items are reserved

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A customer's profile shows notes and wishes only if they agreed to be recognised; an item asked for transfer is reserved
at the sending store until received or cancelled.

## Why

Clienteling must respect consent; transfers must not be sold twice.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| No consent | Purchase history only |
| Transfer asked | Reserved |

## Applies to

- [UC-F10-25 Serve a customer with their profile](../use-cases/10-clienteling-transfers-and-tax-free/uc-f10-25-serve-a-customer-with-their-profile.md)
- [UC-F10-26 Look up and transfer stock between stores](../use-cases/10-clienteling-transfers-and-tax-free/uc-f10-26-look-up-and-transfer-stock-between-stores.md)

None.
