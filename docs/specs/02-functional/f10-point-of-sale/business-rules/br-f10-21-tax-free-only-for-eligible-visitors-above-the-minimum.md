# 📏 BR-F10-21 Tax-free only for eligible visitors above the minimum

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

A tax-free form is issued only for a visitor living outside the EU, on eligible items, above the legal minimum, and the
VAT is refunded only after the export is validated.

## Why

(⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) to read: CGI article 262-0 bis until 2026-12-31, and the CIBS article that takes it over
from 2027-01-01; no France-pack rule reads it.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| EU resident | Refused |
| Validated export | Refund recorded |
