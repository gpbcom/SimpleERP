# 📏 BR-F10-14 Training mode is marked

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Training sessions print "MODE ÉCOLE" on every document and are kept apart from real sales; in France:
[CR-FR-027 Till security and training mode](../../../10-country-packs/france/point-of-sale/cr-fr-027-till-security.md).

## Why

See [D296](../../../06-decisions/f10-point-of-sale/d296-till-certified-before-france.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Training sale | Not in sales totals, marked |

## Applies to

Every F10 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.
