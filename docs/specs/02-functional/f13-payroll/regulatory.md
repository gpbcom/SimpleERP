# ⚖️ Regulatory

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Rules that apply

Every legal value comes from the company's country pack (D293), dated by period. In France: the payslip, the monthly
social declaration, withholding tax, the minimum wage, end-of-contract documents, the equal-pay index (proposed),
working time, overtime, paid leave, records retention, SEPA, benefits; see the list below.
