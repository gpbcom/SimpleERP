# 📏 BR-F13-08 Payslip in the legal format

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Payslips follow the clarified format with the net social amount and are delivered digitally unless the employee refused;
in France: [CR-FR-182 The payslip](../../../10-country-packs/france/payroll/cr-fr-182-payslip.md).

## Why

See [D365](../../../06-decisions/f13-payroll/d365-payroll-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Employee refused digital | Paper payslip |

## Applies to

Every F13 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
