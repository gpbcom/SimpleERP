# 📏 BR-F13-06 Withholding at the sent rate

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Tax is withheld at the rate the tax office sent, or the neutral rate without one; in France:
[CR-FR-184 Withholding tax on wages](../../../10-country-packs/france/payroll/cr-fr-184-withholding-tax.md).

## Why

See [D365](../../../06-decisions/f13-payroll/d365-payroll-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| New hire, no rate | Neutral rate |

## Applies to

Every F13 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
