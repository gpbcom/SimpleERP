# 📏 BR-F13-18 Special regimes declared to their own bodies

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

Pay of intermittent performers and of farm workers is declared and paid to the bodies of their regime (the performers'
funds, or the MSA instead of the general collector) on their own calendars, never mixed with the general declaration.

## Why

Each regime has its own collector, rates and deadlines (D470); in France: CR-FR-178 for occasional organisers through
the simplified service, CR-FR-167 for seasonal farm workers, the rest (Proposed) to read.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Performer hired for 2 shows by an occasional organiser | passes: simplified declaration within 15 days (CR-FR-178) |
| Farm worker of the agriculture company declared to the MSA | passes |
| Farm worker declared to the general collector | fails: refused before filing |
