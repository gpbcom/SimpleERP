# 📏 BR-F13-15 Payroll assistant, own data only

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An employee asking the AI assistant about pay sees only their own payslips and explanations; the payroll officer sees
the run.

## Why

See [D365](../../../06-decisions/f13-payroll/d365-payroll-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| "Why is my net lower?" | Explained from their own payslip |

## Applies to

Every F13 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
