# 📏 BR-F13-11 End documents ready on the last day

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The final pay, work certificate, unemployment certificate and final receipt are ready on the contract's last day; in
France:
[CR-FR-186 End-of-contract documents](../../../10-country-packs/france/payroll/cr-fr-186-end-of-contract-documents.md).

## Why

See [D365](../../../06-decisions/f13-payroll/d365-payroll-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Leaves 30 Nov | Documents on 30 Nov |

## Applies to

Every F13 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
