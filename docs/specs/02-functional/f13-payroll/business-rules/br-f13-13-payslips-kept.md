# 📏 BR-F13-13 Payslips kept

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Payslip copies are kept for the legal period; in France:
[CR-FR-182 The payslip](../../../10-country-packs/france/payroll/cr-fr-182-payslip.md),
[CR-FR-047 HR records retention](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-047-hr-records-retention.md).

The employer's copy is kept 5 years; electronic payslips stay available to the employee 50 years from issue
([D445](../../../06-decisions/f13-payroll/d445-payslips-fifty-years-from-issue.md)).

## Why

See [D365](../../../06-decisions/f13-payroll/d365-payroll-coverage.md).

Legal values from the France country pack:
[CR-FR-182 The payslip](../../../10-country-packs/france/payroll/cr-fr-182-payslip.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Employee left 2020 | Payslips still available, 50 years from each one's issue |

## Applies to

Every F13 use case concerned.

- [UC-F13-08 Issue clarified payslips digitally](../use-cases/02-monthly-pay-run/uc-f13-08-issue-clarified-payslips-digitally.md)

## Exceptions

None.
