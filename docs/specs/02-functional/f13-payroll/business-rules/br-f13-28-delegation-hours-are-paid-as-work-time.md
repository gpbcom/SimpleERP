# 📏 BR-F13-28 Delegation hours are paid as work time

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

Delegation hours used within the mandate's credit are paid as normal work time: they are never deducted from pay, never
treated as absence, and appear on the payslip as hours worked.

## Why

A representative must not lose pay or advancement for carrying out their mandate. The owner asked for staff
representatives' hours ([D532](../../../06-decisions/f13-payroll/d532-benchmark-gaps-f13-payroll.md)). The
France-pack rule on the credit and its limits is written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 6 hours confirmed within the credit | paid as work time, no deduction |
| Hours above the credit with a reason | held, then paid if HR confirms |
| Hours above the credit without a reason | held, not paid as work time |
| A day of delegation hours | counted as a worked day for a days-based contract |
| A mandate ended | no new hours |

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The credit of each mandate, by HR.
