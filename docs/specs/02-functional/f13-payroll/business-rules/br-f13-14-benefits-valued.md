# 📏 BR-F13-14 Benefits valued

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Benefits in kind and meal vouchers are valued and counted in the pay per the country pack; in France:
[CR-FR-114 Staff meals as a benefit in kind](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-114-staff-meals-benefit.md),
[CR-FR-112 Meal vouchers (titres-restaurant)](../../../10-country-packs/france/payments/cr-fr-112-meal-vouchers.md).

## Why

See [D365](../../../06-decisions/f13-payroll/d365-payroll-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Company car | Benefit added |

## Applies to

Every F13 use case concerned.

- [UC-F13-30 Apply the hotels-and-restaurants agreement](../use-cases/09-collective-agreements-by-family/uc-f13-30-apply-the-hotels-and-restaurants-agreement.md)
- [UC-F13-17 Handle benefits](../use-cases/04-events/uc-f13-17-handle-benefits.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Benefits per company.

## Open questions

None.
