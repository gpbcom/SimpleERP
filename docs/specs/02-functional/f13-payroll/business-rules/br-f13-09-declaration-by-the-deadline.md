# 📏 BR-F13-09 Declaration by the deadline

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The monthly social declaration is filed by its deadline, and event declarations within their delay; returns are handled;
in France:
[CR-FR-183 Monthly social declaration (DSN)](../../../10-country-packs/france/payroll/cr-fr-183-dsn-monthly-declaration.md).

## Why

See [D365](../../../06-decisions/f13-payroll/d365-payroll-coverage.md).
