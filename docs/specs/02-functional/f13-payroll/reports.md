# 📈 Reports

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Dashboards

Payroll cost by establishment and department, headcount, overtime, absences, contributions, anomalies, declarations'
state, equal-pay indicators.

## KPIs

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Payroll cost | Gross plus employer contributions |
| Average cost per employee | Payroll cost over headcount |
| Overtime share | Overtime pay over gross |
| First-time acceptance | Declarations accepted without correction |
| Corrections | Corrections over payslips |

## Exports

Payroll ledger, payslips archive, declarations, entries to F8, yearly summaries.
