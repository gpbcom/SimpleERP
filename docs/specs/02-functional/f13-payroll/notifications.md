# 🔔 Notifications

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Notifications

| Event | To | When |
|---|---|---|
| Inputs not closed | HR, payroll | the day before closing |
| Anomalies in the run | payroll | at once |
| Run waiting for approval | owner | at once |
| Payslip available | employee | on publication |
| Declaration due, rejected | payroll | before the deadline, at once |
| Event declaration due (sick leave, end of contract) | payroll | at once |
| New rates from the country pack | payroll, administrator | at once |
| A country rule flagged by a law change (D294) | administrator, payroll | at once |

## Channels

In-app, email; payslip notices by email and phone push.
