# 📏 BR-F18-10 Domains through registrar APIs

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A domain is bought, transferred or connected through an accredited registrar's API, in the company's name, with an
eligible administrative contact; renewals are automatic unless switched off.

## Why

See [D385](../../../06-decisions/f18-hosted-websites-and-domains/d385-hosted-websites-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| .fr for a company outside the EU | Refused, reason shown |

## Applies to

Every F18 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Registrar.

## Open questions

None.
