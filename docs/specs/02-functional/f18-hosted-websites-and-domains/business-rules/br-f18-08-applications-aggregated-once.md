# 📏 BR-F18-08 Applications aggregated once

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Applications from the site, LinkedIn and Indeed land in one F9 pipeline, deduplicated per candidate and opening, with
their channel.

## Why

See [D385](../../../06-decisions/f18-hosted-websites-and-domains/d385-hosted-websites-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Same person via site and Indeed | One application, two channels |

## Applies to

Every F18 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
