# 📏 BR-F18-04 Automatic checks gate publication

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A publication runs the checks (links, forms, mobile and desktop, WCAG 2.2 AA, speed, legal notices, cookie banner,
careers, contact, DNS, certificate); a failed blocking check stops it, a warning is shown.

## Why

See [D386](../../../06-decisions/f18-hosted-websites-and-domains/d386-website-publication-gate.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Broken contact form | Blocked, fix shown |

## Applies to

Every F18 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Speed budget.

## Open questions

None.
