# 📏 BR-F18-07 Job offers checked before posting

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A job offer is checked against the job-offer rule (language, date, no discriminating or misleading wording) before it is
posted on the site, LinkedIn or Indeed.

## Why

See [D385](../../../06-decisions/f18-hosted-websites-and-domains/d385-hosted-websites-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Offer says 'under 30' | Blocked, reason shown |

## Applies to

Every F18 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
