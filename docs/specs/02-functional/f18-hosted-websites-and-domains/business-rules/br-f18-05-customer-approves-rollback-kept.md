# 📏 BR-F18-05 The customer approves; rollback kept

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Only the owner or a granted role publishes, after the checks; the previous version stays one click away.

## Why

See [D386](../../../06-decisions/f18-hosted-websites-and-domains/d386-website-publication-gate.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Bad text published | Rolled back in one click |

## Applies to

Every F18 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Versions kept.

## Open questions

None.
