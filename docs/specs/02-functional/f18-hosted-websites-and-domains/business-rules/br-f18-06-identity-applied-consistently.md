# 📏 BR-F18-06 Visual identity applied consistently

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Logo, colours, fonts and tone come from the company's identity; contrast below WCAG AA is refused.

## Why

See [D385](../../../06-decisions/f18-hosted-websites-and-domains/d385-hosted-websites-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Light grey text | Darkened, user told |

## Applies to

Every F18 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The identity.

None.
