# 📏 BR-F18-02 Built from the ERP's data

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Products, services, contact details, sites, openings and the legal identity on the site come from the ERP; a change in
the ERP updates the preview, never the live site without a publication.

## Why

See [D385](../../../06-decisions/f18-hosted-websites-and-domains/d385-hosted-websites-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| New phone number in F1 | Preview updated, live site on next publication |

## Applies to

Every F18 use case concerned.

- [UC-F18-25 Use a sector page template](../use-cases/06-content-and-search/uc-f18-25-use-a-sector-page-template.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Pages chosen.

## Open questions

None.
