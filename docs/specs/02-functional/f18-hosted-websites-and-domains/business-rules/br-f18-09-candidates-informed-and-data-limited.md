# 📏 BR-F18-09 Candidates informed, data limited

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each application form shows the candidates' information notice; an unsuccessful candidate's data is erased after the
country pack's period unless they asked otherwise; any AI sorting is declared and stays under human review.

## Why

See [D385](../../../06-decisions/f18-hosted-websites-and-domains/d385-hosted-websites-coverage.md).
