# 📏 BR-F18-24 An e-shop publishes its accessibility information

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A hosted site with an online shop is published only with its accessibility information published: the accessibility
requirements that apply and how the service meets them, in the general terms or an equivalent page linked from every
page, available in written and oral form and kept as long as the shop is offered. SimpleERP adds the level reached,
the known gaps and a contact, which the law does not require, and asks for a review every year. Other sites may
publish the same page and are offered it.

## Why

Online shops must be accessible and must say how, and information that hides gaps is worse than none. The owner asked
for accessibility
[D537](../../../06-decisions/f18-hosted-websites-and-domains/d537-benchmark-gaps-f18-hosted-websites-and-domains.md).
The France-pack rule is [CR-FR-222 Accessibility statement (EAA)](../../../10-country-packs/france/digital/cr-fr-222-accessibility-statement.md):
the law (Code de la consommation D412-57 2° and its annex) asks for information on how the service meets the
requirements; it names no "accessibility statement" and does not ask for the gaps or a way to ask for help.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| A shop site with accessibility information published 6 months ago | can be published |
| A shop site with no accessibility information | held, the missing information is named |
| A shop site whose information is 14 months old | held until reviewed (the product's yearly review) |
| A site of a very small service company, no shop | information optional, offered |
| A gap listed with its reason | accepted in the information, not hidden |

## Exceptions

A very small service company (fewer than 10 persons and 2 MEUR or less of turnover or balance sheet), which
[CR-FR-222](../../../10-country-packs/france/digital/cr-fr-222-accessibility-statement.md) exempts.

## Configurable

The review period (12 months by default), by the owner.

None.
