# 📏 BR-F18-24 An e-shop publishes its accessibility statement

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

A hosted site with an online shop is published only with a published accessibility statement that is less than a year
old and says the level reached, the known gaps and a contact; other sites may publish one and are offered it.

## Why

Online shops must be accessible and must say how, and a statement that hides gaps is worse than none. The owner asked
for accessibility
[D537](../../../06-decisions/f18-hosted-websites-and-domains/d537-benchmark-gaps-f18-hosted-websites-and-domains.md).
(Proposed) The France-pack rule on the statement is written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| A shop site with a published statement of 6 months | can be published |
| A shop site with no statement | held, the statement is named |
| A shop site whose statement is 14 months old | held until reviewed |
| A site of a very small service company, no shop | statement optional, offered |
| A gap listed with its reason | accepted in the statement, not hidden |

- [E-F18-22 Accessibility statement](../data/e-f18-22-accessibility-statement.md)

- [E-F18-10 Publication check report](../data/e-f18-10-publication-check-report.md)

  [CR-FR-222 Accessibility statement (EAA)](../../../10-country-packs/france/digital/cr-fr-222-accessibility-statement.md)

## Exceptions

A very small service company without a shop, where the country pack says it is exempt.

## Configurable

The review period (12 months by default), by the owner.

## Open questions

None.
