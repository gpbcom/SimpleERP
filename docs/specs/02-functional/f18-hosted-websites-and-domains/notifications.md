# 🔔 Notifications

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Notifications

| Event | To | When |
|---|---|---|
| Checks failed | the publisher of the change | at once |
| Site published or rolled back | owner, marketing | at once |
| New application | HR | at once |
| Domain or certificate expiring, mail records broken | administrator, maintainer | 30 days before, at once |
| Site down | administrator, maintainer | at once |

## Channels

In-app, email; the maintainer's console.

## Open questions

None.
