# 📈 Reports

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Dashboards

Headcount and turnover, recruitment (sources, velocity, team performance), time off and absences, attendance and
overtime, appraisals, planning versus actual, project profitability, fleet costs.

## KPIs

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Headcount | By department, contract, site |
| Turnover and retention | Departures over average headcount |
| Time to hire | Days from opening to signed offer |
| Absence rate | Absent days over workable days |
| Overtime | Approved hours per team |
| Utilisation | Billable over worked hours |
| Project margin | Revenue minus costs per project |

## Exports

Spreadsheets; the personnel register and the BDESE as documents.
