# 📏 BR-F9-44 Successor lists are seen by HR and the line

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A succession plan, its successors and their readiness levels can be read only by HR, the owner and the line managers of
the position and of the people listed; an employee never sees whether they or a colleague is listed, and only sees their
own career wishes and path.

## Why

Being named a successor is a judgement. Shown to the wrong people it creates promises, rivalry and complaints. The
owner's answer: "Careers and succession"
([D528](../../../06-decisions/f09-hr-and-projects/d528-benchmark-gaps-f09-hr-and-projects.md)).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| HR opens the plan of the works director | shown with all successors |
| The line manager of a successor opens it | shown, for their own team members only |
| The successor opens their career page | their wishes and path, no list |
| A colleague asks the AI assistant who the successors are | refused, the answer is not theirs to read |
| An employee asks for the data held about them | the plan lines about them are given as the law requires |

## Applies to

- [UC-F9-45 Plan careers and successors](../use-cases/15-work-location-headcount-and-learning/uc-f09-45-plan-careers-and-successors.md)
- [E-F9-50 Succession plan](../data/e-f09-50-succession-plan.md)
- [UC-F9-11 Run appraisals and track skills](../use-cases/04-grow/uc-f09-11-run-appraisals-and-track-skills.md)

## Exceptions

The right of access of the person concerned is answered by HR with the data held about them.

## Configurable

No: who sees a succession list is fixed; HR may add a reader for one plan, audited.

## Open questions

None.
