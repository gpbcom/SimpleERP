# 📏 BR-F9-08 Overtime needs approval

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Extra hours beyond the schedule stay "to approve" until a manager approves them, fully or in part; approved overtime
goes to payroll (F13).

## Why

See [D289](../../../06-decisions/f09-hr-and-projects/d289-hr-coverage.md).

Legal values from the France country pack:
[CR-FR-043 Overtime](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-043-overtime.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 2 h over | To approve |
| Approved 1.5 h | 1.5 h to payroll |

## Applies to

Every F9 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
