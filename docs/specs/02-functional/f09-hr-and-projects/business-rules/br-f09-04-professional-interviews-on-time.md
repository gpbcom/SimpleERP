# 📏 BR-F9-04 Professional interviews on time

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each employee gets a professional interview every 2 years and a review every 6 years (Code du travail L6315-1);
reminders go to HR and the manager 3 months before; missing ones are flagged by the legal assistant (F15).

## Why

See [D287](../../../06-decisions/f09-hr-and-projects/d287-french-hr-duties.md).

Legal values from the France country pack:
[CR-FR-039 Professional interviews](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-039-professional-interviews.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Last interview 22 months ago | Reminder |
| 6-year review without the required training | Flagged |

## Applies to

Every F9 use case concerned.

- [UC-F9-21 Schedule professional interviews and health visits](../use-cases/08-hr-duties/uc-f09-21-schedule-professional-interviews-and-health-visits.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
