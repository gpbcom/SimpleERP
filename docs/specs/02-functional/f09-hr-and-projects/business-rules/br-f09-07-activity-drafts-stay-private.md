# 📏 BR-F9-07 Activity drafts stay private

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Activity-based timesheet drafts exist only for employees who turned them on for themselves; the activity data is visible
only to them and deleted once the timesheet is submitted, or after 7 days (D290).

## Why

See [D285](../../../06-decisions/f09-hr-and-projects/d285-activity-drafts-opt-in.md).

Legal values from the France country pack:
[CR-FR-052 Personal data kept no longer than needed](../../../10-country-packs/france/data-protection/cr-fr-052-storage-limitation.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Léa turned it on | Her drafts from her activity |
| Her manager | Sees only her submitted timesheet |

## Applies to

Every F9 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Each employee for themselves.

## Open questions

None.
