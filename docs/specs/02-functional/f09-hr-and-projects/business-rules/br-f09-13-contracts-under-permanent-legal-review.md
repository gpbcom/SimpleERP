# 📏 BR-F9-13 Contracts under permanent legal review

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

While a contract template is edited, and again before each contract is generated from it, the legal assistant checks it
against the law and the collective agreement in force (F15, BR-F15-08): missing mandatory clauses, unlawful or risky
clauses and outdated references are flagged on the article concerned, with the article of law; a template with a
blocking issue cannot be published; a law change re-checks every published template and tells HR.

## Why

See [D291](../../../06-decisions/f09-hr-and-projects/d291-contract-editor.md).

Legal values from the France country pack:
[CR-FR-040 Trial period limits](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-040-trial-period-limits.md),
[CR-FR-041 Fixed-term contracts](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-041-fixed-term-contracts.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| A CDD template without its reason for recourse | Flagged: missing L1242-12, cannot be published |
| A 6-month trial period for an electrician (worker) | Flagged: over the 2-month maximum, 4 with renewal (L1221-19) |
| A law change on notice periods | Templates re-checked, HR told |

## Applies to

Every F9 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.
