# 📏 BR-F9-12 AI calls say they are AI

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An AI voice pre-screening call says it is an AI assistant at once, asks the same questions of every candidate, lets the
candidate decline, ask for a callback or a person; its outputs follow BR-F4-20 and BR-F4-21.

## Why

See [D289](../../../06-decisions/f09-hr-and-projects/d289-hr-coverage.md).

Legal values from the France country pack:
[CR-FR-056 People know they deal with an AI](../../../10-country-packs/france/ai-and-recording/cr-fr-056-people-know-they-deal-with-an-ai.md),
[CR-FR-058 AI in recruitment and work is high-risk](../../../10-country-packs/france/ai-and-recording/cr-fr-058-high-risk-ai-in-employment.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Call starts | "I am Acme Bâtiment's AI assistant..." |
| "I'd rather talk to a person" | Callback planned with a recruiter |

## Applies to

Every F9 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
