# 📏 BR-F9-20 Survey results need 5 answers per group

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

Engagement survey results are shown for a group only with 5 answers or more (Proposed); open answers are summarised
without quoting a person.

## Why

Small groups would reveal who answered.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 4 answers | Hidden |
| 12 answers | Shown |

## Applies to

- [UC-F9-28 Run engagement surveys and exit interviews](../use-cases/10-listen-to-employees/uc-f09-28-run-engagement-surveys-and-exit-interviews.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.
