# 📏 BR-F9-11 Candidate data has a term

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A candidate's data is kept 2 years after the last contact (CNIL recommendation), unless the candidate agrees to longer;
deleted everywhere on request.

## Why

See [D289](../../../06-decisions/f09-hr-and-projects/d289-hr-coverage.md).

Legal values from the France country pack:
[CR-FR-047 HR records retention](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-047-hr-records-retention.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Refused candidate, 2 years later | Deleted |
| Request to delete | Deleted, logged |

## Applies to

Every F9 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Yes: the term, with the candidate's consent.

## Open questions

None.
