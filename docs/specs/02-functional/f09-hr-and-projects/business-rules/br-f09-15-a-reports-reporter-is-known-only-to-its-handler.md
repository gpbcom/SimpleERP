# 📏 BR-F9-15 A report's reporter is known only to its handler

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

A whistleblowing report's identity and files are seen only by the named handlers; it is acknowledged within 7 days and
answered within 3 months.

## Why

Reporters are protected by law (Proposed) to read: Sapin II as amended in 2022.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Owner opens the list | Counts only |
| Day 6 without acknowledgement | Handler reminded |
