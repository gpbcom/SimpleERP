# 📏 BR-F9-14 Pay gaps are computed per category of equal work

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

The equality index follows the legal method, and pay gaps are computed per category of equal work; categories too small
to be anonymous are left out and said so.

## Why

The index and pay transparency compare equal work only (CR-FR-187).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Technicians: 6 % gap | Shown |
| 2 women, 9 men | Left out under 3 (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574: the index method of Code du travail D1142-2 and its annex) |
