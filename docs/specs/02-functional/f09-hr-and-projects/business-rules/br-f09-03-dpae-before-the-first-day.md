# 📏 BR-F9-03 DPAE before the first day

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A hire cannot start before the DPAE has been sent to URSSAF and acknowledged; the employee enters the personnel register
of their establishment on their first day; the register's entries are never deleted.

## Why

See [D287](../../../06-decisions/f09-hr-and-projects/d287-french-hr-duties.md).

Legal values from the France country pack:
[CR-FR-037 Hiring declaration before the first day](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-037-dpae-before-the-first-day.md),
[CR-FR-038 Personnel register](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-038-personnel-register.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Hire starting Monday, DPAE acknowledged Friday | Ready |
| DPAE missing | Start blocked, HR warned |

## Applies to

Every F9 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
