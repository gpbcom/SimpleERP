# 📏 BR-F9-24 No work without vigilance documents

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A subcontractor or freelancer starts on a project only with current vigilance documents (renewed every 6 months above
the legal amount); an invoice beyond what was logged is held.

## Why

The main contractor is jointly liable otherwise (CR-FR-085).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| URSSAF certificate 7 months old | Blocked |
| Invoice 12,000 at 50 % of 18,000 | Held |

## Applies to

- [UC-F9-32 Work with subcontractors and freelancers on projects](../use-cases/11-capacity-rounds-and-partners/uc-f09-32-work-with-subcontractors-and-freelancers-on-projects.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
