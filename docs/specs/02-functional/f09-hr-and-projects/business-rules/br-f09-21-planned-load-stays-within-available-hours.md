# 📏 BR-F9-21 Planned load stays within available hours

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A person's planned hours in a week stay within their contract hours minus time off; more is flagged and saved only with
a reason.

## Why

Overloaded plans are missed plans, and working time is capped (CR-FR-042).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 46 h of 35 | Flagged |
| Reason "go-live week" | Saved |

## Applies to

- [UC-F9-29 Plan capacity across projects](../use-cases/11-capacity-rounds-and-partners/uc-f09-29-plan-capacity-across-projects.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.
