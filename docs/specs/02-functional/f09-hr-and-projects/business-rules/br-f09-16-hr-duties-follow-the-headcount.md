# 📏 BR-F9-16 HR duties follow the headcount

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Duties that start at a headcount (displays, BDESE, the equality index, disability employment) are listed when the
threshold is reached as the law counts it, with their dates.

## Why

Thresholds change duties (CR-FR-046).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 50 for 12 months | BDESE, index listed |
| 20 employees | OETH declaration |

## Applies to

- [UC-F9-22 Keep the displays and the BDESE current](../use-cases/08-hr-duties/uc-f09-22-keep-the-displays-and-the-bdese-current.md)
- [UC-F9-23 Declare disability employment](../use-cases/08-hr-duties/uc-f09-23-declare-disability-employment.md)

## Exceptions

None.
