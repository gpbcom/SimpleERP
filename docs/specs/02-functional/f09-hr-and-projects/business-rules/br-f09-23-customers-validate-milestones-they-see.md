# 📏 BR-F9-23 Customers validate milestones they see

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A customer sees only the tasks and documents shared with them, and validates or refuses a milestone with a comment; only
a validated milestone triggers its invoice.

## Why

Milestone billing needs the customer's agreement.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Validated | Invoice issued |
| Refused | Back with the comment |

## Applies to

- [UC-F9-31 Follow a project as a customer](../use-cases/11-capacity-rounds-and-partners/uc-f09-31-follow-a-project-as-a-customer.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
