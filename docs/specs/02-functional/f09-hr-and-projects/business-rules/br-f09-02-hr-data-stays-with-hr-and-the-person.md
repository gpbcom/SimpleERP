# 📏 BR-F9-02 HR data stays with HR and the person

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Private data (address, bank, family, health visits, appraisals, salary) is visible only to HR and the person; managers
see work data of their team (schedule, time off, skills); nothing else.

## Why

See [D288](../../../06-decisions/f09-hr-and-projects/d288-employee-self-service.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| A manager opens an employee | Work data only |
| The employee | Everything about themselves |

## Applies to

Every F9 use case concerned.

- [UC-F9-20 Publish the equality index and pay-transparency information](../use-cases/07-fair-pay-and-speaking-up/uc-f09-20-publish-the-equality-index-and-pay-transparency-information.md)

- [UC-F9-23 Declare disability employment](../use-cases/08-hr-duties/uc-f09-23-declare-disability-employment.md)

- [UC-F9-26 Declare a work accident](../use-cases/09-train-and-protect/uc-f09-26-declare-a-work-accident.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
