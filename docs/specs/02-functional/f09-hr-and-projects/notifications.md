# 🔔 Notifications

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Notifications

| Event | To | When |
|---|---|---|
| A time-off request, an overtime, a timesheet to validate | manager | at once |
| A key data change to validate | HR | at once |
| DPAE acknowledged or refused | HR | at once |
| A career-path interview (at most every 4 years), an 8-year review, a health visit due | HR, manager | 3 months before |
| A contract or trial period ending | HR | 30 and 15 days before |
| A certification expiring | employee, HR | 90 days before |
| A shift published or changed | employee | at once |
| A new applicant, an interview booked | recruiter | at once |
| A LinkedIn reply classified as a question or unclear, or a positive one | HR who owns the sequence | at once |
| The LinkedIn messages to send (prepared mode, D570): one daily digest listing the messages waiting as tasks with Send in HR's task list ([E-F9-18](data/e-f09-18-project-and-task.md)) and those expiring within 24 hours (7 days after their task was created); no notification per message | HR who owns the sequences | once a day |
| An outreach sequence paused: a limit reached or a sign-in needed | HR; the administrator for a sign-in | at once |
| An outreach sequence ended on day 60, with its results | HR | on day 60 |
| A visitor arrived | host | at once |
| A contract template flagged by the legal assistant | HR | at once |

## Channels

In-app, email, phone push, SMS for visitors' hosts.
