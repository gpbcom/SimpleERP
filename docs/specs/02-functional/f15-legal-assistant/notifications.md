# 🔔 Notifications

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Notifications

| Event | To | When |
|---|---|---|
| A legal warning | whoever acts, and HR or the owner for thresholds and contracts | at once |
| Monthly digest of law changes | HR, owner | first working day of the month |
| A law pack refreshed with changes | administrators | in the monthly digest |
| An official source unreachable for a day | administrators | after a day |

## Channels

In-app; email and phone push per each user's settings (T10).
