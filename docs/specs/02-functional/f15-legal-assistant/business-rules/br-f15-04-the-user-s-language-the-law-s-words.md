# 📏 BR-F15-04 The user's language, the law's words

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The answer is written in the user's language; each quoted article is shown in its original language, followed by a
translation marked "Unofficial translation"; only the original is authoritative.

## Why

Employees may not read the law's language; only the original binds. See
[D197](../../../06-decisions/f15-legal-assistant/d197-legal-language.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| A Portuguese-speaking employee in France | The answer in Portuguese; L3141-3 quoted in French, then "Unofficial translation" in Portuguese |
| A French user | French throughout, no translation |

## Applies to

Every answer of the legal assistant.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
