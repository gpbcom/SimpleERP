# 📏 BR-F15-23 Renewals are warned before their deadline

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

Every right with a renewal deadline has reminders sent to its owner well before the deadline, at 180, 90 and 30 days and
on the day, until the renewal is recorded or the right is let go on purpose.

## Why

A right that lapses by oversight is lost, and winning it back is costly or impossible. The owner asked for the company's
own IP portfolio ([D534](../../../06-decisions/f15-legal-assistant/d534-benchmark-gaps-f15-legal-assistant.md)); the
reminders follow the calendar rule [BR-F15-11](br-f15-11-every-dated-duty-has-an-owner-and-reminders.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| A trademark renewing in 180 days | first reminder sent |
| A renewal recorded at day 60 | reminders stop |
| A right let go on purpose | reminders stop, the decision is kept |
| A right with no renewal date | Unconfirmed, a reminder asks for it |
| A renewal date passed with no payment | Lapsed, the date is kept |

## Applies to

- [UC-F15-19](../use-cases/07-equity-contracts-and-disputes/uc-f15-19-keep-the-companys-ip-portfolio.md)
- [E-F15-20 IP right](../data/e-f15-20-ip-right.md)
- [E-F15-05 Compliance duty](../data/e-f15-05-compliance-duty.md)

## Exceptions

None.
