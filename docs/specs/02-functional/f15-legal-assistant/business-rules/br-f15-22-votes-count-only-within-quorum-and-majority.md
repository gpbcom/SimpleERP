# 📏 BR-F15-22 Votes count only within quorum and majority

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

A vote counts only when the quorum is reached at the opening of the meeting, and a resolution is adopted only when the
votes for it reach the majority required for that kind of resolution, counted on the votes within the quorum; votes
outside the quorum count for nothing.

## Why

A decision taken by too few shareholders or by too small a majority is open to challenge. The owner asked for
shareholder meetings ([D534](../../../06-decisions/f15-legal-assistant/d534-benchmark-gaps-f15-legal-assistant.md)).
(Proposed) The France-pack rule is written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Quorum 25 percent, 78 percent present | reached, the votes count |
| Quorum 25 percent, 10 percent present | not reached, nothing counts |
| Ordinary resolution with 51 percent in favour | adopted |
| Change of statutes with 65 percent in favour | rejected, two thirds needed |
| A proxy given to another holder | counted as present for the holder who gave it |

## Applies to

- [UC-F15-18](../use-cases/07-equity-contracts-and-disputes/uc-f15-18-run-a-shareholders-general-meeting.md)
- [E-F15-19 General meeting](../data/e-f15-19-general-meeting.md)
- [E-F15-15 Shareholding](../data/e-f15-15-shareholding.md)
- France-pack rule, (Proposed) until its law is read:
  [CR-FR-221 Shareholders' general meetings](../../../10-country-packs/france/company/cr-fr-221-general-meetings.md)

## Exceptions

A decision taken by written consultation uses the majority of the consultation, not a quorum.

## Configurable

The quorum and the majority of each resolution kind, from the statutes, by the owner.

## Open questions

None.
