# 📏 BR-F15-19 Grants follow the plan approved by shareholders

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

A grant is created only under a plan approved by the shareholders: its quantity fits what is left of the plan's pool,
its date falls within the plan's period, and its beneficiary is of a kind the plan allows; the plan's approval is linked
to the grant.

## Why

Equity changes the capital: only the shareholders may open a pool, and a grant outside it would be void and costly. The
owner asked for full equity
([D534](../../../06-decisions/f15-legal-assistant/d534-benchmark-gaps-f15-legal-assistant.md)). The
France-pack rule on the conditions of each instrument is written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| A grant of 2,000 with 4,000 left in the pool | accepted |
| A grant of 5,000 with 4,000 left | refused |
| A grant after the plan's end date | refused |
| A grant to a person the plan does not name | refused |
| A lapsed grant | its quantity returns to the pool |

None.

## Configurable

The vesting schedule of each grant within the plan, by the owner.
