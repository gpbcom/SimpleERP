# 🤖 AI

**Status:** ✅ specified

## What the AI assistant can do

Every action of this capability can be triggered from the AI assistant, within the user's roles; changes are confirmed,
irreversible acts prepared for the user
([BR-F3-34](../f03-assistant/business-rules/br-f03-34-every-capability-is-reachable-from-the-assistant.md)).

Answer legal questions for the person's situation, cited and dated; warn on legal risks; check documents against the
law; draft a missing clause through F3's confirmation.

## Permissions

The asker's (D12); legal conversations private (BR-F15-03).

## Models

The "Legal assistant" AI feature setting of F1 (D82), local by default; retrieval from the law pack's corpus (T8, T11);
refreshed unattended (D205); quality followed through answer feedback (D186, D206).

## Open questions

None.
