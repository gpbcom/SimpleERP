# ⚖️ Regulatory

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Rules that apply

- **Legal advice:** in France, giving legal advice for others on a regular basis is reserved to regulated professions
  (loi n° 71-1130 du 31 décembre 1971, article 54); the AI assistant gives legal information and says so (BR-F15-02,
  [Legal advice limits](../../05-non-functional/legal-advice-limits.md)).
- **EU AI Act:** users are told they are talking to an AI; the legal assistant is not used to take employment decisions
  about people (warnings inform, never decide, BR-F15-07).
- **GDPR:** HR data used only within the asker's rights and listed; legal conversations private (BR-F15-03).
- **Reuse of public data:** Légifrance and EUR-Lex texts are reusable under their open licences (Licence Ouverte,
  EUR-Lex reuse notice); copyrighted standards follow
  [Sector technical standards](../../05-non-functional/sector-technical-standards.md).
