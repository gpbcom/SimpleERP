# 📏 BR-F12-14 Scrap writes the waste register

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Scrap declared as waste writes a line of the waste register; in France:
[CR-FR-079 Waste register](../../../10-country-packs/france/environment/cr-fr-079-waste-register.md).

## Why

Saves typing; the register's legal content is still to read.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 30 kg of swarf scrapped | Waste register line, code and carrier asked |

## Applies to

Every F12 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Waste codes per scrap reason.

## Open questions

None.
