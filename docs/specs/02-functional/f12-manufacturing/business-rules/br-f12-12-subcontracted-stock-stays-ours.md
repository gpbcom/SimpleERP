# 📏 BR-F12-12 Subcontracted stock stays ours

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Components sent to a subcontractor stay in the company's stock at the subcontractor's location until consumed.

## Why

See [D312](../../../06-decisions/f12-manufacturing/d312-manufacturing-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 100 blanks sent | Stock at Subcontractor A: 100 |

## Applies to

Every F12 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
