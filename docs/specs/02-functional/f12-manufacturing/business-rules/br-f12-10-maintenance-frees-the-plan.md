# 📏 BR-F12-10 Maintenance frees the plan

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Planned maintenance and breakdowns remove the work centre's capacity, and the orders on it are rescheduled.

## Why

See [D312](../../../06-decisions/f12-manufacturing/d312-manufacturing-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Press down 4 h | Its orders moved, late ones shown |

## Applies to

Every F12 use case concerned.

- [UC-F12-23 Schedule with finite capacity](../use-cases/11-finite-schedule-and-the-tablet/uc-f12-23-schedule-with-finite-capacity.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
