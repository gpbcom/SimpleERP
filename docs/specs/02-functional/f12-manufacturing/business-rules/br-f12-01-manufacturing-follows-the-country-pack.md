# 📏 BR-F12-01 Manufacturing follows the country pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every legal value of manufacturing (product conformity, chemicals in articles, equipment checks, the risk assessment,
waste, classified installations, traceability, stock valuation) comes from the company's country pack; in France:
[CR-FR-074 Conformity of machinery](../../../10-country-packs/france/products/cr-fr-074-machinery-conformity.md),
[CR-FR-075 Periodic checks of work equipment](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-075-work-equipment-periodic-checks.md),
[CR-FR-076 Occupational risk assessment (DUERP)](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-076-occupational-risk-assessment.md),
[CR-FR-077 Substances of very high concern in articles](../../../10-country-packs/france/products/cr-fr-077-reach-svhc-information.md),
[CR-FR-078 SCIP notification of articles](../../../10-country-packs/france/products/cr-fr-078-scip-notification.md),
[CR-FR-079 Waste register](../../../10-country-packs/france/environment/cr-fr-079-waste-register.md),
[CR-FR-080 Classified installations (ICPE)](../../../10-country-packs/france/environment/cr-fr-080-classified-installations.md),
[CR-FR-081 Production cost of manufactured stock](../../../10-country-packs/france/accounting/cr-fr-081-production-cost-of-inventories.md).

## Why

See [D312](../../../06-decisions/f12-manufacturing/d312-manufacturing-coverage.md).
