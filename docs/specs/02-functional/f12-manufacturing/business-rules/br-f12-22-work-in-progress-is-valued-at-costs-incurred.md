# 📏 BR-F12-22 Work in progress is valued at costs incurred

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

At month end, each open order is valued at the components consumed and the labour and machine time recorded at their
rates; the entry reverses the next month.

## Why

Production cost of stock (CR-FR-081).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 14 open orders | Valued, posted |
| No rate | Listed with a warning |

## Applies to

- [UC-F12-27 Value work in progress at month end](../use-cases/05-subcontracting-and-costs/uc-f12-27-value-work-in-progress-at-month-end.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
