# 📏 BR-F12-09 Equipment checks before use

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Equipment whose legal periodic check is overdue is flagged on every order that uses it, and may be blocked;
in France:
[CR-FR-075 Periodic checks of work equipment](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-075-work-equipment-periodic-checks.md).

## Why

Safety practice; the legal periods are still to read.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Crane check overdue | Flagged; blocked if the company chose so |

## Applies to

Every F12 use case concerned.

- [UC-F12-21 Calibrate measuring equipment](../use-cases/10-people-and-tools/uc-f12-21-calibrate-measuring-equipment.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Flag or block.

## Open questions

None.
