# 📏 BR-F12-15 Risk assessment versions kept

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The risk assessment is one document for the company that lists every work unit with its risks, and each version of it
is kept; in France (Code du travail R4121-1):
[CR-FR-076 Occupational risk assessment (DUERP)](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-076-occupational-risk-assessment.md).

## Why

See [D312](../../../06-decisions/f12-manufacturing/d312-manufacturing-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| New press installed | A new version asked, the old one kept |

## Applies to

Every F12 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Work units.

## Open questions

None.
