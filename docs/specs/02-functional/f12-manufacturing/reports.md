# 📈 Reports

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Dashboards

Output and lead times per product and work centre, on-time rate, load and capacity, scrap and non-conformities, OEE and
stops by reason, maintenance, cost variances, subcontracting.

## KPIs

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| OEE | Availability x performance x quality |
| On-time rate | Orders done by their date over orders done |
| Scrap rate | Scrap over output |
| Cost variance | Actual minus standard cost |
| Load | Planned hours over capacity per work centre |
| Mean time between failures | Running time over breakdowns |

## Exports

Orders, consumption and lots as CSV; the safety and waste registers and the risk assessment as PDF for inspections;
entries go to F8.
