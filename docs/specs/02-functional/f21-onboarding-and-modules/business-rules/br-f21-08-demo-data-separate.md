# 📏 BR-F21-08 Demo data kept apart

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Demo data is marked, never mixed with real records, never sent to outside systems, and cleared in one click.

## Why

See [D395](../../../06-decisions/f21-onboarding-and-modules/d395-onboarding-and-modules-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Clear demo | Every demo record removed |

## Applies to

Every F21 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
