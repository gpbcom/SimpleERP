# 📏 BR-F21-02 One click per module

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each functional module is switched on or off with one click from the catalogue; what it adds (menus, roles, screens,
steps) appears at once.

## Why

See [D395](../../../06-decisions/f21-onboarding-and-modules/d395-onboarding-and-modules-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Switch on Point of sale | Menu, roles and its short guide added |

## Applies to

Every F21 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
