# 📏 BR-F21-16 Employees onboard on the phone

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

An employee's first sign-in on the phone walks through profile, password and two-factor, notifications and one first
task of their role, can be done in (Proposed) 5 minutes, and resumes where it stopped.

## Why

Many employees only use a phone (D34, D478).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Carer opens the invitation on the phone | passes: 4 steps then today's visits |
| Employee stops after step 2 | passes: resumes at step 3 |
| Step forcing a desktop | fails: not allowed in phone onboarding |

## Applies to

- [UC-F21-25 Onboard on the phone as an employee](../use-cases/04-learn-and-improve/uc-f21-25-onboard-on-the-phone-as-an-employee.md)
- [UC-F21-17 Welcome an employee at first sign-in](../use-cases/04-learn-and-improve/uc-f21-17-welcome-an-employee-at-first-sign-in.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The first task per role.

## Open questions

None.
