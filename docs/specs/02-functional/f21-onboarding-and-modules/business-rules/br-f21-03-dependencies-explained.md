# 📏 BR-F21-03 Dependencies explained, never hidden

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A module that needs another says so in plain words and offers to switch both on; switching off a module others need is
refused with the list.

## Why

See [D395](../../../06-decisions/f21-onboarding-and-modules/d395-onboarding-and-modules-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Point of sale needs Customers and sales | Both offered |

## Applies to

Every F21 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
