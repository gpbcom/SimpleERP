# 📏 BR-F21-09 The AI assistant at every step

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each step has the AI assistant, which explains, fills in from documents the user gives, and acts within the user's
rights.

## Why

See [D395](../../../06-decisions/f21-onboarding-and-modules/d395-onboarding-and-modules-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 'Fill from our Kbis' | Fields filled, user confirms |

## Applies to

Every F21 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

None.
