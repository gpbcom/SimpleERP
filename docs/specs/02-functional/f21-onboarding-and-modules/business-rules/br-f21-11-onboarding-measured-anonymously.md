# 📏 BR-F21-11 Onboarding measured without people

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Time to first invoice and the steps where companies stop are measured per company, never per person.

## Why

See [D395](../../../06-decisions/f21-onboarding-and-modules/d395-onboarding-and-modules-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Drop-off at import | Counted, no user named |

## Applies to

Every F21 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
