# 📏 BR-F21-10 Hosted prices shown before switching on

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

In the hosted edition, a module or feature that costs is shown with its price excluding VAT before it is switched on.

## Why

See [D395](../../../06-decisions/f21-onboarding-and-modules/d395-onboarding-and-modules-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| A paid feature | Price shown, owner confirms |

## Applies to

Every F21 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
