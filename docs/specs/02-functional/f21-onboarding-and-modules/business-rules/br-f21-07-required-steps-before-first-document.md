# 📏 BR-F21-07 Required steps before the first legal document

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Steps the law needs (identity, VAT, numbering) are completed before the first invoice or payslip; others can wait.

## Why

See [D395](../../../06-decisions/f21-onboarding-and-modules/d395-onboarding-and-modules-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Invoice without a VAT number | Step opened first |

## Applies to

Every F21 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
