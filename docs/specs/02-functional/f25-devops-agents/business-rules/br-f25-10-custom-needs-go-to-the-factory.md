# 📏 BR-F25-10 Custom needs go to the feature factory

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A request useful to one customer only is offered as a feature-factory feature (F19), with its price, instead of a
product change.

## Why

See [D432](../../../06-decisions/f25-devops-agents/d432-devops-agents-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Field only Acme needs | F19 quote offered |

## Applies to

Every F25 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
