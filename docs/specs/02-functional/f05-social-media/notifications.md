# 🔔 Notifications

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Notifications

| Event | To | When |
|---|---|---|
| A post to approve | approvers | at once |
| Approved, refused, change asked | author | at once |
| Published or failed | author | at once |
| Automatic post published or held | rule's owner | at once |
| New inbox item | marketing | grouped every 15 minutes |
| Monthly summary | marketing, owner | first working day |
| Account to reconnect | administrator | at once |

## Channels

In-app; email and phone push per each user's settings (T10).
