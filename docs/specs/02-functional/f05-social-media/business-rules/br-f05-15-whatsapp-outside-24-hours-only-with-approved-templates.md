# 📏 BR-F5-15 WhatsApp outside 24 hours only with approved templates

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A reply is free within 24 hours of the customer's last message; after that, only a template approved by WhatsApp can be
sent, and only to contacts with WhatsApp consent.

## Why

WhatsApp Business rules; breaking them blocks the company's number.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Reply after 5 hours | Sent |
| Message after 30 hours | Template only |

## Applies to

- [UC-F5-20 Message customers on WhatsApp Business](../use-cases/08-pages-forms-surveys-and-whatsapp/uc-f05-20-message-customers-on-whatsapp-business.md)

None.
