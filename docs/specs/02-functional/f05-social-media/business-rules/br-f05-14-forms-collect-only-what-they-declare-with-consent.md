# 📏 BR-F5-14 Forms collect only what they declare, with consent

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A form collects only the fields it shows, states what they are used for, and asks marketing consent per channel with an
unticked box; it cannot be published without that text.

## Why

Data minimisation and proof of consent (CR-FR-063, CR-FR-050).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Consent box pre-ticked | Refused |
| No consent text | Not published |

## Applies to

- [UC-F5-19 Publish a landing page with a form](../use-cases/08-pages-forms-surveys-and-whatsapp/uc-f05-19-publish-a-landing-page-with-a-form.md)

## Exceptions

None.

No.
