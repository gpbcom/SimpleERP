# 📏 BR-F5-06 Leads traced through tracked links

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Links in posts are tracked; a visit, a lead or an order coming from a post is attributed to it (F6); statistics show it
per post, network and month.

## Why

Posts should be judged by business, not only likes. See
[D249](../../../06-decisions/f05-social-media/d249-social-statistics.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| An order after a click on a LinkedIn post | Attributed to that post |

## Applies to

- [UC-F5-06](../use-cases/03-inbox-and-statistics/uc-f05-06-read-statistics.md)
- [UC-F5-19 Publish a landing page with a form](../use-cases/08-pages-forms-surveys-and-whatsapp/uc-f05-19-publish-a-landing-page-with-a-form.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The attribution window, 30 days by default.
