# 📏 BR-F5-04 AI content is marked

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A post written or illustrated with the AI carries the "written with AI help" mention, and AI-generated images and videos
are labelled as such where the network allows (D164).

## Why

EU AI Act transparency for content published to inform the public.

Legal values from the France country pack:
[CR-FR-056 People know they deal with an AI](../../../10-country-packs/france/ai-and-recording/cr-fr-056-people-know-they-deal-with-an-ai.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| An AI-drafted LinkedIn post | Mention added |
| An AI-generated image | Labelled |

## Applies to

Every F5 use case.

- [UC-F5-21 Keep the brand kit and asset library](../use-cases/09-brand-tests-and-return-on-investment/uc-f05-21-keep-the-brand-kit-and-asset-library.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
