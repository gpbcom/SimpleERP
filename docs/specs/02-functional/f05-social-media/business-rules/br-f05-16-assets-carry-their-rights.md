# 📏 BR-F5-16 Assets carry their rights

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An image or video is offered to composers only with its source and usage rights, and is hidden when its rights expire.

## Why

Publishing an image without rights exposes the company.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Licence ends 2026-12-31 | Hidden from 2027-01-01 |
