# 📏 BR-F5-10 Consent is proven and withdrawn at once

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Marketing consent is recorded per channel with the text shown, the date and the source; a withdrawal applies to every
send that starts after it.

## Why

Consent must be proven on request (CR-FR-063).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Form tick | Recorded with text version and date |
| STOP by SMS | SMS consent withdrawn |

## Applies to

- [UC-F5-15 Record and prove consent](../use-cases/06-audiences-consent-and-journeys/uc-f05-15-record-and-prove-consent.md)
- [UC-F5-19 Publish a landing page with a form](../use-cases/08-pages-forms-surveys-and-whatsapp/uc-f05-19-publish-a-landing-page-with-a-form.md)
