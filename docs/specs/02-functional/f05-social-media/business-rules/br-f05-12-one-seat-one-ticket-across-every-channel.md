# 📏 BR-F5-12 One seat, one ticket, across every channel

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A seat or a unit of capacity is sold once across online, box office and partner channels; channel quotas never exceed
the capacity, and unsold quotas return to the common pool at their deadline.

## Why

The one ticketing model of D457 replaces three that could double-sell.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Seat F12 sold online | Shown sold at the box office |
| Quotas 650 for 600 seats | Refused |

## Applies to

- [UC-F5-17 Set up ticketing for an event](../use-cases/07-events-and-ticketing/uc-f05-17-set-up-ticketing-for-an-event.md)
- [UC-F5-18 Sell at the box office and scan tickets](../use-cases/07-events-and-ticketing/uc-f05-18-sell-at-the-box-office-and-scan-tickets.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.
