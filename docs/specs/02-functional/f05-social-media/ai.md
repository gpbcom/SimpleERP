# 🤖 AI

**Status:** ✅ specified

## What the AI assistant can do

Every action of this capability can be triggered from the AI assistant, within the user's roles; changes are confirmed,
irreversible acts prepared for the user
([BR-F3-34](../f03-assistant/business-rules/br-f03-34-every-capability-is-reachable-from-the-assistant.md)).

Draft posts from a topic, product, project or meeting in the company style; generate or edit images and short videos;
suggest times and hashtags; suggest replies; fill automatic post templates.

## Permissions

The author's rights; automatic posts as their rule.

## Models

"Social media drafts" AI feature setting (F1, D82); image and video models (T7); content marked (BR-F5-04).

## Open questions

None.
