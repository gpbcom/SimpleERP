# 📈 Reports

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Dashboards

Sales: pipeline, forecast, conversion; receivables: aged balance, days sales outstanding; subscriptions: recurring
revenue, churn.

## KPIs

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Forecast | Opportunities weighted by stage probability |
| Conversion | Won over closed opportunities |
| Quote acceptance | Accepted over sent quotes |
| Average margin | Per quote and month |
| Days sales outstanding | Receivables over sales, in days |
| Overdue | Amount and count by age band |
| Recurring revenue | Monthly, from subscriptions |

## Exports

Spreadsheets; the aged balance as a document.
