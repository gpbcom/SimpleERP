# 📏 BR-F6-10 Usage is billed from metered data

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Usage is billed from recorded meter readings, against commitments and prepaid credits, and the invoice lists the
readings.

## Why

See [D304](../../../06-decisions/f06-customers-and-sales/d304-f6-benchmark-gaps.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 12 400 units, 10 000 committed | 2 400 billed as overage |

## Applies to

UC-F6-16

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Rate plans.

## Open questions

None.
