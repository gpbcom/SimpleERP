# 📏 BR-F6-23 The legal conformity guarantee applies first

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A defect claim is examined first under the legal guarantee of conformity, which the commercial warranty cannot reduce;
the commercial warranty applies after it or beyond it, and a claim is refused only after both are examined and the
reason is recorded.

## Why

The seller owes the legal guarantee whatever its own warranty says, and the customer must not be given less than the law
([CR-FR-067](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-067-legal-guarantee-of-conformity.md)). The owner asked for
warranty claims ([D525](../../../06-decisions/f06-customers-and-sales/d525-benchmark-gaps-f06-customers-and-sales.md)).
The duties and periods are those of
[CR-FR-223 Legal conformity guarantee](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-223-legal-conformity-guarantee.md): 2 years from
delivery, repair or replacement free of charge within 30 days, then a price reduction or a refund.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| A defect 4 months after delivery on an item with a 1-year commercial warranty | the legal guarantee is examined first |
| A commercial warranty that excludes misuse | the legal guarantee is still examined |
| Repair impossible | replacement, then price reduction or refund |
| A claim refused | the two guarantees and the reason are recorded |
| A claim by phone | created by the office worker with the same checks |

None; the customer's legal rights cannot be waived by the commercial terms.
