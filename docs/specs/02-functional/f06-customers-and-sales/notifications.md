# 🔔 Notifications

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Notifications

| Event | To | When |
|---|---|---|
| A lead assigned | sales person | at once |
| A quote to approve | manager | at once |
| A quote accepted or refused | sales person | at once |
| A quote expiring | sales person | 3 days before |
| An invoice issued | customer | at once |
| A payment received or failed | accounting | at once |
| A reminder or formal notice sent | accounting | daily digest |
| A subscription to renew | sales person, customer | 30 days before |

## Channels

In-app, email, the portal; customers by email with a portal link.

None.
