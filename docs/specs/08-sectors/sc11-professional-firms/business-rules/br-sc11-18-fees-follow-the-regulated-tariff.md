# 📏 BR-SC11-18 Fees follow the regulated tariff

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The fee of an act the regulated tariff covers is computed from the tariff line of its type and never exceeds it. A fee
asked above the tariff is cut to it and the difference is shown, with the travel and the disbursements counted apart.

## Why

The tariff protects the person who pays; charging more is a breach that the profession's body can sanction. The owner
asked for judicial officers ([D555](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d555-benchmark-gaps-sc11-professional-firms.md)).
The tariff lines come from the France pack, written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Summons, tariff 78.50, asked 90.00 | cut to 78.50 |
| Summons with travel 12.00 | fee 78.50 plus travel 12.00 |
| Work set by a fee agreement | shown apart from the tariff |
| Service attempt failed | no fee for the failed attempt unless the tariff allows |
| Tariff changed on a date | the act uses the tariff of its date |
