# 📏 BR-SC11-07 Deadlines never silent

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each deadline has an owner and reminders; a missed reminder escalates to the partner.

## Why

See [D347](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d347-professional-firms-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Appeal deadline in 3 days | Owner and partner alerted |

## Applies to

Every SC11 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Reminder delays.

## Open questions

None.
