# 📏 BR-SC11-04 Fees agreed in writing

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A lawyer's fees are agreed in writing before work, never as a pure share of the result; in France:
[CR-FR-150 Lawyers' fee agreement](../../../10-country-packs/france/professional-firms/cr-fr-150-lawyer-fee-agreement.md).

## Why

See [D347](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d347-professional-firms-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 100 % success fee | Refused |

## Applies to

Every SC11 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Fee models.

## Open questions

None.
