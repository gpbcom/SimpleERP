# 📏 BR-SC11-19 Partners rotate as the law requires

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

A signing partner cannot sign a new opinion for an engagement once the number of years the law sets for rotation has
been reached, and the partner and the firm are warned 12, 6 and 3 months before. The engagement keeps the history of
each partner's years.

## Why

Rotation protects the auditor's independence; an opinion signed by a partner past the limit can be challenged and the
firm sanctioned. The owner asked for audit firms
([D555](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d555-benchmark-gaps-sc11-professional-firms.md)). The number of years
comes from the France pack, to confirm, and the rule is written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Signing partner from 2026, rotation after the pack's years | rotation date computed |
| Opinion signed after the rotation date | refused |
| Replacing partner named | the history keeps both partners |
| Rotation in 6 months | a warning to the partner and the firm |
| Entity not of public interest | the pack's value for that case |
