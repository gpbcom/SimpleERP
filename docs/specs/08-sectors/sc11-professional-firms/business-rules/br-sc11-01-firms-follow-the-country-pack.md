# 📏 BR-SC11-01 Firms follow the country pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every legal value of a professional firm (identity checks, fee agreements, client funds, engagement letters, insurance,
secrecy) comes from the country pack; in France:
[CR-FR-149 Anti-money-laundering vigilance](../../../10-country-packs/france/professional-firms/cr-fr-149-aml-vigilance.md),
[CR-FR-150 Lawyers' fee agreement](../../../10-country-packs/france/professional-firms/cr-fr-150-lawyer-fee-agreement.md),
[CR-FR-151 Lawyers' client funds through the CARPA](../../../10-country-packs/france/professional-firms/cr-fr-151-carpa-client-funds.md),
[CR-FR-152 Chartered accountants' engagement letter](../../../10-country-packs/france/professional-firms/cr-fr-152-accountant-engagement-letter.md),
[CR-FR-153 Architects' insurance and contract](../../../10-country-packs/france/professional-firms/cr-fr-153-architect-insurance-and-contract.md),
[CR-FR-154 Lawyers' professional secrecy](../../../10-country-packs/france/professional-firms/cr-fr-154-lawyer-secrecy.md).

## Why

See [D347](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d347-professional-firms-sector-coverage.md).

| Case | Result |
