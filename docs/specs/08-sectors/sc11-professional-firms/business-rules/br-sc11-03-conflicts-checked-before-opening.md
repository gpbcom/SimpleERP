# 📏 BR-SC11-03 Conflicts checked before opening

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A new matter is checked against all clients and opposing parties; a possible conflict needs a partner's decision.

## Why

See [D347](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d347-professional-firms-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Opponent is a client | Partner decides, recorded |

## Applies to

Every SC11 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Who decides.

## Open questions

None.
