# 📏 BR-SC11-12 Tax calendar per client

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each accounting client has its filing calendar, from its tax regimes, with an owner per filing.

## Why

See [D347](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d347-professional-firms-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Monthly VAT client | 12 filings planned |

## Applies to

Every SC11 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Regimes.

None.
