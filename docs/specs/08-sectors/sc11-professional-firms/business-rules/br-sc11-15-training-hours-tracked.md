# 📏 BR-SC11-15 Training hours tracked

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each professional's continuing training hours are recorded against the yearly requirement of the profession.

## Why

See [D347](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d347-professional-firms-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 12 h of 20 | Reminder in the autumn |

## Applies to

Every SC11 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Requirement per profession.

## Open questions

None.
