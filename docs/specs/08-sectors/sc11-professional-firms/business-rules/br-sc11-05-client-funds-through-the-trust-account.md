# 📏 BR-SC11-05 Client funds through the trust account

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Money held for clients goes to the trust account without delay, kept by the shared client money: a ledger per client
that never goes negative,
[BR-F8-13 Client money is segregated and never negative per client](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/business-rules/br-f08-13-client-money-is-segregated-and-never-negative-per-client.md);
in France:
[CR-FR-151 Lawyers' client funds through the CARPA](../../../10-country-packs/france/professional-firms/cr-fr-151-carpa-client-funds.md).

## Why

See [D347](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d347-professional-firms-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Settlement received | To the CARPA account |

## Applies to

Every SC11 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
