# 📏 BR-SC11-09 Advances within the estimate

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Requests for advances stay within a reasonable estimate and are deducted on the final bill with a detailed statement.

## Why

See [D347](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d347-professional-firms-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Advance 3 000 | Deducted on the final bill |

## Applies to

Every SC11 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
