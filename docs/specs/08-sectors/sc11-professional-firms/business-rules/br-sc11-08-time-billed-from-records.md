# 📏 BR-SC11-08 Time billed from records

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Fees by the hour come from recorded time at the agreed rates; write-offs are recorded with a reason.

## Why

See [D347](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d347-professional-firms-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 6 h at 250 | 1 500 billed |

## Applies to

Every SC11 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Rates.

## Open questions

None.
