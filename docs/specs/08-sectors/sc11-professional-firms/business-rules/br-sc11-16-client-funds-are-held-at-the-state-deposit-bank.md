# 📏 BR-SC11-16 Client funds are held at the state deposit bank

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

The funds received for a deed are held at the state deposit bank in the office's account for that deed, apart from the
office's own money, and are paid out only after the deed is signed and only from the funds of the same deed. A payment
above the deed's balance or before the signature is refused.

## Why

The funds belong to the parties, and the notary answers for them; mixing them or paying early causes losses that the
profession's guarantee fund has to cover. The owner asked for notaries
([D555](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d555-benchmark-gaps-sc11-professional-firms.md)). (Proposed) The France-pack rule
is written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Funds 312,500.00 held, deed not signed | no payment allowed |
| Deed signed, balance 312,500.00 | payments up to the balance |
| Payment of 5,000.00 asked from another deed's funds | refused |
| Deed closed with 0.00 left | allowed |
| Deed closed with funds left | refused until they are paid or returned |

## Exceptions

A deposit paid by a party before the signature may be returned to that party on the deed's cancellation, with the
notary's approval.
