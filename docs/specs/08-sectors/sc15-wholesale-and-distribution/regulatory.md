## Rules that apply

Every legal value comes from the company's country pack (D293). In France:
[CR-FR-171 Yearly written agreement between supplier and distributor](../../10-country-packs/france/trade/cr-fr-171-supplier-distributor-agreement.md),
[CR-FR-172 EORI number and import declarations](../../10-country-packs/france/trade/cr-fr-172-eori-and-imports.md),
[CR-FR-173 EU trade statistics on goods](../../10-country-packs/france/trade/cr-fr-173-eu-trade-statistics.md),
[CR-FR-174 Discounts and later rebates on invoices](../../10-country-packs/france/invoicing/cr-fr-174-discounts-on-invoices.md),
[CR-FR-175 Unfair practices between businesses](../../10-country-packs/france/trade/cr-fr-175-unfair-b2b-practices.md),
[CR-FR-018 Payment terms cap](../../10-country-packs/france/invoicing/cr-fr-018-payment-terms-cap.md),
[CR-FR-019 Late-payment penalties](../../10-country-packs/france/invoicing/cr-fr-019-late-payment-penalties.md),
[CR-FR-015 Mandatory invoice mentions](../../10-country-packs/france/invoicing/cr-fr-015-invoice-mandatory-mentions.md),
[CR-FR-016 Electronic invoicing](../../10-country-packs/france/invoicing/cr-fr-016-electronic-invoicing.md),
[CR-FR-054 Location only when needed and announced](../../10-country-packs/france/data-protection/cr-fr-054-location-with-consent.md).
Discounts on invoices and unfair practices are read in CR-FR-174 and CR-FR-175; Code de commerce L442-1 was amended by
loi n° 2026-796 from 2026-08-20 (repeated tenders, a substantial cut in order volumes during negotiations).
