# 📏 BR-SC15-03 Rebates accrued and settled

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Conditional rebates are accrued as sales happen and settled at the agreed period by credit note or invoice; in France:
[CR-FR-174 Discounts and later rebates on invoices](../../../10-country-packs/france/invoicing/cr-fr-174-discounts-on-invoices.md).

## Why

See [D359](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d359-wholesale-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 2 % over 500 000 | Accrued monthly, settled in January |

## Applies to

Every SC15 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Rebate rules.

## Open questions

None.
