# 📏 BR-SC15-02 Price from the agreement

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An order line takes the customer's agreed price, then its tier, then the list price, with the agreement's validity
checked.

## Why

See [D359](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d359-wholesale-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Agreement expired | List price, rep warned |

## Applies to

Every SC15 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Price order.

## Open questions

None.
