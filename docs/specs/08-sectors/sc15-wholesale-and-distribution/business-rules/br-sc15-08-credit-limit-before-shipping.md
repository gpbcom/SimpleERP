# 📏 BR-SC15-08 Credit limit before shipping

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An order over the customer's available credit is held for approval before shipping.

## Why

See [D359](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d359-wholesale-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Over by 4 000 | Held for accounting |

## Applies to

Every SC15 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Limits.

## Open questions

None.
