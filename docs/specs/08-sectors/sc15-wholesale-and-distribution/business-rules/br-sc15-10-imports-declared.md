# 📏 BR-SC15-10 Imports declared

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Goods from outside the EU are declared to customs with the EORI number and duties computed; in France:
[CR-FR-172 EORI number and import declarations](../../../10-country-packs/france/trade/cr-fr-172-eori-and-imports.md).

## Why

See [D359](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d359-wholesale-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Container from Asia | Declaration prepared, duties booked |

## Applies to

Every SC15 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Customs broker.

## Open questions

None.
