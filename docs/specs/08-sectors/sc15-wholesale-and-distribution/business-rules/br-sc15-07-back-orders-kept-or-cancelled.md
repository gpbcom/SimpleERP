# 📏 BR-SC15-07 Back-orders kept or cancelled

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A partial delivery keeps the rest as a back-order, unless the customer refuses partial deliveries.

## Why

See [D359](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d359-wholesale-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 80 of 100 | 20 on back-order |

## Applies to

Every SC15 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Per customer.

## Open questions

None.
