# 📏 BR-SC15-12 Supplier funds tracked

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Supplier promotions, listing fees and cooperation services are tracked against the agreement and invoiced as agreed.

## Why

See [D359](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d359-wholesale-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Listing fee due | Invoice to the supplier |

## Applies to

Every SC15 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
