# 📏 BR-SC15-09 Pallets labelled

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each pallet carries a GS1 logistic label with its serial shipping code and contents.

## Why

See [D359](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d359-wholesale-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Pallet closed | Label printed |

## Applies to

Every SC15 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

Templates.
