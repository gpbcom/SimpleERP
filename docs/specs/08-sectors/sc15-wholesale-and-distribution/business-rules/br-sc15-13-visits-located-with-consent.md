# 📏 BR-SC15-13 Visits located with consent

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A rep's visit location is recorded at check-in only, with consent; in France:
[CR-FR-054 Location only when needed and announced](../../../10-country-packs/france/data-protection/cr-fr-054-location-with-consent.md).

## Why

See [D359](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d359-wholesale-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Rep checks in | Location kept for that visit |

## Applies to

Every SC15 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
