# 📏 BR-SC15-01 Wholesale follows the country pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every legal value of wholesale (yearly agreements, customs, trade statistics, invoice discounts, payment terms) comes
from the country pack; in France:
[CR-FR-171 Yearly written agreement between supplier and distributor](../../../10-country-packs/france/trade/cr-fr-171-supplier-distributor-agreement.md),
[CR-FR-172 EORI number and import declarations](../../../10-country-packs/france/trade/cr-fr-172-eori-and-imports.md),
[CR-FR-173 EU trade statistics on goods](../../../10-country-packs/france/trade/cr-fr-173-eu-trade-statistics.md),
[CR-FR-174 Discounts and later rebates on invoices](../../../10-country-packs/france/invoicing/cr-fr-174-discounts-on-invoices.md),
[CR-FR-175 Unfair practices between businesses](../../../10-country-packs/france/trade/cr-fr-175-unfair-b2b-practices.md),
[CR-FR-018 Payment terms cap](../../../10-country-packs/france/invoicing/cr-fr-018-payment-terms-cap.md).

## Why

See [D359](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d359-wholesale-sector-coverage.md).
