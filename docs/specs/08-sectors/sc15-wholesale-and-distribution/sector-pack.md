# 🎁 Sector pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Record types

Price agreement, rebate rule, yearly agreement, sales order, pick wave, purchase proposal, return authorisation,
supplier fund, import file, statistics return, visit report, credit limit, supplier scorecard.

## Fields

Agreement and tier on order lines; channel on orders; EORI on the company; route on reps.

## Statuses and workflows

Order: held → confirmed → partly shipped → shipped. Yearly agreement: terms sent → negotiating → signed.

## Roles

Sales representative (U42), office sales (U03), purchasing (U04), warehouse (U15), customers in the portal (U09).

## Documents

Price lists, agreements, EDI messages, pallet labels, credit notes, customs and statistics files.

## Dashboards

Sales against targets, margins net of rebates, back-orders, held orders, supplier scorecards.

## Settings

Price order, rebate rules, credit limits, EDI partners, waves, return rules.

## Open questions

None.
