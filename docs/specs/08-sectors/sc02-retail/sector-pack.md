# 🎁 Sector pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Record types

Store, assortment, product grid, season, replenishment rule, store transfer, shelf label, markdown, store target, unsold
goods donation, customer profile, footfall count.

## Fields

Size and colour on products; store on every sale, stock move and staff shift; unit price and reference price on labels;
season on products.

## Statuses and workflows

Transfer: draft → sent → in transit → received. Season: planned → selling → clearing → closed. Label: to print → done.

## Roles

Store manager (U23); cashier (U17) with labels and counts; the existing warehouse and marketing roles.

## Documents

Shelf labels, transfer slips, count sheets, donation slips, schedules.

## Dashboards

Sales against target per store and staff, stock cover, transfers in transit, labels to print, sales per m2, footfall and
conversion.

## Settings

Stores and their exemptions, safety buffer for online sales, return policy, partner associations, label printers or
electronic labels.

## Open questions

None.
