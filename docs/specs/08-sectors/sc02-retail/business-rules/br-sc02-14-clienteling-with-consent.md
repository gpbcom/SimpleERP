# 📏 BR-SC02-14 Clienteling with consent

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Customer preferences and sizes are kept only with the customer's consent, and marketing follows the consent rule; in
France:
[CR-FR-063 Consent for email and SMS marketing](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-063-electronic-marketing-consent.md).

## Why

See [D320](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d320-retail-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| No consent | Purchases kept for the accounts only |

Every SC02 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

No.
