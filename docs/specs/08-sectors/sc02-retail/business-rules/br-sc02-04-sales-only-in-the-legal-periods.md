# 📏 BR-SC02-04 Sales only in the legal periods

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A markdown marked as sales applies only inside the legal periods, to goods held at least one month before, with no
restocking; in France:
[CR-FR-093 Sales periods (soldes)](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-093-sales-periods.md).

## Why

See [D320](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d320-retail-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Item received 3 weeks before the sales | Refused in the sales |

## Applies to

Every SC02 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The periods' dates per département.

## Open questions

None.
