# 📏 BR-SC02-01 Retail follows the country pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every legal value of retail (price display, reductions, sales periods, unsold goods, Sunday work, receipts, gift cards)
comes from the company's country pack; in France:
[CR-FR-092 Price and unit-price display in shops](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-092-unit-price-display.md),
[CR-FR-034 Consumers see prices including VAT](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-034-prices-shown-including-vat.md),
[CR-FR-035 Reference price of a price reduction](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-035-reference-price-for-reductions.md),
[CR-FR-093 Sales periods (soldes)](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-093-sales-periods.md),
[CR-FR-094 Unsold non-food goods](../../../10-country-packs/france/environment/cr-fr-094-unsold-non-food-goods.md),
[CR-FR-095 Donation of unsold food](../../../10-country-packs/france/environment/cr-fr-095-unsold-food-donation.md),
[CR-FR-096 Sunday and public-holiday opening of shops](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-096-sunday-and-holiday-opening.md),
[CR-FR-031 Paper receipts on request only](../../../10-country-packs/france/point-of-sale/cr-fr-031-paper-receipt-on-request.md).

## Why

See [D320](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d320-retail-sector-coverage.md).

| Case | Result |
