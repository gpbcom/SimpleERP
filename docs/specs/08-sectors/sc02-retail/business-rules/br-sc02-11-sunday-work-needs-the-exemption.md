# 📏 BR-SC02-11 Sunday work needs its exemption

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A staff schedule on a Sunday or public holiday checks the store's exemption, the employee's written consent when
required and the extra pay; in France:
[CR-FR-096 Sunday and public-holiday opening of shops](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-096-sunday-and-holiday-opening.md).

## Why

See [D320](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d320-retail-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Mayor's Sunday, no consent | Shift refused |

## Applies to

Every SC02 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The store's exemptions.

## Open questions

None.
