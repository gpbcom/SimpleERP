# 📏 BR-SC02-08 One stock for all channels

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Online channels sell store stock (click & collect, ship from store, endless aisle) minus a safety buffer per store.

## Why

See [D320](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d320-retail-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 2 left in store, buffer 1 | 1 sold online |

## Applies to

Every SC02 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The buffer per store.

## Open questions

None.
