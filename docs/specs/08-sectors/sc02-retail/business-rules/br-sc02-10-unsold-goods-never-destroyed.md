# 📏 BR-SC02-10 Unsold goods never destroyed

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Unsold goods are donated, reused or recycled, never destroyed, with each donation recorded; in France:
[CR-FR-094 Unsold non-food goods](../../../10-country-packs/france/environment/cr-fr-094-unsold-non-food-goods.md),
[CR-FR-095 Donation of unsold food](../../../10-country-packs/france/environment/cr-fr-095-unsold-food-donation.md).

## Why

See [D320](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d320-retail-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| End-of-season shampoo | Donation slip to the partner association |

## Applies to

Every SC02 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Partner associations.

## Open questions

None.
