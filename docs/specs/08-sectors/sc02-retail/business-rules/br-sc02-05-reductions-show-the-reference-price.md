# 📏 BR-SC02-05 Reductions show the reference price

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every reduction shows the reference price; in France:
[CR-FR-035 Reference price of a price reduction](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-035-reference-price-for-reductions.md).

## Why

See [D320](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d320-retail-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| -30 % | Lowest price of the last 30 days shown |

## Applies to

Every SC02 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
