# 📏 BR-SC02-02 Every product priced, with its unit price

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every product on sale shows its price including VAT, and its unit price when the country pack asks for it; in France:
[CR-FR-092 Price and unit-price display in shops](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-092-unit-price-display.md),
[CR-FR-034 Consumers see prices including VAT](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-034-prices-shown-including-vat.md).

## Why

See [D320](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d320-retail-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Coffee 250 g at 4.20 | Label shows 16.80 EUR/kg |

## Applies to

Every SC02 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
