## Rules that apply

Every legal value comes from the company's country pack (D293). In France:
[CR-FR-124 Tax receipts for gifts and their yearly declaration](../../10-country-packs/france/non-profit/cr-fr-124-gift-tax-receipts.md),
[CR-FR-125 Association accounting](../../10-country-packs/france/accounting/cr-fr-125-association-accounting.md),
[CR-FR-126 Publication of accounts above the grant threshold](../../10-country-packs/france/non-profit/cr-fr-126-accounts-publication.md),
[CR-FR-127 Grant agreement and financial report](../../10-country-packs/france/non-profit/cr-fr-127-grant-agreement-and-report.md),
[CR-FR-128 Temporary bars of associations](../../10-country-packs/france/hospitality/cr-fr-128-temporary-bars.md),
[CR-FR-129 Volunteers' expenses](../../10-country-packs/france/non-profit/cr-fr-129-volunteer-expenses.md),
[CR-FR-063 Consent for email and SMS marketing](../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-063-electronic-marketing-consent.md),
[CR-FR-051 Access and erasure requests](../../10-country-packs/france/data-protection/cr-fr-051-access-and-erasure.md),
[CR-FR-052 Personal data kept no longer than needed](../../10-country-packs/france/data-protection/cr-fr-052-storage-limitation.md),
[CR-FR-023 SEPA payment formats](../../10-country-packs/france/payments/cr-fr-023-sepa-formats.md). All are read in
their official sources (2026-11-27), the donors' tax-reduction rates included (CR-FR-124: individuals 66 % within 20 % of
taxable income, 75 % within 2 000 EUR for listed bodies; companies 60 % up to 2 M EUR and 40 % above).
