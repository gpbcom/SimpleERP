# 📏 BR-SC08-09 Volunteers repaid on proof

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Volunteers' expenses are repaid on receipts or the mileage scale; a waived repayment becomes a gift with its receipt; in
France: [CR-FR-129 Volunteers' expenses](../../../10-country-packs/france/non-profit/cr-fr-129-volunteer-expenses.md).
