# 📏 BR-SC08-11 Bars within the yearly limit

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A temporary bar is opened only with its authorisation and within the yearly number and drink groups; in France:
[CR-FR-128 Temporary bars of associations](../../../10-country-packs/france/hospitality/cr-fr-128-temporary-bars.md).

## Why

See [D338](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d338-non-profit-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

|---|---|
| 6th bar this year | Refused |

None.

No.
