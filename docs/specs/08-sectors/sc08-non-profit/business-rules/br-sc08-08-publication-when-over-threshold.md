# 📏 BR-SC08-08 Publication when over the threshold

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Crossing the grant threshold triggers the auditor and the accounts publication; in France:
[CR-FR-126 Publication of accounts above the grant threshold](../../../10-country-packs/france/non-profit/cr-fr-126-accounts-publication.md).

## Why

See [D338](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d338-non-profit-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 160 000 EUR of grants | Auditor and publication reminded |

## Applies to

Every SC08 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
