# 📏 BR-SC08-19 Funds go to the association, never the page owner

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

Every gift made on a fundraising page is paid to the association's account and recorded as a gift to the association.
The page owner never receives the funds, no page holds a personal payment account, and the tax receipt is issued to the
donor.

## Why

Money that passes through a supporter's hands cannot be receipted and exposes the association and the donor to abuse.
The owner asked for peer-to-peer fundraising
([D552](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d552-benchmark-gaps-sc08-non-profit.md)). (Proposed) The France-pack rule is
written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Donor gives 50.00 EUR on a page | paid to the association, receipt to the donor |
| Owner asks for the funds | refused, no field for it |
| Gift while the page is Suspended | refused |
| Anonymous donor | page shows anonymous, receipt in the donor's name |
| Donor with no address | gift recorded, receipt waits |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC08-24](../use-cases/07-foundations-integration-and-federations/uc-sc08-24-let-supporters-fundraise-for-us.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The wording rules of the pages and the share of fees the supporter may cover, by marketing.
