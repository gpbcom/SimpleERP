# 📏 BR-SC08-14 Members' rights honoured

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Members and donors can see, correct and erase their data, except what the law keeps; in France:
[CR-FR-051 Access and erasure requests](../../../10-country-packs/france/data-protection/cr-fr-051-access-and-erasure.md).

## Why

See [D338](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d338-non-profit-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Erasure asked | Data erased, receipts kept |

## Applies to

Every SC08 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
