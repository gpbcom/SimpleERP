# 📏 BR-SC08-05 The assembly follows the statutes

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An assembly's notice, quorum, proxies and majorities follow the association's statutes, entered as settings; minutes
record the votes.

## Why

See [D338](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d338-non-profit-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Quorum not reached | Second assembly proposed |

## Applies to

Every SC08 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Statute settings.

## Open questions

None.
