# 📏 BR-SC08-15 Gifts in kind valued

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Gifts in kind and bequests are recorded with their value and accepted by the board when the statutes require.

## Why

See [D338](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d338-non-profit-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Car given | Valued, board acceptance asked |

## Applies to

Every SC08 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Acceptance rule.

## Open questions

None.
