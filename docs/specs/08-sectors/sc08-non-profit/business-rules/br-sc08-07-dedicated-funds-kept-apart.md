# 📏 BR-SC08-07 Dedicated funds kept apart

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Gifts and grants given for a purpose and not yet used are kept as dedicated funds and carried over, as the shared
non-profit accounts do,
[BR-F8-15 Dedicated funds keep their purpose](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/business-rules/br-f08-15-dedicated-funds-keep-their-purpose.md);
in France:
[CR-FR-125 Association accounting](../../../10-country-packs/france/accounting/cr-fr-125-association-accounting.md).

## Why

See [D338](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d338-non-profit-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 10 000 EUR for a school, 4 000 spent | 6 000 dedicated |

## Applies to

Every SC08 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
