# 📏 BR-SC08-10 Volunteer time valued if chosen

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Volunteer hours are recorded, and valued in the accounts' notes only if the association chooses to.

## Why

See [D338](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d338-non-profit-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Valuation on | 1 240 h valued in the notes |

## Applies to

Every SC08 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

On or off, hourly value.

## Open questions

None.
