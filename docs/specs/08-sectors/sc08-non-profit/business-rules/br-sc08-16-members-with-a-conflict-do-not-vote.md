# 📏 BR-SC08-16 Members with a conflict do not vote

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

A member of the committee who has declared an interest in an application does not vote on it and does not count in its
quorum. A vote cast by such a member is refused and recorded. The decision is valid only when the quorum is met without
them.

## Why

A decision taken with a conflicted member can be challenged by an applicant or by the tax authority and puts the
foundation's status at risk. The owner asked for grant-making foundations
([D552](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d552-benchmark-gaps-sc08-non-profit.md)). (Proposed) The France-pack rule is
written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Member with no interest | votes |
| Member who declared an interest in application 41 | cannot vote on 41, can vote on 17 |
| Quorum of 5 with 2 members in conflict out of 7 present | quorum met without them if 5 remain |
| Vote cast by a conflicted member | refused and recorded |
| Interest declared after the vote | the vote is reopened |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC08-21](../use-cases/07-foundations-integration-and-federations/uc-sc08-21-give-grants-as-a-foundation.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The quorum and the tie-break rule follow the statutes, entered by the foundation manager.
