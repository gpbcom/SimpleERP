# 📏 BR-SC08-04 Dues make members

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A person is a member for a period once their dues are paid or waived by the board; voting rights follow.

## Why

See [D338](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d338-non-profit-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Dues unpaid | Not on the voting list |

## Applies to

Every SC08 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Periods and rates.

## Open questions

None.
