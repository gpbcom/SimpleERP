# 📏 BR-SC08-03 Receipts declared yearly

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The number of receipts and the total of gifts are declared after each year end, by the legal date; in France:
[CR-FR-124 Tax receipts for gifts and their yearly declaration](../../../10-country-packs/france/non-profit/cr-fr-124-gift-tax-receipts.md).

## Why

See [D338](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d338-non-profit-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Year 2026 | Declaration ready in April 2027 |

Every SC08 use case concerned.

None.
