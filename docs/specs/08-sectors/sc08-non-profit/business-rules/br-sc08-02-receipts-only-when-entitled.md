# 📏 BR-SC08-02 Receipts only when entitled

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Tax receipts are issued only if the association is entitled, on the official model with its identifier, one per gift or
per year, numbered; in France:
[CR-FR-124 Tax receipts for gifts and their yearly declaration](../../../10-country-packs/france/non-profit/cr-fr-124-gift-tax-receipts.md).

## Why

See [D338](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d338-non-profit-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Not entitled | Receipts disabled |

## Applies to

Every SC08 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Entitlement.

## Open questions

None.
