# 🎁 Sector pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Record types

Member, dues payment, meeting, gift, campaign, tax receipt, debit mandate, grant, grant report, mission, volunteer
hours, event, temporary bar, dedicated fund, gift in kind.

## Fields

Membership period on members; purpose on gifts and grants; receipt number on gifts; statute settings (quorum,
majorities) on the association.

## Statuses and workflows

Member: candidate → member → former. Grant: applied → granted → reported. Receipt: draft → sent.

## Roles

Treasurer (U31), volunteer (U32), the president as U13, members and donors as U09.

## Documents

Membership cards, tax receipts, assembly notices and minutes, grant reports, accounts.

## Dashboards

Members and renewals, gifts by campaign, grants and reports due, dedicated funds, volunteer hours.

## Settings

Statutes (quorum, majorities, notice), dues rates, receipt entitlement, valuation of volunteer time.

## Open questions

None.
