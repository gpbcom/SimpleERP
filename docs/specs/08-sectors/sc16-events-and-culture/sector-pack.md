# 🎁 Sector pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Record types

Event, event budget, venue room, artist contract, rider item, performer declaration, ticket order, ticket,
accreditation, shift, sponsorship, stand, event till, safety file, show declaration, membership.

## Fields

Declaration number on the company; seat map on rooms; category and VAT on tickets; zones on badges.

## Statuses and workflows

Event: planned → on sale → sold out → held → closed, or cancelled. Ticket order: paid → refunded or exchanged.

## Roles

Event producer (U43), box-office agent (U44), volunteers (U32), attendees and artists (U09).

## Documents

Contracts, riders, tickets, badges, day sheets, declarations, after-event reports.

## Dashboards

Tickets sold against capacity, budget against actual, admissions, shift gaps, declarations due.

## Settings

Declaration number, seat maps, price categories, hold time, refund policy, zones.

## Open questions

None.
