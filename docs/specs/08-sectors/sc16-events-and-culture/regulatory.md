# ⚖️ Regulatory

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Rules that apply

Every legal value comes from the company's country pack (D293). In France:
[CR-FR-176 Performing-arts organiser's declaration](../../10-country-packs/france/events/cr-fr-176-performing-arts-declaration.md),
[CR-FR-177 Tax on show tickets](../../10-country-packs/france/events/cr-fr-177-show-ticket-tax.md),
[CR-FR-178 Hiring performers as an occasional organiser (GUSO)](../../10-country-packs/france/events/cr-fr-178-occasional-organiser-guso.md),
[CR-FR-179 Music copyright at events](../../10-country-packs/france/events/cr-fr-179-music-copyright-authorisation.md),
[CR-FR-180 Safety register of venues open to the public](../../10-country-packs/france/events/cr-fr-180-venue-safety-register.md),
[CR-FR-181 VAT on tickets](../../10-country-packs/france/tax-and-vat/cr-fr-181-ticket-vat-rates.md),
[CR-FR-128 Temporary bars of associations](../../10-country-packs/france/hospitality/cr-fr-128-temporary-bars.md),
[CR-FR-037 Hiring declaration before the first day](../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-037-dpae-before-the-first-day.md),
[CR-FR-036 Consumer withdrawal right](../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-036-withdrawal-right.md),
[CR-FR-063 Consent for email and SMS marketing](../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-063-electronic-marketing-consent.md).
Music copyright and ticket VAT are read in CR-FR-179 and CR-FR-181 (the VAT rates at the CGI articles until 2026-12-31,
at the CIBS rate articles from 2027-01-01); the cinema tax is read in
[CR-FR-248](../../10-country-packs/france/events/cr-fr-248-cinema-box-office.md), with the receipts declared each week through
Cinedi per film, version and screen; the distributor's share waits for the lawyer's review (D574).
