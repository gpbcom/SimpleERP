# 📏 BR-SC16-13 Bars within the licence

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Event bars sell within their licence or temporary authorisation; in France:
[CR-FR-128 Temporary bars of associations](../../../10-country-packs/france/hospitality/cr-fr-128-temporary-bars.md).

## Why

See [D362](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d362-events-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Spirits at a temporary bar | Refused |

## Applies to

Every SC16 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
