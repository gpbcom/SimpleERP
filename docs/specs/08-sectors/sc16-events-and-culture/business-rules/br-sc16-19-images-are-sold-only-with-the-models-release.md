# 📏 BR-SC16-19 Images are sold only with the model's release

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

A picture that shows an identifiable person is published or sold only when that person has signed a release covering the
use, the duration and the territory. A gallery is published only when every picture in it is covered.

## Why

Publishing or selling the image of a person without their agreement breaches their right to their image and exposes the
photographer to claims. The owner asked for photographers
([D560](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d560-benchmark-gaps-sc16-events-and-culture.md)). (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) The France-pack
rule is still to write: to read the Code civil art. 9 (right to one's image) and the rules on minors' consent.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Gallery with 3 people and 3 releases | published |
| Gallery with 3 people and 2 releases | refused, the pictures of the third are listed |
| Release for web use only | prints are refused |
| Minor with a guardian's release | accepted |
| Release withdrawn | the pictures are taken down |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC16-24](../use-cases/06-cashless-cinemas-tours-and-photos/uc-sc16-24-book-shoots-and-sell-photos.md)
- [E-SC16-22 Gallery](../data/e-sc16-22-gallery.md)
- [E-SC16-22 Gallery](../data/e-sc16-22-gallery.md)

## Exceptions

A picture of a crowd where no person is identifiable needs no release.

## Configurable

The release templates and the default duration, by the photographer.

## Open questions

None.
