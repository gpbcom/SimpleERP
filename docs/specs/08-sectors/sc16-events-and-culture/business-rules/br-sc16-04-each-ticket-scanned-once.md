# 📏 BR-SC16-04 Each ticket scanned once

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A ticket admits once, as the shared ticketing model says,
[BR-F5-13 A ticket opens the door once](../../../02-functional/f05-social-media/business-rules/br-f05-13-a-ticket-opens-the-door-once.md);
a second scan is refused and shown with the first scan's time and gate.

## Why

See [D362](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d362-events-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Copy scanned | Refused, first scan 20:12 gate B |

## Applies to

Every SC16 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Re-entry passes.

## Open questions

None.
