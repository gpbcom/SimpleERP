# 📏 BR-SC16-09 Safety file before opening

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An event or venue opens only with its safety file complete and its register current; in France:
[CR-FR-180 Safety register of venues open to the public](../../../10-country-packs/france/events/cr-fr-180-venue-safety-register.md).

## Why

See [D362](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d362-events-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Check overdue | Opening blocked |

## Applies to

Every SC16 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
