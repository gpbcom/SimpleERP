# 📏 BR-SC16-05 Ticket tax declared per show

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each show's ticket receipts are declared and taxed by the deadline; in France:
[CR-FR-177 Tax on show tickets](../../../10-country-packs/france/events/cr-fr-177-show-ticket-tax.md).

## Why

See [D362](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d362-events-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Show 12 Dec | Declaration due 31 March |

## Applies to

Every SC16 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
