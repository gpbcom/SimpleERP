# 📏 BR-SC16-10 Refunds on cancellation

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A cancelled event refunds every ticket by the original payment means within the set delay, or offers an exchange.

## Why

See [D362](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d362-events-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Show cancelled | 412 refunds started |

## Applies to

Every SC16 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Delay, exchange policy.

## Open questions

None.
