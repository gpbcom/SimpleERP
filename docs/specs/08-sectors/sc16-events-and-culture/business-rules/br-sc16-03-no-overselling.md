# 📏 BR-SC16-03 No overselling

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Tickets never exceed the room's capacity or the seat map: the shared ticketing model sells one seat once across every
channel,
[BR-F5-12 One seat, one ticket, across every channel](../../../02-functional/f05-social-media/business-rules/br-f05-12-one-seat-one-ticket-across-every-channel.md);
a seat is held during payment and released on time-out.

## Why

See [D362](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d362-events-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Last seat in two baskets | First paid wins, the other told |

## Applies to

Every SC16 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Hold time.

## Open questions

None.
