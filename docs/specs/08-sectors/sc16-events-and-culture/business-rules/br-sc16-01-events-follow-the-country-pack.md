# 📏 BR-SC16-01 Events follow the country pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every legal value of events and culture (organiser's declaration, ticket tax, performers' hiring, copyright, venue
safety, ticket VAT) comes from the country pack; in France:
[CR-FR-176 Performing-arts organiser's declaration](../../../10-country-packs/france/events/cr-fr-176-performing-arts-declaration.md),
[CR-FR-177 Tax on show tickets](../../../10-country-packs/france/events/cr-fr-177-show-ticket-tax.md),
[CR-FR-178 Hiring performers as an occasional organiser (GUSO)](../../../10-country-packs/france/events/cr-fr-178-occasional-organiser-guso.md),
[CR-FR-179 Music copyright at events](../../../10-country-packs/france/events/cr-fr-179-music-copyright-authorisation.md),
[CR-FR-180 Safety register of venues open to the public](../../../10-country-packs/france/events/cr-fr-180-venue-safety-register.md),
[CR-FR-181 VAT on tickets](../../../10-country-packs/france/tax-and-vat/cr-fr-181-ticket-vat-rates.md).

## Why

See [D362](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d362-events-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| A French festival sells tickets | Receipt number printed, ticket tax computed |

## Applies to

Every SC16 use case concerned.

None.
