# 📏 BR-SC16-06 Performers declared before the show

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each artist or technician is declared before the show and their contract declared and paid after; in France:
[CR-FR-178 Hiring performers as an occasional organiser (GUSO)](../../../10-country-packs/france/events/cr-fr-178-occasional-organiser-guso.md),
[CR-FR-037 Hiring declaration before the first day](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-037-dpae-before-the-first-day.md).

## Why

See [D362](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d362-events-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Artist not declared at 18:00 | Alert before the show |

## Applies to

Every SC16 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Occasional or regular employer.

## Open questions

None.
