# 📏 BR-SC16-02 Declaration number on tickets

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The organiser's declaration number is printed on tickets, posters and leaflets when required; in France:
[CR-FR-176 Performing-arts organiser's declaration](../../../10-country-packs/france/events/cr-fr-176-performing-arts-declaration.md).

## Why

See [D362](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d362-events-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 8th show this year, no number | Ticket sales blocked |

## Applies to

Every SC16 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
