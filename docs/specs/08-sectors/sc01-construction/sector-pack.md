# 🎁 Sector pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Record types

Site, quote lot, site phase, site diary entry, progress bill, subcontract, equipment assignment, works declaration,
acceptance record, warranty, public tender.

## Fields

Lots and unit-price lines on quotes; site and lot on every time, material and purchase line; the insurance certificate
on the company; the VAT certificate on quotes; the commitment number for public buyers.

## Statuses and workflows

Site: planned → running → accepted → closed. Progress bill: draft → sent → paid. Reservation: open → lifted.
Subcontract: draft → accepted → running → closed.

## Roles

Site manager (U22); field worker (U14) with site entry; the existing sales, purchasing and accounting roles.

## Documents

Quote by lots, progress bill, subcontract, acceptance record with reservations, as-built file, reverse-charge invoice,
VAT certificate, works declaration.

## Dashboards

Sites by state, margin at completion, progress billed vs done, open reservations, warranties running, documents
expiring.

## Settings

Retention rate and release, margin alert threshold, the insurance certificate, the paid-leave fund, Chorus Pro account.

## Open questions

None.
