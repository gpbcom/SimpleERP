# 📏 BR-SC01-17 Calls for funds follow the legal stage caps

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

A call for funds on an off-plan lot never brings the total called above the share the law allows at the stage reached,
and a stage opens only when the site manager has certified it. A call above the cap is cut to the allowed amount and the
rest moves to the next stage.

## Why

The cap protects the buyer, who pays for a building that does not exist yet; calling more is unlawful and exposes the
developer. The owner asked for the property developer
([D545](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d545-benchmark-gaps-sc01-construction.md)). The ceilings are data of
the France pack, written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Lot of 300,000.00 EUR, first cap 35 % | first call at most 105,000.00 EUR |
| Stage not certified | no call |
| Total called 70 % and the next stage allows 95 % | the next call is at most 25 % |
| Delivery balance | called at delivery only |
| Cut call | the cut amount moves to the next stage |

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The ceilings by stage, as data of the country pack; the stages of a programme, by the site manager.

## Open questions

None.
