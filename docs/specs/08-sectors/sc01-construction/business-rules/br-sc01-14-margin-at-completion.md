# 📏 BR-SC01-14 Margin at completion

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A site's margin at completion is its contract and agreed extras minus its actual costs and the estimate to complete; a
margin below the threshold alerts the site manager.

## Why

See [D316](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d316-construction-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Margin falls to 4 % | Alert, threshold 8 % |

## Applies to

Every SC01 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The threshold.

## Open questions

None.
