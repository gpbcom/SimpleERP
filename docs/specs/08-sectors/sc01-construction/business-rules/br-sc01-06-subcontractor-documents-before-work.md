# 📏 BR-SC01-06 Subcontractor documents before work

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A subcontractor works on a site only once accepted by the project owner with its payment terms, and with a vigilance
certificate less than 6 months old when the contract reaches the threshold; in France:
[CR-FR-085 Vigilance over subcontractors](../../../10-country-packs/france/construction/cr-fr-085-subcontractor-vigilance.md),
[CR-FR-086 Subcontractor acceptance and payment guarantee](../../../10-country-packs/france/construction/cr-fr-086-subcontractor-acceptance-and-payment-guarantee.md).

## Why

See [D316](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d316-construction-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Certificate 7 months old | Subcontractor flagged, renewal asked |

## Applies to

Every SC01 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
