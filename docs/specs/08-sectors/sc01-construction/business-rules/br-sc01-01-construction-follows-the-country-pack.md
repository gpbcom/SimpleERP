# 📏 BR-SC01-01 Construction follows the country pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every legal value of construction (insurance mentions, warranties, retention, VAT, subcontracting, site cards, works
declarations, public payment) comes from the company's country pack; in France:
[CR-FR-082 Ten-year liability insurance](../../../10-country-packs/france/construction/cr-fr-082-ten-year-insurance.md),
[CR-FR-090 Builders' warranties and acceptance](../../../10-country-packs/france/construction/cr-fr-090-builders-warranties.md),
[CR-FR-021 Construction retention guarantee](../../../10-country-packs/france/invoicing/cr-fr-021-construction-retention-guarantee.md),
[CR-FR-083 Reverse charge on construction subcontracting](../../../10-country-packs/france/tax-and-vat/cr-fr-083-construction-subcontracting-reverse-charge.md),
[CR-FR-084 Reduced VAT on housing renovation](../../../10-country-packs/france/tax-and-vat/cr-fr-084-renovation-reduced-vat.md),
[CR-FR-085 Vigilance over subcontractors](../../../10-country-packs/france/construction/cr-fr-085-subcontractor-vigilance.md).

## Why

See [D316](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d316-construction-sector-coverage.md).
