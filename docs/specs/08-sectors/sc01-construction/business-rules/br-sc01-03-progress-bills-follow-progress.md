# 📏 BR-SC01-03 Progress bills follow progress

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A progress bill states the cumulative progress of each lot, deducts the earlier bills and deposits, and holds back the
retention; in France:
[CR-FR-021 Construction retention guarantee](../../../10-country-packs/france/invoicing/cr-fr-021-construction-retention-guarantee.md).

## Why

See [D316](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d316-construction-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Lot 2 at 60 %, 40 % billed before | 20 % billed, 5 % held back |

## Applies to

Every SC01 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Retention or bank guarantee per contract.

## Open questions

None.
