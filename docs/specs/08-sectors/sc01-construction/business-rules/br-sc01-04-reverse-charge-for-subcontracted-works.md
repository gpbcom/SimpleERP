# 📏 BR-SC01-04 Reverse charge for subcontracted works

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A subcontractor's invoice of works for a VAT-registered customer has no VAT and says "autoliquidation"; the customer
self-assesses; in France:
[CR-FR-083 Reverse charge on construction subcontracting](../../../10-country-packs/france/tax-and-vat/cr-fr-083-construction-subcontracting-reverse-charge.md).

## Why

See [D316](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d316-construction-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Invoice to a main contractor | 0 VAT, mention printed |

## Applies to

Every SC01 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
