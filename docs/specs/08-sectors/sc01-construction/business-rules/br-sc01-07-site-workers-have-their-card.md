# 📏 BR-SC01-07 Site workers have their card

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A worker assigned to a site needs a declared identification card; in France:
[CR-FR-087 Construction identification card (carte BTP)](../../../10-country-packs/france/construction/cr-fr-087-btp-identification-card.md).

## Why

See [D316](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d316-construction-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| New hire without a card | Assignment flagged |

## Applies to

Every SC01 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
