# 📏 BR-SC01-11 Retention released when due

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The retention is released one year after acceptance unless a reservation notified stays open, or earlier against a bank
guarantee; in France:
[CR-FR-021 Construction retention guarantee](../../../10-country-packs/france/invoicing/cr-fr-021-construction-retention-guarantee.md).

## Why

See [D316](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d316-construction-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| One year, no open reservation | Release invoice proposed |

## Applies to

Every SC01 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
