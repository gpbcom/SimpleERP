# 📏 BR-SC01-13 Consumer works signed off premises

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A quote a consumer signs at home carries the withdrawal right; works start before its end only at the consumer's express
request; in France:
[CR-FR-036 Consumer withdrawal right](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-036-withdrawal-right.md).

## Why

See [D318](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d318-construction-customers.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Signed at home on 2 Dec | Works from 17 Dec, or earlier on written request |

## Applies to

Every SC01 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
