# 📏 BR-SC01-13 Consumer works signed off premises

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A quote a consumer signs at home carries the 14-day withdrawal right; works start before its end only at the
consumer's express request. No payment and no consideration of any kind is taken for 7 days from the signature (Code de
la consommation L221-10), except for urgent maintenance or repair work the consumer expressly asked for, limited to the
parts and work strictly needed, which may be paid at once and has no withdrawal right (L221-28); in France:
[CR-FR-036 Consumer withdrawal right](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-036-withdrawal-right.md). The
owner chose to follow the law, as for trades and home services ([D575](../../../06-decisions/foundations/d575-answers-of-2026-11-27-on-the-last-open-points.md)).

## Why

See [D318](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d318-construction-customers.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Signed at home on 2 Dec | Works from 17 Dec, or earlier on written request |
| Signed at home on 2 Dec, deposit asked on 6 Dec | refused |
| Signed at home on 2 Dec, deposit asked on 10 Dec, not withdrawn | taken; refunded if the customer withdraws by 16 Dec |
| Urgent repair expressly asked at home | the urgent part is done and paid at once, with no withdrawal right |

## Applies to

Every SC01 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The 14-day withdrawal delay and the 7-day payment ban, from the country pack.

## Open questions

None.
