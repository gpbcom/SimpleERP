# 📏 BR-SC01-02 Insurance certificate on quotes and invoices

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Quotes and invoices of works carry the ten-year insurance certificate of the company; an expired certificate blocks
sending them; in France:
[CR-FR-082 Ten-year liability insurance](../../../10-country-packs/france/construction/cr-fr-082-ten-year-insurance.md).

## Why

See [D316](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d316-construction-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Certificate expired on 31 Oct | Quote not sent, the administrator told |

## Applies to

Every SC01 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The certificate and its validity.

## Open questions

None.
