# 📏 BR-SC01-05 Reduced VAT needs the certificate

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A 10 % or 5.5 % rate on housing works applies only once the customer's signed certificate is on the quote or invoice and
kept; otherwise the normal rate; in France:
[CR-FR-084 Reduced VAT on housing renovation](../../../10-country-packs/france/tax-and-vat/cr-fr-084-renovation-reduced-vat.md).

## Why

See [D316](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d316-construction-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Insulation, certificate signed | 5.5 % |
| Same, no certificate | 20 % until signed |

## Applies to

Every SC01 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
