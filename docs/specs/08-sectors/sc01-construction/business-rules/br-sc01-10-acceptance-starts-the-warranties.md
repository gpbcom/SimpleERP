# 📏 BR-SC01-10 Acceptance starts the warranties

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Signing acceptance starts the one-, two- and ten-year warranties and the retention's release date; reservations are
listed in the record; in France:
[CR-FR-090 Builders' warranties and acceptance](../../../10-country-packs/france/construction/cr-fr-090-builders-warranties.md).

## Why

See [D316](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d316-construction-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Accepted on 12 Dec with 3 reservations | Warranties to 12 Dec 2027, 2028, 2036 |

## Applies to

Every SC01 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
