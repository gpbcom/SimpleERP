# 📏 BR-SC09-03 Secrecy by default

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Only the practitioner and the care team they choose see clinical content; the secretary sees administration and billing,
not clinical notes; every access is logged; in France:
[CR-FR-137 Professional secrecy](../../../10-country-packs/france/health-practice/cr-fr-137-medical-secrecy.md).

## Why

See [D341](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d341-health-practices-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Secretary opens a record | Admin tab only |

## Applies to

Every SC09 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Care-team sharing.

## Open questions

None.
