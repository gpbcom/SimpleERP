# 📏 BR-SC09-04 Fees shown before the act

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The practice's fees and sector are displayed, and written information is given before fees over the threshold above the
tariff; in France:
[CR-FR-130 Display of fees in a practice](../../../10-country-packs/france/health-practice/cr-fr-130-fee-display.md).

## Why

See [D341](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d341-health-practices-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Extra fee of 80 EUR | Written estimate printed for signature |

## Applies to

Every SC09 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Fees.

## Open questions

None.
