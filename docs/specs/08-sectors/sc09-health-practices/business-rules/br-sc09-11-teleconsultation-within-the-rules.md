# 📏 BR-SC09-11 Teleconsultation within the rules

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A teleconsultation is billed as such only within the reimbursement conditions; its report goes to the record; in France:
[CR-FR-138 Teleconsultation](../../../10-country-packs/france/health-practice/cr-fr-138-teleconsultation.md).

## Why

See [D341](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d341-health-practices-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Patient outside the territory, no exception | Warned before billing |

## Applies to

Every SC09 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
