# 📏 BR-SC09-01 Health practices follow the country pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every legal value of a practice (fees, records, insurance billing, third-party payment, e-prescription, the national
record, secrecy, teleconsultation, hosting) comes from the country pack; in France:
[CR-FR-130 Display of fees in a practice](../../../10-country-packs/france/health-practice/cr-fr-130-fee-display.md),
[CR-FR-131 Patients' access to their medical record](../../../10-country-packs/france/health-practice/cr-fr-131-medical-record-access.md),
[CR-FR-132 SESAM-Vitale approval of billing software](../../../10-country-packs/france/health-practice/cr-fr-132-sesam-vitale-approval.md),
[CR-FR-133 Third-party payment (tiers payant)](../../../10-country-packs/france/health-practice/cr-fr-133-third-party-payment.md),
[CR-FR-134 E-prescription](../../../10-country-packs/france/health-practice/cr-fr-134-e-prescription.md),
[CR-FR-135 The national health record (Mon espace santé)](../../../10-country-packs/france/health-practice/cr-fr-135-national-health-record.md).

## Why

See [D341](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d341-health-practices-sector-coverage.md).
