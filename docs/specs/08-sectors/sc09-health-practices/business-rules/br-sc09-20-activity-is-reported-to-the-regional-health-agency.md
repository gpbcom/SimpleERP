# 📏 BR-SC09-20 Activity is reported to the regional health agency

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The activity of a health centre is reported to the regional health agency from the centre's own records, only after the
director has confirmed the figures, and the agency's acknowledgement is stored with the report. A period that is not
complete is not sent without a warning.

## Why

The agency follows each centre's health project and may adjust its support; wrong or late figures put the approval at
risk. The owner asked for health centres
([D553](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d553-benchmark-gaps-sc09-health-practices.md)). The dates and the
format come from the France pack, written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| October counted from 1,240 visits | a report with 1,240 visits |
| A visit without a practitioner | listed, not counted |
| Report sent, acknowledged | the period is locked |
| Agency asks for a correction | the period is reopened with the reason |
| Gap below target | the director's comment goes with the report |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC09-25](../use-cases/07-pharmacy-vets-dental-and-centres/uc-sc09-25-run-a-health-centre.md)
- [E-SC09-18 Centre activity](../data/e-sc09-18-centre-activity.md)
- [E-SC09-01 Appointment](../data/e-sc09-01-appointment.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The reporting dates and format, as data of the country pack; the targets of the health project, by the director.

## Open questions

None.
