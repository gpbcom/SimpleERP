# 📏 BR-SC09-02 Health data only on certified hosting

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Clinical records, results and any health information are stored only on certified health-data hosting; a company without
it cannot enable the clinical record; in France:
[CR-FR-055 Health data hosting](../../../10-country-packs/france/data-protection/cr-fr-055-health-data-hosting.md).

## Why

See [D341](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d341-health-practices-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Install without certified hosting | Clinical record disabled |

## Applies to

Every SC09 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
