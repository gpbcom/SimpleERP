# 📏 BR-SC09-12 Costs shared by the key

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Shared practice costs are split between partners by the key of their agreement.

## Why

See [D341](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d341-health-practices-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Rent 3 000, 3 partners equal | 1 000 each |

## Applies to

Every SC09 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Keys per cost.

## Open questions

None.
