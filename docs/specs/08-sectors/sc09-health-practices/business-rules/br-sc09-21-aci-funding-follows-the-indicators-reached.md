# 📏 BR-SC09-21 ACI funding follows the indicators reached

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The funding of a multi-professional practice is computed from the points of the indicators reached in the year, each
indicator counted from the practice's records or declared with its evidence. An indicator with no source earns no points
until it has one.

## Why

The health insurance pays the practice for what it can show; an indicator with no proof is not paid and can be
reclaimed. The owner asked for multi-professional practices
([D553](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d553-benchmark-gaps-sc09-health-practices.md)). The indicators and the
amounts come from the France pack, written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 93 % of patients with a named doctor, target 90 % | 60 points |
| Shared meetings 4 of 6 | partial points, declared with evidence |
| No source for an indicator | 0 points, the source is named |
| Year confirmed before its end | provisional |
| Insurance corrects the points | the funding is recomputed |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC09-26](../use-cases/07-pharmacy-vets-dental-and-centres/uc-sc09-26-run-a-multi-professional-practice.md)
- [E-SC09-19 ACI indicator](../data/e-sc09-19-aci-indicator.md)
- [E-SC09-13 Certified module](../data/e-sc09-13-certified-module.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The indicators, their targets and points, and the amount per point, as data of the country pack and the contract.
