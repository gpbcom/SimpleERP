# 📏 BR-SC09-08 Record shared with the national record

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The documents the law lists are sent to the patient's national health record unless they objected; the yearly summary is
reminded; in France:
[CR-FR-135 The national health record (Mon espace santé)](../../../10-country-packs/france/health-practice/cr-fr-135-national-health-record.md).

## Why

See [D341](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d341-health-practices-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Lab report filed | Sent to the national record |

## Applies to

Every SC09 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
