# 📏 BR-SC09-05 Care sheets through the approved module

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Electronic care sheets are produced and sent only by the approved billing module, with the patient's card or its digital
equivalent; in France:
[CR-FR-132 SESAM-Vitale approval of billing software](../../../10-country-packs/france/health-practice/cr-fr-132-sesam-vitale-approval.md).

## Why

See [D341](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d341-health-practices-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Card read | Care sheet signed and sent |

## Applies to

Every SC09 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
