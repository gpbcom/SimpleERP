# 📏 BR-SC09-06 Third-party payment when due

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Third-party payment is applied automatically in the cases the law lists, and offered otherwise; the insurers' and the
patient's shares follow the shared multi-payer rule,
[BR-F6-13 Each payer pays its share up to its cap](../../../02-functional/f06-customers-and-sales/business-rules/br-f06-13-each-payer-pays-its-share-up-to-its-cap.md);
in France:
[CR-FR-133 Third-party payment (tiers payant)](../../../10-country-packs/france/health-practice/cr-fr-133-third-party-payment.md).

## Why

See [D341](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d341-health-practices-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Maternity | Patient pays nothing upfront |

## Applies to

Every SC09 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Offer it to everyone or not.

## Open questions

None.
