# 📏 BR-SC09-15 Certifications kept current

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each certified module's approval (such as SESAM-Vitale) and Ségur referencing are tracked; an expired approval
disables the module, while a lapsed Ségur referencing only warns the provider, as it is a funding scheme and not an
obligation of the practice; in France:
[CR-FR-136 Ségur software labels and secure health messaging](../../../10-country-packs/france/health-practice/cr-fr-136-segur-labels.md).

## Why

The Ségur programme is a State funding scheme for the publishers of referenced software, not an obligation of the
practice; the Agence du numérique en santé references software at its publisher's request, and the detailed criteria
are in annex 1 of each arrêté ([CR-FR-136 Ségur software labels and secure health messaging](../../../10-country-packs/france/health-practice/cr-fr-136-segur-labels.md));
the approvals the law requires, such as SESAM-Vitale for billing
([CR-FR-132](../../../10-country-packs/france/health-practice/cr-fr-132-sesam-vitale-approval.md)), condition the module.
