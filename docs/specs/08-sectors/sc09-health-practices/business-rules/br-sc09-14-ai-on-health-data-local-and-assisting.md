# 📏 BR-SC09-14 AI on health data local and assisting

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The AI assistant works on health data only with local models, never decides a diagnosis, and says it is an AI; in
France:
[CR-FR-056 People know they deal with an AI](../../../10-country-packs/france/ai-and-recording/cr-fr-056-people-know-they-deal-with-an-ai.md).

## Why
