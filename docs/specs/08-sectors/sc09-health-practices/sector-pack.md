# 🎁 Sector pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Record types

Appointment, patient, clinical record entry, prescription, care sheet, insurer return, patient invoice, health exchange,
room, shared cost, sterilisation cycle, locum agreement, certified module.

## Fields

Insurance and consents on patients; fee sector on practitioners; third-party flag on care sheets; national-record
objection on patients.

## Statuses and workflows

Appointment: booked → confirmed → done or no-show. Care sheet: signed → sent → paid or rejected.

## Roles

Practitioner (U13) with the clinical record; medical secretary (U33) without clinical notes; patients (U09) in the
portal.

## Documents

Prescriptions, care sheets, patient invoices, fee estimates, record copies.

## Dashboards

Agenda fill and no-shows, care sheets rejected, unpaid shares, shared costs, indicators.

## Settings

Fee sector and fees, third-party policy, reminders, cost-sharing keys, certified modules.

## Open questions

None.
