# 📏 BR-SC14-02 Seats and usage billed from records

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Seats are billed from the subscription and usage from metered records, with proration on changes.

## Why

See [D356](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d356-it-and-software-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 5 seats added on day 15 | Half a month billed |

## Applies to

Every SC14 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Proration rules.

## Open questions

None.
