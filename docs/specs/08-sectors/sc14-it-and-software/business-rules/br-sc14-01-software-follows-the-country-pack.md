# 📏 BR-SC14-01 Software follows the country pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every legal value of software and IT services (processor terms, incident reporting, switching, VAT, e-invoicing) comes
from the country pack; in France:
[CR-FR-049 Processors and transfers outside the EU](../../../10-country-packs/france/data-protection/cr-fr-049-processors-and-transfers.md),
[CR-FR-168 Cyber Resilience Act reporting](../../../10-country-packs/france/digital/cr-fr-168-cyber-resilience-reporting.md),
[CR-FR-169 NIS2 incident notification](../../../10-country-packs/france/digital/cr-fr-169-nis2-incident-notification.md),
[CR-FR-170 Switching between cloud services (Data Act)](../../../10-country-packs/france/digital/cr-fr-170-data-act-switching.md),
[CR-FR-071 VAT on distance sales in the EU](../../../10-country-packs/france/tax-and-vat/cr-fr-071-distance-sales-vat-oss.md),
[CR-FR-016 Electronic invoicing](../../../10-country-packs/france/invoicing/cr-fr-016-electronic-invoicing.md).

## Why

See [D356](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d356-it-and-software-sector-coverage.md).
