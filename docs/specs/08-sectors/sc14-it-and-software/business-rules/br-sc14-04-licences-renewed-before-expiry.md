# 📏 BR-SC14-04 Licences renewed before expiry

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A licence or subscription close to its end creates a renewal task and a quote at the set lead time.

## Why

See [D356](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d356-it-and-software-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Ends in 90 days | Renewal opened |

## Applies to

Every SC14 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Lead time.

## Open questions

None.
