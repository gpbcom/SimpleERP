# 📏 BR-SC14-10 Export and switch without obstacle

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A customer can export all its data in a common machine-readable format and switch provider without charges after the
date the law sets; in France:
[CR-FR-170 Switching between cloud services (Data Act)](../../../10-country-packs/france/digital/cr-fr-170-data-act-switching.md).

## Why

See [D356](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d356-it-and-software-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Switch in 2027 | No switching charge |

## Applies to

Every SC14 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
