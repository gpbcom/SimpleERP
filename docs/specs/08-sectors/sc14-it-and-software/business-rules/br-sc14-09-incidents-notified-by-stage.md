# 📏 BR-SC14-09 Incidents notified by stage

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

A significant incident of an essential or important entity is notified in stages; in France:
[CR-FR-169 NIS2 incident notification](../../../10-country-packs/france/digital/cr-fr-169-nis2-incident-notification.md).

## Why

The directive covers medium-sized and large entities of the critical sectors and asks for an early warning within 24
hours, a notification within 72 hours and a final report within one month (CR-FR-169, Directive (EU) 2022/2555 art.
23). Until the French transposition texts are in force, NIS2 does not apply to French entities.
(⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) The French authority, deadlines and thresholds are still to read in the French law once it
is published.
