# 📏 BR-SC14-05 Service levels measured

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each ticket's response and resolution times are measured against the customer's service level, with credits when the
contract says so.

## Why

See [D356](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d356-it-and-software-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| P1 answered after 2 h, level 1 h | Breach, credit proposed |

## Applies to

Every SC14 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Levels per contract.

## Open questions

None.
