# 📏 BR-SC14-07 Vulnerabilities reported in time

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An actively exploited vulnerability or severe incident in a product starts the reporting clock: early warning,
notification, final report; in France:
[CR-FR-168 Cyber Resilience Act reporting](../../../10-country-packs/france/digital/cr-fr-168-cyber-resilience-reporting.md).

## Why

See [D356](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d356-it-and-software-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Exploit known at 09:00 | Early warning due 09:00 next day |

## Applies to

Every SC14 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
