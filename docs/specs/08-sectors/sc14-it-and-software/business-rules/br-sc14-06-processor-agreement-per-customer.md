# 📏 BR-SC14-06 A processor agreement per customer

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each customer whose personal data is processed has a signed processor agreement with the mandatory clauses; in France:
[CR-FR-049 Processors and transfers outside the EU](../../../10-country-packs/france/data-protection/cr-fr-049-processors-and-transfers.md).

## Why

See [D356](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d356-it-and-software-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| No agreement | Onboarding blocked |

## Applies to

Every SC14 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
