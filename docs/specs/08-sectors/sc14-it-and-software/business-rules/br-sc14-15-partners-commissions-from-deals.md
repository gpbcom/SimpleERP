# 📏 BR-SC14-15 Partners' commissions from deals

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Reseller and partner commissions follow the shared commissions,
[BR-F6-18 Commissions follow paid invoices](../../../02-functional/f06-customers-and-sales/business-rules/br-f06-18-commissions-follow-paid-invoices.md),
computed from the deals partners bring and paid on collection.

## Why

See [D356](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d356-it-and-software-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Deal 12 000, 20 % | 2 400 on payment |

## Applies to

Every SC14 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Rates.

## Open questions

None.
