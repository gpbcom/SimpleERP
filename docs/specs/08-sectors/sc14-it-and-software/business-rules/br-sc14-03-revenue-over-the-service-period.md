# 📏 BR-SC14-03 Revenue over the service period

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Subscription revenue is recognised over the service period; billed but not yet earned is deferred.

## Why

See [D356](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d356-it-and-software-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Yearly plan billed in January | 1/12 a month |

## Applies to

Every SC14 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
