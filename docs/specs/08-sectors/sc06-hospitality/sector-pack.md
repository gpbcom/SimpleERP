# 🎁 Sector pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Record types

Recipe, menu dish, hygiene record, waste record, staff benefit, room, rate plan, booking, folio, housekeeping task,
tourist tax line, meal voucher payment.

## Fields

Allergens on ingredients and dishes; home-made on dishes; licence on the venue; room and folio on outlet orders; commune
rate on the hotel.

## Statuses and workflows

Room: dirty → clean → inspected. Booking: booked → arrived → departed or cancelled. Folio: open → settled.

## Roles

Hotel receptionist (U28); the F10 roles of cashier, waiter and kitchen; the venue manager as U23.

## Documents

Menus with allergens, hygiene sheets, folios, booking confirmations, tourist-tax declarations.

## Dashboards

Occupancy, average rate, revenue per available room, covers, food-cost ratio, hygiene readings missed.

## Settings

Licence, hygiene schedules, rate plans, channels, commune tourist-tax rates, tip sharing key.

## Open questions

None.
