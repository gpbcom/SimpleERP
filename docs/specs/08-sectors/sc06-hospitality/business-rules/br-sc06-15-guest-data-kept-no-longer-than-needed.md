# 📏 BR-SC06-15 Guest data kept no longer than needed

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Guest records are kept for the stay and the law's periods, then deleted; in France:
[CR-FR-052 Personal data kept no longer than needed](../../../10-country-packs/france/data-protection/cr-fr-052-storage-limitation.md).

## Why

See [D332](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d332-hospitality-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Guest from 2022 | Personal data deleted, invoices kept |

## Applies to

Every SC06 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
