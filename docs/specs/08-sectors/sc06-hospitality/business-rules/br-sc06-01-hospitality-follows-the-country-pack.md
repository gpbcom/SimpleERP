# 📏 BR-SC06-01 Hospitality follows the country pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every legal value of restaurants and hotels (allergens, labels, licences, hygiene, tourist tax, vouchers, tips, staff
meals) comes from the company's country pack; in France:
[CR-FR-107 Allergen information in restaurants](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-107-allergen-information.md),
[CR-FR-108 The home-made label (fait maison)](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-108-home-made-label.md),
[CR-FR-109 Alcohol licences](../../../10-country-packs/france/hospitality/cr-fr-109-alcohol-licences.md),
[CR-FR-110 Food hygiene and its records](../../../10-country-packs/france/hospitality/cr-fr-110-food-hygiene-records.md),
[CR-FR-111 Tourist tax (taxe de séjour)](../../../10-country-packs/france/tax-and-vat/cr-fr-111-tourist-tax.md).

## Why

See [D332](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d332-hospitality-sector-coverage.md).
