# 📏 BR-SC06-03 Home-made only when it is

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The home-made mention is offered only on dishes whose recipe qualifies; in France:
[CR-FR-108 The home-made label (fait maison)](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-108-home-made-label.md).

## Why

See [D332](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d332-hospitality-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Dish from a bought sauce | Mention not offered |

## Applies to

Every SC06 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
