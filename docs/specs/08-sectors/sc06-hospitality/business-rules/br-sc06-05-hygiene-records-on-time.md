# 📏 BR-SC06-05 Hygiene records on time

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Temperature, cleaning and delivery records are taken on schedule; a missed or out-of-range reading alerts the manager;
in France:
[CR-FR-110 Food hygiene and its records](../../../10-country-packs/france/hospitality/cr-fr-110-food-hygiene-records.md).

## Why

See [D332](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d332-hospitality-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Cold room at 6 °C | Alert, corrective action asked |

## Applies to

Every SC06 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Schedules and ranges.

## Open questions

None.
