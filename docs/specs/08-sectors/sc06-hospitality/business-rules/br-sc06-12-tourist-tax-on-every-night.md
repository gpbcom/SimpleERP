# 📏 BR-SC06-12 Tourist tax on every night

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each taxable night carries the commune's tourist tax, shown apart, and the hotel declares and pays it; in France:
[CR-FR-111 Tourist tax (taxe de séjour)](../../../10-country-packs/france/tax-and-vat/cr-fr-111-tourist-tax.md).

## Why

See [D332](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d332-hospitality-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 2 adults, 3 nights | 6 tax units shown apart |

## Applies to

Every SC06 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Commune rates.

## Open questions

None.
