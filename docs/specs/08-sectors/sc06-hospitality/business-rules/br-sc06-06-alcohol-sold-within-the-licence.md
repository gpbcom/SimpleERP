# 📏 BR-SC06-06 Alcohol sold within the licence

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The till sells alcohol only within the venue's licence (with meals or not) and never to minors; in France:
[CR-FR-109 Alcohol licences](../../../10-country-packs/france/hospitality/cr-fr-109-alcohol-licences.md).

## Why

See [D332](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d332-hospitality-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Spirits without a meal, restaurant licence | Refused at the till |

## Applies to

Every SC06 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The venue's licence.

## Open questions

None.
