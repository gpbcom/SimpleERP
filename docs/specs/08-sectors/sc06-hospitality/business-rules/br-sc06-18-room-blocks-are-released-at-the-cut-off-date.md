# 📏 BR-SC06-18 Room blocks are released at the cut-off date

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

At the cut-off date of a group booking, every room held and not linked to a named guest is released to general sale,
except the share the contract guarantees. Rooms are never held past the option date without a confirmation.

## Why

Unsold rooms kept for a group that did not name them are lost revenue on nights that could be sold elsewhere. The owner
asked for groups and seminars ([D550](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d550-benchmark-gaps-sc06-hospitality.md)).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 30 rooms held and 24 named at the cut-off date | 6 rooms released |
| Option date passed without confirmation | all rooms released |
| Contract guarantees 90 percent | rooms below that share stay billed |
| Organiser asks for more rooms before the cut-off | added if free |
| Rooming list sent after the cut-off | named rooms are re-sold only if still free |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC06-18](../use-cases/06-campsites-catering-groups-and-pricing/uc-sc06-18-book-groups-and-seminars.md)
- [E-SC06-15 Group booking](../data/e-sc06-15-group-booking.md)
- [E-SC06-08 Room reservation](../data/e-sc06-08-room-reservation.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The release time of day and the default attrition share, by the receptionist.

## Open questions

None.
