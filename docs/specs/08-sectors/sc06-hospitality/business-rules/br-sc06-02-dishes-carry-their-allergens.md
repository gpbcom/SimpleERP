# 📏 BR-SC06-02 Dishes carry their allergens

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each dish's allergens come from its recipe's ingredients and are shown on menus, QR menus and kiosks; in France:
[CR-FR-107 Allergen information in restaurants](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-107-allergen-information.md).

## Why

See [D332](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d332-hospitality-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Pesto with pine nuts and cheese | Tree nuts, milk shown |

## Applies to

Every SC06 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
