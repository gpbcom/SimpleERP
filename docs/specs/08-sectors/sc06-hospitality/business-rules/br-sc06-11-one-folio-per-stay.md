# 📏 BR-SC06-11 One folio per stay

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Room, restaurant, bar and extras of a stay go on one folio, settled at check-out; nothing is left unbilled.

## Why

See [D332](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d332-hospitality-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Bar bill charged to room 214 | On the guest's folio |

## Applies to

Every SC06 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
