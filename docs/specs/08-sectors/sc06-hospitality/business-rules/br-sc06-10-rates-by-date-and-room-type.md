# 📏 BR-SC06-10 Rates by date and room type

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Rates are set per room type and date, with rate plans (flexible, non-refundable, breakfast).

## Why

See [D332](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d332-hospitality-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Saturday double, flexible | 145 EUR |

## Applies to

Every SC06 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Rate plans.

## Open questions

None.
