# 📏 BR-SC06-13 Rooms ready before arrival

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Housekeeping status drives check-in: a room is assigned only when clean and inspected.

## Why

See [D332](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d332-hospitality-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Room dirty | Not assignable |

## Applies to

Every SC06 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Inspection step on or off.

## Open questions

None.
