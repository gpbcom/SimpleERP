# 📏 BR-SC06-04 Recipes costed from purchases

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A recipe's cost comes from its ingredients' latest purchase prices and yields; the menu shows the food-cost ratio.

## Why

See [D332](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d332-hospitality-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Beef price up 8 % | Dish cost and ratio updated |

## Applies to

Every SC06 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Target ratio.

## Open questions

None.
