# 📏 BR-SC03-02 Work only under a signed statement of work

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Time is recorded on an engagement only once its statement of work is signed, or on an approved pre-sales budget.

## Why

See [D323](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d323-services-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Time on an unsigned engagement | Refused, pre-sales code offered |

## Applies to

Every SC03 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Pre-sales allowed or not.

## Open questions

None.
