# 📏 BR-SC03-09 Freelancers checked

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A freelancer or subcontractor works only with valid registration and vigilance documents, renewed every 6 months above
the threshold; in France:
[CR-FR-085 Vigilance over subcontractors](../../../10-country-packs/france/construction/cr-fr-085-subcontractor-vigilance.md),
[CR-FR-101 Undeclared work](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-101-undeclared-work.md).

## Why

See [D323](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d323-services-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Certificate expired | Freelancer flagged, time blocked |

## Applies to

Every SC03 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
