# 📏 BR-SC03-07 Revenue follows delivery

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Revenue of fixed-price work is recognised with progress; work done but not billed is work in progress, billed but not
done is deferred.

## Why

See [D323](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d323-services-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 60 % done, 40 % billed | 20 % work in progress |

## Applies to

Every SC03 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Progress by cost or by milestones.

## Open questions

None.
