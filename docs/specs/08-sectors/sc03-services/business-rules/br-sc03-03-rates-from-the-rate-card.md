# 📏 BR-SC03-03 Rates from the rate card

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Billable time is priced from the engagement's rate card by role, with the client's agreed rates winning over the
standard ones.

## Why

See [D323](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d323-services-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Senior day, client rate 950 | 950, not the standard 1 100 |

## Applies to

Every SC03 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Rate cards per client.

## Open questions

None.
