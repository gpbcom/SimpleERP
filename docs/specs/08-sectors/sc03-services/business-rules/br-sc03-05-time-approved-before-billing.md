# 📏 BR-SC03-05 Time approved before billing

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Only approved time and expenses are billed or recognised; rejected entries go back with a reason.

## Why

See [D323](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d323-services-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 3 days approved, 1 rejected | 3 billed |

## Applies to

Every SC03 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Approvers per engagement.

## Open questions

None.
