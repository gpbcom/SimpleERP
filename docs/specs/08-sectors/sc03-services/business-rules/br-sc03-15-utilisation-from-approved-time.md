# 📏 BR-SC03-15 Utilisation from approved time

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Utilisation is billable approved hours over available hours, per consultant, team and month.

## Why

See [D323](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d323-services-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 120 h billable of 150 h | 80 % |

## Applies to

Every SC03 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Target per role.

## Open questions

None.
