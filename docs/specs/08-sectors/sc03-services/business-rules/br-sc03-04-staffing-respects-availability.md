# 📏 BR-SC03-04 Staffing respects availability

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A consultant is staffed within their availability (leave, other engagements, working-time limits); over-allocation is
shown and needs approval; in France:
[CR-FR-042 Working time and rest](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-042-working-time-and-rest.md),
[CR-FR-099 Day-based working time (forfait jours)](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-099-day-based-working-time.md).

## Why

See [D323](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d323-services-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 120 % next week | Shown, manager's approval asked |

## Applies to

Every SC03 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The approval threshold.

## Open questions

None.
