# 📏 BR-SC03-01 Services follow the country pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every legal value of services (staff lending, freelancers, working time, VAT abroad, payment terms) comes from the
company's country pack; in France:
[CR-FR-098 Lending staff to another company](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-098-lawful-staff-lending.md),
[CR-FR-101 Undeclared work](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-101-undeclared-work.md),
[CR-FR-085 Vigilance over subcontractors](../../../10-country-packs/france/construction/cr-fr-085-subcontractor-vigilance.md),
[CR-FR-099 Day-based working time (forfait jours)](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-099-day-based-working-time.md),
[CR-FR-100 VAT on services to clients abroad](../../../10-country-packs/france/tax-and-vat/cr-fr-100-vat-on-services-abroad.md).

## Why

See [D323](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d323-services-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| A French firm bills a German client | Reverse charge applied |

Every SC03 use case concerned.

None.
