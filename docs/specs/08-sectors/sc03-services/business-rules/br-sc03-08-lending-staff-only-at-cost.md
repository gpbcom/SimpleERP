# 📏 BR-SC03-08 Lending staff only at cost

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A consultant lent to a client's team, not delivering the firm's own service, is lent at cost under an agreement and the
consultant's written consent; in France:
[CR-FR-098 Lending staff to another company](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-098-lawful-staff-lending.md).

## Why

See [D323](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d323-services-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Assignment under the client's orders | Flagged: an agreement and consent needed, at cost |

## Applies to

Every SC03 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
