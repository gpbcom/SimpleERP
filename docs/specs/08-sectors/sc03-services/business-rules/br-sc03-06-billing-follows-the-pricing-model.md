# 📏 BR-SC03-06 Billing follows the pricing model

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Time and materials bill approved time and expenses; fixed price bills milestones once accepted; retainers bill the
period and track the balance.

## Why

See [D323](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d323-services-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Milestone 2 accepted | 30 % of the price billed |

## Applies to

Every SC03 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Model per engagement.

## Open questions

None.
