# 📏 BR-SC03-11 Budget alerts

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An engagement alerts its manager when consumed budget or estimate at completion crosses the set thresholds.

## Why

See [D323](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d323-services-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 80 % consumed, 60 % done | Alert |

## Applies to

Every SC03 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Thresholds.

## Open questions

None.
