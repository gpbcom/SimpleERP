# 📏 BR-SC03-12 Clients see only their engagements

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

In the portal a client sees only their engagements, the reports and documents shared with them, and approves
deliverables and timesheets when asked.

## Why

See [D323](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d323-services-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Client opens the portal | Two engagements, nothing else |

## Applies to

Every SC03 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

What is shared.

## Open questions

None.
