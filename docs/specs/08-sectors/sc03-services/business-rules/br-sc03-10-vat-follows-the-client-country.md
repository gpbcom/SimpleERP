# 📏 BR-SC03-10 VAT follows the client's country

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Invoices to business clients abroad apply the place-of-supply rules and their mentions; in France:
[CR-FR-100 VAT on services to clients abroad](../../../10-country-packs/france/tax-and-vat/cr-fr-100-vat-on-services-abroad.md).

## Why

See [D323](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d323-services-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Client in Germany, VAT number valid | No French VAT, "autoliquidation" |

## Applies to

Every SC03 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
