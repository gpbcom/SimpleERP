# 📏 BR-SC03-16 Assignment contracts are signed in two working days

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

The worker's assignment contract is signed within two working days of the start of the assignment. A contract still
unsigned after that deadline is marked Overdue and alerts the engagement manager; the worker is not paid as placed and
the client is not invoiced for them until it is signed.

## Why

A temporary worker without a signed contract in time exposes the agency and the client to the requalification of the
assignment into an open-ended contract. The owner asked for staffing agencies
([D547](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d547-benchmark-gaps-sc03-services.md)). (Proposed) The France-pack rule is written
later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Start Monday | sign by Wednesday |
| Start Thursday | sign by Monday |
| Signed on the second working day | Signed |
| Not signed after the second working day | Overdue, alert |
| Start on a public holiday | the holiday is not a working day |
