# 📏 BR-SC03-17 Media are rebilled at cost plus the agreed fee

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

Media bought for a client is rebilled at its real cost plus the fee agreed in the statement of work, the cost and the
fee shown on separate lines and backed by the supplier's invoice. Nothing is produced or bought before the client's
approval is recorded.

## Why

A client who sees only a lump sum cannot check the agency, and a purchase before approval is a cost the client can
refuse. The owner asked for marketing agencies
([D547](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d547-benchmark-gaps-sc03-services.md)). The mandate duty is written
when its law is read.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Cost 12,400.00 EUR, fee 10 % | invoice 12,400.00 + 1,240.00 = 13,640.00 EUR |
| Supplier credit of 400.00 EUR | passed on in the next invoice |
| Package split 60 % and 40 % | each client is rebilled their share |
| Purchase before approval | blocked |

## Configurable
