# 📏 BR-SC03-14 Payment terms within the law

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Invoices state payment terms within the legal cap and late penalties; in France:
[CR-FR-018 Payment terms cap](../../../10-country-packs/france/invoicing/cr-fr-018-payment-terms-cap.md),
[CR-FR-019 Late-payment penalties](../../../10-country-packs/france/invoicing/cr-fr-019-late-payment-penalties.md).

## Why

See [D323](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d323-services-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 90 days asked | Refused, 60 days cap |

## Applies to

Every SC03 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

None.
