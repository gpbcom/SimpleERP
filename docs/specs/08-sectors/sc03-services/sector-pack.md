# 🎁 Sector pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Record types

Engagement, statement of work, rate card, staffing assignment, billing proposal, milestone, revenue position, freelance
contract, shared item, satisfaction survey.

## Fields

Engagement on every timesheet line, expense and invoice; role and rate on staffing; level and expiry on skills; VAT
number and country on clients.

## Statuses and workflows

Engagement: pre-sales → running → closed. Timesheet: draft → submitted → approved or rejected. Milestone: planned →
accepted → billed.

## Roles

Engagement manager (U24); consultants as office workers (U16) with time and expenses; the existing sales and accounting
roles.

## Documents

Statement of work, billing proposal, time report for the client, status report, lending agreement.

## Dashboards

Utilisation by consultant and team, engagements by margin and budget, work in progress, time missing, satisfaction.

## Settings

Rate cards, budget alert thresholds, utilisation targets, approval rules, pre-sales codes.

## Open questions

None.
