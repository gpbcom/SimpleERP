# 💼 SC03 Services

**Status:** ✅ specified

Interviewed on 2026-11-11 after F12 (D115): D323 to D325.

## Summary

What a consulting firm or an agency adds to F6, F8 and F9: engagements and statements of work, rate cards, staffing and
utilisation, time and expenses, billing by time, fixed price, milestones and retainers, work in progress and revenue,
freelancers, budgets and margins, the client portal, satisfaction and skills
([D323](../../06-decisions/sectors/d323-services-sector-coverage.md)).

## Profile

Consulting firms, digital and creative agencies and engineering offices, 2 to 500 people, billing by the day, by project
or by retainer, with freelancers and clients abroad.
