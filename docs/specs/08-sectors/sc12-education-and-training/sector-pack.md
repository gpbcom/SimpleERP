# 🎁 Sector pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Record types

Course, session, enrolment, training agreement, funding, attendance sheet, e-learning record, certificate, survey
answer, training invoice, trainer profile, quality evidence, yearly report, student, class, grade, pupil absence, school
fee.

## Fields

Declaration number on the company; funder and share on enrolments; half-day on attendance; class and parents on
students.

## Statuses and workflows

Enrolment: enrolled → attended → completed or cancelled. Funding: requested → granted or refused.

## Roles

Training coordinator (U38), trainer (U39), learners, students and parents (U09), director (U13).

## Documents

Quotes, agreements, invitations, attendance sheets, certificates, report cards, invoices.

## Dashboards

Fill rate, funding pending, satisfaction, complaints, attendance, the yearly report figures.

## Settings

Declaration number, completion thresholds, funders, survey templates, school year and terms.

## Open questions

None.
