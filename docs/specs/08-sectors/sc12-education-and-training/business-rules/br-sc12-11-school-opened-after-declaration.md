# 📏 BR-SC12-11 School opened after declaration

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A private school's first year starts only after the declaration period; in France:
[CR-FR-160 Opening a private school](../../../10-country-packs/france/education/cr-fr-160-private-school-declaration.md).

## Why

See [D350](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d350-education-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Declared 1 May | Opening from 1 August |

## Applies to

Every SC12 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
