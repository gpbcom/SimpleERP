# 📏 BR-SC12-19 Bookings close at the cut-off time

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

A meal or activity booking can be created, changed or cancelled freely only until the cut-off time the school sets for
its day. After the cut-off the booking is Locked, and only the coordinator may change it, with a reason that is
recorded.

## Why

The kitchen buys and cooks from the count at the cut-off; later changes cause waste and wrong bills. The owner asked for
canteen and activities ([D556](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d556-benchmark-gaps-sc12-education-and-training.md)).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Booking at 8:30, cut-off 9:00 | accepted |
| Booking at 9:05 | refused, cut-off shown |
| Cancellation after the cut-off | refused, the coordinator may allow it with a reason |
| Absence with a certificate after the cut-off | cancelled by the coordinator, no charge |
| Standing booking on Mondays | meals created, each cancellable before its cut-off |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC12-24](../use-cases/06-apprentices-driving-nurseries-and-canteens/uc-sc12-24-run-the-canteen-and-activities.md)
- [E-SC12-23 Meal booking](../data/e-sc12-23-meal-booking.md)
- [E-SC12-18 School fee](../data/e-sc12-18-school-fee.md)

## Exceptions

The coordinator may change a locked booking with a recorded reason.

## Configurable

The cut-off time and day, the prices and the standing bookings, by the coordinator.

## Open questions

None.
