# 📏 BR-SC12-15 E-learning tracked

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

E-learning progress and time from the learning platform (connector) count as attendance evidence when the programme says
so.

## Why

See [D350](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d350-education-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Module completed | Hours recorded |

## Applies to

Every SC12 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Which modules count.

## Open questions

None.
