# 📏 BR-SC12-13 Grades locked after the council

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Grades are locked once the class council has approved the report cards.

## Why

See [D350](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d350-education-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Council held | Grades read-only |

## Applies to

Every SC12 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.
