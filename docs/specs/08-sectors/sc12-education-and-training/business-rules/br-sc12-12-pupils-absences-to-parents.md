# 📏 BR-SC12-12 Pupils' absences to parents

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A pupil's absence is told to the parents the same day.

## Why

See [D350](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d350-education-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Absent 08:00 | Parents notified at 08:30 |

## Applies to

Every SC12 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Delay.

## Open questions

None.
