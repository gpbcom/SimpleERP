# 📏 BR-SC12-05 Funding split by funder

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A session's price is split between the learner, the employer and funders by the shared multi-payer rule,
[BR-F6-13 Each payer pays its share up to its cap](../../../02-functional/f06-customers-and-sales/business-rules/br-f06-13-each-payer-pays-its-share-up-to-its-cap.md);
training adds that the personal training account's contribution is applied as one of the payers; in France:
[CR-FR-159 Personal training account (CPF)](../../../10-country-packs/france/education/cr-fr-159-personal-training-account.md).

## Why

See [D350](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d350-education-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Training account | Learner pays the contribution |

## Applies to

Every SC12 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Funders.

None.
