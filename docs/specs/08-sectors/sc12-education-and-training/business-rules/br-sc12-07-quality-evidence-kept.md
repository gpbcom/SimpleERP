# 📏 BR-SC12-07 Quality evidence kept

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

(Proposed) The evidence each quality criterion needs (needs assessments, satisfaction, complaints, trainers' skills) is
kept per session; in France:
[CR-FR-157 Qualiopi quality certification](../../../10-country-packs/france/education/cr-fr-157-qualiopi.md). A trainer
is offered for a session only with the skills the session's request type requires, from their trainer profile
([E-SC12-11](../data/e-sc12-11-trainer-profile.md)), checked by
[BR-F9-25](../../../02-functional/f09-hr-and-projects/business-rules/br-f09-25-required-attributes-exclude.md); the
assignment proposal is kept as the evidence of the trainer's skills.

## Why

(Proposed) The criteria are still to read.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Audit | Evidence file per criterion |

## Applies to

Every SC12 use case concerned, and
[E-F2-25 Request type](../../../02-functional/f02-no-code-configuration/data/e-f02-25-request-type.md).

## Exceptions

None.

No.
