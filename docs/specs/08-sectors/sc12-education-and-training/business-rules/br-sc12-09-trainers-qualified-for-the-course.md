# 📏 BR-SC12-09 Trainers qualified for the course

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A trainer is planned only on courses matching their skills and within their availability.

## Why

See [D350](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d350-education-sector-coverage.md).

| Not qualified | Not proposed |
