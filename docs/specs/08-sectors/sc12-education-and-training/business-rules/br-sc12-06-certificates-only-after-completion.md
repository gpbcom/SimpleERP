# 📏 BR-SC12-06 Certificates only after completion

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A certificate is issued only after the attendance and assessment the programme requires.

## Why

See [D350](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d350-education-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 80 % attendance, 90 % required | No certificate |

## Applies to

Every SC12 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Thresholds per programme.

## Open questions

None.
