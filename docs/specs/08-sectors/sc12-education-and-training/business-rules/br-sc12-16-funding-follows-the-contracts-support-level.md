# 📏 BR-SC12-16 Funding follows the contract's support level

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

The funding claimed from the OPCO for an apprenticeship contract follows the support level set for its diploma, one
month at a time, and only for the months the apprentice follows. A contract with no support level or a month after a
break is not claimed.

## Why

The OPCO pays what the level and the months followed allow, and a claim above it is reclaimed. The owner asked for
apprentice training centres ([D556](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d556-benchmark-gaps-sc12-education-and-training.md)).
The levels come from the France pack and the branches, written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Level 7,500.00 a year, one month | 625.00 EUR |
| Break on 2026-12-15 | no claim after that date |
| Diploma with no level | no claim, the level is requested |
| OPCO corrects the level | the claims are recomputed |
| Apprentice changes employer | a new contract for the rest |
