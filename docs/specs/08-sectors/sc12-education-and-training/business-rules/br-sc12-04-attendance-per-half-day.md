# 📏 BR-SC12-04 Attendance per half-day

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each learner signs attendance per half-day, on paper or digitally, and the trainer confirms; the sheet proves the
training to funders.

## Why

See [D350](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d350-education-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Absent in the afternoon | Half-day not billed to the funder |

## Applies to

Every SC12 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Digital or paper.

## Open questions

None.
