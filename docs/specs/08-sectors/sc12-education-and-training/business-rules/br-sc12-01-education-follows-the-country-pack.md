# 📏 BR-SC12-01 Education follows the country pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every legal value of training and schools (declaration, yearly report, quality certification, agreements, training
account, school opening) comes from the country pack; in France:
[CR-FR-155 Training provider's declaration](../../../10-country-packs/france/education/cr-fr-155-training-provider-declaration.md),
[CR-FR-156 Yearly training provider report (BPF)](../../../10-country-packs/france/education/cr-fr-156-training-report-bpf.md),
[CR-FR-157 Qualiopi quality certification](../../../10-country-packs/france/education/cr-fr-157-qualiopi.md),
[CR-FR-158 Training agreement or contract](../../../10-country-packs/france/education/cr-fr-158-training-agreement.md),
[CR-FR-159 Personal training account (CPF)](../../../10-country-packs/france/education/cr-fr-159-personal-training-account.md),
[CR-FR-160 Opening a private school](../../../10-country-packs/france/education/cr-fr-160-private-school-declaration.md).

## Why

See [D350](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d350-education-sector-coverage.md).
