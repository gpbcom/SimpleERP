# 📏 BR-SC12-14 Learners' data kept no longer than needed

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Learners' and pupils' data are kept for the legal periods, then deleted; in France:
[CR-FR-052 Personal data kept no longer than needed](../../../10-country-packs/france/data-protection/cr-fr-052-storage-limitation.md).

## Why

See [D350](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d350-education-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Learner from 2019 | Deleted, certificates kept |

## Applies to

Every SC12 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
