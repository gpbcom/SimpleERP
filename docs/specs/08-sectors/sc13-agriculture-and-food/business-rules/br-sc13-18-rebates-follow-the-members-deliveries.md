# 📏 BR-SC13-18 Rebates follow the members' deliveries

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

The rebate of a member is the surplus decided by the general meeting multiplied by the member's share of the value
delivered in the year. A member who delivered nothing gets no rebate, and the rebates never add up to more than the
surplus decided.

## Why

A rebate that follows anything but the business done with the cooperative breaks its statutes and its tax treatment. The
owner asked for cooperatives ([D557](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d557-benchmark-gaps-sc13-agriculture-and-food.md)).
(⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) The France-pack rule is still to write: to read the Code rural et de la pêche maritime on
agricultural cooperatives (L521-1 and following) and loi n° 47-1775 art. 15 on rebates in proportion to the business
done.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Two members with 60 and 40 percent of the value, surplus 10,000.00 EUR | 6,000.00 EUR and 4,000.00 EUR |
| Member with no delivery | no rebate |
| Rebates above the surplus | refused |
| Delivery without a grade | cannot be settled and counts for nothing in the year |
| Part of the rebate paid in shares | the capital of the member grows by that part |
