# 📏 BR-SC13-10 Shelf life and rotation

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Stock is used first expired, first out; a lot past its date is blocked; unsold food goes to donation where the law asks;
in France:
[CR-FR-095 Donation of unsold food](../../../10-country-packs/france/environment/cr-fr-095-unsold-food-donation.md).
