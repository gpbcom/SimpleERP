# 📏 BR-SC13-02 Every treatment recorded

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

(Proposed) Each plant-protection treatment is recorded with product, dose, plot, date and operator; in France:
[CR-FR-161 Register of plant-protection treatments](../../../10-country-packs/france/agriculture/cr-fr-161-plant-protection-register.md).

## Why

(Proposed) The register's legal content is still to read.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Treatment without operator | Line incomplete, flagged |

## Applies to

Every SC13 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
