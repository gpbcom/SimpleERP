# 📏 BR-SC13-20 Fellings follow the management plan

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

A timber lot is marked only on a parcel with a management plan, in a year the plan allows, and so that the volume felled
in the year does not exceed the volume of the plan. A sale is invoiced on the measured volume, and every load carries
its parcel.

## Why

Felling outside the plan harms the forest and exposes the owner to penalties, and untraced timber cannot be sold. The
owner asked for forestry ([D557](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d557-benchmark-gaps-sc13-agriculture-and-food.md)).
(⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) The France-pack rule is still to write: to read the Code forestier on management plans
(L312-1 and following) and the felling and timber traceability rules.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Plan allows 300 m3, 100 m3 felled, lot of 200 m3 | accepted |
| Same plan, lot of 220 m3 | refused, 200 m3 left |
| Parcel without a plan | no lot |
| Standing sale of 200 m3 measured at 188 m3 | invoiced on 188 m3 |
| Load without a parcel | refused |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC13-25](../use-cases/06-vineyards-contractors-and-cooperatives/uc-sc13-25-manage-forests-and-timber-sales.md)
- [E-SC13-21 Timber lot](../data/e-sc13-21-timber-lot.md)

## Exceptions

A felling made necessary by a storm or a disease may exceed the year's volume with the owner's written note, kept with
the lot.

## Configurable

The plan's years, areas and volumes per parcel, by the manager.

## Open questions

None.
