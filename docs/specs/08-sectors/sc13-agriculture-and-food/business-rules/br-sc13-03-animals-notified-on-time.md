# 📏 BR-SC13-03 Animals notified on time

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Births, movements and deaths are notified within the set delays; in France:
[CR-FR-162 Animal identification and herd register](../../../10-country-packs/france/agriculture/cr-fr-162-animal-identification.md).

## Why

Cattle are tagged within 20 days of birth, the birth is notified within 7 days of tagging, entries and exits within 7
days, and the herd register is kept at least 3 years; sheep and goats are identified within 6 months of birth and
before leaving the holding, movements are notified within 7 days (48 hours from 2026-04-28 to 2026-05-28), and the
register is kept at least 5 years (CR-FR-162). Pigs are identified before leaving the site (an individual
number for breeding pigs, imported pigs within 30 days) and their movements notified within 7 days, the movement part of
the register kept at least 5 years; a horse's registration card is asked within 8 months of birth or 30 days of
introduction or import; poultry holders declare themselves (CR-FR-162).
