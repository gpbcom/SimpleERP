# 📏 BR-SC13-07 Labels from the recipe

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A prepacked product's label comes from its recipe: ingredients, allergens, nutrition, quantity, date, origin;
in France:
[CR-FR-165 Labelling of prepacked food](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-165-prepacked-food-labelling.md),
[CR-FR-107 Allergen information in restaurants](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-107-allergen-information.md).

## Why

The label carries the name, the ingredients in descending order of weight with the allergens highlighted, the net
quantity, the use-by or best-before date, the operator, the nutrition declaration, the lot and the origin when required,
in characters of at least 1.2 mm x-height (0.9 mm on a largest surface under 80 cm2) (CR-FR-165, Regulation (EU)
1169/2011).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Recipe with walnuts | Nuts highlighted on the label |

## Applies to

Every SC13 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Label templates.

## Open questions

None.
