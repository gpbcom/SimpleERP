# 📏 BR-SC13-01 Agriculture follows the country pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every legal value of farms and food processing (registers, identification, aid, labelling, organic, seasonal work,
traceability, hygiene) comes from the country pack; in France:
[CR-FR-161 Register of plant-protection treatments](../../../10-country-packs/france/agriculture/cr-fr-161-plant-protection-register.md),
[CR-FR-162 Animal identification and herd register](../../../10-country-packs/france/agriculture/cr-fr-162-animal-identification.md),
[CR-FR-163 Livestock health register and withdrawal periods](../../../10-country-packs/france/agriculture/cr-fr-163-livestock-health-register.md),
[CR-FR-164 Common agricultural policy (CAP) aid declaration](../../../10-country-packs/france/agriculture/cr-fr-164-cap-aid-declaration.md),
[CR-FR-165 Labelling of prepacked food](../../../10-country-packs/france/consumer/cr-fr-165-prepacked-food-labelling.md),
[CR-FR-166 Organic certification](../../../10-country-packs/france/agriculture/cr-fr-166-organic-certification.md),
[CR-FR-167 Seasonal agricultural workers' contribution exemption](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-167-seasonal-workers-exemption.md).

## Why

See [D353](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d353-agriculture-sector-coverage.md).
