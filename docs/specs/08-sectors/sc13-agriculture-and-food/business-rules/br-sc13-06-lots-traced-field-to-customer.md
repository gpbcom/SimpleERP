# 📏 BR-SC13-06 Lots traced from field to customer

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every harvest and batch lot links its plots or animals, inputs, processing and customers; in France:
[CR-FR-061 Food traceability](../../../10-country-packs/france/products/cr-fr-061-food-traceability.md).

## Why

See [D353](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d353-agriculture-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Recall of flour lot | Plots and customers found |

## Applies to

Every SC13 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
