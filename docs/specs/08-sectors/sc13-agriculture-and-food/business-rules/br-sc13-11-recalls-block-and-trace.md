# 📏 BR-SC13-11 Recalls block and trace

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

[CR-FR-062 Product recall](../../../10-country-packs/france/products/cr-fr-062-product-recall.md).

See [D353](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d353-agriculture-sector-coverage.md).
