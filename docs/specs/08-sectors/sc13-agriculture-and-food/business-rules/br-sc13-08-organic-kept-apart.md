# 📏 BR-SC13-08 Organic kept apart

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Organic plots, lots and products are kept apart and documented for the yearly control; in France:
[CR-FR-166 Organic certification](../../../10-country-packs/france/agriculture/cr-fr-166-organic-certification.md).

## Why

See [D353](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d353-agriculture-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Conventional input on an organic plot | Refused |

## Applies to

Every SC13 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
