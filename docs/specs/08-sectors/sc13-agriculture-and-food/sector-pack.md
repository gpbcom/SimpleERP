# 🎁 Sector pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Record types

Plot, crop plan, field operation, lot, animal, veterinary treatment, machine, seasonal contract, direct sale,
cooperative statement, organic record, batch, hygiene reading, product label, recall.

## Fields

Organic status on plots and lots; withdrawal end on treatments; origin on lots; allergens on ingredients.

## Statuses and workflows

Lot: in stock → blocked → sold or recalled. Plot: conventional → converting → organic.

## Roles

Farmer (U40), seasonal workers (U14), processors through F12 and SC05 roles.

## Documents

Registers, aid declaration draft, labels, recall notices, cooperative statements.

## Dashboards

Treatments, animals to notify, costs per crop and herd, lots blocked, hygiene readings missed.

## Settings

Plots, products and doses, withdrawal periods, label templates, organic status.
