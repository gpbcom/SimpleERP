# 📏 BR-SC07-02 Vehicles within the licence

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A vehicle is used for hire or reward only when covered by a valid licence copy of the right kind; in France:
[CR-FR-115 Haulage registration and licences](../../../10-country-packs/france/transport/cr-fr-115-haulage-registration.md).

## Why

See [D335](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d335-transport-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 19 t truck, licence copy expired | Not assignable |

## Applies to

Every SC07 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
