# 📏 BR-SC07-13 Storage billed by the contract

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Warehouse clients are billed storage by pallet and day and handling per movement, from the warehouse's own records.

## Why

See [D335](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d335-transport-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 40 pallets for 30 days | 1 200 pallet-days billed |

## Applies to

Every SC07 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Rates per client.

## Open questions

None.
