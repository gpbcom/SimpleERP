# 📏 BR-SC07-10 Vehicles inspected on time

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A vehicle past its technical inspection or planned maintenance is flagged and cannot start a trip.

## Why

See [D335](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d335-transport-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Inspection overdue | Vehicle blocked |

## Applies to

Every SC07 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Maintenance plans.

## Open questions

None.
