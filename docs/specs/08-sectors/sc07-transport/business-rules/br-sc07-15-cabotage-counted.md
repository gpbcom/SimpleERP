# 📏 BR-SC07-15 Cabotage counted

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Cabotage operations in another country are counted against its limits; in France:
[CR-FR-122 Cabotage limits](../../../10-country-packs/france/transport/cr-fr-122-cabotage-limits.md).

## Why

See [D335](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d335-transport-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 4th operation in 7 days | Refused |

## Applies to

Every SC07 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
