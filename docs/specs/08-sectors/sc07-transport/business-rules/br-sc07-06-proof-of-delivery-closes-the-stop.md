# 📏 BR-SC07-06 Proof of delivery closes the stop

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A stop closes with a signature, a photo or a code, and any reservation the consignee writes.

## Why

See [D335](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d335-transport-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Signed with reservation "box damaged" | Stop closed, incident opened |

## Applies to

Every SC07 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Proof types per client.

## Open questions

None.
