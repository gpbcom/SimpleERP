# 📏 BR-SC07-04 Documents on board complete

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A trip starts only when its documents are on board, on paper or electronic; in France:
[CR-FR-118 Documents on board and the consignment note](../../../10-country-packs/france/transport/cr-fr-118-documents-on-board.md).

## Why

See [D335](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d335-transport-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| No consignment note | Trip cannot start |

## Applies to

Every SC07 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
