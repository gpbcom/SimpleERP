# 📏 BR-SC07-11 Chartered carriers checked

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A subcontracted carrier is used only with its licence and vigilance documents; in France:
[CR-FR-085 Vigilance over subcontractors](../../../10-country-packs/france/construction/cr-fr-085-subcontractor-vigilance.md).

## Why

See [D335](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d335-transport-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Certificate over 6 months | Charter blocked |

## Applies to

Every SC07 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
