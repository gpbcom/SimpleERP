# 📏 BR-SC07-01 Transport follows the country pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every legal value of transport (licences, tachograph, driving times, documents, training, CO2, dangerous goods,
cabotage, fuel indexation) comes from the company's country pack; in France:
[CR-FR-115 Haulage registration and licences](../../../10-country-packs/france/transport/cr-fr-115-haulage-registration.md),
[CR-FR-116 Smart tachograph](../../../10-country-packs/france/transport/cr-fr-116-smart-tachograph.md),
[CR-FR-117 Driving and rest times](../../../10-country-packs/france/transport/cr-fr-117-driving-and-rest-times.md),
[CR-FR-118 Documents on board and the consignment note](../../../10-country-packs/france/transport/cr-fr-118-documents-on-board.md),
[CR-FR-119 Drivers' initial and continuous training](../../../10-country-packs/france/transport/cr-fr-119-driver-training.md).

## Why

See [D335](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d335-transport-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| A French haulier plans a trip | Licence and driver checks applied |

Every SC07 use case concerned.
