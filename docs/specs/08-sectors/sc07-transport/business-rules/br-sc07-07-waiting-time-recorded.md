# 📏 BR-SC07-07 Waiting time recorded

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Waiting at loading and unloading beyond the free time is recorded from the driver's check-in and check-out and billed
per the contract.

## Why

See [D335](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d335-transport-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 2 h at the dock, 1 h free | 1 h billed |

## Applies to

Every SC07 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Free time and rate per client.

## Open questions

None.
