# 🎁 Sector pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Record types

Transport order, trip, vehicle, driver document, consignment note, stop, transport incident, tachograph file, dangerous
goods declaration, fuel and toll line, vehicle repair, charter, transport invoice line, client stock, cabotage
operation.

## Fields

Lane, weight and pallets on orders; licence copy and inspection on vehicles; documents on drivers; check-in, check-out
and proof on stops; CO2 on invoice lines.

## Statuses and workflows

Order: quoted → ordered → planned → delivered → billed. Stop: planned → arrived → done. Vehicle: available → on trip →
in repair.

## Roles

Dispatcher (U29), driver (U30) seeing only their own trips; the warehouse and accounting roles.

## Documents

Consignment notes, proofs of delivery, transport invoices with surcharges and CO2, storage invoices.

## Dashboards

Trips on the road, late deliveries, empty kilometres, cost per kilometre, documents expiring, infringements.

## Settings

Lanes and rates, fuel index, waiting-time rules, storage rates, maintenance plans, document reminders.

## Open questions

None.
