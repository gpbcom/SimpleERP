# 🎁 Sector pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Record types

Maintenance contract, planned visit, check-up certificate, intervention, site report, household contract, installation,
grant file, label, regulated record, on-call rota, warranty certificate, van stock.

## Fields

The trades a company practises; the legal check-up kind and its due date on contracts; the label and its scope on
installers; the refrigerant certificate on technicians; the call-out terms; the ten-year insurance certificate on works;
the withdrawal notice on quotes.

## Statuses and workflows

Maintenance contract: draft → active → renewal due → ended. Intervention: requested → dispatched → on site → reported →
invoiced, or cancelled. Household contract: draft → active → suspended → ended. Installation: planned → in progress →
completed → commissioned.

## Roles

Owner (U13), dispatcher (U29), field worker as technician (U14), site manager (U22), accounting (U05), purchasing and
stock (U04), customers (U09).

## Documents

Maintenance contracts, check-up certificates, intervention reports, quotes with the withdrawal notice, tax-credit
certificates, grant files, refrigerant records, warranty certificates.

## Dashboards

Visits due by legal date, interventions by state, on-call callouts, certificates and labels expiring, van stock
differences, tax-credit advances waiting.

## Settings

The trades switched on, check-up kinds and intervals, call-out and travel terms, the label scopes, the van reload rules,
the on-call surcharge table, the withdrawal notice text.

## Open questions

None.
