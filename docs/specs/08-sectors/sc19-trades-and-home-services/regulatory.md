## Topics still to read

- Home-services tax credit eligibility: read in CR-FR-103 and CR-FR-102 (50 % of what was paid, yearly ceilings,
  the certificate before 31 March, the declaration or approval).
- Immediate advance and the customer's consent: read in CR-FR-104 (48 hours to check, then validated automatically;
  URSSAF pays the whole service to the provider and debits the household for the remainder).
- RGE label and renovation grants: read in CR-FR-226; that the request comes before the works is still to read.
- Ten-year insurance certificate before works: read in CR-FR-259.
- Refrigerant handling certificates: a duty stated in plain words in the use case that applies it, to be read
  in the official source and written in the France pack.
- Grid connection of solar installations: a duty stated in plain words in the use case that applies it, to be
  read in the official source and written in the France pack.
- Call-out surcharge display: a duty stated in plain words in the use case that applies it, to be read in the
  official source and written in the France pack.
- Withdrawal notice for quotes signed at the customer's home: the 14 days are read in CR-FR-036; no payment for 7
  days from the signature (Code de la consommation L221-10) and no withdrawal right for an urgent repair the customer
  expressly asked for (L221-28), applied in BR-SC19-20 as the owner chose
  ([D575](../../06-decisions/foundations/d575-answers-of-2026-11-27-on-the-last-open-points.md)).
- Legal yearly boiler check-ups: a duty stated in plain words in the use case that applies it, to be read in
  the official source and written in the France pack.
