# 🎁 Sector pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Record types

Production setup, batch recipe, configuration rule, engineered design, customer-supplied part, label template,
controlled document, audit, quality action, supplier rating, 8D report, instrument, operation qualification, management
review.

## Fields

Potency on ingredients; owner on customer parts; version and approval on documents; calibration dates on instruments;
qualification on operations.

## Statuses and workflows

Document: draft → approved → withdrawn. Action: open → done → effective. Supplier: candidate → qualified → suspended.

## Roles

Quality manager (U27); the F12 roles of planner (U21) and operator (U20).

## Documents

Controlled documents, audit reports, 8D reports, calibration certificates, labels.

## Dashboards

Open actions by age, audit findings, supplier ratings, complaints, calibrations due, quality objectives.

## Settings

Production type per site, document approvers, calibration periods, critical items, 8D triggers.

## Open questions

None.
