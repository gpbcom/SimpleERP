# 📏 BR-SC05-01 The sector follows F12 and the country pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The sector pack adds setups and records but no legal value of its own: product, workplace and environment rules come
from F12 and the country pack; in France:
[CR-FR-074 Conformity of machinery](../../../10-country-packs/france/products/cr-fr-074-machinery-conformity.md),
[CR-FR-077 Substances of very high concern in articles](../../../10-country-packs/france/products/cr-fr-077-reach-svhc-information.md),
[CR-FR-078 SCIP notification of articles](../../../10-country-packs/france/products/cr-fr-078-scip-notification.md),
[CR-FR-062 Product recall](../../../10-country-packs/france/products/cr-fr-062-product-recall.md),
[CR-FR-061 Food traceability](../../../10-country-packs/france/products/cr-fr-061-food-traceability.md).

## Why

See [D329](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d329-manufacturing-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Process setup installed | F12's rules apply unchanged |

Every SC05 use case concerned.

None.
