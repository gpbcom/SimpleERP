# 📏 BR-SC05-05 Customer parts stay the customer's

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Parts supplied by a customer are tracked apart, never valued in the company's stock, and returned or consumed only for
that customer.

## Why

See [D329](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d329-manufacturing-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 50 customer castings received | Stock 50, value 0 |

## Applies to

Every SC05 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
