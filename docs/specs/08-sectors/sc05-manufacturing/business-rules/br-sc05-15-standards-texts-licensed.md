# 📏 BR-SC05-15 Standards' texts licensed

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The texts of ISO and other standards are not shipped; the company links its own licensed copies
([sector standards](../../../05-non-functional/sector-technical-standards.md)).

## Why

See [D329](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d329-manufacturing-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| ISO 9001 text needed | The company uploads its copy |

## Applies to

Every SC05 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
