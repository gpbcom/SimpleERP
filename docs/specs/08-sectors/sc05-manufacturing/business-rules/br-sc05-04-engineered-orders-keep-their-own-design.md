# 📏 BR-SC05-04 Engineered orders keep their own design

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An engineer-to-order project has its own bill of materials and routing, versioned and approved before production.

## Why

See [D329](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d329-manufacturing-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Design not approved | Order cannot start |

## Applies to

Every SC05 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Approvers.

## Open questions

None.
