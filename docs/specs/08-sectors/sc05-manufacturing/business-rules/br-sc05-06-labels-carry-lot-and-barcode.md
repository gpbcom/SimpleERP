# 📏 BR-SC05-06 Labels carry lot and barcode

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Unit, case and pallet labels carry the product's barcode, lot and dates; lot labels follow traceability; in France:
[CR-FR-061 Food traceability](../../../10-country-packs/france/products/cr-fr-061-food-traceability.md).

## Why

See [D329](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d329-manufacturing-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Pallet closed | Pallet label with lot and serial shipping code |

## Applies to

Every SC05 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Label templates.

## Open questions

None.
