# 📏 BR-SC05-12 Instruments calibrated before use

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A measuring instrument past its calibration date cannot record a quality check.

## Why

See [D329](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d329-manufacturing-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Caliper overdue | Check refused, instrument flagged |

## Applies to

Every SC05 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Calibration periods.

## Open questions

None.
