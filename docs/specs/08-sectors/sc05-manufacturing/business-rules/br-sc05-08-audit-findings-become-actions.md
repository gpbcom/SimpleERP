# 📏 BR-SC05-08 Audit findings become actions

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every audit finding becomes a corrective action with an owner and a due date.

## Why

See [D329](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d329-manufacturing-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Minor finding | Action CA-41 due in 30 days |

## Applies to

Every SC05 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
