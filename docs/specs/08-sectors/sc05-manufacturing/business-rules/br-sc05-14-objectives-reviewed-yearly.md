# 📏 BR-SC05-14 Objectives reviewed yearly

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Quality objectives have targets and are reviewed at least yearly in the management review, with its decisions recorded.

## Why

See [D329](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d329-manufacturing-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Review held | Decisions and actions recorded |

## Applies to

Every SC05 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Review frequency.

## Open questions

None.
