# 📏 BR-SC05-03 Configured products stay valid

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A configurator only offers combinations its rules allow, and builds the bill of materials and routing of the chosen
combination.

## Why

See [D329](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d329-manufacturing-sector-coverage.md).
