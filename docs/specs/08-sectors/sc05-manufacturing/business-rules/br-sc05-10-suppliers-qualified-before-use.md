# 📏 BR-SC05-10 Suppliers qualified before use

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Purchases of critical items go only to qualified suppliers; ratings come from deliveries, quality and audits.

## Why

See [D329](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d329-manufacturing-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Supplier not qualified | Purchase order blocked for critical items |

## Applies to

Every SC05 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Critical items.

## Open questions

None.
