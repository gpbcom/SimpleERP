# 📏 BR-SC05-11 Complaints analysed in eight steps

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A customer complaint on quality is analysed in the eight disciplines, each step dated, and answered to the customer.

## Why

See [D329](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d329-manufacturing-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Complaint from a car maker | 8D report sent |

## Applies to

Every SC05 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Which complaints need an 8D.

## Open questions

None.
