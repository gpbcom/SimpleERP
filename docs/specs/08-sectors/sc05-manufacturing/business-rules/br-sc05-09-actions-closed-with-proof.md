# 📏 BR-SC05-09 Actions closed with proof

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A corrective or preventive action closes only with its root cause, the action taken and a check that it worked.

## Why

See [D329](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d329-manufacturing-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| No effectiveness check | Cannot close |

## Applies to

Every SC05 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
