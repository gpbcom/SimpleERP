## Topics still to read

- Separation of the authorising officer and the public accountant: a duty stated in plain words in the use
  case that applies it, to be read in the official source and written in the France pack.
- Commitment before order: CGCT L2342-2 is repealed by Ordonnance n° 2025-526; read in CR-FR-250, the mayor keeps
  the commitment accounts since the budget year 2026 (L1612-38) and the accountant pays only within the credits opened
  (L1612-39).
- Payment orders, revenue titles and the exchange with the public treasury: a duty stated in plain words in
  the use case that applies it, to be read in the official source and written in the France pack.
- Budget vote and the prefecture's legality check: read in CR-FR-250 (vote by 15 April, 30 April in a renewal year,
  each section balanced) and CR-FR-262 (enforceability on transmission); M57 is mandatory for the bodies the law lists and optional for the other local bodies (CR-FR-250).
- Public procurement thresholds and publication: read in CR-FR-251; the minimum delays of each procedure are still to
  read.
- Payment period of public contracts: read in CR-FR-091 (30 days, ECB rate plus 8 points, 40 EUR).
- Civil registry: read in CR-FR-260 (margin notes within 3 days, rectification by the president of the tribunal or, for
  a material error, by the officer), who may receive which copy (décret n° 2017-890, art. 30), and a registry kept by
  automated processing under the security and integrity characteristics set by decree, with no software certification.
- Planning-permit instruction delays: read in CR-FR-261, with electronic filing for every legal person in communes
  over 3 500 inhabitants from 2025-01-01.
- M57 depreciation of assets: still to read (M57 itself is mandatory for the bodies the law lists, CR-FR-250).
- CNRACL and IRCANTEC contributions: read in CR-FR-252 for the CNRACL (28 hours, 11.10 % and 37.65 % in 2026); the
  Ircantec rates are on the lawyer's list.
- Statutory pay by index points: the point value is read in CR-FR-252 (5 907.34 EUR a year for index 100); the
  supplements are on the lawyer's list.
- Council convocation, quorum and publication: read in CR-FR-262 (3 or 5 clear days, quorum of the members in office,
  the list of the deliberations posted within one week).
