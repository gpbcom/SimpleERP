# 🎁 Sector pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Record types

Budget, commitment, payment order, treasury exchange, tender file, bid, public contract, citizen request, hall booking,
council decision, grade, deliberation, civil record, permit request, public asset.

## Fields

The budget chapters and the voted credits; the commitment number on orders; the procedure and thresholds on tender
files; the legal delay on requests and permits; the grade, step and index number on employees; the depreciation duration
on assets.

## Statuses and workflows

Budget: draft → voted → executed → closed. Commitment: open → partly used → closed. Payment order: drafted → signed →
sent → paid or rejected. Tender file: draft → published → analysed → awarded. Permit request: filed → complete →
instructed → decided.

## Roles

Public finance officer (U54), elected official (U55), citizen (U56), HR (U06), payroll officer (U45), office worker
(U16). The authorising officer signs; the public accountant is outside the company and only receives the exchanges.

## Documents

Budget documents, commitments, payment orders and revenue titles, tender files and notices, contracts, council
convocations and minutes, civil-status acts and extracts, permit decisions, asset register extracts.

## Dashboards

Credits used against credits voted, commitments open, orders and titles waiting for the public accountant, tenders by
procedure, requests by deadline, permits near their legal delay, deliberations waiting for transmission.

## Settings

The budget structure and chapters, the signature delegations, the procurement thresholds, the legal media, the council
rates for halls, the request types and deadlines, the depreciation durations.

## Open questions

None.
