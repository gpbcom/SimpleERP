# 📏 BR-SC20-15 Contributions follow the employee's scheme

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

Each employee is declared with the contributions of the scheme their status and hours give, with the rates and bases of
the pack, and a change of status moves the scheme from its date and corrects the past months.

## Why

Contributions to the wrong scheme give the employee wrong pension rights and bring a recovery
([D564](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d564-benchmark-gaps-sc20-public-bodies.md)). (Proposed) The France-pack rule is
written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Tenured staff above the hours threshold | local civil servants' scheme |
| Contract staff | general and supplementary schemes |
| Status change in the month | scheme changes, past months corrected |
| Part-time crossing the threshold | scheme changes, officer told |
| Missing rate | the run is held |

None.

## Configurable
