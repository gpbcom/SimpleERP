# 📏 BR-SC20-01 Expenditure stays within voted credits

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

Expenditure stays within the credits voted by chapter: a commitment above the credit left on its chapter is refused
until the council votes more credit. A budget is voted only when each section is balanced.

## Why

Spending beyond the voted credits is irregular for the authorising officer and the public accountant
([D564](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d564-benchmark-gaps-sc20-public-bodies.md)). (Proposed) The France-pack rule is
written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Commitment within the credit left | accepted |
| Commitment above the credit left | refused, more credit must be voted |
| Operation section not balanced | vote cannot be recorded |
| Supplementary budget voted | credits added |
| Budget voted after the legal date | flagged |
