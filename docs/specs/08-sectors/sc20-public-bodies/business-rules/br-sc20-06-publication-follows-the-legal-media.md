# 📏 BR-SC20-06 Publication follows the legal media

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

A tender is published in every medium its procedure requires before its deadline starts, and the deadline is never
shorter than the minimum of the procedure. Each answer to a bidder's question goes to all bidders.

## Why

A notice in too few media or a short deadline cancels the procedure
([D564](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d564-benchmark-gaps-sc20-public-bodies.md)). The media of each procedure are read
in
[CR-FR-251 Public procurement thresholds](../../../10-country-packs/france/trade/cr-fr-251-public-procurement-thresholds.md):
the BOAMP or an approved legal-notices outlet for an adapted procedure, the BOAMP and the JOUE for a formal one.
(⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) The minimum deadlines of each procedure are still to read: Code de la commande publique
R2143-1 and following, R2161-1 and following.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Formal procedure, three media published | deadline starts |
| A required medium missing | not published |
| Deadline of 20 days where 30 are needed | refused |
| Correction after publication | corrective notice, deadline extended if needed |
| Question from one bidder | answer sent to all |

## Applies to

- [E-SC20-05 Tender file](../data/e-sc20-05-tender-file.md)

None.

## Configurable

The media and the minimum deadline by procedure, from the country pack.
