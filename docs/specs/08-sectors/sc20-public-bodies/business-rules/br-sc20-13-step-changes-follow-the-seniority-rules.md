# 📏 BR-SC20-13 Step changes follow the seniority rules

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

An employee's step is changed only at or after the time the statute sets for their step, and an employee is placed only
in a grade of the staff category of their post. A change needs HR's approval and is sent to payroll with its date.

## Why

A wrong step changes the pay and the pension, and a step given early is an overpayment to recover
([D564](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d564-benchmark-gaps-sc20-public-bodies.md)). (Proposed) The France-pack rule is
written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Minimum time reached | listed for a step change |
| Before the minimum time | refused with the date |
| Grade not in the post's category | placement refused |
| Previous service from another body | counted with its proof |
| Promotion | new step by the conversion rule |
