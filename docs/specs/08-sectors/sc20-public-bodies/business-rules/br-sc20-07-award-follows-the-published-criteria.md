# 📏 BR-SC20-07 Award follows the published criteria

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

A bid is scored only on the criteria and weights published with the tender, and a bid received after the deadline is
Late and never opened. The award is recorded with the ranking and the approval of the elected official where the body
requires it.

## Why

Using a criterion that was not published, or opening a late bid, is a ground to cancel the award
([D564](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d564-benchmark-gaps-sc20-public-bodies.md)). (Proposed) The France-pack rule is
written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Bid received 4 minutes after the deadline | Late, not opened |
| Published criteria only | ranking computed |
| An unpublished criterion | refused |
| Abnormally low bid | bidder asked to justify before ranking |
| Award without approval where required | cannot be recorded |
