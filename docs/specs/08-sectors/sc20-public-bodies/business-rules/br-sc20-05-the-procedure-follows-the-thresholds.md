# 📏 BR-SC20-05 The procedure follows the thresholds

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

The procedure of a tender follows the estimated value of the whole need against the thresholds of the country pack, and
related purchases are added up. The file cannot be published with another procedure, and its criteria and weights are
fixed before publication.

## Why

A wrong procedure or a split purchase makes the contract void and exposes the elected official
([D564](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d564-benchmark-gaps-sc20-public-bodies.md)). (Proposed) The France-pack rule is
written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Estimate under the first threshold | free procedure with proof of good use of funds |
| Estimate in between | adapted procedure |
| Estimate above the European threshold | formal procedure |
| Two related purchases | added up |
| Weights not 100 | file stays in draft |

## Configurable
