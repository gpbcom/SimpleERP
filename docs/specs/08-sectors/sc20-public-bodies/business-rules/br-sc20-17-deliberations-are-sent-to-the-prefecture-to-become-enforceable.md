# 📏 BR-SC20-17 Deliberations are sent to the prefecture to become enforceable

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

A deliberation is sent to the prefecture after its adoption and becomes enforceable only once it is transmitted and
published. The convocation is sent within the legal delay, and the council deliberates only with its quorum.

## Why

A deliberation adopted without a valid convocation or quorum, or not transmitted, can be cancelled
([D564](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d564-benchmark-gaps-sc20-public-bodies.md)). (Proposed) The France-pack rule is
written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Convocation sent 7 days before | accepted |
| Convocation sent 1 day before | refused |
| Quorum not met | meeting adjourned |
| Adopted deliberation | transmitted to the prefecture |
| Transmission fails | stays Adopted, officer told |
