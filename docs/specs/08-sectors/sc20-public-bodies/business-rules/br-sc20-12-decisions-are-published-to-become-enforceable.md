# 📏 BR-SC20-12 Decisions are published to become enforceable

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A decision becomes enforceable only when it is both published and, where the law requires it, transmitted to the
prefecture, and the date of each step is kept. A decision published but not transmitted is listed.

## Why

An act applied before it is enforceable can be cancelled, and the dates are the proof
([D564](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d564-benchmark-gaps-sc20-public-bodies.md)). The France-pack rule is
[CR-FR-262 Deliberations and the legality check](../../../10-country-packs/france/company/cr-fr-262-deliberations-legality-check.md):
an act is enforceable once published or notified and, for those the law lists, sent to the State representative (CGCT
L2131-1).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Posted and transmitted | Enforceable from the later date |
| Posted only | stays Published and listed |
| Transmission fails | kept and retried, officer told |
| Mayor's order notified to a person | notification date also kept |
| Decision withdrawn | marked Withdrawn with its reason |
