# 📏 BR-SC20-03 Orders are signed by the authorising officer

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

A payment order is signed by the authorising officer or a delegate whose delegation covers the amount, only from a
commitment and with its supporting documents. The officer who drafts and the one who signs are different people above
the threshold, and the body's staff never pay: the public accountant does.

## Why

Keeping order and payment apart is the basis of public accounting, and an order signed without authority is void
([D564](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d564-benchmark-gaps-sc20-public-bodies.md)). (Proposed) The France-pack rule is
written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Order from a commitment, signed by the mayor | may be sent |
| Signer without delegation | refused |
| Supporting document missing | cannot be signed |
| Order above the commitment | limited to the commitment |
| Rejected by the accountant | corrected and sent again |

## Configurable

The delegations by person and amount, and the threshold for two people, set by the body.
