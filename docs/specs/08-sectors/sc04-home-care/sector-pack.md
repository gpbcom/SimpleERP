# 🎁 Sector pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Record types

Care recipient, funding, care plan, visit, round, care log entry, incident, care invoice, tax-credit certificate,
complaint, carer qualification, activity report.

## Fields

Access codes and contacts on recipients; funder and share on visits; check-in and check-out on visits; travel time on
rounds; qualifications on carers.

## Statuses and workflows

Recipient: assessed → served → paused → left. Visit: planned → done, missed or cancelled. Complaint: open → answered →
closed.

## Roles

Care coordinator (U25), carer (U26) seeing only their own rounds and recipients, family members as customers with shared
access.

## Documents

Care plan, monthly invoices per payer, tax-credit certificate, activity report, incident report.

## Dashboards

Visits done and missed, visits without a carer, travel time per carer, hours by funder, complaints open, certificates
sent.

## Settings

Registrations per territory, funders and rates, travel-time and split-shift limits, complaint answer delay, the
immediate-advance account.

## Open questions

None.
