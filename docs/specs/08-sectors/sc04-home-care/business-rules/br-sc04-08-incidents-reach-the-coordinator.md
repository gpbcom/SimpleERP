# 📏 BR-SC04-08 Incidents reach the coordinator

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An incident reported by a carer reaches the coordinator at once, and serious ones the family contact too.

## Why

See [D326](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d326-home-care-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Fall reported | Coordinator and daughter told |

## Applies to

Every SC04 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Seriousness levels.

## Open questions

None.
