# 📏 BR-SC04-01 Home care follows the country pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every legal value of home care (registration, tax credit, immediate advance, allowances, health data, travel time) comes
from the company's country pack; in France:
[CR-FR-102 Personal-services declaration, approval and authorisation](../../../10-country-packs/france/home-care/cr-fr-102-personal-services-registration.md),
[CR-FR-103 Tax credit for home services and its yearly certificate](../../../10-country-packs/france/tax-and-vat/cr-fr-103-home-services-tax-credit.md),
[CR-FR-104 Immediate advance of the tax credit](../../../10-country-packs/france/tax-and-vat/cr-fr-104-immediate-advance-of-tax-credit.md),
[CR-FR-105 Billing the département for allowances](../../../10-country-packs/france/home-care/cr-fr-105-departmental-allowances-billing.md),
[CR-FR-055 Health data hosting](../../../10-country-packs/france/data-protection/cr-fr-055-health-data-hosting.md),
[CR-FR-106 Travel time and split shifts in home care](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-106-home-care-travel-and-split-shifts.md).

## Why

See [D326](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d326-home-care-sector-coverage.md).
