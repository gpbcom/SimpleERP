# 📏 BR-SC04-10 Family sees what is shared

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A family member sees the visits, the shared part of the log and the invoices of their relative, with the recipient's
consent.

## Why

See [D327](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d327-home-care-personas.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Daughter opens the app | Visits and log, no health details |

## Applies to

Every SC04 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Shared parts.

## Open questions

None.
