# 📏 BR-SC04-14 Complaints answered

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every complaint is recorded, answered within the set delay and followed to its close.

## Why

See [D326](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d326-home-care-sector-coverage.md).

|---|---|
| Complaint on 2 Nov | Answer due 9 Nov |

None.

The delay.
