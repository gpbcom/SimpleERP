# 📏 BR-SC04-18 In agent mode the family is the employer

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

In agent mode the family is the employer: the employment contract, the payslips and the declarations name the family,
never the provider, and the provider invoices only its fee under the signed mandate. Nothing is prepared in the family's
name before the mandate is signed.

## Why

If the provider acted as employer it would take on the duties and risks of an employer it does not wish to be, and the
family could lose its rights as employer. The owner asked for agent mode
([D548](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d548-benchmark-gaps-sc04-home-care.md)). (Proposed) The France-pack rule is
written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Mandate signed, contract in the family's name | accepted |
| Contract naming the provider as employer | refused |
| Mandate unsigned | nothing prepared |
| Fee 38.00 EUR a month | invoiced alone, no wage |
