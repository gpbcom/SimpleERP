# 📏 BR-SC04-03 A care plan before visits

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Visits are planned from a signed care plan: tasks, frequency, hours and funding.

## Why

See [D326](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d326-home-care-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| No signed plan | Only the assessment visit can be planned |

## Applies to

Every SC04 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
