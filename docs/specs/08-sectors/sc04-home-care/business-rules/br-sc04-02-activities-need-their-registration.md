# 📏 BR-SC04-02 Activities need their registration

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A recipient can be offered an activity only if the company's declaration, approval or authorisation covers it in that
territory; quotes and invoices show the numbers; in France:
[CR-FR-102 Personal-services declaration, approval and authorisation](../../../10-country-packs/france/home-care/cr-fr-102-personal-services-registration.md).

## Why

See [D326](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d326-home-care-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Help to an elderly person, no authorisation | Activity not offered |

## Applies to

Every SC04 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Registrations per territory.

## Open questions

None.
