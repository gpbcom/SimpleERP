# 📏 BR-SC04-05 Rounds count travel time

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Rounds include the travel time between visits, which counts as working time; split shifts stay within the agreement's
limits; in France:
[CR-FR-106 Travel time and split shifts in home care](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-106-home-care-travel-and-split-shifts.md),
[CR-FR-042 Working time and rest](../../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-042-working-time-and-rest.md).

## Why

See [D326](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d326-home-care-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 25 min between two visits | 25 min paid |

## Applies to

Every SC04 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Limits per agreement.

## Open questions

None.
