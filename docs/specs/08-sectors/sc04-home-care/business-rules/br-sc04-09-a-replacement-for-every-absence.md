# 📏 BR-SC04-09 A replacement for every absence

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An absent carer's visits are listed at once and replacements proposed; a visit left without a carer is flagged.

## Why

See [D326](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d326-home-care-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Carer sick | 6 visits to cover, 4 replacements proposed |

## Applies to

Every SC04 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
