# 📏 BR-SC04-07 Visit times from check-in

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A visit's real times come from the carer's check-in and check-out on the phone; the location is read only at those
moments and only with consent; in France:
[CR-FR-054 Location only when needed and announced](../../../10-country-packs/france/data-protection/cr-fr-054-location-with-consent.md).

## Why

See [D326](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d326-home-care-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Check-in 09:02, out 10:05 | 1 h 03 recorded |

## Applies to

Every SC04 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Check-in by phone, tag or code.

## Open questions

None.
