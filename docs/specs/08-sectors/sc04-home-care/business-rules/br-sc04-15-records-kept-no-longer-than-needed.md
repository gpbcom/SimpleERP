# 📏 BR-SC04-15 Records kept no longer than needed

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Recipients' records are kept while they are served, then as the law requires, then deleted; in France:
[CR-FR-052 Personal data kept no longer than needed](../../../10-country-packs/france/data-protection/cr-fr-052-storage-limitation.md).

## Why

See [D326](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d326-home-care-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Recipient left 2020 | Personal data deleted, invoices kept |

## Applies to

Every SC04 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
