# 📏 BR-SC04-12 Yearly certificate before 31 March

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each client gets the certificate of the previous year before 31 March; in France:
[CR-FR-103 Tax credit for home services and its yearly certificate](../../../10-country-packs/france/tax-and-vat/cr-fr-103-home-services-tax-credit.md).

## Why

See [D326](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d326-home-care-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 2026 services | Certificates sent in February 2027 |

## Applies to

Every SC04 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
