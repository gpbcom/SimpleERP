# 📏 BR-SC18-04 Cancellation follows the legal notice period

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A cancellation takes effect after the notice the contract states, and never later than the law allows; the club tells
the member the effect date and the last due and bills nothing after the effect date. A renewal is announced to the
member in time before it happens.

## Why

Billing after a cancellation, or hiding the way to cancel, is the main complaint about gym subscriptions. The owner
asked for clubs and gyms ([D562](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d562-benchmark-gaps-sc18-sports-and-fitness.md)).
The notice and the information duties come from the France pack, written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Notice one month, asked on 2026-12-10 | ends on 2027-01-10 |
| Commitment not ended | earliest date shown, exit for a serious reason offered |
| Freeze for two months | no dues those months |
| Due after the end date | blocked |
| Renewal in 30 days | the member is told in time |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC18-04](../use-cases/01-memberships-and-access/uc-sc18-04-freeze-or-cancel-a-membership.md)
- [E-SC18-01 Club membership](../data/e-sc18-01-club-membership.md)

## Exceptions

A freeze or an early exit for a serious reason may exceed the usual limits with a proof.

## Configurable

The freeze limits, the notice and the days before a renewal at which the member is told, by the owner.

## Open questions

None.
