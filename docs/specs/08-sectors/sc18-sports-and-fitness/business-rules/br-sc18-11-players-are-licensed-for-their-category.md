# 📏 BR-SC18-11 Players are licensed for their category

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A player is on a team only with a licence for the season in a category the team allows and, where the competition asks
for it, a valid certificate; a team is registered only when it reaches the minimum size.

## Why

An ineligible player can cost the team its results and the club a penalty. The owner asked for teams and competitions
([D562](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d562-benchmark-gaps-sc18-sports-and-fitness.md)). The categories and
sizes come from the federations' rules, written in the France pack later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| U15 player with a U15 licence | allowed |
| U16 player in a U15 team | refused unless the federation allows it |
| No licence | refused |
| Team of 7 with a minimum of 8 | not registered |
| Certificate expired | cannot play |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC18-11](../use-cases/03-licences-and-competitions/uc-sc18-11-run-teams-and-competitions.md)
- [E-SC18-08 Sports team](../data/e-sc18-08-team.md)
- [E-SC18-08 Sports team](../data/e-sc18-08-team.md)

## Exceptions

A federation's rule may allow a player one category above; the rule is named when used.

## Configurable

The categories, the minimum size and the competitions, by the owner.

## Open questions

None.
