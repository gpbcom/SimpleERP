# 📏 BR-SC18-01 Contracts state the cancellation terms

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A membership contract states, before it is signed, the price, the length of commitment, how it renews and the
cancellation terms; a plan with no cancellation terms cannot be sold and a contract is Active only once signed.

## Why

A member who was not told how to leave a contract disputes the dues and the club loses the claim. The owner asked for
clubs and gyms ([D562](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d562-benchmark-gaps-sc18-sports-and-fitness.md)). The
consumer rules come from the France pack, written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Plan with 12 months and a notice of one month | terms shown and signed before the start |
| Plan with no cancellation terms | cannot be sold |
| Member does not sign | stays Offered |
| Child member | the parent signs |
| Sale at a distance | the withdrawal right is shown |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC18-01](../use-cases/01-memberships-and-access/uc-sc18-01-sell-a-membership.md)
- [E-SC18-01 Club membership](../data/e-sc18-01-club-membership.md)
- [E-SC18-01 Club membership](../data/e-sc18-01-club-membership.md)

## Exceptions

An association's yearly subscription follows its statutes and has no notice.

## Configurable

The plans, the commitment, the notice and the terms text, by the owner.

## Open questions

None.
