# 📏 BR-SC18-12 Results are published once validated

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A result is published in the standings and the app only after a second person has validated it; a published result is
changed only with a recorded reason and a new validation.

## Why

A wrong result published changes the standings and spoils trust. The owner asked for results
([D562](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d562-benchmark-gaps-sc18-sports-and-fitness.md)).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Entered by the coach, not validated | not published |
| Validated by the other coach | published |
| Change after publication | needs a reason and a new validation |
| Contested result | unpublished until settled |
| Result of a race | times and ranks |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC18-12](../use-cases/03-licences-and-competitions/uc-sc18-12-record-results.md)
- [E-SC18-09 Result](../data/e-sc18-09-result.md)
- [E-SC18-09 Result](../data/e-sc18-09-result.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Who may validate and the delay for validation, by the owner.

## Open questions

None.
