# 📏 BR-SC18-19 Supervision ratios follow the youth rules

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

A holiday camp for minors opens only when it is declared and each group has at least the number of qualified animators
its ratio requires for the age of the children; a child is released only to a person on the list the parent gave.

## Why

The safety of children depends on the supervision and on knowing who may take them away. The owner asked for holiday
camps ([D562](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d562-benchmark-gaps-sc18-sports-and-fitness.md)). The ratios and
the declaration come from the France pack, written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 14 children aged 8 to 10, 2 animators, ratio 1 for 8 | ok |
| Same group, 1 animator | the camp cannot open |
| Camp not declared | cannot open |
| Person not on the list | the child is not released |
| Animator with no qualification for the age band | cannot be assigned |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC18-19](../use-cases/05-facilities-events-camps-and-rentals/uc-sc18-19-run-holiday-camps.md)
- [E-SC18-15 Camp registration](../data/e-sc18-15-camp-registration.md)
- [E-SC18-15 Camp registration](../data/e-sc18-15-camp-registration.md)
- France-pack rule, (Proposed) until its law is read:
  [CR-FR-258 Youth holiday camps declaration](../../../10-country-packs/france/education/cr-fr-258-youth-holiday-camps.md)

## Exceptions

None: the ratio and the declaration are legal duties and cannot be relaxed.

## Configurable

The ratios by age band and the delay before health sheets are erased, as data of the country pack.

## Open questions

None.
