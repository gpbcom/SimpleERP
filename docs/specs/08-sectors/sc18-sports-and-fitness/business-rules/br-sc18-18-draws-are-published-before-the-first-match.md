# 📏 BR-SC18-18 Draws are published before the first match

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The draw of a tournament is published to every player who entered before the first match is played, and the first match
cannot start until it is; a draw changed after publication is published again with the reason.

## Why

A draw seen only by some players is unfair and is disputed. The owner asked for tournaments and events
([D562](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d562-benchmark-gaps-sc18-sports-and-fitness.md)).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Draw made, not published | the first match is blocked |
| Draw published to the 32 players | the first match may start |
| A player withdraws after the draw | published again with the reason |
| Entries below the minimum | category merged or cancelled |
| Entry without a licence | refused |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC18-18](../use-cases/05-facilities-events-camps-and-rentals/uc-sc18-18-run-tournaments-and-events.md)
- [E-SC18-14 Tournament](../data/e-sc18-14-tournament.md)
- [E-SC18-14 Tournament](../data/e-sc18-14-tournament.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The categories, minimums, fees and the draw method, by the coach.

## Open questions

None.
