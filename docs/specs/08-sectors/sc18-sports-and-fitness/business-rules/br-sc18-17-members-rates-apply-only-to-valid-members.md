# 📏 BR-SC18-17 Members' rates apply only to valid members

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

The members' rate applies only when the booker holds a valid membership on the day of the booking; a frozen, ended or
unpaid membership pays the public rate, and the rate is shown before payment.

## Why

A members' rate given to the wrong people loses income and treats members unfairly. The owner asked for courts and
facilities ([D562](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d562-benchmark-gaps-sc18-sports-and-fitness.md)).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Valid member | members' rate |
| Frozen membership | public rate |
| Guest of a member | public rate unless the plan allows guest places |
| Peak hour | peak price |
| Court blocked for a class | not shown free |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC18-17](../use-cases/05-facilities-events-camps-and-rentals/uc-sc18-17-book-courts-and-facilities.md)
- [E-SC18-13 Court booking](../data/e-sc18-13-court-booking.md)
- [E-SC18-13 Court booking](../data/e-sc18-13-court-booking.md)

## Exceptions

A plan may include a number of guest places at the members' rate.

## Configurable

The prices by hour, the rates and the hold and cancellation times, by the owner.

## Open questions

None.
