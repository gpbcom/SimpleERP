# 📏 BR-SC18-10 Competitions need a valid certificate

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A member is entered in a competition only when a certificate valid on the day of the competition and covering
competition is recorded; the club records only the date, the validity, the coverage and whether the person is fit, never
the medical details.

## Why

A player without a valid certificate is not covered and exposes the club. Keeping only what is needed protects the
member's health data. The owner asked for medical certificates
([D562](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d562-benchmark-gaps-sc18-sports-and-fitness.md)). The federations'
requirements come from the France pack, written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Competition certificate valid on the day | entry allowed |
| Training-only certificate | no competition |
| Certificate expired | removed from the lists |
| Certificate with no date | refused |
| Diagnosis written in a note | never stored |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC18-10](../use-cases/03-licences-and-competitions/uc-sc18-10-check-medical-certificates.md)
- [E-SC18-07 Medical certificate](../data/e-sc18-07-medical-certificate.md)
- [E-SC18-07 Medical certificate](../data/e-sc18-07-medical-certificate.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The competitions that need a certificate and the days before the end at which it is flagged, by the owner.

## Open questions

None.
