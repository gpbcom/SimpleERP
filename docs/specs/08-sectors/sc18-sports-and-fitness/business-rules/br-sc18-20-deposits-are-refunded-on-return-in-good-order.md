# 📏 BR-SC18-20 Deposits are refunded on return in good order

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The deposit of a rental is refunded in full when the item comes back in the condition the terms describe; it is kept
only for damage or loss recorded with proof, and never above the cost of the repair or the value of the item.

## Why

A deposit kept without proof is a dispute and a consumer complaint. The owner asked for equipment rental
([D562](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d562-benchmark-gaps-sc18-sports-and-fitness.md)). The consumer rules
come from the France pack, written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Racket back in good order | deposit refunded in full |
| Damage costing 12 EUR on a deposit of 30 | 12 kept with the photos, 18 refunded |
| Item lost | the deposit is kept up to its value |
| Late return | extra time charged at the terms' price |
| No photo of the damage | the deposit is refunded unless the owner decides otherwise |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC18-20](../use-cases/05-facilities-events-camps-and-rentals/uc-sc18-20-rent-out-equipment.md)
- [E-SC18-16 Rental item](../data/e-sc18-16-rental-item.md)
- [E-SC18-16 Rental item](../data/e-sc18-16-rental-item.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The prices, the deposit of each item and the delay to settle a dispute, by the owner.

## Open questions

None.
