# 📏 BR-SC18-09 A licence is issued once a year per member

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A member holds at most one licence per season and federation; a second request for the same member, season and
federation is refused, and a licence is requested only when the certificate or questionnaire the federation needs is
valid.

## Why

A double licence is paid twice to the federation and can invalidate a competition result. The owner asked for federation
licences ([D562](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d562-benchmark-gaps-sc18-sports-and-fitness.md)). The rules
per federation come from the France pack, written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| First request of the season | accepted |
| Second request for the same season | refused |
| Certificate expired | held |
| Season ended | Expired |
| Transfer to another category | a new licence, the old one Cancelled |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC18-09](../use-cases/03-licences-and-competitions/uc-sc18-09-issue-federation-licences.md)
- [E-SC18-06 Licence](../data/e-sc18-06-licence.md)
- [E-SC18-06 Licence](../data/e-sc18-06-licence.md)

## Exceptions

A licence for another federation (another sport) is a different licence and is allowed.

## Configurable

The federations, the seasons, the categories and the fees, by the owner.

## Open questions

None.
