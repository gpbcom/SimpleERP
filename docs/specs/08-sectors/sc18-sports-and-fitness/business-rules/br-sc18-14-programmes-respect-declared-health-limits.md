# 📏 BR-SC18-14 Programmes respect declared health limits

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

A programme is activated only after each of its exercises has been checked against the health limits the member
declared; an exercise that breaks a limit is removed or kept only with the coach's written reason and the member's
agreement.

## Why

An exercise that ignores a declared injury can hurt the member and engages the coach's and the club's responsibility.
The owner asked for personal training
([D562](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d562-benchmark-gaps-sc18-sports-and-fitness.md)).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Knee limit and box jumps | flagged |
| No limit declared | the coach records that none were declared |
| Member declares a new limit | the programme is checked again |
| Template reused | checked against the new member's limits |
| Override with a reason and the member's agreement | kept and recorded |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC18-14](../use-cases/04-personal-training/uc-sc18-14-plan-a-training-programme.md)
- [E-SC18-11 Training programme](../data/e-sc18-11-training-programme.md)
- [E-SC18-11 Training programme](../data/e-sc18-11-training-programme.md)

## Exceptions

A coach may keep an exercise with a written reason and the member's agreement.

## Configurable

The exercise library and the kinds of limit, by the owner.

## Open questions

None.
