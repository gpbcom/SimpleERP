## Topics still to read

- Sports educator diplomas and professional card: a duty stated in plain words in the use case that applies
  it, to be read in the official source and written in the France pack.
- Medical certificates: a duty stated in plain words in the use case that applies it, to be read in the
  official source and written in the France pack.
- Federation licences: a duty stated in plain words in the use case that applies it, to be read in the
  official source and written in the France pack.
- Youth holiday camp declaration and supervision ratios: read in CR-FR-258 (declaration before opening, the
  qualified share of animators, 1 animator for 8 children under 6 and 1 for 12 from 6 in a holiday stay); how long the
  health sheets are kept is on the lawyer's list there.
- Equipment rental deposits: a duty stated in plain words in the use case that applies it, to be read in the
  official source and written in the France pack.
- Consumer rules for gym subscriptions and cancellation: a duty stated in plain words in the use case that
  applies it, to be read in the official source and written in the France pack.
