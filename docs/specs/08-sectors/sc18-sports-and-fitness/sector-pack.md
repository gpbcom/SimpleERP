# 🎁 Sector pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Record types

Club membership, dues mandate, access event, class, class booking, licence, medical certificate, team, result, coaching
package, training programme, progress record, court booking, tournament, camp registration, rental item.

## Fields

The cancellation terms on contracts; the activity and the diploma on classes and coaches; the federation, season and
category on licences; the validity and the practice on certificates; the health limits on programmes; the supervision
ratio on camps; the deposit on rental items.

## Statuses and workflows

Club membership: offered → active → frozen or notice given → ended. Class: draft → published → full → held, or
cancelled. Licence: requested → issued → expired. Tournament: draft → open → closed → drawn → running → finished.

## Roles

Owner (U13), coach (U53), treasurer (U31), members and parents (U09).

## Documents

Contracts with cancellation terms, direct-debit mandates, licences, medical certificates, timetables, draws and results,
programmes, camp files, rental receipts.

## Dashboards

Members and cancellations, door entries, class fill rate, licences and certificates due, court occupancy, tournament
entries, camp ratios, rental items out.

## Settings

Membership plans and notice periods, door rules, class capacities and booking cut-off, the season and the federations,
the competitions that need a certificate, the camp ratios, the deposits.

## Open questions

None.
