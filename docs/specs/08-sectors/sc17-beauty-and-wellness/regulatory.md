## Topics still to read

- Cosmetics safety and batch tracing: a duty stated in plain words in the use case that applies it, to be
  read in the official source and written in the France pack.
- Tattoo, piercing and laser declaration: read in CR-FR-257 (declaration to the ARS before starting, hygiene
  training, consent of a guardian for a minor, laser hair removal by doctors, nurses and trained beauticians, the unique batch reference on a tattoo ink's
  label); no batch rule applies to needles, whose batches are recorded as a product choice.
- Hygiene training and aftercare: a duty stated in plain words in the use case that applies it, to be read in
  the official source and written in the France pack.
- Health data of client files and photo consent: a duty stated in plain words in the use case that applies
  it, to be read in the official source and written in the France pack.
- Gift voucher validity: CR-FR-097 is written, its minimum validity and mentions are on the lawyer's list.
- Chair rental contracts and the risk of disguised employment: a duty stated in plain words in the use case
  that applies it, to be read in the official source and written in the France pack.
- Tips sharing: read in CR-FR-113: tips go in full to the staff in contact with customers (Code du travail
  L3244-1), the law sets no sharing key, and they are exempt from contributions and income tax until 2028-12-31.
