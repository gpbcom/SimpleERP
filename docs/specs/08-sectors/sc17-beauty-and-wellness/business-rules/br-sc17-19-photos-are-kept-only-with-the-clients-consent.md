# 📏 BR-SC17-19 Photos are kept only with the client's consent

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A photo of a client is taken and kept only with that client's signed consent for a stated purpose (the file only, or
also the salon's communication); the consent can be withdrawn at any time and the photos it covered are then removed or
no longer shown outside the file.

## Why

Photos and health notes are personal data and an unwanted post harms the client. The owner asked for the client file
with photos ([D561](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d561-benchmark-gaps-sc17-beauty-and-wellness.md)). The
rules on consent and retention come from the France pack, written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Consent 'file only' | the photo stays in the file, never in a post |
| Consent 'communication' | the photo may be used in a post |
| No consent | no photo |
| Consent withdrawn | the photos it covered are removed |
| Erasure request | the file is erased except what the law keeps |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC17-19](../use-cases/05-rentals-care-records-and-loyalty/uc-sc17-19-keep-the-client-file-with-photos.md)
- [E-SC17-13 Salon client file](../data/e-sc17-13-client-file.md)
- [E-SC17-13 Salon client file](../data/e-sc17-13-client-file.md)

## Exceptions

A photo needed as proof of a treatment after a complaint may be kept as long as the claim lasts, with the reason
recorded.

## Configurable

The consent texts and the inactivity period before archiving, by the manager.

## Open questions

None.
