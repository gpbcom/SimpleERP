# 📏 BR-SC17-03 Desk bookings send the same confirmation

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A booking made at the desk or on the phone sends the same confirmation, with the same terms and the same reminder, as an
online booking, by the channel the customer agreed to or by paper.

## Why

A customer booked at the desk must not be treated worse than one booked online: they too must know the deposit and the
cancellation terms. The owner asked for staff and cabin booking
([D561](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d561-benchmark-gaps-sc17-beauty-and-wellness.md)).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Desk booking, customer agreed to texts | the same text as online |
| Customer refused all messages | printed confirmation |
| Deposit asked | payment link sent or card taken |
| Rebook at the chair | same services, four weeks later |
| Phone booking | the same confirmation |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC17-03](../use-cases/01-book-staff-and-cabins/uc-sc17-03-book-at-the-desk.md)
- [E-SC17-02 Salon booking](../data/e-sc17-02-salon-booking.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The default channel and the reminder time, by the manager.

## Open questions

None.
