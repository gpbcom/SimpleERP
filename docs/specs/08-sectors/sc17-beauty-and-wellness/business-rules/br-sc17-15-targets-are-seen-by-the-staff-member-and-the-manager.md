# 📏 BR-SC17-15 Targets are seen by the staff member and the manager

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

A staff member sees only their own targets and progress; the manager sees every target. The progress is computed from
the sales already recorded, never entered by hand.

## Why

Targets seen by everyone create pressure and conflict, and hand-typed progress is disputed. The owner asked for staff
targets ([D561](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d561-benchmark-gaps-sc17-beauty-and-wellness.md)).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| A beautician opens the screen | only their targets |
| The manager opens the screen | everyone's targets |
| A sale with no seller | no progress added |
| Month closed | the targets are locked |
| Team target | the members' sales add up |

## Configurable

The kinds of target and the status thresholds, by the manager.
