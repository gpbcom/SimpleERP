# 📏 BR-SC17-12 Products keep their safety sheet and batch

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A product is used or sold only when its safety record holds the safety sheet, the supplier, the batch, the expiry date
and the responsible company; a recalled batch is blocked at once and the clients who received it are listed.

## Why

An untraceable product cannot be recalled, and a salon that cannot show its records is exposed in an inspection and for
a client's reaction. The owner asked for cosmetics safety records
([D561](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d561-benchmark-gaps-sc17-beauty-and-wellness.md)). The legal duties
come from the France pack, written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Product with sheet, batch and company | Current, may be used |
| No batch on the label | refused at reception |
| Batch recalled by the supplier | blocked and clients listed |
| Batch past its expiry | blocked from use |
| Client reports a reaction | a record with the batch is opened |

## Applies to

- [UC-SC17-12](../use-cases/03-salon-retail-and-stock/uc-sc17-12-keep-cosmetics-safety-records.md)
- [E-SC17-08 Safety record](../data/e-sc17-08-safety-record.md)
- [E-SC17-08 Safety record](../data/e-sc17-08-safety-record.md)

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

The days before the expiry at which a batch is flagged, by the manager.
