# 📏 BR-SC17-06 Membership dues stop at cancellation

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Membership dues are billed only until the cancellation takes effect: the day the member cancels or, if the terms set a
notice, the end of the notice. No due is billed after that date, and a due already paid for a period after it is
refunded pro rata when the terms say so.

## Why

Billing a member after they cancelled is a refund claim and a loss of trust. The owner asked for memberships
([D561](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d561-benchmark-gaps-sc17-beauty-and-wellness.md)). The consumer rules
on cancelling come from the France pack, written later.

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Cancel on 2026-11-30, notice one month | last due 2026-12-31 |
| Cancel with no notice | dues stop at once |
| Pause for two months | no dues for those two months |
| Due paid for a period after the end date | refunded pro rata |
| Plan upgrade | the new fee starts at the next due date |

## Configurable
