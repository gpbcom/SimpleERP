# 📏 BR-SC17-13 Commissions follow the plan in force on the sale date

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

A commission is computed from the plan in force on the date of the sale, not on the date the commission is paid; a plan
change never alters sales made before its start date, and one staff member has only one plan on a given date.

## Why

Using today's plan on past sales changes what people were promised. The owner asked for commissions and tips
([D561](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d561-benchmark-gaps-sc17-beauty-and-wellness.md)).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Plan Spring from 2027-03-01, sale on 2027-02-28 | the Winter plan applies |
| Plan changes on 2027-03-15 | each sale uses its own plan |
| Two plans on the same day | refused |
| Sale refunded in April | the commission is reversed in April |
| Independent renter | no plan |

- [E-SC17-09 Salon commission plan](../data/e-sc17-09-commission-plan.md)

None.

## Configurable
