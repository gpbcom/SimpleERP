## Questions

  CR-FR-257 for tattoo, permanent make-up, piercing and laser hair removal; other kinds still to read.

  consommation beside CR-FR-097 (on the lawyer's list there); its VAT treatment is read in CR-FR-013 (a single-purpose
  voucher bears VAT when sold, a multi-purpose one when used).

  the warning of UC-SC17-17 is final.
- The cosmetics duties of a salon as a distributor: labelling, traceability and withdrawal, to read in the
  official sources.
- Every use case, screen, rule and entity of this sector was confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-27 (D572); the sector
  was interviewed on 2026-11-14 and its pages are written at full depth.
