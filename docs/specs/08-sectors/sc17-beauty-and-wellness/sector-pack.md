# 🎁 Sector pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Record types

Cabin, salon booking, booking deposit, session package, salon membership, gift voucher, product use, safety record,
commission plan, tip, rental contract, care record, client file.

## Fields

Skills and cabin kind on services; deposit and no-show terms on the salon; the batch and the safety sheet on products;
the posted tip-sharing rule; the care kind and the consent on care records; the photo consent on client files.

## Statuses and workflows

Salon booking: held → confirmed → arrived → done, or no show or cancelled. Gift voucher: issued → partly used →
redeemed, or expired. Rental contract: draft → signed → active → ended. Client file: active → archived → erased.

## Roles

Beautician (U52), store manager (U23), customers (U09); independents who rent a chair work with the beautician's rights
on their own bookings and invoices only.

## Documents

Booking confirmations, deposit receipts, vouchers, membership terms, safety sheets, consent forms, rental contracts,
care records, commission statements.

## Dashboards

Bookings and no-shows, cabin and chair occupancy, packages and balances, product stock and safety records, commissions
and tips, rental income.

## Settings

Services and durations, cleaning time between clients, deposit and no-show terms, the voucher validity period, the
tip-sharing rule, the photo consent texts, the care kinds the salon declares.

## Open questions

None.
