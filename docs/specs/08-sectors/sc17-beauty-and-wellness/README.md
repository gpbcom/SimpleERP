# 💇 SC17 Beauty and wellness

**Status:** ✅ specified

Interviewed on 2026-11-14 as a new sector: D561 (the owner's answers of 2026-11-14: G14 and the sector interview G191
and G192).

## Summary

What salons, spas, barbers, nail bars, tattoo and piercing studios and wellness centres add: booking of staff and cabins
with deposits and no-show fees, packages, memberships and gift vouchers, retail and back-bar stock with cosmetics safety
records, commissions and tips, and the four areas the owner added (chair and cabin rental to independents, regulated
care records, the client file with photos, loyalty and referrals).
([D561](../../06-decisions/sectors/d561-benchmark-gaps-sc17-beauty-and-wellness.md))

## Profile

Hair and beauty salons, barbers, nail bars, spas, massage and wellness centres, and studios that tattoo or pierce, from
one chair to a chain of salons. Staff are employees or independents who rent a chair or a cabin, and many salons have
both (D561).

## Personas

[U52 Beautician](../../01-users/personas/u52-beautician.md),
[U23 Store manager](../../01-users/personas/u23-store-manager.md),
[U09 Customer](../../01-users/personas/u09-customer.md).
