# 🎁 Sector pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Record types

Mandate, listing, viewing, offer, client fund, fee scale, lease, deposit, rent call, charge settlement, landlord
statement, diagnostic, co-owners' assembly, call for funds, works fund, co-ownership.

## Fields

Register number on mandates; energy rating on properties and leases; index clause and reference quarter on leases;
shares on lots.

## Statuses and workflows

Mandate: signed → listed → under offer → sold or expired. Lease: signed → running → ended. Rent: called → paid, partly
paid or unpaid.

## Roles

Property manager (U34), real-estate agent (U35), landlords, tenants and co-owners as U09 in the portal.

## Documents

Mandates, leases, inventories, receipts, notices, landlord statements, charge settlements, assembly notices and minutes,
calls for funds.

## Dashboards

Rents collected and unpaid, deposits due, mandates and sales, assemblies to convene, works fund balances.

## Settings

Fee scales, unpaid-rent steps, landlord payment frequency, cities with rent caps, co-ownership keys.

## Open questions

None.
