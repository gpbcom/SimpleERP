# 📏 BR-SC10-07 Letting rent within the caps

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

In a capped city, the base rent at letting stays within the capped reference rent; in France:
[CR-FR-143 Rent caps and energy-inefficient homes](../../../10-country-packs/france/real-estate/cr-fr-143-rent-caps-and-energy-rules.md).

## Why

See [D344](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d344-real-estate-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Lyon, rent over the cap | Refused, cap shown |

## Applies to

Every SC10 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
