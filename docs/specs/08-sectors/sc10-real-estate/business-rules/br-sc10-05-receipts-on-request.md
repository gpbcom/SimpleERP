# 📏 BR-SC10-05 Receipts on request

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A tenant gets a free receipt with rent and charges apart; in France:
[CR-FR-141 Rent receipts](../../../10-country-packs/france/real-estate/cr-fr-141-rent-receipts.md).

## Why

See [D344](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d344-real-estate-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Tenant asks | Receipt in the portal |

## Applies to

Every SC10 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Automatic receipts or on request.

## Open questions

None.
