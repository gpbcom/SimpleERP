# 📏 BR-SC10-04 Deposit within the cap

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A residential deposit stays within the legal cap and is returned by the deadline, else the penalty is computed; in
France:
[CR-FR-140 Security deposit of a residential lease](../../../10-country-packs/france/real-estate/cr-fr-140-security-deposit.md).

## Why

See [D344](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d344-real-estate-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Unfurnished, rent 800 | Deposit at most 800 |

## Applies to

Every SC10 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
