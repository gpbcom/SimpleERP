# 📏 BR-SC10-10 Landlords paid with statements

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Landlords are paid the rents collected, less fees and expenses, with a statement each period.

## Why

See [D344](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d344-real-estate-sector-coverage.md).

|---|---|
| November | Statement and transfer |

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Frequency, fees.
