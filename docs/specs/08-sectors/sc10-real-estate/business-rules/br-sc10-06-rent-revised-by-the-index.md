# 📏 BR-SC10-06 Rent revised by the index

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

With a revision clause, the rent is revised yearly by the reference index, never for energy-inefficient homes, never
retroactively; in France:
[CR-FR-142 Yearly rent revision by the reference index](../../../10-country-packs/france/real-estate/cr-fr-142-rent-revision-irl.md).

## Why

See [D344](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d344-real-estate-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Home rated G | Revision blocked |

## Applies to

Every SC10 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
