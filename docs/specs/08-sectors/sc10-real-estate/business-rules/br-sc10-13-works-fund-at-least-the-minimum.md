# 📏 BR-SC10-13 Works fund at least the minimum

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The works fund's yearly contribution is at least the legal minimum and kept on its own account; in France:
[CR-FR-147 Co-ownership works fund](../../../10-country-packs/france/real-estate/cr-fr-147-works-fund.md).

## Why

See [D344](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d344-real-estate-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Budget 40 000 | At least 2 000 |

## Applies to

Every SC10 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

No.

## Open questions

None.
