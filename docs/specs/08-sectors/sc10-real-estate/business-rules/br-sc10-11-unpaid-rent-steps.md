# 📏 BR-SC10-11 Unpaid rent steps

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An unpaid rent follows the set steps: reminder, formal notice, guarantor, insurer, then legal action.

## Why

See [D344](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d344-real-estate-sector-coverage.md).

| 10 days late | Reminder sent |
