# 📏 BR-SC10-14 Calls for funds by shares

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Co-ownership calls for funds are split by each lot's shares for the budget and by the key of each works decision.

## Why

See [D344](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d344-real-estate-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Lot with 120/10 000 | 1.2 % of each call |

## Applies to

Every SC10 use case concerned.

## Exceptions

None.

## Configurable

Keys.

## Open questions

None.
