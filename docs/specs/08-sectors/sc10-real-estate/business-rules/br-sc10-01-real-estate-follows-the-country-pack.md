# 📏 BR-SC10-01 Real estate follows the country pack

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every legal value of real estate (agents' card and guarantee, deposits, receipts, indexation, rent caps, diagnostics,
charges, co-ownership) comes from the country pack; in France:
[CR-FR-139 Agents' card, client-funds guarantee and mandates register](../../../10-country-packs/france/real-estate/cr-fr-139-hoguet-card-and-guarantee.md),
[CR-FR-140 Security deposit of a residential lease](../../../10-country-packs/france/real-estate/cr-fr-140-security-deposit.md),
[CR-FR-141 Rent receipts](../../../10-country-packs/france/real-estate/cr-fr-141-rent-receipts.md),
[CR-FR-142 Yearly rent revision by the reference index](../../../10-country-packs/france/real-estate/cr-fr-142-rent-revision-irl.md),
[CR-FR-143 Rent caps and energy-inefficient homes](../../../10-country-packs/france/real-estate/cr-fr-143-rent-caps-and-energy-rules.md),
[CR-FR-144 Mandatory property diagnostics](../../../10-country-packs/france/real-estate/cr-fr-144-property-diagnostics.md).

## Why

See [D344](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d344-real-estate-sector-coverage.md).

## Examples

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| A French lease is signed | Deposit capped, index clause set |
