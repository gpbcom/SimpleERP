# ⚖️ CR-FR-050 Data protection by design and by default

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Roles and record rules show each person only the data their task needs, by default.

## Values

- Least privilege by default; personal fields listed in the record.

## Legal references

- [GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) art. 25.

## Effective dates

Since 2018-05-25.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f01-administration/regulatory.md)
- [UC-F1-06 Edit a role and its record rules](../../../02-functional/f01-administration/use-cases/04-edit-a-role/uc-f01-06-edit-a-role-and-its-record-rules.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: GDPR art. 25. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Up to 10 000 000 EUR or 2 % of turnover (GDPR art. 83(4)).

## Exceptions

None.

## Open questions

None.
