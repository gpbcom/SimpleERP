# ⚖️ CR-FR-053 No biometric voice prints

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Speakers are never identified by a voice print.

## Values

- No voice print built or kept.

## Legal references

- [GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) art. 9.

## Effective dates

Since 2018-05-25.

## Enforced by

- [BR-F4-02 No voice prints](../../../02-functional/f04-meetings/business-rules/br-f04-02-no-voice-prints.md)
- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f04-meetings/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: GDPR art. 9. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Up to 20 000 000 EUR or 4 % of turnover (GDPR art. 83(5)).

## Exceptions

None.

## Open questions

None.
