# ⚖️ CR-FR-283 Health data outside care

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

Data concerning health are personal data related to a person's physical or mental health that reveal information
about their health status ([GDPR, art. 4(15)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679),
read 2026-11-27); their processing is forbidden unless an exception of art. 9(2) applies, for a restaurant, a salon or
a sports club in practice the person's explicit consent for one or more specified purposes
([GDPR, art. 9(1) and (2)(a)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679), read
2026-11-27); consent can be shown, asked apart from other matters, withdrawn as easily as given, and is not a condition
of a service that does not need the data ([GDPR, art.
7](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679), read 2026-11-27). The data are limited
to what the purpose needs and kept no longer than it needs
([GDPR, art. 5(1)(c) and (e)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679), read
2026-11-27; [CR-FR-052](cr-fr-052-storage-limitation.md)), and the person can see or erase them
([CR-FR-051](cr-fr-051-access-and-erasure.md)). Health data of patients cared for by health professionals follow
[CR-FR-055](cr-fr-055-health-data-hosting.md).

## Values

- Health data (art. 4(15)): an allergy or diet that reveals a condition, skin and treatment notes, body measures and
  fitness notes, the result of a sports medical certificate.
- Ground: explicit consent for a specified purpose (art. 9(2)(a)), recorded with its date and purpose; a separate
  consent for each other purpose, such as a photo used in a post or an advert (art. 7(2)).
- Consent: demonstrable (art. 7(1)); withdrawable at any time, as easily as given (art. 7(3)); not a condition of a
  service that does not need it (art. 7(4)).
- Minimisation and storage (art. 5(1)(c), (e)): only what the purpose needs, for example the date, validity and
  fitness of a medical certificate rather than the certificate's medical content.
- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) Whether a salon's or a sports club's health notes must be hosted by a certified
  health data host (CR-FR-055) is not settled by the texts read: the hosting duty is written for care.
- SimpleERP enforces: health notes need the person's explicit consent for their purpose, are shown only to the staff
  who need them, and are erased on request or at the end of their period.

## Penalty

## Exceptions
