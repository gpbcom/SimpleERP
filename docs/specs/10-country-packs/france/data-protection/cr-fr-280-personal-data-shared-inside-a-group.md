# ⚖️ CR-FR-280 Personal data shared inside a group

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Companies of one group are separate controllers: each sharing of personal data between them needs a legal basis of
GDPR art. 6(1), and companies of a group may have a legitimate interest in transmitting personal data within the group
for internal administrative purposes, including clients' and employees' data
([GDPR, art. 6 and recital 48](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679), read
2026-11-27); companies that jointly determine the purposes and means are joint controllers and set their respective
duties in an arrangement whose essence is made available to the people concerned
([GDPR, art. 26](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679), read 2026-11-27); and the
people are told who receives their data ([GDPR, art. 13(1)(e)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679),
read 2026-11-27). A group company outside the EU follows [CR-FR-274](cr-fr-274-transfers-to-cloud-suites.md); a
legitimate interest is balanced as in [CR-FR-196](cr-fr-196-legitimate-interest-balancing.md).

## Values

- Legal basis of each sharing: one of GDPR art. 6(1)(a) to (f); for internal administrative purposes, the legitimate
  interest of recital 48, subject to the balance of art. 6(1)(f).
- Joint controllers (art. 26): an arrangement on who answers the people's rights and who gives the information of
  art. 13 and 14; its essence available to the people; the people may exercise their rights against each controller
  (art. 26(3)).
- Information (art. 13(1)(e)): the recipients or categories of recipients, the other group companies included.
- Recital 48 leaves the transfer rules unchanged for a group company outside the EU.
- SimpleERP enforces: master data shared across the group records its legal basis, the companies that receive it and,
  for joint controllers, the arrangement.

## Legal references
