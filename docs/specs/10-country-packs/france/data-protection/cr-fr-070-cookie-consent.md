# ⚖️ CR-FR-070 Consent before cookies and trackers

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Non-essential cookies and trackers are placed or read only after the visitor's consent, after clear information.

## Values

- Consent before any non-essential tracker; essential ones exempt.
- Refusing trackers is as easy as accepting them ([CNIL, lignes directrices et recommandation cookies](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/cookies-et-autres-traceurs/regles/cookies/lignes-directrices-modificatives-et-recommandation), read 2026-11-27).

## Legal references

- [CNIL guidance](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/professionnels): cookies and other trackers (guidelines of 2020-09-17).
- [Loi n° 78-17, art. 82](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000037813978), which transposes
  art. 5(3) of directive 2002/58/EC.

## Effective dates

Loi n° 78-17 art. 82 in its current version in force since 2019-06-01 (ordonnance n° 2018-1125) ([Loi n° 78-17, art. 82](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000037813978), read 2026-11-27);
the CNIL gave until the end of March 2021 to apply its 2020 guidelines ([CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/cookies-et-autres-traceurs/regles/cookies/lignes-directrices-modificatives-et-recommandation), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f11-online-shop/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Loi Informatique et Libertés art. 82; CNIL guidelines. A change flags this rule and warns the
administrators; the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Administrative fine up to 10 000 000 EUR or, for a company, 2 % of the worldwide yearly turnover, whichever is
higher, for a breach of the GDPR or of the law ([Loi n° 78-17, art. 20 IV 7°](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000045072444), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

Trackers strictly needed for the service.
