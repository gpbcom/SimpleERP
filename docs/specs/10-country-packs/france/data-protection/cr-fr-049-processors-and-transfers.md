# ⚖️ CR-FR-049 Processors and transfers outside the EU

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A processor (AI provider, IT provider, outside tool) acts under a contract, receives only what the task needs, and a
transfer outside the EU needs a legal safeguard; the administrator is warned before turning one on.

## Values

- Processor contract (GDPR art. 28).
- Transfers: adequacy decision or safeguards (GDPR art. 44 to 49).

## Legal references

- [GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) art. 28, 44 to 49.

## Effective dates

Since 2018-05-25.

## Enforced by

- [BR-F1-13 External data warning](../../../02-functional/f01-administration/business-rules/br-f01-13-external-data-warning.md)
- [BR-F1-15 Only what the request needs](../../../02-functional/f01-administration/business-rules/br-f01-15-only-what-the-request-needs.md)
- [UC-F1-10 Create a client company on the platform](../../../02-functional/f01-administration/use-cases/08-provider-and-client-companies/uc-f01-10-create-a-client-company-on-the-platform.md)
- [UC-F1-11 Enter a client company to support it](../../../02-functional/f01-administration/use-cases/08-provider-and-client-companies/uc-f01-11-enter-a-client-company-to-support-it.md)
- [BR-F3-17 Only allowed data leaves](../../../02-functional/f03-assistant/business-rules/br-f03-17-only-allowed-data-leaves.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: GDPR art. 28, 44 to 49. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected
rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Up to 20 000 000 EUR or 4 % of turnover for transfers (GDPR art. 83(5)).

## Exceptions

None.

## Open questions

None.
