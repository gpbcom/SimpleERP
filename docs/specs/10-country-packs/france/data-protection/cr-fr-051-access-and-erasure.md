# ⚖️ CR-FR-051 Access and erasure requests

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A person can get a copy of their data and have it erased, AI assistant conversations included, and a company's data can
be deleted on its own.

## Values

- Answer within one month.
- Erasure everywhere the data is, search indexes included.

## Legal references

- [GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) art. 12, 15, 17.

## Effective dates

Since 2018-05-25.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f03-assistant/regulatory.md)
- [UC-F3-08 Manage and share conversations](../../../02-functional/f03-assistant/use-cases/08-manage-and-share-conversations/uc-f03-08-manage-and-share-conversations.md)
- [Data isolation](../../../05-non-functional/data-isolation.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: GDPR art. 12 to 22. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule
ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Up to 20 000 000 EUR or 4 % of turnover (GDPR art. 83(5)).

## Exceptions

Legal retention duties (GDPR art. 17(3)).

## Open questions

None.
