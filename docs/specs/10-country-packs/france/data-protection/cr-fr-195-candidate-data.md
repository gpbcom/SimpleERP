# ⚖️ CR-FR-195 Candidates' personal data

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Candidates are told who processes their data, why, on which basis, for how long, their rights and any automated sorting;
the data of a candidate not hired is kept 2 years at most from the last contact, if they were told, unless they ask for
erasure.

## Values

- 2 years at most in the active base.
- Staff representatives (CSE) informed before a recruitment tool is used or changed.
- Forbidden questions: social security and bank details, family, wish for children, measurements unless the job needs
  them.
- A copy on simple request.

## Legal references

- GDPR art. 13, 15.

  [CNIL, recruitment in small companies](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/recrutement-et-donnees-personnelles-dans-les-tpepme-cinq-questions-incontournables-se-poser),
