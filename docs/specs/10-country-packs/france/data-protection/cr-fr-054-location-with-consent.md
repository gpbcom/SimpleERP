# ⚖️ CR-FR-054 Location only when needed and announced

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A person's location is used only for the stated purpose, announced, and off by default.

## Values

- Assistant: with consent, withdrawn at any time.
- Attendance: at check-in and check-out only.

## Legal references

- [GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) art. 5, 6, 7.
- [CNIL guidance](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/professionnels).

## Effective dates

Since 2018-05-25.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f03-assistant/regulatory.md)
- [UC-F3-09 Use the AI assistant on a phone](../../../02-functional/f03-assistant/use-cases/09-assistant-on-a-phone/uc-f03-09-use-the-assistant-on-a-phone.md)
- [BR-F9-06 Location only at check-in](../../../02-functional/f09-hr-and-projects/business-rules/br-f09-06-location-only-at-check-in.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: GDPR art. 5 to 7. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule
ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Up to 20 000 000 EUR or 4 % of turnover.

## Exceptions

None.

## Open questions

None.
