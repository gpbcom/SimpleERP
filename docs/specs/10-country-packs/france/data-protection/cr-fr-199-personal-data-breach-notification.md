# ⚖️ CR-FR-199 Notifying a personal data breach

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every personal data breach is documented; one that risks people's rights is notified to the CNIL as soon as possible,
within 72 hours if possible (reasons given when later, details completed afterwards); people are told when the risk is
high.

## Values

- Documented: nature, people and records concerned, consequences, measures.
- CNIL: 72 hours, staged notification possible.
- People: when the risk is high; in doubt, ask the CNIL.

## Legal references

- GDPR art. 33, 34.
- [CNIL, notifying a breach](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/notifier-une-violation-de-donnees-personnelles).

## Effective dates

GDPR in application since 2018-05-25 ([CNIL, Règlement général sur la protection des données : ce qui change pour les professionnels](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/comprendre-le-rgpd/reglement-general-sur-la-protection-des-donnees-ce-qui-change-pour-les-professionnels), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f20-hosted-support-assistant/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: GDPR art. 33. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Not on the page read; the CNIL may order people to be told.

## Exceptions

None.
