# ⚖️ CR-FR-048 Record of processing activities

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The company keeps a record of its processing activities, drafted from the settings and updated when they change.

## Values

- Purposes, legal bases, categories, retention, processors and transfers.

## Legal references

- [GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) art. 30.

## Effective dates

Since 2018-05-25.

## Enforced by

- [UC-F1-12 Draft the record of processing activities](../../../02-functional/f01-administration/use-cases/09-administration-reports/uc-f01-12-draft-the-record-of-processing-activities.md)
- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f02-no-code-configuration/regulatory.md)
- [UC-F2-01 Add a custom field to a record type](../../../02-functional/f02-no-code-configuration/use-cases/01-configure-a-record-type/uc-f02-01-add-a-custom-field-to-a-record-type.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: GDPR art. 30. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Up to 10 000 000 EUR or 2 % of turnover (GDPR art. 83(4)).

## Exceptions

Under 250 staff, unless the processing is regular or risky (GDPR art. 30(5)).

## Open questions

None.
