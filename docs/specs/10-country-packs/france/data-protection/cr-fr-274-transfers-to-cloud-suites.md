# ⚖️ CR-FR-274 Transfers outside the EU to a cloud suite

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Personal data go to a provider outside the EU only under the conditions of chapter V of the GDPR, onward transfers
included ([GDPR, art. 44](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679), read
2026-11-27): to a country, territory, sector or organisation the Commission has found adequate (art. 45), or with
appropriate safeguards, such as the Commission's standard contractual clauses, with enforceable rights and effective
remedies for the people concerned (art. 46)
([GDPR, art. 45 and 46](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679), read 2026-11-27);
and the people concerned are told of the intended transfer, of the adequacy decision or its absence, and of the
safeguards and how to get a copy ([GDPR, art.
13(1)(f)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679), read 2026-11-27). The processor
contract and the record are in [CR-FR-049](cr-fr-049-processors-and-transfers.md) and
[CR-FR-048](cr-fr-048-record-of-processing.md).

## Values

- United States: adequate only for organisations on the Data Privacy Framework List kept by the U.S. Department of
  Commerce ([Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795, art.
  1](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32023D1795), read 2026-11-27); in force since
  2023-07-10, no end date ([EUR-Lex, document information](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX:32023D1795),
  read 2026-11-27); the Commission reviews it and may suspend, amend or repeal it (art. 3).
- Standard contractual clauses: those of the annex to Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914, for a controller
  or processor in the EU to an importer outside the GDPR; they also cover the processor terms of GDPR art. 28(3) and
  (4) (art. 1); the former clauses ceased to protect from 2022-12-27 (art. 4)
  ([Decision (EU) 2021/914, art. 1 and 4](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32021D0914),
  read 2026-11-27).
- Other safeguards needing no authorisation: binding corporate rules, an approved code of conduct or certification
  with binding commitments (art. 46(2)); ad hoc clauses need the supervisory authority's authorisation (art. 46(3)).
- Information to the people concerned: the transfer, the adequacy decision or its absence, the safeguards and where to
  get them (art. 13(1)(f)).
- A Commission decision that a country is no longer adequate does not stop transfers made under art. 46 to 49 (art.
  45(7)).
- SimpleERP enforces: before connecting a suite, the administrator records the provider as a processor, the country
  where data go and the transfer ground (adequacy, the provider's Data Privacy Framework listing, or the standard
  clauses), and the privacy notice states it.

## Effective dates

The GDPR applies since 2018-05-25; Decision 2023/1795 since 2023-07-10, with no end date; Decision 2021/914 replaced
the former clauses from 2021-09-27 (texts above, read 2026-11-27).

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: GDPR chapter V and art. 13; Commission decisions 2023/1795 and 2021/914 and the list of
adequacy decisions (art. 45(8)). A change, such as the suspension of an adequacy decision, flags this rule and warns the
administrators; the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

As in [CR-FR-049](cr-fr-049-processors-and-transfers.md): up to 20 000 000 EUR or 4 % of turnover for transfers (GDPR
art. 83(5)).

## Exceptions

The derogations of GDPR art. 49 for specific situations, not read here.

None: the texts were read on 2026-11-27.
