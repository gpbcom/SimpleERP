# ⚖️ CR-FR-196 Legitimate interest needs a documented balance

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Processing personal data on legitimate interest needs a documented balance for each processing: a legitimate interest,
no less intrusive means, and people's rights not prevailing, given what they can reasonably expect.

## Values

- Three conditions, documented.
- Mitigation: an unconditional right to object.
- The interest is stated in the information given to people.

## Legal references

- GDPR art. 6(1)(f).
- [CNIL, legitimate interest](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/les-bases-legales/interet-legitime).
