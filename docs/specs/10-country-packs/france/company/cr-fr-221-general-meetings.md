# ⚖️ CR-FR-221 Shareholders' general meetings

**Status:** 🔄 (Proposed) values to read in the official source

## Statement

(Proposed) A general meeting is convened by a notice that respects a minimum delay, sets an agenda and sends the
documents; it decides only if its quorum is reached and each resolution passes its own majority (simple for ordinary
decisions, higher for changes of the statutes); its minutes are signed and kept, and some decisions are filed with the
register of companies.

## Values

- (Proposed) Notice delay and agenda.
- (Proposed) Quorum and majority by kind of resolution.
- (Proposed) Minutes and filings.
- (Proposed) simple-ERP enforces: votes count only within quorum and majority.
- Read on [Service-public F36714](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F36714) (verified 10 October
  2024), SARL only; SA and SAS are not read:
- Read: an annual approval meeting at least once a year, within 6 months of the close; the court can extend the
  deadline; late meetings stay valid; a manager who does not submit the accounts risks a 9 000 EUR fine.
- Read: notice by registered letter at least 15 days before, unless the articles set longer; documents also within 15
  days.
- Read: quorum for companies created after 4 August 2005: 1/4 of shares at first consultation, 1/5 at the second.
- Read: ordinary meeting, absolute majority at first consultation, relative majority at the second; extraordinary
  meeting, 2/3 of shares represented (post-2005 companies), 3/4 for older companies.
- Read: minutes after every assembly, signed by the manager(s) and chair, entered in the minutes register; a written
  consultation passes if the voting associates hold more than half of the shares.
- Read: Code de commerce L223-1 to L223-43, R225-24.
