# ⚖️ CR-FR-273 Valuations the law requires

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

Some valuations are made by a person the law names, not by the company: a contribution in kind to a société anonyme is
valued by one or more contribution auditors (commissaires aux apports), under their responsibility
([Code de commerce, art. L225-147](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000048539465), read
2026-11-27); in a SARL, the statutes value each contribution in kind on the report of a contribution auditor, unless
the partners waive it unanimously within the limits of the law ([art.
L223-9](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000033613555), read 2026-11-27); and where the law or
the statutes provide for the transfer or buy-back of a partner's shares without a set value, a contested value is fixed
by an expert ([Code civil, art. 1843-4](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000038790979), read
2026-11-27). A valuation made in SimpleERP is an internal estimate and prepares the inputs only.

## Values

- Société anonyme: contribution auditors appointed unanimously by the shareholders or by a court; they value the
  contributions in kind and special benefits; the meeting that approves them records the capital increase; shares paid
  in kind are fully paid up at issue (L225-147, version since 2024-01-01)
  ([Code de commerce, art. L225-147](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000048539465), read
  2026-11-27).
- SARL: each contribution in kind valued in the statutes on a contribution auditor's report; the partners may waive it
  unanimously when no contribution exceeds the amount a decree sets and the unaudited contributions total no more than
  half the capital; without an auditor, or with a value different from the auditor's, the partners are jointly liable
  to third parties for 5 years for the value given (L223-9, since 2016-12-11)
  ([Code de commerce, art. L223-9](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000033613555), read
  2026-11-27).
- Contested price of shares transferred or bought back: an expert appointed by the parties or, failing agreement, by
  the president of the court, who applies the valuation rules of the statutes or of any agreement between the parties
  (1843-4, for claims from 2020-01-01)
  ([Code civil, art. 1843-4](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000038790979), read 2026-11-27).
- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) The amount per contribution below which a SARL may waive the contribution
  auditor is set by a decree not read.
- SimpleERP enforces: a valuation shows its method and every assumption, is labelled an internal estimate, and names
  the professional the law requires when its purpose is one of the cases above.

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Code de commerce L223-9, L225-147; Code civil 1843-4. A change flags this rule and warns the
administrators; the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Without a contribution auditor, or with another value than the auditor's, the SARL partners are jointly liable to
third parties for 5 years for the value given to the contributions (L223-9).

## Exceptions

A sole partner who was a sole trader may contribute, without an auditor, items from the balance sheet of the last
financial year (L223-9).

## Open questions

None: the law was read on 2026-11-27; the waiver threshold is a lawyer item above.
