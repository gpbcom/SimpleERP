# ⚖️ CR-FR-262 Deliberations and the legality check

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The members of a municipal
council are convened in writing 3 clear days (under 3 500 inhabitants) or 5 clear days before the meeting, with the
agenda and, from 3 500 inhabitants, an explanatory note; the council deliberates only if the majority of its
members in office are present; deliberations are adopted by the absolute majority of votes cast; the list of the
deliberations is posted and published within one week (L2121-25); and the acts are enforceable once published or
notified and, for those the law lists, sent to the State representative (L2131-1) ([CGCT, art. L2121-10 to L2121-25](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000006180960), [art. L2131-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000044190563), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Delays and quorum, kept as data: 3 or 5 clear days, 1 clear day in urgency (L2121-11, L2121-12); quorum the
  majority of members in office, none needed when reconvened 3 days later (L2121-17); one proxy each, for at most 3
  sessions (L2121-20) ([CGCT, art. L2121-10 to L2121-25](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000006180960), read 2026-11-27).
- Sizes of bodies, kept as data: from 7 councillors under 100 inhabitants to 69 from 300 000, by population band
  (L2121-2, in force since 2014-03-23) ([CGCT, art. L2121-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000027433884), read 2026-11-27).
- Transmission to the prefecture of the acts listed in L2131-2, as a condition of their enforceability (L2131-1) ([art. L2131-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000044190563), read 2026-11-27).
- SimpleERP enforces: deliberations are sent to the prefecture to become enforceable.
- Read on the
  [DGCL page on the municipal council](https://www.collectivites-locales.gouv.fr/connaitre-les-acteurs-et-les-institutions/structures-territoriales/les-communes/le-conseil-municipal)
  (section dated September-October 2025); the legality check itself (the prefect's delay to refer an act to the
  administrative court) is not on the page:
- Read: convocation 3 clear days before (municipalities under 3 500 inhabitants) or 5 clear days (others); in urgent
  cases no less than 1 clear day, the council ruling on the urgency at the opening.
- Read: the convocation states every agenda item; municipalities of 3 500 or more attach an explanatory summary note.
- Read: quorum is the majority of members in office; if not reached, the council is reconvened at least 3 days later
  with no quorum needed.
- Read: decisions by absolute majority of votes cast; casting vote of the chair on a tie (except secret ballot); one
  proxy per councillor, valid for 3 consecutive sessions.
- Read: publication combined with transmission to the prefect makes the act enforceable; the prefect may ask for a
  council meeting within 30 days.
- Read: CGCT L. 2121-7 to L. 2121-25, L. 2121-27, L. 2121-27-1, L. 2131-1; D. 2121-12; R. 2131-1.

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Council deliberations. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected
rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

No fine: the prefect refers an act listed in L2131-2 that he finds illegal to the administrative court
within two months of its transmission, and may ask for its suspension, decided within one month; for urban
planning, public contracts and public-service delegations a request within ten days suspends the act until the
judge rules (L2131-6) ([CGCT, art. L2131-6](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000044190539), read 2026-11-27).
