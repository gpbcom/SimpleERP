# ⚖️ CR-FR-214 The share-movement register

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

A share transfer in an SAS is recorded in the share-movement register and the shareholder accounts, and becomes
enforceable against the company from that entry.

## Values

- Entry contents: date of the transfer, identity of seller and buyer, number of shares, nature of the operation,
  references of the shares.
- Enforceable against the company from the entry in the shareholder accounts.
- Tax registration of the transfer: within one month of signing the deed, or of the transfer when there is no deed.
- Registration duty: 0.1 % of the price, minimum 25 EUR; 5 % for a predominantly real-estate company; normally paid by
  the buyer unless agreed otherwise.

## Legal references

- [Service-public F36022](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F36022).
- Code de commerce L228-1 (transfer by account entry), R228-8 (registers of registered securities).
