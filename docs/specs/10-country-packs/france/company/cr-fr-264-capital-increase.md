# ⚖️ CR-FR-264 Capital increase decisions and subscriptions

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

In a société anonyme, only the extraordinary general meeting decides a capital increase, on the board's report, and may
delegate it to the board; the increase is carried out within 5 years
([Code de commerce, art. L225-129](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006225049), read
2026-11-27); shareholders have a preferential right to subscribe the new cash shares in proportion to their shares,
which each may waive ([art. L225-132](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000029329359), read
2026-11-27), during at least 5 trading days
([art. L225-141](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006225192), read 2026-11-27); each
subscriber signs a dated subscription form
([art. R225-128](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006261372), read 2026-11-27). In a société
par actions simplifiée the partners take that decision collectively, as the statutes provide
([art. L227-9](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000051322706), read 2026-11-27). A dilution
simulation is none of these acts and has no legal effect.

## Values

- Competent body: the extraordinary general meeting (SA), which may delegate to the board under L225-129-2; the
  partners collectively, under the statutes (SAS, L227-9)
  ([Code de commerce, art. L225-129](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006225049),
  [art. L227-9](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000051322706), read 2026-11-27).
- Time to carry out the increase: 5 years from the decision or delegation, except for rights attached to securities
  giving access to the capital, share options and free shares (L225-129).
- Preferential subscription right: in proportion to the shares held, transferable like the share, waivable by each
  shareholder; waived by the decision to issue securities giving access to the capital (L225-132)
  ([art. L225-132](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000029329359), read 2026-11-27).
- Subscription period: at least 5 trading days from the opening, closed early once every right is exercised or the
  increase is fully subscribed (L225-141)
  ([art. L225-141](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006225192), read 2026-11-27).
- Subscription form: dated and signed by the subscriber, the number of securities written in full, a copy given to the
  subscriber; it states the company name, form, capital, registered office, register mentions, the amount and terms of
  the increase, cash and contributions in kind, who receives the funds, the subscriber's identity and the number
  subscribed (R225-128) ([art. R225-128](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006261372), read
  2026-11-27).
- SimpleERP enforces: a simulation never changes the register and is labelled as having no legal weight.

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Code de commerce L225-129 to L225-141, L227-9, R225-128. A change flags this rule and warns
the administrators; the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

None stated in the articles read.

## Exceptions

The 5-year limit does not apply to increases from rights attached to securities, share options or free shares
(L225-129); the SARL and other forms follow their own articles, not read here.

None: the law was read on 2026-11-27.
