# ⚖️ CR-FR-215 BSPCE and free shares

**Status:** 🔄 (Proposed) values to read in the official source

## Statement

(Proposed) Share warrants for founders and employees (BSPCE) and free shares are allowed only under a plan approved by
the shareholders, within its ceiling and period, for the kinds of beneficiaries the plan names, and carry tax and
social-contribution consequences.

## Values

- (Proposed) The plan's ceiling, period and beneficiaries.
- (Proposed) Conditions of each instrument and its vesting.
- (Proposed) Tax and social-contribution treatment.
- (Proposed) SimpleERP enforces: grants follow the plan approved by shareholders.
- Read on
  [BOFiP BOI-RSA-ES-20-40-10](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/13155-PGP.html/identifiant=BOI-RSA-ES-20-40-10-20260528)
  (published 28 May 2026), BSPCE only; free shares, tax and social rates and the exercise delay are not read:
- Read: issuers are share companies only (SA, SAS, SCA, SE); SARL, SCS and SNC are excluded.
- Read: unlisted, or listed with a market capitalisation below 150 million EUR (60 days of opening prices before the
  grant); grants may continue for three years after crossing it.
- Read: registered in the trade register for less than 15 years on the grant date; subject to corporate income tax.
- Read: individuals hold directly and continuously at least 15 % of the capital, lowered from 25 % for grants from 1
  January 2026.
- Read: beneficiaries are employees, officers taxed as employees and board members; subsidiaries need at least 75 % held
  directly by the parent (percentages multiplied along a chain).
- Read: eligibility is assessed on the grant date; losing a condition stops new grants, earlier ones keep their
  treatment.
- Read: CGI art. 163 bis G (II, II bis, III bis), as amended by law 2026-103 of 19 February 2026, art. 25.
