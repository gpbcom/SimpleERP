# ⚖️ CR-FR-202 Price reductions on invoices

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A rebate, discount or refund earned at the date of the sale and directly linked to it is shown on the invoice, next to
each item's quantity, unit price excluding VAT and VAT rate.

## Values

- Reductions acquired at the sale.
- Early-payment discount stated, or "none".

## Legal references

- [Service-public F31808](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F31808).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-12, the date of this specification.
