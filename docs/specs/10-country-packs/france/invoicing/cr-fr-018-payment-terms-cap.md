# ⚖️ CR-FR-018 Payment terms cap

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Payment terms between businesses do not exceed 60 days from the invoice date, or 45 days end of month.

## Values

- At most 60 days from the invoice date, or 45 days end of month when agreed.
- Default: 30 days after delivery or service when nothing is agreed.

## Legal references

- [Code de commerce](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000005634379/) art. L441-10 I.
