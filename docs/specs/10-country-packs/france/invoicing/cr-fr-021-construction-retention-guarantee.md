# ⚖️ CR-FR-021 Construction retention guarantee

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

On construction works, the customer may hold back at most 5 % of each payment as a retention guarantee, released one
year after acceptance of the works or against a bank guarantee.

## Values

- Cap: 5 % of the contract price.
- Release: one year after acceptance, unless reservations are notified.

## Legal references

- Loi n° 71-584 du 16 juillet 1971.
