# ⚖️ CR-FR-174 Discounts and later rebates on invoices

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Reductions acquired at the date of sale appear on the invoice, with the early-payment and late-payment terms.

## Values

- "Early-payment discount: none" where none applies.
- The late-payment rate and the 40 EUR recovery indemnity for business customers.
- Four new mentions from 1 September 2026 (large companies and ETI) and 1 September 2027 (SMEs and micro-enterprises).

## Legal references

- [Service-public F31808](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F31808).
- Code de commerce L441-9.

## Effective dates

Since 2019-04-26: L441-9 ([Code de commerce, art. L441-9](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000038414397), read 2026-11-27). Until 2026-12-31, the L441-9 version of 2019-04-26; from 2027-01-01, the version amended by Ordonnance n° 2026-671
art. 2, with the same list of mentions and fines, its exemptions now referring to CIBS L216-28 and L216-33 ([Code de commerce, art. L441-9, version of 2027-01-01](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000038414397/2027-01-02), read 2026-11-27). The four new mentions from 2026-09-01 and
2027-09-01, by company size.
