# ⚖️ CR-FR-091 Public contracts: payment period and Chorus Pro

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every invoice of a public contract goes through Chorus Pro, and the public buyer pays within its legal period.

## Values

- 30 days: the State, public bodies, local authorities.
- 50 days: health establishments.
- 60 days: other public companies.
- Late-payment interest at the ECB rate plus 8 points (10.4 % from 1 July to 31 December 2026) and a 40 EUR recovery
  indemnity.

## Legal references

- [Service-public F23386](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F23386) and
  [R52176](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/R52176).
- Code de la commande publique L2192-1, R2192-10 and following.

## Effective dates

Start date not read; in force when read on 2026-11-12 (the page was verified on 25 June 2026).

## Penalty
