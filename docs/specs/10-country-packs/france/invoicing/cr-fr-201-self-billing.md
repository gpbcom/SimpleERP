# ⚖️ CR-FR-201 Self-billing

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A customer may draw up the invoice in its supplier's name only with the supplier's prior agreement (a billing mandate),
and the invoice then reads "Auto-facturation".

## Values

- Prior agreement.
- Mention "Auto-facturation".

## Legal references

- [Service-public F23208](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F23208),
  [F31808](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F31808).
