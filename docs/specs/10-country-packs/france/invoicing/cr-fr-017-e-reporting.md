# ⚖️ CR-FR-017 E-reporting of sales to consumers and abroad

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Sales to consumers (point of sale included) and international sales are reported to the tax administration as
transaction data, and payment data for services, on the e-invoicing timetable.

## Values

- Large and mid-size companies from 2026-09-01; small and micro companies from 2027-09-01.
- Transaction data (CGI art. 290) and payment data (CGI art. 290 A).

## Legal references

- [Code général des impôts](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006069577/) art. 290, 290 A.
- [BOFiP news of 30/09/2026 on e-reporting](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/15176-PGP.html).

## Effective dates

2026-09-01 and 2027-09-01, by company size. Until 2026-12-31, CGI art. 290 and 290 A apply; from 2027-01-01, the Code des impositions sur les biens et services, Book II, the transmissions to the tax administration (L216-47 and L216-48),
in force on that date ([Ordonnance n° 2025-1247](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000053091516), [art. 49](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000054587047) as amended by Ordonnance n° 2026-671, read 2026-11-27); on invoices,
contracts and acts that produce effects until 2028-06-30 (art. 46 II as
amended by Ordonnance n° 2026-671, [BOFiP, BOI-RES-TVA-000253 of 2026-10-07](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/14901-PGP.html/identifiant=BOI-RES-TVA-000253-20261007), read 2026-11-27), a reference to the CGI is read as one to the CIBS
article that takes it over ([Ordonnance n° 2025-1247](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000053091516), [art. 46](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/article_jo/JORFARTI000053091753), read 2026-11-27).
From 2027-01-01 the CGI rules on the electronic transmission of data (art. 290, 290 A) stay
applicable until the regulatory part of the CIBS takes them over ([BOFiP, BOI-RES-TVA-000253 of 2026-10-07](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/14901-PGP.html/identifiant=BOI-RES-TVA-000253-20261007), 2.3 and 3.1, read 2026-11-27).

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CGI art. 290, 290 A, 256 C. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the
corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

From 2026-02-21 to 2026-12-31: 500 EUR per missing transmission of the transaction data (art. 290)
or the payment data (art. 290 A), at most 15 000 EUR a calendar year (CGI 1788 D I, II) ([CGI, art. 1788 D](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053546693), read 2026-11-27). From 2027-01-01: 250 EUR per transmission for a
breach of CIBS L216-47 or L216-48, at most 15 000 EUR a year; 750 EUR per transmission for an intermediary
platform, at most 45 000 EUR a year ([CGI, art. 1788 D, version of 2027-01-01](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053546693/2027-01-02), read 2026-11-27). In both versions, no fine for a first breach
corrected on the company's own initiative or within 30 days.

## Exceptions

None.
