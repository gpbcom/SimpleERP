# ⚖️ CR-FR-017 E-reporting of sales to consumers and abroad

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Sales to consumers (point of sale included) and international sales are reported to the tax administration as
transaction data, and payment data for services, on the e-invoicing timetable.

## Values

- Large and mid-size companies from 2026-09-01; small and micro companies from 2027-09-01.
- Transaction data (CGI art. 290) and payment data (CGI art. 290 A).

## Legal references

- [Code général des impôts](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006069577/) art. 290, 290 A.
- [BOFiP news of 30/09/2026 on e-reporting](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/15176-PGP.html).

## Effective dates

2026-09-01 and 2027-09-01, by company size.
