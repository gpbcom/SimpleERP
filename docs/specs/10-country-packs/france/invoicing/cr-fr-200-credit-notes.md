# ⚖️ CR-FR-200 Credit notes and corrective invoices

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An invoice is never changed once paid: it is corrected or cancelled by a credit note, numbered in the same continuous
sequence; before payment, a corrective invoice reads "cancels and replaces invoice no. ..." with a new number.

## Values

- Credit note: its own number, same sequence.
- Corrective invoice before payment.

## Legal references

- CGI annexe II art. 242 nonies A.
- [Service-public F23208](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F23208).

## Effective dates

Since 2025-01-01: 242 nonies A ([CGI annex II, art. 242 nonies A](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000050811276), read 2026-11-27).
