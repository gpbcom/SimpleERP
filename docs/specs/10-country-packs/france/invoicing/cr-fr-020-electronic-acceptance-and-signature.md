# ⚖️ CR-FR-020 Proof of acceptance and e-signature

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An accepted quote or a signed document keeps the proof of who accepted it, when, and which version.

## Values

- Click acceptance: name, date, time, IP address, exact version, "bon pour accord"; the version is frozen.
- E-signature: simple or advanced, with its proof file.

## Legal references

- [eIDAS, Regulation (EU) 910/2014](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/910/oj).
- [Code civil](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006070721/) art. 1366, 1367.

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.
