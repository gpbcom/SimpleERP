# ⚖️ CR-FR-014 Invoice numbering

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Invoices and credit notes carry a unique number from one continuous sequence, or from series per site; an issued invoice
is frozen and only a credit note corrects it.

## Values

- One sequence per company, or one series per site, chosen before the first invoice of the fiscal year.
- No gap, no duplicate, no deletion.

## Legal references

- CGI annex II art. 242 nonies A
  ([Code général des impôts](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006069577/)).

## Effective dates

Since 2025-01-01: 242 nonies A ([CGI annex II, art. 242 nonies A](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000050811276), read 2026-11-27).
