# ⚖️ CR-FR-125 Association accounting

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Associations that keep accounts use the association chart of accounts, ANC regulation 2018-06, in force since
2020-01-01.

## Values

- The 1999 chart repealed on 2019-12-31.
- Valuing volunteer time is optional.

## Legal references

- ANC regulation 2018-06.
- [Accounting rules](https://www.associations.gouv.fr/le-plan-comptable-applicable-depuis-le-1er-janvier-2020).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc08-non-profit/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: ANC 2018-06. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Not stated in the sources read.

## Exceptions

None.
