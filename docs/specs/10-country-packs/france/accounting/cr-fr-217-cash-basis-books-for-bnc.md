# ⚖️ CR-FR-217 Cash-basis books for BNC professions

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A liberal professional under the controlled-declaration regime keeps a chronological book of receipts and payments and a
register of fixed assets.

## Values

- Journal: day-by-day detail of professional receipts and payments; for each fee, the client's identity, amount, date
  and method of payment.
- The journal may be numbered and initialled by the clerk of the commercial court; an electronic version is allowed if
  identified, numbered and dated by means guaranteeing authenticity.
- Fixed-asset register: acquisition or creation date, cost price, depreciation made, disposal price and date.
- Retention: journal 6 years from the close of the financial year; supporting documents 6 years from their creation;
  fixed-asset register 6 years from the last operation; up to 10 years if the tax office suspects concealed activity.

## Penalty

## Exceptions

(Proposed) Professions that opt for accrual accounting.
