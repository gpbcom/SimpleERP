# ⚖️ CR-FR-213 When a statutory auditor is required

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A company must appoint a statutory auditor when it crosses two of three size thresholds; the duty starts the financial
year after the thresholds are crossed.

## Values

- Rule: two of three thresholds exceeded.
- SARL, EURL, SA, SAS, SCA, SNC, SCS and parents of small groups: balance sheet 5 000 000 EUR, turnover excluding VAT 10
  000 000 EUR, 50 employees.
- Subsidiaries controlled by a parent of a small group: 2 500 000 EUR, 5 000 000 EUR, 25 employees.
- Civil companies carrying on an economic activity: 1 550 000 EUR, 3 100 000 EUR, 50 employees.
- Private training organisations: 230 000 EUR, 153 000 EUR, 3 employees.
- Start: not required for the financial year in which a threshold is crossed; applies from the following year.
- End: not required if below the thresholds at the close of the two years before the mandate expires.
- Mandate: 6 years (renewable), 3 years if voluntary; the full mandate is served even if the company falls below.
- Two auditors if the company prepares consolidated accounts.
- Partners holding at least 1/3 of the capital can appoint one; partners holding at least 10 % can ask the court to
  appoint one even below the thresholds.
- Appointed by the ordinary general meeting (SARL, SA, SCA, SNC, SCS), the sole partner (EURL) or the collective
  decision of the partners on the president's proposal (SAS).

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Statutory audit thresholds and the auditor's rights. A change flags this rule and warns the
administrators; the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Failing to appoint a mandatory auditor: 2 years' imprisonment and a 30 000 EUR fine (as the page states).

## Exceptions

Below the thresholds no auditor is required; associations and foundations are not covered by the page read.
