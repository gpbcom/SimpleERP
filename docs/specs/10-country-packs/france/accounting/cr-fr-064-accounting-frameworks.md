# ⚖️ CR-FR-064 Accounting frameworks of the books

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A French company keeps its statutory accounts under French GAAP; a group that consolidates may use IFRS, which listed
groups must use for their consolidated accounts.

## Values

- Statutory accounts: Plan comptable général.
- Consolidated accounts: French consolidation rules or IFRS; IFRS required for groups listed on an EU regulated market.

## Legal references

- [Code de commerce](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000005634379/) art. L123-12 and L233-24.
- Regulation (EC) 1606/2002 (IFRS).

## Effective dates

Since 2016-01-01: L123-12 in force since 2000-09-21, L233-24 since 2016-01-01 ([Code de commerce, art. L123-12](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006219304), [art. L233-24](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000030927225), read 2026-11-27).
