# ⚖️ CR-FR-250 Public accounting M57

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

A local body votes its budget by
15 April (L1612-2), after a report on its budget orientations debated within the ten weeks before (L1612-26), each
section (operation, investment) balanced (L1612-4, L1612-22); the budget documents are sent digitally to the State
representative (L1612-40), who checks their legality; the mayor keeps the commitment accounts (L1612-38), and
the accountant pays only within the credits opened (L1612-39) ([CGCT, art. L1612-21 to L1612-41](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGIARTI000051731808/2026-01-01), read 2026-11-27). M57 is mandatory for
the bodies the law lists and optional for the other local bodies ([DGCL, Le référentiel M57 en pratique](https://www.collectivites-locales.gouv.fr/gerer-les-finances-publiques-locales/budget/instructions-budgetaires-et-comptables/le-referentiel-m57/le-referentiel-m57-en-pratique), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Budget vote date: 15 April, 30 April in the year the assemblies are renewed (L1612-2); each section, operation
  and investment, voted in balance, with sincere estimates and the loan principal of the year covered by own
  resources (L1612-4) ([CGCT, art. L1612-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000051731704), [art. L1612-4](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000051731697), read 2026-11-27).
- Commitment accounts: kept by the mayor or the president, under a joint ministerial order, since the budget
  year 2026 (L1612-38, created by Ordonnance n° 2025-526, which repealed L2342-2); payment only within the credits
  opened (L1612-39) ([CGCT, art. L1612-21 to L1612-41](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGIARTI000051731808/2026-01-01), read 2026-11-27).
- Dates and sizes kept as data.
- SimpleERP enforces: expenditure stays within voted credits.
- Read on Legifrance, [CGCT L1612-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000051731704) and
  [CGCT L2312-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000051731867) (both in force since 1 January
  2026, read 15 November 2026); the M57 framework itself (an instruction), the balance of each section (L1612-4) and the
  commitment-before-order rule are not read:
- Read: L1612-2, budget not adopted before 15 April of the year (30 April in the year of renewal of the deliberating
  bodies): the State representative refers it without delay to the regional audit chamber, which proposes within one
  month, by public opinion, how to settle the budget; the State representative then sets the budget and makes it
  enforceable, with a stated reason if he departs from the chamber's proposals.
- Read: L1612-2, from the referral until the budget is set, the assembly cannot pass any deliberation on the budget of
  the current year.
- Read: L1612-2, not applicable if the information needed to prepare the budget was not sent before 31 March; the
  assembly then has 15 days from receiving it.
- Read: L2312-1, the commune's report (for L1612-26) is sent to the State representative, published and debated in the
  municipal council; not applicable to communes under 3,500 inhabitants; in communes over 10,000 it also presents the
  structure and trend of spending and staff.
- Read: L1612-2 since 1 January 2013 (law 2012-1510 of 29 December 2012, art. 37 II D); L2312-1 applies from budget year
  2026 (ordinance 2025-526 of 12 June 2025, art. 1); the referral and the setting of the budget are the consequence of a
  late budget, with no fine on the page.
