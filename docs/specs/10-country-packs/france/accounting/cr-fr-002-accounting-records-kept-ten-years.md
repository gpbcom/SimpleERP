# ⚖️ CR-FR-002 Accounting records kept 10 years

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Accounting documents and their supporting documents (invoices, credit notes, entries, stock moves, purchase and sale
documents) are kept 10 years.

## Values

- Retention: 10 years from the end of the fiscal year they belong to.

## Legal references

- [Code de commerce](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000005634379/) art. L123-22.

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CCom L123-22. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

None stated in the sources read.

## Exceptions

Personal data in those documents is not kept longer than the documents themselves.
