# ⚖️ CR-FR-007 Annual stock count

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A full physical count of stock is made at least once per fiscal year.

## Values

- At least one full count per fiscal year; cycle counts may add to it.

## Legal references

- [Code de commerce](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000005634379/) art. L123-12.

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.
