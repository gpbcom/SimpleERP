# ⚖️ CR-FR-004 Balanced entries, continuous numbers

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every entry is balanced and each journal numbers its entries continuously within the fiscal year.

## Values

- Debit equals credit for every entry.
- One continuous sequence per journal and fiscal year, no gap.

## Legal references

- [Code de commerce](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000005634379/) art. L123-12 to L123-28.
- Plan comptable général (règlement ANC 2014-03).
