# ⚖️ CR-FR-081 Production cost of manufactured stock

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Manufactured stock is valued at its production cost: the costs directly linked to the units produced,
and a systematic allocation of the fixed and variable production overheads, the fixed ones on the normal
capacity, the unallocated part expensed (213-32); losses and wastage are excluded (213-30); administrative costs
are excluded, except those of dedicated structures (213-31); storage and selling costs are not named in
213-30 to 213-32 ([ANC, PCG consolidated at 1 January 2026](https://www.anc.gouv.fr/files/anc/files/1_Normes_fran%C3%A7aises/Reglements/Recueils/PCG_janvier2026/PCG--1er-janvier-2026.pdf), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Costs included and excluded as in the statement (PCG art. 213-30 to 213-32) ([ANC, PCG consolidated at 1 January 2026](https://www.anc.gouv.fr/files/anc/files/1_Normes_fran%C3%A7aises/Reglements/Recueils/PCG_janvier2026/PCG--1er-janvier-2026.pdf), read 2026-11-27).

## Legal references

- Plan comptable général (ANC regulation 2014-03) art. 213-30 to 213-35, consolidated version
  of 1 January 2026 ([ANC, PCG consolidated at 1 January 2026](https://www.anc.gouv.fr/files/anc/files/1_Normes_fran%C3%A7aises/Reglements/Recueils/PCG_janvier2026/PCG--1er-janvier-2026.pdf)).

## Effective dates

ANC regulation 2014-03 has no first financial year of its own: it applies from 2014-10-16, the day after the
homologating arrêté was published ([Arrêté of 8 September 2014](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000029583307), [ANC regulation 2014-03, art. 1 and 2](https://www.anc.gouv.fr/files/anc/files/1_Normes_fran%C3%A7aises/Reglements/2014/Reglt2014-03/Reglt%202014-03_Plan%20comptable%20general.pdf), read 2026-11-27); the consolidated version of
1 January 2026 gives no date per article ([ANC, PCG consolidated at 1 January 2026](https://www.anc.gouv.fr/files/anc/files/1_Normes_fran%C3%A7aises/Reglements/Recueils/PCG_janvier2026/PCG--1er-janvier-2026.pdf), read 2026-11-27).
