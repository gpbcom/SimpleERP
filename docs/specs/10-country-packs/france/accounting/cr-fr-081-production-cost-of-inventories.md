# ⚖️ CR-FR-081 Production cost of manufactured stock

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

(Proposed) Manufactured stock is valued at its production cost: materials and the direct and attributable indirect
production costs; abnormal waste, general administration, storage and selling costs are excluded.

## Values

- (Proposed) Costs included and excluded as stated.
