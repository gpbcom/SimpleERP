# ⚖️ CR-FR-006 French chart of accounts

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

French companies keep their books on the Plan comptable général, which the pack installs as the starting chart of
accounts, journals and taxes.

## Values

- Chart: PCG classes 1 to 8, with the standard accounts.
- Journals and tax accounts of the French set-up.

## Legal references

- Plan comptable général (règlement ANC 2014-03, as amended).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [F8 Accounting](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/README.md)
- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/regulatory.md)
- [UC-F8-01 Set up the books](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/use-cases/01-set-up-and-post/uc-f08-01-set-up-the-books.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Règlement ANC 2014-03. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected
rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

None stated in the sources read.

## Exceptions

Associations and other bodies use the ANC regulation that applies to them (sector packs).
