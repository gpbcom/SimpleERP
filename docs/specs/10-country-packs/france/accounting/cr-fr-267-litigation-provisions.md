# ⚖️ CR-FR-267 Provisions for litigation

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The annual accounts follow the prudence principle: the necessary provisions are made even without a profit, and
liabilities born in the year or before are taken into account even when known between the closing date and the date the
accounts are drawn up ([Code de commerce, art.
L123-20](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000030927181), read 2026-11-27). Under French GAAP, a
dispute the company will probably or certainly lose, existing at the closing date and that can be measured reliably, is
recognised as a provision at the best estimate of the outflow; a possible loss is a contingent liability, not recognised
but disclosed in the notes ([ANC regulation 2014-03, art. 321-5, 321-6, 322-1 to 322-5 and
323-2](https://www.anc.gouv.fr/files/anc/files/1_Normes_fran%C3%A7aises/Reglements/2014/Reglt2014-03/Reglt%202014-03_Plan%20comptable%20general.pdf),
consolidated version of 2026-01-01, read 2026-11-27). The accounting framework of each book is in
[CR-FR-064](cr-fr-064-accounting-frameworks.md); the tax deduction of the provision is in
[CR-FR-268](../tax-and-vat/cr-fr-268-tax-deduction-of-provisions.md).

## Values

- A provision is a liability whose timing or amount is not set precisely (321-5).
- Recognised when, at the closing date, the obligation exists and it is probable or certain, at the date the accounts
  are drawn up, that it will cause an outflow with no equivalent return (322-1, 322-2); even without a profit or with too
  little (322-3); not recognised in the exceptional case where the amount cannot be measured reliably (322-4).
- Contingent liability: a possible obligation whose existence depends on uncertain future events, or an obligation whose
  outflow is not probable; not recognised, mentioned in the notes (321-6, 322-5).
- Measured at the best estimate of the outflow needed to settle the obligation; for a single obligation with several
  outcomes, in general the most likely one, the uncertainty of the others being mentioned in the notes (323-2); only the
  costs that directly settle the obligation count (323-3); before income tax (323-4).
- Accounts: 1511 Provisions for litigation; the charge through 6815 (operating) and the reversal through 7815
  ([ANC regulation 2014-03, chart of accounts](https://www.anc.gouv.fr/files/anc/files/1_Normes_fran%C3%A7aises/Reglements/2014/Reglt2014-03/Reglt%202014-03_Plan%20comptable%20general.pdf),
  read 2026-11-27).
- SimpleERP enforces: a probable loss with an estimate gets a provision proposal that accounting approves and posts; a
  possible loss is listed for the notes.
