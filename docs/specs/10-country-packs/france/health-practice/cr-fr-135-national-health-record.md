# ⚖️ CR-FR-135 The national health record (Mon espace santé)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Practitioners add the listed documents to the patient's national health record, and the attending doctor a summary at
least once a year, unless the patient objected.

## Values

- Documents: lab reports, operation reports, prescriptions, letters to other professionals.
- Summary by the attending doctor at least yearly.
- Opened 6 weeks after the letter unless the patient objects.

## Legal references

- CSP L1111-15, R1111-27, R1111-40 to R1111-52; arrêté du 26 avril 2022.
- [Service-public F36151](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F36151).

## Effective dates

In force in its current version since 2024-01-01 ([Code de la santé publique, art. L1111-15](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000043908072), read 2026-11-27); it sets the yearly summary by the attending doctor.
