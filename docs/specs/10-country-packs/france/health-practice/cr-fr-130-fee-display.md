# ⚖️ CR-FR-130 Display of fees in a practice

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A practice displays its consultation and act fees in the waiting room, states its fee sector, and gives written
information before fees of 70 EUR or more above the insurance tariff.

## Values

- Waiting-room display.
- Written information from 70 EUR above the tariff.
- No extra fees for patients on complementary solidarity health insurance, except on their own request.

## Legal references

- CSP L1111-1 to L1111-9, R4127-53; arrêté du 30 mai 2018.
- [Service-public F19948](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F19948).

## Effective dates

In force in its current version since 2023-12-31 ([Code de la santé publique, art. L1111-3-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000046812436), read 2026-11-27); the display and the prior quote are set there.
