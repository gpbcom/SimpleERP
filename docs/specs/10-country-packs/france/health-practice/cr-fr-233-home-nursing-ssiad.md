# ⚖️ CR-FR-233 Home nursing services (SSIAD)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A home autonomy service gives nursing care at home on a medical prescription, which the coordinating nurse assesses;
nursing assistants work under the coordination of that nurse; the services and how they are delivered are set in the
individual care document (DIPEC); the care is funded by a yearly global allowance from the ARS ([CASF, annex 3-0, 3.3,
3.4.3 and 4.2.3.1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000047851019), in force since 2023-07-17,
read 2026-11-27; CASF D312-3, D312-5, L314-2-1).

## Values

- The coordinating nurse assesses the care on the basis of the medical prescription, and a person without one is sent to
  a professional who can prescribe ([CASF, annex 3-0,
  3.3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000047851019), read 2026-11-27); no text requires the
  prescription to state the acts and the period. Read: the service gives nursing care on a medical prescription or its
  renewal, and keeps for each person a record of the periods, prescriptions and therapeutic indications ([Code de
  l'action sociale et des familles, art.
  D312-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006074069/LEGISCTA000006198642/), in force since
  2023-07-17, read 2026-11-27); the acts and the period on the prescription are not stated there.
- Coordination by a nurse: the service includes a coordinating nurse for the nursing assistants
  ([Code de l'action sociale et des familles, art. D312-5](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006074069/LEGISCTA000006198642/),
  in force since 2023-07-17, read 2026-11-27).
- The care activity is funded by a yearly global care allowance paid by the director general of the ARS, not billed per
  act ([Code de l'action sociale et des familles, art. L314-2-1,
  II](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000046803484), in force since 2023-06-30, read
  2026-11-27).
- SimpleERP enforces: care acts need a prescription.
- Read on [Service-public F246](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F246) (verified 2 November
  2026); prescription, health-insurance funding and ARS authorisation are not on the page:
- Read: SSIAD (home nursing services) have been replaced since 2023 by home autonomy services (SAD), open to older,
  disabled or chronically ill people.
- Read: eligible are people of at least 60 who are ill or losing autonomy, disabled people, and people under 60 with a
  chronic disease or a long-term illness.
- Read: possible aids are PCH, APA, home help, tax credits and mutuelle aid; the person asks the service for an
  assessment and a quote; the page mentions no medical prescription and no specific authorisation.
- Read: Code de l'action sociale et des familles D312-1 to D312-5.

## Legal references

- Code de l'action sociale et des familles D312-1 to D312-5, L314-2-1, annex 3-0; décret n° 2023-608.

## Effective dates

Home autonomy services (CASF D312-1) in their current version since 2024-01-01
([Code de l'action sociale et des familles, art. D312-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000047852078),
décret n° 2023-608 du 13 juillet 2023, read 2026-11-27).

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Home nursing services. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected
rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

No penalty specific to care without a prescription. Under the general control of social and medico-social services, the
authority orders the manager to remedy a breach within a set time; if not done, a daily penalty of at most 1 000 EUR, a
ban of up to 3 years on new authorisations, a financial sanction of at most 5 % of the turnover in the field (100 000
EUR when there is none) and a provisional administrator (L313-14, since 2022-12-25); the activity may be suspended or
stopped when the health, safety or well-being of the people is threatened (L313-16, since 2018-01-19) ([Code de l'action
sociale et des familles, art. L313-13 to
L313-16](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006074069/LEGISCTA000006174403/), read 2026-11-27).
