# ⚖️ CR-FR-233 Home nursing services (SSIAD)

**Status:** 🔄 (Proposed) values to read in the official source

## Statement

(Proposed) A home nursing service gives care at home on a doctor's prescription; the prescription states the acts and
the period, nursing assistants work under the coordination of a nurse, and the care is paid by the health insurance.

## Values

- (Proposed) Prescription with acts and period.
- (Proposed) Coordination by a nurse.
- (Proposed) Billing to the health insurance.
- (Proposed) simple-ERP enforces: care acts need a prescription.
- Read on [Service-public F246](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F246) (verified 2 November
  2026); prescription, health-insurance funding and ARS authorisation are not on the page:
- Read: SSIAD (home nursing services) have been replaced since 2023 by home autonomy services (SAD), open to older,
  disabled or chronically ill people.
- Read: eligible are people of at least 60 who are ill or losing autonomy, disabled people, and people under 60 with a
  chronic disease or a long-term illness.
- Read: possible aids are PCH, APA, home help, tax credits and mutuelle aid; the person asks the service for an
  assessment and a quote; the page mentions no medical prescription and no specific authorisation.
- Read: Code de l'action sociale et des familles D312-1 to D312-5.
