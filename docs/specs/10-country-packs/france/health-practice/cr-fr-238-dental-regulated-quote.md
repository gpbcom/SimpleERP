# ⚖️ CR-FR-238 Regulated dental quote

**Status:** 🔄 (Proposed) values to read in the official source

## Statement

(Proposed) Before prosthetic care the dentist gives the patient a written quote that states each act, its price, the
amount the health insurance and the complementary cover cover, and the remaining cost, and the patient accepts it before
the care. Some prosthetic acts belong to a package with no remaining cost, with price caps.

## Values

- (Proposed) Quote content and acceptance before care.
- (Proposed) Packages and price caps, kept as data.
- (Proposed) simple-ERP enforces: prosthetics need the regulated quote before care.
- Read on Legifrance,
  [Code de la sante publique L1111-3-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000046812436) (in force
  since 31 December 2023, read 15 November 2026); the article is general to all liberal professionals and does not name
  dentists, prosthetic care, the 100 % sante baskets, price caps, the amount above which the quote is due or patient
  acceptance before care:
- Read: fee information is given by display in the waiting areas and by a prior quote above a certain amount.
- Read: for a made-to-measure device (Regulation (EU) 2017/745, article 1) the standard quote shows separately the
  selling price of each product and each service, the matching reimbursement base, and where relevant the excess charged
  and the amount covered by the health insurance bodies.
- Read: the professional also hands over the documents that guarantee the traceability and safety of the materials used.
- Read: in force on 31 December 2023 at the latest, on a date set by decree (law 2022-1616 of 23 December 2022, art.
  53); no penalty is on the page; the dental-specific articles and the decree were not located.
