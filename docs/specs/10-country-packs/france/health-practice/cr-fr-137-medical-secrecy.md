# ⚖️ CR-FR-137 Professional secrecy

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Everything a health professional knows about a patient is secret; members of the care team may share it, others only
with the patient's consent.

## Values

- General and absolute secret.
- Care team sharing; outside it, consent.

## Legal references

- CSP L1110-4, R4127-4; Code pénal 226-13, 226-14.
- [Service-public F34302](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F34302).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Penalty

Up to 1 year and 15 000 EUR.
