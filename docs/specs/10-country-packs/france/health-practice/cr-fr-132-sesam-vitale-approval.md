# ⚖️ CR-FR-132 SESAM-Vitale approval of billing software

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Software that bills health insurance electronically (electronic care sheets) is approved after testing; since 2026-11-02
the CNDA is the single entry point under one approval contract.

## Values

- One approval contract since 2026-11-02.
- Specifications: addendum 8 (April 2025).

## Legal references

- [SESAM-Vitale: care sheets](https://www.sesam-vitale.fr/web/sesam-vitale/fse-et-dre-epsl),

## Effective dates
