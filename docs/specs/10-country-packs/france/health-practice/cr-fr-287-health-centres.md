# ⚖️ CR-FR-287 Health centres (centres de santé)

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

A health centre gives local primary care without accommodation, mainly services refunded by health insurance, open to
everyone ([Code de la santé publique, art.
L6323-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006072665/LEGISCTA000006171586), read 2026-11-27);
it is run by a non-profit body, a local authority, a public or private health establishment or a cooperative, and
distributes no profit (L6323-1-3, L6323-1-4); its professionals are salaried (L6323-1-5); it applies third-party payment
and charges no fee above the agreed tariffs (L6323-1-7); it draws up a health project and operating rules (L6323-1-10)
and, before opening, gives the regional health agency the project and a commitment of conformity, whose receipt
authorises care to insured people (L6323-1-11); and its manager sends the agency each year the information on its
activities and running (L6323-1-13) ([Code de la santé publique, art. L6323-1 to
L6323-1-15](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006072665/LEGISCTA000006171586), read
2026-11-27). Third-party payment is in [CR-FR-133](cr-fr-133-third-party-payment.md).

## Values

- Managers allowed (L6323-1-3): non-profit bodies, départements, communes and their groupings, public health
  establishments, managers of private health establishments, cooperatives of collective interest.
- No profit distributed: reserved or reinvested in the centre or another centre or non-profit structure of the same
  manager (L6323-1-4).
- Salaried professionals; volunteers may take part (L6323-1-5 I); the identity and functions of every doctor and
  dentist shown in the premises and online, and the professional identifiable from the booking (L6323-1-5 III).
- Third-party payment and no excess fees; full payment for care not yet given may not be required (L6323-1-7).
- Health project and operating rules (L6323-1-10); commitment of conformity before opening, its receipt authorising
  care (L6323-1-11 I); dental and ophthalmology activities need the agency's approval, provisional for one year
  (L6323-1-11 II and III).
- Yearly information to the regional health agency on activities and running, as an order of the health minister sets
  (L6323-1-13).
- No advertising for the centre or its services (L6323-1-9).
- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) The content of the yearly information, of the health project and of the
  commitment of conformity is set by orders not read.
- SimpleERP enforces: activity is reported to the regional health agency each year, and third-party payment is
  applied to every act.

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Code de la santé publique L6323-1 to L6323-1-15. A change flags this rule and warns the
administrators; the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

For a failing affecting care quality or safety, a missing commitment of conformity or a breach of the rules on health
centres, after notice and injunction: an administrative fine of up to 500 000 EUR and a daily penalty of up to
5 000 EUR, suspension or closure (L6323-1-12)
([Code de la santé publique, art. L6323-1-12](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006072665/LEGISCTA000006171586),
read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

A centre may give diagnosis only (L6323-1); sexual-health centres open after a call for interest (L6323-1-14-1).
