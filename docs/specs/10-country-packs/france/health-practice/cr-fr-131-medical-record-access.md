# ⚖️ CR-FR-131 Patients' access to their medical record

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A patient gets their record at the latest 8 days after asking, and not before 48 hours; 2 months when the information is
over 5 years old; no retention period is set by law for private practice.

## Values

- 8 days, or 2 months for information over 5 years old; at least 48 hours.
- Retention: none set for private practice; the 20 years after the last visit of health establishments is advised.

## Legal references

- CSP L1110-1 to L1110-13, R1111-1 to R1111-7; arrêté du 5 mars 2004.
- [Service-public F12210](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F12210).
