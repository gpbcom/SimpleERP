# ⚖️ CR-FR-133 Third-party payment (tiers payant)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Patients on complementary solidarity health insurance, state medical aid, maternity, work accidents, long-term illness,
abortion, contraception under 26 and some others pay nothing upfront; the practitioner may offer it to anyone on the
compulsory part.

## Values

- Automatic cases as listed.

## Legal references

- CSS L162-1-12, L162-1-21, L432-1, L861-3.
- [Service-public F167](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F167).

## Effective dates

In force in its current version since 2023-12-28 ([Code de la sécurité sociale, art. L162-1-21](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000048703201), read 2026-11-27) (maternity, long-term illness, abortion).
