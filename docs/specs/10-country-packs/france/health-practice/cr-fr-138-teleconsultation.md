# ⚖️ CR-FR-138 Teleconsultation

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A teleconsultation is reimbursed within the care pathway, by video or phone, from a practitioner of the territory,
alternating with visits; its report goes to the patient's record.

## Values

- Exceptions: direct-access specialists, under 16, emergencies, no attending doctor, detainees, care-home residents.
- Sick leave at most 3 days.
- Tariff examples: general practitioner 25 EUR.

## Legal references

- CSP R6316-1 to R6316-4; arrêté du 16 août 2018.
- [Service-public F34696](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F34696).

## Effective dates

In force in its current version since 2021-06-05 ([Code de la santé publique, art. R6316-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000043600549), read 2026-11-27) (definition of teleconsultation).
