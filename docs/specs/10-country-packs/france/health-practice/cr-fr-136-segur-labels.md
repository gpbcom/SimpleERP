# ⚖️ CR-FR-136 Ségur software labels and secure health messaging

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The Ségur programme is a State funding scheme, not an obligation of the practice: the Agence du numérique en santé
references software at its publisher's request, and the funding goes to the publishers of referenced software, one
arrêté per software type and wave; the practice orders the referenced update under the programme's rules ([arrêté du 11
août 2021, vague 1, art. 1 to 5](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000043934133), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- The criteria and the messaging rules: the detailed requirements are in annex 1 (REM-MDV-LGC) of each arrêté, published
  by the Agence du numérique en santé ([arrêté du 11 août 2021, art. 3 and
  8](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000043934133), read 2026-11-27). Vague 1 software integrates INS,
  DMP compatibility, secure health messaging (MSSanté), Pro Santé Connect, the Carte Vitale app and the e-prescription,
  produces the structured medical summary, sends documents by MSSanté to professionals or the patient and to the DMP,
  imports documents received, and meets ergonomics and data-portability requirements; vague 2 simplifies DMP
  consultation and MSSanté imports, strengthens security, and adds the child's health book and the vaccination note.
  Since 2023 a Ségur-referenced practice software is a condition of the full forfait structure, replaced from 2026 by
  the forfait numérique ([Agence du numérique en santé: Ségur for city
  doctors](https://esante.gouv.fr/segur/medecin-de-ville), read 2026-11-27).

## Legal references

- Code de la santé publique L1111-24; arrêtés of 11 August 2021 (vague 1) and 30 April 2025 (vague 2) for the doctors'
  practice software; Agence du numérique en santé (annexes).

## Effective dates

Vague 1 under the arrêté of 11 August 2021 ([arrêté du 11 août 2021](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000043934133)); vague 2 (update of the practice software)
under the arrêté of 30 April 2025 ([arrêté du 30 avril 2025](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000051586547), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc09-health-practices/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: ANS. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships in a
pack update (D294).

## Penalty

For the publishers, not the practice: on a breach the agency withdraws the referencing and claims the funding back,
and on fraud it may bring criminal proceedings ([arrêté du 11 août 2021, art. 7](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000043934133), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

None.
