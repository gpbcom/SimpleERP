# ⚖️ CR-FR-228 Second-hand purchase register

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A business selling second-hand movable goods keeps a register of every object bought, sold or deposited.

## Values

- Paper: numbered pages, initialled by the police commissioner or the mayor; entries daily in indelible ink, no blanks,
  erasures or abbreviations; one register per establishment open to the public.
- Digital: automated processing compliant with ISO 14641-1; each consultation logged (user, date, time, purpose) and the
  logs kept one year.
- Retention: paper 5 years from closing; digital 10 years from recording.
- Official models set by the arrete of 15 May 2020.

## Legal references

- [Service-public F39552](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F39552).
- Code penal 321-7 and 321-8 (sanctions), R321-3 to R321-5 (entries), R321-6 (paper), R321-6-1 (digital); arrete of 15
  May 2020.
