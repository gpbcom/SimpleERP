# ⚖️ CR-FR-175 Unfair practices between businesses

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A business may not impose a significant imbalance on a partner, nor end an established relationship abruptly without
written notice ([Code de commerce, art. L442-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000047381704), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- No fixed period: the written notice takes account of the length of the relationship, trade usage or
  interprofessional agreements and market conditions; a notice of 18 months protects the party ending it from a claim
  of insufficient notice; no notice is due for the other party's non-performance or force majeure
  ([Code de commerce, art. L442-1 II](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000047381704), read 2026-11-27).

## Legal references

- [Code de commerce](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000047381704) L442-1, L442-4.

## Effective dates

- Until 2026-08-19: L442-1 in its version of 2023-04-01 (loi n° 2023-221): I lists 1° to 5° (no item on repeated
  tenders), and II does not cover a substantial cut in order volumes during negotiations
  ([Code de commerce, art. L442-1 on 2026-08-19](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000005634379/LEGISCTA000006146076/2026-08-19/), read 2026-11-27).
- From 2026-08-20: L442-1 as written above (loi n° 2026-796), adding I 6° (repeated tenders creating an imbalance)
  and, in II, a substantial cut in order volumes during negotiations ([Code de commerce, art. L442-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000047381704), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CCom L442-1. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

The author repairs the harm caused (L442-1); a civil fine, asked by the minister for the economy or the public
prosecutor, up to the highest of 5 000 000 EUR, three times the advantages unduly obtained, or 5 % of the turnover in
France ([Code de commerce, art. L442-4](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000005634379/LEGISCTA000006146076/), read 2026-11-27).
