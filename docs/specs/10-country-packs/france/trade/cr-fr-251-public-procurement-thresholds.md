# ⚖️ CR-FR-251 Public procurement thresholds

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A public buyer publishes a notice from 60 000 EUR for supplies and services and 100 000 EUR for works, and must use a
formal procedure above the European thresholds.

## Values

- Amounts exclude VAT.
- Notice duty from 60 000 EUR (supplies and services) and 100 000 EUR (works); below, no publication required.
- Works: 100 000 to 5 403 999.99 EUR, BOAMP or approved legal-notices outlet (adapted procedure); from 5 404 000 EUR,
  BOAMP and JOUE (formal procedure).
- Supplies and services: 60 000 to 89 999.99 EUR, free or adapted publicity; from 90 000 EUR, BOAMP or approved outlet
  up to 139 999.99 EUR (central authority), 215 999.99 EUR (other authority), 431 999.99 EUR (contracting entity); from
  140 000 / 216 000 / 432 000 EUR, BOAMP and JOUE (formal procedure).
- Until 31 December 2026, works for heat pump installation or district-heating connection under 140 000 EUR may be
  awarded without publicity or competition; from 1 January 2027 the no-publicity ceiling for works rises to 140 000 EUR.

## Effective dates

Heat pump exception until 31 December 2026; otherwise as of the verification date. Other start dates not read; in force
when read on 2026-11-15 (the page was verified on 21 August 2026).
