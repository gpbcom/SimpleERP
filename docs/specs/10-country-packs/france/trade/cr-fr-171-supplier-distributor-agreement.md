# ⚖️ CR-FR-171 Yearly written agreement between supplier and distributor

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A supplier and a distributor put the result of their yearly negotiation in a written agreement signed by 1 March, after
the supplier's terms are sent by 1 December; it lists prices, reductions, services and penalties.

## Values

- Signed by 1 March (seasonal products: within 2 months of the season's start).
- Terms sent 3 months before; answer within 1 month for everyday consumer goods.
- 1 to 3 years; price revision clause beyond 1 year.
- Logistic penalties at most 2 % of the products ordered; none for breaches over 1 year old.

## Legal references

- Code de commerce L441-3 to L441-7.
- [Service-public F35018](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F35018).

## Effective dates

Code de commerce L441-3 in its current version in force since 2023-04-01 (loi n° 2023-221) ([Code de commerce, art. L441-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000047381742), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc15-wholesale-and-distribution/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CCom L441-3. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Company: up to 375 000 EUR (1 000 000 EUR for missing the deadline), doubled on repeat.

## Exceptions

Some fresh fruit and vegetables, poultry and rabbit meat, eggs, honey.
