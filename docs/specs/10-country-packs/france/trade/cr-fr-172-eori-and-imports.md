# ⚖️ CR-FR-172 EORI number and import declarations

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A company doing customs operations holds an EORI number, and every import from outside the EU is declared to customs
under the Union Customs Code.

## Values

- EORI: FR + SIREN or FR + SIRET, free, active 24 hours after issue.
- Since 2026-11-01, a 2 EUR handling fee on small parcels from outside the EU, besides the 3 EUR flat duty per article
  category.

## Legal references

- Union Customs Code.
- [Customs: EORI](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/demarche/enregistrer-votre-entreprise-aupres-de-la-douane-numero-eori),
  [import](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/professionnels/commerce-international/import-export/vous-souhaitez-importer).
