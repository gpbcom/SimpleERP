# ⚖️ CR-FR-253 Franchise disclosure document

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The franchisor gives the candidate a pre-contractual information document at least 20 days before the franchise
agreement is signed.

## Values

- Delay: at least 20 days before signature.
- Content: franchisor identity (name, seat, legal form, capital, SIREN, RCS), network leaders and experience, the last 2
  years of accounts and financial guarantees, the concept, contract terms (term, renewal, termination, transfer), rights
  and duties (assistance, training), investments (initial costs, royalties, advertising and training fees), territories
  and exclusivity, ongoing disputes, number and addresses of franchisees and contract transfers or terminations.
- Forecast operating accounts are not required; if given they must be sincere and serious.

## Legal references

- [Service-public F37342](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F37342).
- Code de commerce L330-3 (delivery), R330-1 (content).
