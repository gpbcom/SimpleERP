# ⚖️ CR-FR-227 Fixed book price (5 % cap)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The publisher or importer sets the price of new books and retailers must apply it, with a discount of at most 5 %.

## Values

- Applies to new print and digital books; audiobooks and second-hand books are excluded.
- Maximum discount 5 % in store or on remote orders collected in store.
- Up to 9 % when the buyer is the State, a local authority, a teaching or research body, a representative union, a works
  council or a public-library operator; school books may be sold at any discount to the State, local authorities,
  schools and certain parent or student associations.
- Since 7 October 2023, delivery of new-book orders under 35 EUR costs at least 3 EUR; over 35 EUR at least 0.01 EUR.

## Effective dates

Minimum delivery fee since 7 October 2023 (as the page states). Other start dates not read; in force when read on
2026-11-15 (the page was verified on 23 December 2023).

## Penalty

None on the page read; disputes can go to the book mediator.

## Exceptions

Up to 9 % for public and teaching buyers and any discount on school books for the buyers listed under Values; audiobooks
and second-hand books are excluded.
