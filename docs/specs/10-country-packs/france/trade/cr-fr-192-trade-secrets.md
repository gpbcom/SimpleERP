# ⚖️ CR-FR-192 Trade secrets

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Confidential information with economic value, kept secret by reasonable measures, is protected as a trade secret without
any registration; it gives no exclusive right, so a competitor who reaches the same result on its own may use it.

## Values

- Reasonable measures to keep the secret are a condition of the protection.
- No registration formality.
- Independent creation of the same product or process cannot be stopped.
