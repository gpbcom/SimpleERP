# ⚖️ CR-FR-173 EU trade statistics on goods

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A seller of goods to other EU countries answers the monthly statistical survey when customs asks it by letter, and files
the VAT recapitulative statement of its EU deliveries.

## Values

- Monthly, on request.
- VAT recapitulative statement for EU deliveries; declaration of European services for services.

## Legal references

- Regulation (EU) 2019/2152; CGI art. 289 B.
- [Customs: selling goods in the EU](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/professionnels/commerce-international/echanges-au-sein-de-lunion-europeenne/vous-vendez-des-biens).

## Effective dates

Regulation (EU) 2019/2152 applies from 2021-01-01; its art. 5(2) to (4) and 11 to 15 from 2022-01-01 (art. 26)
([Regulation (EU) 2019/2152, EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32019R2152), read 2026-11-27).
