# ⚖️ CR-FR-271 General terms and prices for business buyers

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A business that sets general terms of sale includes in them its payment terms and how its prices are set (the unit
price list and any reductions), and gives them on a durable medium to any business buyer who asks; when the price of a
service cannot be set in advance, it gives on request the method of calculation or a detailed quote
([Code de commerce, art. L441-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000038414469), read
2026-11-27). A contract, a fixed price included, changes only by mutual consent or for a cause the law allows
([Code civil, art. 1193](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006070721/LEGISCTA000032009282),
read 2026-11-27). Payment terms and late-payment penalties are in
[CR-FR-018](../invoicing/cr-fr-018-payment-terms-cap.md) and [CR-FR-019](../invoicing/cr-fr-019-late-payment-penalties.md).

## Values

- General terms of sale: payment terms, unit price list, price reductions (L441-1 I); they may differ by category of
  buyer, and are then given for the buyer's category (L441-1 II); they are the sole basis of the commercial negotiation,
  and specific terms agreed in it need not be given to others (L441-1 III)
  ([Code de commerce, art. L441-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000038414469), read
  2026-11-27).
- Service whose price cannot be set in advance: the method of calculation or a detailed quote, on request (L441-1 III).
- A fixed price binds both parties: a later change of the catalogue's price does not touch an order already made
  (Code civil 1193); a party may ask for a renegotiation only when an unforeseeable change of circumstances makes
  performance excessively onerous and it had not accepted that risk (Code civil 1195)
  ([Code civil, art. 1193 to
  1195](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006070721/LEGISCTA000032009282), read 2026-11-27).
- SimpleERP enforces: a fixed-price change keeps the price and the date it had when ordered, and nothing is charged
  before the customer accepts the change.

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Code de commerce L441-1; Code civil 1193 to 1195. A change flags this rule and warns the
administrators; the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Not giving the general terms of sale to a business buyer who asks: an administrative fine of up to 15 000 EUR for a
natural person and 75 000 EUR for a legal person (L441-1 IV)
([Code de commerce, art. L441-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000038414469), read
2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

Specific terms negotiated with one buyer are not given to others (L441-1 III).
