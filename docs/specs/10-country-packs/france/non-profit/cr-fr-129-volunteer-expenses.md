# ⚖️ CR-FR-129 Volunteers' expenses

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An association repays volunteers' real and justified expenses, with receipts kept, or a flat mileage allowance on the
employees' scale; a volunteer who waives repayment makes a gift and gets a tax receipt.

## Values

- Mileage: the employees' scale, the distance proved.
- Waiver: receipt on Cerfa 11580.
- Volunteer time may be valued in the accounts, not an obligation.

## Legal references

- CGI art. 200.
