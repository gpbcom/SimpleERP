# ⚖️ CR-FR-127 Grant agreement and financial report

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A grant above 23 000 EUR needs an agreement setting its purpose, amount and conditions; a grant for a specific action is
followed by a financial report within 6 months of the year end.

## Values

- Agreement above 23 000 EUR.
- Report within 6 months.

## Legal references

- Loi n° 2000-321 art. 10.
- [Service-public F3180](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F3180).

## Effective dates

Loi n° 2000-321 art. 10 in its current version since 2022-02-23 (loi n° 2022-217) ([loi n° 2000-321, art. 10](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000045213404), read 2026-11-27);
the 23 000 EUR threshold is set by décret n° 2001-495.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc08-non-profit/regulatory.md)
- [UC-SC08-21 Give grants as a foundation](../../../08-sectors/sc08-non-profit/use-cases/07-foundations-integration-and-federations/uc-sc08-21-give-grants-as-a-foundation.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Loi 2000-321 art. 10. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected
rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Misuse: breach of trust, up to 3 years; repayment.

## Exceptions

Social rental housing grants.
