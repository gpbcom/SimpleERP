# ⚖️ CR-FR-277 Conflicts of interest in associations and foundations

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

A non-profit body keeps its tax status only if its management is disinterested: it is in principle run by volunteers
who have, themselves or through another person, no direct or indirect interest in its results, it distributes no
profit and its members cannot receive any of its assets ([Code général des impôts, art. 261, 7, 1°,
d](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000054374042), read 2026-11-27). A non-trading private body
with an economic activity, or an association receiving the public grants of L612-4, presents to its deliberative body a
report on the agreements made, directly or through another person, with one of its directors or officers, and that
body rules on it ([Code de commerce, art. L612-5](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000019981373),
read 2026-11-27). A committee member with an interest in an application therefore takes no part in its decision.

## Values

- Disinterested management (CGI 261, 7, 1°, d): volunteer managers with no direct or indirect interest in the results;
  no distribution of profit in any form; no share of the assets for members, except the return of contributions.
- Paid managers allowed for an association, a foundation recognised as of public utility or a corporate foundation only
  if the statutes provide for it, the deliberative body decides it by a two-thirds majority, the finances are
  transparent, the managers are elected regularly and controlled by the members, and the resources (excluding public
  funding, certified by a statutory auditor) average more than 200 000 EUR (1 manager), 500 000 EUR (2) or
  1 000 000 EUR (3) over the 3 last closed years; each paid at most 3 times the social security ceiling
  ([CGI, art. 261, 7, 1°, d](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000054374042), read 2026-11-27).
- Related-party agreements (L612-5): a report by the legal representative or the statutory auditor on each agreement
  with a director or officer, or with a company sharing one of them or a shareholder of more than 10 %; the deliberative
  body rules on it; an agreement not approved still has effect, and the harm it causes can be charged to the director;
  current agreements on normal terms that are significant for neither party are outside it
  ([Code de commerce, art. L612-5](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000019981373), read
  2026-11-27).
- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) The rule that a member who declared an interest neither votes nor counts in
  the quorum is not written in the texts read: it comes from the statutes of each foundation (the model statutes of
  foundations recognised as of public utility approved by the Conseil d'État, not read). SimpleERP takes the
  conservative reading: the conflicted member does not vote and does not count in the quorum.
- SimpleERP enforces: members with a conflict do not vote.

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CGI art. 261, 7, 1°, d; Code de commerce L612-5. A change flags this rule and warns the
administrators; the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

A body whose management is not disinterested loses the exemption of CGI 261, 7, 1°; the harm of an agreement not
approved may be charged to the director concerned (L612-5).

## Exceptions

Current agreements made on normal terms and significant for neither party (L612-5).
