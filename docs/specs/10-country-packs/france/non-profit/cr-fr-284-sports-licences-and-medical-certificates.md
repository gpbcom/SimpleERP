# ⚖️ CR-FR-284 Sports licences and medical certificates

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

A sports licence is issued by a sports federation or in its name and gives the right to take part in the activities
of the federation and its affiliated clubs; the federation's statutes may require the members of its clubs to hold one
([Code du sport, art. L131-6](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000045293968), read 2026-11-27).
In each sport, one approved federation receives the State's delegation for a set period, under a delegation contract
([art. L131-14](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000054616132), read 2026-11-27). For adults, a
licence may require a medical certificate, on the conditions the federal rules set; for minors, a licence requires a
health questionnaire filled in with the parents, and a certificate only when an answer leads to a medical examination;
for sports with particular constraints listed by decree, a certificate under one year old is always required; entry to
an official competition requires a competition licence, or for a non-licensed adult possibly a certificate, for a
non-licensed minor the questionnaire ([art. L231-2 to
L231-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006071318/LEGISCTA000006167042), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Licence (L131-6): issued by or in the name of the federation; opens the federation's activities; to issue it, clubs
  collect the full identity of the people concerned by the honour check of L212-9.
- Delegation (L131-14): one approved federation per sport, for a set period, under a contract with the minister.
- Adult licence (L231-2 I and II): a medical certificate may be required; the federal rules set when, and the nature,
  frequency and content of the examinations.
- Minor licence (L231-2 III): a health questionnaire filled in jointly by the minor and the parents; a certificate only
  if an answer leads to an examination.
- Particular-constraint sports (L231-2-3): a certificate under one year old for every licence, renewal and competition,
  after a specific examination; the list of sports is set by decree.
- Competition entry (L231-2-1): a competition licence of the sport; for non-licensed adults a certificate may be
  required; for non-licensed minors the questionnaire.
- A federation doctor's certificate of contra-indication suspends participation in competitions until it is lifted
  (L231-3).
- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) The list of particular-constraint sports and the questionnaire's content are set
  by decree and order, not read; each federation's rules are entered by the club.
- SimpleERP enforces: a licence or an entry is accepted only with the certificate or questionnaire its federation and
  category require, keeping only the date, validity and fitness ([CR-FR-283](../data-protection/cr-fr-283-health-data-outside-care.md)).

Watched in the law corpus: Code du sport L131-6, L131-14, L231-2 to L231-3. A change flags this rule and warns the

## Penalty

None stated in the articles read.

## Exceptions

In border départements, participants in a competition follow the rules of their place of residence for entry
(L231-2-1 IV).
