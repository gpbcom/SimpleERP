# ⚖️ CR-FR-286 Appeals to public generosity

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

A body that appeals to the public's generosity for a scientific, social, family, humanitarian, philanthropic,
educational, sporting, cultural or environmental cause declares it to the prefect: before the appeal when the funds
collected that way exceeded, in one of the two previous years, a threshold set by decree that cannot exceed
153 000 EUR, or during the year as soon as they exceed it; above a threshold set by decree it draws up a yearly account
of the use of the funds collected from the public, kept at its registered office and made known to the public
([Loi n° 91-772 du 7 août 1991, art. 3, 3 bis and 4](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000162114), read
2026-11-27). The donor's tax receipt is in [CR-FR-124](cr-fr-124-gift-tax-receipts.md).

## Values

- Declaration to the prefect: before the appeal when the funds collected by appeals exceeded the threshold in one of
  the two previous years, otherwise during the year once they exceed it; it states the aims of the appeal; one yearly
  declaration may cover several appeals (art. 3).
- Joint appeals: the declaration states how the funds are shared, and the people asked are told (art. 3 bis).
- Yearly account of the use of funds collected from the public, by type of spending, above the threshold; kept at the
  registered office and made public; part of the notes when the body draws up annual accounts; checked by the
  statutory auditor where there is one (art. 4).
- A gift through a supporter's page is a gift to the body: the receipt goes to the donor
  ([CR-FR-124](cr-fr-124-gift-tax-receipts.md)).
- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) The two decree thresholds (at most 153 000 EUR for the declaration) are not
  read.
- SimpleERP enforces: gifts collected by appeals are totalled per year against the thresholds, and the body is warned
  before it crosses them.

## Legal references

- [Loi n° 91-772 du 7 août 1991, art. 3, 3 bis and 4](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000162114),

## Effective dates

Art. 3, 3 bis and 4 in their version since 2021-07-03 (loi n° 2021-875)
([Loi n° 91-772](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000162114), read 2026-11-27).

## When the law changes

administrators; the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

None stated in the articles read; the control bodies may ask for the accounts (art. 4).
