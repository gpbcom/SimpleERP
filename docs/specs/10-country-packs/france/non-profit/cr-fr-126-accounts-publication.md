# ⚖️ CR-FR-126 Publication of accounts above the grant threshold

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An association receiving more than 153 000 EUR of cash grants a year produces a balance sheet, income statement and
notes, appoints an auditor and a deputy, and publishes accounts and the auditor's report within 3 months of their
approval.

## Values

- Threshold: 153 000 EUR of cash grants.
- Publication in the official journal of associations, online.

## Legal references

- Code de commerce D612-5; décret n° 2009-540.
- [Service-public F3180](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F3180).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc08-non-profit/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CCom D612-5. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Directors: 9 000 EUR fine if accounts are not produced.

## Exceptions

None.
