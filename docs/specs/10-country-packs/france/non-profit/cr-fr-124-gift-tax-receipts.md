# ⚖️ CR-FR-124 Tax receipts for gifts and their yearly declaration

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An association entitled to issue tax receipts gives each donor a receipt on the official model, with its SIREN or RNA
number, and declares each year the number of receipts and the total of gifts.

## Values

- Declaration within 3 months of the year end; for a 31 December year end, by the second working day after 1 May.
- (Proposed) The donors' reduction rates and ceilings, still to read.

## Legal references

- CGI art. 200, 238 bis, 222 bis; loi n° 2021-1109 art. 19.
- [Receipts](https://www.associations.gouv.fr/dons-effectues-par-les-particuliers-le-recu),
  [declaration](https://www.associations.gouv.fr/declaration-des-dons-et-des-recus-fiscaux).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.
