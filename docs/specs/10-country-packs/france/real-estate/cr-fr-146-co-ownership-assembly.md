# ⚖️ CR-FR-146 Co-owners' general assembly

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Co-owners meet at least once a year, convened at least 21 days before; the minutes go out within one month, and a
decision can be challenged within two months of them.

## Values

- Notice 21 days, unless urgent or longer by the rules.
- Minutes within 1 month.
- Challenge within 2 months.

## Legal references

- Loi n° 65-557.
- [Service-public N31341](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/N31341).
