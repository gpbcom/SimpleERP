# ⚖️ CR-FR-148 National register of co-ownerships

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each co-ownership is registered in the national register and its data updated each year by its manager
([CCH, art. L711-1 to L711-7](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006074096/LEGISCTA000028777991/); [CCH, art. R711-10](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006074096/LEGISCTA000033072985/), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Data: name, address, creation date, number and nature of lots, the manager's name, any insolvency procedure or
  prefectoral order, and the financial and technical data, including the mandatory diagnostics (L711-2); filed and
  updated online by the manager, or by the notary at creation (L711-4) ([CCH](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006074096/LEGISCTA000028777991/), read 2026-11-27).
- Deadline: the yearly declaration within two months of the general assembly that approves the accounts (R711-10);
  other data updated when they change ([CCH, art. R711-10](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006074096/LEGISCTA000033072985/), in force since 2016-08-29, read 2026-11-27).

## Legal references

- Code de la construction et de l'habitation L711-1 to L711-7, R711-1 to R711-21.

## Effective dates

L711-1 and L711-3 since 2017-01-29, L711-2 since 2024-04-11, L711-4 to L711-7 since 2014-03-27 ([CCH](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006074096/LEGISCTA000028777991/), read 2026-11-27).
Data declared (R711-8, R711-9): the current list applies until décret n° 2025-831 du 19 août 2025 enters into force, 18
months after its publication of 2025-08-21 (about 2027-02-21; the decree gives no calendar date); from then, debtor
co-owners only above two quarters of charges, the works fund amount added, and a new list of technical data (lifts,
construction period, energy performance, heating, works plan, structural diagnoses) ([décret n°
2025-831](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000052120251), art. 1 and 2, read 2026-11-27). The yearly
declaration deadline (R711-10) does not change.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc10-real-estate/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Code de la construction et de l'habitation L711-1 A change flags this rule and warns the
administrators; the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

After a formal notice left one month without effect, a periodic penalty (astreinte) on the manager of at most 20
EUR per lot and per week, not passed on to the co-owners unless the manager is unpaid; no public grant without
registration and current data ([CCH, art. L711-6](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006074096/LEGISCTA000028777991/), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

None.
