# ⚖️ CR-FR-142 Yearly rent revision by the reference index

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

If the lease has a revision clause, the rent is revised once a year by the rent reference index of the same quarter, not
retroactively; homes rated F or G cannot be revised.

## Values

- New rent = rent x index of the quarter / index of the same quarter a year before, rounded to 2 decimals.
- Lapses after one year if not applied.
- No revision for F or G homes (leases since 2022-08-24).

## Legal references

- Loi n° 89-462 art. 17-1.
