# ⚖️ CR-FR-239 Commercial leases (3-6-9, ILC/ILAT)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The tenant may leave a commercial lease at the end of each three-year period, and either party may ask for a rent
revision every three years on the index that fits the activity, with the rise capped.

## Values

- Tenant exit without reason at the end of each 3-year period (after 3, 6 or 9 years), notice at least 6 months before
  the period ends, by commissaire de justice act or registered letter with acknowledgement; late notice moves the end to
  the next period.
- Triennial exit may be excluded for leases over 9 years, single-activity leases, office-only premises and storage
  premises.
- Revision may be asked every 3 years after the start, a renewal or the last revision, even if the lease does not
  provide for it; the request states the rent asked for.
- Index: ILC for commercial or craft activities; ILAT for liberal, tertiary, office and logistics-warehouse uses; ICC no
  longer a reference for triennial revision since September 2014 (still usable in an indexation clause).
- Cap: for leases concluded or renewed since September 2014, the first revision may not raise the rent by more than 10 %
  over the previous year's rent; a rise above the cap is also spread at 10 % a year.
- Indexation clause: if the rent moves by more than a quarter, either party may ask for revision, increase capped at 10
  %.
- Leases concluded or renewed from 28 May 2026: "tunnel" indexation clause limited to plus or minus 3 % or 5 %.
- Landlord exit at a triennial date for rebuilding, heritage restoration or conversion to housing, 6 months notice by
  commissaire de justice act; outside it only for tenant fault or resolutory clause after a one-month commandement.

## Effective dates

Tunnel indexation clause for leases concluded or renewed from 28 May 2026; ICC rule since September 2014. Other start
dates not read; in force when read on 2026-11-15 (the page was verified on 28 May 2026 (F31925 and F31707) and 30
January 2025 (F20564)).
