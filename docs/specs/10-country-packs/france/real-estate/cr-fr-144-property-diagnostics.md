# ⚖️ CR-FR-144 Mandatory property diagnostics

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Sales and lettings come with the technical diagnostics file; the diagnostics required depend on the building's age,
equipment and zone.

## Values

- Sale: energy performance (DPE); lead before 1949; asbestos before 1 July 1997; electricity and gas over 15 years old;
  termites, risks and noise by zone; an energy audit for houses rated E to G.
- Rental: the file attached to the lease at signature and renewal.

## Legal references

- [Service-public F10798](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F10798) (sale) and
  [F33463](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F33463) (rental).
- Code de la construction et de l'habitation.
