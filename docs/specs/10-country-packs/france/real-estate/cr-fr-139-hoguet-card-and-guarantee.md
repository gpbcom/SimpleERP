# ⚖️ CR-FR-139 Agents' card, client-funds guarantee and mandates register

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An estate agent holds a professional card for each activity, a financial guarantee for client funds and liability
insurance.

## Values

- Cards: transactions (T), management (G), trusteeship (S), issued by the CCI president, renewed every 3 years.
- Guarantee: at least 30 000 EUR in the first 2 years, then 110 000 EUR.

## Legal references

- [Service-public F38657](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F38657),
  [R13999](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/R13999) and
  [R73064](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/R73064).
- Loi n° 70-9 (Hoguet); décret n° 72-678.

## Effective dates

Card rule (loi n° 70-9, art. 3) in its current version since 2025-07-17 ([loi n° 70-9, art. 3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000051898206), read 2026-11-27); penalties
(art. 14) since 2018-11-25 ([loi n° 70-9, art. 14](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000037670974), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Loi n° 70-9 (Hoguet) A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected
rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

No card: 6 months and 7 500 EUR; practising while banned: 5 years and 375 000 EUR.

## Exceptions

None.
