# ⚖️ CR-FR-140 Security deposit of a residential lease

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An unfurnished lease's deposit is at most one month's rent without charges (two months furnished), and is returned
within one month of the keys if the exit inventory matches the entry one, two months otherwise.

## Values

- Late return: +10 % of the monthly rent per month started.
- In a co-ownership, up to 20 % may be held until the building's accounts are approved, then settled within the month.
- No deposit when rent is paid quarterly in advance.

## Legal references

- Loi n° 89-462.
- [Service-public F31269](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F31269).

## Effective dates

Loi n° 89-462 art. 22 in its current version since 2014-03-27 ([loi n° 89-462, chapter III](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/JORFTEXT000000509310/LEGISCTA000006118908/), read 2026-11-27).

## When the law changes

in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

+10 % of the monthly rent per month of delay.

## Exceptions

None.
