# ⚖️ CR-FR-141 Rent receipts

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The landlord or agency gives the tenant a free receipt on request, showing rent and charges apart.

## Values

- Free, on request.
- Rent and charges apart.

## Legal references

- Loi n° 89-462 art. 4, 21.
- [Service-public F35247](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F35247).
