# ⚖️ CR-FR-288 Furnished tourist rentals

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

Whoever offers a furnished tourist rental (a furnished villa, flat or studio let to passing guests who do not live
there, by the day, week or month) first declares it in person on the national teleservice, which gives a declaration
number shared with the commune; the declaration says whether the home is the host's main residence, is kept up to date
and renewed; a main residence is let at most 120 days a calendar year, which the commune may lower to 90, and the host
gives the commune the number of days let on request
([Code du tourisme, art. L324-1-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000050623378), read
2026-11-27). The guest pays the tourist tax ([CR-FR-111](../tax-and-vat/cr-fr-111-tourist-tax.md)) and a foreign guest
fills in the police form ([CR-FR-282](../hospitality/cr-fr-282-police-form-for-foreign-guests.md)).

## Values

- Declaration before letting, on the national teleservice; an electronic receipt with the declaration number at once;
  updated on any change and renewed at the end of a period set by decree (L324-1-1 III).
- Main residence: stated in the declaration with proof; at most 120 days a calendar year, except professional duty,
  health or force majeure; a commune may lower it down to 90 days (L324-1-1 IV).
- Days let: given to the commune within 1 month of its request, until 31 December of the following year (L324-1-1 IV).
- The mayor may suspend a number and order platforms to remove the listing for a home under a safety or insalubrity
  order (L324-1-1 III bis).
- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) The renewal period of the declaration and its documents are set by a decree not
  read.
- SimpleERP enforces: each home carries its declaration number, and the nights let in a year of a main residence are
  counted against the commune's limit.

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Code du tourisme L324-1-1 and its decree. A change flags this rule and warns the
administrators; the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Failing to declare: an administrative fine of up to 10 000 EUR; a false declaration or a false number: up to
20 000 EUR; letting a main residence beyond the limit: a civil fine of up to 15 000 EUR (L324-1-1 V)
([Code du tourisme, art. L324-1-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000050623378), read
2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

The 120-day limit does not apply in case of professional duty, health reasons or force majeure (L324-1-1 IV).
