# ⚖️ CR-FR-143 Rent caps and energy-inefficient homes

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

In the cities with rent caps, the base rent at letting does not exceed the capped reference rent; homes rated F or G
cannot be raised at revision or renewal, nor carry a rent supplement.

## Values

- Cities: Paris, Est Ensemble, Plaine Commune, Bordeaux, Grenoble (part), Lille, Lyon and Villeurbanne, Montpellier,
  Pays Basque.
- Paris: the source gives an end on 2026-11-24, to check.
- F and G: no increase at renewal since 2024-08-22.

## Legal references

- Loi n° 89-462; loi ELAN.
- [Service-public F1314](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F1314).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc10-real-estate/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Loi 89-462. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Up to 5 000 EUR (person), 15 000 EUR (company).

## Exceptions

None.
