# ⚖️ CR-FR-225 Off-plan sales (VEFA) calls for funds

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

In an off-plan sale the buyer pays in stages that follow the works, up to fixed maximum shares of the price.

## Values

- 35 % of the total price at completion of the foundations (maximum).
- 70 % when the home is weathertight (hors d'eau).
- 95 % at completion of the building.
- The remaining 5 % at delivery, unless there are reservations on conformity; the buyer can place the balance in escrow
  when defects are reported.

## Legal references

- [Service-public F39743](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F39743).
- Code de la construction et de l'habitation L261-1 to L261-22 (L261-3 and L261-10 named), R261-11 to R261-16 (R261-14
  named); the page does not tie each percentage to an article.
