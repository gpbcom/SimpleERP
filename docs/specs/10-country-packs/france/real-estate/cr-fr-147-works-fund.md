# ⚖️ CR-FR-147 Co-ownership works fund

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A residential co-ownership builds a works fund, from 10 years after construction, in its own bank account, with at least
5 % of the budget a year (and 2.5 % of planned works with a works plan).

## Values

- At least 5 % of the budget; with a multi-year works plan, also 2.5 % of the planned works.
- Separate account opened by the manager.
- Voted by absolute majority.

## Legal references

- Loi n° 65-557 art. 14-2, 14-2-1, 18.
- [Service-public F34026](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F34026).

## Effective dates

Loi n° 65-557 art. 14-2-1 (10 years, 5 % and 2.5 %) since 2023-01-01 ([loi n° 65-557, art. 14-2-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000043967792), read 2026-11-27).
