# ⚖️ CR-FR-180 Safety register of venues open to the public

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A venue open to the public keeps a safety register (fire staff, instructions, checks, works) and, for the larger
categories, a fire-safety service while the public is present; the safety commission inspects it.

## Values

- Checks every 3 or 5 years.
- Paper or digital register, kept current.
- Safety agents: 3 on site, one at the security post, when used.

## Legal references

- Code de la construction et de l'habitation R143-2 to R143-44.
- [Service-public F31684](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F31684).

## Effective dates

Code de la construction et de l'habitation R143-2 in force since 2021-07-01 (décret n° 2021-872) ([Code de la construction et de l'habitation, art. R143-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000043818941/), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc16-events-and-culture/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CCH R143-44. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Closure by the mayor after notice.

## Exceptions

None.
