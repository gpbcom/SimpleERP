# ⚖️ CR-FR-179 Music copyright at events

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

An organiser playing music gets Sacem's prior authorisation and declares the event, and declares to the Spré even for
royalty-free music.

## Values

- 20 % reduction when declared before the sound system is set up.
- A contract of 1 year, renewed tacitly.
- Sacem and Spré invoices payable within 25 days.
- Minimum yearly billing 113.45 EUR excluding VAT for cafés and restaurants, 107.22 EUR for shops.

## Legal references

- [Service-public F3094](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F3094) and
  [R55115](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/R55115).
- Code de la propriété intellectuelle.

## Effective dates

(⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) Start date not read (the Sacem and Spré terms are contractual); in force when read on 2026-11-12 (the page was verified on 12 August 2025).
