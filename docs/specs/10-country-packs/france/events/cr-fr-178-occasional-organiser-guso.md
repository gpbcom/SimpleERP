# ⚖️ CR-FR-178 Hiring performers as an occasional organiser (GUSO)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An occasional organiser hiring artists or technicians on fixed-term contracts uses the single simplified service: hiring
declaration before the show, simplified declaration and payment within 15 days after the contract.

## Values

- Declaration up to 2 hours before the show.
- Simplified declaration and payment within 15 days after the contract, by direct debit.
- Late: 6 %, then 1 % a month.

## Legal references

- Code du travail L7122-22 to L7122-26.
- [Service-public R14535](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/R14535),
  [F1928](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F1928).

## Effective dates

Code du travail L7122-22 in force since 2008-05-01 ([Code du travail, art. L7122-22](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/LEGISCTA000006195968/2017-09-27/), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CT L7122-22. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Late payment surcharges.

## Exceptions

None.
