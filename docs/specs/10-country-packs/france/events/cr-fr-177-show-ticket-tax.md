# ⚖️ CR-FR-177 Tax on show tickets

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The holder of the ticketing declares each show or tour and pays 3.5 % of ticket receipts excluding VAT to the National
Music Centre; for free shows, 3.5 % of the performance contract.

## Values

- Declared by the last day of the third month after the show.
- Paid 30 days end of month after the notice.
- Not collected under 80 EUR a year, still declared.

## Legal references

- Code des impositions sur les biens et services L452-14 to L452-27.
- [National Music Centre: the tax](https://cnm.fr/taxe/).

## Effective dates

CIBS L452-14 in force since 2024-01-01 (ordonnance n° 2023-1210) ([Code des impositions sur les biens et services, art. L452-14 to L452-27](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000044595989/LEGISCTA000048626026/), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc16-events-and-culture/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CIBS L452-14. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Assessment with a 40 % surcharge after notice.

## Exceptions

School shows; traditional music with no copyright.
