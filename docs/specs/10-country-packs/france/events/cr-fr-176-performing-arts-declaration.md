# ⚖️ CR-FR-176 Performing-arts organiser's declaration

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An organiser whose main activity is shows, or who gives 7 or more performances a year, declares its activity and shows
the receipt number on posters, leaflets and tickets.

## Values

- Categories: venue operator, producer, broadcaster.
- Valid 5 years; the prefect has 30 days to object.
- Under 7 performances a year as a side activity: no formality.

## Legal references

- Code du travail L7122-1 to L7122-18.
- [Service-public F22365](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F22365).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc16-events-and-culture/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CT L7122-1. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Number missing: 800 EUR (person), 2 000 EUR (company); no declaration: 1 500 EUR or 7 500 EUR and closure up to 1 year.

## Exceptions

Fewer than 7 performances a year.
