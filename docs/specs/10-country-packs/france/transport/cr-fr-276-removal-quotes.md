# ⚖️ CR-FR-276 Removal quotes and delivery reserves

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

Before any removal contract, the company gives the customer a free quote with its general terms, stating among other
things the date, the places and distance, the volume, the service chosen, the payment terms, the company's liability
and the price excluding and including VAT, which is final: nothing more may be charged unless the quote is expressly
changed; the invoice explains any difference from the accepted quote; the prices are displayed in the company
([Arrêté du 27 avril 2010 relatif à la publicité des prix des prestations de
déménagement](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000022167232), read 2026-11-27). A consumer may object to
the condition of the goods by a reasoned registered letter within 10 calendar days of receiving them, even without
reserves at delivery ([Code de la consommation, art.
L224-63](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032221615), read 2026-11-27).
A quote signed at the customer's home follows the 7-day payment ban and the withdrawal right of
[CR-FR-036](../consumer/cr-fr-036-withdrawal-right.md).

## Values

- Quote mentions (arrêté of 2010-04-27, art. 1, kept as data): the company's references; the customer's name and
  address; the planned period or date; the loading and delivery places and the outward distance; the date of the quote;
  the volume of the furniture; the kind of trip (grouped or dedicated); the exact service chosen (packing by the company
  or the customer...); how the customer can make reserves; the payment terms; the company's liability and how to claim,
  arbitration included; the price excluding and including VAT, with the statement that it is final and that no extra
  cost can be charged unless the quote is expressly changed
  ([Arrêté du 27 avril 2010, art. 1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000022167232), read 2026-11-27).
- Except for grouped trips, the latest loading and unloading dates are on the consignment note given before the move
  (art. 2).
- Delivery note: a copy of the consignment note given at reception, where the customer writes that all was received
  without reserve or writes the reserves, and that states the 10 calendar days to object by registered letter (art. 3);
  the invoice explains any difference from the accepted quote (art. 4); the prices of labour, of haulage by volume band
  with a fixed and a per-kilometre part, of supplies and of small equipment hire, and of furniture storage, are displayed
  (art. 5 and 6) ([Arrêté du 27 avril 2010](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000022167232), read
  2026-11-27).
- Objection period: 10 calendar days from reception; 3 months when the reserves procedure was not given to the consumer
  as the order requires (L224-63, since 2017-02-23)
  ([Code de la consommation, art. L224-63](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032221615),
  read 2026-11-27).
- Quote signed at home: no payment or deposit for 7 days (L221-10); a move inside the withdrawal period starts only on
  the consumer's express request on paper or a durable medium, and a consumer who then withdraws pays the part already
  done, in proportion to the agreed price (L221-25) ([CR-FR-036](../consumer/cr-fr-036-withdrawal-right.md);
  [Code de la consommation, art. L221-25](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000044563179), read
  2026-11-27).
- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) Whether a removal is a "transport of goods to be provided on a set date",
  which L221-28 12° excludes from the withdrawal right: the texts read do not say; SimpleERP takes the conservative
  reading and keeps the withdrawal right.
- SimpleERP enforces: the removal quote binds once signed.

## Effective dates

2022-05-28 (texts above, read 2026-11-27).

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Arrêté du 27 avril 2010 (removal prices); Code de la consommation L224-63, L221-25, L221-28. A
change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

None stated in the texts read; a delivery note without the reserves procedure extends the objection period to 3 months
(L224-63). The payment ban of an off-premises contract is in [CR-FR-036](../consumer/cr-fr-036-withdrawal-right.md).

## Exceptions

The latest loading and unloading dates are not required for grouped trips (art. 2); a consumer who has paid the removal
company cannot be sued for payment by its subcontracted carrier (L224-64).

## Open questions

None: the law was read on 2026-11-27; the withdrawal right of a removal is a lawyer item above.
