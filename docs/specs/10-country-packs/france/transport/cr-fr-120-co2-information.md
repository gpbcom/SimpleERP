# ⚖️ CR-FR-120 CO2 information to clients

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The carrier tells the client the greenhouse-gas emissions of each transport service ([Code des transports, art. L1431-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000031066016), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Timing: for goods, on the date agreed with the client, otherwise within 2 months of the end of the service; for
  passengers, before the ticket is bought.
- Method: computed per segment using one means of transport, energy used (consumption rate times distance) times
  the emission factor of the energy set by order, upstream energy production included; values of level 1 (set by
  order) to level 4 (measured); averages over 3 years at most, updated as often
  ([Code des transports, art. D1431-1 to D1431-23](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000023086525/LEGISCTA000028996805/), read 2026-11-27).

## Legal references

- [Code des transports](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000031066016) L1431-3; décret n° 2017-639 and [art. D1431-1 to D1431-23](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000023086525/LEGISCTA000028996805/).

## Effective dates

L1431-3 in its current version in force since 2025-01-01 (loi n° 2021-1104) ([Code des transports, art. L1431-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000031066016), read 2026-11-27).
