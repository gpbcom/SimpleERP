# ⚖️ CR-FR-122 Cabotage limits

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A haulier established abroad may make at most 3 cabotage operations in France within 7 days after an international trip
into France, with the evidence on board.

## Values

- 3 operations within 7 days.

## Legal references

- Regulations (EC) 1071/2009, 1072/2009 and 1073/2009; Code des transports R3411-13.
- [Ecologie: cabotage](https://www.ecologie.gouv.fr/transport-international-marchandises-et-cabotage).

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.
