# ⚖️ CR-FR-116 Smart tachograph

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Vehicles over 3.5 t used for hire or reward carry a smart tachograph; vehicles operating abroad had to be retrofitted by
the set dates, and light vans over 2.5 t on international haulage since 2026-07-01.

## Values

- Retrofit deadlines: 2024-12-31 (first generation), 2025-08-18 (second generation version 1), 2026-07-01 (vans over 2.5
  t abroad).

## Legal references

- Regulation (EU) 165/2014.
- [Ecologie: tachograph](https://www.ecologie.gouv.fr/tachygraphe-numerique-et-nouveau-tachygraphe-intelligent-2019).
