# ⚖️ CR-FR-123 Fuel price indexation

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A road freight price is revised by law for the
change in energy (propulsion) costs between the contract and the transport, and the invoice shows those costs, for
contracts concluded from 2023-01-01 ([Code des transports, art. L3222-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000046194417), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Without a clause, the energy costs are set at the contract date from the CNR prices and the share of energy in the
  CNR indices, and revised by the change in the CNR index of that energy, or else the diesel index, between the
  contract and the transport; the same for autonomous refrigeration units ([Code des transports, art. L3222-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000041468961/), read 2026-11-27).
- Until 2022-12-31 (contracts concluded before 2023): the revision ran between the order and the transport, on the
  CNR diesel index (former version of L3222-1 and L3222-2).

## Legal references

- [Code des transports](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000046194417) L3222-1 and following.

## Effective dates

L3222-1 in its current version in force since 2023-01-01, for contracts concluded from that date (loi
n° 2022-1158) ([Code des transports, art. L3222-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000046194417), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc07-transport/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Code des transports L3222-1 and following A change flags this rule and warns the
administrators; the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

None: L3222-1 and L3222-2 state no penalty, the revision applies by operation of law ([Code des transports, art. L3222-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000046194417), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

None.
