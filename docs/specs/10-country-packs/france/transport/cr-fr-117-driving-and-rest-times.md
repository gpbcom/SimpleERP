# ⚖️ CR-FR-117 Driving and rest times

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Drivers stay within the daily and weekly driving limits and take the breaks and rests the regulation sets ([Ministère de la Transition écologique, temps de travail des conducteurs](https://www.ecologie.gouv.fr/politiques-publiques/temps-travail-conducteurs-routiers-transport-marchandises), read 2026-11-27);
the tachograph data is downloaded and kept ([Code des transports, art. R3315-11 3° c)](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000023086525/LEGISCTA000033450205/), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Driving: 9 hours a day, 10 hours twice a week; 56 hours a week; 90 hours over two consecutive weeks.
- Break: 45 minutes after 4 h 30 of driving, or 15 then 30 minutes.
- Daily rest: 11 hours (or 3 + 9 hours), reduced to 9 hours up to three times between weekly rests.
- Weekly rest: 45 hours, reduced to 24 hours once over two weeks, compensated before the end of the third week
  ([Ministère de la Transition écologique](https://www.ecologie.gouv.fr/politiques-publiques/temps-travail-conducteurs-routiers-transport-marchandises), read 2026-11-27).
- Downloads: driver card at most every 28 days, vehicle unit at most every 95 days; downloaded data available 365
  days at the driver's base; download certificates kept one year ([Arrêté du 6 juillet 2005, art. 1 and 3](https://legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000607239/2026-09-04), read 2026-11-27).

## Legal references

- Regulation (EC) 561/2006, as the [ministry page](https://www.ecologie.gouv.fr/politiques-publiques/temps-travail-conducteurs-routiers-transport-marchandises) applies it; Code des transports R3315-10, R3315-11.

## Effective dates

Regulation (EC) 561/2006 in force since 2007-04-11 (art. 29), as amended by Regulation (EU) 2020/1054
([Regulation (EC) 561/2006, consolidated text of 2020-08-20, Publications Office](http://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/dd7bc0dc-e6dc-11ea-ad25-01aa75ed71a1.0007.02/DOC_1), read 2026-11-27); the download rules since
2005-10-11, the certificate rule since 2022-11-27 ([Arrêté du 6 juillet 2005](https://legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000607239/2026-09-04), read 2026-11-27).

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Regulation (EC) 561/2006 A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected
rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Contravention of the 4th class for the smaller overruns of driving limits and short rests (art. R3315-10, since
2022-08-13); of the 5th class for the larger overruns, weekly rest breaches and records not kept (art. R3315-11,
since 2024-12-31) ([Code des transports, art. R3315-10 and R3315-11](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000023086525/LEGISCTA000033450205/), read 2026-11-27).

None.
