# ⚖️ CR-FR-119 Drivers' initial and continuous training

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Professional drivers hold their initial qualification and renew their continuous training on time ([Code des transports, art. R3314-1 and R3314-10](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000023086525/LEGISCTA000033450205/), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Initial training: long route at least 280 hours, accelerated route (FIMO) at least 140 hours (R3314-2, R3314-5).
- Continuous training every 5 years, the first within 5 years of the initial qualification (R3314-10); 35 hours,
  in parts of at least 7 hours (R3314-11); may be done in the year before the deadline (R3314-12)
  ([Code des transports, art. R3314-1 to R3314-14](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000023086525/LEGISCTA000033450205/), read 2026-11-27).

## Legal references

- [Code des transports](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000023086525/LEGISCTA000033450205/) R3314-1 to R3314-14, R3315-7, R3315-8.

## Effective dates

R3314-1 and R3314-10 in force since 2022-02-01 ([Code des transports](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000023086525/LEGISCTA000033450205/), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc07-transport/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Code des transports R3314-1, R3314-10 A change flags this rule and warns the administrators;
the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Contravention of the 4th class per driver for an employer who does not ensure the training (art. R3315-7);
3rd class for a driver who cannot show proof at once, 4th class after 5 days (art. R3315-8) ([Code des transports, art. R3315-7 and R3315-8](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000023086525/LEGISCTA000033450205/), read 2026-11-27).
