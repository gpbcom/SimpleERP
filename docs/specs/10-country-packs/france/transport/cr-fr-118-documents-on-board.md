# ⚖️ CR-FR-118 Documents on board and the consignment note

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every vehicle carrying goods by road in France carries its licence copy, the consignment note and, when they apply, the
rental document, the driver attestation, the cabotage evidence and the driver's contract; the company keeps consignment
notes 2 years.

## Values

- Consignment note, rental, cabotage evidence and contract may be shown on paper or electronically.
- Consignment notes kept 2 years.

## Legal references

- Code des transports R3411-13 (since 2023-12-22).
