# ⚖️ CR-FR-115 Haulage registration and licences

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A road haulage company is authorised by the regional prefect once it meets establishment, good repute, financial and
professional capacity, is registered in the national register of road transport companies, and keeps the licence
matching its vehicles.

## Values

- Licence: EU licence when a vehicle exceeds 3.5 t; domestic licence up to 3.5 t; up to 10 years, not transferable.
- Financial capacity (mainland): 9 000 EUR for the first vehicle over 3.5 t and 5 000 EUR per extra one; 1 800 EUR and
  900 EUR up to 3.5 t.
- The prefect decides within 3 months.
