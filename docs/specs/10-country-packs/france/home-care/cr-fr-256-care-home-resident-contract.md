# ⚖️ CR-FR-256 Care-home resident contract

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A care home (EHPAD) and the resident sign a written stay contract that sets out the services and their prices, with a
withdrawal right.

## Values

- Contract needed as soon as the stay may exceed 2 months.
- Homes entitled to social aid (ASH): contract handed over within 15 days of admission and signed within 1 month; other
  homes: signed before admission.
- Content: care objectives, living conditions, price of each service (also during absence or hospitalisation),
  withdrawal rights, minimum mandatory services, annual price changes; objectives and services updated each year.
- Withdrawal: in writing within 15 days of signing (or of admission, if later), no notice; the resident pays for the
  time already spent.
- Termination by the resident: in writing at any time after that; a 48-hour reflection period after the home is
  notified; notice period only if the contract states it.
- Termination by the home: breach of the contract or rules (unless linked to impaired faculties), closure, or the
  resident no longer meets admission criteria or needs care the home does not give.
