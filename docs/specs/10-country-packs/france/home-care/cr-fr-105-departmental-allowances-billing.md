# ⚖️ CR-FR-105 Billing the département for allowances

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

(Proposed) For recipients of the elderly or disability allowance, the provider bills the département its share and the
recipient the rest.

## Values

- (Proposed) The share per care plan and hour.
