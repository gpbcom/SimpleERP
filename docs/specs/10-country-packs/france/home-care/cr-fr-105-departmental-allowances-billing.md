# ⚖️ CR-FR-105 Billing the département for allowances

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The recipient is paid the allowance and pays the provider; the département may pay its share directly to the provider.
Read for the elderly allowance (APA): the département may pay the part of the allowance for a home-help service directly
to the service the recipient chose ([Code de l'action sociale et des familles, art.
L232-15](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000036390671), read 2026-11-27); the recipient pays a
share of the care plan ([Service-public F10009](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F10009),
verified 2026-06-01, read 2026-11-27). For the disability allowance (PCH): the human-help element is paid directly to
whoever bears the cost when the recipient does not pay it ([CASF, art.
L245-8](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000006157603/), in force since 2016-01-28, read 2026-11-27); a
recipient who uses a provider declares it and the sums paid to it to the département ([CASF, art.
D245-51](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006074069/LEGISCTA000006178373/), since 2013-10-21,
read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- The recipient's share (participation) depends on the monthly resources and the care plan: none up to 933.89 EUR a
  month, rising from 0 % to 90 % of the plan between 933.89 EUR and 3 439.31 EUR, 90 % above; a couple's resources are
  divided by 1.7; the plan is capped by GIR level (2 080.33, 1 682.30, 1 215.99 and 811.52 EUR a month for GIR 1 to 4)
  ([Service-public F10009](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F10009), verified 2026-06-01, read
  2026-11-27).

## Legal references

- Code de l'action sociale et des familles L232-1 to L232-20, R232-10 to D232-11-1; décret n° 2016-2010.

## Effective dates

Direct payment to the service: L232-15 in its current version since 2018-01-01 ([Code de l'action sociale et des familles, art. L232-15](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000036390671), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CASF. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships in a
pack update (D294).

## Penalty

None stated in CASF L232-15; the services paid this way are subject to a quality control ([Code de l'action sociale et des familles, art. L232-15](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000036390671), read 2026-11-27).
