# ⚖️ CR-FR-230 CESU vouchers

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The declarative CESU declares the pay of home staff; the prefinanced CESU is a prepaid voucher used to pay.

## Values

- Declarative: digital or 20-stub booklet; declares pay to Urssaf, pays contributions, produces payslips.
- Prefinanced: fixed-value voucher, cheque booklet or e-CESU; pays an approved or declared provider, a childcare
  facility, an employee directly or an approved childminder.
- Funders: employers, the CSE, and bodies giving social benefits (departments, social centres, funds, mutuelles).
- A prefinanced voucher is paid by cheque, transfer, cash or Cesu+ if joined.
- If a Caf or MSA contribution is received for an approved childminder or home childcare employee, declare through
  Pajemploi.

## Legal references

- [Service-public F13607](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F13607).
- Code du travail L1271-1 to L1271-8; Code de la securite sociale L133-5-6 to L133-5-12.
