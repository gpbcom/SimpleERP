# ⚖️ CR-FR-102 Personal-services declaration, approval and authorisation

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A personal-services company declares its activities; services to fragile people also need the State's approval
(agrément) or the département's authorisation, and quotes and invoices show the registration numbers.

## Values

- Declaration: any of the 26 activities, on NOVA.
- Approval (5 years): care of children under 3 and of disabled children, help to elderly, disabled or chronically ill
  people in agency or placement mode.
- Authorisation (15 years, by the département): help and accompaniment of fragile people in provider mode.
- Quarterly activity statements and a yearly report in NOVA.

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Code du travail L7232-1-2. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the
corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

No tax or social advantage without it; withdrawal after repeated default.

## Exceptions

Micro-entrepreneurs and firms under 11 employees exempt from the exclusive-activity condition since 2025-01-01.
