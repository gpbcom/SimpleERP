# ⚖️ CR-FR-059 Recordings are announced

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

No meeting or call is recorded without everyone being told first, and anyone can stop it.

## Values

- Online: a named recorder and a chat notice.
- In person: the organiser confirms everyone was told.
- Phone: a spoken message first.

## Legal references

- [Code pénal](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006070719/) art. 226-1.
- [GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) art. 13, 14.

## Effective dates

Code pénal 226-1 in its current version in force since 2024-03-23 (loi n° 2024-247) ([Code pénal, art. 226-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000049312755), read 2026-11-27).
