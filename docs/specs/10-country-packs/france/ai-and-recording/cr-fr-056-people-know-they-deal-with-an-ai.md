# ⚖️ CR-FR-056 People know they deal with an AI

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Whoever talks to an AI, or reads content it wrote, is told so.

## Values

- Assistant, support agents and AI calls say they are AI.
- Published content: "written with AI help"; AI images and videos labelled where the network allows.

## Legal references

- [AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) art. 50.

## Effective dates

Art. 50 applies from 2026-08-02.
