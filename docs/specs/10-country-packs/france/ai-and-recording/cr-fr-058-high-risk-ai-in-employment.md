# ⚖️ CR-FR-058 AI in recruitment and work is high-risk

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

AI that scores candidates or employees is high-risk: off until the company accepts its deployer duties, every score
validated by a person, logged and explained, never the only basis of a decision.

## Values

- Deployer duties: information, human validation, logs, bias checks, explanation on request.
- Provider duties: risk management, documentation, conformity assessment, CE marking, EU database registration.

## Legal references

- [AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) Annex III point 4, art. 9 to 15,
  26.
- [GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) art. 22.

## Effective dates

- Until 2027-12-01: the high-risk duties do not apply yet to Annex III uses (employment included); the AI Omnibus,
  in force since 2026-07-27, extended their date.
- From 2027-12-02: they apply to Annex III uses; from 2028-08-02 to Annex I systems
  ([European Commission, AI Act](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai), read 2026-11-27).

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: AI Act Annex III, art. 26, 113. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the
corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Administrative fine up to 15 000 000 EUR or, for an undertaking, 3 % of its worldwide yearly turnover, whichever is
higher; for an SME, whichever is lower for a breach of the provider's (art. 16) or the deployer's (art. 26)
obligations ([AI Act Service Desk, art. 99(4) and (6)](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-99), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

None.
