# ⚖️ CR-FR-028 Daily, monthly and yearly closures

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The till closes each day, month and fiscal year, each closure storing the period's grand total and the perpetual grand
total.

## Values

- Closures: daily, monthly, yearly.
- Totals: period grand total and perpetual grand total; counters reset only on new hardware or software, never on a
  minor update.

## Legal references

- [BOI-TVA-DECLA-30-10-30](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/doctrine/pgp/10691-PGP) §170.

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f10-point-of-sale/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: BOI-TVA-DECLA-30-10-30 §170. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the
corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

As CR-FR certified till software.

## Exceptions

None.
