# ⚖️ CR-FR-029 Till data kept six years

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every line of every sale is kept six years; keeping only the daily totals is not enough.

## Values

- Retention: 6 years, line by line.

## Legal references

- [Livre des procédures fiscales](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006069583/) art. L102 B.
- [BOI-TVA-DECLA-30-10-30](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/doctrine/pgp/10691-PGP) §155 to §200.

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f10-point-of-sale/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: LPF L102 B. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

As CR-FR certified till software.

## Exceptions

None.
