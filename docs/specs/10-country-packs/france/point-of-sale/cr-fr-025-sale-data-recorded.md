# ⚖️ CR-FR-025 Data recorded for each sale

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each sale records the data the tax administration needs, at the time of the sale.

## Values

- Receipt number, date and time to the minute, till number.
- Each line: label, quantity, unit price, line total excluding VAT, VAT rate.
- Total including VAT, payment methods, and the trail of every correction.

## Legal references

- [BOI-TVA-DECLA-30-10-30](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/doctrine/pgp/10691-PGP) §50.

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f10-point-of-sale/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: BOI-TVA-DECLA-30-10-30 §50. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the
corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty
