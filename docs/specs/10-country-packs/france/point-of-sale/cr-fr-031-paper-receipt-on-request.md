# ⚖️ CR-FR-031 Paper receipts on request only

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Till receipts, card slips and discount vouchers are not printed or handed out automatically; the customer gets one on
request, or a digital receipt; the shop displays the legal notice.

## Values

- Default: no print; print on request; offer a digital receipt.
- Notice: "sauf exception légale, l'impression et la remise des tickets de caisse et de carte bancaire ne sont réalisées
  qu'à la demande du consommateur".

## Legal references

- [Code de l'environnement](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006074220/) art. L541-15-10 IV,
  D541-370 to D541-372; Code de commerce L441-9.
- [Service-public F37910](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F37910).

## Effective dates

Code de l'environnement D541-370 to D541-372 in force since 2023-08-01 (décret n° 2022-1565, as amended by décret
n° 2023-237) ([Code de l'environnement, art. D541-370 to D541-372](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006074220/LEGISCTA000046739191/), read 2026-11-27).

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CEnv L541-15-10. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule

## Penalty

Not stated in the source read.

## Exceptions

Receipts showing a legal conformity period (appliances); weighing instruments; cancelled, failed, pre-authorised or
credited card payments; automatic tickets for a service (parking, toll); professional purchases needing an invoice;
services of 25 EUR or more needing a note.
