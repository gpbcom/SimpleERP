# ⚖️ CR-FR-030 Till archives

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Till data is archived at least once a year in an open format with a French notice; data is purged only after a complete
archive is written to external media, and an archive can be restored in the format the administration asks for.

## Values

- Frequency: at most yearly, chosen by the company.
- Open format, French explanatory notice, traceable generation.

## Legal references

- [BOI-TVA-DECLA-30-10-30](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/doctrine/pgp/10691-PGP) §220 to §260.
- [Code général des impôts](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006069577/) art. 286, version in
  force since 2026-06-27.

## Effective dates

Since 2018-01-01, for every VAT-registered business recording payments from consumers in a till system ([impots.gouv.fr, champ d'application de l'obligation](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/professionnel/questions/quel-est-le-champ-dapplication-de-lobligation-de-detenir-un-logiciel-de), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f10-point-of-sale/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: BOI-TVA-DECLA-30-10-30; CGI art. 286. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators;
the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

As CR-FR certified till software.

## Exceptions

None.
