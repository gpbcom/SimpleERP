# ⚖️ CR-FR-032 Restaurant and bar bills

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A restaurant or bar always gives the customer a bill.

## Values

- The establishment's name and address.
- Each item's price, taxes and service included, matching the displayed prices.
- The total.

## Legal references

- [Service-public F22387](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F22387).
- Arrêté n° 25-361 du 8 juin 1967, as amended.
