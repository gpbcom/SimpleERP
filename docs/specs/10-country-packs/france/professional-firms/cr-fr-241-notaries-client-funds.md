# ⚖️ CR-FR-241 Notaries' client funds

**Status:** 🔄 (Proposed) values to read in the official source

## Statement

(Proposed) A notary receives the funds of the parties and the taxes due to the state for a deed, holds them at the state
deposit bank apart from the office's own money, and pays them out only as the deed requires, once it is signed. The
office keeps an account per deed and the regulator may inspect it. Lawyers' funds
([CR-FR-151](cr-fr-151-carpa-client-funds.md)) follow a different regime.

## Values

- (Proposed) Funds held at the state deposit bank.
- (Proposed) An account per deed.
- (Proposed) Payment only once the deed is signed.
- (Proposed) simple-ERP enforces: client funds are held at the state deposit bank.
- Read on Legifrance, [decree 45-0117 of 19 December 1945](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000682446)
  (consolidated text; article 15 in force since 30 May 2014, articles 16 and 16 A since 30 November 2004; read 15
  November 2026); the cash-limit amount (set by an order), payment only once the deed is signed, inspection by the
  regulator and a full one-account-per-deed rule are not on the articles:
- Read: art. 15, cash kept at the office for more than two working days may not exceed a figure set by order of the
  justice minister, and never more than 5 % of all the funds the notary holds.
- Read: art. 15, sums held for third parties beyond that limit are deposited on current accounts (comptes de
  disponibilites courantes) opened at the Caisse des depots, through the public finance accountants acting as its
  agents; only third-party funds may go on these accounts; debits only to settle the affairs that gave rise to the
  deposits; except where assets are frozen under the Code monetaire et financier (book V, title VI, chapter II).
- Read: art. 15, sums still held on current accounts after three months are transferred to compulsory deposit accounts
  (comptes de depots obligatoires) at the Caisse des depots; these accounts move only with the current accounts, and
  each movement is identified affair by affair; a joint order of the economy and justice ministers sets the way
  movements are made.
- Read: art. 16, each notary keeps accounts of cash receipts and payments and of securities in and out for clients: at
  least a cash journal, a register of deed costs, a cash ledger, a securities journal and a quarterly balance register,
  on a model set by the justice minister; the two journals are numbered and initialled by the chamber of notaries.
- Read: art. 16 A, a receipt from a numbered book is given for every sum received, with the date, the payer's name and
  address, the cause of the payment and the destination of the funds; the series has no gaps.
- Read: the decree is amended by decree 2014-551 of 27 May 2014, art. 33, and decree 2004-1304 of 26 November 2004; no
  penalty is on the page.
