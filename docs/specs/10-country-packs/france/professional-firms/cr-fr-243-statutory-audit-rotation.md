# ⚖️ CR-FR-243 Statutory auditor rotation

**Status:** 🔄 (Proposed) values to read in the official source

## Statement

(Proposed) A statutory auditor is appointed for a mandate of several financial years; the firm must check its
independence before accepting, and for public-interest entities the signing partner must rotate after a set number of
years, and the firm itself after a longer period.

## Values

- (Proposed) Mandate length.
- (Proposed) Independence check before acceptance.
- (Proposed) Rotation periods, kept as data and to confirm from the official texts.
- (Proposed) SimpleERP enforces: partners rotate as the law requires.
- Read on Legifrance,
  [Code de commerce L821-40 to L821-52](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000005634379/LEGISCTA000006161409/)
  (in force since 1 January 2024, read 15 November 2026); the rotation of the signing partner (Regulation 537/2014
  article 17(7)) and the independence check before acceptance (L821-25 to L821-36) are not read:
- Read: L821-44, mandate of six financial years; the duties end after the meeting that approves the sixth year's
  accounts.
- Read: L821-46, for a voluntary appointment or the cases of L821-43, the company may limit the mandate to three years.
- Read: L821-45 I, a single auditor of a public-interest entity cannot certify for more than 10 years; a new six-year
  mandate is possible if the conditions of paragraphs 2 to 5 of article 16 of Regulation (EU) 537/2014 are met.
- Read: L821-45 II, the 10 years may be extended to at most 24 years when the entity then uses several auditors (joint
  report), or an independent third-party body and an auditor.
- Read: L821-45 III, the Haute autorite de l'audit may allow an exceptional extension of at most 2 years.
- Read: L821-45 IV, the auditor, or a network member in the EU, cannot take a mandate at that entity within 4 years of
  the end of its mandate.
- Read: L821-41, entities that publish consolidated accounts appoint at least two auditors; L821-42, a public-interest
  entity appoints at least one; L821-43, controlling groups above decree thresholds appoint at least one.
- Read: in force since 1 January 2024 (ordinance 2023-1142 of 6 December 2023, art. 17 and 32; Regulation (EU) 537/2014
  articles 16, 17, 18); the former L823-3-1 is now L821-45; no penalty is on the page.
