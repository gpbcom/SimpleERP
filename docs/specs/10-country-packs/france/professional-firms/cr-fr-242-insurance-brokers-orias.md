# ⚖️ CR-FR-242 Insurance brokers and ORIAS

**Status:** 🔄 (Proposed) values to read in the official source

## Statement

(Proposed) An insurance broker must be registered with the national registry of insurance intermediaries (ORIAS),
renewed each year with proof of professional-liability insurance and a financial guarantee, and may place policies only
while registered. The broker gives the client advice and information before the policy.

## Values

- (Proposed) Yearly renewal of the registration.
- (Proposed) Professional-liability insurance and financial guarantee.
- (Proposed) Advice and information before the policy.
- (Proposed) simple-ERP enforces: brokers keep their ORIAS registration current.
- Read on [Service-public R13044](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/R13044) (verified 24 May 2019);
  professional liability insurance, the financial guarantee, the renewal calendar and the conditions of placing policies
  are not read:
- Read: registration on the national intermediaries register is mandatory for anyone who presents or proposes insurance
  or reinsurance contracts, helps conclude them or prepares them; it is renewable each year and the register is public.
