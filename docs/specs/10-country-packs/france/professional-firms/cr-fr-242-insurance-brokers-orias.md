# ⚖️ CR-FR-242 Insurance brokers and ORIAS

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An insurance broker must be registered with the national registry of insurance intermediaries (ORIAS),
renewed each year with proof of professional-liability insurance and a financial guarantee, and may place policies only
while registered ([Code des assurances, art. L512-1, L514-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006073984/LEGISCTA000006142927/), read 2026-11-27). The renewal takes effect on 1 March, asked
at least one month before the registration ends, with the liability insurance and financial guarantee certificates
where they apply and the fee ([Code des assurances, art. A512-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006073984/LEGISCTA000006142607/), in force since 2022-04-01, read 2026-11-27). The broker
gives the client advice and information before the policy ([Code des assurances, art. L521-4](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006073984/LEGISCTA000006142927/), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Yearly renewal of the registration on the single public register, after paying a yearly fee of at most 250 EUR;
  unpaid within 30 days of the notice, the registration is struck off ([Code des assurances, art. L512-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006073984/LEGISCTA000006142927/), read 2026-11-27).
- Professional-liability insurance, unless the insurer or intermediary it acts for covers it (L512-6); a financial
  guarantee for an intermediary that handles funds for insurers or insured persons, unless an insurer's written
  mandate covers collecting premiums (L512-7) ([Code des assurances](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006073984/LEGISCTA000006142927/), in force since 2018-10-01, read 2026-11-27).
- Advice and information before the policy: the distributor writes down the client's demands and needs, gives
  objective information and advises a consistent contract with the reasons ([Code des assurances, art. L521-4](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000036920558),
  in force since 2018-10-01, read 2026-11-27).
- SimpleERP enforces: brokers keep their ORIAS registration current.
- Read on [Service-public R13044](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/R13044) (verified 24 May 2019);
  professional liability insurance, the financial guarantee, the renewal calendar and the conditions of placing policies
  are not read:
- Read: registration on the national intermediaries register is mandatory for anyone who presents or proposes insurance
  or reinsurance contracts, helps conclude them or prepares them; it is renewable each year and the register is public.

## Legal references

- Code des assurances L512-1, L512-6, L512-7, L514-1, L521-4, A512-1 to A512-4.

## Effective dates

L512-1 in its current version since 2019-05-24; L512-6, L512-7 and L521-4 since 2018-10-01 ([Code des assurances](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006073984/LEGISCTA000006142927/),
read 2026-11-27).

## Penalty

Breaches of the chapter on registration: 2 years in prison and 6 000 EUR, or one of them ([Code des assurances,
art. L514-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006073984/LEGISCTA000006142927/), read 2026-11-27).
