# ⚖️ CR-FR-255 Certification body accreditation

**Status:** 🔄 (Proposed) values to read in the official source

## Statement

(Proposed) A certification body works under an accreditation that requires qualified and impartial auditors, a decision
separate from the audit, and a surveillance programme between certifications.

## Values

- (Proposed) Qualified and impartial auditors.
- (Proposed) Decision separate from the audit.
- (Proposed) Surveillance audits.
- (Proposed) simple-ERP enforces: certificates lapse without their surveillance audit.
- Read on Legifrance,
  [Code de la consommation L433-3 to L433-10](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032222803/)
  (read 15 November 2026); qualified and impartial auditors, the decision separate from the audit and surveillance
  audits come from the accreditation standard and decree 2008-1401, not read:
- Read: L433-3, certification is the activity by which a body, distinct from the manufacturer, importer, seller,
  provider or client, attests that a product or service meets the characteristics of a certification standard; the body
  writes the standard after hearing the interested parties.
- Read: L433-4, only bodies accredited by the national accreditation body, or by that of another EU member state signed
  up to the multilateral mutual-recognition agreements, may certify.
- Read: L433-5, a body not yet accredited may certify, under conditions set by decree, if it has applied for
  accreditation.
- Read: L433-6, any reference to certification in advertising, labelling or commercial documents carries clear
  information on the certified characteristics.
- Read: L433-9, it is forbidden to issue a certificate in breach of L433-3 to L433-7, or to suggest falsely that a body
  meets these conditions; a prohibition only, no penalty is on the page.
- Read: L433-3 to L433-6 and L433-9 to L433-10 since 1 July 2016; L433-7 and L433-8 since 15 December 2019 (ordinances
  2016-301 and 2019-1169); L433-8 excludes agricultural, food and sea products and some official marks.
