# ⚖️ CR-FR-254 Advertising agent mandate and media prices

**Status:** 🔄 (Proposed) values to read in the official source

## Statement

(Proposed) An agency that buys advertising space for a client acts with a written mandate, and the media purchase is
shown to the client with its real price and the agency's fee apart.

## Values

- (Proposed) Written mandate.
- (Proposed) Real price and fee shown apart.
- (Proposed) simple-ERP enforces: media are rebilled at cost plus the agreed fee.
- Read on Legifrance, [law 93-122 of 29 January 1993](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000711604)
  articles 20 to 26, as originally published in the Journal officiel (not a consolidated text; read 15 November 2026);
  the consolidated current text and its penalties were not read, so no amount of the 1993 text is applied here:
- Read: art. 20, the intermediary buys advertising space or print-distribution services only for an advertiser and under
  a written mandate contract.
- Read: art. 20, the contract sets the agent's pay, detailing the services and the pay of each, and lists the other
  services outside the mandate and their total pay; any discount or price advantage given by the seller must appear on
  the invoice to the advertiser and may be kept by the intermediary only under an express clause of the mandate.
- Read: art. 20, even if the advertiser does not pay the seller directly, the seller sends the invoice directly to the
  advertiser.
- Read: art. 21, the agent may receive no payment other than from the principal, and no pay or benefit from the seller.
- Read: art. 23, the seller reports to the advertiser within the month after the advertisement runs; where a mandate is
  used, the report is due from the seller to the agent and from the agent to the advertiser.
- Read: art. 24 and 25 (media-planning advisers), general terms must state financial ties with sellers; pay from the
  seller is forbidden.
- Read: the penalties of article 25 exist for a missing written contract, an agent paid by anyone but its principal and
  a seller who does not invoice the advertiser directly, with possible exclusion of legal persons from public contracts
  for up to five years; their amounts are in francs in the 1993 text and are not read as current.
- Not read: "media rebilled at cost plus the agreed fee" is not stated as such; the text only requires the discount to
  appear on the invoice and the agent's pay to be fixed in the mandate; the start date is not on the page.
