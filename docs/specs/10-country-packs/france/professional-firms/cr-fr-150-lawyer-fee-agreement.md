# ⚖️ CR-FR-150 Lawyers' fee agreement

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A lawyer agrees fees in writing with the client (amount or method, expected fees and disbursements), never by a share of
the result alone, asks reasonable advances, and gives a detailed final statement.

## Values

- Written agreement, except urgency, force majeure or legal aid.
- No pure result-based fee (quota litis).
- No fee sharing with non-lawyers.

## Legal references

- Loi n° 71-1130 art. 10; national rules of the profession (RIN) art. 11.
- [RIN title 2](https://www.cnb.avocat.fr/rin/titre-deuxieme-des-activites).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Penalty

Disciplinary.

## Exceptions

Urgency, force majeure, legal aid.
