# ⚖️ CR-FR-149 Anti-money-laundering vigilance

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

(Proposed) A regulated professional identifies each client and beneficial owner, applies risk-based vigilance, keeps the
documents, and reports suspicions to the financial intelligence unit.

## Values

- Read: since 2025-02-01, lawyers' and CARPA's suspicion reports are filed electronically on ERMES, through the bar
  president.
- (Proposed) Retention period and penalties.
