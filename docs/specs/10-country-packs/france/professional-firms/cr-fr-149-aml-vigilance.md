# ⚖️ CR-FR-149 Anti-money-laundering vigilance

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A regulated professional identifies each client and beneficial owner, applies risk-based vigilance, keeps the
documents, and reports suspicions to the financial intelligence unit (chartered accountants L561-2 12°; lawyers and
notaries 13°; L561-4-1, L561-5, L561-12, L561-15) ([Code monétaire et financier, art. L561-1 to L561-50](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006072026/LEGISCTA000006154830/), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Read: since 2025-02-01, lawyers' and CARPA's suspicion reports are filed electronically on ERMES, through the bar
  president.
- Retention: 5 years from the end of the relationship, and 5 years from each operation for its records
  ([Code monétaire et financier, art. L561-12](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000041577784), read 2026-11-27).
- Penalties: injunction, temporary ban on management duties, and a fine of up to 1 million EUR or twice the benefit
  drawn, imposed by the professional's supervisor ([Code monétaire et financier, art. L561-36-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000042498737), read 2026-11-27).

## Legal references

- Code monétaire et financier L561-2 and following.
- [National Bar Council: AML](https://www.cnb.avocat.fr/lutte-contre-la-fraude-et-le-blanchiment-d-argent).

## Effective dates

L561-5 and L561-15 since 2016-12-03, L561-4-1 and L561-12 since 2020-02-14, L561-36-3 since 2020-11-06, L561-2 in
its current version since 2026-08-05 ([Code monétaire et financier](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006072026/LEGISCTA000006154830/), read 2026-11-27).

## Penalty

Up to 1 million EUR, or twice the benefit drawn, with an injunction and a temporary ban on management duties;
the decision may be published ([Code monétaire et financier, art. L561-36-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000042498737), read 2026-11-27).
