# ⚖️ CR-FR-154 Lawyers' professional secrecy

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A lawyer's secrecy is general, absolute and unlimited in time: consultations, correspondence, the client's name, the
agenda and fund handling; the lawyer enforces it on staff and partners.

## Values

- Limits: the lawyer's own defence and cases the law allows.
- Third parties' requests answered only to the client.

## Legal references

- RIN art. 2.1 to 2.3, 3.2; Code pénal 226-13.
- [RIN title 1](https://www.cnb.avocat.fr/rin/titre-premier-des-principes).

## Effective dates

Code of ethics article 4 (secrecy of public order, absolute, general and unlimited in time) since 2023-07-03
([décret n° 2023-552 du 30 juin 2023, art. 4](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000047774060), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc11-professional-firms/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: RIN art. 2.1. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Disciplinary fault; criminal under Code pénal 226-13: 1 year and 15 000 EUR ([Code pénal, art. 226-13](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006417945), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

The lawyer's own defence; cases the law allows.
