# ⚖️ CR-FR-152 Chartered accountants' engagement letter

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A chartered accountant agrees each assignment in an engagement letter ([décret n° 2012-432, art. 151](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000039401698), read 2026-11-27)
and keeps clients' information secret ([ordonnance n° 45-2138, art. 21](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000033678954), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Content: a written contract with each client that defines the assignment and the rights and obligations of each
  party, refers to the professional rules of the national council, may take the form of an engagement letter, and
  states any mandate given and, if any, its length (by default the length of the letter)
  ([décret n° 2012-432, art. 151](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000039401698), read 2026-11-27).

## Legal references

- Ordonnance n° 45-2138 art. 21; décret n° 2012-432 art. 141 to 169 (the profession's code of ethics).

## Effective dates

Article 151 in its current version since 2023-01-01 (décret n° 2022-1732) ([décret n° 2012-432, art. 151](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000039401698),
read 2026-11-27).

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Ordonnance 45-2138. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule
ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Secrecy: the penalties of Code pénal 226-13, 1 year and 15 000 EUR ([ordonnance n° 45-2138, art. 21](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000033678954);
[Code pénal, art. 226-13](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006417945), read 2026-11-27). A missing engagement letter: no penalty stated in article 151.
