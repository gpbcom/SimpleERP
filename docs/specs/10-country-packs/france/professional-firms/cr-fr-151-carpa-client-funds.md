# ⚖️ CR-FR-151 Lawyers' client funds through the CARPA

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A lawyer who handles client funds deposits them without delay with the bar's CARPA, escrow funds with a copy of the
escrow agreement.

## Values

- Without delay.
- CARPA also reports suspicions.

## Legal references

- RIN art. 6.2, 6.3.2; décret n° 91-1197.
- [RIN title 2](https://www.cnb.avocat.fr/rin/titre-deuxieme-des-activites).

## Effective dates

Deposit on a CARPA account (décret n° 91-1197, art. 240) in its current version since 1996-07-09
([décret n° 91-1197, art. 236 to 242](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/JORFTEXT000000356568/LEGISCTA000006145648/), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc11-professional-firms/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: RIN art. 6.2. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Disciplinary.

## Exceptions

None.
