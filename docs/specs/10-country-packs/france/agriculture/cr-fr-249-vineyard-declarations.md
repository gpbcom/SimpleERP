# ⚖️ CR-FR-249 Harvest and stock declarations

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

(⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) A grower declares the harvest after it, and declares the stock of wine on a fixed date each
year, from registers kept up to date. Each appellation sets a maximum yield per hectare and the grower declares the
plots by area, variety and appellation. Wine removed for consumption is liable to excise
([CR-FR-234](../tax-and-vat/cr-fr-234-alcohol-excise.md)).

## Values

- Harvest declaration and yearly stock declaration: harvest and production declarations by 10 December for growers,
  10 January for co-operatives and merchant-winemakers ([arrêté du 23 novembre 2023, art. 1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000048493459), read 2026-11-27); the stock held
  on 31 July, declared by 10 September; all online only ([douane.gouv.fr: online wine declarations](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/fiche/les-declarations-viticoles-en-ligne-obligatoires), updated
  2026-08-07, read 2026-11-27).
- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) Yield limit by appellation: set in each appellation's specifications, not read. Plots:
  plantings, uprootings and re-graftings are declared on PARCEL within one month of the end of the works
  ([douane.gouv.fr: PARCEL](https://www.douane.gouv.fr/services-en-ligne/declarations-foncieres-parcel), read
  2026-11-27).
- Dates and limits kept as data.
- SimpleERP enforces: harvest and stock declarations are filed on time.
- Read on [Service-public R33065](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/R33065) (verified 20 August
  2024); the deadlines, the harvest declaration, yields per hectare and plot declarations are not read:
- Read: holders of wine and must stock declare it each year, online only; the declaration covers the stock still in the
  warehouses on 31 July.

## Legal references

- Regulations (EU) 2018/273 and 2018/274; arrêté du 23 novembre 2023; douane.gouv.fr (VENDANGES, STOCK, PARCEL).

## Effective dates

Harvest and production deadlines from the 2023-2024 campaign ([arrêté du 23 novembre 2023](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000048493459), read 2026-11-27).
