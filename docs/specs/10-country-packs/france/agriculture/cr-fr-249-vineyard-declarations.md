# ⚖️ CR-FR-249 Harvest and stock declarations

**Status:** 🔄 (Proposed) values to read in the official source

## Statement

(Proposed) A grower declares the harvest after it, and declares the stock of wine on a fixed date each year, from
registers kept up to date. Each appellation sets a maximum yield per hectare and the grower declares the plots by area,
variety and appellation. Wine removed for consumption is liable to excise
([CR-FR-234](../tax-and-vat/cr-fr-234-alcohol-excise.md)).

## Values

- (Proposed) Harvest declaration and yearly stock declaration.
- (Proposed) Yield limit by appellation.
- (Proposed) Dates and limits kept as data.
- (Proposed) SimpleERP enforces: harvest and stock declarations are filed on time.
- Read on [Service-public R33065](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/R33065) (verified 20 August
  2024); the deadlines, the harvest declaration, yields per hectare and plot declarations are not read:
- Read: holders of wine and must stock declare it each year, online only; the declaration covers the stock still in the
  warehouses on 31 July.
