# ⚖️ CR-FR-162 Animal identification and herd register

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Farm animals are identified and their births, movements and deaths notified within the set
delays; the herd register is kept.

## Values

- Cattle: tagged at birth or within 20 days (art. 13, since 2025-01-16); birth notified within 7 days of tagging
  (art. 39), entry and exit within 7 days (art. 40, 42), deaths notified (art. 26); herd register kept at least 3 years
  (art. 3) ([arrêté du 6 août 2013, bovine identification](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000027876223), read 2026-11-27).
- Sheep and goats: identified within 6 months of birth and before leaving the birth holding (art. 9); movements
  notified within 7 days (art. 19-1), except from 2026-04-28 to 2026-05-28, when the delay was 48 hours
  ([arrêté du 30 mars 2026](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000053762000), read 2026-11-27); register kept at least 5 years (art. 23) ([arrêté du 19 décembre 2005](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000633936),
  read 2026-11-27).
- Pigs: identified with the site's number before leaving it (and an individual number for breeding pigs), imported
  pigs within 30 days (art. 8); movements notified within 7 days of the movement (art. 9-5, CRPM D212-42); the movement
  part of the register kept at least 5 years (art. 12) ([arrêté du 24 novembre 2005, porcine identification](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000243330), read 2026-11-27).
- Horses: the owner asks for the registration card within 8 months of birth or 30 days of introduction or import
  (CRPM D212-50, since 2025-10-24) ([Code rural et de la pêche maritime, art. D212-14-7 to D212-77-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006071367/LEGISCTA000006168174/), read 2026-11-27).
- Poultry: holders must declare themselves (D212-77-1), a third-class fine otherwise (R215-17) ([CRPM](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000006152479/), read 2026-11-27).

## Legal references

- Code rural et de la pêche maritime D212-14-7 to D212-77-1; arrêtés of 6 August 2013 (cattle) and 19 December 2005
  (sheep and goats).

## Effective dates

Cattle: notifications since 2013-08-23, tagging delay since 2025-01-16. Sheep and goats: 7 days, with 48 hours from
2026-04-28 to 2026-05-28 and 7 days again from 2026-05-29 (art. 19-1 in force since 2026-05-28 per Légifrance)
([arrêté du 19 décembre 2005](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000633936); [arrêté du 30 mars 2026](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000053762000), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc13-agriculture-and-food/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Code rural et de la pêche maritime A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the
corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

A third-class fine (at most 450 EUR) for breaching the identification or the notification of births, movements and
deaths: cattle R215-11 and sheep and goats R215-12 (current versions since 2026-01-01), pigs R215-13 and horses
R215-14 (since 2025-10-24); a fifth-class fine (at most 1 500 EUR, 3 000 EUR on a repeat) for not declaring oneself
as a holder ([Code rural et de la pêche maritime, art. R215-1 to R215-18](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000006152479/); [Code pénal, art. 131-13](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006417259), read 2026-11-27).
Until 2025-12-31 the cattle and sheep-and-goat penalties followed the former versions of R215-11 and R215-12 (not read).

## Exceptions

None.
