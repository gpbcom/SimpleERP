# ⚖️ CR-FR-163 Livestock health register and withdrawal periods

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

Veterinary treatments of food animals are recorded, with the withdrawal period even when it is nil, and the records are
kept 5 years for inspection; the withdrawal period is the minimum time between the last administration and obtaining
food from the animal that keeps the food free of harmful residues ([Regulation (EU) 2019/6, art. 4(34) and
108](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/FR/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32019R0006), applicable since 2022-01-28, read
2026-11-27); a ban on selling is not written as such in the texts read.

## Values

- Contents: for each veterinary medicine given, its name (or active substance), the animals treated, the route, the
  daily dose per animal and the start and end dates, or the prescription reference (art. 7); the vet records the
  treatments prescribed, the animals and the withdrawal periods (art. 9).
- Retention: at least 5 years after the year of the last entry; 3 years for poultry outside prescriptions (art. 11)
  ([arrêté du 5 juin 2000 relatif au registre d'élevage](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000583065), read 2026-11-27).

## Legal references

- Code rural et de la pêche maritime; arrêté du 5 juin 2000 relatif au registre d'élevage.

## Effective dates

The livestock register since 2000-06-30 (art. 15), annex I (poultry) since 2018-08-25 ([arrêté du 5 juin 2000](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000583065), read 2026-11-27).
