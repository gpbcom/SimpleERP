# ⚖️ CR-FR-161 Register of plant-protection treatments

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

A farm records each plant-protection treatment in an electronic, machine-readable register kept by each establishment
(SIRET) that uses the products or has them used; the operator's name is not required ([arrêté du 24 décembre 2025, art.
2 and 4, annex I](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000053228465), read 2026-11-27); professional users hold a
certificate, renewed periodically ([Code rural et de la pêche maritime, art. L254-3, II and III](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000038543173), in force
since 2025-08-13, read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Contents: SIRET (and the beneficiary's for a service provider), product trade name and authorisation number,
  application date, dose per unit, area, volume or seed quantity treated, crop or zone, location (RPG plot or GPS),
  organic status; where relevant times, early re-entry, growth stage and seed lot.
- Retention: at least 5 years after recording; given to the control authority on request; paper entries converted to
  electronic by 31 January of the next year until 2029, within 30 days from 2030 ([arrêté du 24 décembre 2025](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000053228465), read 2026-11-27).

## Legal references

- Code rural et de la pêche maritime L254-1 and following, R257-1; Regulation (EC) 1107/2009 art. 67; arrêté du 24
  décembre 2025.

## Effective dates

Until 2025-12-31: the register of the arrêté du 16 juin 2009, on any medium (not re-read). From 2026-01-01: the
electronic register ([arrêté du 24 décembre 2025, art. 7](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000053228465), read 2026-11-27).

## Penalty

(⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) Not read: the penal article for a missing or incomplete register was transferred in 2023
(décret n° 2023-1276) and its current number and class could not be confirmed. Read: the former R254-30 punished, with
a fourth-class fine, not keeping the registers of L254-3-1 and L254-6 (distributors and advisers), until its transfer
on 2023-12-29 ([Code rural et de la pêche maritime, art. R254-30](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000024870711),
read 2026-11-27); the article for the users' register was not found.
