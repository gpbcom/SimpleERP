# ⚖️ CR-FR-164 Common agricultural policy (CAP) aid declaration

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An active farmer declares CAP aid each year online on Télépac, with SIRET, plots and animal aids; corrections are
possible after the deadline until the date the campaign sets.

## Values

- The 2026 declarations are closed; changes were possible until 20 September.
- The Télépac code no longer needs yearly renewal.

## Legal references

- [Télépac](https://www.telepac.agriculture.gouv.fr/telepac/auth/accueil.action);
  [Service-public R14416](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/R14416).
