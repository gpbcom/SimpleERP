# ⚖️ CR-FR-164 Common agricultural policy (CAP) aid declaration

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An active farmer declares CAP aid each year online on Télépac, with SIRET, plots and animal aids; corrections are
possible after the deadline until the date the campaign sets.

## Values

- The 2026 declarations are closed; changes were possible until 20 September.
- The Télépac code no longer needs yearly renewal.

## Legal references

- [Télépac](https://www.telepac.agriculture.gouv.fr/telepac/auth/accueil.action);
  [Service-public R14416](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/R14416).

## Effective dates

The single application under the national strategic plan since 2023-01-01, deadline 15 May (CRPM D614-36)
([décret n° 2022-1755 du 30 décembre 2022](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000046847535), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc13-agriculture-and-food/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Télépac. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships in a
pack update (D294).

## Penalty

A late application loses 1 % of the aid per working day; more than 25 calendar days late, it is inadmissible (CRPM
D614-41) ([décret n° 2022-1755](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000046847535), read 2026-11-27); for the
2026 campaign only, the 1 % reduction ran from 2026-05-19 and an application filed after 2026-06-09 was inadmissible
([décret n° 2026-322 du 28 avril 2026, art. 1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000053975168), read
2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

None.
