# ⚖️ CR-FR-166 Organic certification

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An organic operator is certified by an approved body that controls it at least once a year, more often on risk and
without notice; the certification bodies are supervised by the national institute.

## Values

- At least one control a year.

## Legal references

- Regulation (EU) 2018/848.
- [INAO: organic farming](https://www.inao.gouv.fr/agriculture-biologique).
