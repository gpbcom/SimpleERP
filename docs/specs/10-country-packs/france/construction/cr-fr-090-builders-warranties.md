# ⚖️ CR-FR-090 Builders' warranties and acceptance

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Works are accepted in a signed record, with or without reservations; from acceptance run the one-year completion
warranty, the two-year warranty on removable equipment and the ten-year warranty on the structure.

## Values

- 1 year: every defect listed at acceptance or reported during the year.
- 2 years: removable equipment.
- 10 years: damage to the structure or inseparable equipment.
- Visible defects not listed at acceptance are deemed accepted.

## Legal references

- Code civil 1792, 1792-2, 1792-3, 1792-6.
- [Service-public F2958](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2958),
  [F2034](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F2034).
