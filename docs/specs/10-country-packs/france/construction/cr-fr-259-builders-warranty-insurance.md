# ⚖️ CR-FR-259 Builders' ten-year insurance certificate

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A builder takes out ten-year liability insurance before work starts and gives the client the insurance certificate,
which is attached to quotes and invoices.

## Values

- Cover of damage appearing within 10 years, from the day after the signed reception report.
- Subcontractors are excluded (no direct contract with the client); foreign builders must show cover of French ten-year
  liability.
- Insurance must be in place before work begins; the certificate is given to the client before the site opens, and
  attached to quotes and invoices.
- When a building under 10 years old is sold, the deed states whether the mandatory insurance exists.
- The client takes out dommages-ouvrage insurance.

## Legal references

- [Service-public F2034](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2034).
- Code civil 1792, 1792-4-1, 1792-6, 2241; Code des assurances L241-1, L241-2, L242-1, L242-2, L243-2 (certificate),
  L243-3 (penalty), R243-1 to R243-3, A243-1 to A243-5 (certificate models).
