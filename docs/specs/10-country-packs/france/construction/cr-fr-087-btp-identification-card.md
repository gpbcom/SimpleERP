# ⚖️ CR-FR-087 Construction identification card (carte BTP)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The employer declares, from the hiring date, each employee who carries out, directs or organises building or civil works
on a site, and gets their identification card.

## Values

- 9.80 EUR per card, valid for the contract (re-read on 2026-11-12 on service-public F33599, which the site checked on
  2025-10-06).
- Temporary workers: 5 years, deactivated between missions.
- Posted workers: declare the posting first.

## Legal references

- Code du travail L8291-1 to L8291-3, R8291-1 to R8291-6.
- [Service-public F33599](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F33599).

## Effective dates

The fine of L8291-2 in its current version since 2019-08-23 ([Code du travail, art. L8291-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000038951536), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc01-construction/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Code du travail L8291-1. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected
rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Up to 4 000 EUR per employee, 8 000 EUR for a repeat within 2 years, 500 000 EUR in total ([Code du travail, art.
L8291-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000038951536), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

Architects, diagnosticians, quantity surveyors, safety coordinators, drivers, couriers, surveyors, staff with no site
mission, supervised interns.
