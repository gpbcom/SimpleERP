# ⚖️ CR-FR-086 Subcontractor acceptance and payment guarantee

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The main contractor has each subcontractor accepted and its payment terms approved by the project owner; in
public contracts the subcontractor is paid directly, in private ones its payment is guaranteed ([loi n° 75-1334, art. 3,
6 and 14](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000889241), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Direct payment in public contracts above the threshold: 600 EUR (loi n° 75-1334, art. 6), 600 EUR including VAT
  ([Code de la commande publique, art. R2193-10](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000037729603), since 2019-04-01, read 2026-11-27); due even if the main contractor is
  in liquidation.
- Bank guarantee or delegation of payment in private contracts: a personal joint surety from an approved institution,
  unless the project owner's payment is delegated to the subcontractor, on pain of nullity of the subcontract
  ([loi n° 75-1334, art. 14](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000889241), read 2026-11-27).
- Read on [Service-public F32137](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F32137) (verified 1 January
  2025): the buyer accepts the subcontractor and its payment terms (21 days of silence is acceptance); direct payment
  from 600 EUR including VAT, the contractor has 15 days to accept or refuse on Chorus; the payment guarantee not on the
  page.

## Legal references

- Loi n° 75-1334 art. 3, 6, 14, 14-1; Code de la commande publique R2193-10.

## Effective dates

Art. 3 since 1976-01-03, art. 6 since 2019-04-01, art. 14 since 2016-10-01, art. 14-1 since 2006-01-01 ([loi n° 75-1334](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000889241),
read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc01-construction/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Loi n° 75-1334. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule
ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

No fine is stated: without acceptance the main contractor cannot rely on the subcontract against the subcontractor
(art. 3); without the guarantee the subcontract is void (art. 14); the project owner who knows of an unaccepted
subcontractor must give formal notice (art. 14-1) ([loi n° 75-1334](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000889241), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

None.
