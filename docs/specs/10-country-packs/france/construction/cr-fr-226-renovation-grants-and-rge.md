# ⚖️ CR-FR-226 Renovation grants and the RGE label

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

MaPrimeRenov' works must be done by an RGE professional, who visits the site first and puts the visit date on the quote
and the invoice.

## Values

- RGE required for both the single-measure route and the whole-home renovation; not required for heat-network connection
  (and, overseas, solar protection of glazing or walls).
- Site visit before works; the visit date appears on the quote and the invoice.
- Owner-occupiers, landlords and holders of a real property right can apply.
- Whole-home renovation: dwelling rated E, F or G, at least 15 years old, not overseas; occupation 3 years (owner) or
  rental as main home 6 years (landlord); step 1 gains at least 2 DPE classes with at least 2 of 4 envelope works each
  covering at least 25 % of the surface; from 1 September 2026 no aid if gas heating remains in individual houses.
- Whole-home aid rates on costs excluding VAT: blue 80 %, yellow 60 %, violet 45 %, pink 10 %; spending caps 30 000 EUR
  (2 classes) and 40 000 EUR (3 or more); total aid caps 100 %, 90 %, 80 %, 50 % of costs including VAT.
- Single measure (mainland), cap then aid blue / yellow / violet: geothermal or solar-thermal heat pump 18 000 EUR, 11
  000 / 9 000 / 6 000 EUR; air-to-water heat pump 12 000 EUR, 5 000 / 4 000 / 3 000 EUR; heat network 1 800 EUR, 1 200 /
  800 / 400 EUR; oil tank removal 4 000 EUR, 1 200 / 800 / 400 EUR; audit 800 EUR, 500 / 400 / 300 EUR.
- Accompaniment (Mon Accompagnateur Renov'): 100 % / 80 % / 40 % / 20 %, capped at 2 000 EUR (4 000 EUR for the poorest
  unfit housing).
- A second single-measure grant within 5 years is allowed, combined total at most 20 000 EUR.
- Income is the reference tax income of year N-1; one-person ceilings outside Ile-de-France: blue 17 363 EUR, yellow 22
  259 EUR, violet 31 185 EUR.

## Legal references

- [Service-public F35083](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F35083).

## Effective dates

Gas-heating exclusion from 1 September 2026 (as the page states). Other start dates not read; in force when read on
2026-11-15 (the page was verified on 1 September 2026).
