# ⚖️ CR-FR-085 Vigilance over subcontractors

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A customer of a subcontract of 5 000 EUR or more obtains the subcontractor's URSSAF vigilance certificate and its
registration documents at signature and every 6 months.

## Values

- Threshold 5 000 EUR excluding VAT.
- Every 6 months until the end of the contract.
- A subcontractor unpaid one month after formal notice may claim from the project owner directly (loi 75-1334 art. 12).

## Legal references

- Code du travail L8222-1 to L8222-7; loi n° 75-1334 art. 12.
- [Service-public F36261](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F36261).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Code du travail L8222-1. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected
rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Joint liability for the subcontractor's undeclared-work debts.

## Exceptions

None.
