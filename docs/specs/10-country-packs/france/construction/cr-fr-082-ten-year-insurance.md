# ⚖️ CR-FR-082 Ten-year liability insurance

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A construction company holds ten-year liability insurance and gives the customer its certificate before the site opens;
the certificate is attached to the quote and the invoice.

## Values

- Certificate: insurer's name and address, policy number, validity, activities covered, geographic extent of the works
  covered.
- Cover: 10 years from the day after acceptance is signed.
- Not concerned: individuals building for themselves, public-law persons.

## Legal references

- Code civil 1792 and following; Code des assurances L241-1, L241-2, A243-1.
- [Service-public F2034](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F2034),
  [model certificate R44868](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/R44868).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc01-construction/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Code des assurances L241-1. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the
corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Up to 6 months' imprisonment and a 75 000 EUR fine.

## Exceptions

Individuals building for themselves; public-law persons.
