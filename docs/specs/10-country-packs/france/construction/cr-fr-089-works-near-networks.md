# ⚖️ CR-FR-089 Works near networks (DT-DICT)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Before works near networks, the project owner files a project declaration (DT) and the contractor an intention to start
works (DICT) through the single window run by Ineris; no work starts before every receipt.

## Values

- DT answers within 9 days online, 15 otherwise.
- DICT answers within 7 days online, 9 otherwise.
- An operator that must locate its network gets 15 extra days.
- Renewed after 3 months without contract or start.

## Legal references

- [Service-public F23491](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F23491) and
  [R17295](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/R17295).
- Code de l'environnement L554-1 to L554-4, R554-1 to R554-39;
  [INERIS single window](https://www.reseaux-et-canalisations.ineris.fr/gu-presentation/construire-sans-detruire/textes-reglementaires.html).
