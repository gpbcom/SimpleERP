# ⚖️ CR-FR-094 Unsold non-food goods

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

New unsold non-food goods are never thrown away: they are donated or reused, else recycled; hygiene, cleaning and
baby-care products must be donated.

## Values

- Donation under an agreement with an association or a social-economy structure.
- Recycling may be skipped when no recycler is within 1 500 km or it costs over 20 % of the sale price.
- Products with under 3 months to their durability date are excluded from mandatory donation.

## Legal references

- Code de l'environnement L541-15-8 (AGEC law).
- [Service-public F37829](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F37829).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc02-retail/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CEnv L541-15-8. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule
ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Administrative fine up to 3 000 EUR (person) or 15 000 EUR (company).

## Exceptions

No reuse possible; durability date under 3 months.
