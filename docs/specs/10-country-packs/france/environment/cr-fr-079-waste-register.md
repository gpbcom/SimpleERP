# ⚖️ CR-FR-079 Waste register

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every producer or holder of waste keeps a tracking register; dangerous waste and some other kinds go on Trackdéchets
instead.

## Values

- Quantity, nature, origin, destination, collection, transport and treatment, per the order of 31 May 2021.
- Kept at least 3 years (R541-43).
- Not needed for waste declared on Trackdéchets.
- On Trackdéchets: dangerous waste, asbestos, refrigerants, DASRI and end-of-life vehicles.

## Legal references

- [Service-public F37825](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F37825).
- Code de l'environnement R541-43; order of 31 May 2021.

## Effective dates

- Until 2026-06-04: R541-43 in its version of 2021-04-01, with the same 3-year retention, the same exemption once
  the national register is fed, and e-tracking slips counting as that report ([Code de l'environnement, art. R541-43 on 2026-06-04](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006074220/LEGISCTA000043307254/2026-06-04/), read 2026-11-27).
- From 2026-06-05: R541-43 as amended by décret n° 2026-433; the register is kept at least 3 years, and a person who
  has reported to the national register no longer keeps its own ([Code de l'environnement, art. R541-43](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000043308132), read 2026-11-27).

## Penalty

750 EUR or 3 750 EUR (contravention of the 4th class), or up to 4 years and 150 000 EUR (750 000 EUR for a company).
