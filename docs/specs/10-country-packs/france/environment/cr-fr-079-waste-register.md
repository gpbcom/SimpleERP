# ⚖️ CR-FR-079 Waste register

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every producer or holder of waste keeps a tracking register; dangerous waste and some other kinds go on Trackdéchets
instead.

## Values

- Quantity, nature, origin, destination, collection, transport and treatment, per the order of 31 May 2021.
- Kept at least 3 years (R541-43).
- Not needed for waste declared on Trackdéchets.
- On Trackdéchets: dangerous waste, asbestos, refrigerants, DASRI and end-of-life vehicles.

## Legal references

- [Service-public F37825](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F37825).
- Code de l'environnement R541-43; order of 31 May 2021.

## Effective dates

Start date not read; in force when read on 2026-11-12.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f12-manufacturing/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CEnv R541-43. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

750 EUR or 3 750 EUR (contravention of the 4th class), or up to 4 years and 150 000 EUR (750 000 EUR for a company).

## Exceptions

None.
