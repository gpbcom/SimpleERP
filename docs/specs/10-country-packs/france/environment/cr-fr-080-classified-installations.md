# ⚖️ CR-FR-080 Classified installations (ICPE)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A site with classified installations records each installation's regime and rubrics; the most demanding regime applies.

## Values

- Three regimes, from the least to the most dangerous: declaration (rubrics D or DC, before commissioning and works),
  registration, authorisation (rubrics A or A GF, with financial guarantees).
- Below the thresholds: not classified.
