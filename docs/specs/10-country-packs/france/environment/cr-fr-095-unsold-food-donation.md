# ⚖️ CR-FR-095 Donation of unsold food

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A food shop of more than 400 m2 offers a donation agreement to one or more food-aid associations, within one year of
opening or of crossing the threshold, and never destroys edible unsold food.

## Values

- Threshold 400 m2 of sales area.
- The agreement sets sorting, the recipient's right to refuse, a slip per donation, traceability and storage until
  pick-up.
- Excluded: use-by date within 48 hours, most animal products.

## Legal references

- Code de l'environnement L541-15-5, L541-15-6.
- [Service-public F37829](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F37829).

## Effective dates

Code de l'environnement L541-15-6 in its current version in force since 2020-02-12 (loi n° 2020-105) ([Code de l'environnement, art. L541-15-6](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000041598864), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc02-retail/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CEnv L541-15-6. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule
ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Fines up to 15 000 EUR (person) or 75 000 EUR (company) after notice; read from part of the page only.

## Exceptions

Use-by date within 48 hours.
