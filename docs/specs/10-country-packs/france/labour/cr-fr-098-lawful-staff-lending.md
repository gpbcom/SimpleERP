# ⚖️ CR-FR-098 Lending staff to another company

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A company lends an employee to another only without profit, billing at most salary, charges and expenses, with the
employee's written consent in an amendment, a lending agreement between the companies, and the staff committee consulted
first.

## Values

- Lending for profit: only temporary-work agencies and the other listed cases.
- Agreement: duration, employee, qualification, how salary, charges and expenses are billed.
- A refusal cannot be sanctioned.

## Legal references

- Code du travail L8241-1 to L8241-3, R8241-1, R8241-2.
- [Service-public F22542](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F22542).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc03-services/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Code du travail L8241-1. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected
rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Up to 2 years and 30 000 EUR (150 000 EUR for a company); more when aggravated; loss of public aid and temporary
closure.

## Exceptions

Companies of 5 000 or more employees lending to a young company, an SME or a non-profit, up to 3 years.
