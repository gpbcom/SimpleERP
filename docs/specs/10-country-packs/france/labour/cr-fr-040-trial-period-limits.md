# ⚖️ CR-FR-040 Trial period limits

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A trial period does not exceed its legal maximum for the employee's category, renewal included.

## Values

- Workers and employees: 2 months, 4 with renewal.
- Supervisors and technicians: 3 months, 6 with renewal.
- Managers (cadres): 4 months, 8 with renewal.
- One renewal, only if the branch agreement allows it.

## Legal references

- [Code du travail](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/) art. L1221-19, L1221-21.

## Effective dates

Since 2008-06-27: L1221-19 and L1221-21 ([Code du travail, art. L1221-19](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000019071113), [art. L1221-21](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000019067609), read 2026-11-27).

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CT L1221-19 to L1221-25. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected
rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

No fine in the articles read (L1221-19 to L1221-26). An employer who does not give the notice
when ending a trial owes the employee an indemnity equal to the pay of that notice, unless for gross misconduct
(L1221-25) ([Code du travail, art. L1221-19 to L1221-26](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000019067609), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

Shorter durations of the collective agreement or the contract.
