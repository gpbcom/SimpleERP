# ⚖️ CR-FR-099 Day-based working time (forfait jours)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Staff on a yearly day count work at most the agreed number of days, under a company or branch agreement and a written
individual convention they agree to, with their workload checked regularly and a yearly interview.

## Values

- Autonomous cadres, or employees whose time cannot be predetermined.
- 218 days a year at most; 235 when rest days are waived, with a pay supplement of at least 10 %.
- Rest, leave and public holidays apply.

## Legal references

- [Service-public F19261](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F19261).
- Code du travail L3121-58 to L3121-66.

## Effective dates

Since 2017-12-22: L3121-58 in force since 2016-08-10, L3121-64 since 2017-12-22 ([Code du travail, art. L3121-58](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000033003228), [art. L3121-64](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000036262805), read 2026-11-27).
