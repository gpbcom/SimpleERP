# ⚖️ CR-FR-046 Headcount thresholds

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

When the headcount crosses a legal threshold, HR and the owner are warned of the duties it brings.

## Values

- 11 employees for 12 consecutive months: elect a social and economic committee (CSE).
- 50 employees: the economic and social database (BDESE) and the wider CSE duties.

## Legal references

- [Code du travail](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/) art. L2311-2, L2312-18.

## Effective dates
