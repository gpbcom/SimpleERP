# ⚖️ CR-FR-285 Paid sports instructors

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

Only the holder of a diploma, professional title or certificate of professional qualification that guarantees the
safety of participants and third parties in the activity and is registered in the national directory of professional
certifications may, for pay, teach, lead or supervise a physical or sports activity or train its participants, as a
main or side, regular, seasonal or occasional occupation; trainees preparing such a qualification may do so under its
rules ([Code du sport, art. L212-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000037388193), read
2026-11-27). They declare their activity to the authority
([art. L212-11](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006071318/LEGISCTA000006167040), read
2026-11-27). The other qualifications the law ties to tasks are in
[CR-FR-210](cr-fr-210-qualifications-tied-to-tasks.md).

## Values

- Qualification (L212-1 I): a diploma, title or certificate guaranteeing safety competence in the activity and
  registered in the national directory (Code du travail L6113-5); a foreign diploma admitted as equivalent (II); a
  trainee within the rules of the qualification prepared.
- Declaration of activity to the authority (L212-11).
- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) The professional card issued after the declaration, its validity and renewal,
  and the list of qualifications per activity are set by decree and order, not read.
- SimpleERP enforces: a paid class or coaching session is assigned only to a coach whose qualification for that
  activity and declaration are recorded and current.

## Legal references

- [Code du sport, art. L212-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000037388193),
  [L212-11 and L212-12](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006071318/LEGISCTA000006167040),
  read 2026-11-27.

## Effective dates

L212-1 in its version since 2019-01-01 (loi n° 2018-771); L212-11 and L212-12 since 2006-05-25 (texts above, read
2026-11-27).

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Code du sport L212-1 to L212-14. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the
corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Working for pay without the declaration: 1 year's imprisonment and a fine of 15 000 EUR (L212-12)
([Code du sport, art. L212-12](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006071318/LEGISCTA000006167040),
read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

People who held the right to work for pay before their qualification was listed keep it (L212-1 IV); volunteers
are outside L212-1, which covers paid work.

None: the law was read on 2026-11-27; the professional card is a lawyer item above.
