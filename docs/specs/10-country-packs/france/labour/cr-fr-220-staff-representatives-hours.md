# ⚖️ CR-FR-220 Staff representatives' delegation hours

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each titular member of the works council (CSE) delegation gets a monthly credit of hours that depends on the headcount.

## Values

- Titular members only; alternates attend only in place of a titular member.
- 11 to 24 employees: 1 titular, 10 hours each, 10 in total; 25 to 49: 2 titular, 10 hours each, 20 in total.
- 50 or more: at least 18 hours per titular member; 50 to 74: 4 titular, 18 hours, 72 in total; 100 to 124: 6, 21, 126;
  300 to 399: 11, 22, 242; 1 000 to 1 249: 17, 24, 408; 5 000 to 5 249: 29, 29, 841; 10 000: 35, 34, 1 190.
- CSE meeting time is paid as working time and is not deducted; training time is not deducted either.

## Legal references

- [Service-public F34474](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F34474).
- Code du travail L2315-7 to L2315-13 (hours), R2314-1 (members and hours by headcount).
