# ⚖️ CR-FR-045 Occupational health visits

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each employee has an information and prevention visit after hiring and periodic visits; reminders come before each one
is due.

## Values

- First visit within 3 months of starting.
- Then at most every 5 years (more often for reinforced follow-up).

## Legal references

- [Code du travail](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/) art. R4624-10, R4624-16.
