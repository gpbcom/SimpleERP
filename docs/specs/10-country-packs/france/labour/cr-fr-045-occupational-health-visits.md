# ⚖️ CR-FR-045 Occupational health visits

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each employee has an information and prevention visit after hiring and periodic visits; reminders come before each one
is due.

## Values

- First visit within 3 months of starting.
- Then at most every 5 years (more often for reinforced follow-up).

## Legal references

- [Code du travail](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/) art. R4624-10, R4624-16.

## Effective dates

Since 2017-01-01: R4624-10 and R4624-16 ([Code du travail, art. R4624-10](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000033769085), [art. R4624-16](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000033769063), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [E-F9-05 HR duty](../../../02-functional/f09-hr-and-projects/data/e-f09-05-hr-duty.md)
- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f09-hr-and-projects/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CT R4624-10 to R4624-28. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected
rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

A breach of L4621-1 to L4624-9 repeated within 3 years: 4 months' imprisonment and a 3 750 EUR
fine, with the judgment displayed and published (L4745-1) ([Code du travail, art. L4745-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000033024930), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

None.
