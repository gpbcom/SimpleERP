# ⚖️ CR-FR-044 Paid leave

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Employees earn paid leave each month and public holidays are counted by the rules of their type.

## Values

- 2.5 working days per month of work, 30 working days a year.

## Legal references

- [Code du travail](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/) art. L3141-3.
