# ⚖️ CR-FR-044 Paid leave

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Employees earn paid leave each month and public holidays are counted by the rules of their type.

## Values

- 2.5 working days per month of work, 30 working days a year.

## Legal references

- [Code du travail](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/) art. L3141-3.

## Effective dates

Since 2016-08-10: L3141-3 ([Code du travail, art. L3141-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000033020826), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [BR-F9-05 Time off follows its type](../../../02-functional/f09-hr-and-projects/business-rules/br-f09-05-time-off-follows-its-type.md)
- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f09-hr-and-projects/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CT L3141-1 to L3141-33. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected
rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

The fine of a 5th-class contravention, up to 1 500 EUR, once per employee concerned; repeat
punished under Code pénal 132-11 and 132-15 (R3143-1) ([Code du travail, art. R3143-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000033471401), [Code pénal, art. 131-13](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006417259), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

More favourable rules of the collective agreement.
