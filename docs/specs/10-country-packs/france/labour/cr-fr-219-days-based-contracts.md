# ⚖️ CR-FR-219 Days-based contracts (forfait jours)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A day-based flat rate needs a collective agreement and the employee's written consent, and the employer follows the
workload.

## Values

- Written individual agreement signed by the employee; consent is mandatory.
- Eligible: managers autonomous in their schedule and not clocked, and employees whose working time cannot be fixed in
  advance.
- Cap: 218 days a year; a collective agreement may set fewer.
- Daily and weekly maximum durations do not apply; daily and weekly rest, paid leave and closed public holidays do.
- The employer regularly checks that the workload is reasonable and that work and private life are balanced.
- Annual interview on pay, unless the agreement sets other terms.
- Waiving rest days: pay increase of at least 10 % by written amendment; cap 235 days unless the agreement differs.
- Pay clearly unrelated to the workload: the employee can claim damages at the labour court.
