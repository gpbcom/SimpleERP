# ⚖️ CR-FR-075 Periodic checks of work equipment

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Work equipment under a periodic check (lifting, pressure, electrical installations) is checked
on time by a qualified person, and each result is written in the safety register ([Code du travail, art. R4323-23 to R4323-27](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000018489764), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- The period per equipment kind, set by orders of the labour or agriculture minister (R4323-23) ([Code du travail, art. R4323-23](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000018531479), read 2026-11-27):
  - lifting equipment: every 12 months; every 6 months for the equipment of II and III of art. 20 of the order
    and for powered equipment that carries people or raises a work station; every 3 months for hand-powered
    equipment that raises a work station; lifting accessories every 12 months ([Arrêté of 1 March 2004, art. 23](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006680469), [art. 24](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000032078725), read 2026-11-27);
  - electrical installations: every year from the initial check, extendable to 2 years when the last report has no
    observation or its observations were dealt with ([Arrêté of 26 December 2011, art. 3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000025046978), read 2026-11-27);
  - pressure equipment in service: periodic inspection at most every 2 years for steam generators and quick-opening
    vessels, every 4 years for the others (the first within 3 years of putting in service), less if their state
    requires; periodic requalification at most every 10 years, 6 years with a toxic or corrosive fluid, 3 years for
    the listed corrosive fluids ([Arrêté of 20 November 2017, art. 15](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000036131127), in force since 2018-01-01, [art. 18, as published](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/article_jo/JORFARTI000036128723), read 2026-11-27).
- The labour inspectors have access to the check reports and the register (L4711-3); the employer keeps the list
  of the people who do the checks available to the inspectorate (R4323-24); results go in the safety register,
  external reports attached to it (R4323-25, R4323-26) ([Code du travail, art. L4711-1 to L4711-5](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000006178110), [art. R4323-24 to R4323-26](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000018489764), read 2026-11-27).

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CT R4323-23, L4711-5. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected
rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

A 10 000 EUR fine for the employer or its delegate at fault, applied once per worker
concerned; on repeat, 1 year's imprisonment and a 30 000 EUR fine (L4741-1) ([Code du travail, art. L4741-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000032376248), read 2026-11-27).
