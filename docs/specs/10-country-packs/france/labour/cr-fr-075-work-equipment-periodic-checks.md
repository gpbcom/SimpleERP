# ⚖️ CR-FR-075 Periodic checks of work equipment

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

(Proposed) Work equipment under a periodic check (lifting, pressure, electrical installations) is checked on time by a
competent person, and each result is written in the safety register.

## Values

- (Proposed) The period per equipment kind, from the orders under R4323-23.
- (Proposed) The register kept available to the labour inspectorate.

## Legal references
