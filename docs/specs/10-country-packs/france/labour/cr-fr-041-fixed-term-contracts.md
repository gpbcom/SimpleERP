# ⚖️ CR-FR-041 Fixed-term contracts

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A fixed-term contract states its reason for recourse and is renewed at most twice.

## Values

- Mandatory reason and mentions (L1242-12).
- At most 2 renewals (L1243-13).

## Legal references

- [Code du travail](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/) art. L1242-12, L1243-13.
