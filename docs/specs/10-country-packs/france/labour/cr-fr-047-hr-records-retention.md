# ⚖️ CR-FR-047 HR records retention

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

HR records are kept for their own legal period, not the accounting one.

## Values

- Personnel register: 5 years after departure.
- Payslip copies: the employer's, 5 years; electronic payslips available 50 years from issue (CR-FR-182, D445).
- Working-time records: 1 year.
- Applicants: 2 years after the last contact (CNIL).
- Position at check-in: 1 year (D286).

## Legal references

- [Code du travail](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/) art. R1221-26, L3243-4,
  D3171-16.
- [CNIL guidance](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/professionnels).

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CT R1221-26, L3243-4, D3171-16. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the
corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Personnel register: the fine of a 4th-class contravention, up to 750 EUR, once per person
employed (R1227-7) ([Code du travail, art. R1227-7](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000030422217), [Code pénal, art. 131-13](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006417259), read 2026-11-27). The other periods: not
read yet.
