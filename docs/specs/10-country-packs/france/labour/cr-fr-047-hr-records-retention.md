# ⚖️ CR-FR-047 HR records retention

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

HR records are kept for their own legal period, not the accounting one.

## Values

- Personnel register: 5 years after departure.
- Payslip copies: the employer's, 5 years; electronic payslips available 50 years from issue (CR-FR-182, D445).
- Working-time records: 1 year.
- Applicants: 2 years after the last contact (CNIL).
- Position at check-in: 1 year (D286).

## Legal references

- [Code du travail](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/) art. R1221-26, L3243-4,
  D3171-16.
- [CNIL guidance](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/professionnels).
