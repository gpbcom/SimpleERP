# ⚖️ CR-FR-106 Travel time and split shifts in home care

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Travel between two visits counts as working time, and split shifts are limited in number and length by
the home-care collective agreement ([IDCC 2941, art. 7 and 13](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/conv_coll/id/KALIARTI000025805644/?idConteneur=KALICONT000025805800), [avenant 36-2017](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/conv_coll/id/KALITEXT000037351356/?idConteneur=KALICONT000025805800), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Travel between two consecutive work sequences of the same half-day is effective working time, paid as such;
  between non-consecutive sequences it is reconstituted as working time; the half-day runs from the first visit to
  the meal break, then from the first visit after it to the last; the paid-travel and travel-cost paragraphs apply
  only once approved and funded by all the funders ([avenant 36-2017](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/conv_coll/id/KALITEXT000037351356/?idConteneur=KALICONT000025805800), read 2026-11-27).
- Part-time staff: at most 3 unpaid interruptions a day, 5 hours in all; more than 5 hours on at most 5 days in 2
  weeks; the contract gives one compensation: an amplitude of at most 11 hours, 2 extra rest days a year, or the
  travel between the sites paid as if the visits were consecutive (art. 13) ([IDCC 2941, art. 7 and 13](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/conv_coll/id/KALIARTI000025805644/?idConteneur=KALICONT000025805800), read 2026-11-27).
- Amplitude of the day: at most 12 hours, 13 hours in nursing services; 13 hours on at most 7 days a month in an
  exceptional need (art. 7) ([IDCC 2941, art. 7 and 13](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/conv_coll/id/KALIARTI000025805644/?idConteneur=KALICONT000025805800), read 2026-11-27).

## Legal references

- Convention collective nationale de la branche de l'aide, de l'accompagnement, des soins et des
  services à domicile of 21 May 2010 (IDCC 2941), art. 7 and 13 ([IDCC 2941, art. 7 and 13](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/conv_coll/id/KALIARTI000025805644/?idConteneur=KALICONT000025805800)).
- Its avenant 36-2017 of 25 October 2017 on travel time and costs ([avenant 36-2017](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/conv_coll/id/KALITEXT000037351356/?idConteneur=KALICONT000025805800)).

## Effective dates

The agreement extended by the arrêté of 23 December 2011 (JORF 2011-12-29); avenant 36-2017
extended by the arrêté of 29 May 2019 (JORF 2019-06-04), its paid-travel paragraphs only once approved and funded
([IDCC 2941, art. 7 and 13](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/conv_coll/id/KALIARTI000025805644/?idConteneur=KALICONT000025805800), [avenant 36-2017](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/conv_coll/id/KALITEXT000037351356/?idConteneur=KALICONT000025805800), read 2026-11-27).

## Penalty

Wages below those of the extended agreement, or a breach of its pay accessories: the fine of a
4th-class contravention, up to 750 EUR, once per employee concerned (R2263-3, R2263-4) ([Code du travail, art. R2263-1 to R2263-5](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000018485370), [Code pénal, art. 131-13](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006417259), read 2026-11-27).
