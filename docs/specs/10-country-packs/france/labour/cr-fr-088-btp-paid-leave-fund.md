# ⚖️ CR-FR-088 Construction paid-leave fund

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

Construction employers join a paid-leave fund (caisse de congés payés du BTP), which pays
their workers' leave against contributions: the firms covered by the extended building and public works
collective agreements, their attached quarries and workshops, and foreign firms posting workers to France
(L3141-32, D3141-12 to D3141-14) ([Code du travail, art. L3141-32](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000033008678), [art. D3141-12 to D3141-28](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000018486741), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) The fund's rates: set by each fund, not in the Code du travail articles read; to read in the funds'
  approved rules.
- Declarations: the employer declares its employees to the competent fund, except those on a fixed-term contract
  of at least one year (D3141-23), and tells them the fund's name and address (D3141-28) ([Code du travail, art. D3141-23 and D3141-28](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000018486741), read 2026-11-27).
- Read on [Service-public F39855](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F39855) (verified 22 October
  2026): the CIBTP network runs the bad-weather scheme; declarations to the BTP paid-leave fund within 120 hours; which
  employers must join not on the page.
