# ⚖️ CR-FR-037 Hiring declaration before the first day

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An employee does not start before the hiring declaration (DPAE) is sent to URSSAF and acknowledged.

## Values

- Sent at most 8 days before the start and at the latest before work starts.

## Legal references

- [Code du travail](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/) art. L1221-10, R1221-5.

## Effective dates

Since 2025-12-28: L1221-10 in force since 2008-05-01, R1221-5 since 2025-12-28 ([Code du travail, art. L1221-10](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000006195587), [art. R1221-4 and R1221-5](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000018482811), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [BR-F9-03 DPAE before the first day](../../../02-functional/f09-hr-and-projects/business-rules/br-f09-03-dpae-before-the-first-day.md)
- [UC-F9-03 Comply with French HR duties](../../../02-functional/f09-hr-and-projects/use-cases/01-people/uc-f09-03-comply-with-french-hr-duties.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CT L1221-10 to L1221-12. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected
rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

A penalty of 300 times the hourly minimum guaranteed wage (L1221-11), and the fine of a 5th-class contravention, up to 1 500 EUR (R1227-1) ([Code du travail, art. L1221-11](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000006195587), [art. R1227-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000018483004), [Code pénal, art. 131-13](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006417259), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

None.
