# ⚖️ CR-FR-037 Hiring declaration before the first day

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An employee does not start before the hiring declaration (DPAE) is sent to URSSAF and acknowledged.

## Values

- Sent at most 8 days before the start and at the latest before work starts.

## Legal references

- [Code du travail](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/) art. L1221-10, R1221-5.

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.
