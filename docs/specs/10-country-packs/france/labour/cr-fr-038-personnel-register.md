# ⚖️ CR-FR-038 Personnel register

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each establishment keeps a personnel register: an entry on the first day, updated on departures, never deleted.

## Values

- Entries in hiring order; kept 5 years after the employee leaves.

## Legal references

- [Code du travail](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/) art. L1221-13, R1221-26.

## Effective dates
