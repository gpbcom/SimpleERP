# ⚖️ CR-FR-076 Occupational risk assessment (DUERP)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The employer assesses the risks of each work unit in a single document (DUERP) from the first employee, updates it on
any change of working conditions or new risk information, and keeps every version.

## Values

- Mandatory from the first employee.
- Updated at least once a year from 11 employees.
- Each version kept 40 years from its making (R4121-4).
- Sent to the occupational health service at each update from 11 employees.
- No portal filing.

## Legal references

- [Service-public F35360](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F35360).
- Code du travail L4121-3-1, R4121-1 to R4121-4, R4741-1, L2317-1, L8115-1, L8115-3.

## Effective dates

Start date not read; in force when read on 2026-11-12 (the page was verified on 22 July 2026).

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f12-manufacturing/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CT L4121-3-1, R4121-1. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected
rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Contravention of the 5th class: up to 1 500 EUR for a natural person (3 000 on repeat), 7 500 EUR for a legal person (15
000 on repeat), R4741-1; obstruction (L2317-1) up to 1 year and 7 500 EUR; administrative fine up to 4 000 EUR per
worker (8 000 on repeat), L8115-1 and L8115-3.

## Exceptions

None.
