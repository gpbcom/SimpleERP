# ⚖️ CR-FR-101 Undeclared work

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Hiding an employee or an activity is forbidden: every employee declared with their real hours, every activity
registered; a client checks its contractors' vigilance certificates.

## Values

- Contracts over 5 000 EUR: vigilance certificate at signature and every 6 months.
- Social reassessment on a flat 25 % of the annual social security ceiling, plus 35 % or 50 %.

## Legal references

- Code du travail L8221-1, L8221-2, L8224-1 to L8224-6, L8222-1 to L8222-7; Code de la sécurité sociale L242-1-2.
- [Service-public F31490](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F31490).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc03-services/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Code du travail L8221-1. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected
rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Up to 3 years and 45 000 EUR (225 000 EUR for a company); loss of public aid up to 5 years, exclusion from public
contracts, closure up to 3 months.

## Exceptions

None.
