# ⚖️ CR-FR-096 Sunday and public-holiday opening of shops

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Shops do not employ staff on Sundays except in the cases the law lists: food shops until 13:00, the mayor's Sundays,
tourist and commercial zones, stations, and prefect's exemptions.

## Values

- Mayor's Sundays: up to 12 a year, listed before 1 January.
- Food shops: until 13:00; staff in shops over 400 m2 get at least 30 % more.
- Mayor's Sundays: volunteers with written consent, at least double pay and a rest day.
- Alsace-Moselle: its own rules.

## Legal references

- Code du travail L3132-3 and following.
- [Service-public F22606](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F22606).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc02-retail/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CT L3132-3. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Not stated in the source read.

## Exceptions

Tourist zones, commercial zones, large stations, prefect's exemptions up to 3 years.
