# ⚖️ CR-FR-043 Overtime

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Hours beyond 35 a week are overtime, approved before payroll, with their legal premium.

## Values

- Premium by default: 25 % for the first 8 hours, 50 % beyond.

## Legal references

- [Code du travail](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/) art. L3121-28, L3121-36.

## Effective dates

Since 2016-08-10: L3121-28 and L3121-36 ([Code du travail, art. L3121-28](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000033020373), [art. L3121-36](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000033020341), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [BR-F9-08 Overtime needs approval](../../../02-functional/f09-hr-and-projects/business-rules/br-f09-08-overtime-needs-approval.md)
- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f09-hr-and-projects/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CT L3121-28 to L3121-40. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected
rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

The fine of a 4th-class contravention, up to 750 EUR, once per employee concerned, for a
breach of the overtime premium rules (R3124-7) ([Code du travail, art. R3124-7](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000018486532), [Code pénal, art. 131-13](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006417259), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

Rates of the collective agreement, at least 10 %.
