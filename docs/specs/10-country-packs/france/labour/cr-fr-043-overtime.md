# ⚖️ CR-FR-043 Overtime

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Hours beyond 35 a week are overtime, approved before payroll, with their legal premium.

## Values

- Premium by default: 25 % for the first 8 hours, 50 % beyond.

## Legal references

- [Code du travail](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/) art. L3121-28, L3121-36.

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.
