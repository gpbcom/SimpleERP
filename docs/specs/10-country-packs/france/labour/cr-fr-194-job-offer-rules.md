# ⚖️ CR-FR-194 Rules for job offers

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A job offer for work in France is written in French, carries its publication date, makes no false or misleading claim,
and mentions neither sex nor age, nor anything that may discriminate.

## Values

- French: work in France, or abroad for a French employer.
- Publication date shown.
- No sex (except artists, models), no age or age range (except a legal age condition or a senior-employment plan).
- Pay not mandatory yet: the EU pay-transparency directive, being transposed, will require pay or a range.

## Legal references

- Code du travail L5331-2, L5331-3, L5321-2, L5332-1, L1142-1, R1141-2, L5334-1; Code pénal 225-1 to 225-4.
- [Service-public F39133](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F39133).

## Effective dates

Since 2008-05-01: L5331-2 and L1142-1 ([Code du travail, art. L5331-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006903793), [art. L1142-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006900801), read 2026-11-27).

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CT L5331-3. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Misleading offer: one year's imprisonment and 37 500 EUR; discrimination: three years and 45 000 EUR.

## Exceptions

None.
