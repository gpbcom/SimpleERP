# ⚖️ CR-FR-229 Temporary-work assignment contracts

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A temporary-work worker has a written mission contract with the agency, with a maximum length and an end-of-mission
bonus.

## Values

- Written, signed contract handed over within 2 working days of the start.
- Content: reason, end date if set, qualification, place and hours, pay, trial period, protective equipment, pension and
  welfare bodies, a clause allowing the client to hire.
- Maximum 18 months including renewals; 24 months for export orders, abroad and certain replacements; 9 months for some
  urgent safety works or waiting for a permanent hire; collective agreements may differ.
- Up to 2 renewals within the maximum.
- Trial period if no agreement: 2 days (under 1 month), 3 days (1 to 2 months), 5 days (over 2 months).
- End-of-mission bonus at least 10 % of total gross pay (exceptions, such as a permanent offer from the client);
  paid-leave indemnity at least 10 % of gross pay including the bonus.
- Waiting period between contracts: one third of the previous contract's length for 14 days or more, half for shorter.
