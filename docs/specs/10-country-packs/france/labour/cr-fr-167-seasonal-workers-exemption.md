# ⚖️ CR-FR-167 Seasonal agricultural workers' contribution exemption

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Farm employers may claim an exemption of employer contributions for occasional workers on low wages, a scheme now
permanent.

## Values

- Wage ceiling 1.25 times the minimum wage since 2024-05-01.

## Legal references

- Code rural et de la pêche maritime L741-16, D741-58 to D741-63-5.
- [Service-public F31205](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F31205),
  [MSA](https://www.msa.fr/lfp/employeur/exonerations-travailleurs-occasionnels).

## Effective dates

Since 2026-01-01: L741-16 as amended by Loi n° 2025-1403 of 30 December 2025; the 1.25 and 1.6 times the minimum
wage apply to pay periods from 2024-05-01, with no end date ([Code rural et de la pêche maritime, art. L741-16](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053280545), read 2026-11-27).
