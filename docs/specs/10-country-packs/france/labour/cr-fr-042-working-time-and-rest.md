# ⚖️ CR-FR-042 Working time and rest

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Schedules and shifts respect the legal working week, the daily and weekly maxima and the minimum rests; a breach is
warned, not blocked.

## Values

- Legal week: 35 hours.
- Maxima: 10 hours a day, 48 hours a week, 44 hours on average over 12 weeks.
- Daily rest: 11 consecutive hours.
- Weekly rest: 24 hours plus the daily 11.

## Legal references

- [Code du travail](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/) art. L3121-18, L3121-20,

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CT L3121-18 to L3121-27, L3131-1, L3132-2. A change flags this rule and warns the
administrators; the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

The fine of a 4th-class contravention, up to 750 EUR, once per employee concerned, for the
legal week or the daily maximum (R3124-3), the weekly maxima (R3124-11) and the daily rest (R3135-1); the fine
of a 5th-class contravention, up to 1 500 EUR, once per employee concerned, for the weekly rest (R3135-2) ([Code du travail, art. R3124-3 and R3124-11](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000018486532), [art. R3135-1 and R3135-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000018486686), [Code pénal, art. 131-13](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006417259), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

Derogations of the collective agreement within legal limits.
