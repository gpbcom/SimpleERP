# ⚖️ CR-FR-113 Tips and their sharing

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Tips customers give for the service, and
service charges added to the bill, go in full to the staff in contact with customers (L3244-1), on top of
their fixed wage unless the employer guarantees a minimum wage (L3244-2); the sharing key is not in the
articles read ([Code du travail, art. L3244-1 and L3244-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000006178029), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Until 2028-12-31, tips customers give willingly to staff in contact with them, in cash or through the employer, are
  exempt from social contributions when monthly pay is under 1.6 Smic; service charges added to the bill are not
  ([URSSAF, Les pourboires](https://www.urssaf.fr/accueil/employeur/beneficier-exonerations/les-pourboires-exoneres.html), read 2026-11-27); exempt from income tax too, for tips received until 2028-12-31 (Loi de finances
  2026, art. 5) ([DGFiP, IR 2026 brochure, what is new](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/www2/fichiers/documentation/brochure/ir_2026/pdf_som/nouveautes.pdf), read 2026-11-27). From 2029-01-01, no exemption unless the law extends it again.
- Read on [Service-public F2301](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F2301) (verified 1 June 2026):
  tips are part of pay, exempt from social contributions when monthly pay does not exceed 1.6 Smic gross (2 987.22 EUR),
  for pay periods up to 31 December 2028; sharing among staff not on the page.

## Legal references

- [Code du travail](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/)
  art. L3244-1, L3244-2.
- [Service-public F2301](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F2301) (the social exemption; it
  cites no text for it).

## Effective dates

L3244-1 and L3244-2 in force since 2008-05-01 ([Code du travail, art. L3244-1 and L3244-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000006178029), read 2026-11-27); the exemption from 2022-01-01 to 2028-12-31 (Loi de
finances 2022, art. 5, extended by Loi de finances 2026, art. 5) ([URSSAF, Les pourboires](https://www.urssaf.fr/accueil/employeur/beneficier-exonerations/les-pourboires-exoneres.html), read 2026-11-27).

## Penalty

The fine of a 3rd-class contravention, up to 450 EUR, for a breach of L3244-1 or L3244-2 (R3246-3) ([Code du travail, art. R3246-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000018487305), [Code pénal, art. 131-13](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006417259), read 2026-11-27).
