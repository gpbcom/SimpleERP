# ⚖️ CR-FR-113 Tips and their sharing

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

(Proposed) Tips paid by customers, in cash or by card, go to the staff and are shared by the agreed key.

## Values

- (Proposed) Tax and social treatment to read.
- Read on [Service-public F2301](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F2301) (verified 1 June 2026):
  tips are part of pay, exempt from social contributions when monthly pay does not exceed 1.6 Smic gross (2 987.22 EUR),
  for pay periods up to 31 December 2028; sharing among staff not on the page.
