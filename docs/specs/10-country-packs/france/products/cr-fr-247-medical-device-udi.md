# ⚖️ CR-FR-247 Medical device UDI traceability

**Status:** 🔄 (Proposed) values to read in the official source

## Statement

(Proposed) A distributor of medical devices checks that each device bears its conformity marking, its label with the
unique device identifier (UDI) and the instructions in the required language, keeps the devices under the manufacturer's
storage conditions, and keeps records that allow it to identify the customer of each device. It passes complaints and
incidents to the manufacturer and, for serious incidents, to the authority.

## Values

- (Proposed) UDI recorded for each lot.
- (Proposed) Delays and retention period to read.
- (Proposed) Field safety corrective actions.
- (Proposed) simple-ERP enforces: every lot is traceable by its UDI.
- Read in the
  [ANSM FAQ on the obligations of economic operators](https://ansm.sante.fr/documents/reference/faq-reglement-dm-obligations-des-operateurs-economiques)
  (no date on the page), distributor checks only; UDI checks and storage conditions are not on the page:
- Read: article 14 applies to each distributor, even inside a group; the manufacturer's checks do not replace it.
- Read: the distributor checks the CE marking and the EU declaration of conformity of every device, whatever its class,
  before making it available, and bears the responsibility; an invoice or a delivery note is not an acceptable proof;
  sampling is allowed if suited to the activity (ISO 28590).
- Read: a non-conforming device is not placed on the market and the manufacturer is informed; the duty to report to the
  authority is separate.
- Read: stock in the chain may be sold only while the expiry date is valid; a distributor placing devices on the
  national market declares its activity to ANSM.
- Read: transitional rules for legacy devices run to 31 December 2027, 2028 or 2029 by class; Regulations (EU) 2017/745
  and 2017/746, articles 13, 14, 15, 16, 29, 110 and 120.
