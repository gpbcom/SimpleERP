# ⚖️ CR-FR-290 Cosmetic products used and sold

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

Only a cosmetic product with a responsible person designated in the EU is placed on the market
([Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009, art. 4](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32009R1223),
read 2026-11-27); its label shows among other things the responsible person, the date of minimum durability or the
period after opening, the precautions, the batch number and the ingredients (art. 19(1)); a distributor checks before
making it available that the responsible person, the precautions, the batch, the ingredients and the language are on
the label and that the date has not passed, does not supply a product it believes non-compliant, takes or ensures the
corrective measures, withdrawal or recall, and keeps storage conditions safe (art. 6); and the responsible person and
distributors notify serious undesirable effects to the authority without delay (art. 23)
([Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009, art. 6, 19 and
23](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32009R1223), read 2026-11-27). Recalls follow
[CR-FR-062](cr-fr-062-product-recall.md).

## Values

- Label (art. 19(1)): (a) responsible person and, for imports, country of origin; (b) nominal content; (c) date of
  minimum durability, or the period after opening for products lasting more than 30 months; (d) precautions; (e) batch
  number; (f) function; (g) ingredients.
- Distributor checks before sale (art. 6(2)): the label items of 19(1)(a), (e) and (g) and 19(3) and (4), the language
  of 19(5), and the date of minimum durability.
- Non-compliant product: not made available; corrective measures, withdrawal or recall; if it presents a risk to human
  health, the responsible person and the authorities informed at once (art. 6(3)).
- Serious undesirable effect: notified without delay with the product's name and the measures taken (art. 23(1)).
- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) Whether a salon that only uses products on its clients is a distributor under
  the Regulation, and any duty to trace a batch to the clients treated: not in the articles read. SimpleERP takes the
  conservative reading: the salon checks labels as a distributor and keeps the batch used per client.
- SimpleERP enforces: the batch of each product used or sold is recorded, a recalled batch is blocked and the clients
  who received it are listed.

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009, art. 4, 6, 19 and 23. A change flags this rule and warns the
administrators; the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

None stated in the articles read; the French penalties are not read.

## Exceptions

The batch may appear only on the packaging when the product is too small (art. 19(1)(e)).
