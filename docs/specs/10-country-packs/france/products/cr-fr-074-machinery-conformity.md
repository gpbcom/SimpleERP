# ⚖️ CR-FR-074 Conformity of machinery

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

(Proposed) Machinery placed on the market carries the CE marking, an EU declaration of conformity and its instructions;
the manufacturer keeps its technical file.

## Values

- (Proposed) Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 applies from 2027-01-20, replacing Directive 2006/42/EC.
- (Proposed) Technical file and declaration kept 10 years after the last unit.

## Legal references

- Regulation (EU) 2023/1230; Code du travail R4312-1, L4321-1 (not read yet).
