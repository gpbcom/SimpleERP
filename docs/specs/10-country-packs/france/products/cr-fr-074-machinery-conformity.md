# ⚖️ CR-FR-074 Conformity of machinery

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

Machinery placed on the market carries the CE marking, an EU declaration of conformity and its instructions; the
manufacturer keeps its technical file (art. 10(2), (3) and (7)) ([Regulation (EU) 2023/1230, Official Journal, Publications Office](https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/8aa8fc19-3063-11ee-9e98-01aa75ed71a1/language-en), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 applies from 2027-01-20; machinery placed on the market before then follows Directive
  2006/42/EC ([European Commission, Machinery](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/mechanical-engineering/machinery_en), read 2026-11-27).
- Technical documentation and EU declaration of conformity
  kept for at least 10 years after the machinery is placed on the market or put into service (art. 10(3))
  ([Regulation (EU) 2023/1230](https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/8aa8fc19-3063-11ee-9e98-01aa75ed71a1/language-en), read 2026-11-27).

## Legal references

- [Regulation (EU) 2023/1230](https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/8aa8fc19-3063-11ee-9e98-01aa75ed71a1/language-en); Code du travail R4312-1, L4321-1 (not read).

## Effective dates

- Until the regulation applies: Directive 2006/42/EC.
- From 2027-01-20: Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 ([European Commission, Machinery](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/mechanical-engineering/machinery_en), read 2026-11-27).
The Official Journal text of 2023-06-29 read 14 January 2027 (art. 54); a corrigendum fixed this clerical error in
the application dates, and machinery placed on the market before 2027-01-20 follows the directive
([European Commission, Machinery](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/mechanical-engineering/machinery_en), read 2026-11-27).

## Penalty

Member States set effective, proportionate and dissuasive penalties, which may be criminal (art. 50)
([Regulation (EU) 2023/1230](https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/8aa8fc19-3063-11ee-9e98-01aa75ed71a1/language-en), read 2026-11-27). (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) The French penalty.
