# ⚖️ CR-FR-061 Food traceability

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

For food and feed, every move records where it came from and where it went, one step back and one step forward.

## Values

- Item, quantity, lot, locations, who and when; moves never deleted, only reversed.

## Legal references

- [Regulation (EC) 178/2002](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2002/178/oj) art. 18.

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Exceptions
