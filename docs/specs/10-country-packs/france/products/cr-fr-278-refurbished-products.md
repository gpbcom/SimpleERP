# ⚖️ CR-FR-278 Refurbished products

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A second-hand product or spare part may be called "refurbished" (reconditionné) only when all its functions have been
tested to show that it meets the legal safety duties and the use a consumer may legitimately expect, and, where needed,
it has been repaired to restore them, including the erasure of every data of a former use or user before it changes
owner ([Code de la consommation, art. L122-21-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000041557068)
and [R122-4](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000045186097), read
2026-11-27). It is never called "as new", and "refurbished in France" only when all the work is done in France
([art. R122-5 and R122-6](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000045186097),
read 2026-11-27). Being second-hand, its conformity defects are presumed to have existed at delivery for 12 months
([Code de la consommation, art. L217-7](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000044152587), read
2026-11-27; [CR-FR-223](../consumer/cr-fr-223-legal-conformity-guarantee.md)).

## Values

- Conditions for the word "refurbished" (R122-4): tests of all the functions (safety and expected use); where needed,
  interventions to restore them; erasure of all the data of a former use or user before the change of owner.
- Words forbidden with "refurbished": "état neuf", "comme neuf", "à neuf" or any equivalent (R122-5).
- "Reconditionné en France": only when every operation of R122-4 is done in France (R122-6).
- Presumption of conformity defects for a second-hand good: 12 months from delivery (L217-7, for contracts from
  2022-01-01) ([Code de la consommation, art. L217-7](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000044152587),
  read 2026-11-27).
- A grade (A, B, C...) is a commercial term the law does not define: the criteria are the company's, stated to the
  buyer, and never "as new".
- SimpleERP enforces: every refurbished unit is graded and tested, with its erasure certificate, before sale.

## Effective dates

L122-21-1 since 2020-02-12 (loi n° 2020-105); R122-4 to R122-6 since 2022-01-01; L217-7 for contracts made from
2022-01-01 (texts above, read 2026-11-27).
