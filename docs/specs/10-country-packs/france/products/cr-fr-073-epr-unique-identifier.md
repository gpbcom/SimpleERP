# ⚖️ CR-FR-073 Extended producer responsibility identifier

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A company in an extended-producer-responsibility stream obtains a unique identifier (IDU) per stream through its
eco-organisation, shows it in its terms of sale and on its website, and gives it on request.

## Values

- One IDU per stream.
- In the terms of sale (or another contractual document) and on the website.

## Legal references

- [Service-public F37937](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F37937).
- Code de l'environnement L541-10-13.

## Effective dates

Code de l'environnement L541-10-13 in force since 2022-01-01 (loi n° 2020-105) ([Code de l'environnement, art. L541-10-13](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000041583847/2021-05-03), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f11-online-shop/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CEnv L541-10-13. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule
ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Administrative fine up to 30 000 EUR, imposed by the minister for the environment, when the IDU is not shown
([Service-public F37937, verified 2025-08-04](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F37937), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

None.
