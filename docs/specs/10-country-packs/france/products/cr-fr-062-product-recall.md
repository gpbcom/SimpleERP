# ⚖️ CR-FR-062 Product recall

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A recall lists every customer and stock holding the lot and blocks the remaining stock.

## Values

- Recall list by lot; stock blocked at once.

## Legal references

- [General Product Safety Regulation (EU) 2023/988](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj).

## Effective dates

Since 2024-12-13.
