# ⚖️ CR-FR-071 VAT on distance sales in the EU

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Above 10 000 EUR a year of EU distance sales of goods to consumers, the buyer's country's VAT applies, with registration
there or the optional one-stop shop (OSS).

## Values

- Below 10 000 EUR a year excluding VAT: the seller's country's VAT.
- Above: the buyer's country's VAT.
- OSS effective the first day of the quarter after the request.
- Franchise sellers report quarterly within a month.
