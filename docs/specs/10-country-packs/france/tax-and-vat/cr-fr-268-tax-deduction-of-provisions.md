# ⚖️ CR-FR-268 Tax deduction of provisions

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

A provision is deducted from the taxable profit only when it covers losses or charges clearly specified that events in
progress make probable, and only if it is actually recorded in the books of the year
([Code général des impôts, art. 39, 1, 5°](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053542680), read
2026-11-27). Its recognition in the accounts is in [CR-FR-267](../accounting/cr-fr-267-litigation-provisions.md).

## Values

- Conditions of deduction: a loss or charge clearly specified, made probable by events in progress at the closing date,
  and recorded in the books of the year (CGI 39, 1, 5°, first paragraph, in the version in force since 2026-02-21)
  ([CGI, art. 39](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053542680), read 2026-11-27).
- Not deductible: provisions for retirement or early-retirement allowances of staff or corporate officers (39, 1, 5°).
- A provision for a probable loss on a dispute that accounting has not posted in the year is not deductible for that
  year: the legal side proposes, accounting posts in the year (39, 1, 5°).
- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) The statement of provisions to file with the tax return and its penalty are in
  later paragraphs of CGI 39, 1, 5° and in other articles not read.
- SimpleERP enforces: the provision proposal names the dispute, the probable loss and its estimate, so that the charge is
  clearly specified when accounting posts it.

## Legal references

- [Code général des impôts, art. 39, 1, 5°](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053542680),
  version in force since 2026-02-21 (loi n° 2026-103), read 2026-11-27.

## Effective dates

Art. 39 in its version since 2026-02-21 (loi n° 2026-103 du 19 février 2026)
([CGI, art. 39](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053542680), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [BR-F15-24 A probable loss gets a provision proposal in F8](../../../02-functional/f15-legal-assistant/business-rules/br-f15-24-a-probable-loss-gets-a-provision-proposal-in-f8.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CGI art. 39, 1, 5°. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule
ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

None stated in the paragraph read; a provision that does not meet the conditions is not deducted.

## Exceptions

Retirement allowances (39, 1, 5°); losses on work in progress are deductible only up to the excess of the cost of the
work done over its sale price (39, 1, 5°).

## Open questions

None: the law was read on 2026-11-27; the statement of provisions is a lawyer item above.
