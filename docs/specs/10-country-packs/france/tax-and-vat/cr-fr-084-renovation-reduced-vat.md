# ⚖️ CR-FR-084 Reduced VAT on housing renovation

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Works on a dwelling completed more than two years ago may bear 10 % or, for energy renovation, 5.5 % VAT, when the
customer certifies the conditions on the quote or the invoice.

## Values

- 10 %: improvement, transformation and maintenance works.
- 5.5 %: energy-renovation works meeting the technical requirements; solar panels up to 9 kW consumed on site, by a
  certified firm.
- 20 %: non-residential premises, new-builds under 5 years, extensions over 10 % of the floor area.
- The certificate in two copies: the company keeps one with its accounts.

## Legal references

- CGI art. 279-0 bis, 278-0 bis A, 278-0 bis P; CGI annex IV art. 30-0 D.
- [Service-public F23568](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F23568).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc01-construction/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CGI art. 279-0 bis. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule
ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

A customer who certified wrongly may have to pay the missing VAT.

## Exceptions

Replacing equipment is excluded from 10 %.
