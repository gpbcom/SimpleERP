# ⚖️ CR-FR-010 When VAT is due

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

VAT on goods is due on delivery; VAT on services is due on payment, unless the company opted to pay it on invoicing
(debits).

## Values

- Goods: chargeable on delivery.
- Services: on collection, or on invoicing with the option for debits.

## Legal references

- [Code général des impôts](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006069577/) art. 269.
