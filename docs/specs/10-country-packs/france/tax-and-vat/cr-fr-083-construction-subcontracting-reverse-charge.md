# ⚖️ CR-FR-083 Reverse charge on construction subcontracting

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A subcontractor of building or civil works for a VAT-registered customer invoices without VAT, with the mention
"autoliquidation"; the customer declares and deducts the VAT.

## Values

- Mention "autoliquidation" on the invoice.
- The customer declares the amount excluding VAT under other taxable operations; the subcontractor under other
  non-taxable operations.

## Legal references

- CGI art. 283, 2 nonies; CGI annex II art. 242 nonies A; CGI art. 1788 A.
- [Service-public F31983](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F31983).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc01-construction/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CGI art. 283, 2 nonies. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected
rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

5 % of the deductible VAT when the customer forgets to self-assess.

## Exceptions

A subcontractor under the VAT franchise: no VAT at all.
