# ⚖️ CR-FR-103 Tax credit for home services and its yearly certificate

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Clients get a tax credit of half of what they paid for home services; the company sends each client, before 31 March, a
certificate of the previous year's services and of what the client actually paid.

## Values

- 50 % of expenses borne, after allowances.
- Ceiling 12 000 EUR a year per household, up to 20 000 EUR in some cases; gardening 5 000, IT help 3 000, small repairs
  500.
- Certificate: company, declaration number and date, client, services (carers, dates, durations), amount actually paid
  after the immediate advance, allowances or prepaid vouchers.
