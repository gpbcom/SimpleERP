# ⚖️ CR-FR-008 VAT rates

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Documents use the French VAT rate of each product or service, by its date: a rate change applies from its date and never
to an issued document.

## Values

- 20 % normal rate (CGI art. 278).
- 10 % intermediate rate (CGI art. 279).
- 5.5 % reduced rate (CGI art. 278-0 bis).
- 2.1 % special rate (CGI art. 281 quater and following).
- Each rate kept with its validity dates.

## Legal references

- [Code général des impôts](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006069577/) art. 278 to 281 nonies.

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CGI art. 278 to 281 nonies. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the
corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

None stated in the sources read.

## Exceptions

Overseas departments (Guadeloupe, Martinique, La Réunion) and Corsica have their own rates, carried by the pack per
territory.
