# ⚖️ CR-FR-013 VAT on gift cards and vouchers

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A single-purpose voucher bears VAT when it is sold; a multi-purpose voucher bears VAT only when it is used.

## Values

- Single-purpose (place of supply and VAT known when sold): VAT at sale.
- Multi-purpose: VAT at use, on the amount paid for the voucher, or its face value if unknown, less VAT.

## Legal references

- [Code général des impôts](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006069577/) art. 256 ter (version
  in force until 2027-01-01) and 266.
