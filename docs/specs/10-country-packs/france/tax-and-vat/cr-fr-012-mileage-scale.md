# ⚖️ CR-FR-012 Mileage allowance scale

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Mileage expenses use the official yearly scale (barème kilométrique) for the vehicle's power and the distance.

## Values

- The scale of the year of the trip, by fiscal horsepower and yearly distance.

## Legal references

- Barème kilométrique, published each year (BOI-BAREME-000001).

## Effective dates

The scale of each year, from its publication.

## Enforced by

- [F8 Accounting](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/README.md)
- [BR-F8-05 Expenses need a receipt and an approval](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/business-rules/br-f08-05-expenses-need-a-receipt-and-an-approval.md)
- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: BOI-BAREME-000001. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule
ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

None.

## Exceptions

None.
