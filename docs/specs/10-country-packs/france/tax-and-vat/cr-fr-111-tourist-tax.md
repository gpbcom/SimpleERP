# ⚖️ CR-FR-111 Tourist tax (taxe de séjour)

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

Where the commune levies it, a hotel collects the tourist tax per guest and night (real regime) or owes it on its
capacity (flat regime), shows it apart on the bill, and pays it to the commune on the set dates. The tax is due by
people not domiciled in the commune, at a rate per person and per night set for each nature and category of
accommodation (campsites and furnished tourist rentals included), and minors, seasonal workers employed in the commune,
people in emergency housing and occupants below a rent set by the council are exempt
([Code général des collectivités territoriales, art. L2333-29 to L2333-31](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006070633/LEGISCTA000006197550), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Rate per night set by the council within the floor and ceiling of the hotel's category.
- Département add-on 10 %; Île-de-France add-on 200 %.
- Real regime: shown apart on the invoice.
- Rates displayed at the hotel.
- Who pays: people not domiciled in the commune (L2333-29); rate per person and per night, for each nature and category
  of accommodation, set by the council before 1 July for the next year (L2333-30); exempt: minors, seasonal workers
  employed in the commune, emergency or temporary rehousing, occupants below a rent set by the council (L2333-31)
  ([CGCT, art. L2333-29 to L2333-31](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006070633/LEGISCTA000006197550), read 2026-11-27).
- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) Collection by booking platforms on the host's behalf (CGCT L2333-34) is not
  read.

## Legal references

- CGCT L2333-26 to L2333-47, R2333-43 to R2333-57.
- [Service-public F743](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F743).

## Penalty

Late interest 0.20 % a month; assessment by the commune after notice.

## Exceptions

Guests living in the commune; flat-regime reductions of 10 % to 80 %.
