# ⚖️ CR-FR-111 Tourist tax (taxe de séjour)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Where the commune levies it, a hotel collects the tourist tax per guest and night (real regime) or owes it on its
capacity (flat regime), shows it apart on the bill, and pays it to the commune on the set dates.

## Values

- Rate per night set by the council within the floor and ceiling of the hotel's category.
- Département add-on 10 %; Île-de-France add-on 200 %.
- Real regime: shown apart on the invoice.
- Rates displayed at the hotel.

## Legal references

- CGCT L2333-26 to L2333-47, R2333-43 to R2333-57.
- [Service-public F743](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F743).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc06-hospitality/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CGCT L2333-26. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule
ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Late interest 0.20 % a month; assessment by the commune after notice.

## Exceptions

Guests living in the commune; flat-regime reductions of 10 % to 80 %.
