# ⚖️ CR-FR-009 VAT exemption and reverse-charge mentions

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

When no French VAT is charged, the document states why, with the mention the law requires.

## Values

- Small-business franchise: "TVA non applicable, art. 293 B du CGI".
- Intra-EU supply of goods: "Exonération de TVA, art. 262 ter I du CGI".
- Reverse charge: "Autoliquidation".

## Legal references

- [Code général des impôts](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006069577/) art. 293 B, 262 ter I,
  283.
- CGI annex II art. 242 nonies A.
