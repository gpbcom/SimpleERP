# ⚖️ CR-FR-009 VAT exemption and reverse-charge mentions

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

When no French VAT is charged, the document states why, with the mention the law requires.

## Values

- Small-business franchise: "TVA non applicable, art. 293 B du CGI".
- Intra-EU supply of goods: "Exonération de TVA, art. 262 ter I du CGI".
- Reverse charge: "Autoliquidation".
- Franchise mention from 2027-01-01: "TVA non applicable, article L. 223-3 du CIBS", the CGI wording still
  accepted until 2028-06-30 ([BOFiP, BOI-RES-TVA-000253 of 2026-10-07](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/14901-PGP.html/identifiant=BOI-RES-TVA-000253-20261007), 3.1; [impots.gouv.fr, micro-entrepreneur and VAT](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/professionnel/questions/je-suis-micro-entrepreneur-ou-la-tete-dune-micro-entreprise-ai-je-des), read 2026-11-27); reverse charge: "Autoliquidation", with no article ([economie.gouv.fr, Mentions obligatoires d'une facture](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/entreprises/gerer-son-entreprise-au-quotidien/gerer-sa-comptabilite-et-ses-demarches/mentions-obligatoires-dune-facture-tout-savoir), read 2026-11-27).
- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) The CIBS article for the intra-EU supply exemption mention, for invoices after 2028-06-30: not read
  (the CGI wording stays possible until then, [BOFiP, BOI-RES-TVA-000253 of 2026-10-07](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/14901-PGP.html/identifiant=BOI-RES-TVA-000253-20261007), read 2026-11-27).

## Legal references

- [Code général des impôts](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006069577/) art. 293 B, 262 ter I,
  283.
- CGI annex II art. 242 nonies A.

## Effective dates

Since 2026-03-14: art. 293 B in force since 2025-03-01, art. 262 ter since 2025-06-19, art. 283 since 2026-03-14 ([CGI, art. 293 B](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000052488142), [art. 262 ter](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000051764907), [art. 283](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000051214570), read 2026-11-27). Until 2026-12-31, CGI art. 293 B, 262 ter and 283 apply; from 2027-01-01, the Code des impositions sur les biens et services, Book II, the franchise (L223-1 to L223-24), the exemptions (L213-9 to L213-54) and the persons liable (L215-1 to L215-25),
in force on that date ([Ordonnance n° 2025-1247](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000053091516), [art. 49](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000054587047) as amended by Ordonnance n° 2026-671, read 2026-11-27); on invoices,
contracts and acts that produce effects until 2028-06-30 (art. 46 II as
amended by Ordonnance n° 2026-671, [BOFiP, BOI-RES-TVA-000253 of 2026-10-07](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/14901-PGP.html/identifiant=BOI-RES-TVA-000253-20261007), read 2026-11-27), a reference to the CGI is read as one to the CIBS
article that takes it over ([Ordonnance n° 2025-1247](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000053091516), [art. 46](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/article_jo/JORFARTI000053091753), read 2026-11-27).
