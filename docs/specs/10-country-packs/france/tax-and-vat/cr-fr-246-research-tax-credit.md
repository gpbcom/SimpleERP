# ⚖️ CR-FR-246 Research and innovation tax credits

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A company that does research (CIR) or, as a small company, designs prototypes of a new product (CII) gets a tax credit
on its eligible expenses, justified on form 2069-A-SD.

## Values

- CIR eligible: companies under the real regime (IS or IR) and some exempt ones; not micro-entrepreneurs; work in the EU
  or an EEA state with an administrative assistance agreement.
- CIR expenses: depreciation of new assets, researchers and technicians, inventor bonuses, standardisation meetings,
  design and model filing costs, contracted research; operating costs at 40 % of personnel and 75 % of depreciation;
  public subsidies deducted.
- CIR rate: metropolitan France 30 % up to 100 million EUR, 5 % above; overseas departments 50 % up to 100 million EUR,
  5 % above.
- CIR justification: form 2069-A-SD; plus 2069-A-1-SD from 10 million EUR (PhD share, FTE count, pay), and for over 100
  million EUR a description of the research programme.
- CII eligible: SMEs (under 250 staff and turnover up to 50 million EUR or balance sheet up to 43 million EUR), for
  prototypes or pilot installations of a new, not yet marketed product; production phase excluded.
- CII expense ceiling: 400 000 EUR; expenses already used for the CIR are excluded; subsidies deducted.
- CII rate: metropolitan France 20 %; overseas departments 60 %; Corsica 40 % (small) and 35 % (medium).
- CII period: expenses up to 31 December 2027.
- Calculated per calendar year; declared with the result return (IS: by the 15th of the 4th month after year end; IR: 15
  days after the 2nd working day following 1 May).
- Use: set against the tax at the balance payment, carried over 3 years, then refunded; immediate refund for some
  companies (young innovative companies, companies under 2 years, companies in proceedings).
