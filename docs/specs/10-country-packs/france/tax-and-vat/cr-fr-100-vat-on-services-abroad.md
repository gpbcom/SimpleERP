# ⚖️ CR-FR-100 VAT on services to clients abroad

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Services to a VAT-registered client in another EU country are taxed in the client's country: the invoice has no French
VAT, says "autoliquidation" and shows both VAT numbers; the supplier files the declaration of European services (DES).

## Values

- Mention "autoliquidation".
- The client's EU VAT number on every invoice.
- Exceptions: services linked to property, short-term vehicle rental, passenger transport, events, on-site food.
- Non-EU business client: "TVA non applicable – art. 259-1 du CGI" (read in part of the page only).

## Legal references

- CGI art. 259, 259-1, 283-1; CGI annex II art. 242 nonies A.
- [Service-public F37527](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F37527).
