# ⚖️ CR-FR-104 Immediate advance of the tax credit

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

Through URSSAF, the 50 % tax credit is
deducted from each payment request; once the client has validated it, URSSAF debits the client for the remainder
and pays the whole service to the provider ([URSSAF, Le service Avance immédiate](https://www.urssaf.fr/accueil/services/services-particuliers/service-avance-immediate.html), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Read: optional and free since 2022; excludes child care under 6 and services funded by allowances.
- Joining: the provider is authorised on one of the two URSSAF APIs (Tiers de prestation, Tierce déclaration) and
  registers the client; the tax administration checks the client's tax number and a return of N-1 or N-2; the
  client activates the account from a link valid 7 days; requests then cover services from that date.
- Validation: 48 hours to check the request, then validated automatically; URSSAF debits the remainder 2 days after.
- Financial guarantee: required from the first advance or when the services declared reach 200 000 EUR in a year
  (CSS D133-22); filing deadline 31/10/2026 ([URSSAF, Le service Avance immédiate](https://www.urssaf.fr/accueil/services/services-particuliers/service-avance-immediate.html), read 2026-11-27).

## Legal references

- [URSSAF, Le service Avance immédiate](https://www.urssaf.fr/accueil/services/services-particuliers/service-avance-immediate.html) (updated 3 August 2026).
- Code de la sécurité sociale D133-22 (financial guarantee), as the page cites it.

## Effective dates

Optional and free since 2022; the financial guarantee checked from the 31/10/2026 deadline
([URSSAF, Le service Avance immédiate](https://www.urssaf.fr/accueil/services/services-particuliers/service-avance-immediate.html), read 2026-11-27).
