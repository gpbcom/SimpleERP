# ⚖️ CR-FR-181 VAT on tickets

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

Tickets for shows, museums, monuments, zoos and cinema bear the VAT rate of their category.

## Values

- Live shows (theatres, cabarets, circuses, concerts, variety shows except where drinks are usually served during the
  show), and the entry to sports events and video-game competitions: 5.5 % (CGI 278-0 bis F and J)
  ([CGI, art. 278-0 bis](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053562844), read 2026-11-27).
- 2.1 % for the first 140 paid performances of a new dramatic, lyrical, musical or choreographic work or of a classic in
  a new staging, and of circus shows made only of original creations; 5.5 % from the 141st
  ([CGI, art. 281 quater](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000028416981),
  [CGI annex III, art. 89 ter](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006298715), read 2026-11-27).
- Zoos meeting the conditions of a ministerial order: 5.5 % (CGI 278-0 bis L)
  ([CGI, art. 278-0 bis](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053562844), read 2026-11-27).
- Cinema: admissions to cinema screenings 5.5 %, whatever the medium of the works shown (278-0 bis G); the rights to
  films shown in cinema screenings or at film festivals 5.5 % (278-0 bis H); other rights on films 10 % (279 g)
  ([CGI, art. 278-0 bis](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053562844),
  [art. 279](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053562872), read 2026-11-27).
- Admissions to exhibitions, sites and cultural, recreational, educational or professional installations (museums,
  monuments): 10 % (279 b nonies), not for what falls under 278-0 bis
  ([CGI, art. 279](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053562872), read 2026-11-27).
- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) Museums and monuments run by public or non-profit bodies: whether their
  admissions are outside VAT or exempt is not in the articles read.
- Corsica and overseas differ.
