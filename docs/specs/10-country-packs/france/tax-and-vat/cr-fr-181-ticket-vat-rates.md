# ⚖️ CR-FR-181 VAT on tickets

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Tickets for shows, museums, monuments, zoos and cinema bear the VAT rate of their category.

## Values

- Live shows: 5.5 %; 2.1 % for the first 140 paid performances of a new creation or staging, 5.5 % from the 141st.
- Museums and monuments of public or non-profit bodies: exempt; other cultural sites: 10 %.
- Zoos: 5.5 %.
- Cinema: 5.5 % only for non-commercial screenings or festivals; other film rights 10 %.
- Corsica and overseas differ.
