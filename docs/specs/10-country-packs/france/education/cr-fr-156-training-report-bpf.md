# ⚖️ CR-FR-156 Yearly training provider report (BPF)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A declared training provider sends its yearly teaching and financial report (bilan pédagogique et financier) on the past
financial year to the regional prefect before 30 April of each year, and may send it online through the declaration
teleservice ([Code du travail, art. R6352-22 to R6352-24](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000018522310), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Deadline: before 30 April of each year, for the activity of the past financial year (R6352-23, in force since
  2021-07-08) ([Code du travail, art. R6352-23](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000018522310), read 2026-11-27).
- Channel: to the regional prefect; it may be sent online through the teleservice of R6351-13, and then also reaches the
  ministry in charge of vocational training (R6352-23) ([Code du travail, art. R6352-23](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000018522310), read 2026-11-27).
- Content: the training run in the financial year, the number of trainees and apprentices, the hours followed and given
  by nature, level, field and length, the funds received by nature and the amount invoiced, and the accounting data of
  the training services (R6352-22) ([Code du travail, art. R6352-22](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000018522310), read 2026-11-27).
- On request of the regional prefect, the provider gives the list of training services done or to do (R6352-24)
  ([Code du travail, art. R6352-24](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000018522310), read 2026-11-27).

## Legal references

- [Code du travail, art. L6351-6 and L6351-7-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000021343346) (lapse of the declaration, public list of providers up to date),

## Effective dates

R6352-22 and R6352-24 in their current version since 2019-11-09, R6352-23 since 2021-07-08
([Code du travail, art. R6352-22 to R6352-24](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000018522310), read 2026-11-27); L6351-6 since 2009-11-26
([Code du travail, art. L6351-6](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000021343346), read 2026-11-27).

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CT R6352-22. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

The declaration of activity lapses when the report shows no training activity or is not sent; only providers up to date
with the report are on the public list ([Code du travail, art. L6351-6 and L6351-7-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000021343346), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

None.
