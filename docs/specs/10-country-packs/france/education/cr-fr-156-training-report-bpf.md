# ⚖️ CR-FR-156 Yearly training provider report (BPF)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A training provider files each year, online, the report of the previous year's training and finances, by the date the
ministry sets.

## Values

- Usually around April or May.
- Online; paper only when online is impossible.

## Legal references

- Code du travail R6352-22 to R6352-24.
- [Service-public F19087](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F19087).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc12-education-and-training/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CT R6352-22. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

The declaration lapses without it.

## Exceptions

None.
