# ⚖️ CR-FR-160 Opening a private school

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A private school outside contract is declared to the rector, opens 3 months later unless opposed, and is inspected from
its first year.

## Values

- Opposition grounds: public order, child protection, the director's conditions.
- Refusing inspection allows closure.

## Legal references

- Code de l'éducation L441-1 to L441-4, L442-1 to L442-3.
- [Service-public F33876](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F33876).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc12-education-and-training/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CE L441-1. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships in
a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Opening without declaration: 1 year and 15 000 EUR, and a ban on running a school.

## Exceptions

None.
