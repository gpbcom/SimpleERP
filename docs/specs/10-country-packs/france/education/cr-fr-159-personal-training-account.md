# ⚖️ CR-FR-159 Personal training account (CPF)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A learner funding training through the personal training account pays a fixed contribution unless exempt; some trainings
are capped; foreign providers cannot be funded.

## Values

- Contribution 150 EUR, indexed each 1 January; paid by the learner, employer or another funder.
- Caps since 2026-02-26: specific certifications 1 500 EUR, skills reviews 1 600 EUR, driving licence 900 EUR.

## Legal references

- Code du travail L6323-1 to L6323-19, D6323-5.
- [Service-public F10705](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F10705).

## Effective dates

Contribution: from 2026-01-01 to 2026-04-01, 103.20 EUR ([arrêté du 30 décembre 2025, art. 1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000053275994), read 2026-11-27); from
2026-04-02, 150 EUR, for requests after that date ([décret n° 2026-234 du 30 mars 2026](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000053742996),
read 2026-11-27); caps from 2026-02-26 ([travail-emploi.gouv.fr: the CPF](https://travail-emploi.gouv.fr/le-compte-personnel-de-formation-cpf), read 2026-11-27).
