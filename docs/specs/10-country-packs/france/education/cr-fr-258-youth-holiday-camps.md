# ⚖️ CR-FR-258 Youth holiday camps declaration

**Status:** 🔄 (Proposed) values to read in the official source

## Statement

(Proposed) A holiday camp for minors is declared to the authority before it opens, its animators hold the required
qualifications, the number of children per animator follows the age of the children, and the children's health sheets
are kept private and for a short time.

## Values

- (Proposed) Declaration before opening.
- (Proposed) Qualifications of animators.
- (Proposed) Ratios by age band, kept as data.
- (Proposed) simple-ERP enforces: supervision ratios follow the youth rules.
- Read on
  [jeunes.gouv.fr](https://www.jeunes.gouv.fr/organisateurs-ce-qu-il-faut-savoir-sur-les-accueils-collectifs-de-mineurs-217)
  (no date on the page); supervision ratios and the qualified share are not on the page:
- Read: the organiser declares the camp online to the departmental education office (DSDEN) in two forms: the initial
  form about 2 months before the start in most cases, the complementary form at the latest 8 days before the start of a
  holiday stay; scouting stays over three nights at least one month ahead.
- Read: premises housing minors are declared to the DSDEN where they are, on cerfa 12751*01.
- Read: parents give sealed health information, vaccination information and, where needed, a medical certificate for
  some sports; staff show proof of mandatory vaccines; a designated team member keeps a care register.
- Read: CASF L227-1 to L227-4, R227-1, R227-12 to R227-28; arretes of 3 November 2014, 9 and 13 February 2007, 20 March
  2007.
