# ⚖️ CR-FR-244 Apprenticeship funding by the OPCO

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An apprenticeship contract is signed between an employer and an apprentice; the training centre is paid by the
employer's skills operator (OPCO), which applies a support level for each diploma, and the contract is registered with
it. The yearly amount is counted pro rata of the days done under the contract and paid in instalments; after a break,
when the apprentice goes on in the centre to find a new employer, payment goes on pro rata of the days for up to six
months or until the new contract ([Code du travail, art. R6332-25,
III](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000042907445), for contracts from 2025-07-01, read
2026-11-27).

## Values

- Support level by diploma, kept as data with the branches: the branches set the level by the field of the title or
  diploma, taking France compétences' recommendations into account ([Code du travail, art. L6332-14](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000038951599/), read 2026-11-27);
  it is a yearly amount by diploma or title ([Code du travail, art. D6332-78](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/LEGISCTA000018498694/), read 2026-11-27).
- Registration of the contract: it is sent to the OPCO, which files it ([Code du travail, art. L6224-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/LEGISCTA000006178187/), read 2026-11-27).
- For a contract of more than one year, the OPCO pays 40 % of the yearly amount within 30 days of the centre's invoice,
  30 % in the seventh month, 20 % in the tenth month, and the balance with the next year's advance or after the final
  invoice, which is lost if not sent within four months of the end; a contract under one year gets one 50 % advance; the
  amount is counted pro rata of the days done ([Code du travail, art.
  R6332-25](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000042907445), in force since 2025-06-30, for
  contracts from 2025-07-01, read 2026-11-27). On a break during the trial period, the employer's share is 50 % of the
  support, at most 750 EUR, pro rata of the days ([Code du travail, art.
  R6332-25-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000051818943), read 2026-11-27). Contracts
  concluded before 2025-07-01 keep the former version (not re-read). Also read: D6332-78 to D6332-84 set a yearly amount
  and give no monthly or pro-rata rule and no rule for an early break ([Code du
  travail](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/LEGISCTA000018498694/), read
  2026-11-27).
- SimpleERP enforces: funding follows the contract's support level.
- Read on [Service-public F11244](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F11244) (verified 8
  September 2026); the support level per diploma is not on the page:
- Read: the contract is written and signed by employer and apprentice (cerfa 10103), and by the legal representative for
  a minor.
- Read: the employer sends the contract and the training agreement to the OPCO within 5 working days after the contract
  starts, free of charge; the OPCO then files the contract with the ministry.
- Read: the OPCO decides on funding within 20 days; silence means refusal; a refusal is reasoned if the contract is
  non-compliant (age, pay, supervisor, training centre Qualiopi certification).
- Read: for contracts from 1 July 2025 the employer contributes to the training cost of some apprentices.
- Read: entry at the training centre within 3 months; information and prevention visit within 2 months.
- Read: Code du travail L6222-4 to L6222-6, R6222-2 to R6222-5, D6224-1 to D6224-8, L6243-2 to L6243-3, D6243-1 to
  D6243-5, L6222-18 to L6222-22; the 20-day delay has no article on the page.

## Legal references

- Code du travail L6224-1, L6332-14, D6332-78 to D6332-84, R6332-25, R6332-25-1; décret n° 2025-585 du 27 juin 2025.
