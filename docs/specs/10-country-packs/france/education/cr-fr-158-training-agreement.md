# ⚖️ CR-FR-158 Training agreement or contract

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

(Proposed) Each training is sold under a signed agreement or contract with the mandatory mentions; an individual paying
themselves has a withdrawal period; attendance is proved.

## Values

- Read: signed by both sides; a missing mention can lead to refusal of registration.
- (Proposed) Mentions, withdrawal period, attendance proof.
- Read on [Service-public F19087](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F19087): a provider needs at
  least one signed agreement or contract to declare its activity within 3 months; the mandatory mentions not on the
  page.

## Legal references

- Code du travail L6353-1 and following.
- [Service-public F19087](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F19087).
