# ⚖️ CR-FR-158 Training agreement or contract

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each training is sold under a signed agreement or contract with the mandatory mentions; an individual paying
themselves has a withdrawal period; attendance is proved ([Code du travail, art. L6353-1 to L6353-11](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000006178215);
[R6332-26](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000029643294), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Read: signed by both sides; a missing mention can lead to refusal of registration.
- Mentions: a contract with an individual states, on pain of nullity, the nature, duration, programme and objectives,
  the expected headcount, the prior level required, the delivery and assessment conditions, the trainers'
  qualifications, and the payment terms and the financial terms of an early end or abandonment (L6353-4); an agreement
  with a buyer follows a decree (L6353-1) ([Code du travail, art. L6353-1 to L6353-11](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000006178215), read 2026-11-27).
- Withdrawal period: 10 days from signing, by registered letter; nothing is paid before it ends, then at most 30 % of
  the price (L6353-5, L6353-6) ([Code du travail](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000006178215), read 2026-11-27).
- Attendance proof: a skills operator pays only after checking that the training was done, on the documents an arrêté
  lists ([Code du travail, art. R6332-26](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000029643294), in
  force since 2026-03-01, read 2026-11-27): the invoices, the employer's expense statements and a certificate of
  completion drawn up by the provider ([arrêté du 21 décembre 2018, art.
  1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000037880305), in force since 2024-05-13, read 2026-11-27). Read:
  the provider informs the funders of the start, interruptions and end of each trainee's training (L6353-10, since
  2026-06-27) ([Code du travail](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000006178215), read 2026-11-27); no
  attendance proof is named.
- Read on [Service-public F19087](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F19087): a provider needs at
  least one signed agreement or contract to declare its activity within 3 months; the mandatory mentions not on the
  page.

## Legal references

- Code du travail L6353-1 and following.
- [Service-public F19087](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F19087).

## Effective dates

L6353-1 since 2019-01-01, L6353-3 since 2009-11-26, L6353-4 to L6353-7 since 2008-05-01, L6353-10 since
2026-06-27 ([Code du travail](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000006178215), read 2026-11-27).

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CT L6353-1. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Until 2026-06-26: a fine of 4 500 EUR for no contract with an individual, a missing mention, an early payment or a
missing pre-enrolment document (L6355-18 to L6355-22). From 2026-06-27: those fines are repealed (loi n° 2026-534 du 25
juin 2026, art. 70). In both periods: a contract with an individual missing a mention is void (L6353-4); the registration can be cancelled when the
rules on carrying out training are not followed ([Code du travail, art. L6351-4, 2°](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/LEGISCTA000006178213/), since 2026-06-27, read 2026-11-27). The
4 500 EUR fines of L6355-18 to L6355-22 (no contract, missing mentions, early payment, missing document) were repealed
on 2026-06-27 by loi n° 2026-534 du 25 juin 2026, art. 70 ([Code du travail, art. L6355-17-1 to L6355-24](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/LEGISCTA000006178217/), read 2026-11-27).
