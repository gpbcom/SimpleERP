# ⚖️ CR-FR-155 Training provider's declaration

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A training provider declares its activity within 3 months of its first training agreement, shows its declaration number
on agreements, quotes and invoices, and reports changes within 30 days.

## Values

- The regional service answers within 2 months; silence means registered.
- The declaration lapses if the yearly report is not filed.

## Legal references

- Code du travail L6351-1 to L6351-8, R6351-1 to D6351-12.
- [Service-public F19087](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F19087).

## Effective dates

L6351-1 in its current version since 2019-01-01; L6351-3 and L6351-4 (refusal and cancellation) since 2026-06-27
([Code du travail, art. L6351-1 A to L6351-8](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006072050/LEGISCTA000006178213/), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc12-education-and-training/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CT L6351-1. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

A new declaration is needed after a missing report.

## Exceptions

None.
