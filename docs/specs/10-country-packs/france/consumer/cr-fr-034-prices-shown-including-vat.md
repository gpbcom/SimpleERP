# ⚖️ CR-FR-034 Consumers see prices including VAT

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Prices shown to consumers include all taxes.

## Values

- Shelf, catalogue, till and receipt prices shown including VAT.

## Legal references

- [Code de la consommation](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/) art. L112-1.
- Arrêté du 3 décembre 1987 on consumer price information, as amended.

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.
