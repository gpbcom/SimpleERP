# ⚖️ CR-FR-068 Consumer reviews published online

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A shop that publishes consumer reviews says whether and how they are checked, shows each review's date, tells a consumer
why a review was rejected, and lets a product's owner report a doubtful review for free.

## Values

- Published: the checking process, the review date and updates, the reason for a refusal.
- A free reporting function for doubtful reviews.

## Legal references

- [Code de la consommation](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/) art. L111-7-2 (loi n°
  2024-449).

## Effective dates

Since 2024-02-17.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f11-online-shop/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CConso L111-7-2, D111-17. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the
corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Administrative fine up to 75 000 EUR (natural person) or 375 000 EUR (legal person) for breaches of L111-7-2 ([Code de la consommation, art. L131-4](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032221077/), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

None.
