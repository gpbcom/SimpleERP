# ⚖️ CR-FR-093 Sales periods (soldes)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Sales happen only in the two legal periods of 4 weeks, on goods offered for at least one month before, without
restocking; sale goods carry the word "soldes", are set apart, and show the reference price.

## Values

- Winter: from the second Wednesday of January at 08:00 (the first if the second is after the 12th).
- Summer: from the last Wednesday of June at 08:00 (the penultimate if the last is after the 28th).
- Some départements have other dates.
- "Soldes" outside the periods is forbidden.

## Legal references

- Code de commerce L310-3, L310-5, R310-16, R310-17; arrêté du 27 mai 2019.
- [Service-public F24037](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F24037).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc02-retail/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CCom L310-3. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

15 000 EUR (person) or 75 000 EUR (company) for sales outside the periods or on goods held under one month.

## Exceptions

Other dates in some départements.
