# ⚖️ CR-FR-036 Consumer withdrawal right

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A consumer who buys at a distance can withdraw within 14 days without giving a reason; an online shop offers a free
withdrawal function, available at all times, and refunds within 14 days. For a contract signed away from the business
premises (at the customer's home), the professional takes no payment and no consideration of any kind before 7 days
have passed since the contract, except for maintenance or repair work the consumer expressly asked to be done urgently
at home, limited to the parts and work strictly needed for the emergency
([Code de la consommation, art. L221-10](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000032226864), read
2026-11-27); that urgent work also has no withdrawal right
([art. L221-28 8°](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000044563170), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- 14 days: from receipt of the goods (the last item of a multi-item order), or from the contract for services.
- Online: a free withdrawal function, accessible at all times, with an acknowledgement on a durable medium (since
  2026-06-19).
- Refund of all sums, delivery included (not an extra-cost delivery option), within 14 days, by the same payment method;
  it may wait for the return of the goods or proof of dispatch.
- Off-premises contract: no payment or consideration before 7 days from the contract; not applied to a home subscription
  to a daily publication, to recurring personal services of L7231-1 of the Code du travail by an approved body, to
  contracts signed at a home sales party the consumer agreed to host, and to urgent maintenance or repair work
  expressly asked by the consumer, within the parts and work strictly needed for the emergency (L221-10 1° to 4°,
  since 2016-07-01); for the first two, the consumer may terminate at any time, without notice or cost, and is refunded
  pro rata within 15 days ([Code de la consommation, art.
  L221-10](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000032226864), read 2026-11-27).
- The 14-day withdrawal right stays for an off-premises contract; only the urgent work itself has none (L221-28 8°,
  since 2022-05-28) ([Code de la consommation, art.
  L221-28](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000044563170), read 2026-11-27).

## Effective dates

Withdrawal function since 2026-06-19; contracts in progress then keep the former rules. The 7-day payment ban since
2016-07-01 (L221-10); the list of exceptions of L221-28 in its version since 2022-05-28
([Code de la consommation, art. L221-10](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000032226864),
[art. L221-28](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000044563170), read 2026-11-27).

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CConso L221-18 to L221-28. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the
corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Administrative fine up to 15 000 EUR (natural person) or 75 000 EUR (legal person) for breaches of L221-18, L221-21 and
L221-23 to L221-27 (art. L242-13); a late refund increases the sum due automatically, from the legal interest rate up
to 50 % and then 5 points a month (art. L242-4); a clause waiving the right is void (art. L242-3); asking for or taking a payment or consideration before the 7 days of an
off-premises contract: 2 years' imprisonment and a fine of 150 000 EUR
([art. L242-7](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000034072627), read 2026-11-27) ([Code de la consommation, art. L242-1 to L242-51](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032221799/), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

The 13 cases of CConso L221-28 (made to measure, perishable, unsealed hygiene goods, urgent repair work asked at home,
digital content started with consent, dated leisure services...); the 4 exceptions of L221-10 to the 7-day payment ban
of an off-premises contract, among them urgent repair work expressly asked by the consumer
([Code de la consommation, art. L221-10](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000032226864), read
2026-11-27).
