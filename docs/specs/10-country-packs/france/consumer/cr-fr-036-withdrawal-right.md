# ⚖️ CR-FR-036 Consumer withdrawal right

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A consumer who buys at a distance can withdraw within 14 days without giving a reason; an online shop offers a free
withdrawal function, available at all times, and refunds within 14 days.

## Values

- 14 days: from receipt of the goods (the last item of a multi-item order), or from the contract for services.
- Online: a free withdrawal function, accessible at all times, with an acknowledgement on a durable medium (since
  2026-06-19).
- Refund of all sums, delivery included (not an extra-cost delivery option), within 14 days, by the same payment method;
  it may wait for the return of the goods or proof of dispatch.

## Legal references

- [Code de la consommation](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/) art. L221-18, L221-21
  (version in force since 2026-06-19, ordonnance n° 2026-2), L221-24, L221-28.

## Effective dates

Withdrawal function since 2026-06-19; contracts in progress then keep the former rules.
