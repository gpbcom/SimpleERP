# ⚖️ CR-FR-063 Consent for email and SMS marketing

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Marketing by email or SMS to a consumer needs their prior consent; to a professional it is allowed for offers related to
their trade unless they object; every message lets the person opt out, free and at once.

## Values

- Consumers: prior, specific consent, kept with its date and source.
- Professionals: offers related to their trade, with the right to object.
- Every message: sender identified and a free opt-out.

## Legal references

- Code des postes et des communications électroniques, art. L34-5.
- [Code de la consommation](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/) art. L223-1 and
  following (phone canvassing, Bloctel).
- [CNIL guidance](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/professionnels).

## Effective dates

CPCE L34-5 in its current version in force since 2020-07-26 (loi n° 2020-901) ([Code des postes et des communications électroniques, art. L34-5](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000032235707), read 2026-11-27).
- Until 2027-10-30: L34-5 applies in full, the paragraphs declared unconstitutional by decision n° 2026-1210 QPC of
  2026-06-25 included.
- From 2027-10-31: the sixth, eighth and penultimate paragraphs of L34-5 are repealed by that decision
  ([Code des postes et des communications électroniques, art. L34-5](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000032235707), read 2026-11-27).

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CPCE L34-5; CConso L223-1. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the
corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Administrative fine up to 75 000 EUR for a natural person and 375 000 EUR for a legal person ([Code des postes et des communications électroniques, art. L34-5](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000032235707), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

Existing customers, for similar products and services of the same company, if they could refuse at collection.
