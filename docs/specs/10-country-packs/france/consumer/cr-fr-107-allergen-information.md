# ⚖️ CR-FR-107 Allergen information in restaurants

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A restaurant tells customers in writing, on the menu or on a document freely available, which of the 14 allergens each
dish served on the premises contains.

## Values

- The 14: cereals with gluten, crustaceans, eggs, fish, peanuts, soybeans, milk, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame,
  sulphites above 10 mg/kg or l, lupin, molluscs.
- Intermediaries give the restaurant the allergens of each product.

## Legal references

- Regulation (EU) 1169/2011 art. 9 and annex II; Code de la consommation R412-12 to R412-16.
- [Service-public F32192](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F32192).

## Effective dates

CConso R412-12 in force since 2016-07-01 (décret n° 2016-884) ([Code de la consommation, art. R412-12](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000032807850), read 2026-11-27).
