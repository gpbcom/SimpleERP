# ⚖️ CR-FR-066 Delivery within 30 days

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Goods are delivered on the date announced, or without undue delay and within 30 days of the contract when no date was
given, with their instructions for use.

## Values

- No date given: at most 30 days after the contract.
- Instructions for use and installation, and the commercial guarantee if any, come with the goods.

## Legal references

- [Code de la consommation](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/) art. L216-1, L216-4.

## Effective dates

Since 2022-01-01 for contracts concluded from that date.

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f11-online-shop/regulatory.md)
