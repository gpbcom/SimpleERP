# ⚖️ CR-FR-165 Labelling of prepacked food

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Prepacked food carries its name, ingredients, allergens highlighted, nutrition declaration, net quantity, date, operator
and origin when required ([DGCCRF, Étiquetage des denrées alimentaires](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/dgccrf/les-fiches-pratiques/etiquetage-des-denrees-alimentaires-les-regles-connaitre), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Particulars: name; ingredients in descending order of weight, allergens highlighted; quantity of the ingredients
  highlighted on the label or in the name; net quantity (drained weight in a covering liquid); use-by or best-before
  date; alcoholic strength above 1.2 %; the operator, established in the EU; instructions for use and storage
  conditions when needed; nutrition declaration; origin for some foods (honey, meats) and whenever omitting it would
  mislead; also the lot number ([DGCCRF, Étiquetage des denrées alimentaires](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/dgccrf/les-fiches-pratiques/etiquetage-des-denrees-alimentaires-les-regles-connaitre), read 2026-11-27).
- Formats: clearly legible, in a conspicuous place, not hidden; x-height at least 1.2 mm, 0.9 mm when the largest
  surface is under 80 cm2 (art. 13(1) to (3)); name, net quantity and alcoholic strength in the same field of vision
  (art. 13(5)) ([Regulation (EU) 1169/2011, consolidated text, Publications Office](http://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/608bc3f4-1b51-4942-a1e7-059fb88efd76.0020.02/DOC_3), read 2026-11-27).

## Legal references

## Effective dates

Applies from 2014-12-13; the nutrition declaration from 2016-12-13 (art. 55) ([Regulation (EU) 1169/2011, Publications Office](http://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/608bc3f4-1b51-4942-a1e7-059fb88efd76.0020.02/DOC_3), read 2026-11-27).

Watched in the law corpus: Regulation (EU) 1169/2011 A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the
corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Fine of a 5th-class contravention, up to 1 500 EUR: the provisions of Regulation (EU) 1169/2011 are implementing
measures of the former CConso L214-1 (now L412-1) ([décret n° 2014-1489, notice](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/id/JORFTEXT000029884889/), read 2026-11-27), and breaches of
those measures are punished under R451-1 ([Code de la consommation, art. R451-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032808068/), read 2026-11-27); ([Code pénal, art. 131-13](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006417259/), read 2026-11-27).
