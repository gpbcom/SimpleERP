# ⚖️ CR-FR-092 Price and unit-price display in shops

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Every product shows its price on it or next to it, also in windows and for goods kept out of sight; most pre-packaged
food, hygiene and cleaning products also show their unit price and net quantity.

## Values

- Unit price per kg or litre, with the net quantity.
- Lots: lot price, composition and price of each item.
- Since 2024-07-01, shops over 400 m2 selling everyday goods flag a smaller pack at a higher unit price, for 2 months.
- Food promotions: at most 34 % of the value and 25 % of the agreed volume.

## Legal references

- Code de la consommation L112-1 to L112-9, L131-5; Code de commerce L443-1; arrêté du 3 décembre 1987.
- [Service-public F34344](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F34344).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc02-retail/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CConso L112-1. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule
ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Administrative fine up to 3 000 EUR (person) or 15 000 EUR (company); a false reduction is a misleading practice: 2
years and 300 000 EUR (person), 1 500 000 EUR (company).

## Exceptions

None.
