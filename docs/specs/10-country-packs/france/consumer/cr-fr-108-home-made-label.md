# ⚖️ CR-FR-108 The home-made label (fait maison)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A dish is labelled home-made only when cooked on the premises from raw products, with the listed exceptions; the logo or
mention sits next to each such dish, or once on the menu when all dishes qualify.

## Values

- Allowed pre-prepared items: cheese, pasta, bread, cured meats, dry biscuits, spices, some bases with written notice.
- Logo: a pan under a roof, black or white, at least 5 x 6 mm.

## Legal references

- Code de la consommation L122-19 to L122-21, D122-1 to D122-3; arrêté du 11 juillet 2014.
- [Service-public F32212](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F32212).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc06-hospitality/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CConso L122-19. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule
ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Misleading practice: 2 years and 300 000 EUR.

## Exceptions

None.
