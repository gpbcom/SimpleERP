# ⚖️ CR-FR-069 Website legal notices

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A website names its editor (identity, registered office, phone, company register, share capital), its publication
director and its host, in an open format.

## Values

- Editor, publication director, host: name or company name, address and phone.

## Legal references

- Loi n° 2004-575 (LCEN) art. 1-1 I (since 2024-05-23, loi n° 2024-449).

## Effective dates

Since 2024-05-23.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f11-online-shop/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: LCEN art. 1-1, 19. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule
ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Not read yet.

## Exceptions

None.
