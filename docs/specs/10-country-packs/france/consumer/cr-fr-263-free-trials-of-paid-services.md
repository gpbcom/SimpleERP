# ⚖️ CR-FR-263 Free trials of paid services

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

A trial offered free to a consumer is free only if nothing but the unavoidable costs is ever charged for it
([Code de la consommation, art. L121-4 19°](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000044563107), read
2026-11-27); any payment added to the main price needs the consumer's express consent, never a default choice
([art. L121-17](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000032227250), read 2026-11-27); online, the
order that makes the consumer pay recalls the price and the length of the contract and is confirmed with a button that
says it obliges to pay ([art. L221-14](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000032226854), read
2026-11-27); and a service renewed tacitly, or ended online, follows L215-1 and L215-1-1
([art. L215-1 to L215-5](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032221235),
read 2026-11-27). The information before the order and the withdrawal right are in
[CR-FR-065](cr-fr-065-online-pre-contract-information.md) and [CR-FR-036](cr-fr-036-withdrawal-right.md).

## Values

- "Free", "at no cost" or similar words are a misleading practice when the consumer has to pay anything other than the
  unavoidable costs of answering the offer and taking delivery (L121-4 19°, since 2022-05-28)
  ([Code de la consommation, art. L121-4](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000044563107), read
  2026-11-27).
- An extra payment taken on a consent given by default (a paid option the consumer did not refuse) is refunded on
  request (L121-17, since 2016-07-01) ([Code de la consommation, art.
  L121-17](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000032227250), read 2026-11-27).
- Order button: "commande avec obligation de paiement" or an unambiguous equivalent; before the order, the essential
  characteristics, the price, the length of the contract and any minimum commitment are recalled; the means of payment
  are shown at the latest when the order starts (L221-14, since 2016-07-01)
  ([Code de la consommation, art. L221-14](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000032226854), read
  2026-11-27).
- A fixed-term service with tacit renewal: the consumer is told in writing, by a dedicated letter or email, 3 months at
  the earliest and 1 month at the latest before the last date to refuse the renewal, with that date in a visible box;
  without it, the consumer may end the contract free at any time from the renewal (L215-1). A contract that can be
  signed online can be ended online, through a free function, with a confirmation on a durable medium (L215-1-1, since
  2023-06-01). Both also protect non-professionals (L215-3) and are reproduced in the contract (L215-4)
  ([Code de la consommation, art. L215-1 to
  L215-5](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032221235), read 2026-11-27).
- A business is not a consumer: the distance-selling rules extend to a business with 5 employees or fewer only for an
  off-premises contract outside its main activity (L221-3), not for an order online
  ([Code de la consommation, art. L221-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000032226882), read
  2026-11-27).
- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) Whether a trial that turns into a paid subscription at its end needs a new
  order with the payment button, or whether an order placed at the start of the trial is enough: the articles read do
  not say. SimpleERP takes the conservative reading: the trial never turns paid without an order confirmed with the
  payment button.
- SimpleERP enforces: a trial states the price after the trial, what happens to the data and how to stop, and is never
  charged without an order confirmed with the payment button.

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Code de la consommation L121-4, L121-17, L215-1 to L215-5, L221-3, L221-14. A change flags
this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

A misleading practice (L121-4): 2 years' imprisonment and a fine of 300 000 EUR, which may reach 10 % of the average
yearly turnover; 5 years and 750 000 EUR when committed online
([Code de la consommation, art. L132-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000049532070), read
2026-11-27). A renewal without the L215-1 notice: the consumer may end the contract free at any time and is refunded
the advances within 30 days (L215-1).

## Exceptions

Insurance, mutual and provident contracts follow their own codes for renewal and termination (L215-5); water and
sanitation operators are outside L215-1 but not L215-1-1 (L215-2)
([Code de la consommation, art. L215-1 to
L215-5](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032221235), read 2026-11-27).
