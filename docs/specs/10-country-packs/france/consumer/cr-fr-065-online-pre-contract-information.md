# ⚖️ CR-FR-065 Information before an online order

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Before an online order, the consumer sees the essential characteristics, the price with taxes and delivery, the delivery
date, the seller's identity and contacts, the guarantees, the mediator, and the withdrawal right with its form and where
the withdrawal function is.

## Values

- The list of CConso L221-5 I, 1° to 11° (version in force since 2026-06-19), and L111-1.
- Online sellers: name, address, email and phone, company register number, VAT number, always one click away; prices
  state whether taxes and delivery are included (LCEN art. 19).

## Legal references

- [Code de la consommation](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/) art. L111-1, L221-5.
- Loi n° 2004-575 (LCEN) art. 19.

## Effective dates

L221-5 version in force since 2026-06-19.
