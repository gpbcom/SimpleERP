# ⚖️ CR-FR-065 Information before an online order

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Before an online order, the consumer sees the essential characteristics, the price with taxes and delivery, the delivery
date, the seller's identity and contacts, the guarantees, the mediator, and the withdrawal right with its form and where
the withdrawal function is.

## Values

- The list of CConso L221-5 I, 1° to 11° (version in force since 2026-06-19), and L111-1.
- A price personalised on the basis of automated decision-making is stated as such before the contract (L221-5 I 11°,
  since 2026-06-19) ([Code de la consommation, art. L221-5](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000053310511), read 2026-11-27).
- Online sellers: name, address, email and phone, company register number, VAT number, always one click away; prices
  state whether taxes and delivery are included (LCEN art. 19).

## Legal references

- [Code de la consommation](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/) art. L111-1, L221-5.
- Loi n° 2004-575 (LCEN) art. 19.

## Effective dates

L221-5 version in force since 2026-06-19.

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CConso L111-1, L221-5; LCEN art. 19. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators;
the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Administrative fine up to 15 000 EUR (natural person) or 75 000 EUR (legal person) for breaches of L221-5 ([Code de la consommation, art. L242-10](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032221799/), read 2026-11-27);
up to 3 000 EUR or 15 000 EUR for L111-1 1° to 4° and 6°, and 15 000 EUR or 75 000 EUR for L111-1 5° ([Code de la consommation, art. L131-1 and L131-1-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032221077/), read 2026-11-27).
