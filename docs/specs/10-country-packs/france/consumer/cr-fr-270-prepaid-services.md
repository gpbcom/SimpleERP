# ⚖️ CR-FR-270 Prepaid service packs

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

Before a consumer is bound by a paid contract, the professional gives, legibly, the essential characteristics of the
service, its price, the date or delay of performance, its identity and contacts, the guarantees and the mediator
([Code de la consommation, art. L111-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000044142438), read
2026-11-27), including the payment and performance terms, the complaint handling and, where relevant, the duration of
the contract or, for an open-ended or tacitly renewed one, how to end it
([art. R111-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032807114), read
2026-11-27). A pack of prepaid hours therefore states its price, its validity and how it ends. Sold online, the
information before the order and the withdrawal right apply
([CR-FR-065](cr-fr-065-online-pre-contract-information.md), [CR-FR-036](cr-fr-036-withdrawal-right.md)); renewed
tacitly, the renewal notice of [CR-FR-263](cr-fr-263-free-trials-of-paid-services.md) applies. Sold to a business, the
general terms of sale of [CR-FR-271](../trade/cr-fr-271-terms-and-prices-for-businesses.md) apply.

## Values

- Information before the contract (L111-1, since 2021-10-01, for contracts from 2022-01-01): essential characteristics;
  price; date or delay of performance when not immediate; identity and contacts; legal and commercial guarantees and
  after-sales service; consumer mediator ([Code de la consommation, art.
  L111-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000044142438), read 2026-11-27).
- Details (R111-1, since 2022-10-01): name, address, phone and email; payment, delivery and performance terms and
  complaint handling; duration of the contract, or how to end an open-ended or tacitly renewed one; the mediator's
  contacts ([Code de la consommation, art.
  R111-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032807114), read 2026-11-27).
- On request, a service provider whose price is not set in advance gives the price, or the method of calculation, or a
  detailed quote (R111-3) ([art. R111-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032807114),
  read 2026-11-27).
- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) Whether unused prepaid hours may expire at the end of their validity without a
  refund, and the minimum validity, if any: no article read sets it. SimpleERP takes the conservative reading: the
  validity and what happens to unused hours are stated before the purchase and on every statement of the balance.
- SimpleERP enforces: the pack shows its price, its validity, what happens to unused hours and how to stop, before the
  purchase and in the customer's contract view.
