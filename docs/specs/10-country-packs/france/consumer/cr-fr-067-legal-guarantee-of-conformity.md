# ⚖️ CR-FR-067 Legal guarantee of conformity

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The seller answers for any lack of conformity appearing within 2 years of delivery; a defect appearing within 24 months
(12 for used goods) is presumed to have existed at delivery.

## Values

- Guarantee: 2 years from delivery.
- Presumption: 24 months, 12 months for used goods.
- Goods with digital elements: 2 years, or the supply period if longer.

## Legal references

- [Code de la consommation](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/) art. L217-3, L217-7.

## Effective dates

For contracts from 2022-01-01.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f11-online-shop/regulatory.md)
- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f16-customer-support-agents/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CConso L217-3 to L217-28. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the
corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Civil fine up to 300 000 EUR, or up to 10 % of the average yearly turnover, for obstructing the guarantee in bad faith
(L217-8 to L217-19, art. L241-5); administrative fine up to 15 000 EUR or 75 000 EUR for breaches of L217-11, L217-12,
L217-16 and L217-17 (art. L241-9 to L241-11); a late refund is increased by 10 %, 20 % or 50 % (art. L241-7) ([Code de la consommation, art. L241-1 to L241-15](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032221767/), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

None.
