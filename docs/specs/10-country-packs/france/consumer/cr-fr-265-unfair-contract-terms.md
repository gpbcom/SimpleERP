# ⚖️ CR-FR-265 Contract terms the law limits

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

In a contract with a consumer or a non-professional, a term that creates a significant imbalance between the parties'
rights and obligations is unfair ([Code de la consommation, art. L212-1 and
L212-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032221213), read 2026-11-27):
12 kinds of terms are always unfair and 10 are presumed unfair unless the professional proves otherwise
([art. R212-1 to R212-5](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032807194),
read 2026-11-27). Between any parties, a term that deprives the debtor's essential obligation of its substance is
deemed unwritten, and so is a non-negotiable term of a standard-form contract that creates a significant imbalance
([Code civil, art. 1170 and 1171](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006070721/LEGISCTA000032008690),
read 2026-11-27). Payment terms and late-payment penalties between businesses are in
[CR-FR-018](../invoicing/cr-fr-018-payment-terms-cap.md) and [CR-FR-019](../invoicing/cr-fr-019-late-payment-penalties.md),
the significant imbalance and abrupt termination between businesses in
[CR-FR-175](../trade/cr-fr-175-unfair-b2b-practices.md).

## Values

- Terms always unfair with a consumer (R212-1, kept as data): 1° adherence to terms the consumer could not see before
  signing; 2° limiting the professional's duty to honour what its staff or agents committed to; 3° the professional alone changing the
  duration, characteristics or price; 4° the professional alone judging conformity or interpreting the contract; 5°
  the consumer bound while the professional does not deliver; 6° removing or reducing the consumer's right to damages;
  7° forbidding the consumer to end the contract for non-performance; 8° a discretionary right to terminate for the
  professional only; 9° keeping sums for services not done when the professional terminates; 10° a longer notice for
  the consumer in an open-ended contract; 11° a fee for the consumer ending an open-ended contract; 12° shifting the
  burden of proof to the consumer ([Code de la consommation, art.
  R212-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032807194), read
  2026-11-27).
- Terms presumed unfair (R212-2, kept as data): 1° a firm commitment of the consumer against a condition the
  professional controls; 2° keeping a deposit without the same right for the consumer (double for arrhes); 3° a
  manifestly disproportionate penalty; 4° termination by the professional without reasonable notice; 5° assigning the
  contract without consent where rights decrease; 6° other unilateral changes; 7° an indicative performance date where
  the law does not allow it; 8° stricter termination conditions for the consumer; 9° unduly limiting the consumer's
  evidence; 10° hindering court action, notably by compulsory arbitration or alternative dispute resolution
  ([Code de la consommation, art.
  R212-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032807194), read
  2026-11-27).
- Not judged for unfairness: the main subject of the contract and the adequacy of the price, when clearly written
  (L212-1; Code civil 1171).
- Both lists also protect non-professionals (L212-2, R212-5).
- SimpleERP enforces: the clause library flags a clause that matches a listed term or departs from a legal limit.

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Code de la consommation L212-1, R212-1 to R212-5, L241-2; Code civil 1170, 1171. A change
flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

A term of the R212-1 list in a consumer contract: an administrative fine of up to 15 000 EUR for a natural person and
75 000 EUR for a legal person, up to 4 % of turnover for a large-scale EU infringement
([Code de la consommation, art. L241-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032221775),
read 2026-11-27); an unfair term, or one of Code civil 1170 and 1171, is deemed unwritten.

## Exceptions

R212-1 3° and R212-2 4° and 6° do not apply to securities and prices tied to a rate the professional does not
control, nor to currency purchases; financial services may change a rate or end an open-ended contract for a
legitimate reason with prompt notice; an open-ended contract may change its price with reasonable notice so the
consumer can terminate; technical changes are allowed when price and quality do not change (R212-3, R212-4)
([Code de la consommation, art. R212-3 and
R212-4](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000032807194), read 2026-11-27).
