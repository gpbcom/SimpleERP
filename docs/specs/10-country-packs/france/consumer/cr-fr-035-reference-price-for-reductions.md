# ⚖️ CR-FR-035 Reference price of a price reduction

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A price reduction shown to consumers states the previous price, which is the lowest price charged in the 30 days before
the reduction.

## Values

- Reference: lowest price in the previous 30 days, for all consumers.
- Successive reductions: the price before the first one.

## Legal references

- [Code de la consommation](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/) art. L112-1-1
  (ordonnance n° 2021-1734).
