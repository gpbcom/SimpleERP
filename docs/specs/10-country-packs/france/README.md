# 🇫🇷 France

**Status:** ✅ specified

The country pack shipped with SimpleERP (France first, [D8](../../06-decisions/foundations/d08-france-first.md)).

## What it holds

- **The law corpus:** the official French and EU sources the legal assistant reads, pulled unattended from official
  APIs ([D199](../../06-decisions/f15-legal-assistant/d199-law-packs.md),
  [D205](../../06-decisions/f15-legal-assistant/d205-law-packs-fed-from-official-apis.md)).
- **The rules to enforce:** one page per rule, `CR-FR-nnn`, sorted by theme below. Each gives the values the
  capabilities read, its articles and effective dates, and who enforces it.
- **The country set-up:** the chart of accounts, journals and taxes the accounting starts from
  ([F8](../../02-functional/f08-accounting/README.md)).
