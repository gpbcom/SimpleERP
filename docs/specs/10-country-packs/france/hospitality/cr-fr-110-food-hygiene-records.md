# ⚖️ CR-FR-110 Food hygiene and its records

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A commercial restaurant has at least one person trained in food hygiene, keeps the cold chain and cooking temperatures,
and keeps the records an inspector asks for.

## Values

- Training: at least 14 hours, no renewal.
- Cold 0 to 3 °C; hot holding at 63 °C; cooling to 3 °C.
- Records: temperatures, cleaning and pest control, deliveries, frying oil, training, non-conformities and actions.
- A declaration to the veterinary services before opening.

## Legal references

- Regulation (EC) 852/2004; Code rural L233-4, D233-11 to D233-13; arrêté du 12 février 2024.
- [Service-public F32189](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F32189).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../08-sectors/sc06-hospitality/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Regulation (EC) 852/2004. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the
corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

From a warning to a report and closure.

## Exceptions

A hotel serving only breakfast is exempt from the training.
