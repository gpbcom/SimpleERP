# ⚖️ CR-FR-128 Temporary bars of associations

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

An association may open a temporary bar at its own events with the mayor's authorisation, selling only soft drinks and
drinks up to 18 degrees, up to 5 times a year.

## Values

- 5 a year at own events.
- Sports venues: up to 48 hours, 10 a year for approved sports associations, 4 for tourism and 2 for agricultural
  events.
- Takings may be taxable when the activity is significant.

## Legal references

- Code de la santé publique.
- [Service-public F24345](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F24345).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

## When the law changes

## Penalty

Alcohol brought into a sports venue: 7 500 EUR and one year.
