# ⚖️ CR-FR-282 Police form for foreign guests

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Hotels, holiday villages and family holiday homes, tourist residences, furnished tourist rentals and guest rooms, and
campsites have every foreign guest fill in and sign, on arrival, an individual police form on the official model, keep
it 6 months and hand it to the police or gendarmerie on request
([Code de l'entrée et du séjour des étrangers et du droit d'asile, art. R814-1 to
R814-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006070158/LEGISCTA000042803266), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Who: hotels, holiday villages and family holiday homes, tourist residences and villages, furnished tourist rentals,
  guest rooms, campsites, caravan sites and other equipped grounds; not those who let unfurnished premises (R814-1).
- When: on the foreign guest's arrival, signed by the guest (R814-1).
- Data (R814-2): surname and first names; date and place of birth; nationality; usual home address; mobile phone and
  email; date of arrival and planned departure; children under 15 may be on an accompanying adult's form.
- Kept 6 months; handed over on request of the police or gendarmerie, electronically if wished (R814-3).
- SimpleERP enforces: a foreign guest's check-in collects the form's data and signature, and the form is deleted after
  6 months.
