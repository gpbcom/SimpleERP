# ⚖️ CR-FR-109 Alcohol licences

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A place selling alcohol holds the licence for its category, declared at the town hall at least 15 days before opening,
with the operating permit for on-site sales, and displays its licence.

## Values

- Bars: licence III (up to 18 degrees) or IV.
- Restaurants serving alcohol with meals: small or full restaurant licence.
- Operating permit: training of at least 20 hours over 3 days, valid 10 years, renewal training of at least 6 hours.

## Legal references

- Code de la santé publique L3321-1, L3331-2, L3331-3, L3332-1-1, L3332-3, R3332-7.
- [Service-public F22379](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F22379),

## Effective dates

CSP L3331-1 and L3331-2 in force since 2016-01-01; L3332-3 since 2019-03-25; L3332-1-1 since 2020-01-30 ([Code de la santé publique, title III Débits de boissons](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/id/LEGISCTA000006155036), read 2026-11-27).

## Penalty

Serving a minor: 7 500 EUR and up to one year without the licence.
