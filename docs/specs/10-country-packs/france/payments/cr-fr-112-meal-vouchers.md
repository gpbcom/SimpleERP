# ⚖️ CR-FR-112 Meal vouchers (titres-restaurant)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Meal vouchers are part-paid by the employer, exempt within a yearly limit, and used for food within a daily limit.

## Values

- Employer's share: 50 % to 60 % of the voucher.
- Exempt within 7.32 EUR per voucher for 2026 vouchers.
- Used up to 25 EUR a day, one voucher per meal.
- Any food product until 31 December 2026.

## Legal references

- [Service-public F21059](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F21059).
- Code du travail R3262-4 to R3262-11;
  [Code général des impôts](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/texte_lc/LEGITEXT000006069577/) art. 81, 19°.
