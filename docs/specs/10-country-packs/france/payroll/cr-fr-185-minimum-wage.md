# ⚖️ CR-FR-185 Minimum wage (SMIC)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

No employee is paid below the minimum wage, revalued each 1 January and when prices rise 2 % or more; the test includes
base pay, benefits in kind and productivity bonuses only.

## Values

- 12.31 EUR an hour gross, 1 867.02 EUR a month for 35 hours (as read on 2026-06-01).
- Under 18 with under 6 months' practice: reduced rates.
- Excluded from the test: expenses, overtime premiums, profit-sharing, seniority and year-end bonuses.

## Legal references

- Code du travail L3231-2, L3231-3.
- [Service-public F2300](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2300).

## Effective dates

Since 2008-05-01: L3231-2 ([Code du travail, art. L3231-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006902832), read 2026-11-27); the amount, from each
yearly revaluation.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f13-payroll/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CT L3231-2. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Up to 1 500 EUR, and damages.

## Exceptions

None.
