# ⚖️ CR-FR-187 Equal-pay index

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Companies with at least 50 employees publish their equal-pay index each year by 1 March.

## Values

- Published on Égapro, on the company's website and in the BDESE.
- 4 indicators from 50 to 250 employees, 5 from 251.
- Below 75: corrective measures; from 75 to 85: objectives.
- Kept for 2027, before the pay-transparency law.

## Legal references

- [Service-public F35103](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F35103) and
  [R67297](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/R67297).
- Code du travail L1142-8.

## Effective dates

Since 2021-12-27: L1142-8 ([Code du travail, art. L1142-8](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000044605453), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f13-payroll/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CT L1142-8. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Up to 1 % of pay.

## Exceptions

None.
