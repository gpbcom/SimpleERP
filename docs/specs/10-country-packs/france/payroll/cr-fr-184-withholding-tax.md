# ⚖️ CR-FR-184 Withholding tax on wages

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

The employer withholds income tax at the rate the tax office sends (applied within 60 days), or the neutral rate without
one, declares it in the monthly social declaration and pays it over the next month.

## Values

- Neutral rate from the official grid.
- Quarterly payment possible under 11 employees.

## Legal references

- CGI art. 204 A, 204 E, 204 F, 204 M, 1759-0 A.
- [Service-public F34732](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F34732).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f13-payroll/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CGI art. 204 A. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule
ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

5 % to 80 % of the sums, at least 250 EUR per declaration; 5 % late surcharge.

## Exceptions

None.
