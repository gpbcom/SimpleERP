# ⚖️ CR-FR-291 Commissions in pay

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

Pay is the basic or minimum wage and every other benefit and extra paid, directly or indirectly, in cash or in kind, by
the employer to the employee because of the job ([Code du travail, art.
L3221-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006902819), read 2026-11-27); a commission is part
of it. For the minimum wage, the hourly pay compared is that of an hour of actual work, counting benefits in kind and the
extras that in fact complete the wage, without expense refunds, legal overtime increases and the transport bonus
([art. D3231-6](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000018533876), read 2026-11-27). The
commission appears on the payslip ([CR-FR-182](cr-fr-182-payslip.md)) and the minimum wage is in
[CR-FR-185](cr-fr-185-minimum-wage.md).

## Values

- A commission is pay (L3221-3), counted in the hourly pay compared with the minimum wage when it completes the wage
  (D3231-6).
- Not counted for the minimum wage: expense refunds, legal overtime increases, transport bonus (D3231-6).
- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) That a commission plan is agreed in writing and changed only with the
  employee's agreement comes from the employment contract and case law, not from the articles read.
- SimpleERP enforces: commissions are computed by the plan recorded for the employee and paid on the payslip, and the
  month's pay is checked against the minimum wage.
