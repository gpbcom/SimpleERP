# ⚖️ CR-FR-252 Public-sector contributions

**Status:** 🔄 (Proposed) values to read in the official source

## Statement

(Proposed) Tenured staff who work above a weekly-hours threshold are affiliated to the local civil servants' pension
scheme, other staff to the general scheme and the supplementary scheme of public contract staff; each scheme has its own
rates and bases, and employer and employee both pay. They are declared through the monthly social declaration
([CR-FR-183](cr-fr-183-dsn-monthly-declaration.md)).

## Values

- (Proposed) Scheme by status and weekly hours.
- (Proposed) Rates, bases and thresholds kept as data.
- (Proposed) Index points and supplements of statutory pay.
- (Proposed) simple-ERP enforces: contributions follow the employee's scheme.
- Read on the CNRACL employer pages,
  [calculation of contributions](https://www.cnracl.retraites.fr/employeur/cotisations-declarations/cotisations/calcul-des-cotisations)
  (updated 17 December 2025),
  [affiliation of agents](https://www.cnracl.retraites.fr/employeur/immatriculation-affiliation/affiliation-des-agents/affiliation-des-agents-cnracl-comment-proceder)
  and
  [working-time conditions](https://www.juris-cnracl.retraites.fr/immatriculation-affiliation/affiliation/conditions-generales-de-laffiliation/conditions-relatives-la-duree-du-travail)
  (published 9 May 2022), read 15 November 2026; the Ircantec rates, index points and statutory pay supplements are not
  read:
- Read: the contribution base is gross indexed pay, the NBI bonus, the CTI supplement and the special bonus of care
  assistants; reduced if pay is reduced.
- Read: CNRACL rates in percent of base, employee deduction then employer contribution: 2020 to 2023, 11.10 and 30.65;
  2024, 11.10 and 31.65; 2025, 11.10 and 34.65; 2026, 11.10 and 37.65 (earlier years from 2012 are on the page).
- Read: the rates are fixed by decree and are those in force for the period for which the contributions are due.
- Read: a civil servant in a permanent post at less than full time is affiliated if the weekly duration is at least 28
  hours; below 28 hours the affiliation ends and the person comes under the general social security scheme and the
  Ircantec, even if tenured.
- Read: full time is 35 hours a week since 1 January 2002; part-time may not be below half time.
- Read: a civil servant working for several local bodies is affiliated if the total weekly duration reaches 28 hours;
  each employer pays in proportion to the time worked.
- Read: home-help social agents of a CCAS or CIAS may get an exemption from the employer contribution.
- Read: declaration is by the monthly social declaration (DSN); affiliation is automatic from the DSN data.
- Read: decrees 2022-244 of 25 February 2022 and 2022-754 of 29 April 2022, 2001-623 of 12 July 2001 and 2002-9 of 4
  January 2002; Code general de la fonction publique L612-1; no penalty is on the pages.
