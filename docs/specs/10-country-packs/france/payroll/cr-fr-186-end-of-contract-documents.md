# ⚖️ CR-FR-186 End-of-contract documents

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

At the end of a contract the employer gives the work certificate, the unemployment certificate and the final receipt in
two copies; a signed receipt can be challenged within 6 months.

## Values

- Work certificate: dates, jobs, health cover kept.
- Unemployment certificate: online from 11 employees.
- Final receipt: binding after 6 months if signed.

## Legal references

- Code du travail L1234-19, L1234-20, R1234-9 to R1234-12.
- [Service-public F87](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F87),
  [F2867](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2867),
  [F86](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F86).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f13-payroll/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: CT L1234-19. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Certificate missing: 750 EUR; unemployment certificate missing: up to 1 500 EUR (7 500 EUR for a company).

## Exceptions

None.
