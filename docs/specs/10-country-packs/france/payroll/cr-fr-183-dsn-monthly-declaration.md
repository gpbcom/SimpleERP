# ⚖️ CR-FR-183 Monthly social declaration (DSN)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each employer files a monthly social declaration per establishment by the 15th of the next month (the 5th from 50
employees), and event declarations within 5 working days.

## Values

- Under 50 employees: the 15th.
- 50 or more: the 5th, or the 15th when pay is offset.
- Events (end of contract, sick leave): within 5 working days.
- Corrected within 30 days: no penalty for errors.

## Legal references

- Code de la sécurité sociale L133-5-3, R133-14, R243-6.
- [Service-public F34059](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F34059).

Watched in the law corpus: CSS L133-5-3. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Not filed: 20.02 EUR per employee; late: 60 EUR per employee per month; errors 13.22 to 40.05 EUR per employee.

## Exceptions

None.
