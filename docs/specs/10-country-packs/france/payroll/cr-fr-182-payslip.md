# ⚖️ CR-FR-182 The payslip

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

Each pay comes with a payslip in the clarified format of seven zones, including the net social amount, delivered
electronically unless the employee refuses; the employer keeps a copy.

## Values

- Zones: employer and job; gross and bases; contributions and employer cost; net before tax and withholding; net social
  amount; withholding base, rate and amount; final mentions.
- The employer's copy kept 5 years.
- Electronic payslips kept available 50 years from issue (D445); the official pages state the other option differently
  (6 years after retirement on F559, until age 75 on F10029): see Q12.
- The employee may challenge for 3 years.

## Legal references

- Code du travail L3243-1 to L3243-5, R3243-1 to R3243-9.
- [Service-public F559](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F559) and
  [F10029](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F10029), read on 2026-11-12; neither names the
  article.

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

Watched in the law corpus: CT R3243-2. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships
in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Up to 450 EUR per payslip not delivered, and damages.

## Exceptions

None.
