# ⚖️ CR-FR-266 Renewing trademarks, designs and patents

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

A French trademark lasts 10 years from filing, renewable indefinitely
([Code de la propriété intellectuelle, art. L712-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006279687),
read 2026-11-27); a design lasts 5 years, extendable by 5-year periods up to 25 years
([art. L513-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000043975026), read 2026-11-27); a patent lasts 20
years from filing ([art. L611-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000041573176), read 2026-11-27)
and is kept only while its yearly fee is paid
([art. L612-19](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000019910654),
[L613-22](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000020254241), read 2026-11-27). Each right has its own
window to renew or pay and a 6-month grace period with a surcharge.

## Values

- Trademark: renewal declared, with the fee, in the year before the expiry date; still possible in the 6 months after
  it with a surcharge; the renewal starts the day after expiry; INPI warns the holder at the latest 6 months before
  expiry, but a missing warning does not stop the expiry (R712-24, since 2019-12-11)
  ([Code de la propriété intellectuelle, art. R712-24](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000039490690),
  read 2026-11-27).
- Design: extension declared, with the fee, in the 6 months ending on the last day of the month in which the period
  ends; still possible in the next 6 months with a surcharge; the first extension may be asked at filing (R513-1, since
  2014-07-01); 25 years at most, 10 years for the spare parts of L513-6 4° (L513-1)
  ([art. R513-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000029122812),
  [L513-1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000043975026), read 2026-11-27).
- Patent: a yearly fee for each year, the filing fee covering the first; due on the last day of the month of the filing
  anniversary, not accepted more than a year early (R613-46, since 2020-03-09); payable within a 6-month grace period
  with a surcharge (L612-19); unpaid, the rights lapse from the due date (L613-22). Utility certificate: 10 years;
  supplementary protection certificate: at most 7 years after the patent (L611-2)
  ([art. R613-46](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000041708169),
  [L612-19](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000019910654),
  [L613-22](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000020254241),
  [L611-2](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000041573176), read 2026-11-27).
- The fee amounts are not kept in the pack: the professional records the amount paid for each right.
- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) Restoring a right lost after a missed deadline (the restoration request) and its
  delay are in articles not read.
- Rights filed outside France (EU trademark, international registration, European patent) follow their own office's
  dates, entered right by right.
- SimpleERP enforces: each right keeps its own renewal or fee date, its grace period, and lapses when the grace period
  ends unpaid.

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Code de la propriété intellectuelle L513-1, R513-1, L611-2, L612-19, L613-22, R613-46, L712-1,
R712-24. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

No fine: a patent whose fee is not paid in time lapses from the due date (L613-22), and a trademark or design not
renewed in time expires.

## Exceptions

Designs filed before 2001-10-01 keep their 25-year protection without extension (L513-1).

None: the law was read on 2026-11-27; restoration is a lawyer item above.
