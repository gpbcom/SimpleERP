# ⚖️ CR-FR-190 Traceability of traders on a marketplace

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A marketplace verifies and displays its sellers' contact details and makes reasonable efforts to check the products
offered; micro and small companies have lighter duties.

## Values

- Seller details verified and shown.
- Random checks or traceability tools.

## Legal references

- Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (Digital Services Act).
- [DSA package](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/digital-services-act-package),
  [questions and answers](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/digital-services-act-questions-and-answers).

## Effective dates

Start date to read in the official source; in force when read on 2026-11-11, the date of this specification.

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f14-plugin-store/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Regulation (EU) 2022/2065. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the
corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Up to 6 % of global turnover (Commission).

## Exceptions

Micro and small companies: lighter duties.
