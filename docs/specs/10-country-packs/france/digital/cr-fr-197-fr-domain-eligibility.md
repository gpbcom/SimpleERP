# ⚖️ CR-FR-197 Who may hold a .fr domain

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A .fr domain is registered through an accredited registrar by a person resident, or an organisation established, in the
EU, Switzerland, Liechtenstein, Norway or Iceland, with an eligible, reachable administrative contact; AFNIC checks
eligibility on report and may delete the domain.

## Values

- Through an accredited registrar.
- Name free of public-order and IP conflicts, not a public body's name without a legitimate interest.
- Failed check: deletion.

## Legal references

- Code des postes et des communications électroniques L45-4.
- [AFNIC FAQ](https://www.afnic.fr/noms-de-domaine/faq/),
  [creating a domain](https://www.afnic.fr/noms-de-domaine/tout-savoir/creer-un-nom-de-domaine/).
