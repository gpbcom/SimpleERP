# ⚖️ CR-FR-222 Accessibility statement (EAA)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A site with an online shop gives, in its general terms or an equivalent document, the
information on how its service meets the accessibility requirements, publicly available in written and oral form and
kept as long as the service is offered ([Code de la consommation, art. D412-57 2° and its annex](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000048180495), read 2026-11-27); the
texts read do not name an "accessibility statement" nor require saying what is not met or how to ask for help. The duty itself is in
[CR-FR-072](../consumer/cr-fr-072-web-accessibility-eaa.md); this rule covers that information.

## Values

- Content: the applicable requirements; where
  needed a general description of the service in accessible formats, the explanations needed to understand how it
  works, and how it meets the requirements ([Code de la consommation, annex to art. D412-57](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000048180495), read 2026-11-27).
- Service providers with fewer than 10 persons and a turnover or balance sheet of 2 MEUR or less are exempt
  ([Code de la consommation, art. D412-60](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000048180103/), read 2026-11-27).
- SimpleERP enforces: an e-shop publishes its accessibility information.
- Read on [Service-public R77385](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/R77385) (verified 11 June 2026),
  the declaration to the DGCCRF only; the content and publication of the accessibility information, the standard and the
  penalties are not read:
- Read: manufacturers, importers, distributors and service providers (sole traders and companies, not
  micro-entrepreneurs) declare non-compliance or invoke an exemption (significant modification, or disproportionate
  burden), online on demarche.numerique.gouv.fr.
- Read: dispensed from this declaration, unless the investigators ask: turnover or balance sheet of 2 million EUR or
  less and fewer than 10 employees.

## Legal references

- Code de la consommation [D412-57 and its annex](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000048180495), D412-60, R451-4 (décret n° 2023-931).

## Effective dates

Services provided after 2025-06-28 ([Code de la consommation, annex to art. D412-57](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000048180495), read 2026-11-27).

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Accessibility information (Code de la consommation D412-57). A change flags this rule and
warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Fine of a 5th-class contravention for a provider who does not establish, publish or keep this information
([Code de la consommation, art. R451-4 10°](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000048180406/), read 2026-11-27), up to 1 500 EUR ([Code pénal, art. 131-13](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006417259/), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

Very small service companies (fewer than 10 persons, 2 MEUR or less) ([Code de la consommation, art. D412-60](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/section_lc/LEGITEXT000006069565/LEGISCTA000048180103/), read 2026-11-27).
