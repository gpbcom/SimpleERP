# ⚖️ CR-FR-222 Accessibility statement (EAA)

**Status:** 🔄 (Proposed) values to read in the official source

## Statement

(Proposed) A site with an online shop publishes an accessibility statement that says how the requirements of the
European Accessibility Act are met, what is not and how to ask for help. The duty itself is in
[CR-FR-072](../consumer/cr-fr-072-web-accessibility-eaa.md); this rule covers the statement.

## Values

- (Proposed) Content and wording of the statement.
- (Proposed) Very small service companies may be exempt.
- (Proposed) SimpleERP enforces: an e-shop publishes its accessibility statement.
- Read on [Service-public R77385](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/R77385) (verified 11 June 2026),
  the declaration to the DGCCRF only; the content and publication of the accessibility statement, the standard and the
  penalties are not read:
- Read: manufacturers, importers, distributors and service providers (sole traders and companies, not
  micro-entrepreneurs) declare non-compliance or invoke an exemption (significant modification, or disproportionate
  burden), online on demarche.numerique.gouv.fr.
- Read: dispensed from this declaration, unless the investigators ask: turnover or balance sheet of 2 million EUR or
  less and fewer than 10 employees.
