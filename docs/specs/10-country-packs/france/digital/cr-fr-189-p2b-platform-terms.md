# ⚖️ CR-FR-189 Platform-to-business terms

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

A platform's terms for business users are clear, explain ranking, give notice before restricting or suspending a
business user, and offer an internal complaint system ([Regulation (EU) 2019/1150, Publications Office](http://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/6c6c2c06-a3b4-11e9-9d01-01aa75ed71a1.0006.03/DOC_1), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Terms in plain and intelligible language, easily available, with the grounds for suspension or termination
  (art. 3(1)); changes notified at least 15 days ahead on a durable medium (art. 3(2)).
- A statement of reasons on a durable medium when restricting or suspending (art. 4(1)); at least 30 days' notice
  with reasons before terminating (art. 4(2)).
- The main ranking parameters set out in the terms (art. 5(1)).
- A free internal complaint-handling system (art. 11(1)); small enterprises are exempt (art. 11(5))
  ([Regulation (EU) 2019/1150, Publications Office](http://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/6c6c2c06-a3b4-11e9-9d01-01aa75ed71a1.0006.03/DOC_1), read 2026-11-27).

## Legal references

## Effective dates

- Since 2020-07-12 (art. 19(2)) ([Regulation (EU) 2019/1150](http://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/6c6c2c06-a3b4-11e9-9d01-01aa75ed71a1.0006.03/DOC_1), read 2026-11-27).
- A repeal was proposed on 2025-11-19; until a repeal is adopted and in force, the regulation applies.

Applies since 2020-07-12; the Commission proposed its repeal on 2025-11-19, still a proposal on the page
updated 2026-11-20 ([European Commission, Platform-to-business trading practices](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/platform-business-trading-practices), read 2026-11-27).

([Regulation (EU) 2019/1150](http://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/6c6c2c06-a3b4-11e9-9d01-01aa75ed71a1.0006.03/DOC_1), read 2026-11-27). (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) The French penalty.
