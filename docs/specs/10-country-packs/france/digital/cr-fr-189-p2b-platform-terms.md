# ⚖️ CR-FR-189 Platform-to-business terms

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Statement

(Proposed) A platform's terms for business users are clear, explain ranking, give notice before restricting or
suspending a business user, and offer an internal complaint system.
