# ⚖️ CR-FR-168 Cyber Resilience Act reporting

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A manufacturer of a product with digital elements reports actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents
through the EU single reporting platform: an early warning within 24 hours, a notification within 72 hours, then a final
report.

## Values

- Reporting applies since 2026-09-11; the other obligations from 2027-12-11.
- Final report: 14 days after a fix is available (vulnerabilities), one month after the notification (incidents).
- Security across design, development and maintenance; vulnerability handling for the product's life; CE marking.
