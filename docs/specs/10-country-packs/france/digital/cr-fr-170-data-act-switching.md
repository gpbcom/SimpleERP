# ⚖️ CR-FR-170 Switching between cloud services (Data Act)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A cloud or software-as-a-service provider removes obstacles to switching: open interfaces, export in a common
machine-readable format, and contracts with the switching terms; switching charges end on 2027-01-12.

## Values

- Applies since 2025-09-12.
- Until 2027-01-12, only the costs of switching may be charged; then nothing.

## Legal references

- Regulation (EU) 2023/2854, chapter VI.
- [Data Act](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/data-act),
  [explained](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/data-act-explained).

## Effective dates

Applies since 2025-09-12; reduced switching charges allowed until 2027-01-12, none after ([European Commission, Data Act explained](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/data-act-explained), read 2026-11-27).
