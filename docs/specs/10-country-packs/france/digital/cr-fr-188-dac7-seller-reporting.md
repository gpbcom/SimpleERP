# ⚖️ CR-FR-188 Reporting sellers' income (DAC7)

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Statement

A platform operator collects its sellers' identification and reports their income to the tax administration each year
([BOFiP, BOI-INT-AEA-30-30-10](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/13764-PGP.html/identifiant=BOI-INT-AEA-30-30-10-20230111), read 2026-11-27).

## Values

- Excluded sellers: fewer than 30 sales of goods in the period for a total of 2 000 EUR or less
  ([BOFiP, BOI-INT-AEA-30-10](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/13729-PGP.html/identifiant=BOI-INT-AEA-30-10-20230111), read 2026-11-27).
- Deadline: filed electronically by 31 January of the year after the period reported, corrections included
  ([BOFiP, BOI-INT-AEA-30-30-10](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/13764-PGP.html/identifiant=BOI-INT-AEA-30-30-10-20230111), read 2026-11-27).
- Data reported: the operator's identity; for each seller who is a person, name, date of birth, main address, tax
  number and VAT number; for an entity, name, registered office, tax number, VAT number, company registration number
  and permanent establishments; the financial account the consideration is paid to and, if different, its holder;
  the consideration net of the platform's fees, the fees being reported separately
  ([BOFiP, BOI-INT-AEA-30-40](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/13765-PGP.html/identifiant=BOI-INT-AEA-30-40-20230111), read 2026-11-27).

## Legal references

- Directive (EU) 2021/514; CGI art. 1649 ter A to 1649 ter E, 1736 XI, 1740 E, as the BOFiP pages above apply them.

## Effective dates

- Until 2022-12-31: operations were reported under the Écollab scheme of CGI art. 242 bis.
- From 2023-01-01: operations are reported under DPI-DAC7 (CGI art. 1649 ter A to 1649 ter E, loi n° 2021-1900,
  art. 134), the first declaration being due in 2024 ([impots.gouv.fr, DPI-DAC7](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/transfert-dinformations-en-application-des-dispositifs-dpi-dac7-plateformes-deconomie-collaborative), read 2026-11-27).

## Enforced by

- [Regulatory](../../../02-functional/f14-plugin-store/regulatory.md)

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Directive (EU) 2021/514. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected
rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Flat fine up to 50 000 EUR for breaches of CGI 1649 ter A and 1649 ter D I or III (CGI 1736 XI), modulated by
the breach (10 EUR or 20 EUR per error or omission, up to 10 000, 25 000 or 50 000 EUR for no filing); an operator
outside the EU may lose its registration number (CGI 1740 E) ([BOFiP, BOI-INT-AEA-30-50](https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/14086-PGP.html/identifiant=BOI-INT-AEA-30-50-20231213), read 2026-11-27).

## Exceptions

None.
