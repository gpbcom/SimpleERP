# ⚖️ CR-FR-169 NIS2 incident notification

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

An essential or important entity notifies significant incidents to the national authority in stages and manages its
cybersecurity risks ([Directive (EU) 2022/2555, art. 21 and 23, Publications Office](https://op.europa.eu/o/opportal-service/download-handler?identifier=9b84d482-85bd-11ed-9887-01aa75ed71a1&format=pdfa2a&language=en&productionSystem=cellar), read 2026-11-27). In France this applies
only once the transposition texts are in force (see Effective dates).

## Values

- Scope: medium-sized and large entities of the critical sectors (energy, transport, health, finance, water, digital
  infrastructure, digital services, waste, manufacturing, postal services, public administration, space)
  ([European Commission, NIS 2 Directive](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/nis2-directive), read 2026-11-27).
- Deadlines: an early warning within 24 hours of becoming aware of a significant incident; an incident notification
  within 72 hours; an intermediate report on request; a final report within one month of the notification
  (art. 23(4)) ([Directive (EU) 2022/2555, Publications Office](https://op.europa.eu/o/opportal-service/download-handler?identifier=9b84d482-85bd-11ed-9887-01aa75ed71a1&format=pdfa2a&language=en&productionSystem=cellar), read 2026-11-27).
- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) The French authority, the French deadlines and the French thresholds, once the law is published.

## Effective dates

- Until the French transposition texts are in force: NIS 2 does not apply to French entities; the directive
  asked Member States to apply it from 2024-10-18 ([Directive (EU) 2022/2555, art. 41](https://op.europa.eu/o/opportal-service/download-handler?identifier=9b84d482-85bd-11ed-9887-01aa75ed71a1&format=pdfa2a&language=en&productionSystem=cellar), read 2026-11-27).
- From the date the French law, decrees and orders set: the duties above apply.

Not in force in France yet: NIS 2 applies once all the transposition texts (law, decrees, orders) are promulgated; the
bill was adopted by the Senate on 2025-03-12 and by the National Assembly's special committee on 2025-09-10
([ANSSI, Avancement de la transposition de la directive NIS 2, updated 2025-09-19](https://aide.monespacenis2.cyber.gouv.fr/fr/article/avancement-de-la-transposition-de-la-directive-nis-2-1b3j1da/), read 2026-11-27). No promulgated law
was found on Légifrance on 2026-11-27.

Watched in the law corpus: Directive (EU) 2022/2555. A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the
corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

The directive sets the maximum fines Member States must provide at least: 10 000 000 EUR or 2 % of the worldwide
yearly turnover for an essential entity, 7 000 000 EUR or 1.4 % for an important entity, whichever is higher
(art. 34(4) and (5)) ([Directive (EU) 2022/2555, Publications Office](https://op.europa.eu/o/opportal-service/download-handler?identifier=9b84d482-85bd-11ed-9887-01aa75ed71a1&format=pdfa2a&language=en&productionSystem=cellar), read 2026-11-27). (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) The French amounts, once the law
is published.
