# ⚖️ CR-FR-269 Hosting user posts (notice and action)

**Status:** ✅ specified, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Statement

A service that stores what its users post is not liable for it as long as it does not know it is illegal and, once it
knows, acts expeditiously to remove it or disable access ([Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (Digital Services Act), art.
6](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32022R2065), read 2026-11-27); it offers an easy
electronic way to report illegal content, confirms receipt, decides in a timely, diligent and objective way and tells
the notifier its decision (art. 16), gives the author of a restricted post a clear statement of reasons with the ways
to challenge it (art. 17), and reports to the authorities any suspicion of an offence that threatens someone's life or
safety (art. 18) ([DSA, art. 16 to 18](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32022R2065), read
2026-11-27). It keeps the data that identify the authors of content for the periods of the decree
([Décret n° 2021-1362 du 20 octobre 2021](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000044228912), read
2026-11-27). Posts of private people are personal data ([CR-FR-050](../data-protection/cr-fr-050-privacy-by-design.md),
[CR-FR-052](../data-protection/cr-fr-052-storage-limitation.md)); AI answers show that they come from an AI
([CR-FR-056](../ai-and-recording/cr-fr-056-people-know-they-deal-with-an-ai.md)).

## Values

- Notice form (art. 16(2)): the reasons why the content is said to be illegal; its exact location (URL); the notifier's
  name and email (except for child sexual abuse offences); a statement of good faith. Receipt confirmed without undue
  delay; the decision sent with the ways to challenge it; the use of automated means stated (art. 16(4) to (6)).
- Statement of reasons to the author (art. 17(3)): what is restricted and for how long; the facts, and whether it
  followed a notice; any automated means; the legal or contractual ground; the ways to challenge (internal complaint,
  out-of-court settlement, court). Only where the author's electronic contact is known (art. 17(2)).
- Internal complaint system for online platforms (art. 20): free, electronic, open for at least 6 months after the
  decision; decisions taken under qualified staff, not solely by automated means; a micro or small enterprise
  (Recommendation 2003/361/EC) is exempt from this section (art. 19)
  ([DSA, art. 16 to 20](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32022R2065), read 2026-11-27).
- Data kept (décret n° 2021-1362, kept as data): the civil identity given by the user (names, postal and email
  addresses, phone), 5 years after the end of the contract (art. 2); the login, pseudonyms and password-check data, and
  the payment data, 1 year after the account is closed (art. 3 and 4); the connection identifier and protocols of each
  creation, change or deletion of content, 1 year from that operation (art. 5 and 7); only data actually collected
  (art. 8) ([Décret n° 2021-1362](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000044228912), read 2026-11-27).
- (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) Whether a company running a forum for its own customers is a hosting provider
  under the LCEN, art. 6, I, 2, bound by the retention decree, and the text of that article in force: not read.
- SimpleERP enforces: a reported post is hidden and waits in the moderation queue, and a removed post gives its author
  the reason and one appeal.

## Effective dates

The Digital Services Act applies since 2024-02-17 (art. 93); the decree since 2021-10-21 (texts above, read
2026-11-27).

## When the law changes

Watched in the law corpus: Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, art. 6 and 16 to 20; loi n° 2004-575, art. 6; décret n° 2021-1362.
A change flags this rule and warns the administrators; the corrected rule ships in a pack update (D294).

## Penalty

Member States set the penalties; a fine for failing an obligation of the Regulation is capped at 6 % of the yearly
worldwide turnover (art. 52(3))
([DSA, art. 52](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32022R2065), read 2026-11-27); the
French penalties are not read.

## Exceptions

Micro and small enterprises are exempt from the online-platform section, the internal complaint system included (art.
19); the liability exemption does not cover a user acting under the host's authority or control (art. 6(2)).

## Open questions

None: the texts were read on 2026-11-27; the LCEN status of a customer forum is a lawyer item above.
