# ⚖️ Benchmark

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Scope and method

SimpleERP against 13 named contenders, read on 2026-11-11
([D381](../../06-decisions/vision/d381-named-competitor-benchmark.md)): the classic ERPs Odoo, ERPNext and Dolibarr; the
home-care software Ximi by Xelya; the AI meeting assistant Noota; the AI support-agent platform Nebesta; the AI-native
ERPs Rillet, Campfire, DualEntry, Light, Lensing, Doss and ERPClaw. Two research agents read about 140 public pages
(vendor sites, pricing, docs, code repositories, reviews) and scored 65 criteria in five groups. Vendor statements are
marketing, not tested. SimpleERP's column is its specification, not a product.

## How to read

📐 specified for SimpleERP's first version, not built yet · ✅ in the standard offer · 💰 paid add-on, higher plan or
partner module · 🟡 partial, limited, beta or announced · ❌ no, or out of the product's scope · 🔍 not found in public
sources · ℹ️ context only · ⬜ not specified yet

## Key findings

- 🇫🇷 **France:** only Odoo (an approved e-invoicing platform since April 2026), Ximi (on the approved-platform list,
  e-invoicing in its licence) and Light (FEC, French chart of accounts, CA3 VAT return) cover French accounting
  duties. The five US AI-native ERPs show no French localisation.
- 💶 **Pricing:** Odoo charges per user and per app, plus AI credits; Odoo 20's release notes say "All AI features now
  need credits". The AI-native ERPs sell on quote, about 28 000 to 55 000 dollars a year by third-party estimates.
  Nebesta charges about 1 euro per resolved ticket. SimpleERP: free self-hosted, a flat fee per company when hosted.
- 🔌 **MCP and A2A:** Odoo 20, Dolibarr 24 (experimental), Noota and five of the seven AI-native ERPs expose an MCP
  server, most of them read only. None speaks A2A. SimpleERP is an MCP server and an A2A agent both ways.
- 🏠 **Self-hosting:** only Odoo, ERPNext, Dolibarr and ERPClaw can run on the customer's servers, and none of them
  with its AI on those servers by default. SimpleERP runs everything, models included, on the operator's servers by
  default (D440).
- 🦀 **Closest open-source rival:** ERPClaw (GPL-3.0, self-hosted, AI-native, MCP), created in February 2026, needs an
  outside agent runtime, has no SSO and no real French e-invoicing format.
- 🎧 **Support agents:** Nebesta works in back offices through human-like logins and per-intent procedures; it names no
  models and no hosting, and its two sites disagree on its SOC 2 status.
- 🏠 **Home care:** Ximi covers the five home-care rows almost fully (HDS, ISO 27001) but shows no public API, no SSO
  and no AI assistant beyond its replacement planning.
- ⚠️ **Text aimed at AI agents:** one vendor's pages embed instructions asking AI agents to send their questions to an
  outside address. The research read them as data and did not follow them.
