# 🛣️ P2: Platform core

**Status:** 🟡 Draft · **Sprints:** S08 to S15 · **Dates:** 29 Mar to 16 Jul 2027 · **Tickets:** 88

> [!NOTE]
> P2 builds the thirteen technical capabilities T1 to T13 on the P0 kit, so that every platform feature of P3 and every business area after it finds companies kept apart, sign-in and rights, an inalterable audit trail, the plugin runtime, the API, MCP and A2A, the AI gateway, search, install and backups, languages and notifications, country packs, connectors and maps already in place ([D16](../../../06-decisions/foundations/d16-platform-first.md): the whole platform before any business area).

## <a id="goal"></a>🎯 Goal

At the end of P0 the repository has its layout, the shared models and libraries, the laptop profile, the Helm and Terraform skeletons and the back-office shell; at the end of P1 the DevOps agents turn specification tickets into pull requests. Nothing yet stores a company, signs a user in or calls a model. At the end of P2:

- Each company has its own database, bucket and vector collection, its context is checked at the gateway, in every service and by row-level security, and one isolation suite proves it on every pull request ([TC-01](../../../03-technical/challenges/tc-01-isolation-at-every-layer.md)).
- Every user signs in through Keycloak with two factors, and every service, the API, MCP, A2A, search and maps take the same decision from one policy library; every refusal is audited ([T2](../../../03-technical/t02-identity-and-access/README.md)).
- Every action, AI and agent actions included, is in an append-only audit trail hash-chained per company, searchable, exportable and erasable where the law says ([T3](../../../03-technical/t03-audit-trail/README.md)).
- Modules switch on in one click; signed plugins install on a copy first and run in a sandbox through the public API only; planning and calculation run as workers ([T4](../../../03-technical/t04-plugin-runtime/README.md)).
- Every service publishes its OpenAPI behind one gateway, with paging, idempotency, problem+json, rate limits, signed webhooks, contract tests and the offline sync protocol ([T5](../../../03-technical/t05-api-and-webhooks/README.md)); the same tools reach MCP clients and A2A agents with confirmations, audit and metering ([T6](../../../03-technical/t06-mcp-and-a2a-gateway/README.md)).
- Every model call goes through one AI gateway, local on CPU by default, metered, masked, traced and gated by evaluation sets ([T7](../../../03-technical/t07-ai-providers-and-models/README.md)); search follows changes and rights ([T8](../../../03-technical/t08-rag-platform/README.md)).
- SimpleERP installs with Compose on a laptop within 8 GB, with Helm on any Kubernetes and with Terraform on OVHcloud and Scaleway; backups, restores of one company and upgrades on a copy work ([T9](../../../03-technical/t09-install-backups-and-updates/README.md)).
- Strings are reviewed, money is exact, one service notifies on every channel ([T10](../../../03-technical/t10-translations-and-notifications/README.md)); country packs are signed and read by date of the fact, the law is ingested and its changes flag their rules ([T11](../../../03-technical/t11-legal-corpora/README.md)).
- The connector catalogue covers every category, credentials sit in the vault, connectors run in a sandbox, in static or agentic mode, and the factory generates new ones ([T12](../../../03-technical/t12-connectors-and-scrapers/README.md)); maps, geocoding, routing, positions and the scheduling engine run on the company's own servers ([T13](../../../03-technical/t13-maps-and-geodata/README.md)).

The phase ends with the [demo](#demo) and the [exit criteria](#exit). Functional screens that use these capabilities (administration, no-code configuration, the assistant) come in P3.

## <a id="risks"></a>🎲 Risks

- **The 8 GB laptop budget** is a promise only once measured ([REQ-T09-03](../../../03-technical/t09-install-backups-and-updates/requirements/req-t09-03-laptop-profile.md)): Keycloak, PostgreSQL, OpenBao, the event bus, the workflow engine, the AI gateway and Milvus together may not fit. T2.17 measures it in S09, early enough to drop Milvus with the AI off ([REQ-T08-01](../../../03-technical/t08-rag-platform/requirements/req-t08-01-milvus-collections.md)) or to cut the profile.
- **The connector volume** ([REQ-T12-01](../../../03-technical/t12-connectors-and-scrapers/requirements/req-t12-01-connector-catalogue.md), [REQ-T12-16](../../../03-technical/t12-connectors-and-scrapers/requirements/req-t12-16-fifty-ats-crm.md)): fifty CRM and ATS plus every catalogue category in two sprints. T2.83 and T2.84 count on the factory (T2.75); if it lands late, the remaining connectors move to P3 alongside F24, and the exit criterion on the catalogue becomes (Proposed).
- **Paid tools' sandboxes** (CRMs, ATS, e-invoicing platforms, Teams, Zoom, Meet) need accounts that take time to obtain; tests run on recorded stand-ins until they arrive (TEST-2), and the daily contract tests ([REQ-T12-17](../../../03-technical/t12-connectors-and-scrapers/requirements/req-t12-17-contract-tests-daily.md)) only count once they run against the real sandbox.
- **Capabilities used before their features exist:** e-invoicing adapters before F8 invoices (P6), meeting capture before F4 meeting records (P3), the scheduling engine before F9's attributes and screens (P7) ([REQ-T12-19](../../../03-technical/t12-connectors-and-scrapers/requirements/req-t12-19-e-invoicing-platforms.md), [REQ-T12-18](../../../03-technical/t12-connectors-and-scrapers/requirements/req-t12-18-meeting-capture.md), [REQ-T13-07](../../../03-technical/t13-maps-and-geodata/requirements/req-t13-07-scheduling-engine.md)). Each ticket tests with fixtures and leaves the business wiring to its feature.
- **(Proposed) placements and choices still open:** the policy engine (Cedar or OPA, [D414](../../../06-decisions/technical/d414-security-design.md)), the event bus (NATS or Kafka) and the S3-compatible server (Garage or MinIO) are chosen in T2.03 and T2.08; the T13 requirements are Must (Proposed) or Should (Proposed), and the cadastre pick and drawing import of [REQ-T13-04](../../../03-technical/t13-maps-and-geodata/requirements/req-t13-04-areas-and-outlines.md) and [REQ-T13-08](../../../03-technical/t13-maps-and-geodata/requirements/req-t13-08-floor-and-venue-plans.md) stay out until the owner confirms them.
- **Terraform on two clouds** needs real accounts and costs money at every apply; each run is recorded and the projects are destroyed after it.
