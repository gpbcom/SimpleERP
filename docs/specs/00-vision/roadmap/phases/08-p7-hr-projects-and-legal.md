# 🛣️ P7: HR, projects and legal

**Status:** 🟡 Draft · **Sprints:** S34 to S37 · **Dates:** 10 Apr to 2 Jun 2028 · **Tickets:** 88

> [!NOTE]
> P7 builds F9 HR and projects and F15 Legal assistant, step 6 of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md): people, French HR duties, recruitment, time, shifts, projects, field service and scheduling, then the legal assistant that answers for a person's real situation from F9's data and warns on legal risks. It comes now because F9 needs the platform (P2, P3) and the books (P6), and F15's French HR warnings need F9's contracts, schedules and headcount; F15's placement here is (Proposed), as in the roadmap.

## <a id="goal"></a>🎯 Goal

At the end of P6, SimpleERP sells, buys, stocks and keeps the books, but knows nothing of the people who work there or of the law that governs them. P7 closes that gap:

- **People and French duties.** HR keeps employee records, contracts from templates, onboarding and offboarding plans and self-service, declares every hire to URSSAF before the first day and keeps the personnel register, the interviews, the health visits, the displays, the BDESE and the OETH as the France pack dates them ([D287](../../../06-decisions/f09-hr-and-projects/d287-french-hr-duties.md), [D466](../../../06-decisions/f09-hr-and-projects/d466-f09-gaps-and-improvements.md)).
- **Recruitment.** Job positions on the career site and the boards, the applicant pipeline with AI CV reading and voice pre-screening, offers signed and turned into hires, referrals, and LinkedIn sourcing through Unipile with HR approving every shortlist ([D518](../../../06-decisions/f09-hr-and-projects/d518-linkedin-sourcing-through-unipile.md)).
- **Time and work.** Time off, attendance with location at check-in only, remote work, shifts with auto-planning, timesheets with opt-in drafts, projects with Gantt, profitability, capacity, risks and the customer's view, subcontractors with their vigilance documents.
- **Field service and scheduling.** Worksheets on the phone offline, rounds ordered by the shared optimiser ([D457](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d457-gaps-and-improvements-round.md)), work requests scheduled to the best eligible person with an automatic mode that stops on a problem, the planning map, and live positions only where a team opted in.
- **The legal assistant.** Legal answers in the assistant panel that cite each article with its version and date, for the asker's real situation, with their confidence and a lawyer hand-off; warnings that inform and never block; document checks; the compliance calendar, contracts' notice dates, company registers, data-subject requests, the AI uses inventory; the cap table, equity, general meetings, the clause library, IP and litigation.

The pilot of [D451](../../../06-decisions/vision/d451-dated-pilot-before-the-release.md) runs F1 to F9: P7 completes its scope.
