# 🛣️ P5: Purchasing and stock

**Status:** ✅ specified (D569) · **Sprints:** S28 to S30 · **Dates:** 17 Jan to 25 Feb 2028 · **Tickets:** 35

> [!NOTE]
> P5 builds [F7 Purchasing and stock](../../../02-functional/f07-purchasing-and-stock/README.md) from procure to pay and from receipt to shipment, with the supplier portal and the warehouse barcode app, because accounting (P6) books what F7 buys, values and counts, and the platform (P2, P3) and sales orders (P4) it stands on are in place. It is step 4 of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md) ([D483](../../../06-decisions/vision/d483-roadmap-devops-agents-first.md)); its exit criteria are the roadmap's: F7 passes its acceptance criteria.

## <a id="goal"></a>🎯 Goal

At the end of P4, a company quotes, sells and invoices, but it cannot buy, receive, store or ship what it sells. P5 delivers the whole of F7 as the specifications hold it ([D266](../../../06-decisions/f07-purchasing-and-stock/d266-purchasing-and-stock-scope.md) to [D275](../../../06-decisions/f07-purchasing-and-stock/d275-no-negative-stock.md), [D36](../../../06-decisions/f07-purchasing-and-stock/d36-what-a-location-holds.md), [D305](../../../06-decisions/f07-purchasing-and-stock/d305-f7-benchmark-gaps.md), [D464](../../../06-decisions/f07-purchasing-and-stock/d464-f07-gaps-and-improvements.md), [D526](../../../06-decisions/f07-purchasing-and-stock/d526-benchmark-gaps-f07-purchasing-and-stock.md)):

- **Procure to pay:** a purchase request is approved by amount and budget ([BR-F7-01](../../../02-functional/f07-purchasing-and-stock/business-rules/br-f07-01-purchases-approved-by-amount-and-budget.md)), becomes a purchase order at the contract price ([BR-F7-11](../../../02-functional/f07-purchasing-and-stock/business-rules/br-f07-11-contract-prices-apply-within-their-validity.md)), is confirmed by the supplier in the supplier portal ([D17](../../../06-decisions/foundations/d17-portal-sign-in.md)) or by EDI ([BR-F7-23](../../../02-functional/f07-purchasing-and-stock/business-rules/br-f07-23-edi-messages-are-acknowledged-or-retried.md)), and its supplier invoice, received from the e-invoicing platform ([D454](../../../06-decisions/f08-accounting/d454-e-invoicing-platforms.md)) or read by the AI, is matched three ways ([BR-F7-03](../../../02-functional/f07-purchasing-and-stock/business-rules/br-f07-03-supplier-invoices-are-matched.md)).
- **Receive, store and ship:** goods are received in 1, 2 or 3 steps, checked and put away by rules and capacities ([D271](../../../06-decisions/f07-purchasing-and-stock/d271-warehouse-operations.md)), picked by batch, wave or cluster with FEFO ([BR-F7-04](../../../02-functional/f07-purchasing-and-stock/business-rules/br-f07-04-expiring-items-leave-first-expired-first.md)), packed and shipped with carriers, returned or scrapped; every move is traced and never deleted ([BR-F7-05](../../../02-functional/f07-purchasing-and-stock/business-rules/br-f07-05-every-move-is-traced.md)), and stock never goes negative unless a manager allows it ([BR-F7-08](../../../02-functional/f07-purchasing-and-stock/business-rules/br-f07-08-no-negative-stock.md)).
- **Replenish:** reordering rules, safety stock and min/max ([BR-F7-14](../../../02-functional/f07-purchasing-and-stock/business-rules/br-f07-14-safety-stock-triggers-a-suggestion.md)) and the AI forecast suggest orders that a buyer confirms ([BR-F7-02](../../../02-functional/f07-purchasing-and-stock/business-rules/br-f07-02-reorders-are-suggested-then-confirmed.md)).
- **Count, trace and value:** cycle and yearly counts on phones with approvals ([BR-F7-06](../../../02-functional/f07-purchasing-and-stock/business-rules/br-f07-06-count-differences-need-approval.md)), lots and serials traced and recalled, stock valued by its category's costing method ([BR-F7-07](../../../02-functional/f07-purchasing-and-stock/business-rules/br-f07-07-costing-method-per-category.md)), perpetual or periodic ([BR-F7-21](../../../02-functional/f07-purchasing-and-stock/business-rules/br-f07-21-periodic-valuation-posts-only-at-period-end.md)), with landed costs and stock analytics.
- **The warehouse barcode app** on phones, offline, where a scan that does not match stops the step ([BR-F7-20](../../../02-functional/f07-purchasing-and-stock/business-rules/br-f07-20-a-scan-that-does-not-match-the-expected-item-stops-the-step.md)).
- **Supplier agreements, compliance and the benchmark additions:** contracts and rebates, supplier performance, approved catalogues and punch-out, eco-contributions, dangerous goods, purchase carbon, calls for tenders, trade between group companies, third-party warehouses, repairs, consignment stock, returnable packaging and slotting.
- **For agents:** every F7 action reachable from the assistant ([BR-F3-34](../../../02-functional/f03-assistant/business-rules/br-f03-34-every-capability-is-reachable-from-the-assistant.md)), the MCP tools and the A2A skill of [F7 integrations](../../../02-functional/f07-purchasing-and-stock/integrations.md).

The France country pack gives F7 its legal values ([D293](../../../06-decisions/country-packs/d293-capabilities-take-legal-rules-from-the-pack.md)): the yearly count ([CR-FR-007](../../../10-country-packs/france/accounting/cr-fr-007-annual-stock-count.md)), e-invoicing reception ([CR-FR-016](../../../10-country-packs/france/invoicing/cr-fr-016-electronic-invoicing.md)), the payment terms cap ([CR-FR-018](../../../10-country-packs/france/invoicing/cr-fr-018-payment-terms-cap.md)), food traceability ([CR-FR-061](../../../10-country-packs/france/products/cr-fr-061-food-traceability.md)) and product recall ([CR-FR-062](../../../10-country-packs/france/products/cr-fr-062-product-recall.md)), with the other rules the F7 use cases cite.

## <a id="risks"></a>🎲 Risks

- **Area locks on the stock service:** S30 holds seven tickets of `backend/services/stock`, which run one at a time (TKT-16); if one slips, T5.34 Slotting or T5.32 Repair products moves to the first sprint of P6.
- **Carrier and EDI partners:** the carrier connectors and the EDI exchange need sandboxes or recorded answers; without a supplier's EDI test account, T5.33 is proven against recorded messages only.
- **The forecast with little history:** a new company has too little history for the AI forecast; [UC-F7-05](../../../02-functional/f07-purchasing-and-stock/use-cases/03-replenish/uc-f07-05-replenish-stock.md) 1e says so and falls back on rules only, which the demo must show rather than hide.
- **E-invoicing platforms:** reception depends on [D454](../../../06-decisions/f08-accounting/d454-e-invoicing-platforms.md) and the open [Q13](../../../07-questions/q13-e-invoicing-platforms.md); a platform change moves T5.16's connector, not its matching.
- **Accounting comes after:** F7 publishes entries that only P6 posts; until then the valuation is checked on its events and reports, not in the books.
