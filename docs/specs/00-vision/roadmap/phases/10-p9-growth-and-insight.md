# 🛣️ P9: Growth and insight

**Status:** ✅ specified (D569) · **Sprints:** S44 to S46 · **Dates:** 28 Aug to 6 Oct 2028 · **Tickets:** 36

> [!NOTE]
> P9 builds social media and marketing (F5), competitive analysis (F17) and what-if simulation (F23), the three capabilities that read the data of every business area built in P4 to P8. Its placement, step 8 of the roadmap, is adopted with option A ([D569](../../../06-decisions/vision/d569-roadmap-option-a-in-twelve-phases.md)) ([D483](../../../06-decisions/vision/d483-roadmap-devops-agents-first.md), [Q20](../../../07-questions/q20-which-roadmap-option-orders-v1.md)).

## <a id="goal"></a>🎯 Goal

At the end of P8, SimpleERP runs the whole company: sales, purchasing and stock, accounting, HR, projects, the legal assistant and the first-party plugins. P9 adds the three capabilities that grow it and help it decide, each in its own service cut by business domain ([D405](../../../06-decisions/technical/d405-architecture-and-scalability.md)):

- **`marketing` (F5):** marketing prepares, approves and publishes posts on the ten networks, answers one inbox, reads statistics, and runs email, SMS, WhatsApp, push and postal campaigns on consented segments, with journeys, A/B tests and the return of each campaign; events sell tickets online, at the box office and at the door through the one ticketing model shared with F10 and the sectors; landing pages and forms bring leads to F6.
- **`competition` (F17):** the owner and marketing follow competitors from lawful public and paid sources only, every fact with its source, and turn the signals into comparisons, sourced SWOT, market shares, battle cards on the F6 deals, alerts, digests and price suggestions that a person approves.
- **`simulation` (F23):** the owner and accounting ask "what happens if" and get the impact on every area against the baseline forecast, computed on a copy through the ERP's own rules, with ranges, sensitivity, comparison, counter-measures, goals, financing, a collaborative budget, group scenarios and valuation; the AI assistant builds and explains scenarios, and nothing changes the live company until a person applies an action.

## <a id="demo"></a>🎤 Demo

On the laptop profile, with the demo company of P4 to P8:
