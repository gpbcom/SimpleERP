# 🛣️ P6: Accounting

**Status:** 🟡 Draft · **Sprints:** S31 to S33 · **Dates:** 28 Feb to 7 Apr 2028 · **Tickets:** 36

> [!NOTE]
> P6 builds [F8](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/README.md), the company's books end to end, on top of the invoices of [F6](../../../02-functional/f06-customers-and-sales/README.md) (P4) and the bills and stock of [F7](../../../02-functional/f07-purchasing-and-stock/README.md) (P5): the ledger with the France pack, banks and payments, VAT and filings, e-reporting and Peppol, assets, books, closing and year end, and the work of the external accountant and the auditor, because [F9](../../../02-functional/f09-hr-and-projects/README.md) (P7) and [F13](../../../02-functional/f13-payroll/README.md) (P8) post their entries into it ([UC-F8-02](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/use-cases/01-set-up-and-post/uc-f08-02-post-and-review-entries.md)).

## <a id="goal"></a>🎯 Goal

At the end of P5, SimpleERP sells, buys, stocks and issues and receives e-invoices, but nothing reaches a ledger. P6 delivers [F8](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/README.md) as its specification states it ([D277](../../../06-decisions/f08-accounting/d277-accounting-coverage.md) to [D284](../../../06-decisions/f08-accounting/d284-ai-in-bookkeeping.md), [D306](../../../06-decisions/f08-accounting/d306-f8-benchmark-gaps.md), [D465](../../../06-decisions/f08-accounting/d465-f08-gaps-and-improvements.md), [D527](../../../06-decisions/f08-accounting/d527-benchmark-gaps-f08-accounting.md)):

- **The ledger** of a French company is set up from the France country pack (PCG, VAT, FEC), its entries are balanced, numbered without gaps, inalterable and hash-chained, and periods lock as [BR-F8-02](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/business-rules/br-f08-02-lock-dates.md) says.
- **Sales and purchases post** into it: F6 invoices and credit notes, F7 bills and stock, through one posting contract that F9 and F13 reuse in their phases.
- **Banks** are connected through an aggregator or statement files, every line is reconciled ([BR-F8-03](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/business-rules/br-f08-03-every-bank-line-is-reconciled.md)), customers pay by transfer, direct debit, cheque or LCR, and suppliers are paid by payment runs and payment initiation with IBAN checks and two approvals ([BR-F8-08](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/business-rules/br-f08-08-new-ibans-are-checked.md), [BR-F8-26](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/business-rules/br-f08-26-payments-above-the-limit-need-two-approvals.md)).
- **VAT returns** are prepared, validated by an accountant and filed through the EDI partner ([BR-F8-04](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/business-rules/br-f08-04-filings-need-an-accountant.md)); e-reporting and payment statuses go through the e-invoicing platforms of [D454](../../../06-decisions/f08-accounting/d454-e-invoicing-platforms.md), and Peppol serves only flows outside them ([BR-F8-30](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/business-rules/br-f08-30-peppol-serves-only-flows-outside-the-french-platforms.md)).
- **Assets, loans, leases, revaluation, books** (French GAAP, IFRS, management, cash basis), analytics, budgets, planning and the daily cash position.
- **The closing and the year end**: checklist, hard lock, corporate tax, annual accounts, doubtful debts, consolidation, the continuous close with agents and the monthly flux analysis.
- **The external accountant and the statutory auditor** work in their own workspaces ([D280](../../../06-decisions/f08-accounting/d280-bookkeepers.md), [BR-F8-28](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/business-rules/br-f08-28-the-auditor-reads-only-and-every-view-is-logged.md)); client money, grants, non-profit accounts and the sustainability report complete the capability ([D457](../../../06-decisions/sectors/d457-gaps-and-improvements-round.md)).

## <a id="risks"></a>🎲 Risks

- **The dated pilot of [D451](../../../06-decisions/vision/d451-dated-pilot-before-the-release.md) cannot meet its date with this calendar.** D451 runs F1 to F9 with e-invoicing before 1 September 2027, when small companies must issue e-invoices ([CR-FR-016](../../../10-country-packs/france/invoicing/cr-fr-016-electronic-invoicing.md), [e-invoicing platforms](../../../05-non-functional/e-invoicing-platforms.md)); P6 ends on 24 March 2028 and F9 in P7. Either the pilot's date or the order of the phases changes ([Q16](../../../07-questions/q16-pilot-before-the-first-release.md)). (Proposed: the owner decides.)
- **The bank aggregator is not named yet** ([UC-F8-06](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/use-cases/03-bank-and-collect/uc-f08-06-connect-banks-and-reconcile.md): one aggregator chosen at T12, with its list of French banks). (Proposed: T6.06 builds on the one E2.12 chose, statement files covering the rest.)
- **Several France-pack rules are still being read** ([CR-FR-212](../../../10-country-packs/france/payments/cr-fr-212-lcr-and-cheque-remittances.md), [CR-FR-216](../../../10-country-packs/france/accounting/cr-fr-216-leases-in-french-gaap.md); the conversion differences rule of [UC-F8-32](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/use-cases/11-treasury-and-payment-means/uc-f08-32-revalue-foreign-currency-items.md); the CGI and Code de commerce articles of [UC-F8-24](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/use-cases/08-year-end-duties/uc-f08-24-compute-and-pay-corporate-tax-and-local-business-taxes.md) to [UC-F8-26](../../../02-functional/f08-accounting/use-cases/08-year-end-duties/uc-f08-26-provision-doubtful-debts.md)): their tickets build the behaviour the use cases state, and a pack update corrects the values ([D293](../../../06-decisions/country-packs/d293-capabilities-take-legal-rules-from-the-pack.md)).
- **The area lock serialises the phase:** all 36 tickets change the accounting service, so they run one at a time in it; the sprint plan holds only if each ticket's own tasks run in parallel.
- **Sandboxes of outside partners** (EDI partner, card and travel providers, payment initiation) may be missing or slow to open; their tickets wait for them as external items.
