# 🛣️ P4: Customers and sales

**Status:** ✅ specified (D569) · **Sprints:** S24 to S27 · **Dates:** 8 Nov 2027 to 14 Jan 2028 · **Tickets:** 48

> [!NOTE]
> P4 builds the whole sales cycle of [F6](../../../02-functional/f06-customers-and-sales/README.md), from the first lead to the last payment with the customer portal, French e-invoicing and Chorus Pro, then the customer support agents of [F16](../../../02-functional/f16-customer-support-agents/README.md), which resolve customers' requests with F6's data. It comes now because the platform core (P2) and the platform features (P3) are in place, every later business area (stock, accounting, HR) starts from F6's customers, orders and invoices, and small and micro companies must issue e-invoices from 1 September 2027 ([CR-FR-016](../../../10-country-packs/france/invoicing/cr-fr-016-electronic-invoicing.md)).

## <a id="goal"></a>🎯 Goal

At the end of P3, a company runs on SimpleERP with its administration, no-code configuration, assistant, meetings,
onboarding, cost management and integrations hub, but sells nothing yet. P4 delivers the first business area and the
support around it:

- **Lead to quote** ([FL-F6-01](../../../02-functional/f06-customers-and-sales/flows/fl-f06-01-lead-to-quote.md)): leads captured, scored, assigned and enriched; the pipeline and its weighted forecast; price lists, discounts with approval ([D255](../../../06-decisions/f06-customers-and-sales/d255-discount-approval.md)), the product configurator and versioned quotes, VAT shown everywhere ([D259](../../../06-decisions/f06-customers-and-sales/d259-vat-visible.md)).
- **Quote to cash** ([FL-F6-02](../../../02-functional/f06-customers-and-sales/flows/fl-f06-02-quote-to-order.md), [FL-F6-03](../../../02-functional/f06-customers-and-sales/flows/fl-f06-03-invoice-and-get-paid.md)): acceptance by click or e-signature ([D254](../../../06-decisions/f06-customers-and-sales/d254-quote-acceptance.md)), orders, deposit, progress and final invoices with the retention guarantee ([D258](../../../06-decisions/f06-customers-and-sales/d258-invoicing-schemes.md)), e-invoices through the company's registered platform ([D454](../../../06-decisions/f08-accounting/d454-e-invoicing-platforms.md)), online payments with no card number in SimpleERP ([D253](../../../06-decisions/f06-customers-and-sales/d253-online-payments.md)) and fully automatic reminders ([D256](../../../06-decisions/f06-customers-and-sales/d256-automatic-reminders.md)).
- **Everything else F6 holds** ([D463](../../../06-decisions/f06-customers-and-sales/d463-f06-gaps-and-improvements.md), [D525](../../../06-decisions/f06-customers-and-sales/d525-benchmark-gaps-f06-customers-and-sales.md)): subscriptions, usage and rentals; account hierarchies, credit limits, frame contracts and solvency checks; Chorus Pro, multi-payer invoices, pro-formas and statements; instalment plans and disputes; offline orders on the road; targets, commissions, partners, reports and warranty claims; and the customer portal ([D252](../../../06-decisions/f06-customers-and-sales/d252-customer-portal.md)).
- **Customer support agents** ([D237](../../../06-decisions/f16-customer-support-agents/d237-customer-support-agents.md), [D262](../../../06-decisions/f16-customer-support-agents/d262-f16-scope-confirmed.md)): every request taken in as one work request ([D513](../../../06-decisions/f16-customer-support-agents/d513-one-work-request-whatever-the-channel.md)), intents with versioned procedures, agents that resolve requests in SimpleERP or inside an outside helpdesk ([D263](../../../06-decisions/f16-customer-support-agents/d263-built-in-and-outside-helpdesks.md)), shadow first and automatic only above the bar ([D261](../../../06-decisions/f16-customer-support-agents/d261-go-live-bar.md)), sampled reviews ([D264](../../../06-decisions/f16-customer-support-agents/d264-human-sampling.md)), the people's workspace, the customer side, live chat, the voice agent, outreach and prepaid hours ([D473](../../../06-decisions/f16-customer-support-agents/d473-f16-gaps-and-improvements.md), [D535](../../../06-decisions/f16-customer-support-agents/d535-benchmark-gaps-f16-customer-support-agents.md)).

Two services carry it, cut by business domain ([D405](../../../06-decisions/technical/d405-architecture-and-scalability.md)): `sales` for F6 and `support` for F16, with their screens in the back office, the customer portal and the mobile app ([D407](../../../06-decisions/technical/d407-front-end.md), [D438](../../../06-decisions/technical/d438-repository-layout.md)).

## <a id="demo"></a>🎤 Demo

On the laptop profile, with the example companies:

1. Distribution Lemoine receives a form lead from Café Bellecour: it is deduplicated, scored, assigned to Lea by the Lyon
   rule and enriched from its SIRET; Lea converts it and the forecast moves.
2. Lea builds a quote with the Restaurants price list and an 18 % discount; the manager approves; Claire Durand signs it
   in the portal on her phone and the 30 % deposit opens.
3. The order is held at the credit limit, released by accounting with a reason; the invoice is issued as Factur-X and
   sent through the platform sandbox; Acme Bâtiment's progress invoice to a town hall is deposited on Chorus Pro.
4. A card payment matches its invoice; a disputed line pauses its reminders only; an instalment plan replaces the due
   date.
5. Nuvola Logiciels' subscription bills 12 seats, then a pro-rata upgrade; the home-care service splits November between
   the département and Mme Roche.
6. A customer writes about a late delivery: the agent classifies it, refunds 40.00 EUR within its limit and says it is an
   AI; a second identical request triggers no second refund; a fifth refund in 60 days is held for a person.
7. The support lead sees the intent pass 99 % over 212 shadow cases and switches it to automatic; the dashboard shows
   the resolved share, satisfaction and service levels.
8. A customer asks for a person in live chat: Ana takes it with the whole transcript; a phone call is answered by the
   voice agent, which says it is an AI and transfers.
9. The assistant answers "which invoices of Brasserie du Parc are overdue?" from a query and drafts a reminder for
   confirmation.
