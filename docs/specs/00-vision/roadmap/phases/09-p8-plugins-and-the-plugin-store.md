# 🛣️ P8: Plugins and the plugin store

**Status:** ✅ specified (D569) · **Sprints:** S38 to S43 · **Dates:** 5 Jun to 25 Aug 2028 · **Tickets:** 72

> [!NOTE]
> P8 builds the four industry plugins (point of sale, online shop, manufacturing with recipes, payroll) on the public API only, like a third party would ([D15](../../../06-decisions/foundations/d15-industry-areas-as-plugins.md)), and the plugin store that distributes them and every other plugin, now that the platform core (P2), customers and sales (P4), purchasing and stock (P5), accounting (P6) and HR (P7) they post into are merged. The plugins and the store run in parallel, one epic each, and the phase ends with the four plugins installed, updated and uninstalled from the store.

## <a id="goal"></a>🎯 Goal

At the end of P7, SimpleERP sells, buys, stocks, keeps the books and manages people, but it has no till, no online shop,
no production and no payroll, and plugins can only be switched on by hand through the module registry
([REQ-T04-03](../../../03-technical/t04-plugin-runtime/requirements/req-t04-03-module-registry.md)). P8 delivers:

- **Point of sale** ([F10](../../../02-functional/f10-point-of-sale/README.md)): shop counter, payments, receipts,
  restaurant floors and kitchen screens, self-order and kiosks, offline selling and delivery platforms, built to the
  France till rules of the country pack from its first version
  ([D296](../../../06-decisions/f10-point-of-sale/d296-till-certified-before-france.md)).
- **Online shop** ([F11](../../../02-functional/f11-online-shop/README.md)): the website, catalogue, checkout, payments,
  delivery, withdrawals, special sales, marketplaces through connectors
  ([D309](../../../06-decisions/f11-online-shop/d309-shop-channels-through-connectors.md)) and the legal pages.
- **Manufacturing** ([F12](../../../02-functional/f12-manufacturing/README.md)): bills of materials, planning, orders,
  the shop-floor tablet, quality, maintenance, costs, machines through connectors
  ([D314](../../../06-decisions/f12-manufacturing/d314-machines-through-connectors.md)), compliance, recipes and batches.
- **Payroll** ([F13](../../../02-functional/f13-payroll/README.md)): gross to net with collective-agreement rules and
  withholding tax, clarified payslips, SEPA payments, the monthly social declaration (DSN), corrections, events, special
  contracts and schemes, with rates updated each 1 January and 1 July through the country pack
  ([D365](../../../06-decisions/f13-payroll/d365-payroll-coverage.md)).
- **Plugin store** ([F14](../../../02-functional/f14-plugin-store/README.md)): browse, install with permissions, update,
  uninstall with export, buy support and updates ([D443](../../../06-decisions/vision/d443-paid-plugins-sell-support-updates-hosting.md)),
  the publisher's submission with automated checks, security review and signing, money and seller reporting, private
  stores, withdrawals, release channels and end of life.

Each plugin is a uv package under `plugins/<plugin>/` that imports `erp_models` and reaches SimpleERP only through the
public API (T5) with its declared permissions; first-party plugins run as services
([REQ-T04-01](../../../03-technical/t04-plugin-runtime/requirements/req-t04-01-sandbox-by-trust.md)) and declare their
back-office screens with the design system's components
([REQ-T04-06](../../../03-technical/t04-plugin-runtime/requirements/req-t04-06-declared-screens.md)), built with the
plugin SDK ([D425](../../../06-decisions/technical/d425-plugin-sdk-and-cli.md)).

## <a id="demo"></a>🎤 Demo

On the laptop profile, with the seeded company of the plugin SDK's sandbox:

1. The administrator opens the plugin store, installs the point-of-sale plugin with its permissions shown first, and
   sets up a till with its scale, printer and card terminal (T8.61, T8.01).
2. The cashier logs in with a PIN, opens the session with its float, sells weighed items and a combo, takes a card
   payment, gives a digital receipt, refunds one item with the manager's approval, sells offline while the network is
   cut, and closes the session; the closure, its grand totals and the chained event log are checked, and the session
   is posted to the books and stock (T8.03 to T8.08).
3. A waiter takes a table's order, the kitchen screen shows it, the table splits the bill; a customer orders by QR at
   another table (T8.09 to T8.11).
4. A customer buys in the online shop as a guest, pays, chooses click & collect, receives the order confirmation and
   the invoice, then uses the withdrawal function; the gift card bought online is used at the till (T8.21 to T8.23,
   T8.32).
5. A manufacturing order is launched from a bill of materials, followed on the shop-floor tablet offline, its lots
   traced both ways and its cost posted (T8.33 to T8.38, T8.43).
6. The payroll officer runs the month: inputs from HR, gross to net under the Syntec agreement, the anomaly check,
   approval, clarified payslips, the SEPA file and the DSN (T8.47 to T8.52, T8.58).
7. A publisher submits a plugin, it passes the automated checks, the security review and signing, goes to the beta
   channel and rolls out in stages; the administrator updates, rolls back, and uninstalls it with its export kept
   (T8.63, T8.64, T8.69, T8.62).
