# 🛣️ P11: Hosted edition services

**Status:** ✅ specified (D569) · **Sprints:** S53 to S56 · **Dates:** 22 Jan to 16 Mar 2029 · **Tickets:** 48

> [!NOTE]
> P11 builds the publisher's three closed services of the hosted edition, hosted websites and domains (F18), the feature factory (F19) and the hosted support assistant (F20), last, because each one drives a finished product from outside: it reaches SimpleERP only through the public API, MCP, the plugin runtime and the read-only namespace access built in P2, and reads the data of the business areas built in P3 to P10 ([D388](../../../06-decisions/vision/d388-hosted-edition-open-core.md), [D483](../../../06-decisions/vision/d483-roadmap-devops-agents-first.md)).

## <a id="goal"></a>🎯 Goal

At the end of P10 every ERP capability runs, open and self-hostable ([D447](../../../06-decisions/vision/d447-country-pack-updates-free-hosted-only-named.md)). P11 adds what only the hosted edition sells, as separate programs that never link or copy AGPL code ([D388](../../../06-decisions/vision/d388-hosted-edition-open-core.md)):

- **Websites and domains (F18):** a company builds its website from its ERP data and visual identity, with a contact page that feeds leads, a careers page synchronised with LinkedIn and Indeed, its own domain, DNS, certificates and mail records driven through the providers' APIs, and every publication checked on a private preview and approved by the customer ([D385](../../../06-decisions/f18-hosted-websites-and-domains/d385-hosted-websites-coverage.md), [D386](../../../06-decisions/f18-hosted-websites-and-domains/d386-website-publication-gate.md), [D475](../../../06-decisions/f18-hosted-websites-and-domains/d475-f18-gaps-and-improvements.md), [D537](../../../06-decisions/f18-hosted-websites-and-domains/d537-benchmark-gaps-f18-hosted-websites-and-domains.md)).
- **Feature factory (F19):** the owner describes a need to a chatbot, approves a specification and a quote of 300 EUR a month excluding VAT; the factory generates a plugin that is checked, reviewed, signed, tried on a copy and installed; a promoted feature reaches every hosted customer and earns its promoter a credit ([D389](../../../06-decisions/f19-feature-factory/d389-generated-features-are-plugins.md), [D390](../../../06-decisions/f19-feature-factory/d390-feature-price-and-promotion-credit.md), [D476](../../../06-decisions/f19-feature-factory/d476-f19-gaps-and-improvements.md), [D538](../../../06-decisions/f19-feature-factory/d538-benchmark-gaps-f19-feature-factory.md)).
- **Hosted support assistant (F20):** a customer who may not be technical talks a problem through; the assistant reads the customer's own namespace read-only, explains the cause in plain words, and fixes only with a [SimpleERP maintainer](../../../01-users/personas/u47-simpleerp-maintainer.md)'s yes; incidents, maintenance, release notes, service credits and health reports keep customers informed ([D393](../../../06-decisions/f20-hosted-support-assistant/d393-hosted-support-assistant-coverage.md), [D477](../../../06-decisions/f20-hosted-support-assistant/d477-f20-gaps-and-improvements.md), [D539](../../../06-decisions/f20-hosted-support-assistant/d539-benchmark-gaps-f20-hosted-support-assistant.md)). A customer's request reaches the DevOps agents of P1 as a USER feature.

**Where the code lives.** The closed services are outside this repository: they live in separate private repositories, checked in CI so that no AGPL source is copied into them and no closed code reaches a plugin ([D415](../../../06-decisions/technical/d415-supply-chain-controls.md), [repository layout](../../../../technical/architecture/repository-layout.md), [supply chain and licences](../../../../technical/security/supply-chain-and-licences.md)). The `area` of a P11 ticket therefore names the private repository it changes: `hosted/websites`, `hosted/factory` and `hosted/support-assistant`. What a service produces inside a company's SimpleERP (a page, a plugin, a configuration) follows the product's licence ([D377](../../../06-decisions/vision/d377-plugins-agpl-only.md)), and the generated plugins are AGPL-3.0 ([BR-F19-17](../../../02-functional/f19-feature-factory/business-rules/br-f19-17-generated-code-is-agpl-and-stays-usable.md)).

## <a id="risks"></a>🎲 Risks

- **The placement is adopted** ([D483](../../../06-decisions/vision/d483-roadmap-devops-agents-first.md), [D569](../../../06-decisions/vision/d569-roadmap-option-a-in-twelve-phases.md)): a later decision may move one of the three services earlier, for example F20 to bring customers' requests to the DevOps agents sooner.
- **The open-core boundary** needs a lawyer's review before launch ([D388](../../../06-decisions/vision/d388-hosted-edition-open-core.md)); a finding may change how a service reaches the product.
- **The VAT of the promotion credit** is confirmed by a tax adviser before launch ([D390](../../../06-decisions/f19-feature-factory/d390-feature-price-and-promotion-credit.md), [BR-F19-12](../../../02-functional/f19-feature-factory/business-rules/br-f19-12-credit-is-a-credit-note.md), CR-FR-205).
- **Values** confirmed by the owner (D572): the 30 days of site backups ([BR-F18-21](../../../02-functional/f18-hosted-websites-and-domains/business-rules/br-f18-21-sites-backed-up-daily-restored-to-the-preview.md)), 20 business days of delay and 10 of defect ([BR-F19-15](../../../02-functional/f19-feature-factory/business-rules/br-f19-15-a-delivery-date-is-committed-and-kept.md), [BR-F19-16](../../../02-functional/f19-feature-factory/business-rules/br-f19-16-nothing-charged-without-an-accepted-feature.md)), 7 days of maintenance notice ([BR-F20-14](../../../02-functional/f20-hosted-support-assistant/business-rules/br-f20-14-maintenance-announced-ahead.md)), 5 business days for the incident report ([BR-F20-16](../../../02-functional/f20-hosted-support-assistant/business-rules/br-f20-16-written-report-after-a-major-incident.md)), 10 % and 50 % of service credits ([BR-F20-17](../../../02-functional/f20-hosted-support-assistant/business-rules/br-f20-17-service-credits-for-missed-targets.md)); the 24 hours for the NIS2 facts ([BR-F20-19](../../../02-functional/f20-hosted-support-assistant/business-rules/br-f20-19-nis2-facts-on-time.md)) follow the directive's early warning (CR-FR-169); the French deadlines wait for the French NIS2 law, a lawyer review item.
- **Outside APIs:** registrars, DNS providers, certificate authorities, LinkedIn and Indeed change their APIs and terms; the connectors' contract tests and drift checks catch it (T12).
- **Health data:** a namespace holding health data stays out of the assistant's models unless the hosting and the model path are HDS-certified ([BR-F20-13](../../../02-functional/f20-hosted-support-assistant/business-rules/br-f20-13-health-data-out-of-reach.md)); until then an engineer handles those requests.
- **A wording to settle:** the [front end](../../../../technical/architecture/frontend.md) page lists the public website (F18) among the front ends, while the [repository layout](../../../../technical/architecture/repository-layout.md) puts the closed services outside this repository; this page follows the layout and [D415](../../../06-decisions/technical/d415-supply-chain-controls.md).
