# 🇫🇷 Country rules

**Status:** ✅ specified

## First country

France first: electronic invoicing through a certified platform, the FEC accounting export and French VAT. Other
countries come later as modules. See [D8](../../06-decisions/foundations/d08-france-first.md).

## Other countries

Other countries come as country packs: each holds the country's law corpus and the legal rules SimpleERP enforces there
([D292](../../06-decisions/country-packs/d292-one-country-pack-per-country.md)). See
[🌍 Country packs](../../10-country-packs/README.md) and the [🇫🇷 France pack](../../10-country-packs/france/README.md).
