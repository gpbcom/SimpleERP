# ⚖️ Country rule template

Copy this file for a new page of this kind. Keep every section, in this order; write "None" or link a question in
`07-questions/` rather than leave one blank.

## Statement

_The legal rule SimpleERP enforces, in one sentence._

## Values

_The values the capabilities read from the pack (rates, limits, durations, mentions), each with its effective date._

## Legal references

_Each article or official text, its version in force, and its official source link._

## Effective dates

_From when the rule applies, and until when if it is repealed or replaced._

## Enforced by

_The capabilities, business rules, entities and screens that apply it, linked._

## When the law changes

_The articles watched in the law corpus; a change flags the rule and warns the administrators (D294)._

## Penalty

_What a company risks if the rule is broken, with its reference, or "None stated"._

## Exceptions

_Who or what is outside the rule, with its reference._

## Open questions

_Links to open questions, or "None"._
