# 🔀 Flow template

Copy this file for a new page of this kind. Keep every section, in this order; write "None" or link a question in
`07-questions/` rather than leave one blank.

## Summary

_What the flow achieves, from start to end._

## Personas

_Links to the personas who take part._

## Flowchart

_A Mermaid `flowchart` of the whole flow, including the other personas' steps._

## Sequence diagram

_A Mermaid `sequenceDiagram` of the messages between personas and systems._

## Steps

_A table: step, persona, screen (link), use case (link), what happens._

### Entities and screens

_At the end of the Steps section, after the step table: one row per entity the flowchart or a step names. Columns:
entity (linked, harmonised name), its states in this flow (from its Lifecycle), the steps and screens that show it ("1
S-F2-29; 2 S-F2-10", linked), or "Shown elsewhere: <screen>", "Record page, step S7" or "Admin list: <list page>"
(linked), and its aggregated view (the view's name)._

## Errors

_What can fail, and where the flow resumes._

## Images

_A table: step (or E1, E2 for errors), persona, device, light and dark PNG links, as built by the mockup tools (D120)._

## Open questions

_Links to open questions, or "None"._
