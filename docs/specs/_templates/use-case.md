# 📝 Use case template

Copy this file for a new page of this kind. Keep every section, in this order; write "None" or link a question in
`07-questions/` rather than leave one blank.

## Summary

_One or two sentences: who does what, and why._

## Personas

_Links to the personas who act in it, in `01-users/personas/`._

## Goal

_What the persona wants to have achieved at the end._

## Trigger

_What starts it: an action, an event, a date._

## Frequency

_How often it happens, per persona and per company._

## Preconditions

_What must be true before it starts._

## Postconditions

_What is true once it succeeded, and once it failed._

## Main flow

_Numbered steps of the normal path, each naming who acts and what the system does._

## Alternative flows

_Numbered variants, each branching from a step of the main flow._

## Error flows

_What can go wrong at each step, what the user sees, and what is kept._

## Business rules

_Links to the rules in `business-rules/` that apply._

## Entities

_Links to the entities in `data/` it reads, creates, changes or deletes._

## Permissions

_Which roles may do it, and the record rules that limit it._

## Notifications

_What is sent, to whom, when, on which channel._

## Audit events

_What the audit trail records, with which details._

## AI involvement

_What the assistant may do in it, and what it must never do._

## API, MCP and A2A exposure

_The API operations, MCP tools and A2A skills that expose it._

## Screens

_Links to the screens in `screens/` it uses, in order._

## Flowchart

_A Mermaid `flowchart` of the main, alternative and error flows._

## Sequence diagram

_A Mermaid `sequenceDiagram` between the persona, the front end and the services._

## Acceptance criteria

_Numbered Given / When / Then statements, each testable._

## Non-functional notes

_Volumes, response times, accessibility and security points of this use case._

## Regulatory references

_The laws or standards that apply, with their article and source._

## Related use cases

_Links to use cases that come before, after or instead of this one._

## Open questions

_Links to the questions in `07-questions/` that are still open, or "None"._
