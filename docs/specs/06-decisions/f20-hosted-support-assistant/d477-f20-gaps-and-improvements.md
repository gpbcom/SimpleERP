# 📜 D477 F20 Hosted support assistant (hosted edition): gaps and improvements

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The gaps-and-improvements round of 2026-11-12 (D457), for F20 and the sectors that rely on it.

## Options

- **Planned-maintenance notices and upgrade release notes.** 1 and 0 mentions.
- **Written incident report to customers after an outage.** 2 mentions.
- **Service credits when plan targets are missed.** UC-F20-19 sets targets with no consequence.
- **Support language (French, English).** Absent.
- **NIS2 information duties of customers.** Incident facts these customers need within 24 hours (general knowledge).
- **Confirm the waiting proposals.** 34 items, UC-F20-19, BR-F20-11.
- **More flows.** Only 4 flows for 20 use cases.
- **Employee use cases.** U01 has 2.

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12:

- F20 Hosted support assistant: all: All: maintenance notices, release notes, incident reports; service credits,
  languages, NIS2 facts; flows and employee use cases; "Confirm F20's proposals"

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

- The chosen gaps and improvements become use cases, rules, entities, screens and flows of F20.
- F20's waiting proposals are confirmed (D457).
