# 📜 D204 Legal assistant reports

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What reports the legal assistant gives the company.

## Options

- Warnings and law changes.
- Counts only.
- No reports.

## Choice

For HR and the owner: warnings shown and dismissed per kind and site, never per person on sensitive topics; and a
monthly digest of law changes that affect the company's sector, size and collective agreements.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F15-03 still applies.
