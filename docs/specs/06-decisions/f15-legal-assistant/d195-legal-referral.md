# 📜 D195 Notice and referral

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

When the legal assistant refers to a professional.

## Options

- A notice on every answer, and a referral by topic.
- A notice only.
- A referral always.

## Choice

Every answer ends with a short "information, not legal advice" line; disputes, dismissals, discrimination or harassment,
health and safety emergencies, and questions the corpus does not cover get "I don't know" or a referral: a lawyer, the
labour inspectorate with its local office, the staff representatives, the Défenseur des droits.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

See [Legal advice limits](../../05-non-functional/legal-advice-limits.md) (BR-F15-02).
