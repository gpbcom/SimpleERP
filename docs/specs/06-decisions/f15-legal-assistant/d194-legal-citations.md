# 📜 D194 Legal citations

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How legal answers cite the law.

## Options

- Article, version and date, with "as of" a past date.
- Article and link only.
- A sources list at the end.

## Choice

Each statement cites its article, the version in force and its date, linked to the official text; the user can ask "as
of" a past date and gets the version then in force.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

T11 keeps every version of every article with its dates (BR-F15-01).
