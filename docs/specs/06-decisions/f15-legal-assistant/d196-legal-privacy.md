# 📜 D196 Legal questions are private

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who can see what an employee asked.

## Options

- Nobody, HR included.
- As F3's conversations.
- HR can see them.

## Choice

Legal conversations are private to the asker, never visible to managers, HR or administrators unless the asker shares
them; reports show counts only; the audit trail records that a question was asked, not its content; sensitive topics
(harassment, discrimination) are never counted per team.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Stricter than F3 (D185) (BR-F15-03).
