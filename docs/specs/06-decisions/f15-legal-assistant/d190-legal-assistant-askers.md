# 📜 D190 Who asks the legal assistant what

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who uses the legal assistant, and about what.

## Options

- Employees about their own rights.
- Managers and HR about the company's duties.
- The owner about commercial and tax rules.
- Accountants about tax and social rules.

## Choice

All four, and the technical standards of each activity sector simple-ERP can be configured for (the owner: "You will
also add technical normes applicable to each activity sectors configurable in Simple-ERP.").

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

The corpus covers labour, commercial, tax and social law, and sector technical rules and standards (T11); the standards
are researched and documented in the specifications.
