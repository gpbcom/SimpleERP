# 📜 D198 One legal assistant use case

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Proposed: eight use cases (employees' rights, the company's duties, commercial and tax, tax and social, sector
standards, warnings, document checks, set-up).

## Options

- Eight use cases.
- Without document checks.
- Merge the four question use cases.

## Choice

All eight confirmed, merged into one use case, UC-F15-01 Use the legal assistant (the owner: "Confirm all of that. But
merge everything from 01 to 08."); its four flows keep them apart.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Questions, warnings, document checks and set-up are alternative flows of UC-F15-01.
