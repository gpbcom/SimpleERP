# 📜 D472 F15 Legal assistant: gaps and improvements

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The gaps-and-improvements round of 2026-11-12 (D457), for F15 and the sectors that rely on it.

## Options

- **Compliance calendar.** The company's dated legal duties (filings, approval of accounts, registers, mandatory
  trainings, inspections) with reminders; "deadline" gets 1 mention. Serves U13, U05, all sectors.
- **Contract lifecycle.** Tacit-renewal and notice dates appear nowhere in the specs (leases, insurance, suppliers).
  Serves U13, U04, SC10, SC14.
- **Company-law housekeeping.** General meetings, decisions register, beneficial-owner updates; absent in F15 (only SC08
  has assemblies).
- **GDPR and AI Act duties of the company.** DPIA, data-subject requests, inventory of AI uses: 1 to 2 mentions; F1 only
  drafts the processing register.
- **Confidence level and lawyer hand-off.** A package with the question, facts and sources; "confidence" is absent.
- **Confirm the waiting proposals.** Scope, D193, UC-F15-01 to 06 details, BR-F15-06/07/09/10.
- **Screens for UC-F15-05 and UC-F15-06.** Law-pack refresh and standards register have no screen.
- **Catalogue of warning triggers per block.** UC-F15-02 says "an action the ERP sees as a legal risk" without a list.
- **Accountant use case.** A tax question answered with the client's data. Serves U08.

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12:

- F15 Legal assistant: gaps and improvements: All four groups: compliance calendar; contracts and company law; GDPR and
  AI Act duties; confidence and lawyer hand-off, screens, warning triggers, accountant use case
- F15 Legal assistant: proposals: "Confirm all of F15"

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

- The chosen gaps and improvements become use cases, rules, entities, screens and flows of F15.
- F15's waiting proposals are confirmed (D457).
