📜 D197 Language of legal answers

# 📜 D197 Language of legal answers

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The answer's language when the law is in another.

## Options

- The user's language, with the original.
- The law's language only.
- The user's language only.

## Choice

The answer is in the user's language; each quoted article is shown in the original language, with a translation marked
"unofficial translation".

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Only the original is authoritative (BR-F15-04).
