# 📜 D193 Legal warnings on by default

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Whether the legal assistant warns on its own.

## Options

- Warnings on.
- On demand only.
- Warnings to HR only.

## Choice

It warns on its own by default; the warnings can be turned off, and then it answers on demand only (the owner: "If shall
warn on its own by default. This options may be turned down, in this case only on demand.").

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

The administrator turns warnings off for the company, per kind of warning.
