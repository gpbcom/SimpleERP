# 📜 D191 Answers for the real situation

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Whether the legal assistant uses the ERP's data to answer for a person's real situation.

## Options

- Yes, within the asker's rights, listing the facts used.
- General answers only.
- Only when asked each time.

## Choice

Yes: it answers for the real situation (contract, seniority, collective agreement, company size, sector) from the ERP's
data, only what the asker may see, and lists the facts it used; a "general answer only" switch is always offered.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Personal answers need F9's HR data; until F9, answers use what exists (D12).
