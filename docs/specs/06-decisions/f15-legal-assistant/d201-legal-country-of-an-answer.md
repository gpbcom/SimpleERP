# 📜 D201 Whose law applies

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Whose law applies in a company with sites in several countries.

## Options

- The contract's or the site's, switchable.
- The head office's.
- The user chooses each time.

## Choice

For an employee, the law of their contract or place of work; for company questions, the site's country; the answer says
which country and the user can switch.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F15-05.
