# 📜 D482 F25 DevOps agents (hosted edition): gaps and improvements

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The gaps-and-improvements round of 2026-11-12 (D457), for F25 and the sectors that rely on it.

## Options

- **Severity and priority triage with target times.** 2 mentions.
- **Cyber Resilience Act vulnerability handling.** The CRA is cited, but coordinated disclosure and reporting of
  exploited vulnerabilities are not specified (BR-F25-12 only keeps security reports private).
- **Checks on agents' spec pull requests.** Invented facts and contradictions with existing decisions; absent.
- **Confirm the waiting proposals.** 30 items.
- **Deepen the thinnest flow.** fl-f25-02 (290 words).

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12:

- F25 DevOps agents: all: All: triage with target times and CRA vulnerability handling; checks on agents' spec pull
  requests; deeper flow and public votes; "Confirm F25's proposals"

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

- The chosen gaps and improvements become use cases, rules, entities, screens and flows of F25.
- F25's waiting proposals are confirmed (D457).
