# 📜 D383 Every lawful source, public or paid

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How far collection may go, given 'as exhaustive as possible'.

## Options

- All public and paid sources, lawfully.
- Public sources only.

## Choice

Every public source and the paid data providers the customer subscribes to, through T12 connectors; each site's terms
and robots rules respected; no profiling of individual people, no trade secrets, no fake accounts; every fact keeps its
source and date.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F17-02 to BR-F17-05; the rules in the country pack.
