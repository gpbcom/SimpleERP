# 📜 D474 F17 Competitive analysis: gaps and improvements

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The gaps-and-improvements round of 2026-11-12 (D457), for F17 and the sectors that rely on it.

## Options

- **Watch for tenders to answer.** BOAMP/TED opportunities, not only tenders won by others (UC-F17-05); answering lives
  only in SC01 UC-13.
- **Data-provider costs linked to F22 budgets.** Cost and budget are absent in F17.
- **Price-index trend feeding F6 price-list suggestions, with approval.** Elasticity is absent.
- **Confirm the waiting proposals.** 35 items, plus the scope.
- **More flows.** Only 4 flows for 20 use cases.
- **Battle cards fed by F4 call topics.**
- **More reports.** Share of voice and price-index trend (3 KPIs today).

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12:

- F17 Competitive analysis: gaps, improvements, proposals: All: tender watch and data costs (F22); price index to F6
  with approval; more flows, battle cards from F4, reports; "Confirm F17's proposals"

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

- The chosen gaps and improvements become use cases, rules, entities, screens and flows of F17.
- F17's waiting proposals are confirmed (D457).
