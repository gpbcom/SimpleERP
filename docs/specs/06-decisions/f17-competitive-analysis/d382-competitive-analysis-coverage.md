# 📜 D382 Competitive analysis coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner asked for competitive analysis, information about contenders as exhaustive as possible (PLAN D32).

## Options

- A module for the customers' own competitors.
- SimpleERP's own benchmark only.
- Both.

## Choice

A module for the customers' own competitors: a competitor list, profiles from registers and filings, products and
prices, job offers, tenders won, patents and trademarks, reviews, news and social media, funding and legal events,
side-by-side comparison, sourced SWOT and positioning, battle cards, win and loss analysis from lost deals, alerts,
digests, market shares, restricted access, and the AI assistant.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Twenty use cases (D384); every feature reachable from the AI assistant (BR-F3-34).
