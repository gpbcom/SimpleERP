# 📜 D58 Setup steps

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The steps of the guided setup (D54), and their order.

## Options

- The seven steps shown.
- Other steps.

## Choice

1. Company identity: name, legal form, SIREN, VAT number, address, logo.
2. Money: currency, fiscal year start, VAT regime.
3. Sites: head office and other sites (address, hours, stock, manager).
4. Sector pack: construction, retail, services, home care, manufacturing, hospitality, transport, non-profit, or none.
5. Users: invite by email, or turn on single sign-on (D55).
6. Data import: customers, suppliers, products, opening balances.
7. Done: a checklist of what is left, resumable.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

The setup wizard has these seven steps, in this order.
