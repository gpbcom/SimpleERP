# 📜 D74 Former-employee access

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What a leaver keeps access to after deactivation.

## Options

- Their payslips and HR papers.
- Nothing.

## Choice

A read-only access to their own payslips and HR papers (certificates), through an email link, like the portals (D17).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

How long it stays open is [Q12](../../07-questions/q12-payslip-availability-period.md).
