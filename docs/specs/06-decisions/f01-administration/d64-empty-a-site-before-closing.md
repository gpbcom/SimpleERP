# 📜 D64 Empty a site before closing it

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Closing a site that still holds stock or staff.

## Options

- Empty it first.
- Close, then sort out.
- Move all to the head office.

## Choice

Closing is refused until the site's stock is moved and its staff are moved to other sites; simple-ERP lists what is
left.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Nothing is ever left on a closed site.
