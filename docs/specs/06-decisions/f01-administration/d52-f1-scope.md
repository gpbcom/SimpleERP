# 📜 D52 F1 Administration scope

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What F1 Administration covers: the screens an administrator uses, the engines behind them being technical capabilities
(T1, T2).

## Options

- Company and sites setup.
- Users lifecycle.
- Roles and record rules editor.
- Connections and AI settings.

## Choice

All four: company and sites setup, users lifecycle, the roles and record rules editor, and the connections and AI
settings (external MCP servers and A2A agents, the AI provider per feature, single sign-on, email sending).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

F1 is the administrator's single place to set simple-ERP up.
