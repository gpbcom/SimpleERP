# 📜 D66 Site documents and numbering

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What documents issued by a site show, and how they are numbered.

## Options

- Show the site's address.
- One company-wide numbering.
- A numbering series per site.

## Choice

Documents issued from a site show its address and contacts, next to the company's legal identity. The owner ticked both
numbering options: read as the company's choice between one company-wide sequence and one continuous series per site (to
be corrected by the owner if wrong).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

The choice is a company setting; each sequence is continuous with no gap, as French invoicing rules require.
