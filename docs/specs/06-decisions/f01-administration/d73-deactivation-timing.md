# 📜 D73 When a leaver is deactivated

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

When a leaver's account is deactivated.

## Options

- At once or on the last day.
- At once only.

## Choice

At once, or automatically at the end of a chosen last working day (taken from the HR record's end date when the account
is linked to it, F9).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Leavers keep working normally until their last day.
