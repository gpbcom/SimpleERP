# 📜 D67 What an invitation sets

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What the administrator sets when inviting a user.

## Options

- Roles.
- Site and manager.
- Language.
- Link to an HR record.

## Choice

All four: one or more roles, the user's site and manager, their language (which they can change), and the link to their
HR record (F9), so that self-service shows their own data.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

An invited user is ready to work as soon as they accept.
