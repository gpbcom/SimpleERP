# 📜 D93 GDPR register draft

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Whether F1 produces the company's record of processing activities (GDPR Article 30).

## Options

- Yes, a draft to complete.
- No.

## Choice

Yes: SimpleERP drafts the register from what it knows (capabilities used, data kept, retention, connections and AI
providers receiving data); the company completes and exports it.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

A new use case, UC-F1-12.
