# 📜 D59 Every setup step required

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What must be filled before the company can be used.

## Options

- A minimum (name, country, currency), the rest later.
- Every step.

## Choice

Every step must be done before the company can be used. A step is done once answered: choosing no sector pack or nothing
to import completes it (as read from the owner's answer). The setup can still be left and resumed (D54).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Until the seventh step, the company shows only the setup wizard.
