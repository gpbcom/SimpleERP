# 📜 D71 What follows a user to another site

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

When a user moves to another site, what follows them.

## Options

- Their open items.
- Pending approvals re-routed.
- Ask the administrator.

## Choice

Their open items (quotes, tasks, requests) stay theirs and are seen under the new site; requests waiting for their old
site's manager go to the new site's manager.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Nothing waits on a manager who no longer manages the person.
