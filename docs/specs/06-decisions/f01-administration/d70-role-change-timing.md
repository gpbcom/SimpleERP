# 📜 D70 When a role change applies

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

When a change of a user's roles takes effect.

## Options

- At once.
- From a chosen date.
- Both.

## Choice

Both: at once by default, or from a date the administrator chooses, applied automatically.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Dated changes wait in the user's page until their date.
