# 📜 D92 Notification channels

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

On which channels F1 notifies.

## Options

- In-app and email.
- In-app, email and phone push.
- Email only.

## Choice

In-app (the bell of the top bar) and by email; each user can turn emails off per kind of notification.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

No phone push from F1.
