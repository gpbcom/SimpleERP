# 📜 D75 A leaver's personal data

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What happens to a leaver's personal data.

## Options

- Kept per legal durations.
- All kept.
- Deleted at once.

## Choice

Kept per legal durations: business records stay, with the leaver's name in their history; their private assistant
history is deleted at deactivation; meeting recordings where they speak follow the meetings' retention rule (F4).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

The AI assistant history is the only data deleted at once.
