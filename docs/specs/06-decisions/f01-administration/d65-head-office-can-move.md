# 📜 D65 The head office can move

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Whether the head office can move to another site.

## Options

- Yes, with confirmation.
- No.

## Choice

Yes: the administrator picks another site as head office, with a confirmation; the change is dated and kept in the
history.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

The legal address may change with it.
