# 📜 D63 Who changes sites

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who can add or change a site.

## Options

- The administrator adds; a site's manager edits hours.
- The administrator only.
- The administrator and the owner.

## Choice

The administrator adds, changes and closes sites; a site's manager can change their own site's opening hours and
contacts.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Site managers get a narrow right on their own site.
