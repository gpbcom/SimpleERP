# 📜 D347 Professional firms sector coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What the sector pack adds for law, accounting, architecture and engineering firms, and which French rules it enforces.

## Options

- Everything, plus French law.
- Matters, time and fees only.

## Choice

Client intake with identity checks and conflict checks, engagement letters and fee agreements, matters, time and
disbursements, fees (hourly, fixed, success, advances), client funds through trust accounts, deadlines and limitation
periods, documents with versions and e-signature, the client portal, accountants' portfolio and tax calendar, filings,
architects' phases, work in progress, anti-money-laundering, access walls, partners' shares, training hours,
performance; the French rules in the country pack.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Twenty use cases (D349).
