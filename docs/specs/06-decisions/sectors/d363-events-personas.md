# 📜 D363 Events personas

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who produces events and who runs the box office.

## Options

- One new persona, U43.
- Two new personas, U43 and U44.

## Choice

U05 Accounting; U13 Owner.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences
