# 📜 D116 A sectors section

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Where the sectors' specifications live.

## Options

- A new section 08-sectors/.
- Inside each capability.

## Choice

A new section `08-sectors/`, one folder per sector shaped like a capability (use cases, flows, rules, data, screens),
plus its sector pack and its regulatory points; the example companies link to them.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

The sector packs (D2) are specified there.
