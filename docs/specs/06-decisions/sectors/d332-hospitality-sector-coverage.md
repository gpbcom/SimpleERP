# 📜 D332 Hospitality sector coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What the hospitality sector pack adds to F10, and which French rules it enforces.

## Options

- Restaurants and hotels, plus French law.
- Restaurants only.

## Choice

Restaurants: costed recipes and menus, allergens and the home-made label, food-hygiene records, perishables and waste,
tips, staff meals. Hotels: rooms and rates, direct and channel bookings through T12, check-in and check-out,
housekeeping, one folio for room, restaurant and bar, the tourist tax. Meal vouchers and performance. French rules in
the country pack.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Fifteen use cases (D334).
