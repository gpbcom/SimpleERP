# 📜 D341 Health practices sector coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How far simple-ERP goes for health practices: clinical records and health-insurance billing need certified software in
France.

## Options

- Practice management, clinical through connectors.
- Full practice software, certified.
- Admin only.

## Choice

Full practice software, certified: appointments, patient files and insurance, the clinical record, prescriptions and
e-prescriptions, electronic billing to health insurance with the Carte Vitale, third-party payment and returns, patient
billing and fee display, the national health record and secure messaging, results, teleconsultation, schedules and
rooms, cost sharing, consumables and sterilisation, practice accounts, locums, patients' rights, indicators; simple-ERP
goes through the SESAM-Vitale approval and the Ségur listing. Health data on certified hosting only.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Twenty use cases (D343); the approvals become a step of T-capabilities' design and of the release plan.
