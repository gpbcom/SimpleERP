# 📜 D327 Home care personas

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who plans and who visits.

## Options

- Two new personas, U25 and U26.
- One new persona, carers as field workers.

## Choice

Two new personas: U25 Care coordinator and U26 Carer; recipients and families as U09 Customer; U05 Accounting and U13
Owner take part.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Persona pages U25, U26.
