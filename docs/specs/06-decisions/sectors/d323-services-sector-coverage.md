# 📜 D323 Services sector coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What the services sector pack adds to F6, F8 and F9 for consulting firms and agencies, and which French rules it
enforces.

## Options

- Everything, plus French law.
- Time and billing only.

## Choice

Engagements and statements of work, rate cards and pricing models, staffing by skills, resource planning and
utilisation, time and expense approval, billing by time, fixed price, milestones and retainers, work in progress and
revenue recognition, freelancers and subcontractors, budget and margin, the client portal and satisfaction, skills and
certifications, foreign clients; the French rules in the country pack: lawful staff lending, undeclared work and
vigilance, day-based working time, VAT on services abroad.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Fifteen use cases (D325).
