# 📜 D326 Home care sector coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What the home-care sector pack adds, and which French rules it enforces.

## Options

- Everything, plus French law.
- Scheduling and billing only.

## Choice

Care recipients and their funding, needs assessment and care plans, rounds with travel times and optimisation, the
carer's phone with check-in and check-out offline, the care log and incidents, replacements, family access, billing to
recipients and funders, the yearly tax-credit certificate, the immediate advance, travel time and split shifts, quality
and complaints, carers' skills, reports to funders; the French rules in the country pack: personal-services
registration, the tax credit and its certificate, the immediate advance, allowances, health-data hosting, the home-care
collective agreement.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Fifteen use cases (D328).
