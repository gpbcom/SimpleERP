# 📜 D329 Manufacturing sector coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What the manufacturing sector pack adds on top of F12.

## Options

- Industry variants and ISO.
- Only the F12 setup.

## Choice

Ready-made setups for discrete, batch and process production (recipes, potency, yields), make to order with a
configurator, engineer to order, customer-supplied parts, packaging and labelling, and the quality-system records of ISO
9001: controlled documents, internal audits, corrective and preventive actions, supplier quality, complaints,
calibration, staff qualifications, the management review. No new law beyond F12's country rules.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Fifteen use cases (D331).
