# 📜 D362 Events and culture sector coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What the events and culture sector pack adds, and which French rules it enforces.

## Options

- Everything, plus French law.
- Ticketing and events only.

## Choice

Events and budgets, venues and rooms, artists' contracts and riders, intermittent performers' pay inputs, ticketing with
seat maps, access control, the box office, accreditations, staff and volunteer shifts, sponsors, exhibitors, bars and
merchandise, safety files, copyright, the ticket tax, museum memberships, cancellations and refunds, after-event
reports, attendee communication; the rules in the country pack.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Twenty use cases (D364).
