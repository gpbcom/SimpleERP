# 📜 D320 Retail sector coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What the retail sector pack adds to F10 and F11, and which French rules it enforces.

## Options

- Everything, plus French law.
- Multi-store stock only.

## Choice

Several stores and their assortments, size and colour grids and seasons, replenishment and transfers, shelf labels with
unit prices, sales periods and markdowns, store counts, selling and returning across channels, clienteling, store
targets, staff schedules and Sunday openings, unsold goods, store performance; the French rules in the country pack:
price and unit-price display, sales periods, unsold goods, Sunday opening, receipts on request, gift cards.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Fifteen use cases (D322).
