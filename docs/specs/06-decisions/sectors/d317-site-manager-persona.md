# 📜 D317 Site manager persona

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who runs construction sites.

## Options

- A new persona U22 Site manager.
- Existing personas only.

## Choice

A new persona U22 Site manager (conducteur de travaux), on a phone or tablet on site and a laptop at the office; U14
Field worker, U03 Sales, U04 Purchasing, U05 Accounting, U13 Owner and U09 Customer take part.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Persona page U22, its views.
