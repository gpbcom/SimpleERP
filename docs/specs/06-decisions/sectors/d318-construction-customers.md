# 📜 D318 Private and public customers

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Which customers construction companies serve.

## Options

- Private and public.
- Private only in v1.

## Choice

Consumers and companies, with the off-premises and renovation rules for consumers; public buyers, with tenders, invoices
through Chorus Pro and public payment periods.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

UC-SC01-12, UC-SC01-13; BR-SC01-12, BR-SC01-13.
