# 📜 D339 Non-profit personas

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who runs an association's money and who volunteers.

## Options

- Two new personas, U31 and U32.
- One new persona, U31.

## Choice

Two new personas: U31 Treasurer and U32 Volunteer; U13 as the president, U12 for campaigns, U09 as members and donors.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Persona pages U31, U32.
