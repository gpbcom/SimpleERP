# 📜 D344 Real estate sector coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Which real-estate activities the sector pack covers, and which French rules it enforces.

## Options

- All three, plus French law.
- Property management only.

## Choice

Agency sales and lettings (mandates and register, listings through T12, viewings, offers, client funds, fees), property
management (lettings, rents, receipts, indexation, deposits, charges, landlord payments, diagnostics, unpaid rents),
co-ownership management (assemblies, budget and calls, works fund and works, co-owners' accounts, the register), the
portal and the AI assistant; the French rules in the country pack.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Twenty use cases (D346).
