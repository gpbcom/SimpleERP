# 📜 D338 Non-profit sector coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What the association sector pack adds, and which French rules it enforces.

## Options

- Everything, plus French law.
- Members, donations and receipts only.

## Choice

Members and dues, the general assembly and the board, donations online and offline, campaigns, tax receipts, regular
donors, grants and their reports, volunteers and their time, events and ticketing, temporary bars, association accounts
with dedicated funds and contributions in kind, accounts publication, bequests and gifts in kind, communication with
consent, members' data rights, the dashboard; the French rules in the country pack.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Twenty use cases (D340).
