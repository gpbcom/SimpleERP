# 📜 D336 Transport personas

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who plans and who drives.

## Options

- Two new personas, U29 and U30.
- One new persona, drivers as field workers.

## Choice

Two new personas: U29 Dispatcher and U30 Driver; U15 Warehouse, U03 Sales, U05 Accounting, U13 Owner and U09 Customer
take part.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Persona pages U29, U30.
