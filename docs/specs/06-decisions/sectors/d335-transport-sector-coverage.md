# 📜 D335 Transport sector coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What the transport and logistics sector pack adds, and which EU and French rules it enforces.

## Options

- Everything, plus EU and French law.
- Trips and fleet only.

## Choice

Orders and quotes, trip planning and routes, fleet and trailers, drivers and documents, consignment notes and e-CMR, the
driver's phone, proofs of delivery, tachograph data and driving times, dangerous goods, fuel cards and tolls,
maintenance and inspections, chartering, transport billing with surcharges, warehouse logistics for clients and its
billing, the client portal, CO2 information, margins, cabotage and posted drivers; the rules in the country pack.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Twenty use cases (D337).
