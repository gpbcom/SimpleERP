# 📜 D316 Construction sector coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What the construction sector pack adds to F6 to F9, and which French rules it enforces.

## Options

- Everything, plus French law.
- Core site management only.

## Choice

Sites, quotes by lots and unit-price schedules, site planning, time and materials per site offline, the site diary,
progress bills with the retention guarantee, subcontracting, equipment, works declarations and safety documents,
acceptance with reservations, the as-built file and warranties, site margins; the French rules in the country pack:
ten-year insurance, builders' warranties, reverse charge, renovation VAT, subcontractor vigilance and acceptance, the
BTP card, the BTP paid-leave fund, works near networks, public payment periods.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Fifteen use cases (D319).
