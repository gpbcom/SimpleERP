# 📜 D356 IT and software sector coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What the IT and software sector pack adds, and which EU and French rules it enforces.

## Options

- Everything, plus EU and French law.
- Subscriptions and support only.

## Choice

Subscriptions with plans and seats, usage metering, revenue recognition, licences and renewals, support with service
levels (F16), managed-service contracts and customers' assets, IT projects in sprints, release notes, customer health,
renewals and expansion, processor agreements, the software bill of materials and vulnerabilities, incident reporting,
data export and switching, EU VAT on digital services, dunning, the customer portal, SaaS metrics, partners; and, at the
owner's request, following features and products in Jira, GitHub or an equivalent through MCP, an API or T12 connectors.
The rules in the country pack.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Twenty-one use cases (D358).
