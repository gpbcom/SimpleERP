# 📜 D350 Education sector coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What the education and training sector pack adds, and which French rules it enforces.

## Options

- Training providers and private schools, plus French law.
- Training providers only.

## Choice

Catalogue and sessions, enrolments with quotes and agreements, funding by employers, skills operators and the personal
training account, invitations and needs assessments, attendance per half-day, e-learning through T12, assessments and
certificates, satisfaction, billing, trainers, the quality file, the yearly report, complaints; private schools with
enrolment, classes, timetables, grades, attendance and fees; the French rules in the country pack.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Twenty use cases (D352).
