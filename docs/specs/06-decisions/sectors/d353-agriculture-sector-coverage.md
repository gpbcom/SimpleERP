# 📜 D353 Agriculture and food sector coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What the agriculture and food sector pack adds, and which EU and French rules it enforces.

## Options

- Farms and food processors, plus EU and French law.
- Food processors only.

## Choice

Farms: plots and crops, treatments and fertilisation registers, harvests and lots, animal identification and health
register, CAP aid, machinery, seasonal workers, direct sales, cooperatives, organic records. Processors: recipes and
batches, traceability from field to customer, HACCP, labelling, shelf life and recalls. Costs and reports. The rules in
the country pack.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Twenty use cases (D355).
