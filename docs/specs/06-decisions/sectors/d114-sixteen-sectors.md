# 📜 D114 Sixteen sectors

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner asked that every possible company type be interviewed, home care included ("Make sure all the possible company
types are subjects to an interview").

## Options

- The eight example companies' sectors.
- Health practices, real estate, professional firms, education and training.
- Agriculture and food, IT and software, wholesale and distribution, events and culture.

## Choice

Sixteen sectors, each with its own interview, specification folder and sector pack: Construction, Retail, Services, Home
care, Manufacturing, Restaurant and hospitality, Transport and logistics, Non-profit association, Health practices, Real
estate, Professional firms, Education and training, Agriculture and food, IT and software, Wholesale and distribution,
Events and culture.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Each sector gets an example company and a folder in `08-sectors/`.

Extended on 2026-11-14 to twenty sectors by the benchmark-gap interview: SC17 Beauty and wellness
([D561](d561-benchmark-gaps-sc17-beauty-and-wellness.md)), SC18 Sports and fitness
([D562](d562-benchmark-gaps-sc18-sports-and-fitness.md)), SC19 Trades and home services
([D563](d563-benchmark-gaps-sc19-trades-and-home-services.md)) and SC20 Public bodies
([D564](d564-benchmark-gaps-sc20-public-bodies.md)).
