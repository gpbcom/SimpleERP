# 📜 D321 Store manager persona

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who runs a store.

## Options

- A new persona U23 Store manager.
- Existing personas only.

## Choice

A new persona U23 Store manager, on a tablet on the shop floor and a laptop in the back office; U17 Cashier, U15
Warehouse, U12 Marketing, U13 Owner and U09 Customer take part.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Persona page U23, its views.
