# 📜 D359 Wholesale and distribution sector coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What the wholesale sector pack adds to F6 and F7, and which EU and French rules it enforces.

## Options

- Everything, plus EU and French law.
- Price agreements and rebates only.

## Choice

Customer price agreements and tiers, rebates and year-end discounts, the yearly agreement with retailers, EDI orders,
orders on the road offline, back-orders, drop-shipping, wave picking and cross-docking, pallets and GS1 labels, returns
and credit notes, supplier promotions and listing fees, imports and customs, EU trade statistics, sales reps' visits and
targets, margins, credit limits, buying against forecasts, the B2B portal, supplier performance; the rules in the
country pack.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Twenty use cases (D361).
