# 📜 D471 F14 Plugin store: gaps and improvements

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The gaps-and-improvements round of 2026-11-12 (D457), for F14 and the sectors that rely on it.

## Options

- **SBOM and AGPL licence-compatibility checks of plugin dependencies.** Both absent; SC14 has its own SBOM use case.
- **Abandoned plugins.** End of life, deprecation notice and maintainer take-over are absent.
- **Beta channel and staged rollout for publishers.** Absent.
- **Listing and plugin translations.** Absent.
- **Support terms of paid plans.** Response times; "support" gets 7 mentions.
- **Confirm the waiting proposals.** 36 items, plus BR-F14-11, 14 and 15.
- **Deepen UC-F14-04 uninstall.** Export format and check of dependent plugins.
- **Installer-side rollback to the previous version.**
- **More flows.** Only 5; fl-f14-05 has 228 words.

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12:

- F14 Plugin store: gaps and improvements: All four groups: SBOM and licence checks; abandoned plugins, beta channel and
  staged rollout; translations and support terms; uninstall, rollback and flows
- F14 Plugin store: proposals: "Confirm all of F14"

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

- The chosen gaps and improvements become use cases, rules, entities, screens and flows of F14.
- F14's waiting proposals are confirmed (D457).
