# 📜 D368 Plugin store coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner asked that F14 cover everything the benchmark's app stores offer (D30), with EU and French law.

## Options

- Everything, plus EU and French law.
- Install and update only.

## Choice

Everything: browsing, installing with permissions, compatible updates, uninstall with export, paid plugins and
subscriptions, licences and seats, reviews, the publisher portal with automated checks, security review and signing,
releases, prices and plans, payouts and statements, seller reporting, private stores, withdrawals, refunds and disputes,
publisher dashboards, moderation with transparent ranking, the AI assistant; the rules in the country pack.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Twenty use cases (D370); every feature reachable from the AI assistant (BR-F3-34).

Revised by D443 on 2026-11-12: licences and seats are withdrawn: paid plugins sell support, updates and hosting, with no
enforced licence check (BR-F14-05 withdrawn).
