# 📜 D94 F2 No-code configuration scope

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What can be configured without a developer (D2).

## Options

- Fields, statuses and forms.
- New record types.
- Document and email templates.
- Workflows and automations.

## Choice

All four: custom fields, statuses and form layouts on existing records; new record types with their list and form;
document and email templates; workflows and automations.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

F2 is where a company adapts simple-ERP to its field (D2), alongside plugins (T4) and sector packs.
