# 📜 D135 Pack sharing

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who can make and share packs.

## Options

- Export, import, and publish to the store.
- Files only.
- Shipped packs only.

## Choice

Any configurator exports a pack as a file; administrators import one; a pack can be published in the plugin store (F14)
as a sector pack.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Companies and IT providers can share their configurations.
