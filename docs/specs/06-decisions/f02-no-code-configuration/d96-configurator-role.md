# 📜 D96 The Configurator role

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who may configure.

## Options

- The administrator and a Configurator role.
- The administrator only.
- Any manager for their team.

## Choice

The administrator, and users given the ready-made Configurator role, limited to the capabilities their own role can see.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

A new ready-made role, Configurator (see the roles page).
