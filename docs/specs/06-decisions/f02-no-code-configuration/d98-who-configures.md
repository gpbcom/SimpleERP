# 📜 D98 Who holds the Configurator role

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who typically holds the Configurator role (D96).

## Options

- U02 Manager.
- U16 Office worker.
- U13 Owner.
- U12 Marketing.

## Choice

All four: managers for their department, office workers and marketing for document and email templates, and the owner in
small companies.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

These personas act in F2's use cases.
