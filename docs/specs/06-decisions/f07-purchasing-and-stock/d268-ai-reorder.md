# 📜 D268 Reordering

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How reordering works.

## Options

- Suggested from an AI forecast.
- Minimum levels only.
- Manual.

## Choice

automatically.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F7-02; the forecast model is an AI feature setting (F1).
