# 📜 D272 Valuation and counts

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Which valuation and count features.

## Options

- Costing methods.
- Landed costs.
- Cycle counts.
- Stock analytics.

## Choice

All four: standard, average and FIFO per category, perpetual or periodic; landed costs; cycle counts per location, a
yearly count, approvals; aging, dead stock, turnover and ABC analysis.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F7-06, BR-F7-07; UC-F7-07, UC-F7-09, UC-F7-11.
