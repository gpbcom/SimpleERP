# 📜 D270 Replenishment features

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Which replenishment features (the owner asked to choose before adding).

## Options

- Reordering rules.
- Order-driven supply.
- Lead times and forecast.
- The replenishment report.

## Choice

All four: min, max and multiples per item and location, automatic or manual, visibility and horizon days; make-to-order,
dropshipping, cross-docking, resupply between warehouses; supplier, security and purchase lead times, forecasted stock
with stock-out alerts; a replenishment report with vendor choice, blanket orders, order to max and snooze.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

UC-F7-05, UC-F7-13.
