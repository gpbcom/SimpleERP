# 📜 D305 F7 benchmark additions

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner asked that F2 to F14 cover every feature of the benchmark set (PLAN D30); a reading of the benchmark found
these gaps in F7: calls for tenders and supplier rating, trade between group companies, third-party warehouses and
channels, repairs.

## Options

- Add them all.
- Add with changes.

## Choice

Added, as confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-11: UC-F7-14 Run a call for tenders and rate suppliers; UC-F7-15 Trade
between group companies; UC-F7-16 Work with third-party warehouses and channels; UC-F7-17 Repair products. Each is
reachable from the AI assistant within the user's roles (BR-F3-34); a legal value it needs comes from the country pack
(D293).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-11, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

New use cases, rules, entities, screens and the flow Benchmark additions.
