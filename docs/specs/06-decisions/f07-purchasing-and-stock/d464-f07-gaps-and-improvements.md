# 📜 D464 F7 Purchasing and stock: gaps and improvements

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The gaps-and-improvements round of 2026-11-12 (D457), for F7 and the sectors that rely on it.

## Options

- **Supplier contracts and price agreements.** Validity, volume rebates and renewal notice are absent; blanket orders
  exist. Serves SC15 UC-11/17, SC06.
- **Packaging and EPR eco-contributions (AGEC law).** About 1 mention (general knowledge). Serves SC02, SC15, SC13.
- **Dangerous-goods storage rules and safety data sheets.** Absent in F7. Serves SC05, SC07, SC13 (plant-protection
  products).
- **Approved catalogues and punch-out for employee requests.** U01 has 1 use case.
- **Explicit safety stock and min/max per item.** 2 mentions.
- **Purchase-related carbon data (scope 3).** Absent (general knowledge, CSRD value chain).
- **Confirm the waiting proposals.** 39 items: UC-F7-01 to 13 and their entities and screens, plus BR-F7-07.
- **Deepen the thinnest flows.** fl-f07-03 replenish (233 words) and fl-f07-02 receive (234 words).
- **Offline and loading states on scanner screens.** Serves U15.
- **Supplier performance dashboard.** Quality rejects, price variance and lead-time drift (SC15 UC-19); today only
  on-time receipts.

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12:

- F7 Purchasing and stock: gaps: All four groups: supplier contracts; EPR and dangerous goods; catalogues, punch-out and
  min/max; purchase carbon (scope 3)
- F7 Purchasing and stock: improvements: All three: deeper flows; scanner screen states; supplier performance
- F7 Purchasing and stock: proposals: "Confirm all of F7"

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

- The chosen gaps and improvements become use cases, rules, entities, screens and flows of F7.
- F7's waiting proposals are confirmed (D457).
