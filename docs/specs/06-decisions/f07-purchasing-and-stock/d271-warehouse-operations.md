# 📜 D271 Warehouse operations

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Which warehouse features.

## Options

- Routes and steps.
- Putaway and capacity.
- Removal and reservation.
- Picking and barcode.

## Choice

All four: several warehouses, locations, push and pull routes, 1, 2 or 3 steps; putaway rules and storage categories
with capacities; FIFO, LIFO, FEFO, closest, least packages, reservation modes; batch, cluster and wave picking, a
barcode app working offline.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F7-04; UC-F7-03, UC-F7-06.
