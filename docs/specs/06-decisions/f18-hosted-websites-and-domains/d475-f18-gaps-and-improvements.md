# 📜 D475 F18 Hosted websites and domains (hosted edition): gaps and improvements

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The gaps-and-improvements round of 2026-11-12 (D457), for F18 and the sectors that rely on it.

## Options

- **Blog and news section fed by F5 posts.** Absent.
- **Redirects when an existing site is replaced.** 2 mentions in all the specs; needed to keep search ranking.
- **Mailboxes on the domain.** Only mail records exist.
- **Spam protection on forms.** No captcha; spam gets 4 mentions.
- **Sector page templates.** SC06 menu with allergens, SC09 fee display (cr-fr-130), SC10 listings.
- **Confirm the waiting proposals.** 32 items.
- **Search-engine basics.** Sitemap and structured data; SEO gets 1 mention.
- **Site backups and restore use case.** 2 mentions.

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12:

- F18 Hosted websites and domains: all: All: blog, redirects, mailboxes; spam protection and sector templates; SEO
  basics and site backups; "Confirm F18's proposals"

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

- The chosen gaps and improvements become use cases, rules, entities, screens and flows of F18.
- F18's waiting proposals are confirmed (D457).
