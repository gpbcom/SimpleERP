# 📜 D479 F22 Unified platform cost management: gaps and improvements

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The gaps-and-improvements round of 2026-11-12 (D457), for F22 and the sectors that rely on it.

## Options

- **Foreign-currency provider prices.** Providers bill in USD; "currency" gets 1 mention.
- **Self-hosted compute cost.** Hardware and electricity for the local models (D440) are not counted.
- **Savings recommendations.** Local model, caching, cheaper model per feature; 2 mentions.
- **Confirm the waiting proposals.** 33 items.
- **More reports.** Only 3 KPIs.
- **IT provider re-billing beyond one use case.** Serves U11.

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12:

- F22 Unified platform cost management: all: All: foreign-currency prices and self-hosted compute cost; savings
  recommendations; more KPIs and IT-provider re-billing; "Confirm F22's proposals"

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

- The chosen gaps and improvements become use cases, rules, entities, screens and flows of F22.
- F22's waiting proposals are confirmed (D457).
