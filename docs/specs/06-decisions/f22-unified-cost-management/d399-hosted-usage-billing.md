# 📜 D399 Hosted usage: quota, then per unit

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How the hosted edition bills AI, connector and scraping usage.

## Options

- An included quota, then per unit at a published price list.
- At cost plus a stated margin.
- The customer's own keys only.

## Choice

Each hosted plan includes a monthly quota of AI, connector and scraping usage; beyond it, usage is billed per unit at a
published price list excluding VAT, shown live before and after each run; the customer may bring its own provider keys
and pay the provider directly.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F22-09, BR-F22-10; the vision's business model.
