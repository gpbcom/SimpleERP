# 📜 D470 F13 Payroll (plugin): gaps and improvements

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The gaps-and-improvements round of 2026-11-12 (D457), for F13 and the sectors that rely on it.

## Options

- **Mandatory health and provident schemes.** Absent.
- **Apprentices, interns and company officers.** Their specific rules are absent.
- **Special regimes.** Intermittent performers (only in SC16) and farm workers under MSA (SC13). Ximi handles specific
  agreements (D22).
- **Wage garnishment and pay advances.** Garnishment is absent; advances get 4 mentions.
- **Profit-sharing and employee savings.** Participation is mandatory at 50+ staff; absent.
- **Paid-leave pay valuation.** The "maintien versus dixième" comparison is absent.
- **Update benchmark row D22.** It is still ⬜.
- **Confirm the waiting proposals.** 36 items.
- **Split UC-F13-05 per collective-agreement family.** Hotels and restaurants for SC06, home help for SC04, Syntec for
  SC03/SC14, construction for SC01. Today it has 3 steps.
- **Answer Q12 (payslip availability).** It comes from F1.
- **Payroll cost per site, project and analytic axis.**

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12:

- F13 Payroll: gaps and improvements: All four groups: health, provident and special contracts; intermittent performers
  and MSA; garnishment, profit-sharing and leave pay; agreements per family, payroll analytics, benchmark D22
- F13 Payroll: proposals: "Confirm all of F13"

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

- The chosen gaps and improvements become use cases, rules, entities, screens and flows of F13.
- F13's waiting proposals are confirmed (D457).
