# 📜 D478 F21 Onboarding and module activation: gaps and improvements

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The gaps-and-improvements round of 2026-11-12 (D457), for F21 and the sectors that rely on it.

## Options

- **Migration from named tools.** UC-F21-09 has 3 steps, and B11 is still "⬜ details". DualEntry and ERPClaw import from
  QuickBooks, NetSuite, Odoo and ERPNext; French SMEs come from EBP, Sage, Pennylane and Excel.
- **Invite the external accountant during onboarding.** Absent; needed for opening balances and FEC import.
- **Opening-balance check.** Against last year's FEC.
- **Language choice.** Absent.
- **Confirm the waiting proposals.** 33 items.
- **Deepen the thinnest flow.** fl-f21-03 team and data (230 words).
- **Onboarding on a phone for employees.** Serves U01.

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12:

- F21 Onboarding and modules: all: All: migration from named tools and FEC opening check; accountant invitation and
  language; deeper flow and phone onboarding; "Confirm F21's proposals"

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

- The chosen gaps and improvements become use cases, rules, entities, screens and flows of F21.
- F21's waiting proposals are confirmed (D457).
