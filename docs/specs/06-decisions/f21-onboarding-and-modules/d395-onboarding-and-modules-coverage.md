# 📜 D395 Onboarding and one-click modules

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner: SimpleERP must be configurable by a non-technical person if possible; step by step, explained clearly,
interactive; every functional module enabled separately in one click (PLAN D32).

## Options

- A new capability, all guided.
- Extend F1 and F2.

## Choice

A new capability, confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12: F21 owns the first-run path (an interactive step-by-step guide
in plain words with examples, the AI assistant at every step, sector templates, demo data, a checklist to resume) and
the module catalogue, where each functional capability F1 to F21 is switched on or off in one click with its
dependencies explained and its data kept when off. F1's guided set-up (D54 to D62) becomes steps of F21's guide; F1 and
F2 keep their administration screens and link to it.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Twenty use cases (D396); every feature reachable from the AI assistant (BR-F3-34).
