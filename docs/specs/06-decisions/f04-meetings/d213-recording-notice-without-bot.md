# 📜 D213 Recording notice without a bot

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How people are told about a recording in person and on the phone.

## Options

- A notice, and start and stop.
- The organiser's duty only.
- An explicit yes from all.

## Choice

In person: the app shows "Recording" and the organiser confirms everyone was told before it starts. On the phone: a
short spoken message opens the call ("This call is recorded and transcribed") and the other party can ask to stop.
Anyone can stop a recording.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Extends D23 to every source (BR-F4-01).
