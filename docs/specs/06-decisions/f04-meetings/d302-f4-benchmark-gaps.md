# 📜 D302 F4 benchmark additions

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner asked that F2 to F14 cover every feature of the benchmark set (PLAN D30); a reading of the benchmark found
these gaps in F4: team chat, appointments booked by customers and incoming call routing.

## Options

- Add them all.
- Add with changes.

## Choice

Added, as confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-11: UC-F4-17 Chat with colleagues; UC-F4-18 Let customers book appointments;
UC-F4-19 Route incoming calls. Each is reachable from the AI assistant within the user's roles (BR-F3-34); a legal value
it needs comes from the country pack (D293).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-11, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

New use cases, rules, entities, screens and the flow Benchmark additions.
