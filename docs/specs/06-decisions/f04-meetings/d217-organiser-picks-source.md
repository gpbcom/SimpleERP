# 📜 D217 Platform transcript or bot

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Whether a platform's own transcript is used instead of the bot.

## Options

- The platform first.
- The bot always.
- The user chooses.

## Choice

The organiser chooses per meeting: the platform's own transcript, imported after the meeting, or the bot.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F4-07.
