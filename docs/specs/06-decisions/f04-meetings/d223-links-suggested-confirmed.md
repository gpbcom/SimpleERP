# 📜 D223 Linking meetings and calls

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How recordings are linked to records.

## Options

- Suggested, confirmed at review.
- Automatic, no review.
- By hand.

## Choice

Calls are matched to contacts by phone number, in-person meetings by the calendar event or the user's current task; the
link is suggested, and confirmed at review (UC-F4-05).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F4-12.
