# 📜 D227 Reusing past meetings

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What past meetings feed.

## Options

- The AI assistant and the next agenda.
- Search only.
- The AI assistant only.

## Choice

The AI assistant answers from transcripts the user may see; for recurring meetings, the next agenda is drafted from open
actions and points.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F4-16; transcripts indexed by T8 with BR-F4-04's access.
