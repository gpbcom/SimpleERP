# 📜 D461 F4 Meetings: gaps and improvements

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The gaps-and-improvements round of 2026-11-12 (D457), for F4 and the sectors that rely on it.

## Options

- **Topic and trend analysis across calls.** Objections, competitor mentions and feature requests over many calls
  (absent). Would feed F17 win/loss and F25. Serves U03, U13, SC14.
- **Live captions and translation.** Only summaries are translated (BR-F4-05). Needed for accessibility and foreign
  customers.
- **External participants' objection and erasure rights.** "Withdraw/object" appears twice; GDPR rights of outside
  speakers.
- **Transcription accuracy KPI per language and model.** "Accuracy" appears once.
- **Room and equipment booking.** SC09, SC12 and SC16 each re-specify it; F4 only books customer appointments
  (UC-F4-18).
- **Client video meetings for SC09 and SC11.** Video hosting is out of scope; decide the link to F24 UC-F24-07.
- **Confirm the waiting proposals.** BR-F4-01, UC-F4-01/02/05/06/08 and the report KPIs are still proposals.
- **Deepen the thinnest flows.** fl-f04-05 HR meeting (250 words) and fl-f04-09 scoring (260 words).
- **Customer-side use case.** The customer receives the agreed actions after a meeting.
- **Two-way sync of meeting actions.** Keep actions in step with F9 task status.

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12:

- F4 Meetings: gaps and improvements: All four groups: call topics and live captions; outside speakers' rights and
  accuracy per language; rooms (shared booking) and client video; customer side, action sync with F9, deeper HR and
  scoring flows
- F4 Meetings: proposals: "Confirm all of F4"

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

- The chosen gaps and improvements become use cases, rules, entities, screens and flows of F4.
- F4's waiting proposals are confirmed (D457).
