# 📜 D214 Speaker names

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How speakers are named in transcripts.

## Options

- From the platform, and labels renamed by the organiser.
- Voice recognition.
- Unnamed.

## Choice

Online: names from the platform. In person and on the phone: "Speaker 1, 2...", renamed by the organiser during review.
No voice prints.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

No biometric data (GDPR article 9) (BR-F4-02).
