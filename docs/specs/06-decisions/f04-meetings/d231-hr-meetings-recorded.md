# 📜 D231 HR meetings are recorded

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How HR meetings (interviews, annual reviews) are recorded.

## Options

- Off, the employee says yes.
- Like other meetings.
- Never.

## Choice

Like other meetings: recorded by default from the calendar, announced (BR-F4-01), visible to HR and the employee only.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F4-18.
