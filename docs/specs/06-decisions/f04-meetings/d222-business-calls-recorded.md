# 📜 D222 Which calls are recorded

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Which calls are recorded.

## Options

- A default per user.
- All business calls.
- Started by hand.

## Choice

All business calls: every external call through the phone system is recorded, announced by the opening message
(BR-F4-01).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F4-11.

Revised by D449 on 2026-11-12: call recording is off by default, switched on per purpose by an administrator after the
staff committee is consulted, people told (BR-F4-11).
