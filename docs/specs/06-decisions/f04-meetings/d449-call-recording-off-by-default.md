# 📜 D449 Call recording off by default; call scoring high-risk

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

R12: call recording by default (BR-F4-11) and call scoring (UC-F4-12) not treated as worker evaluation, a high-risk AI
use (AI Act Annex III point 4); the staff committee's consultation and the CNIL's proportionality missing.

## Options

- Off by default, on per purpose.
- On by default (D222).

## Choice

Call recording is off by default; an administrator switches it on per purpose (training, proof of an order, quality),
with the staff committee (CSE) consulted beforehand and people told, employees and callers. Call scoring is worker
evaluation and is treated as high-risk AI. The country pack gets rules for the CSE's consultation and the CNIL's
proportionality.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12 at 14:57, round 3 of the decisions on the independent review (R12, PROGRESS.md, step 21).

## Consequences

Revises D222 and extends D234 to call scoring. BR-F4-11, UC-F4-04, UC-F4-12, AI governance and Meeting recording say so;
CR-FR-206 and CR-FR-207 are written, their values until read.
