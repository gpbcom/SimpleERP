# 📜 D211 Who sees a meeting

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who sees a meeting's transcript and summary.

## Options

- Participants, and the linked record's readers for the summary.
- Participants only.
- As the linked record.

## Choice

Participants see the transcript and summary; those who may see the linked record see the summary only; HR meetings
(interviews, reviews) are visible to HR and the person concerned only.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Transcripts never travel beyond the room.
