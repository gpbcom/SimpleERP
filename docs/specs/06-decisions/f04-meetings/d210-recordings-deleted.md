# 📜 D210 Recordings deleted after the transcript

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What happens to audio and video once transcribed.

## Options

- Deleted once the transcript is checked.
- Kept with the meeting.
- Never stored.

## Choice

Audio and video are deleted once the transcript is checked, 7 days by default; an administrator can choose a longer
period. The transcript and summary stay.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Less personal data kept (GDPR data minimisation).
