# 📜 D212 F4 use cases

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The first use cases of F4.

## Options

- The eight proposed.
- Merge the four recording ones.
- Without HR meetings.

## Choice

Confirmed: UC-F4-01 Record an online meeting with the bot; UC-F4-02 Import a platform's own transcript; UC-F4-03 Record
an in-person meeting on the phone or a computer; UC-F4-04 Record a phone call; UC-F4-05 Review the transcript and
summary, confirm decisions and actions; UC-F4-06 Find and reuse past meetings; UC-F4-07 HR meeting with restricted
access; UC-F4-08 Set up meeting sources and retention.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Each is written in its own round.
