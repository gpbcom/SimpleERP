# 📜 D215 Meeting languages

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How languages are handled.

## Options

- Detected, and translated on demand.
- The organiser chooses.
- The company's language only.

## Choice

The language is detected, mixed-language meetings included; the summary is in the organiser's language, and any reader
can translate it into theirs.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F4-05.
