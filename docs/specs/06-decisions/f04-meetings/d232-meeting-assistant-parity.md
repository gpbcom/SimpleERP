# 📜 D232 Meeting assistant parity

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner asked that the meeting assistant cover everything a market-leading AI meeting assistant does (its website
given on
2026-11-10).

## Options

- Cover every capability found.
- Cover a selection.

## Choice

Every capability of the benchmarked meeting assistant found on 2026-11-10 is covered by F4 (or F3, F6, F9 where it
belongs), as
listed on the benchmark page; four conflicts were decided separately (D233 to D236).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

UC-F4-09 to UC-F4-16; the benchmark page maps each capability to where simple-ERP covers it.
