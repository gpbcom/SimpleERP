# 📜 D234 Scoring calls and candidates

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

the benchmarked product scores sales calls and candidates.

## Options

- Calls scored, candidates pre-filled only.
- Everything, like the benchmarked product.
- No scoring.

## Choice

Everything: sales call scorecards (criteria out of 5, a total out of 100), talk-time per speaker, candidate evaluation
grids with scores, and a candidate match score against the job's criteria.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Candidate scoring is a high-risk AI system under the EU AI Act (Annex III, employment), with obligations for simple-ERP
as its provider and the company as deployer (BR-F4-21, [AI governance](../../05-non-functional/ai-governance.md)).

Revised by D449 on 2026-11-12: call scoring (UC-F4-12) is worker evaluation, high-risk like candidate scoring: the same
duties apply.
