# 📜 D224 Summary templates per meeting kind

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The shape of a meeting summary.

## Options

- Templates per kind.
- One fixed shape.
- Free text.

## Choice

A default shape (summary, decisions, actions, open points), and summary templates per meeting kind (site meeting
minutes, sales call, team meeting) that configurators adapt (F2).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F4-13.
