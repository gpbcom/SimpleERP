# 📜 D216 The bot joins by default

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How the bot is brought into a meeting.

## Options

- A switch per meeting, or a pasted link.
- A pasted link only.
- Every calendar meeting by default.

## Choice

Every meeting of a connected calendar is recorded by default; it can be switched off per meeting.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

The recording notice (BR-F4-01) matters even more (BR-F4-06).
