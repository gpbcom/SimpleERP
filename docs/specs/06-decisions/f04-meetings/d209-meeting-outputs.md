# 📜 D209 What a meeting produces

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What SimpleERP produces from a meeting.

## Options

- Transcript and summary.
- Decisions and actions.
- Links to records.
- A follow-up draft.

## Choice

All four: a transcript with speakers and times and a summary; decisions and action items, each action offered as a task
through the confirmation card (UC-F3-03); links to the customer, project, deal or employee concerned; a follow-up email
drafted in the company style, sent by the user (BR-F3-12).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Meetings feed the AI assistant and RAG (D139, T8).
