# 📜 D218 Who starts a recording

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who may start a recording.

## Options

- The organiser or a delegate.
- Any internal participant.
- The organiser only.

## Choice

Any internal participant; anyone can stop it (BR-F4-01).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F4-08.
