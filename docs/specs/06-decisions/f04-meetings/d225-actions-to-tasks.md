# 📜 D225 Action items

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What becomes of action items.

## Options

- Colleagues' as tasks, outsiders' in the follow-up.
- All as tasks.
- Listed only.

## Choice

Actions for colleagues are offered as tasks (owner, due date) through the confirmation card (UC-F3-03); actions for
outside people are listed in the follow-up email draft.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F4-14.
