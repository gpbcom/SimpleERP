# 📜 D226 Meetings never reviewed

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What happens if the organiser never reviews.

## Options

- Remind, then publish as not checked.
- Stay private.
- Published at once.

## Choice

A reminder after 1 working day; after 3, the summary is published to participants marked "not checked"; links stay
suggestions and actions are not turned into tasks.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F4-15.
