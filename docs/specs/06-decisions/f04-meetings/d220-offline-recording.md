# 📜 D220 Offline recording

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

In-person recording without a network.

## Options

- Record, upload later.
- Online only.
- Transcribe on the phone.

## Choice

The phone app records offline, keeps the audio encrypted on the phone, uploads it when the network is back, then removes
it from the phone; transcription happens on the server.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F4-10.
