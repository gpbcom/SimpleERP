# 📜 D170 Outside results are marked

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How outside results are shown, and whether they can be saved.

## Options

- Marked, saved through confirmation.
- Marked, read only.
- Merged with ERP data.

## Choice

Results carry the tool's name and an "outside source" badge, kept apart from ERP data; saving them into records goes
through the confirmation card (UC-F3-03).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Users always know what comes from outside (BR-F3-18).
