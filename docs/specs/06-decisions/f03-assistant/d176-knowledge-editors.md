# 📜 D176 Knowledge editors and proposals

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who adds and changes knowledge.

## Options

- Editors, and anyone proposes.
- Managers only.
- Editors, no proposals.

## Choice

HR, administrators and the owner manage the knowledge base and can name knowledge editors per folder; any user can
propose an item or a fix, which an editor approves.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Knowledge grows from the people who use it (BR-F3-23).
