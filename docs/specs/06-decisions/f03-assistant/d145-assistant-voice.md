# 📜 D145 Voice in and out

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Whether users can talk to the AI assistant by voice.

## Options

- Speech to text.
- Voice in and out.
- Text only.

## Choice

Both: a microphone button turns speech into the question, and answers can be read aloud for hands-free use.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Speech-to-text reuses the meetings' setting; reading aloud is a seventh AI feature setting, "Assistant
voice", local by default (D5).
