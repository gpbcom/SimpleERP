# 📜 D164 Texts drafted with the AI assistant are traced

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Whether texts drafted with the AI assistant are marked.

## Options

- In the history, a visible mention optional.
- Always a visible mention.
- Never.

## Choice

The record history notes "drafted with the assistant"; a visible "written with AI help" mention for recipients is a
company setting, off by default, and on for content published to social media (F5).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Transparency where the EU AI Act asks for it (BR-F3-13).
