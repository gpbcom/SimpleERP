# 📜 D141 Shareable conversations

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What happens to a user's conversations.

## Options

- Private, kept until deleted.
- Private, kept 90 days.
- Shareable.

## Choice

Private by default; the user can share a conversation with colleagues by link.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

What a shared conversation shows to someone with fewer rights is a question of the next round.
