# 📜 D177 Shared conversations continue as a copy

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What a colleague can do with a conversation shared with them.

## Options

- Read, and continue as their own copy.
- Read only.
- Continue together.

## Choice

They read it as the author saw it (D144); "Continue" makes their own copy, where new answers use only their own rights;
they never change the author's conversation.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Sharing never lends the author's rights to new questions (BR-F3-25).
