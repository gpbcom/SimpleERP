# 📜 D142 F3 use cases and personas

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who uses the AI assistant, and its first use cases.

## Options

- The 9 use cases proposed, for every internal persona.
- Changes.

## Choice

Confirmed: every internal persona (U01 to U07, U12 to U16) uses it, through 9 use cases: ask about the company's data,
ask about what is on screen, have it create or change records, summarise and draft, find and get guided, use an outside
tool, manage the company knowledge base (HR, administrator, owner), manage and share conversations, use it on a phone.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Each use case gets its own file.
