# 📜 D183 Ask about a photo

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Whether the user can take a photo and ask about it.

## Options

- Yes, read it and act.
- Attach only.
- No photos.

## Choice

A photo or scan of a delivery note, a meter, a damaged part or a business card is read by the AI assistant, local model
by default, which offers to fill records through the confirmation card.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

A vision-capable model is needed (T7) (BR-F3-30).
