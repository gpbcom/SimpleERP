# 📜 D178 Share with chosen colleagues

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who a conversation can be shared with.

## Options

- Chosen colleagues or a team.
- Any colleague with the link.
- Colleagues and outside people.

## Choice

Named colleagues of the same company, or a team; the link opens only for them, signed in; the author can stop sharing at
any time.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Refines D141's "by link": the link is personal (BR-F3-26).
