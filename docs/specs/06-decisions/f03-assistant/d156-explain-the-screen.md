# 📜 D156 The AI assistant explains the screen

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Whether the AI assistant explains fields, refused saves and unavailable actions.

## Options

- All of it.
- Fields and errors only.
- No.

## Choice

It explains a field's meaning, the rule that refused a save, and why an action is unavailable (a missing right, a
status), and says who can help, without revealing hidden data.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Fewer support requests; explanations follow BR-F3-06.
