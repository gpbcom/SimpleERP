# 📜 D161 What the AI assistant summarises and drafts

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What the AI assistant summarises and drafts.

## Options

- All, from templates.
- Summaries and emails.
- Summaries only.

## Choice

Summaries of a record's history, a document, a thread or a period; drafts of emails, quote and invoice texts, letters,
reports and minutes, using the company's document templates (F2) when one fits.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Templates of F2 are the drafting base.
