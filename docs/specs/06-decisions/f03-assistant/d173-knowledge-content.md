# 📜 D173 Knowledge base content

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What can be put into the company knowledge base.

## Options

- Files, pages and synced folders.
- Files and pages.
- Files only.

## Choice

Uploaded files (PDF, Word, spreadsheets, slides), pages written in the ERP, and folders synced from a shared drive or a
website section, re-read when they change.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

T8 indexes them, deletes included (BR-F3-24).
