# 📜 D171 Personal connections

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Whether users connect their own accounts.

## Options

- Company and personal, allowed by the administrator.
- Company only.
- Personal, free.

## Choice

Company connections set by the administrator (UC-F1-07), plus personal connections a user signs into themselves, for the
services the administrator allows.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

A "My connections" screen for each user (BR-F3-19).
