# 📜 D162 Company writing style

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How the AI assistant matches the company's way of writing.

## Options

- A company style and each user's signature.
- Each user's own style.
- None.

## Choice

An administrator sets a company writing style: tone, formal or informal address, words to use or avoid, sample texts;
each user has a signature; drafts are in the recipient's language when known, otherwise the user's.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

One voice for the company (BR-F3-11).
