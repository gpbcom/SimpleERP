# 📜 D138 F3 Assistant scope

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What the AI assistant can do for a user.

## Options

- Answer from the ERP's data.
- Act for the user.
- Summarise and draft.
- Find and guide.

## Choice

All four: answer questions on the records and documents the user may see, with sources; create or change records on the
user's behalf, within their rights; summarise and draft; find records and screens and explain how to do things.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

The AI assistant is a way to use every capability, under the same permissions (D12).
