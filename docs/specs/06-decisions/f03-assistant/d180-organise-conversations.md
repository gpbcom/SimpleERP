# 📜 D180 Organising conversations

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How users organise their conversations.

## Options

- Search, rename, pin.
- The same plus folders and tags.
- A list only.

## Choice

A history with full-text search, rename, pin, delete, export as a document, plus folders and tags (the owner: "Plus
folders").

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

A conversation folder entity (E-F3-12).
