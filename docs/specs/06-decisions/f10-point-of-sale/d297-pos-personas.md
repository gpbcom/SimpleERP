# 📜 D297 Point of sale personas

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

None of the 16 personas works a till, a floor or a kitchen.

## Options

- Three personas.
- One shop and floor persona.
- Reuse U01 Employee.

## Choice

Three new personas: U17 Cashier, U18 Waiter, U19 Kitchen staff, each with its app shell, home screen and journeys, and
the ready-made roles Cashier, Waiter and Kitchen.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Personas U17 to U19; roles page.
