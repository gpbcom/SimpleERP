# 📜 D295 Point of sale coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner asked that F10 cover everything the benchmark's point of sale offers (D30).

## Options

- Everything the benchmark offers.
- Shop checkout only.
- Feature by feature.

## Choice

Everything found by the benchmark reading (30 pages): sessions and cash control, selling and baskets, products,
variants, combos and weights, pricelists, discounts, promotions, loyalty, gift cards and e-wallet, customers and
invoices, payments and terminals, hardware, offline selling, restaurant floors, kitchen screens, bill splitting,
bookings, QR self-order and kiosks, mobile selling, staff login and rights, stock and accounting posting, reports,
several shops; selling modes: shop counter, restaurant and bar, self-order and kiosk, mobile and field selling; devices:
tablets and PCs offline, receipt and kitchen printers, cash drawer, scanner, customer display, card terminals, scales.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Twenty use cases (D299); every feature reachable from the AI assistant (BR-F3-34).
