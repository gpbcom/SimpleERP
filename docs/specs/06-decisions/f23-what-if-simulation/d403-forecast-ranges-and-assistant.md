# 📜 D403 Baseline, ranges, and the AI assistant

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How it forecasts, how it treats uncertainty, and how far the AI assistant goes.

## Options

- Baseline, scenarios and ranges; the AI assistant builds, runs and explains.
- Deterministic only; the AI assistant explains only.

## Choice

A baseline forecast from history and plans; scenarios as changes over a chosen horizon; uncertain inputs as ranges with
best, likely and worst cases and a probability range; sensitivity; side-by-side comparison. In plain words the AI
assistant
builds a scenario, runs it, explains the impact with figures and sources, proposes and compares counter-measures, and
warns when a real change hits the forecast. Nothing changes the live company until a person applies an action.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F23-07 to BR-F23-12.
