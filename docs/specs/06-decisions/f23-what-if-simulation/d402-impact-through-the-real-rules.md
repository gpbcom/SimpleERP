# 📜 D402 Impact through the ERP's own rules

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What impact a simulation shows.

## Options

- Every area, through the real models.
- Financial statements only.

## Choice

The scenario runs on a copy of the company's data through the same rules the ERP uses: sales and margin, cash and
treasury, profit and loss and balance sheet, stock and purchases, production and capacity, staffing, payroll and
planning, projects, taxes and legal thresholds crossed, platform costs; shown against the current forecast, with what
changed, why, and the risks.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F23-03 to BR-F23-06.
