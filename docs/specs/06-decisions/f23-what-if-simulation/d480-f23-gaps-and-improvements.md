# 📜 D480 F23 What-if simulation and impact: gaps and improvements

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The gaps-and-improvements round of 2026-11-12 (D457), for F23 and the sectors that rely on it.

## Options

- **Goal seeking and break-even.** "What must change to reach X"; absent.
- **Financing scenarios.** Loans, leasing, covenants; 1 to 3 mentions.
- **Forecast accuracy per driver, with recalibration.** Only a "baseline against what happened" KPI exists.
- **Overlap with F8 UC-F8-19 (plan and forecast).** To be resolved.
- **Confirm the waiting proposals.** 30 items.
- **Sector drivers.** Occupancy for SC06, utilisation for SC03, fill rate for SC12.
- **One use case per thin persona.** For the five personas that appear once.

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12:

- F23 What-if simulation: all: All: goal seeking, break-even and financing; forecast accuracy and the F8 overlap
  resolved; sector drivers and one use case per thin persona; "Confirm F23's proposals"

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

- The chosen gaps and improvements become use cases, rules, entities, screens and flows of F23.
- F23's waiting proposals are confirmed (D457).
