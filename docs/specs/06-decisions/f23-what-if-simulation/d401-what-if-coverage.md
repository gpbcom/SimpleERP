# 📜 D401 What-if simulation coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner asked for a capability answering "what happens if one or several of those elements change?", forecast or
simulated, with the impact on the rest of the company, usable from the AI assistant (PLAN D36).

## Options

- Any element of the company.
- Financial elements only.
- Platform costs only.

## Choice

Any element of the company, confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12: prices and volumes, customers and suppliers, costs
(purchases, energy, the platform costs of F22), staff (hiring, leaving, absence, pay), capacity (machines, sites,
vehicles), payment terms, interest and exchange rates, taxes and legal values of the country pack, competitors' moves
(F17); several changes combine in one scenario.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Twenty use cases (D404); every feature reachable from the AI assistant (BR-F3-34).
