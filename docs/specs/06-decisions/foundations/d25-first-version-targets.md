# 📜 D25 First-version targets

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Which figures the first version must reach on one install.

## Options

- 250 users and a million documents.
- 1,000 users and ten million documents.
- 50 users and 100,000 documents.

## Choice

First-version targets per company: 250 users at once, a million documents, 95 % of screens within 1 s (answers Q9).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.
