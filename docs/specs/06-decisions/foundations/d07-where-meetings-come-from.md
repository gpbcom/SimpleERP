# 📜 D7 Where meetings come from

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and Zoom meetings get into the ERP.

## Options

- Import the platforms' transcripts.
- A bot joins the meeting.
- Upload a recording.
- Record from the browser.

## Choice

Meetings come from the platforms' own transcripts and from a self-hosted bot.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.
