# 📜 D23 Meeting bot consent

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How the meeting bot handles consent to recording (GDPR).

## Options

- Announced, and refusable.
- Explicit consent first.
- Internal meetings only.

## Choice

The meeting bot is announced, and any participant can make it stop or leave (answers Q3).
