# 📜 D11 Users outside the company

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who outside the client company gets access.

## Options

- The external accountant.
- Customers through a portal.
- Suppliers through a portal.
- IT provider administrators.

## Choice

Outside users: the external accountant, customers and suppliers through portals, IT provider administrators.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.
