# 📜 D16 The whole platform first

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How much platform is built before the first business area.

## Options

- A minimal platform that grows per area.
- The full platform first.
- The bare minimum.

## Choice

The whole platform (P1 to P10) is built before the first business area.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

See the related pages.
