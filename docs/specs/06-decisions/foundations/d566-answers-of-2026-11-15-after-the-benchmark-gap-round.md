# 📜 D566 Answers of 2026-11-15 after the benchmark-gap round

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Three points the benchmark-gap round (GH-2 step 36n) left to the owner: two pairs of close entities, and the example
prices of F19.

## Options

- SC20: keep E-SC20-10 Deliberation and E-SC20-12 Council decision, or merge them.
- F16: keep E-F16-17 Canned reply next to E-F16-07 Macro, or merge it into Macro.
- F19: prices in the catalogue data, keep the example figures, or other figures.

## Choice

- SC20: "Keep both (Recommended)": a deliberation can be adopted and not yet enforceable; the council decision carries
  the transmission and publication dates.
- F16: "Merge into Macro (Recommended)": a canned reply is a macro whose action is to send a text; its typed variables
  and the block when one is missing move into E-F16-07.
- F19: "Prices in the catalogue data": no price in the specification; each price lives in the hosted edition's catalogue
  data, set by the maintainer; figures shown are examples.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-15, after the benchmark-gap round.

## Consequences

E-F16-17 becomes a redirect to E-F16-07 (merge round of 2026-11-15); the F19 catalogue and maintenance-plan pages state
where prices come from.
