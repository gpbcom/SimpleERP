# 📜 D565 New personas U49 to U56

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Context

The 2026-11-14 benchmark-gaps interview added gaps for audit firms, shareholders, partners and three new sectors (beauty
and wellness, sports and fitness, public bodies) whose users are not in the list of personas U01 to U48
(interview record (the owner's interview of 2026-11-14 on the benchmark gaps, not kept in the repository), section "New personas").

## Options

- Add eight personas for them.
- Reuse an existing persona (a treasurer, an employee) and widen its role.
- Leave the new users without a persona and write their screens for an administrator.

## Choice

The owner chose "Proposed, confirm in batch (Recommended)" and confirmed the eight personas on 2026-11-14 at 15:32 ("Go
on both."). U31 Treasurer is reused for loans and cash.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-14, in the benchmark-gaps interview
(interview record (the owner's interview of 2026-11-14 on the benchmark gaps, not kept in the repository), section "Block by block").

## Consequences

- Eight persona pages, U49 to U56, written in the persona template.
- Their default roles are proposed in each page and are added to the roles page by the main session.
- Their views, workspaces and journeys are written in the views step.
- Their images follow the persona images check when the views exist.
