# 📜 D21 Languages

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Which languages the screens and documents support.

## Options

- French and English, open to more.
- French only.
- Many from launch.

## Choice

French and English at launch, every text in translation files, documents in the customer's language.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.
