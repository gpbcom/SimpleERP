# 📜 D15 Point of sale, shop, manufacturing and payroll as plugins

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

When point of sale, online shop, manufacturing and payroll are built within the first version.

## Options

- As plugins, after the core.
- Next to the area they extend.
- All at the very end, in the core.

## Choice

Point of sale, online shop, manufacturing and payroll are built last, as plugins on the public API (answers Q1).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.
