# 📜 D499 The work is carried out as GH-2 steps 36c to 36l

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who carries out the wording, the aggregated views and the state screens.

## Options

- New steps of GH-2.
- New tickets under feature #2.
- This session, after GH-2 pauses.

## Choice

The plan becomes steps 36c to 36l of GH-2, run by GH-2's own session before its final acceptance (step 36), with at most
4 agents in parallel; no step starts before the owner's go.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the interview on aggregated views, wording and state screens (interview
record (the owner's interview notes of 2026-11 for the specifications, not kept in the repository), answer 16, and follow-up answer 19).

## Consequences

- Steps S0 to S9 of the plan (the owner's interview notes of 2026-11 for the specifications, not kept in the repository) map to 36c to 36l in GH-2's
  plan (the owner's interview notes of 2026-11 for the specifications, not kept in the repository).
- Each step follows GH-2's rules: its checks print nothing, it is committed with its evidence, and pushes and squashes
  need the owner's yes.

## Related

- Plan: one wording, aggregated views, and a screen for every entity
  state (the owner's interview notes of 2026-11 for the specifications, not kept in the repository)
- GH-2 plan (the owner's interview notes of 2026-11 for the specifications, not kept in the repository)
- Interview record (the owner's interview notes of 2026-11 for the specifications, not kept in the repository)
