# 📜 D17 Portal sign-in

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How customers and suppliers sign in to their portals.

## Options

- An email link plus an optional account.
- An account only.
- An email link only.

## Choice

Portals open by a secure link per document, with an optional account for the whole history (answers Q7).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.
