# 📜 D312 Manufacturing coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner asked that F12 cover everything the benchmark's manufacturing apps offer (D30), with the EU and French law.

## Options

- Everything, plus EU and French law.
- Core manufacturing only.

## Choice

Everything: bills of materials, kits, by-products and variants, work centres and routings, the master plan and material
requirements, capacity, manufacturing and work orders, the shop-floor tablet, lots and serials, quality checks and
non-conformities, maintenance, subcontracting, engineering changes (PLM), unbuild and repair, production costs, OEE; the
EU and French rules on machinery, chemicals in articles, work equipment checks, the risk assessment, waste and
classified installations in the country pack.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Twenty use cases (D315); every feature reachable from the AI assistant (BR-F3-34).
