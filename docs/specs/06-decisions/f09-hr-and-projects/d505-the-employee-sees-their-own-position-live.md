# 📜 D505 The employee sees their own position live

**Status:** 🔄 draft (GH-2 step S4b, from VD-23 / D505)

## Context

What the employee sees when their position is shared. The owner did not tick "The employee" among those who see
positions ([D503](d503-who-sees-peoples-positions.md)); the next question settled their own view, which the law requires
in some form.

## Options

- The interview record keeps the owner's answer, not every option the question offered.

## Choice

A live indicator on their phone while their position is shared, what is recorded and who sees it, a pause during breaks,
and their history in their profile.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-13, in the interview on maps, degrees and scheduling ([interview
record](../../../tickets/GH-2/resources/flowcharts-screens/scheduling-maps-attributes.md), VD-23).

## Consequences

- New screen [S-F9-34 My position
  (phone)](../../02-functional/f09-hr-and-projects/screens/12-schedule-customer-requests/s-f09-34-my-position.md) and
  use case
  [UC-F9-37](../../02-functional/f09-hr-and-projects/use-cases/12-schedule-customer-requests/uc-f09-37-share-my-position-during-work.md).
- The pause stops the recording at once
  ([REQ-T13-05](../../03-technical/t13-maps-and-geodata/requirements/req-t13-05-live-positions.md)).
