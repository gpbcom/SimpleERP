# 📜 D287 French HR duties automated

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

French obligations beyond the benchmark.

## Options

- Automated.
- Registers only.
- As the benchmark.

## Choice

The personnel register (registre unique du personnel), the DPAE to URSSAF before each hire through its API, professional
interviews every 2 years and the 6-year review, occupational health visits, mandatory displays, the BDESE for companies
of 50 or more, with reminders and the legal assistant's warnings (F15).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F9-03, BR-F9-04.
