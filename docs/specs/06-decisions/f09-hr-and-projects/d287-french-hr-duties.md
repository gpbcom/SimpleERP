# 📜 D287 French HR duties automated

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

French obligations beyond the benchmark.

## Options

- Automated.
- Registers only.
- As the benchmark.

## Choice

The personnel register (registre unique du personnel) of each establishment, the DPAE to URSSAF before each hire through
its API, the career-path interviews (in the first year after hiring, then at most every 4 years, and the 8-year review,
Code du travail L6315-1 since 2025-12-31,
[CR-FR-039](../../10-country-packs/france/labour/cr-fr-039-professional-interviews.md); professional interviews every 2
years and a 6-year review until 2025-12-30), occupational health visits, mandatory displays, the BDESE for companies
of 50 or more, with reminders and the legal assistant's warnings (F15).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F9-03, BR-F9-04.
