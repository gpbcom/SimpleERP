# 📜 D291 Contract editor

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner: add a contract editor that can create, update, list and delete templated contracts, MUST be drag and drop for
the articles, and under the legal assistant's permanent scrutiny.

## Options

- A drag-and-drop editor under permanent legal review.

## Choice

A contract template editor: templates listed, created, updated, deleted; articles from a clause library dragged and
dropped into place and reordered; fields filled from the employee and the job; the legal assistant checks the template
continuously while it is edited and before it is used.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

UC-F9-19, BR-F9-13; reuses F15's document check (BR-F15-08) and F2's templates.
