# 📜 D285 Activity-based timesheet drafts

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The benchmark drafts timesheets from computer and browser activity; French law requires monitoring to be proportionate
and announced.

## Options

- The employee's own opt-in.
- Company-wide, announced.
- Not included.

## Choice

Each employee may turn it on for themselves; the activity stays private to them and only drafts their own timesheet;
managers see only submitted timesheets.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F9-07.
