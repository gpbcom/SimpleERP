# 📜 D514 Scheduling objectives

**Status:** 🔄 draft (GH-2 step S4b, from VD-32 / D514)

## Context

What scheduling optimises once the ineligible people are excluded
([D511](d511-required-or-preferred-attributes-legal-ones-always-required.md)).

## Options

- The interview record keeps the owner's answer, not every option the question offered.

## Choice

Travel and lateness; customer preferences (continuity, preferred or refused people, language); fair workload
(working-time rules, rest, overtime); cost and margin.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-13, in the interview on maps, degrees and scheduling ([interview
record](../../../tickets/GH-2/resources/flowcharts-screens/scheduling-maps-attributes.md), VD-32).

## Consequences

- Default order (D517): legal and required attributes always hard, then lateness, travel, customer preferences,
  fairness, cost; adjustable per request type
  [BR-F9-35](../../02-functional/f09-hr-and-projects/business-rules/br-f09-35-default-order-of-objectives.md).
- [E-F9-28 Assignment proposal](../../02-functional/f09-hr-and-projects/data/e-f09-28-assignment-proposal.md) keeps each
  candidate's score and reasons; [REQ-T13-07 Scheduling
  engine](../../03-technical/t13-maps-and-geodata/requirements/req-t13-07-scheduling-engine.md).
