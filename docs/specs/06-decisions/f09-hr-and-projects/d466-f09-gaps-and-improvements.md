# 📜 D466 F9 HR and projects: gaps and improvements

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The gaps-and-improvements round of 2026-11-12 (D457), for F9 and the sectors that rely on it.

## Options

- **Pay transparency and the gender equality index.** The EU directive 2023/970 (to be in national law by June 2026) and
  the French index for 50+ staff appear nowhere in the specs. Serves U06, U13, U45.
- **Whistleblowing channel.** Mandatory for 50+ employees (Sapin II and Waserman laws); appears nowhere in the specs.
- **Training plan, OPCO funding and mandatory trainings.** Absent in F9. Serves SC07 (driver training), SC09, SC11
  (continuing training).
- **Work accidents and occupational health.** No 48-hour declaration, and the risk assessment (DUERP) sits only in F12
  UC-F12-17, though every employer needs it. Serves SC01, SC04, SC06.
- **Capacity and resource planning across projects.** Absent. Serves SC03 UC-04, SC11.
- **Route and travel-time optimisation for field service.** The field-service map exists. Serves SC04, SC07. Ximi scores
  ✅ on E01 and D18.
- **Confirm the waiting proposals.** 39 items.
- **Split UC-F9-03 per duty.** It covers six duties in 4 steps; add disability employment (OETH, absent).
- **Engagement surveys and exit interviews.** Absent; they would explain the turnover KPI.
- **Customer use case.** The customer sees project progress and validates milestones. U09 has 1 use case.
- **Subcontractors and freelancers on projects.** Absent in F9. Serves SC01, SC03 UC-09.

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12:

- F9 HR and projects: gaps: All four groups: pay transparency, equality index and whistleblowing; training plan and
  OPCO; work accidents and DUERP; capacity planning (routes: shared rounds engine)
- F9 HR and projects: improvements: All four: split UC-F9-03 and add OETH; surveys and exit interviews; customer project
  view; subcontractors on projects
- F9 HR and projects: proposals: "Confirm all of F9"

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

- The chosen gaps and improvements become use cases, rules, entities, screens and flows of F9.
- F9's waiting proposals are confirmed (D457).
