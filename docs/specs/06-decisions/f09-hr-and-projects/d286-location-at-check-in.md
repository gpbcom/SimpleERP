# 📜 D286 Location at check-in only

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Whether attendance records a location.

## Options

- At check-in only.
- No location.
- Continuous tracking.

## Choice

For field workers, the phone's position is recorded only at check-in and check-out, never in between, shown to the
employee; off by default, turned on per team after informing staff.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F9-06.
