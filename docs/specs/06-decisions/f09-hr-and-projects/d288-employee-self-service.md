# 📜 D288 Employee self-service

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What employees do themselves.

## Options

- Broad, HR validates key data.
- Read mostly.
- Full, no validation.

## Choice

Employees see and update their details (bank account and family status validated by HR), request time off, check in,
fill timesheets, submit expenses, see payslips and documents, sign, request certificates, book training, see their
appraisals.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F9-01.
