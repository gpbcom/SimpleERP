# 📜 D289 HR coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner asked that F9 cover everything a leading open-source ERP offers about HR.

## Options

- Everything found.
- A selection.

## Choice

Everything found, and the gaps the benchmark leaves filled: certification expiry reminders, mandatory days, copying last
week's planning, CV reading by the AI, attendance anomaly flags.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Eighteen use cases.
