# 📜 D280 Who keeps the books

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who keeps the books.

## Options

- Both, with review.
- In-house only.
- Accountant only.

## Choice

In-house accounting (U05) and the external accountant (U08) with their own access: review, annotations and questions on
entries, period lock by the accountant, FEC and exports to the accountant's software.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

UC-F8-14.
