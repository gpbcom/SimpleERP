# 📜 D306 F8 benchmark additions

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner asked that F2 to F14 cover every feature of the benchmark set (PLAN D30); a reading of the benchmark found
these gaps in F8: several books, ifrs 15 revenue, continuous close with agents, spend control, planning and
profitability.

## Options

- Add them all.
- Add with changes.

## Choice

Added, as confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-11: UC-F8-15 Keep several books; UC-F8-16 Recognise revenue under IFRS 15;
UC-F8-17 Close continuously with agents; UC-F8-18 Control spend with cards and limits; UC-F8-19 Plan and forecast;
UC-F8-20 Analyse profitability. Each is reachable from the AI assistant within the user's roles (BR-F3-34); a legal
value it needs comes from the country pack (D293).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-11, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

New use cases, rules, entities, screens and the flow Benchmark additions.
