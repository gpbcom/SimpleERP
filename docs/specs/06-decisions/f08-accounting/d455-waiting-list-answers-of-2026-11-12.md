# 📜 D455 The owner's answers on the waiting list, by class

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The waiting list held about 2,200 pages of items Claude proposed and the owner had not confirmed (D2). The owner
answered them by class on 2026-11-12 at 15:20, during the review of the whole specification (step 21).

## Options

- Confirm page by page.
- Confirm by class, and read the law in batches.

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12 at 15:20, by class:

- The generated use-case details (goal, trigger, frequency, notifications, AI involvement, the non-functional note)
  stand as defaults; the non-functional note is governed by D408's targets.
- The persona pages U17 to U46 stand as written.
- The benchmark additions of F2 to F8 and F4's parity with the benchmark are confirmed in bulk.
- The country rules still to read are read in batches by web-fetch agents; what stays unreadable keeps its mark for a
  lawyer before launch.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

- The marks of these classes are removed and their pages leave the waiting list.
- Other proposals (API exposure, flows, rules, country rules) still wait.
