# 📜 D261 Going automatic

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

When an intent may go from shadow to automatic.

## Options

- 99 % over 200 cases.
- 95 % over 100 cases.
- Set per intent.

## Choice

At least 99 % accuracy on at least 200 real requests in shadow, judged by the AI judge and a human sample, then a
manager's switch; below 97 % for a week in automatic, back to shadow by itself.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F16-01.
