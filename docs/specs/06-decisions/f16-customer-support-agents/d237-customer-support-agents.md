# 📜 D237 Customer support agents

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner asked for the connectors and scrapers needed to do everything a market-leading AI customer support agent
company does (its website given on 2026-11-10, not named here at the owner's request).

## Options

- A new capability for support agents, and one for connectors and scrapers.
- Spread across existing capabilities.

## Choice

Two new capabilities: F16 Customer support agents (resolving customer requests end to end) and T12 Connectors and
scrapers (every outside system they read or act in, with or without an API).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Interviewed at steps 11a (F16, after F6) and 30a (T12, after T11).
