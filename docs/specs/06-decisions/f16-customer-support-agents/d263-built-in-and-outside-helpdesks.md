# 📜 D263 Built-in inbox and outside helpdesks

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Where requests are handled.

## Options

- Built-in and outside.
- Built-in only.
- Outside only.

## Choice

SimpleERP has its own request inbox (email, portal, forms, social inbox, phone), and agents also work inside outside
helpdesks through connectors (T12).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

UC-F16-06.
