# 📜 D264 Human sampling

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How many automatic resolutions a person checks.

## Options

- 5 % and all low scores.
- Low scores only.
- None.

## Choice

A random 5 % plus every resolution the AI judge scores below its bar.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences
