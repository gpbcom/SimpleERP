# 📜 D244 Approval by role

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How posts are approved.

## Options

- Approval by role.
- No approval.
- Approval always.

## Choice

Authors draft; a post goes out only after approval by someone with the approver right, except in automatic mode (D246);
scheduled posts wait in a calendar.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F5-01.
