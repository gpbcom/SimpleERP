# 📜 D245 AI for social media

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What the AI does.

## Options

- Draft posts.
- Images and video.
- Best time and hashtags.
- Reply suggestions.

## Choice

All four: drafts in the company style (D162) with the AI mention (D164); generated or edited images and short videos
(captions, subtitles, cuts); times and hashtags from past results; replies suggested, sent by a person.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

A "Social media drafts" provider already exists (D82); image and video generation need a model (T7).
