# 📜 D303 F5 becomes social media and marketing

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner asked that F2 to F14 cover every feature of the benchmark set (PLAN D30); a reading of the benchmark found
these gaps in F5: email and sms campaigns, marketing journeys, events, surveys and leads from social media; f5 is
renamed.

## Options

- Add them all.
- Add with changes.

## Choice

Added, as confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-11: UC-F5-08 Run email campaigns; UC-F5-09 Send SMS campaigns; UC-F5-10
Automate marketing journeys; UC-F5-11 Organise events and sell tickets; UC-F5-12 Run surveys and live polls; UC-F5-13
Turn social interactions into leads. Each is reachable from the AI assistant within the user's roles (BR-F3-34); a legal
value it needs comes from the country pack (D293).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-11, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

New use cases, rules, entities, screens and the flow Benchmark additions.
