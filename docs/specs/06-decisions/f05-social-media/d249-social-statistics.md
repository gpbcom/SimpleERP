# 📜 D249 Social statistics

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Which statistics.

## Options

- Per post, a summary, and leads.
- Per post only.
- None.

## Choice

Reach, impressions, engagement, clicks and followers per post and per network; a monthly summary; leads and orders
traced back to posts through tracked links (F6).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F5-06.
