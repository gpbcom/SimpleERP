# 📜 D258 Invoicing schemes

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Which invoicing schemes beyond a simple invoice.

## Options

- Deposits, progress and retention.
- Deposits only.
- Simple invoices.

## Choice

Deposits; progress invoices by percentage or line; the retention guarantee and its release; a final invoice deducting
them.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F6-06.
