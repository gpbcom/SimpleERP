# 📜 D255 Discount approval

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Whether large discounts need approval.

## Options

- Above a threshold.
- Never.
- Always.

## Choice

Above a threshold per role (15 % by default) or below a margin floor, a manager approves before the quote is sent.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F6-04.
