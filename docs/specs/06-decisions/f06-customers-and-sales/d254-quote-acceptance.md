# 📜 D254 Quote acceptance

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How a quote is accepted.

## Options

- A click, or an e-signature per document type.
- A click only.
- An e-signature always.

## Choice

By default, a click (name, date, time and IP kept with "good for agreement"); for document types the company chooses
(contracts, large amounts), an e-signature through a provider (Yousign, DocuSign), simple or advanced under eIDAS.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F6-01.
