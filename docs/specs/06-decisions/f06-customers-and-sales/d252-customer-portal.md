# 📜 D252 The customer portal

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

What customers can do in their portal (D17).

## Options

- Everything.
- Documents and payment.
- Documents only.

## Choice

Everything: see and accept quotes, sign, pay invoices online, download documents, follow orders and work, send requests
(handled by F16 or a person), update their details.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Portal access as D17: a secure link per document, an optional account for the whole history.
