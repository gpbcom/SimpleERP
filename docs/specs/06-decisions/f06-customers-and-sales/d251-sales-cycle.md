# 📜 D251 The whole sales cycle

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Which parts of the sales cycle F6 covers.

## Options

- Leads and pipeline.
- Quotes and orders.
- Invoices and payments.
- Subscriptions.

## Choice

All four: contacts, companies, leads and an opportunity pipeline with activities and forecasts; quotes with price lists,
discounts, options and versions, orders and their follow-up; invoices, deposits, credit notes, payments, reminders and
dunning, e-invoicing; recurring contracts with renewals and price indexation.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

F6 is the largest business capability; F8 receives its invoices and payments.
