# 📜 D304 F6 benchmark additions

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner asked that F2 to F14 cover every feature of the benchmark set (PLAN D30); a reading of the benchmark found
these gaps in F6: lead scoring and enrichment, product configurator, rental, usage and milestone billing.

## Options

- Add them all.
- Add with changes.

## Choice

Added, as confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-11: UC-F6-13 Score, assign and enrich leads; UC-F6-14 Configure products in
a quote; UC-F6-15 Rent out equipment; UC-F6-16 Bill usage, commitments and milestones. Each is reachable from the
AI assistant within the user's roles (BR-F3-34); a legal value it needs comes from the country pack (D293).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-11, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

New use cases, rules, entities, screens and the flow Benchmark additions.
