# 📜 D259 VAT visible

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner: "Make sure that the VAT is visible if relevent."

## Options

- VAT shown wherever an amount is priced, invoiced or paid.

## Choice

Every quote, order, invoice, credit note, subscription, portal page and payment request shows the amounts excluding VAT,
the VAT per rate and the amounts including VAT; a VAT exemption or reverse charge shows its legal mention instead of a
VAT amount.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F6-08; reports say whether their figures are excluding or including VAT.
