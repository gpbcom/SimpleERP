# 📜 D256 Reminders fully automatic

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How late payers are reminded.

## Options

- Automatic, the formal notice approved.
- Fully automatic.
- Manual.

## Choice

Fully automatic: the reminder sequence and the formal notice go out with no approval; a payment, a promise or a dispute
stops it.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F6-05.
