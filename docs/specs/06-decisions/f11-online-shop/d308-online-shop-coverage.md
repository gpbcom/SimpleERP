# 📜 D308 Online shop coverage

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner asked that F11 cover everything the benchmark's online shop and website offer (D30), with the French
online-selling law.

## Options

- Everything, plus French law.
- Shop only, no website builder.

## Choice

Everything found by the benchmark reading (27 pages): website builder and AI configurator, catalogue, variants,
pricelists, promotions, loyalty, gift cards and e-wallet, cart and checkout, payments, shipping and click & collect,
returns, customer portal, B2B and B2C, subscriptions, rentals, digital products, cross-selling, wishlists, comparisons,
reviews, abandoned carts, SEO, several websites and languages, analytics, cookie consent; the French online-selling
rules (withdrawal function since 19 June 2026, pre-contract information, delivery within 30 days, legal guarantee,
reviews, legal notices, trackers) in the country pack.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Twenty use cases (D311); every feature reachable from the AI assistant (BR-F3-34).
