📜 D468 F11 Online shop (plugin): gaps and improvements

# 📜 D468 F11 Online shop (plugin): gaps and improvements

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The gaps-and-improvements round of 2026-11-12 (D457), for F11 and the sectors that rely on it.

## Options

- **Fraud screening, 3-D Secure and chargeback handling.** Absent.
- **Product feeds (Google Shopping, price comparison).** Absent.
- **GPSR product-safety information on listings.** Absent in F11.
- **Pre-orders and back-in-stock reservations.** Absent.
- **PIM-grade product content.** Attributes, media and translations per channel. Serves SC02 grids, SC15 B2B catalogue.
- **B2B quote requests and punch-out.** Quote requests are mentioned twice, punch-out is absent. Serves SC15 UC-18.
- **Confirm the waiting proposals.** 37 items, plus BR-F11-02 and BR-F11-11.
- **Deepen the withdrawal process.** UC-F11-09 covers a legal 14-day process in 3 steps; fl-f11-03 has 257 words.
- **Customer-facing states.** Empty cart, out of stock, payment pending (U09 appears in 11 use cases).
- **Ship-from-store and picking of online orders.** Serves U15, SC02 UC-08.
- **More reports.** Funnel per checkout step, customer lifetime value, return reasons.

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12:

- F11 Online shop: gaps and improvements: All four groups: fraud and chargebacks; feeds, GPSR and pre-orders; product
  content and B2B; withdrawal, shopper states, store fulfilment and reports
- F11 Online shop: proposals: "Confirm all of F11"

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

- The chosen gaps and improvements become use cases, rules, entities, screens and flows of F11.
- F11's waiting proposals are confirmed (D457).
