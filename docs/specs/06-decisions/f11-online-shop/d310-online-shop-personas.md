# 📜 D310 Online shop personas

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who runs the online shop day to day.

## Options

- U12 Marketing and U03 Sales.
- A new e-commerce manager persona.

## Choice

U12 Marketing runs the site, catalogue and promotions; U03 Sales handles B2B quotes and orders; U09 Customer buys; U07
Administrator sets payments, carriers and legal pages. No new persona.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

F11 personas.
