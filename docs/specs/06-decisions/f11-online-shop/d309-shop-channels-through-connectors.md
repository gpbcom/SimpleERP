# 📜 D309 Marketplaces through connectors

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How marketplaces and other shop platforms (Amazon, eBay, Shopify, TikTok Shop, WooCommerce, Shopee) are reached.

## Options

- Through T12 connectors.
- Built into F11.
- Out of scope for v1.

## Choice

Each marketplace or shop platform is a T12 connector, static or agentic: orders in, stock and prices out; a new one
needs no F11 change.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

UC-F11-18; BR-F11-12.
