# 📜 D427 Integrations hub

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner asked for connectors to European open-source office suites, starting with openDesk and La Suite numérique, and
for the 50 leading ATS and CRM by default (PLAN D37).

## Options

- A new capability F24 plus a rule for every connector.
- Two capabilities.
- Inside T12 and the existing capabilities.

## Choice

A new capability F24 Integrations hub, confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12: the catalogue of ready connectors, the
European open-source office suites first, the 50 leading ATS and CRM by default, then the rest; built on T12's connector
contract.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Twenty use cases (D431).
