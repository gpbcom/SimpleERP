# 📜 D429 Fewest steps for every connector

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner: for all outside connectors, enabling the integration must take as few steps and actions as possible.

## Options

- A measured target of three actions.
- Best effort.

## Choice

Best effort, confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12: every connector, in every capability (F5, F6, F7, F8, F9, F16, F17,
F18, F22, F24 and T12), is enabled with as few steps and actions as possible: sign-in through OAuth where the tool
offers it, settings discovered and pre-filled, the AI assistant doing the rest; no fixed number.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F24-02 applies to every connector of the product.
