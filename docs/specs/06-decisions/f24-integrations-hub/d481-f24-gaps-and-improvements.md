# 📜 D481 F24 Integrations hub: gaps and improvements

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The gaps-and-improvements round of 2026-11-12 (D457), for F24 and the sectors that rely on it.

## Options

- **Catalogue categories beyond office suites, CRM and ATS.** Banks, payments, shops, accounting, payroll and carriers
  are absent in F24. Light lists 586 apps and Doss 100+ (B08).
- **Automation tools (n8n, Make, Zapier).** Absent; webhooks get 1 mention. Dolibarr ships Zapier in core (B05).
- **French HR and payroll SaaS (Silae, PayFit, Lucca).** Used before F13 exists; absent.
- **Steps to connect the e-invoicing platform.** Q13 is still waiting.
- **Confirm the waiting proposals.** 30 items.
- **Deepen the thinnest flow.** fl-f24-01 (201 words).
- **Employee use cases.** U01 has 4.

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12:

- F24 Integrations hub: all: All: more catalogue categories; automation tools and French HR SaaS; e-invoicing connection
  steps (D454), deeper flow, employee use cases; "Confirm F24's proposals". **The owner added:** "Also add icons in the
  web UI for the different integration."

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

- The chosen gaps and improvements become use cases, rules, entities, screens and flows of F24.
- F24's waiting proposals are confirmed (D457).
