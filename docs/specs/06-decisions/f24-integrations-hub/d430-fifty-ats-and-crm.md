# 📜 D430 The 50 ATS and CRM by default

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Which ATS and CRM are integrated by default, and how the list is kept.

## Options

- A curated list reviewed yearly.
- Chosen by usage.

## Choice

A curated list of 25 CRM and 25 ATS, confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12 (BR-F24-05), kept in a catalogue and reviewed
every year; each connector syncs contacts, companies and deals, or candidates, jobs and stages, both ways where the tool
allows.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F24-05, BR-F24-06.
