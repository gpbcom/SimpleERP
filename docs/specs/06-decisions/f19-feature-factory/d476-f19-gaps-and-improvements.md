# 📜 D476 F19 Feature factory (hosted edition): gaps and improvements

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The gaps-and-improvements round of 2026-11-12 (D457), for F19 and the sectors that rely on it.

## Options

- **Delivery date and delay handling.** No due date or delay case.
- **Ownership of generated code and the customer's rights on cancellation.** Absent.
- **Security and privacy review of generated plugins.** DPIA when personal data is handled; "security", GDPR and AI Act
  are all absent in F19.
- **Refund when acceptance fails.**
- **Confirm the waiting proposals.** 31 items, BR-F19-12, and D390 VAT of the credit (D418 tax adviser).
- **Deepen the thinnest flows.** fl-f19-01 (276 words) and fl-f19-05 (277 words).
- **More accounting use cases.** U05 has 3.

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12:

- F19 Feature factory: all: All: delivery dates, delays and refunds; ownership and security review (DPIA); flows and
  accounting; "Confirm F19's proposals"

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

- The chosen gaps and improvements become use cases, rules, entities, screens and flows of F19.
- F19's waiting proposals are confirmed (D457).
