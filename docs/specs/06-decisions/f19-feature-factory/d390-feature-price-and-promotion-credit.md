# 📜 D390 Feature price and promotion credit

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner: each feature costs 300 EUR a month; a customer who promotes a feature earns 50 % of the feature cost; VAT and
legal obligations taken into account.

## Options

- 300 EUR excluding VAT, ordered by the owner or an administrator; the share as a credit on the customer's own invoices.
- A payout as a publisher.
- Credit first, payout above.

## Choice

300 EUR a month excluding VAT per feature, VAT added as the country pack says (French VAT, or the reverse charge for an
EU business customer), ordered only by the owner or an administrator after a quote, within a spending limit, cancellable
monthly. The promoting customer earns 50 % of what other hosted customers pay for that feature, as a credit note on its
own hosted invoices, up to their amount, the rest carried forward; no payout, no seller status. A tax adviser
confirms the credit is a price reduction and not a supply by the customer.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

BR-F19-08 to BR-F19-12; country rules CR-FR-200 to 205.

Revised by D443 on 2026-11-12: the 300 EUR a month pays the feature's hosting, support and updates, not a licence: the
generated plugin is AGPL and runs with no licence check; a lawyer confirms the model before launch.
