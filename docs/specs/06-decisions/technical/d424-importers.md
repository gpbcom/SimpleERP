# 📜 D424 Importers from other ERPs

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Step 19m of the technical interview, from TC-18.

## Options

- Importers on one mapping model.
- Spreadsheets only.

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12: importers for Odoo, Dolibarr, Sage, EBP and spreadsheets on one mapping model,
with a dry run, opening balances and history, and the AI assistant helping to map.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

The data migration chapter's target design.
