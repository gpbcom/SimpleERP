# 📜 D423 Release train and long-term support

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Step 19l of the technical interview.

## Options

- SemVer, a release train and LTS.
- Continuous delivery.

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12: semantic versions, a monthly minor release, a yearly LTS supported 24 months,
packs and plugins versioned apart, upgrades from the last 12 months, expand-then-contract migrations.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

The release chapter's target design.
