# 📜 D32 Several locations per company

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Stated by the owner with the personas: "Make sure that the companies has several location possible (HQ + others)."

## Options

- Not discussed: the owner stated it.

## Choice

A company can have several locations: a head office and other sites.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Sites matter for T1 and F1 (set-up), F7 (stock per site), F9 (staff per site) and permissions; what a site holds is
asked in the next round.
