# 📜 D374 Revenue and pricing

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

AGPL with no paid edition (D9): the money comes from around the product.

## Options

Revenue: managed hosting, support subscriptions, plugin store share, services. Pricing: flat per company, per user, flat
plus AI usage, size bands.

## Choice

Four revenue sources: managed hosting, support subscriptions (support, guaranteed updates and country-pack updates), a
share of the plugin store (F14) and services (installation, migration, training, custom development). Pricing: a flat
fee per company, plus metered AI use (meeting minutes, agent tickets) when the publisher hosts the AI.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-11, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

No per-user and no per-app price. A self-hosted company running its own models pays nothing for AI.

Revised by D443 on 2026-11-12: paid plugins sell support, updates and hosting, with no seat and no licence check.

Revised by D447 on 2026-11-12: country-pack updates are free for everyone, so support subscriptions sell support and
guaranteed updates; with the feature factory (D390), the revenue sources are five.
