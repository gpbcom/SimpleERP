# 📜 D569 Roadmap option A, cut into twelve phases

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

[Q20](../../07-questions/q20-which-roadmap-option-orders-v1.md) asked in which order V1 is built. The
[roadmap options](../../00-vision/roadmap/options/README.md) compared six orders: the adopted ten steps in a row
(option A), parallel tracks (B), the pilot first (C), sector waves (D), the factory first (E) and a combination of C, B
and D (F, Claude's recommendation). The roadmap had been cut into twelve phases for the ticket harness
([Phases](../../00-vision/roadmap/phases/README.md)), with the placements of steps 8 to 10 and of F14, F15, F16 and F24
marked as Claude's proposals. Option A cannot meet the date of the dated pilot of
[D451](d451-dated-pilot-before-the-release.md), F1 to F9 before 1 September 2027.

## Options

- A: the ten build steps in a row, completed with T13 and SC17 to SC20.
- B: the platform, then parallel tracks.
- C: the pilot first, as a gate.
- D: sector waves.
- E: the factory first.
- F: the pilot spine of C, with B's tracks and D's sector waves.

## Choice

**Option A**: the ten build steps of the [roadmap](../../00-vision/roadmap/roadmap.md) in a row, as cut into the twelve
phases of [Phases](../../00-vision/roadmap/phases/README.md): P0 Foundations, P1 DevOps agents, P2 Platform core (T1 to
T13), P3 Platform features, P4 Customers and sales, P5 Purchasing and stock, P6 Accounting, P7 HR, projects and legal,
P8 Plugins and the plugin store, P9 Growth and insight, P10 Sectors (SC01 to SC20) and P11 Hosted edition services.
Every placement Claude had proposed for a build step is confirmed.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-27.

## Consequences

- [Q20](../../07-questions/q20-which-roadmap-option-orders-v1.md) is answered. The roadmap, [What V1
  holds](../../00-vision/roadmap/inventory.md), the options and the twelve phase pages say option A is adopted; options B
  to F are kept as the record of what was compared, not adopted.
- T13 Maps and geodata is built in step 2 (P2), and SC17 to SC20 in step 9 (P10) with the other sectors.
- **The pilot's date of [D451](d451-dated-pilot-before-the-release.md) is superseded by the phase calendar.** The
  pilot starts once step 6 (P7, estimated to end on 2 June 2028) is built and runs F1 to F4 and F6 to F9 with
  e-invoicing through an approved platform; F5 follows in step 8 (P9). The pilot therefore no longer runs before
  1 September 2027, the date small companies must issue e-invoices
  ([CR-FR-016](../../10-country-packs/france/invoicing/cr-fr-016-electronic-invoicing.md)). D451 stays for the rest:
  a pilot on a real company before the single release of [D380](d380-every-capability-first-confirmed.md).
- The phase dates are those of [Phases](../../00-vision/roadmap/phases/README.md), from 7 December 2026 (P0) to
  16 March 2029 (P11), an estimate corrected as tickets are planned.
