# 📜 D443 Paid plugins and features sell support, updates and hosting

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

R5: every plugin is AGPL (D377) while D368, BR-F14-05, D374 and D390 speak of licence checks, seats and per-feature
fees; the AGPL lets users remove such restrictions.

## Options

- Support, updates and hosting only.
- Licence checks and seats as written.

## Choice

Paid plugins (F14) and the feature factory's features (F19) sell support, updates and hosting only: no enforced licence
check, no seats. A lawyer confirms the F14 and F19 models before launch.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12 at 14:53, round 1 of the decisions on the independent review (R5, PROGRESS.md, step 21).

## Consequences

Revises D368 (licences and seats withdrawn), D374 (the store's share is on support, updates and hosting), D377 and D390
(the 300 EUR a month pays a feature's hosting, support and updates). BR-F14-05 is withdrawn; the F14 scope, UC-F14-05,
UC-F14-06 and E-F14-05 lose seats and licence checks.
