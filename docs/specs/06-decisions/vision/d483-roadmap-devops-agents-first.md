# 📜 D483 Roadmap: everything in V1, the DevOps agents first

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The owner asked on 2026-11-12 at 21:20 for an exhaustive roadmap: "EVERY THING present on this specs MUST BE in V1
correct the pages that says otherwise. The FIRST STEP of the roadmap will be the DevOps Agents." The roadmap had a "To
be decided" row (F5, F14 to F17, F23, F24) and a separate hosted row.

## Options

- The DevOps agents first, or the platform first (D16).
- Capabilities without a step left to decide, or each placed in a step.

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12: everything the specifications hold is in V1, and the first step is the DevOps
agents (F25). The platform becomes step 2; the business areas and plugins keep their order (D3, D15, D16); the
capabilities that had no step are placed in steps 3 to 10.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

- The roadmap lists ten steps; no capability, sector or topic is left without one.
- Pages that placed something after V1 or without a step are corrected.
- D380 stands: V1 is released once every step is built; the pilot of D451 runs after step 6.
