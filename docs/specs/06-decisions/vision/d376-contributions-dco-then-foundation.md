# 📜 D376 Contributions: DCO now, a foundation later

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How outside contributions work, and who holds the project.

## Options

- DCO sign-off.
- A CLA.
- DCO now, a foundation later.
- No outside code in the core.

## Choice

Contributors sign each commit (Developer Certificate of Origin) and keep their copyright. The project moves to a
foundation or an association once several companies stand behind it.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-11, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Nobody can ever relicense the code: there will be no dual licence.
