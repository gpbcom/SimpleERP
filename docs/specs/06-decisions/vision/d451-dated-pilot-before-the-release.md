# 📜 D451 A dated pilot before the release

**Status:** ✅ decided

Superseded by [D569](d569-roadmap-option-a-in-twelve-phases.md) for its date.

## Context

R24: D380 (a release only once every capability and sector is built) against the French e-invoicing date of 1 September
2027 for small companies; Q13 and Q16 open.

## Options

- A dated pilot with e-invoicing, D380 kept.
- An early release.

## Choice

A dated pilot runs F1 to F9 with e-invoicing through an approved platform before 1 September 2027; D380 is kept for the
release.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12 at 14:57, round 3 of the decisions on the independent review (R24, PROGRESS.md, step 21).

## Consequences

Answers Q16; revises D380; Q13's platform is chosen for the pilot. The roadmap says so.
