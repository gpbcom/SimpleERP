# 📜 D447 Country-pack updates free; hosted-only services named

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

R9: "no paid edition holding back features" against the hosted-only F18 and paid country-pack updates (D374), while
packs are pulled automatically (D205).

## Options

- Country-pack updates free for everyone, hosted-only services named.
- Paid pack updates.

## Choice

Country-pack updates are free for everyone, self-hosted included. What is hosted-only is named plainly: F18 websites and
domains, F19 the feature factory, F20 the support assistant and F25 the DevOps agents, the closed services of the hosted
edition (D388). Every ERP feature stays open and self-hostable.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12 at 14:55, round 2 of the decisions on the independent review (R9, PROGRESS.md, step 21).

## Consequences

Revises D374 (support subscriptions sell support and guaranteed updates; five revenue sources) and D373. The vision
pages say so.
