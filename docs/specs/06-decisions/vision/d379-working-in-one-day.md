# 📜 D379 Working in one day

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The time-to-value promise for a new company.

## Options

- Working in one day.
- Working in one hour (hosted).
- One week with import.

## Choice

In one day a company installs simple-ERP, sets itself up, imports its data from its old tools and sends its first
invoice, without a consultant.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-11, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

The guided setup (D54), the sector packs and the data migration (step 19m) are measured against it.
