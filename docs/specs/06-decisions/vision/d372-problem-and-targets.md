# 📜 D372 The problem and who has it

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The problem page was empty: which problems simple-ERP solves, and for whom first.

## Options

Problems: tool sprawl, ERPs too heavy, data sovereignty, AI bolted on. Targets: small companies of 1 to 50 people, SMEs
of 50 to 250, IT providers hosting clients, accounting firms running it for clients.

## Choice

All four problems, and all four targets, whatever the sector. No target comes first.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-11, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

The first version serves a one-person company and a 250-person SME alike, run alone or by a provider (D1). Each sector
keeps its pack (08-sectors).
