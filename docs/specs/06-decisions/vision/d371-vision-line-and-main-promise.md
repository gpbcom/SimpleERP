# 📜 D371 The vision line and the main promise

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The vision needed one sentence at the top of the specs, and one main promise among the four problems the owner named.

## Options

- "One open, sovereign tool that runs the whole company, and its AI does the work."
- "One self-hosted suite replaces the ERP, the meeting assistant, the support agents and the rest."
- "The AI-native ERP that belongs to you: open source, on your servers, set up in a day."

## Choice

The vision line is "simple-ERP is one open, sovereign tool that runs the whole company, and its AI does the work." The
main promise, in the owner's words: one tool for everything, with AI doing the job. Sovereignty, simplicity and AI by
design follow from it.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-11, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

The specs home and the vision open with this line. A feature earns its place when it removes a separate tool or lets the
AI do the work.
