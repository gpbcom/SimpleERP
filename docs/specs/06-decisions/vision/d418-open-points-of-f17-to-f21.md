# 📜 D418 Open points of F17 to F21

**Status:** ✅ decided, ⚖️ lawyer review before launch (D574)

## Context

Three points left open when F17 to F21 were written, asked on 2026-11-12.

## Options

- The open-core wording: keep it and name the hosted services; make F18 to F20 open; present a paid hosted edition.
- The VAT of the promotion credit: a price reduction until an adviser confirms; a supply by the customer through
  self-billing; later.
- F18 providers: European first; European only; any provider.

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12: keep the open-core sentence and name the hosted services, every ERP feature
staying open and self-hostable; the credit stays a price reduction (credit note), (⚖️ lawyer review before launch, D574) until a tax adviser
confirms before launch
([CR-FR-205](../../10-country-packs/france/tax-and-vat/cr-fr-205-promotion-credit-vat-treatment.md)); European providers first (for example OVHcloud, Scaleway, Gandi), others later behind the same
interface.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

The positioning page names the hosted services; D385 states the providers; CR-FR-205 stays.
