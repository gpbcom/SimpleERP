# 📜 D375 Managed hosting in the EU only

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The sovereignty promise meets a worldwide ambition: where the publisher's hosting runs.

## Options

- Per region, on local clouds.
- EU only.
- The publisher hosts in France, partners elsewhere.

## Choice

Every hosted customer, worldwide, is served from European clouds. Outside the EU, a company self-hosts or uses an IT
provider (D1).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-11, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

One hosting region to certify and run. Data residency outside the EU is reached by self-hosting.
