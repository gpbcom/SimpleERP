# 📜 D378 A worldwide ambition, four measures, no number

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The objectives and the measures of success.

## Options

Ambition: French reference, European, worldwide, sustainable product. Measures: companies in production, switch without
a consultant, tools replaced, community reach. Target: 1 000, 10 000, 100 000 or none.

## Choice

Worldwide, with country packs written by the community. Four measures, tracked but without a number: companies in
production; a company switching without a consultant; the paid tools a customer cancels and the money saved; outside
contributors, plugins and country packs.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-11, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

The measures are reported, not targeted.
