# 📜 D34 Full phone experience

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Which personas need a full experience on the phone, not only on a desktop.

## Options

- All employees (self-service).
- Managers and owner.
- Sales on the road.
- Customers and suppliers.

## Choice

All four: every employee's self-service, managers and the owner, sales on the road, and the customer and supplier
portals work fully on a phone.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Every screen these personas use is designed for the phone too.
