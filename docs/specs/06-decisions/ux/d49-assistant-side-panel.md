# 📜 D49 Assistant side panel

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Where the AI assistant (F3, F15) lives on screen.

## Options

- A side panel, everywhere.
- Its own page.
- A floating bubble.

## Choice

A panel that opens on the right of any screen (full screen on phones), aware of what the user is looking at, so they can
ask about the current invoice or employee.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

The panel is a component of the design system; F3 and F15 specify what it can do.
