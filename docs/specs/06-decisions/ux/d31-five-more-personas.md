# 📜 D31 Five more personas

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The 11 personas taken from the roles miss the people of the new modules and those away from a desk.

## Options

- Marketing and communication.
- Owner.
- Field worker.
- Warehouse operator.
- Office worker (added by the owner).

## Choice

Add marketing and communication (U12), the owner (U13), the field worker (U14), the warehouse operator (U15), and the
office worker, on site or remote (U16).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

16 personas, each with a home screen and journeys in `04-ux/views/`.
