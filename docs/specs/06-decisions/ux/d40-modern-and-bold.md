# 📜 D40 Modern and bold

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Which personality the visual identity conveys.

## Options

- Calm and trustworthy.
- Friendly and warm.
- Modern and bold.

## Choice

Modern and bold: strong contrast, a vivid primary colour, large headings, energetic words.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Every visual page follows it.
