# 📜 D44 Typefaces

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Which typefaces, served by SimpleERP itself.

## Options

- Plus Jakarta Sans and Inter.
- Inter only.
- System fonts.

## Choice

Plus Jakarta Sans for headings and dashboard figures, Inter for body text, tables and forms, JetBrains Mono for codes
and IDs, all served by SimpleERP itself (no call to Google Fonts).

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

The font files ship in the images, so no visitor's address reaches a third party.
