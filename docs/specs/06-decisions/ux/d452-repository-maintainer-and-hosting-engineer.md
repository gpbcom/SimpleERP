# 📜 D452 Repository maintainers apart from hosting engineers

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

R28: U47 is both the publisher's hosting engineer and the approver in the open repository planned to move to a
foundation (D376).

## Options

- Two personas.
- One persona.

## Choice

U47 becomes the repository maintainer: reviews and approves pull requests, the DevOps agents' included, removes the
approval label, merges and releases. A new persona, U48 Hosting engineer, runs the hosted edition: the support
assistant's fixes, incidents, domains, the feature factory's reviews and store, the platform's costs, and the DevOps
agents' runners and rights.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12 at 14:57, round 3 of the decisions on the independent review (R28, PROGRESS.md, step 21).

## Consequences

Revises D392, D393, D434 and D435. F18 to F22 and F25 name U48 where the hosted edition is run; roles, glossary and the
persona list say so.
