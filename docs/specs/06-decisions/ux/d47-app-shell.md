# 📜 D47 App shell

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

How the application frame is laid out on a desktop.

## Options

- Left sidebar and top bar.
- Top navigation.
- App launcher grid.

## Choice

A collapsible left sidebar with the capabilities the role can use; a top bar with global search, the AI assistant,
notifications, the site switcher and the user menu. On phones: a bottom tab bar.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Each persona's sidebar lists what their role can use.
