# 📜 D490 Example images for every persona that can open a view

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Which example images each aggregated list view gets.

## Options

- The main persona, 4 shots.
- Every persona that can open the view.
- Desktop only.

## Choice

Every persona that can open the view gets its images: desktop and phone, light and dark.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the interview on aggregated views, wording and state screens (interview
record (the owner's interview notes of 2026-11 for the specifications, not kept in the repository), answer 7).

## Consequences

- About 7,200 list-view images (1,802 persona-views × 4), by the count of 2026-11-12.
- They are drawn with GH-2's mockup kit, as the other images ([D120](d120-png-mockups.md),
  [D121](d121-mockup-sizes.md)).
- Where they live: [D500](d500-images-live-in-the-specs-folders.md).
