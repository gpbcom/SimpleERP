# 📜 D51 "Vous" in French

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Which form of address the French screens use.

## Options

- Vous.
- Tu.
- Tu inside, vous on portals.

## Choice

The French screens, documents and messages say "vous" everywhere, portals included.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Translation files and the AI assistant's French answers use "vous".
