# 📜 D33 Design for an office worker

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Who is the least experienced user the screens must work for, without training.

## Options

- A rare software user.
- An office worker.
- A trained power user.

## Choice

The screens work, without training, for an office worker used to email, spreadsheets and online forms.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Field workers' phone screens may need more; see [Q10](../../07-questions/q10-field-worker-skill-level.md).
