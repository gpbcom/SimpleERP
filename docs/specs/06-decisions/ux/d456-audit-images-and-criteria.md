# 📜 D456 Mockup images per device, and the derived acceptance criteria

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

The requirements audit of 2026-11-12 (step 31) left five points for the owner: the tablet size, the choices made while
drawing every persona's device, the images' radii and paddings against the design tokens, the derived acceptance
criteria AC10 to AC16 of GH-2, and the journeys with few steps.

## Options

- Confirm each point.
- Change it.
- Leave it for a later ticket.

## Choice

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-12 at 17:47 ("Finish everything from 1 to 5"):

- A tablet is drawn at 820 x 1180 px, portrait; the sidebar collapses to icons and the AI assistant panel covers the
  right half.
- A touchscreen till or kitchen screen (U17, U19) is drawn at the desktop size, 1440 x 900 px.
- An error image is drawn for every device of its own persona.
- A screen whose responsive behaviour is desktop only shows "Continue on a larger screen" on a phone.
- The images follow the design tokens (radii and paddings) and are drawn again from them.
- GH-2's derived acceptance criteria AC10 to AC16 stand.
- The journeys are drawn again once every image exists, from the steps the specs give each persona.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-12, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

- [Responsive](../../04-ux/design-system/responsive.md) loses its mark.
- U17 and U19 say their drawing size.
- Every image is rendered again.
