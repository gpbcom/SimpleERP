# 📜 D50 Field screens for rare software users

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Field workers and carers often use software rarely, while the design target is an office worker (D33). Asked as
[Q10](../../07-questions/q10-field-worker-skill-level.md).

## Options

- Yes, for the field worker's phone screens.
- No, the office worker level everywhere.

## Choice

The field worker's phone screens (today, time, requests) are designed for a rare software user: one task per screen,
large touch targets, icons with words, no jargon. D33 applies everywhere else.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

U14's screens follow this level; the other personas keep D33.
