# 📜 D43 Light and dark modes

**Status:** ✅ decided

## Context

Light and dark modes.

## Options

- Both, following the system.
- Light only.
- Dark by default.

## Choice

Light and dark themes, following the device's setting, with a manual switch per user. Both meet the contrast rules.

## Decided by

The owner, on 2026-11-10, in the specification interview.

## Consequences

Every colour has a light and a dark value.
