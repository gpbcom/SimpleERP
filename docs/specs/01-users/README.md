# 👤 Users

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Inside the company

Every employee is a user, managers and office staff included, and their role decides what they see and do. Everyone
uses the self-service part: their own time off, timesheets, expenses, payslips, meeting summaries and the
assistant. See [D14](../06-decisions/foundations/d14-every-employee-is-a-user.md).

## Outside the company

| User | Can |
|---|---|
| External accountant | Read the books and get the exports, without editing sales documents |
| Customer, through a portal | See their quotes, orders and invoices, accept a quote and pay online |
| Supplier, through a portal | See their purchase orders, and send their invoices and delivery dates |
| IT provider administrator | Install, update and support several client companies from one place |

Customers and suppliers open a document through a secure link in its email, valid for that document only. One who
wants to see their whole history creates a portal account, with two-factor authentication. See
[D11](../06-decisions/foundations/d11-outside-users.md), [D17](../06-decisions/foundations/d17-portal-sign-in.md).
