# 🎭 Events and culture

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Company

Scènes du Fleuve, a fictional company that runs a 600-seat theatre season and a summer music festival.

## Activity

Theatre and concert seasons, a three-day festival with bars, stands and sponsors, and season memberships.

## Size

14 permanent staff (the director, 3 event producers, 3 box-office agents, accounting, technical and
communication), about 40 intermittent performers and technicians a season and 150 volunteers at the festival; about
30,000 tickets a year. The sector's profile goes from one-off events to full seasons. Several locations are possible
([D32](../../06-decisions/technical/d32-several-locations.md)).

## Personas

- [Owner](../personas/u13-owner.md)
- [Event producer](../personas/u43-event-producer.md)
- [Box-office agent](../personas/u44-box-office-agent.md)
- [Volunteer](../personas/u32-volunteer.md)
- [Accounting](../personas/u05-accounting.md)
- [Customer (attendee, artist)](../personas/u09-customer.md)

## What SimpleERP must fit

Events and their budgets; venues and rooms; artist contracts and riders; intermittent performers' pay inputs; ticketing
with seat maps and price categories, access control by scanning, the box office on the day, cancellations and refunds;
accreditations, staff and volunteer shifts; sponsors and exhibitors; bars and merchandise; the safety file and register;
copyright royalties and the ticket tax; memberships and passes; attendee messages and reports after the event. From the
sector's coverage ([D362](../../06-decisions/sectors/d362-events-sector-coverage.md)), flows and record types.

Sector: [SC16 Events and culture](../../08-sectors/sc16-events-and-culture/README.md).
