# 📦 Wholesale and distribution

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Company

Distribution Lemoine, a fictional food and drinks wholesaler supplying restaurants, shops and supermarkets.

## Activity

Buying from producers and importers, storing in its warehouse and delivering to its customers; sales reps on
the road, retail chains ordering by EDI.

## Size

60 employees: 8 sales representatives, 4 inside sales people, 3 buyers, 22 warehouse operators, drivers,
accounting and the owner; one warehouse; about 6,000 items and 1,500 customers. The sector's profile covers wholesalers
of food, building, industrial and consumer goods. Several locations are possible
([D32](../../06-decisions/technical/d32-several-locations.md)).

## What SimpleERP must fit

Customer price agreements and tiers, rebates and year-end discounts, the yearly agreement with retailers; orders by EDI,
by reps on the road offline and in the B2B portal; back-orders, partial deliveries and drop-shipping; wave picking,
cross-docking, pallets and GS1 labels; returns and credit notes; supplier promotions and listing fees; imports and EU
statistics; supplier performance; reps' visits, routes and targets; margins per customer, product and rep; customer
credit limits. From the sector's coverage ([D359](../../06-decisions/sectors/d359-wholesale-sector-coverage.md)), flows
and record types.
