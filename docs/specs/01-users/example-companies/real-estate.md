# 🏘️ Real estate

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Company

(Proposed) Rive Sud Immobilier, a fictional estate agency that sells, lets and manages homes and manages co-ownerships.

## Activity

(Proposed) Sales and lettings of homes, rental management for landlords, and co-ownership management.

## Size

(Proposed) 16 employees: the owner, 5 real-estate agents, 4 property managers, 2 co-ownership managers, 2 accountants
and 2 assistants; two agencies; about 600 managed leases and 30 co-ownerships. The sector's profile goes from one agent
to networks. Several locations are possible ([D32](../../06-decisions/technical/d32-several-locations.md)).

## Personas

- [Owner](../personas/u13-owner.md)
- [Property manager](../personas/u34-property-manager.md)
- [Real-estate agent](../personas/u35-real-estate-agent.md)
- [Accounting](../personas/u05-accounting.md)
- [Customer (landlord, tenant, co-owner)](../personas/u09-customer.md)

## What simple-ERP must fit

Mandates numbered in the register, listings on portals, viewings, offers and agency fees; client funds under the
financial guarantee; leases, deposits, rent calls and receipts, indexation within the caps, yearly service charges,
landlord statements, diagnostics and unpaid rents; co-owners' assemblies, budgets, calls for funds, the works fund and
co-owners' accounts; a portal for landlords, tenants and co-owners. From the sector's coverage
([D344](../../06-decisions/sectors/d344-real-estate-sector-coverage.md)), flows and record types.

Sector: [SC10 Real estate](../../08-sectors/sc10-real-estate/README.md).
