# 💻 IT and software

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Company

(Proposed) Nuvola Logiciels, a fictional software company that sells an online booking software to small businesses
across the EU and runs an IT services team.

## Activity

(Proposed) A SaaS sold by subscription with plans and seats, usage billing, set-up and managed services.

## Size

(Proposed) 45 employees: 18 developers and consultants, 6 customer success managers, 5 sales people, a support team of
6, finance and the owners; about 2,500 subscribed customers in 9 EU countries. The sector's profile covers publishers,
SaaS companies and IT service providers. Several locations are possible
([D32](../../06-decisions/technical/d32-several-locations.md)).

## Personas

- [Owner](../personas/u13-owner.md)
- [Customer success manager](../personas/u41-customer-success-manager.md)

- [Sales](../personas/u03-sales.md)

## What SimpleERP must fit

Subscriptions with plans and seats, usage metering, subscription revenue recognition, EU VAT on digital services and
dunning; licences, keys and renewals, partners and resellers; customer health and churn risk; support with service
levels and managed services; sprints, release notes and features followed in the development tool; processor agreements,
the software bill of materials, vulnerabilities and incident reporting; data export and switching; the customer portal
and SaaS metrics. From the sector's coverage
([D356](../../06-decisions/sectors/d356-it-and-software-sector-coverage.md)), flows and record types.

Sector: [SC14 IT and software](../../08-sectors/sc14-it-and-software/README.md).
