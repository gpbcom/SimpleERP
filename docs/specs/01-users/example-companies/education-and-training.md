# 🎓 Education and training

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Company

(Proposed) Atelier Compétences, a fictional training provider for working adults that also runs a small private
vocational school.

## Activity

(Proposed) Short courses in management, office software and safety, in its rooms, at clients' sites and online; a
two-year vocational school course.

## Size

(Proposed) 9 permanent staff (the director, 3 training coordinators, an accountant, 4 in administration), 30 employed
and freelance trainers, about 1,800 learners a year and 120 school students; two training sites. The sector's profile
covers providers of 1 to 500 trainers. Several locations are possible
([D32](../../06-decisions/technical/d32-several-locations.md)).

## Personas

- [Owner (director)](../personas/u13-owner.md)
- [Training coordinator](../personas/u38-training-coordinator.md)

## What SimpleERP must fit

The course catalogue, sessions with trainers and rooms; enrolment with quotes, agreements and funding; invitations and
needs assessments; attendance signed per half-day, e-learning, assessments and certificates; satisfaction; billing
learners, employers and funders; the quality evidence file and the yearly report; school enrolment, classes, timetables,
grades, absences and school fees. From the sector's coverage
([D350](../../06-decisions/sectors/d350-education-sector-coverage.md)), flows and record types.
