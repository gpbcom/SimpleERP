# 🛍️ Retail

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Company

A shop chain.

## Activity

Selling goods in shops and online.

## Size

10 to 50 employees, several shops and a warehouse. Several locations are possible
([D32](../../06-decisions/technical/d32-several-locations.md)).

- [Owner](../personas/u13-owner.md)

- [Marketing and communication](../personas/u12-marketing.md)
- [Customer](../personas/u09-customer.md)

## What SimpleERP must fit

Stock per shop, point of sale, online shop, promotions.
