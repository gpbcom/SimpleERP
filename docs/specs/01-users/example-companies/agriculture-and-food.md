# 🌾 Agriculture and food

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Company

(Proposed) Ferme des Trois Chênes, a fictional family farm with a farm shop and a small cheese workshop.

## Activity

(Proposed) Cereals and fodder crops, a dairy herd, cheese made from the farm's milk, and sales at the farm shop, on
markets and to a cooperative.

## Size

(Proposed) 3 partners, 4 permanent workers and up to 12 seasonal workers at harvest; 180 hectares and 70 dairy cows; one
cheese workshop and one farm shop; often offline in the fields. The sector's profile covers farms of every size and
small food processors. Several locations are possible ([D32](../../06-decisions/technical/d32-several-locations.md)).

## Personas

- [Farmer](../personas/u40-farmer.md)

- [Accounting](../personas/u05-accounting.md)
- [Customer](../personas/u09-customer.md)

## What SimpleERP must fit

Plots and crop plans; treatment and fertilisation registers; harvests, yields and lots; the herd register and veterinary
treatments; CAP aid; machinery, fuel and maintenance; seasonal workers; direct sales and cooperative deliveries; organic
records; recipes and batches; traceability from field to customer; HACCP and hygiene records; labels, shelf life and
recalls; costs per crop, herd and product; working offline. From the sector's coverage
([D353](../../06-decisions/sectors/d353-agriculture-sector-coverage.md)), flows and record types.

Sector: [SC13 Agriculture and food](../../08-sectors/sc13-agriculture-and-food/README.md).
