# 🏠 Home care

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Company

A home-care service.

## Activity

Carers visiting elderly or disabled people at home.

## Size

30 to 250 employees, several local agencies. Several locations are possible
([D32](../../06-decisions/technical/d32-several-locations.md)).

## Personas

- [Owner](../personas/u13-owner.md)
- [Manager](../personas/u02-manager.md)
- [Care coordinator](../personas/u25-care-coordinator.md)
- [Carer](../personas/u26-carer.md)
- [Field worker](../personas/u14-field-worker.md)
- [Human resources](../personas/u06-hr.md)

## What SimpleERP must fit

Schedules of visits, carers' time and travel, labour rules, collective agreement.
