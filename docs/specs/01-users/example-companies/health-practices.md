# 🩺 Health practices

**Status:** 🔄 waiting for the owner

## Company

(Proposed) Maison de santé des Coteaux, a fictional multi-professional health centre in a mid-sized town, owned by its
practitioners.

## Activity

(Proposed) Consultations by general practitioners, a dentist, physiotherapists and nurses; appointments online and at
the desk, teleconsultation, care at the centre and at home.

## Size

(Proposed) 9 practitioners (4 general practitioners, 1 dentist, 2 physiotherapists, 2 nurses), 3 medical secretaries and
a part-time accountant; one building with 8 consulting rooms; about 120 appointments a day. The sector's profile covers
practices of 1 to 50 practitioners. Several locations are possible
([D32](../../06-decisions/technical/d32-several-locations.md)).

## Personas

- [Owner (practitioner)](../personas/u13-owner.md)
- [Medical secretary](../personas/u33-medical-secretary.md)
- [Accounting](../personas/u05-accounting.md)
- [Customer (patient)](../personas/u09-customer.md)

## What SimpleERP must fit

Appointments online and at the desk with reminders and no-shows; patient files, the clinical record and prescriptions;
billing to health insurance with the Carte Vitale, third-party payment and insurers' returns; the national health
record, secure messaging and results; rooms, equipment, sterilisation, shared costs and locums; patients' rights,
certified modules and health data on certified hosting. From the sector's coverage
([D341](../../06-decisions/sectors/d341-health-practices-sector-coverage.md)), flows and record types.

Sector: [SC09 Health practices](../../08-sectors/sc09-health-practices/README.md).
