# 🏗️ Construction

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Company

A building company working on several construction sites.

## Activity

Building work for private and public customers.

## Size

20 to 100 employees, a head office and a yard. Several locations are possible
([D32](../../06-decisions/technical/d32-several-locations.md)).

## What SimpleERP must fit

Quotes with many lines, purchases per site, time per site, subcontractors.
