# ⚖️ Professional firms

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Company

Delorme Conseil, a fictional firm of business lawyers and chartered accountants.

## Activity

Business law advice and litigation, and bookkeeping, tax filings and advice for small companies.

## Size

22 people: 3 partners, 12 professionals (lawyers and accountants), 5 assistants and 2 in the back office; two
offices; about 400 open matters and 250 accounting clients. The sector's profile covers firms of 1 to 500 professionals.
Several locations are possible ([D32](../../06-decisions/technical/d32-several-locations.md)).

## Personas

- [Owner (managing partner)](../personas/u13-owner.md)
- [Professional](../personas/u36-professional.md)
- [Professional's assistant](../personas/u37-assistant.md)
- [Accounting](../personas/u05-accounting.md)
- [Customer (client)](../personas/u09-customer.md)

## What SimpleERP must fit

Client intake with identity, beneficial owner and conflict checks; engagement letters and fee agreements; matters and
documents with versions and e-signature; time and disbursements, fee bills, advances and trust accounts; deadlines,
hearings and limitation periods; accountants' client portfolios, tax calendar and filings; anti-money-laundering
vigilance, professional secrecy with access walls; partners' targets and profit shares, continuing training and the
firm's performance. From the sector's coverage
([D347](../../06-decisions/sectors/d347-professional-firms-sector-coverage.md)), flows and record types.

Sector: [SC11 Professional firms](../../08-sectors/sc11-professional-firms/README.md).
