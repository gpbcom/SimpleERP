# 👤 U48 Hosting engineer

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

An engineer of the publisher's hosted edition (D452): approves the support assistant's fixes on customers' namespaces,
runs incidents and the status page, watches domains, DNS and certificates, reviews and signs the feature factory's
features before the hosted store, follows the platform's costs, and runs the DevOps agents' isolated runners and their
rights.

## Company examples

The publisher's hosting team. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Keep every hosted customer running.
- Approve safe fixes fast.
- Ship only reviewed, signed features.

## Pains

- Vague problem reports.
- Fixes that touch several customers.
- Costs that run away.

## Daily tasks

- Approve or refuse proposed fixes.
- Follow incidents and alerts.
- Review generated features.

## Monthly tasks

- Promotions to the hosted store.
- Renewals of domains and certificates.
- Platform costs and quality reviews.

## Devices

A laptop with the operations console; a phone for on-call approvals.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
