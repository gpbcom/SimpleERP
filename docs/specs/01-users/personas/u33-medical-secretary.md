# 👤 U33 Medical secretary

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Runs the reception of a practice: appointments and the phone, patient registration and insurance cards, billing to
patients and health insurance, rejections, mail and documents, under professional secrecy.

## Company examples

Medical, dental, physiotherapy and nursing practices, health centres. See the
[example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- A full, well-kept schedule.
- Every act billed and paid.

## Pains

- The phone ringing all day.
- No-shows.
- Insurance rejections to chase.

## Daily tasks

- Book and move appointments.
- Register and bill patients.
- Handle rejections and mail.

## Monthly tasks

- Practice statistics, cost sharing between partners.

## Devices

A desktop at the reception with a card reader; a headset.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
