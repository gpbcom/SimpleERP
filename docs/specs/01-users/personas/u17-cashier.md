# 👤 U17 Cashier

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Serves customers at the shop counter or on the move: scans, takes payment, gives receipts, handles returns.

## Company examples

Retail, bakery, garden centre, market stall, events. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Serve the queue fast, with no mistake in the till.
- Close the till with no difference.

## Pains

- Slow card terminals and network cuts.
- Price and promotion errors at the till.
- Cash differences at closing.

## Daily tasks

- Open the till and count the float.
- Sell, take payment, refund or exchange.
- Close the till.

## Monthly tasks

- Take part in the monthly closing checks.

## Devices

A touchscreen till, a tablet or a phone, with a scanner, a printer, a cash drawer, a card terminal and sometimes a
scale.

The touchscreen till is drawn at the desktop size, 1440 x 900 px
([D456](../../06-decisions/ux/d456-audit-images-and-criteria.md)).

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
