# 👤 U03 Sales

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Finds and serves customers: contacts, quotes, orders, follow-up.

## Company examples

Construction, retail, services, manufacturing, transport. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Send a correct quote fast, even at the customer's site.
- Know a customer's whole history.

## Pains

- Quotes rebuilt by hand in a spreadsheet.
- Not knowing stock or delivery dates when quoting.

## Daily tasks

- Update contacts and opportunities.
- Write and send quotes.
- Turn accepted quotes into orders.

## Monthly tasks

- Review their pipeline and results.

## Devices

Desktop at the office; phone or tablet on the road. [D34](../../06-decisions/ux/d34-phone-experience.md)

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
