# 👤 U09 Customer

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

A customer of the client company, outside it.

## Company examples

All companies that sell. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Accept a quote and pay in a few taps.
- Find their documents again.

## Pains

- PDFs lost in email.
- No simple way to pay online.

## Daily tasks

- Open a document from an email link.

## Monthly tasks

- Look at their history in the portal, if they have an account.

## Devices

Phone first. [D34](../../06-decisions/ux/d34-phone-experience.md)

## Skill level

Any member of the public: the portal needs no account to open a document.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
