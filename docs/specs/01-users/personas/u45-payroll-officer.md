# 👤 U45 Payroll officer

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Runs the monthly pay: collects pay inputs, computes and checks the pay run, issues payslips, files the monthly social
declaration, pays employees and contributions, handles corrections and end-of-contract documents.

## Company examples

Companies with an in-house payroll, payroll service bureaus, accounting firms. See the
[example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Every employee paid right and on time.
- Declarations accepted the first time.

## Pains

- Inputs arriving late.
- Rates changing twice a year.
- Rejections to understand.

## Daily tasks

- Collect inputs, check anomalies.
- Answer employees' questions.

## Monthly tasks

- The pay run, the declaration, payments, accounting entries, yearly summaries.

## Devices

A desktop.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
