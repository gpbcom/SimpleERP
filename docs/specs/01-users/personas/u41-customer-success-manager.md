# 👤 U41 Customer success manager

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Follows software customers after the sale: onboarding, usage and health, renewals and expansion, churn risks, and the
features customers wait for.

## Company examples

Software publishers, SaaS companies, IT services. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Customers who renew and grow.
- Know a risk before the customer leaves.

## Pains

- Usage data in another tool.
- Renewals noticed too late.
- Feature requests lost in tickets.

## Daily tasks

- Review health scores.
- Call at-risk accounts.
- Prepare renewals.

## Monthly tasks

- Quarterly reviews, churn and expansion figures.

## Devices

A laptop; a phone between calls.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
