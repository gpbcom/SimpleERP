# 👤 U49 Statutory auditor

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

An auditor, from an audit firm, who certifies the accounts of a client company: reads the books, asks for documents and
explanations, takes samples of entries and invoices, and writes the audit findings. They work for the firm, not for the
audited company, and are invited for an engagement.

## Company examples

Audit firms and the companies they audit. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- See every book and its supporting document without asking for exports.
- Send requests and receive answers with the evidence, in one place.
- Prove which entries and documents were sampled.

## Pains

- Requests by email that get lost.
- Exports that do not match the ledger.
- Waiting for access at the start of every engagement.

## Daily tasks

- Read ledgers, entries and documents.
- Draw samples and test them.
- Send and follow requests to the client.

## Monthly tasks

- Interim and year-end visits.
- Findings and the management letter.
- Confirmation of balances with third parties.

## Devices

A laptop with two screens at the audit firm or the client's office; the desktop is the main device.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.

## Default role

Statutory auditor: a read-only role with no right to change a record, granted by the owner of the audited
company for a dated engagement, to be added to the [roles](../roles.md).
