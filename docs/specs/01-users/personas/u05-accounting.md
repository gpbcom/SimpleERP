# 👤 U05 Accounting

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Keeps the books in-house: invoices, payments, VAT, bank, exports to the external accountant.

## Company examples

All of them; in a small company often the owner or an office worker. See the
[example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Close each month without retyping.
- Give the external accountant clean exports.

## Pains

- Bank lines matched by hand.
- Missing supporting documents.

## Daily tasks

- Record and match payments.
- Send reminders for unpaid invoices.

## Monthly tasks

- Prepare the VAT return.
- Close the month and export the FEC.

## Devices

Desktop.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
