# 👤 U30 Driver

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Drives the trips: loading and unloading stops, scans, consignment notes and proofs of delivery, incidents, fuel and
tolls, often offline.

## Company examples

Road hauliers and couriers. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Know the next stop and what to load.
- No paperwork at the end of the day.

## Pains

- Changes not passed on.
- Waiting not recorded.
- Lost delivery papers.

## Daily tasks

- Follow the trip.
- Scan and sign at each stop.
- Report incidents and waiting.

## Monthly tasks

- Training renewals, vehicle checks.

## Devices

A phone, often offline; the truck's tachograph.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
