# 👤 U32 Volunteer

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Gives time to an association: signs up for missions and events, records hours and expenses, receives news.

## Company examples

Associations, clubs, foundations. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Find a mission that fits their time.
- Be repaid quickly.

## Pains

- Sign-ups by email chains.
- Expenses forms on paper.

## Daily tasks

- Sign up for a mission.
- Record hours.

## Monthly tasks

- Expenses, news.

## Devices

A phone.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
