# 👤 U02 Manager

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Leads a team, a department or a site, and approves what their team asks for.

## Company examples

All of them; one per site in companies with several locations (D32). See the
[example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Approve requests quickly, from anywhere.
- See how their team and site are doing.

## Pains

- Approvals stuck in email.
- No up-to-date view of workload, absences and costs.

## Daily tasks

- Approve time off, expenses, purchases and quotes above a limit.
- Read their dashboard.

## Monthly tasks

- Review the team's timesheets and project profitability.
- Prepare reviews.

## Devices

Desktop at the office, phone on the move. [D34](../../06-decisions/ux/d34-phone-experience.md)

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
