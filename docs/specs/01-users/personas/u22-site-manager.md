# 👤 U22 Site manager

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Runs several construction sites (conducteur de travaux): plans phases, crews, equipment and subcontractors, orders
materials, follows progress and costs, prepares progress bills, leads acceptance and reservations.

## Company examples

Construction and renovation companies, building trades. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Finish each site on time and within budget.
- Know each site's state without driving to it.

## Pains

- Crews and materials in the wrong place.
- Progress known only at the month's end.
- Reservations that never get lifted.

## Daily tasks

- Visit sites, read the diaries and photos.
- Adjust crews, equipment and orders.
- Answer customers and subcontractors.

## Monthly tasks

- Progress bills, subcontractor payments, site margins.

## Devices

A phone and a tablet on site, often offline; a laptop at the office.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
