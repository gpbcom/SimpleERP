# 👤 U37 Professional's assistant

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Supports professionals: opens client files and identity checks, keeps the deadline diary, prepares documents and bills,
files the mail, under the same secrecy.

## Company examples

Law firms, accounting firms, architecture offices. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Every file complete and every deadline in the diary.
- Bills out on time.

## Pains

- Missing identity documents.
- Deadlines in several diaries.

## Daily tasks

- Open files, check identities.
- Update the diary.
- Prepare bills.

## Monthly tasks

- Disbursement statements, archives.

## Devices

A desktop.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
