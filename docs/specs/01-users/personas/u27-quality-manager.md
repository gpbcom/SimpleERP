# 👤 U27 Quality manager

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Runs the quality system of a manufacturer: controlled documents, internal audits, corrective and preventive actions,
supplier quality, customer complaints, calibration, staff qualifications and the management review, often for an ISO
9001 certification.

## Company examples

Manufacturers and food processors. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Pass the certification audit.
- Fix causes, not symptoms.

## Pains

- Documents out of date on the shop floor.
- Actions that never close.
- Evidence scattered before an audit.

## Daily tasks

- Follow open actions and complaints.
- Approve document changes.
- Check calibrations due.

## Monthly tasks

- Internal audits, supplier reviews, quality indicators, the management review.

## Devices

A laptop; a tablet on the shop floor for audits.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
