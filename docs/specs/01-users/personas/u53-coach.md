# 👤 U53 Coach

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

A sports and fitness professional: runs group classes and personal training sessions, takes the attendance, follows each
member's progress and sells sessions and memberships.

## Company examples

Gyms, studios, sports clubs, personal trainers. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md); the
sector's own example company is written with the sector.

## Goals

- See who is booked in each class.
- Record attendance in seconds.
- Follow a member's progress and renewals.

## Pains

- No-shows and last-minute cancellations.
- Members whose membership ran out unnoticed.
- Paper attendance lists.

## Daily tasks

- Read the day's classes and sessions.
- Take attendance.
- Note a member's progress.

## Monthly tasks

- Plan the next month's classes.
- Follow memberships that end.

## Devices

A phone, in the gym or on the field.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
