# 👤 U55 Elected official

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

A member of a council or a board who votes the budget, approves awards of public contracts and decisions, and follows
what the public body spends; they sit on a part-time basis and rely on clear documents.

## Company examples

Councillors, board members. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md); the sector's own example
company is written with the sector.

## Goals

- Understand the budget and its follow-up without a finance background.
- Vote with the documents in hand.
- Know what was decided and what it cost.

## Pains

- Long documents sent late.
- Figures that differ between documents.
- No way to see an award's reasons.

## Daily tasks

- Nothing: they use the system around meetings.

## Monthly tasks

- Read the budget follow-up.
- Vote the decisions of the meeting.
- Approve the award of a contract.

## Devices

A desktop or laptop at home or at the town hall.

## Skill level

Occasional user with no ERP experience: the screens need no training.
[D33 Design for an office worker](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)

## Default role

Elected official: reads the budget and the decisions of their body and votes, to be added to the
[roles](../roles.md) with the public bodies sector pack.
