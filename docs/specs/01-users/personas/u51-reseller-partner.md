# 👤 U51 Reseller partner

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

An outside company or person that sells the company's products or services and earns a commission: receives the leads
the company forwards, registers the leads they find, follows the quotes and sees their commissions.

## Company examples

Resellers, agents, referrers, integrators. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Receive qualified leads fast.
- Know which deals are theirs and what they earn.
- Be paid on time with a clear statement.

## Pains

- Two partners claiming the same lead.
- Commission statements that arrive late or unexplained.
- No view of the company's price lists and stock.

## Daily tasks

- Follow forwarded leads and quotes.
- Register a new lead.

## Monthly tasks

- Read the commission statement.
- Invoice the commission when the programme asks.

## Devices

A laptop in their own office; the portal is the only screen they use.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33 Design for an office worker](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)

- [F6 Customers and sales](../../02-functional/f06-customers-and-sales/README.md)
- [F11 Online shop](../../02-functional/f11-online-shop/README.md)

## Default role

Reseller partner: a portal account that sees only the leads, quotes and commissions of the partner, to be
added to the [roles](../roles.md).
