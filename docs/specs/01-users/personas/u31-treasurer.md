# 👤 U31 Treasurer

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Keeps an association's money in order, often as a volunteer board member: dues, donations and tax receipts, grants and
their reports, expenses, the accounts and their approval by the assembly.

## Company examples

Associations of every size, foundations. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Accounts the assembly approves without questions.
- Every donor thanked and receipted on time.

## Pains

- Little time, as a volunteer.
- Grant reports with different formats.
- Receipts done by hand.

## Daily tasks

- Record dues and gifts.
- Pay expenses.
- Answer the funders.

## Monthly tasks

- Yearly receipts, accounts, grant reports, the assembly.

## Devices

A laptop at home; a phone for approvals.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
