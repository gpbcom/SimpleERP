# 👤 U39 Trainer

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Teaches sessions or classes: takes attendance, follows the programme, assesses learners and enters grades.

## Company examples

Training providers, schools. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Teach, not fill forms.
- Know who attends and what they need.

## Pains

- Paper attendance sheets.
- Assessment forms in many formats.

## Daily tasks

- Teach; take attendance.
- Assess, enter grades.

## Monthly tasks

- Availability, programme updates.

## Devices

A tablet or laptop in the room; a phone.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
