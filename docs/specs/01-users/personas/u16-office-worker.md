# 👤 U16 Office worker

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Does administrative work, on site or remotely: answering customers, entering documents, filing.

## Company examples

All of them. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Work the same way at the office and at home.

## Pains

- Tools that only work at the office.
- Documents scattered across email and folders.

## Daily tasks

- Enter and file documents.
- Answer customers and suppliers.

## Monthly tasks

- Help with month-end tasks.

## Devices

Desktop at the office or at home; phone for self-service. [D34](../../06-decisions/ux/d34-phone-experience.md) Remote
work over the internet with two-factor authentication.
[D37](../../06-decisions/security-and-compliance/d37-remote-access.md)

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
