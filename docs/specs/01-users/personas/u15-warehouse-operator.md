# 👤 U15 Warehouse operator

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Receives goods, picks orders and counts stock.

## Company examples

Retail, manufacturing, construction, transport. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Know what to receive and what to pick, in order.

## Pains

- Paper picking lists.
- Stock counts that never match.

## Daily tasks

- Receive deliveries.
- Pick and pack orders.

## Monthly tasks

- Count stock.

## Devices

A handheld scanner, a tablet or a phone.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
