# 👤 U44 Box-office agent

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Sells and checks tickets: at the box office, on the phone and on the day, exchanges and refunds, access control and
accreditation desk.

## Company examples

Venues, theatres, festivals, museums. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Short queues.
- No fake or double tickets.

## Pains

- Seat maps on paper.
- Refunds by hand after a cancellation.

## Daily tasks

- Sell and print.
- Scan at the door.
- Answer questions.

## Monthly tasks

- Season subscriptions, memberships.

## Devices

A desktop at the box office; a scanner phone at the door.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
