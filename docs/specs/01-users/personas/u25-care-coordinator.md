# 👤 U25 Care coordinator

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Takes on care recipients, assesses their needs, writes their care plans, plans the carers' rounds, finds replacements
and follows quality and complaints.

## Company examples

Home-help and home-care services, associations and companies. See the
[example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Every planned visit done by a suitable carer.
- Rounds that waste no time on the road.

## Pains

- Last-minute absences.
- Families calling to know who comes.
- Funders' paperwork.

## Daily tasks

- Fix today's absences.
- Plan next week's rounds.
- Visit a new recipient.

## Monthly tasks

- Reports to funders, care-plan reviews, carers' training.

## Devices

A laptop at the office; a phone on call.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
