# 👤 U28 Hotel receptionist

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Works the front desk of a hotel: bookings, check-in and check-out, the guest's folio with room, restaurant and bar, the
tourist tax, payments, and requests to housekeeping.

## Company examples

Hotels, guest houses, holiday residences. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Check guests in and out without queues.
- Every night and extra billed.

## Pains

- Overbookings from several channels.
- Bar bills forgotten at check-out.
- Rooms not ready on arrival.

## Daily tasks

- Prepare arrivals.
- Check guests in and out.
- Answer requests, take bookings.

## Monthly tasks

- Close the month's tourist tax, night audits.

## Devices

A desktop at the front desk; a tablet for check-in in the lobby.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
