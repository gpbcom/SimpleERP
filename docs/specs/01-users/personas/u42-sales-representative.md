# 👤 U42 Sales representative

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Visits customers on a route: takes orders on a tablet, often offline, presents promotions, checks prices, credit and
stock, and follows targets.

## Company examples

Wholesalers and distributors of food, building materials, industrial supplies. See the
[example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Visit the right customers and sell more.
- Promise only what can be delivered.

## Pains

- No signal in shops and warehouses.
- Prices and promotions out of date.
- Orders retyped at the office.

## Daily tasks

- Visit customers, take orders.
- Check stock and credit.
- Report visits.

## Monthly tasks

- Targets, route planning, customer reviews.

## Devices

A tablet and a phone, often offline; a laptop at home.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
