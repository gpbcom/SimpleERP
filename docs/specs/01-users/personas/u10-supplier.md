# 👤 U10 Supplier

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

A supplier of the client company, outside it.

## Company examples

All companies that buy. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Confirm orders and delivery dates quickly.
- Get paid on time.

## Pains

- Orders by email with no structure.

## Daily tasks

- Open a purchase order from an email link and confirm it.

## Monthly tasks

- Send invoices.
- Look at their history in the portal, if they have an account.

## Devices

Phone first. [D34](../../06-decisions/ux/d34-phone-experience.md)

## Skill level

Any member of the public: the portal needs no account to open a document.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
