# 👤 U04 Purchasing and stock

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Buys from suppliers and keeps stock right.

## Company examples

Retail, manufacturing, construction, hospitality, transport. See the
[example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Order the right quantity at the right time.
- Compare suppliers.

## Pains

- Stock-outs found too late.
- Supplier invoices that do not match the order.

## Daily tasks

- Create purchase orders.
- Check receipts against orders.
- Follow late deliveries.

## Monthly tasks

- Review supplier performance.
- Plan seasonal purchases.

## Devices

Desktop.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
