# 👤 U24 Engagement manager

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Runs client engagements in a consulting firm or an agency: scope and statement of work, staffing, budget, time and
expense approval, billing and the client relationship.

## Company examples

Consulting firms, digital and creative agencies, engineering offices. See the
[example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Deliver what was sold, on time and within budget.
- Keep consultants busy on the right work.

## Pains

- Time entered late or not at all.
- Overruns found at month end.
- Chasing the client for approvals.

## Daily tasks

- Approve time and expenses.
- Adjust staffing.
- Talk to the client, update the plan.

## Monthly tasks

- Bill the month, review margin and work in progress, client satisfaction.

## Devices

A laptop; a phone for approvals between meetings.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
