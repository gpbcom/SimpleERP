# 👤 U35 Real-estate agent

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Sells and lets properties: mandates, listings on portals, viewings, offers, sale agreements and fees.

## Company examples

Estate agencies. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Sign mandates and close sales.
- Never miss a buyer's call.

## Pains

- Listings to update on every portal.
- Viewings rescheduled.
- Paperwork for each offer.

## Daily tasks

- Visits, viewings, calls.
- Update listings.
- Follow offers.

## Monthly tasks

- Mandates register review, fees and commissions.

## Devices

A phone and a tablet on the move; a laptop.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
