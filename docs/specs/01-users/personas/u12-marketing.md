# 👤 U12 Marketing and communication

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Communicates for the company: social networks, posts, videos, campaigns.

## Company examples

Retail, hospitality, services, non-profit. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Publish consistent posts on every network from one place.
- Know what works.

## Pains

- One tool per network.
- Posts approved by email.

## Daily tasks

- Write and schedule posts.
- Answer comments.

## Monthly tasks

- Plan the editorial calendar.
- Report on statistics.

## Devices

Desktop and phone.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
