# 👤 U56 Citizen

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

A resident who deals with a public body: sends a request or a complaint, follows its answer, books a hall or equipment,
pays a fee online and reads the published decisions.

## Company examples

Residents of a council's area. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md); the sector's own example
company is written with the sector.

## Goals

- Send a request in a few minutes and know when it will be answered.
- Book a hall and pay online.
- Read what the council decided.

## Pains

- Opening hours that do not fit.
- Not knowing who handles a request.
- Paper forms and cheques.

## Daily tasks

- Nothing: they use the portal when they need it.

## Monthly tasks

- Send a request.
- Book a hall.
- Pay a fee.

## Devices

A phone.

## Skill level

Everyday phone user, with no ERP experience: the portal needs no training.
[D33 Design for an office worker](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)

## Capabilities used

- [F11 Online shop](../../02-functional/f11-online-shop/README.md)
- [F6 Customers and sales](../../02-functional/f06-customers-and-sales/README.md)

## Default role

Citizen: a portal account that sees only the person's own requests, bookings and payments, to be added to the
[roles](../roles.md) with the public bodies sector pack.
