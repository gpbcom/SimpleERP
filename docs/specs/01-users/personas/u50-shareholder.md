# 👤 U50 Shareholder

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

A person or company that holds shares of the company: sees their holding, the share register and the documents of the
general meetings, receives the convocation and votes, before or at the meeting, from their phone.

## Company examples

Founders, business angels, employees with shares, family holders. See the
[example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- See their holding and its history.
- Receive the convocation and the documents on time.
- Vote without travelling.

## Pains

- Paper convocations and proxies.
- Not knowing their dilution after a round.
- Unclear results of a vote.

## Daily tasks

- Nothing: they use the portal a few times a year.

## Monthly tasks

- Read the company's reports when published.
- Vote at a general meeting.
- Sign a share transfer or a subscription.

## Devices

A phone, for the convocation, the documents and the vote; a laptop to sign.

## Skill level

Everyday phone and email user, with no ERP experience: the portal needs no training.
[D33 Design for an office worker](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)

## Capabilities used

- [F15 Legal assistant](../../02-functional/f15-legal-assistant/README.md)
- [F8 Accounting](../../02-functional/f08-accounting/README.md)

## Default role

Shareholder: a portal account that sees only the holder's own holdings, documents and votes, to be added to
the [roles](../roles.md).
