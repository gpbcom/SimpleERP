# 👤 U38 Training coordinator

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Runs a training provider's or a school's organisation: catalogue, sessions, enrolments, funding, invitations,
attendance, certificates, the quality file and the yearly report.

## Company examples

Training providers, private schools, company academies. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Full sessions, every learner funded.
- A quality file ready for the audit.

## Pains

- Funding files that differ by funder.
- Attendance sheets on paper.
- The yearly report by hand.

## Daily tasks

- Enrol learners, chase funding.
- Send invitations.
- Check attendance.

## Monthly tasks

- Session planning, the yearly report, quality reviews.

## Devices

A desktop.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
