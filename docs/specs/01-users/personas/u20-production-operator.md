# 👤 U20 Production operator

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Makes the products on the shop floor: follows the work instructions of each operation, records quantities, times, scrap
and quality measures, reports a machine stop.

## Company examples

Manufacturing, food processing, workshops, construction prefabrication. See the
[example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Know what to make next and how.
- Record the work without leaving the machine.

## Pains

- Paper instructions out of date.
- Typing the same counts twice.
- Waiting for components or a repair.

## Daily tasks

- Start and finish operations.
- Record quantities, scrap, lots and checks.
- Report a stop or a defect.

## Monthly tasks

- Take part in safety and quality reviews.

## Devices

A shared shop-floor tablet or a terminal at the work centre, a badge or PIN to sign in, a scanner; sometimes gloves, so
large touch targets.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
