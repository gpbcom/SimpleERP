# 👤 U07 Administrator

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Sets the company up in SimpleERP: users, roles, sites, configuration, connected tools and AI providers.

## Company examples

All of them; in a small company the owner or a trusted employee. See the
[example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Set SimpleERP up without a consultant.
- Adapt it to the company's field without a developer.

## Pains

- Complex configuration screens.
- Fear of breaking something.

## Daily tasks

- Add and remove users.
- Answer access problems.

## Monthly tasks

- Adjust roles, forms and workflows.
- Install plugins and connect external tools.

## Devices

Desktop.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
