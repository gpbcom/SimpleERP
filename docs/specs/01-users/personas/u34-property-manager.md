# 👤 U34 Property manager

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Manages rented homes for landlords and co-ownerships for co-owners: leases, rent calls, receipts, deposits, indexation,
charges, landlord statements, assemblies, budgets, calls for funds and works.

## Company examples

Property management and co-ownership firms. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Every rent collected and every landlord paid on time.
- Assemblies held and works done.

## Pains

- Unpaid rents.
- Yearly charge settlements by hand.
- Co-owners' questions all day.

## Daily tasks

- Follow rents and unpaid ones.
- Answer tenants and co-owners.
- Order repairs.

## Monthly tasks

- Landlord statements, indexations, charge settlements, assemblies.

## Devices

A desktop; a tablet for inventories and building visits.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
