# 👤 U18 Waiter

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Serves tables in a restaurant, café or bar: seats guests, takes orders, sends them to the kitchen, splits bills and
takes payment at the table.

## Company examples

Restaurants, cafés, bars, hotels, events. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Take orders quickly and send them to the right station.
- Know which table waits for what.

## Pains

- Orders lost between the floor and the kitchen.
- Splitting a bill by hand.
- Guests waiting to pay.

## Daily tasks

- Seat guests and take orders.
- Follow courses and send the next one.
- Split and take payment at the table.

## Monthly tasks

- None specific.

## Devices

A phone or a small tablet, a payment terminal brought to the table.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
