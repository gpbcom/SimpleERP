# 👤 U54 Public finance officer

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

An officer of a council or another public body who prepares the budget, commits expenditure against voted credits,
issues payment orders and revenue titles and exchanges with the public treasury.

## Company examples

Councils, public agencies, inter-municipal bodies. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md); the
sector's own example company is written with the sector.

## Goals

- Spend only within voted credits.
- Pay suppliers within the legal term.
- Give the elected officials a clear budget follow-up.

## Pains

- Commitments made outside the system.
- Treasury files rejected for format.
- Budget changes that nobody can trace.

## Daily tasks

- Commit and check credits.
- Issue payment orders.
- Answer the treasury's rejections.

## Monthly tasks

- Budget follow-up for the elected officials.
- Preparation of the next budget.

## Devices

A desktop at the town hall, with two screens.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
