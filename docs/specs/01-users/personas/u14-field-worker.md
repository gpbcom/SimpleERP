# 👤 U14 Field worker

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Works away from a desk: technician, carer, driver, on-site staff.

## Company examples

Construction, home care, transport, hospitality, manufacturing. See the
[example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- See their schedule and their tasks on the phone.
- Record their time without paperwork.

## Pains

- Paper timesheets.
- Schedule changes by phone call.

## Daily tasks

- Check today's schedule.
- Record time and interventions.

## Monthly tasks

- Request time off.
- Download their payslip.

## Devices

Phone only. [D34](../../06-decisions/ux/d34-phone-experience.md) Field tasks work offline.
[D38](../../06-decisions/ux/d38-offline-field-tasks.md)

## Skill level

Rare software user on the phone screens (today, time, requests): one task per screen, large touch targets, icons with
words, no jargon. See [D50](../../06-decisions/ux/d50-field-screens-for-rare-users.md).
