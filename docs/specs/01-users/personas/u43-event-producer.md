# 👤 U43 Event producer

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Runs events end to end: budget, venue, artists and their riders, ticketing, staff and volunteers, sponsors and
exhibitors, safety, declarations and the after-event report.

## Company examples

Event organisers, venues, theatres, festivals, museums. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- A safe, sold-out event within budget.
- Every declaration done on time.

## Pains

- Budgets in spreadsheets.
- Artists' riders lost in emails.
- Declarations after each show.

## Daily tasks

- Follow sales and the budget.
- Confirm artists and suppliers.
- Plan staff.

## Monthly tasks

- Declarations, reports, next season.

## Devices

A laptop; a phone on site.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
