# 👤 U47 SimpleERP maintainer

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

A maintainer of the SimpleERP repository
([D452](../../06-decisions/ux/d452-repository-maintainer-and-hosting-engineer.md)): reviews and approves pull requests,
the DevOps agents' included, removes the needs-maintainer-approval label, merges and releases. With the project's move
to a foundation (D376), maintainers come from several companies; running the hosted edition is the
[U48 Hosting engineer](u48-hosting-engineer.md)'s job.

## Company examples

The project's maintainers, the publisher's among them. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Merge only reviewed, tested changes.
- Keep the agents' work useful and safe.
- Release on time.

## Pains

- Vague requests.
- Large pull requests.
- Agent work that needs rework.

## Daily tasks

- Review pull requests and agent tickets.
- Approve or refuse agent work.
- Answer contributors.

## Monthly tasks

- Releases and their notes.
- The agents' quality review.
- The yearly review of the connector catalogue.

## Devices

A laptop; a phone for approvals.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)

- [F25 DevOps agents](../../02-functional/f25-devops-agents/README.md)
- [F24 Integrations hub](../../02-functional/f24-integrations-hub/README.md)

SimpleERP maintainer, in the [roles](../roles.md).
