# 👤 U29 Dispatcher

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Plans and follows trips: takes transport orders, builds trips and routes, assigns vehicles and drivers within driving
times, charters other carriers, follows deliveries and incidents.

## Company examples

Road hauliers, couriers, removal and logistics companies. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Every load moved on time at the lowest cost.
- Drivers within their legal times.

## Pains

- Last-minute orders and cancellations.
- Empty return trips.
- Waiting at loading docks.

## Daily tasks

- Plan tomorrow's trips.
- Follow today's deliveries.
- Handle incidents and waiting time.

## Monthly tasks

- Fleet costs, subcontracting, driver documents due.

## Devices

A desktop with two screens; a phone on call.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
