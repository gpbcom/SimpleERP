# 👤 U01 Employee

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Any employee of a client company, whatever their job: every employee is a user (D14).

## Company examples

All of them. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Do their own HR tasks without asking anyone: time off, timesheets, expenses, payslips.
- Find an answer about their work or their rights quickly.

## Pains

- Paper forms and emails for time off and expenses.
- Not knowing their remaining days off.

## Daily tasks

- Record their time.
- Read the meeting summaries they were part of.
- Ask the AI assistant.

## Monthly tasks

- Request time off.
- Submit expenses.
- Download their payslip.

## Devices

A phone for everything self-service, and a desktop when their job has one.
[D34](../../06-decisions/ux/d34-phone-experience.md)

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
