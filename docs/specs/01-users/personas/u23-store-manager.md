# 👤 U23 Store manager

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Runs one store: its stock and orders, transfers, labels and sales periods, the staff schedule and Sunday openings, the
targets, the customers and the visual merchandising.

## Company examples

Fashion, home, sports, food and specialist retail chains, franchises and independent shops. See the
[example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Have the right product on the shelf at the right price.
- Hit the store's targets with the right staff.

## Pains

- Stock-outs while another store has too much.
- Labels out of date after a price change.
- Schedules rebuilt by hand every week.

## Daily tasks

- Open the store and read yesterday's figures.
- Receive, transfer and count stock.
- Coach the team, serve key customers.

## Monthly tasks

- Targets review, inventory, sales periods, schedules for the next month.

## Devices

A tablet or phone on the shop floor, the till, a laptop in the back office.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
