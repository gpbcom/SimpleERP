# 👤 U08 External accountant

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

The company's chartered accountant (expert-comptable), outside the company.

## Company examples

Every company that uses one. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Get the books and exports without asking for them.

## Pains

- Incomplete or late documents from the client.

## Daily tasks

- Read entries and supporting documents.

## Monthly tasks

- Download the FEC and other exports.
- Check the VAT and year-end closing.

## Devices

Desktop.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
