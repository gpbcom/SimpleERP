# 👤 U46 Plugin publisher

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

An outside developer or company who builds and sells SimpleERP plugins and sector packs: submits versions, passes the
checks and the security review, sets prices, follows installs, revenue and reviews, and gets paid.

## Company examples

Software houses, integrators, independent developers, IT providers. See the
[example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Get plugins approved fast.
- Reach customers and get paid on time.

## Pains

- Long manual reviews.
- Unclear ranking.
- Payouts hard to reconcile.

## Daily tasks

- Fix and submit versions.
- Answer reviews.

## Monthly tasks

- Payouts and statements, pricing.

## Devices

A laptop with the developer tools.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)

- [T4 Plugin runtime](../../03-technical/t04-plugin-runtime/README.md)

Plugin publisher, in the [roles](../roles.md).
