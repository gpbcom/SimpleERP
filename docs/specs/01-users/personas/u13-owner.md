# 👤 U13 Owner

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Owns and runs the company; in a small company also does accounting, HR or administration.

## Company examples

All of them. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- See the company's health at a glance.
- Know the company's legal obligations.

## Pains

- Too many tools.
- Legal rules found too late.

## Daily tasks

- Read the dashboards.
- Approve what needs their approval.

## Monthly tasks

- Review results with the external accountant.
- Decide investments and hiring.

## Devices

Phone and desktop. [D34](../../06-decisions/ux/d34-phone-experience.md)

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
