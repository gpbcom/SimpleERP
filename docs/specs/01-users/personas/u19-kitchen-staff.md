# 👤 U19 Kitchen staff

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Prepares orders in the kitchen or at the bar from a preparation screen, and marks them ready.

## Company examples

Restaurants, take-away, bakeries with a counter, events. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- See every order in the right order, with its notes.
- Know what is late.

## Pains

- Paper tickets lost or unreadable.
- Rushes with no view of what is late.

## Daily tasks

- Prepare orders and mark them ready.
- Snooze an item that runs out.

## Monthly tasks

- None specific.

## Devices

A large touchscreen in the kitchen or at the bar, sometimes a ticket printer.

The large touchscreen is drawn at the desktop size, 1440 x 900 px
([D456](../../06-decisions/ux/d456-audit-images-and-criteria.md)).

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
