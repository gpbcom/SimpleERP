# 👤 U40 Farmer

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Runs a farm: plots and crops, herds, treatments and inputs, harvests, machinery, seasonal workers, aid declarations,
direct sales and deliveries to the cooperative, often with processing on the farm.

## Company examples

Farms, wine estates, farm shops, cooperatives' members. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Good yields at the right cost.
- Registers and aid files right without evenings of paperwork.

## Pains

- Registers on paper in the cab.
- Aid declarations once a year from scratch.
- Weather and prices.

## Daily tasks

- Fieldwork and animals, recorded from the phone.
- Sell at the farm or the market.

## Monthly tasks

- Aid declaration, cooperative statements, costs per crop.

## Devices

A phone in the field and the cab, often offline; a laptop at home.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
