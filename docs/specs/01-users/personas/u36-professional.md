# 👤 U36 Professional

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

A lawyer, chartered accountant, architect or engineer who runs client matters: advice, documents, deadlines, time and
fees, under professional secrecy and anti-money-laundering duties.

## Company examples

Law firms, accounting firms, architecture and engineering offices. See the
[example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Never miss a deadline.
- Bill the time really spent.

## Pains

- Time entered late and lost.
- Conflicts found too late.
- Documents in many versions.

## Daily tasks

- Work matters, record time.
- Check deadlines.
- Meet clients.

## Monthly tasks

- Bill, review work in progress, training hours.

## Devices

A laptop; a phone between meetings and hearings.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
