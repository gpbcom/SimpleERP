# 👤 U52 Beautician

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

A beauty and wellness professional in a salon or institute: serves booked clients in a cabin, sells care products,
follows packages and memberships, and earns a commission and tips.

## Company examples

Hair and beauty salons, spas, nail bars. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md); the sector's own
example company is written with the sector.

## Goals

- Know today's clients and what each booked.
- Sell products and packages without leaving the cabin.
- See their commission and tips.

## Pains

- Gaps and double bookings.
- Forgetting a client's balance of sessions.
- Commission statements nobody can check.

## Daily tasks

- Read the day's bookings.
- Record the services and the products used.
- Sell products and take payments at the desk.

## Monthly tasks

- Read the commission and tips statement.
- Check their targets.

## Devices

A tablet at the cabin or the desk; a phone between clients.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33 Design for an office worker](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)

the beauty and wellness sector pack.
