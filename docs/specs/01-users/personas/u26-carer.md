# 👤 U26 Carer

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Visits care recipients at home: help with daily life, meals, hygiene and company; checks in and out on the phone, writes
the care log, reports incidents.

## Company examples

Home-help and home-care services. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Know where to go, when, and what to do.
- Be paid for the time on the road.

## Pains

- Rounds changed at the last minute.
- Paper logs.
- No signal in some homes.

## Daily tasks

- Follow the day's rounds.
- Check in and out, fill the log.
- Report anything worrying.

## Monthly tasks

- Training, availability for next month.

## Devices

A phone, often offline in homes.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
