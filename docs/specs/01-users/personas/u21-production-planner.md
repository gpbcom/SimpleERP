# 👤 U21 Production planner

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Plans and follows production: bills of materials and routings, the master plan, capacity, manufacturing orders, quality
alerts, maintenance and production costs.

## Company examples

Manufacturing, food processing, workshops. See the [example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Deliver on time with the least stock.
- Keep machines and people busy, not overloaded.

## Pains

- Late components.
- Plans broken by a machine stop.
- Costs known only at the month's end.

## Daily tasks

- Review the plan and release orders.
- Follow late orders and alerts.
- Answer sales about delivery dates.

## Monthly tasks

- Master plan, capacity review, cost variances, maintenance plan.

## Devices

A desktop with a large screen; a phone for alerts.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
