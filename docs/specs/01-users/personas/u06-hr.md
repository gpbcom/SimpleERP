# 👤 U06 Human resources

**Status:** ✅ specified

## Who

Manages the employees' records, contracts, absences and their rights.

## Company examples

Mid-size companies; in small ones the owner or an office worker. See the
[example companies](../example-companies/README.md).

## Goals

- Keep every employee record complete and compliant.
- Answer employees' questions about their rights.

## Pains

- Labour rules that change and differ by collective agreement.
- Absence counters kept in spreadsheets.

## Daily tasks

- Handle absences and their counters.
- Answer employees.

## Monthly tasks

- Prepare payroll variables.
- Follow mandatory registers and thresholds.

## Devices

Desktop.

## Skill level

Office worker level or above: the screens need no training at that level.
[D33](../../06-decisions/ux/d33-design-for-an-office-worker.md)
