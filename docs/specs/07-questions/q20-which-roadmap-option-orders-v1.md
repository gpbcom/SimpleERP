# ❓ Q20 Which roadmap option orders V1

**Status:** ✅ answered

## Question

In which order is V1 built: the adopted ten steps (option A), parallel tracks (B), the pilot first (C), sector waves
(D), the factory first (E), or the combination of C, B and D (F)?

## Why it matters

Every option builds the same V1, but the order decides whether the dated pilot of
[D451](../06-decisions/vision/d451-dated-pilot-before-the-release.md) can start before 1 September 2027, how many
streams of work the maintainer reviews at once, and when the sectors are proved on their example companies. The options
are compared in [Roadmap options](../00-vision/roadmap/options/README.md).

## Options

- A: ten steps in a row, the adopted roadmap completed with T13 and SC17 to SC20.
- B: the platform, then parallel tracks.
- C: the pilot first, as a gate.
- D: sector waves.
- E: the factory first.
- F: the pilot spine of C, with B's tracks and D's sector waves; it keeps every decision of the owner.

## Owner

The owner of SimpleERP.

## Answer

The owner, 2026-11-27: option A, the ten build steps in a row, as cut into the twelve phases of
[Phases](../00-vision/roadmap/phases/README.md) (P0 Foundations, P1 DevOps agents, P2 Platform core T1 to T13, P3
Platform features, P4 to P11); the pilot's date of D451 is superseded by the phase calendar
([D569](../06-decisions/vision/d569-roadmap-option-a-in-twelve-phases.md)).
