# ❓ Q19 Which LinkedIn search sourcing uses

**Status:** ❓ open question

## Question

Which LinkedIn search does a sourcing search run on: the classic search of any account, Sales Navigator, or Recruiter,
which offer different criteria?

## Why it matters

The owner asked for a rich set of criteria
([D518](../06-decisions/f09-hr-and-projects/d518-linkedin-sourcing-through-unipile.md)). Years of experience, open to
work or past employers are not offered by every kind of LinkedIn account;
[UC-F9-40](../02-functional/f09-hr-and-projects/use-cases/02-recruit/uc-f09-40-search-and-rank-linkedin-profiles.md)
needs to know which criteria LinkedIn applies and which the AI assistant applies to the profiles returned.

## Options

- (Proposed) The search the HR user's account offers, as Unipile reports it; the criteria it lacks are applied by the AI
  assistant to the profiles returned, and the screen says which.
- The classic search only, the same for every company.
- Recruiter or Sales Navigator required for LinkedIn sourcing.

## Owner

The owner of simple-ERP.

## Answer

Open.
