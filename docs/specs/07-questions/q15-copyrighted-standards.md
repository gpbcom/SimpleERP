# ❓ Q15 Copyrighted technical standards

**Status:** ✅ answered

## Question

D205 asks that technical norms and standards be pulled from official sources with no human intervention, like the law.
AFNOR and ISO forbid putting their standards (and, for AFNOR, their metadata) into AI systems and opt out of text and
data mining. How does simple-ERP cover them?

## Why it matters

Published law and most official guidance can be pulled in full; standards such as NF DTU, NF C 15-100, Eurocodes or ISO
9001 cannot, without a licence. See [Sector technical standards](../05-non-functional/sector-technical-standards.md),
sections 18 and 19.

## Options

- (Recommended) Free law and guidance in full, pulled unattended; standards as a register (reference, title, edition,
  the laws that cite them, how to obtain them), from ISO Open Data and legal texts, with notes in our own words; a
  company may add its own licensed copies to its private corpus, opt-in, after attesting an AI-use licence; a licence
  with AFNOR sought later.
- Free law and the register only, no customer copies.
- Seek an AFNOR or ISO licence before shipping standards at all.

## Owner

The owner of simple-ERP.

## Answer

The owner, 2026-11-10: the recommended option, a register and companies' own licensed copies
([D207](../06-decisions/f15-legal-assistant/d207-copyrighted-standards-register.md)).
