# ❓ Q11 Establishment SIRET

**Status:** ✅ answered

## Question

In France each establishment has its own SIRET, which payroll declarations (DSN) and some mandatory registers use. D36
gives a location no legal identity: where is an establishment's SIRET kept?

## Why it matters

It decides whether payroll (F13) and HR registers (F9) can declare per establishment.

## Options

- A location may carry an optional SIRET, used only by payroll and registers (chosen, D453).
- Each establishment is a separate company in SimpleERP.
- Not needed.

## Owner

The owner of SimpleERP.

## Answer

Answered on 2026-11-12 by the owner: the SIRET is kept on the site, optional
([D453](../06-decisions/f01-administration/d453-optional-siret-on-a-site.md)).
