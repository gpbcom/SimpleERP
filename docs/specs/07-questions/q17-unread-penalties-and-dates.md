## Why it matters

A warning that names a penalty, or a rule applied from the wrong date, misleads the company; the pack must say plainly
what was not read (audit finding A7, 2026-11-12).

## Options

- Read by web-fetch agents in batches, as the other rules (D455).
- Read by a lawyer before launch (chosen for what the agents could not read).
- Shown as "not read" until then.

## Owner

The owner of SimpleERP.

## Answer

Answered on 2026-11-27. The legal agents read the penalties and start dates in official sources in four passes after
[D572](../06-decisions/foundations/d572-proposals-confirmed-in-bulk.md), and the rules they read left these lists: on
2026-11-12 the lists held 75 rules without a penalty and 138 without a start date. The rules still listed above have a
penalty no article states or a start date no official page gives: they are reviewed by a lawyer before launch
([D574](../06-decisions/foundations/d574-lawyer-review-before-launch.md)) and are on the lawyer's checklist in
[Waiting for the owner](../09-waiting/README.md). Until the lawyer has read them, each rule says plainly what was not
read.
