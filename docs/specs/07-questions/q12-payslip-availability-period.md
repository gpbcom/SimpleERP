# ❓ Q12 Payslip availability period

**Status:** ✅ answered

## Question

For how long must a former employee be able to get their payslips, and so how long does their read-only access (D74)
stay open? For electronic payslips, the Code du travail is believed to require availability for 50 years or until the
employee turns 75; this must be checked.

Read on 2026-11-12 on service-public F559 and F10029: the employer keeps its copy 5 years; electronic payslips are
available 50 years from issue, or a second option the two pages state differently (6 years after retirement on F559,
until age 75 on F10029); neither page names the article (D3243-8 was not found cited). SimpleERP keeps the 50-year
option, which satisfies both ([D445](../06-decisions/f13-payroll/d445-payslips-fifty-years-from-issue.md)).

## Why it matters

It sets how long the former-employee access lasts, and the payroll plugin's (F13) archive.

## Options

- The legal period, once checked (chosen: 50 years from issue, D445).
- A shorter period, with documents sent on request after it.

## Owner

The owner of SimpleERP.

## Answer

Answered on 2026-11-12 by the owner: former employees keep read-only access to their payslips for 50 years from issue
([D445](../06-decisions/f13-payroll/d445-payslips-fifty-years-from-issue.md)). The article behind the pages' second
option is still to read, in CR-FR-182.
