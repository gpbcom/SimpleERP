# ❓ Q10 Field worker skill level

**Status:** ✅ answered

## Question

Field workers and carers often use software rarely, while the design target is an office worker (D33). Do their phone
screens need the level of a rare software user?

## Why it matters

It sets how simple the self-service phone screens of U14 must be.

## Options

- Yes: the field worker's phone screens are designed for a rare software user (the proposal, chosen).
- No: the office worker level applies everywhere.

## Owner

The owner of simple-ERP.
