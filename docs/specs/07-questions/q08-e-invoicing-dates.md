# ❓ Q8 E-invoicing dates

**Status:** ✅ answered

## Question

French e-invoicing dates: receiving through a certified platform from 2026 for every company, sending from 2027 for
small and medium ones. Check the dates in force, since they decide when B1 and B2 must support it.

## Why it matters

It decides when F6 (customers and sales) and F7 (purchasing and stock) must support electronic invoices.

## Options

Not discussed yet: the dates in force must be checked first.

## Owner

The owner of SimpleERP.

## Answer

Answered by the research of 2026-11-10 (official sources): receiving for every company from 1 September 2026; issuing
from 1 September 2026 for large and mid-size companies, and from 1 September 2027 for small and micro companies;
e-reporting on the same timetable. See
[E-invoicing platforms](../05-non-functional/e-invoicing-platforms.md#rules-in-force).
