# ❓ Q2 NF525 certification

**Status:** ✅ answered

## Question

French point-of-sale software must be certified (NF525). Who gets the certification, and when?

## Why it matters

It decides whether the point-of-sale plugin (F10) can be sold in France, and when.

## Options

- Certify before French sale.
- Certify later, rules now.
- Outside France first.

## Owner

The owner of SimpleERP.

## Answer

Certify before selling in France: built to the till rules of the country pack from the first version, sold in France
once certified by an accredited body; see [D296](../06-decisions/f10-point-of-sale/d296-till-certified-before-france.md)
and
[CR-FR-024 Till software holds a certificate or an attestation](../10-country-packs/france/point-of-sale/cr-fr-024-certified-till-software.md).
